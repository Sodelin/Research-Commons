## Overview

The programme separates seven jobs: **G1** compresses structure without losing the declared information; **G2** proves that the actual stochastic process and observations fit together; **G3** asks whether data have any admitted finite positive source; **G4** asks when finitely many legal observations determine one fixed target against all rivals; **G5** recovers a specified structural target; **G6** controls approximation and uncertainty; **G7** characterizes legal experiment strategies and their cost.

October did not turn all seven into one finished empirical solver. It did:
- complete and preserve major original G1, documented G2 and binary-M3 G5 proof packages;
- retain complete **hand characterizations** of G6 and G7 while advancing their distinct formal/native components;
- close important G4 COMMON/BOTH branches without closing finite INDEPENDENT-only forcing;
- develop source-faithful G3 tools and rule out specific shortcuts, without a general terminating NO procedure;
- produce substantial sequence, observation-model, quantum/materials, measurement and empirical-method results;
- turn several newer cross-field arguments into complete **proposed source chains**, which still need checking;
- record negative findings and corrections instead of counting them as successful master solutions.

### Evidence key

- **Inherited:** already established before the event being described; recovery or reuse is not a new theorem.
- **Hand reviewed:** a proof reviewed at stated assumptions, generally by a coordinating AI research context, not external human peer review.
- **Lean accepted:** named source/context checks actually ran with a specified receipt. It does not mean the entire repository was rebuilt.
- **Executed finite check:** specified code/data cases ran; neither a general proof nor empirical validity follows automatically.
- **Source-only / UNELABORATED:** proof/code text exists, possibly with static review, but no successful new Lean check.
- **Open:** the exact master implication is still missing. A component, candidate, finite search or prior-work match does not remove it.
- **Novelty unassessed:** mathematical correctness and historical originality are separate.
