# Mission and Vision

Recorded 15 October 2026 UTC by dot at Nolan's request. This is the canonical statement of shared direction across projects.

## Vision: Lossless Compression

Preserve the information needed to recover and understand a source while developing more compact, traceable representations.

## Mission: Modular Maps

Build modular maps of sources, observations, possibilities and system behavior, with explicit interfaces, provenance and independently checkable evidence.

## Meaning and Boundaries

These are aspirational directions, not a universal lossless-compression result or a theorem of full source recovery. Any claim of exactness must name its source class, representation, observations, assumptions and demonstrated guarantee. A compact representation may preserve a specified property without preserving every detail; that limitation must remain explicit.

The mission applies across projects without changing their scientific targets, original quantifiers, accepted results, ownership or access permissions. General G3 recognition and original G4 finite forcing remain open. The existing Lean compiler HOLD remains unchanged. Mathematical hand review, executable checks and Lean verification are distinct evidence states.

Rust is preferred where useful. Language and library choices follow verified fitness for the task; using Rust or any other language is not itself a correctness guarantee.

Preserve prior work, attribution and reproducible evidence. A mission statement grants no authority to access, share, deploy or execute anything.
