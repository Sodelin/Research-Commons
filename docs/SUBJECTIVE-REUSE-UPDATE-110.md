### 1. CB-MEMORY-003: exact stochastic summaries preserve adaptive records

**Why it enters:** It supplies a concrete reusable replacement contract: push the initial law, commute the transition kernels, decode observations, and factor the actual controller through the retained history. Then the entire finite joint observation/action record is preserved, not merely separate marginal predictions. The correlated-sensor treatment and equal-traces-without-a-commuting-profile-kernel counterexample make it useful both constructively and as a guard against an invalid reduction.

**Consequence:** Before replacing a probabilistic process with a smaller one, there is an explicit checklist for whether downstream adaptive experiments still have the same law.

**Limit:** This is arbitrary-carrier, countably supported PMF mathematics, not automatically continuous-kernel or infinite-path theory. Omitted controller/sensor state can invalidate the application. It preserves the declared experiment, not reconstruction of every original hidden history.

**Attribution/evidence:** Strong lumpability and stochastic abstraction are classical. Its value here is the explicit completed source contract and archived checked implementation, not a new general probability principle. [Current exact card](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/b121ad1e68878cceb412d68b99adc6ed9c4cddb3/docs/CLAIM-BOARD.md#cb-memory-003); [original stochastic source](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/research/open-problems/time-self-reference/predictive-memory/StochasticAbstraction.lean).

### 2. CB-TRANSFER-002: local error budgets control the whole history

**Why it enters:** It is a small, quantitative interface that many sequential arguments need: initial and every-history conditional TV budgets give the sharp prefix bound one minus the product of the agreement budgets, with the consistent infinite-path extension proved separately. It explains why small persistent local errors can still lead to completely distinguishable infinite records.

**Consequence:** Once the actual conditional-law adapter is justified, attach one explicit whole-record error budget rather than checking isolated times or vaguely saying the simulator is close.

**Limit:** Matching one-time marginals or choosing incompatible simulators at different prefixes is insufficient. The PMF microscopic/posterior-mixture and policy adapter reserved by MEMORY-003 is not automatically finished by this theorem. Nor does its standard-Borel path construction automatically cover arbitrary carriers. The separate almost-everywhere-to-all-parameter result retains its fixed-kernel, full-support and continuity hypotheses.

**Attribution/evidence:** Classical coupling/common-submeasure and continuity methods; completed scoped hand proof, no Lean verification of this whole theorem. This is the already accepted quantitative supplier for MEMORY-003's approximate target, not a second discovery of that same finite bound. [Current exact card](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/b121ad1e68878cceb412d68b99adc6ed9c4cddb3/docs/CLAIM-BOARD.md#cb-transfer-002); [full path proof](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f87a6c50029c1ae13f0340818b3bf358b1bf3de3/research/2026-09-30-general-transfer-closure/continuity-and-paths.md).

### 3. CB-TRANSFER-001: one experiment can simulate another without necessarily recovering its answer

**Why it enters:** This supplies the widest conceptual gate of the three: require one parameter-independent ordinary kernel, distinguish that from abstract comparison, and separate law simulation from measurable answer recovery. The corresponding decision-risk interpretation makes the contract useful beyond one biological family.

**Consequence:** An attempted transfer can state exactly which experiment is simulated, with which common rule, and what downstream decisions actually inherit the guarantee.

**Limit:** Ordinary-kernel attainment needs the stated common domination and standard-Borel target assumptions. The abstract nondominated theorem is not an ordinary measurable simulator; existence is not an efficient sampler, physical implementation, or automatic source-to-target correspondence.

**Attribution/evidence:** This is explicitly Blackwell–Le Cam and related classical representation theory, with a completed written proof/boundary audit. High reuse here is not a claim of newly invented theory. [Current exact card](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/b121ad1e68878cceb412d68b99adc6ed9c4cddb3/docs/CLAIM-BOARD.md#cb-transfer-001); [dominated comparison proof](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f87a6c50029c1ae13f0340818b3bf358b1bf3de3/research/2026-09-30-general-transfer-closure/dominated.md).

## Why not a fourth change?

**TRANSFER-003 is the practical reserve, not an automatic entrant.** Its exact rational checker certifies the actual returned shared kernel over a supplied compact parameter cover. Separating a numerical proposal from an exact acceptance check is highly reusable. But the cover, valid moduli, law-error certificates and sampling provenance are external premises; the safe fallback can be uninformative, and exact zero need not terminate. If the intended use shifts from composing mathematical arguments to building finite-alphabet certification tools, I would swap TRANSFER-001 for TRANSFER-003, not promote all four simply because they were recovered together. Its optimization, concentration and comparison ingredients remain classical. [Exact certificate scope](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/b121ad1e68878cceb412d68b99adc6ed9c4cddb3/docs/CLAIM-BOARD.md#cb-transfer-003).

MEMORY-001 is another close reserve: deterministic controlled quotients and its actual restriction adapters are valuable, but MEMORY-003 already carries a proved deterministic point-mass adapter and is my broader stochastic selection. That does not duplicate or erase MEMORY-001's separate predictive-quotient universal property.

The sequence, feedback, genome, population, Alexander and Wong additions have important exact results and source adapters, but on this broad reuse criterion their observation/model contracts are generally narrower. The Cross-Scale conceptual framework is useful for questions and planning, but its proposed comparative research benefit has not been evaluated. None is downgraded in correctness or novelty because it misses this subjective list.
