## 1. Strongest existing end-to-end chain

For **actual source → legal observations → identifying policy → exact resource optimum**, the strongest already-proved integration inspected here is the **G7 complete-original-registry exact-design theorem**, with a direct **NANUQ output/readout composition** on the common admitted source class [G7, NANUQ]. The G7 accepted hand proof already composes:

**Complete original-source census → joint-forest polynomial observation compiler → same-source transcript consistency → rank/deletion reduction → QE/CAD policy extraction → exact feasible path-resource frontier, or an admitted-source lower certificate.**

This is not merely an abstract game waiting for a mathematical biological provider. The accepted proof supplies the census, actual shared-source compiler and winning-action selection.

Its precise contract matters:

- **Source:** finite binary rooted-LSA outer-labelled-planar cut-child networks with `n ≥ 2`, parallel arcs allowed, positive finite coalescent lengths and interior inheritance. A complete registry of exactly `r` original hybrid IDs and ordered parents is supplied. Each rival uses one graph/parameter assignment for its entire response history.
- **Observation/action:** fixed finite contemporaneous sampling/readout menu; finite unranked genealogy-topology laws; original-ID controls; effectively semialgebraic affine randomized programmes with exact responses. COMMON or INDEPENDENT may be specified, or belong to a finite tagged union.
- **Target:** displayed quartet/split union, or another declared finite computable graph target.
- **Cost:** executed exact-law calls and unions of positively supported sites/configurations along a trajectory, with reset/deletion closure. This is not sequencing sample complexity, physical actuation cost without calibration, installed-library size, or unrestricted expected stopping time.
- **Conclusion/evidence:** terminating exact-real mathematical policy/Pareto construction or response-consistent admitted-source obstruction. Independently hand accepted; named formal components are checked. The complete formal/native policy-engine assembly and large executions are separate validation/engineering work, not missing hand mathematics [Catalogue].

The complete registry bounds the **original** graph. G1's bounded decorated core cannot replace this input: its physical serial words and original IDs need not be bounded.

**The positive NANUQ join:** choose the declared finite computable G7 target to be precisely NANUQ's complete distinct-displayed-quartet response table, with pruning and deduplication rather than switching multiplicities. On sources satisfying both programmes' admission hypotheses, in particular NANUQ's `n ≥ 4` structural class, a successful G7 policy returns that table. The accepted NANUQ construction then computes its circular distance and exact displayed-split support. No conversion of raw concordance factors into quartet sets is being assumed. An infeasible supplied menu instead receives G7's lower certificate. This is ordinary reuse of the accepted output contracts, not a new identification theorem or a new formal integration receipt. NANUQ's support-query complexity is **not** the physical exact-law cost of the G7 policy.

## 3. What an all-component biological workflow would still need

The inspected records do **not** establish one universal pipeline from measured biological data through all six components to a cost-optimal exact answer. The missing composite interface is specific, not a reason to reopen its components:

1. A finite-data acquisition/action adapter with actual statistical and cost guarantees. The G7-to-NANUQ route above is already an ideal **exact-law** route when its supplied menu is identifying; it is not a finite-sequence provider or a way to replace the complete registry by an unknown-size source. A direct physical implementation of NANUQ's support-query learner would likewise need its exact oracle contract justified, not inferred from a sequence-likelihood or concordance-factor normalization.
2. A declared common experiment linking G5's full timed-law input to G7's finite unranked exact-law actions, or an explicitly different shared target/readout route. No such conversion follows merely from the common split-union target.
3. An empirical acquisition/error and actuator model satisfying G6's actual robust-fibre assumptions, together with any desired native implementation and calibration. A robust fibre containing incompatible targets cannot be silently assigned a guaranteed answer.

No argument here closes unknown-source G3 recognition/global termination or the finite INDEPENDENT-only G4 bridge. No combination above needs those gaps reopened to retain its already accepted conclusion.

**Plain-language conclusion.** Much of the proposed programme already exists: there is a proved ideal-experiment design engine whose successful quartet-table output can feed NANUQ, a checked small-timed-panel identification chain, a sharp structural querying programme, and an honest theory of what noisy evidence can certify. These are reusable parts with real integrations, not isolated ideas. The remaining all-stack question is whether one actual experiment satisfies their different input contracts and costs. Calling that entire empirical workflow established would overstate it; calling the completed mathematical components unfinished would understate it.
