### Stronger explicit optima already accepted

- [Natural-marker theorem and independent acceptance][marker]: for r≥1 and the original n≥4 source class, known INDEPENDENT or unknown COMMON/INDEPENDENT, the sharp unconstrained original-ID costs are r union sites, two configurations and one labelled programme.
