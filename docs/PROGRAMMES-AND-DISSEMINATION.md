# Programmes, completed results and Get It Out

27 October 2026 · dot (OpenAI), following the project owner's organizational direction.

## Five distinct objects

1. **Programme:** an ongoing creative/research inquiry. It provides a purpose and scope, not a requirement to finish every possible question before publishing anything.
2. **Project:** a bounded question or production task within one or more programmes, with an explicit stopping condition and owner.
3. **Claim and evidence:** a precise assertion with assumptions, prior-work attribution, proof or observation, review status and stable ID in the existing claim register. A complete hand proof remains complete when formalization is absent; a partial component does not close its master.
4. **Deliverable:** a finite paper, subtitle edition, audit, reproducible package or other reader-facing artifact with its own acceptance criteria.
5. **Dissemination:** presenting that deliverable to a specified audience through an appropriate archive, journal, repository or collaboration. Public availability, endorsement, peer review, acceptance and payment are separate events.

**Get It Out is the cross-programme pipeline, not another research programme.** It runs alongside research. A manuscript can close at its declared scope while its parent programme continues. Extensions belong in separate linked work items, not an endlessly moving publication threshold.

## Publication readiness is a separate contract

For every candidate output record: audience; exact result and exclusions; canonical claim IDs; original source/version; claimed contribution versus prior work; actual human and AI roles; review and execution receipts; release checklist; unresolved risks; next action; destination and permission state.

The gates are:

1. Freeze the claim and all quantifiers, assumptions and exclusions.
2. Audit correctness and the evidence chain at the level actually claimed.
3. Audit prior work and contribution. Correctness alone is not novelty.
4. Prepare a self-contained manuscript and reproduction material. A mathematical paper normally needs a motivated question, precise definitions and theorem, prior-work comparison, complete proof or inspectable computer-assisted certificate, limitations and references.
5. Establish truthful human accountability and venue-specific AI, authorship, prior-publication and archival policies. An AI audit is not independent human review. Do not invent human proof checking or add honorary authors.
6. Obtain the needed submission authorization, then record submitted, revised, accepted and published as distinct evidence-backed states.
7. Update the claim ledger's dissemination links and accurate CV/bibliographic status. Neither a preprint nor a curator approval is automatically a peer-reviewed journal article.

An output is **ready for submission** only when its checklist is satisfied and its proposed claims match its evidence. It is **submitted** only with a receipt. It is **accepted** only with the venue's decision. A usable restoration edition instead needs exact edition/source identity, readable synchronized display, explicit unresolved material and a tested delivery package. An audit's completion refers to its declared corpus and review layers, not verification of every underlying historical fact.

## Current policy examples, not venue selection

The [London Mathematical Society AI policy](https://www.lms.ac.uk/publications/policies/AI), checked 27 October 2026, permits responsible AI assistance, requires an AI declaration covering its contribution and leaves human authors accountable. AI cannot be an author; editorial discretion remains. This does not establish that an LMS journal is the best venue for this particular theorem.

[arXiv registration](https://info.arxiv.org/help/registerhelp.html) allows Independent affiliation. [Endorsement](https://info.arxiv.org/help/endorsement.html) may be required and is not peer review. Institutional affiliation must be accurate; an alumnus does not automatically publish on behalf of the institution. No account, submission, outreach or endorsement request is performed by this document.
