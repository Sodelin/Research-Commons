# RNA programme: what is proved, what connects, and what still needs a source bridge

**Dated evidence map: 23 October 2026.** Prepared by dot (OpenAI), research assistance for Nolan, from the completed source-recovery reports and their pinned original artifacts. This is a navigation and evidence reconciliation, not a new theorem, new build, or empirical RNA result.

## In plain language

The programme has several completed mathematical pieces: processing a represented scaffold, summing declared derivation weights, relating an admitted sampler to an independently defined RNA ensemble, checking leading-class certificates, tracking coordinates and partner ownership, and bounding the bias of an exact finite sampling grid. They answer different questions. Together they provide useful interfaces for a reliable RNA tool, but they are **not yet a proof that the actual C++ executable samples the intended physical distribution or certifies its leading classes**.

The remaining work is mainly to establish the connecting premises for the same real implementation, inputs, weights, outputs and randomness. Those missing connections do not reopen the completed conditional theorems. Nor do component proofs, historical checks or diagnostic tests demonstrate biological prediction accuracy.

**Publication snapshot:** all six groups are now indexed in the [118-card register][register], published and read back at commit `72c43ec17e8e1cce2e306d386ac0465f7e00273d` (claims blob `ebe7a376366fbd787767275404371d4497d1128e`). The newly indexed [CB-RNA-005 decoder/BE group][card005] and [CB-RNA-006 grid group][card006] preserve the already completed October 2 mathematics and its exact premises. Their publication does not close the implementation joins below. This map does not add further claim groups.

## 1. Six pieces, with their actual contracts

### RNA001: represented scaffold, parser and partner-table connection

The accepted chain starts from an independently specified balanced `.x()` scaffold and models character/stack processing, bounded close actions, close-to-open followed by open-to-close partner writes, the represented partner-table filter, and a fixed generated-pair observable. Its normalized root carrier excludes VP_CLOSED and VP_DIRECT parent events; raw children can still contain VP_DIRECT.

The **represented partner/scaffold relation is already proved**. Actual C++ strings, vectors, context reads, helper behavior and size/arithmetic/resource conditions are separate implementation premises. This is not a full generated-output or physical-weight theorem. [Source and independent parser/carrier review][parser].

### RNA002: an independent ensemble's ideal class law

The supported finite-trace theorem permits nonnegative factors and terminal weights with **one positive starting partition**. The independent Γ ensemble is not defined merely as “whatever the program emits”: it has its own scaffold/extension geometry, matching and endpoint constraints, inclusive-span component density at most two, hard-unpaired conditions, and independently supplied energy with positive R and T.

The admission interface requires positive-output coverage and injectivity, lossless rendering and classifier agreement, and trace weight equal to **one input/root-wide positive constant κ** times the intended weight. Both zero complements are handled. It concludes the selected-class probability as the weight sum over the entire independent Γ ensemble divided by its partition.

Its energy interface is not an implemented DP09 model; source support, weights and classification must actually meet the admission. It supplies neither an efficient class-sum algorithm nor an implemented random generator. [Exact class-law theorem][gamma-source] and [admission review][gamma].

### RNA003: the ordered weighted interpreter

For every finite item set, the reference observer-bearing ascending update loop computes the full **derivation-occurrence** weight sum in an arbitrary semiring with decidable equality. Multiplication and child occurrences are ordered; no positivity order or commutative multiplication is assumed. Duplicate deduction occurrences and repeated child slots are retained.

Two load-bearing premises are universal: **every provider occurrence's children come strictly earlier**, including skipped deductions, and **every callback preserves the whole provider/allowed/local-weight semantic view**, not only the callbacks seen in one test. The adjoining valid-child adapter proves a specific normalization correspondence, not arbitrary-input validity. A later source-footprint audit exists, but is not a C++ memory/alias/refinement theorem. [Final interpreter endpoint][interpreter-source] and [dependency/compile manifest package][interpreter]; [source-footprint audit][frame].

### RNA004: sound leading-class certificates

For a finite discovered set and a selected subset of size k, valid intervals on every discovered class and a valid upper bound on **every undiscovered class** yield a strict top-k **set** when selected lower bounds exceed all outsider bounds. Simultaneous validity at every time permits a successful finite data-selected-time certificate; it does not supply the simultaneous event's probability or eventual success. A proved all-class discrepancy η widens the bounds appropriately.

Separately, exact class sums from one finite nonnegative weighted ensemble with positive partition leave residual mass that bounds each omitted class. A strict residual threshold certifies the selected set. These are sufficient tests, not an internal ranking or tie resolver. Class discovery, concentration, termination and an actual class-SUM engine remain separate. [Certificate, stability and residual sources][certificates].

### RNA005: bounded root decoding and BE ownership

For each supplied finite slot-indexed root tree using the **17-family/57-rule** schema, valid keys, family dispatch and numeric guards give exact bounded parent-event coordinates. Repeated slots remain distinct. Additional primitive endpoint-origin premises propagate ownership of every reached BE outer and anchor pair: partner(i)=j or partner(j)=i. An arbitrary partner function is not thereby proved an involution or matching. Same-anchor leaves inherit ownership from their callers.

These are **two linked proved conclusions**, not a new conjunction theorem. Coordinate conversion does not establish legal RNA geometry, full emitted-output equality or actual factory membership. The provider's right-coordinate progress, including its same-right BE exception, is **not** RNA003's strict earlier-child schedule. Finiteness comes from the supplied tree, not a proved executable fuel bound. [Decoder source][decoder-source], [ownership source][ownership-source] and [BE source review][decoder].

### RNA006: exact finite-grid sampling bias

For an ordered nonnegative table with positive sum, strict prefix crossing on an exact uniform **N-point midpoint grid, N>0,** gives half-open selector fibers. Cumulative error is at most 1/(2N), individual branch error at most 1/N, and table total variation at most (b−1)/(2N).

The completed finite-trace wrapper yields class-probability error at most **d(b−1)/(2N)** under exact arithmetic, the same state/successor/continuation model, positive partitions **at every state and remaining depth**, a literal equivalence of **all** length-d traces with outputs, and exact pointwise source-weight equality. Here d is the total number of sequential stochastic production steps, not nesting height or RNA length. Uniform grid input must hold conditionally on the preceding history.

At MAXLOOP=30, the source-inspired Sample_V count gives at most 498 slots, hence local 497/(2N), only after its guarded executed-vector correspondence is established. It is not an all-routine bound. N=2^52 is a mathematical specialization, not an IEEE or pseudorandom-generator certificate. [Final grid-class endpoint][grid-source] and [selector-to-class package][grid].

## 2. Which arrows are already proved?

The generic linear dε error accumulation is reused classical kernel comparison, not a new TV result; the register already includes a stronger common-history comparison in CB-TRANSFER-002.

**Actual imports or proved internal compositions**

- RNA003: observer-frame theorem → scheduled hypergraph correctness → ordered derivation sum. The child-normalization adapter is a separately proved supplier, **not an import into that chain**.
- RNA002: finite trace law → supported trace law → paper-observable bridge → independent Γ admission. Its child-adapter import supplies a datatype, not a whole executable-admission theorem.
- RNA005: child-schema definitions and provider-boundary contract → root decoder → BE ownership. The decoder also imports Γ's SourceEvent datatype; this does not import a proof that its outputs satisfy Γ admission.
- RNA006: exact selector fibers/counting → normalized grid production kernel, combined with ideal finite traceback and conditional-kernel error composition → the stated weighted-class error endpoint.
- RNA004: the stability and residual modules import the deterministic certificate provider and prove their displayed conditional consequences.

**Useful prospective applications, not already proved end-to-end joins**

- RNA001 and RNA005 could help admit actual source inputs/trees to RNA003 and RNA002. The source must still satisfy their respective execution, geometry and support premises. Right-coordinate bounds cannot replace a strict schedule.
- RNA003's derivation sum is not automatically RNA002's sum over distinct structures. Multiplicity, complete support, independent weights and rendering/classification still have to agree.
- RNA002 does **not automatically upgrade RNA006**. Root positivity and a positive-support bijection do not supply the grid wrapper's global positive partitions or whole-trace equivalence. A common admitted continuation/kernel model, with justified dead-state handling or a proved support-restricted replacement, remains necessary.
- A valid RNA006 law-discrepancy bound could feed RNA004's η-widening interface. It would not create simultaneous statistical coverage, an exact class-SUM procedure or a stopping guarantee.

These distinctions are retained in the [later reconciled E8 scope][scope]. Listing related modules together is not an integration proof.

## 3. Exact remaining implementation and application joins

| Join | What still has to be established for the actual tool |
|---|---|
| Source and execution | Actual factory/input/child/callback behavior implements the represented contracts; an appropriate root-reachable carrier, valid schedule, complete pending-continuation model and total stochastic-step bound are supplied. Manual schema definitions and finite supplied trees are not those proofs. |
| Physical weights | Implemented local factors, multiplicities and denominators agree with the independently intended energy law, up to the permitted single root-wide κ where applicable. Numeric, transcendental, overflow and normalization errors need their own treatment. |
| Support and outputs | Actual positive traces cover the intended independent ensemble with the required injectivity/zero-support handling, and decoded outputs preserve the required geometry. The grid wrapper's stronger whole-carrier interface must not be silently substituted. |
| Renderer and classifier | The actual rendered, potentially colored representation yields the intended fixed class function. Equality of uncolored pair maps alone is insufficient for the inspected L5 classifier. |
| Randomness and arithmetic | Actual grid generation has the required conditional law given the full prior history; real-number scan formulas refine the actual arithmetic. Fixed seeds, regressions or correct one-step marginals alone do not establish this. |
| Class-SUM and confidence | Exact or certified inverse-image **SUM** uses the same support, multiplicities, weights and classifier; MaxProduct is not that operation. Alternatively, the actual sampler must provide the required simultaneous all-class/all-time uncertainty event. Discovery, stopping and sample complexity are not supplied by conditional certificate soundness. |

## 4. Three original diagnostics, and no broader conclusion

**VPR factor mismatch.** The pinned SCFG2 source used a left-gap factor k−i for a VPR split whose inspected reference formula uses the right gap j−k. The historical one-line intervention, with the baseline preserved, was tested on bounded fixtures and restored the reported scale behavior. A later public-source inspection found the analogous k−i expression in PRISM's partition and sampling VPR routines, while its minimum-energy counterpart used j−k. Thus inside/sampler agreement alone need not establish agreement with the intended physical weight. The recorded SCFG2 tests are not an all-input theorem, a test of an unchanged PRISM executable, or an upstream repair. [Original source/equation and intervention record][vpr]; [PRISM comparison and broader bounded regression][vpr-prism].

**Raw auxiliary schedule failure, not root failure.** A saved 21-position unit-natural interpreter fixture found a WMBP cell at index 3311 reading a future BE cell at index 3662: cached value 7, final-child recurrence value 9. The root answer was 207 under both examined orders, and the inspected raw root cone had no future-child dependency. This refutes the unqualified **all-cache** earlier-child premise for that raw provider/schedule. It does not refute the conditional interpreter theorem, the root law, or the actual physical-energy API. The fixture's alternative order is not an all-input repair. [Original countercheck and execution limits][schedule].

**Pair map versus L5 rendering.** The independently reviewed unchanged classifier slice gave L5 `()` for `((....))` and `(())` for `([....])`, although both encode the same uncolored pair set {(0,7),(1,6)}; both reported L6 outputs were empty. This defeats arbitrary recoloring invariance for that exact L5 slice. It does **not** exhibit two positive outputs of one fixed source instance or refute the conditional Γ theorem. A correct canonical/source-aware rendering bridge remains possible but must be proved. The historical review records its own rebuild/run of the slice, not a full-engine certificate. [Independent classifier-slice review][classifier].

No diagnostic was rerun for this map. These are attributed, scoped archived checks, not new accusations or novelty claims.
