# Public corpus coverage roadmap

**Snapshot: 23 October 2026. Prepared by dot (OpenAI), for Nolan.**

This roadmap distinguishes recovered completed research from unfinished **indexing or evidence recovery**. It does not estimate how many discoveries exist, assign a completion percentage, or certify every proof in the corpus.

The [canonical register][claims] contains **104 scoped cards** at commit **f87a6c50029c1ae13f0340818b3bf358b1bf3de3**, version 1.11-alexander-source-recovery. Original proof dates, recorded checks and narrower source scopes remain authoritative. The three Alexander additions are now published, preserving their original completion and novelty boundaries. Remaining recovery work below is not a request to re-prove completed mathematics.

## Five different evidence stages

1. **Source preserved:** a public source exists at a specified immutable pin. This does not imply every dependency has been fetched or checked.
2. **Body read:** actual arguments or formal bodies were inspected at the stated scope. A ledger, README or build total alone is not a whole-proof reading.
3. **Status reconciled:** the exact theorem was compared with its review/receipt and later corrections. Mathematical completion, formal checking, empirical support and novelty remain separate.
4. **Card mapped:** the same scoped statement has an actual published card, a prepared insertion, or a precise remaining mapping step. Several source families can legitimately share one card.
5. **Contribution traced:** source attribution, published questions and method antecedents are recorded separately from the original question-to-proof chronology. The latter has **not** been reconstructed corpus-wide; authorship or priority must not be inferred from a build receipt.

In the table, “scoped body/status reconciled” reports the accumulated bounded source readings recorded by the linked cards, not a new transitive proof audit. Every original Samuel row has an immutable public source link. The [companion TSV](CORPUS-COVERAGE-SAMUEL32.tsv) records each row's preservation, exact reading scope, status, card and attribution stage separately.

### What those mappings do not collapse

- T05/T06 share a card, but minimum-width **adjacency rigidity**, exact **fair encoding**, and **conditional full-schedule periodic realization** are different statements. Finite slots do not force periodicity.
- T08/T09 share a card, but productive pruning preserves the **entire infinite language** while deletion-only repair preserves **avoidance** and can disconnect the graph.
- A01 is not an alias of the already-published capped-host [CB-ALEXANDER-001][CB-ALEXANDER-001]. Terminal cloning and A03's complete blow-up preserve different properties. A02/A03 retain the [public classical-method/novelty qualification][ordinal-entry], without downgrading the completed mathematics.
- B05 is a preserved precursor within B06's completed group, not another full result. B04's written source-transport/common-limit proof is not silently promoted into its narrower Lean components.
- M01/M03 are predecessor and continuation. M04's countably supported PMF theorem is not a general continuous-kernel result. M05 is a completed general written proof, not a Lean endpoint.
- Formal audit totals are endpoint/declaration counts with reused suppliers, not discovery counts. New archival reading does not constitute a new compiler run or external peer review.

## Finish a recovery without reopening completed mathematics

For a selected family, preserve its original source contract and attribution, inspect the relevant argument, identify the exact acceptance/successor and claimed receipt, and either link the existing matching card or prepare one justified grouped entry. Record missing source bytes, limited reading, unresolved priority and genuine theorem gaps **as different things**. Public problem-site visibility, recorded checks, mathematical review and empirical validation are also different evidence layers.

This roadmap changes no theorem status, register object or subjective ranking. It is a dated coverage guide, not an exhaustive corpus census.
