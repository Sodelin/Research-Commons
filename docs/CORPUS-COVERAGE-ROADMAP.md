## Five different evidence stages

1. **Source preserved:** a public source exists at a specified immutable pin. This does not imply every dependency has been fetched or checked.
2. **Body read:** actual arguments or formal bodies were inspected at the stated scope. A ledger, README or build total alone is not a whole-proof reading.
3. **Status reconciled:** the exact theorem was compared with its review/receipt and later corrections. Mathematical completion, formal checking, empirical support and novelty remain separate.
4. **Card mapped:** the same scoped statement has an actual published card, a prepared insertion, or a precise remaining mapping step. Several source families can legitimately share one card.
5. **Contribution traced:** source attribution, published questions and method antecedents are recorded separately from the original question-to-proof chronology. The latter has **not** been reconstructed corpus-wide; authorship or priority must not be inferred from a build receipt.

In the table, “scoped body/status reconciled” reports the accumulated bounded source readings recorded by the linked cards, not a new transitive proof audit. Every original Samuel row has an immutable public source link. The [companion TSV](CORPUS-COVERAGE-SAMUEL32.tsv) records each row's preservation, exact reading scope, status, card and attribution stage separately.
