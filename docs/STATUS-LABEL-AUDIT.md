## Repeated work is not all the same

Record whether a repeated route adds nothing, supplies an independent proof/check, sharpens a bound or assumption, establishes a new application/adapter, or excludes a previously viable route. Recovery and integration can also be useful without being new mathematics. Do not count an already accepted result as newly discovered; do not discard a genuine improvement merely because it shares a starting point.
