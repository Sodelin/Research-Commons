# Candidate questions worth retaining

- Date/session: 2026-09-30 UTC / commons-builder
- Project: cross-scale causal formalization
- Label: hypothesis
- Source: Nolan's shadows, changing-lens, and missed-scope ideas; assistant interpretations below.

Three directions remain distinct:

1. **Identification:** Which permitted observations distinguish the outcome we need?
2. **Transfer:** Which state and intervention mappings preserve that outcome between levels?
3. **Discovery:** Can a deliberate change of representation reveal a consequential target that ordinary checking misses?

A promising combined target is the least-cost probe that certifies a useful decision despite bounded calibration drift. A different target is whether attributed cross-chat handoffs improve candidate generation rather than only prevent information loss.

These proposals are not novelty claims. Keep alternatives and counterexamples rather than forcing everything into one self-similarity theorem. Next: see the [worked argument](../notes/2026-09-30-commons-builder-cross-scale-argument.md) and propose one concrete model family.
