## B. Bioinformatics study or methods artifact

**Deliverable:** either (B1) a scientifically interpretable hemlock study using established suitable methods, or (B2) a reusable analysis method/software contribution with a precisely stated input and output contract. These are distinct claims and may be completed separately.

**Acceptance for B1:** compatible data/model/implementation contract; prespecified new comparisons; uncertainty and adequacy checks; appropriate ILS/capture/error alternatives; reproducible numerical diagnostics and sensitivity analysis. Any historical discrimination must survive the stated calibration checks. If the data cannot discriminate, quantify or localize that limitation without promoting failure of one fitting method to biological impossibility.

**Acceptance for B2:** a defined improvement over relevant established baselines on matched tasks, with reproducible correctness checks, realistic resource measurements and an empirical validation design appropriate to the intended use. Report negative cases and unsupported inputs. A reliable restricted tool can be valuable; a broad superiority claim needs broad evidence.

**Current state:** a usable, audited practical prototype and exact arithmetic/checker controls exist. Its fixed-six-copy, nine-parameter clock-JC/pulse contract does not admit the hemlock alignments. Near-exact arithmetic examples demonstrate conditional precision; finite-data examples retain genuine ambiguity. Fresh biological intake, justified error calibration and broad empirical utility are not established.

**Novelty threshold:** identify the specific new method, finding, validation result or reproducibility contribution, and compare it with the relevant primary literature/software. New packaging, test counts or re-expression of established discordance do not alone establish a new scientific method or discovery. An artifact suitable for review is not a guarantee of publication.

**First priority:** choose B1 or a specific B2 claim before expanding implementation. For B1, complete the hemlock model-admissibility gate and use established methods where justified. For B2, define a matched benchmark and the claim it can test. Do not force either into the existing nine-parameter model.

## C1. Standalone mathematical paper or proof portfolio

**Deliverable:** a self-contained, independently reviewable paper about a substantive scoped theorem, obstruction, algorithm, or formalization. A restricted theorem can support a paper without resolving a master problem.

**Acceptance:** exact statement and assumptions, complete argument, clear relationship to prior work, independently checked dependencies, examples explaining the consequence, and limitations. Separate mathematical correctness, historical novelty, significance and verification status. A kernel-checked conditional statement must expose its uninstantiated hypotheses. For a portfolio, select already accepted results and link their original proofs rather than rewriting the archive.

**Current state:** candidate material exists, including the accepted G5 generalizations and recent G4 structural/restricted results. Selecting a coherent claim, checking primary prior work and obtaining independent assessment remain necessary before calling a manuscript submission-ready. No journal acceptance is implied.

**First priority:** prepare a small theorem-to-evidence index and choose one defensible central claim. This can proceed alongside the empirical dossier and full-scope proof attempts.

## C2. Original general mathematical closure

**Deliverable:** a proof resolving a named original master statement, or a rigorously proved obstruction/impossibility theorem with an explicit reduction covering its exact quantifiers. Each goal is separately finishable; a theorem about G3 is not automatically G4 or the whole programme.

**G3 acceptance:** a terminating exact recognizer for every finite supplied algebraic observation menu over all admitted original real-parameter sources, preserving shared/static parameters, original observation IDs, all cores and unknown finite word length; or a valid original-scope negative theorem. A full affirmative route needs an input-effective one-witness bound or complete NO-certification mechanism, together with the required source-faithful transport. Bounded search, closure membership and generic matrix hardness do not discharge this obligation.

**G4 acceptance:** the original universal forcing/determination obligation over every prefix-fitting rival, including the required later-law agreement and any required target-derived effective finite legal certificate. A negative route must meet the original fixed-target quantifiers: one fixed admitted target with exact matching rivals at every finite prefix, with the required later disagreement. Changing the target with depth, approximate matches or nonphysical signed-time operations do not suffice. A single G3 witness bound or a restricted-target result does not establish the universal claim.

**Evidence:** complete inspectable argument, pinned prior dependencies, explicit independent correctness and prior-work review, and faithful theorem statements. Mark hand proof, executed computation and kernel-checked formalization separately. Formalize the claimed scope where available; a conditional Lean theorem cannot supply missing source hypotheses. An executed solver is needed for an implementation claim, not as a substitute for proving an algorithm terminates.

**Current state:** restricted theorems, sound conditional certificates and bounded exact machinery are preserved; original general G3/G4 remain open. The retained-slot-to-original-registry compiler and coupled BOTH-row transport also remain missing in the current executable bridge. Source-complete coverage/localization is the decisive mathematical obligation, not another local test suite.

**Novelty threshold:** the exact theorem or reduction must go beyond an identified prior result, or be explicitly labelled a new proof/formalization of an existing result. Failure of a proof strategy is scoped evidence, not undecidability.

**First priority:** discharge one full master obligation or expose a valid exact reduction. Reuse existing tools. Do not replace the full goal with an unannounced restricted case.

A deliberately restricted end-to-end system may finish with restricted mathematics; the full original unrestricted programme requires the original general obligations too.

**Current state:** substantial components exist, but the chain is incomplete. A working interface, successful synthetic controls or a general theorem alone does not fill the biological, statistical and implementation gaps.

## Near-term transition package and allocation

The first stable handoff should contain (1) a selective theorem/evidence index with exact open obligations, (2) the CWU dossier's authenticated input inventory and model-admissibility verdict, and (3) a reproducible practical release with unsupported inputs and UNKNOWN outcomes documented. These are concrete finish lines that do not depend on a general-proof deadline. Advance an empirical or methods manuscript only after the corresponding validation gate passes.

Codex's existing practical workers can own executable intake, replay, tests and packaging through the [scoped hemlock handoff](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/75622c909e1b3e1d837a20f338026e5b2caac449/handoffs/2026-10-09-codex-hemlock-study/SCOPED-HEMLOCK-STUDY-HANDOFF.md). Publication of a handoff is not acknowledgment or evidence that execution has started. General proof effort retains its original scope; no additional workers are authorized by this map.

A strategic reset should be triggered by repeated dependence on the same unproved premise, recycling an already defeated tactic, or a completed attempt with no route to the master quantifiers. Re-read the full statement and accepted results; test a different representation, a stronger applicable prior theorem, or the opposite conclusion. Preserve failed attempts. Restarting itself is not evidence of increased creativity or a guarantee of a discovery. Small lemmas are worth pursuing when their connection to the chosen finish line is explicit.

## Common reporting axes

Every checkpoint should state four independent dimensions rather than one ambiguous “done” label:

1. **Scope:** original general / explicitly restricted / conditional / empirical application.
2. **Result:** theorem, exact algorithm, implemented component, empirical finding, or failure/obstruction. A failure names the precise method, input class or premise it defeats.
3. **Evidence:** authored argument / independently reviewed argument / actual computation or rerun / kernel-checked formal statement / empirical validation. Record which applies; none silently implies the others.
4. **Prior work and usefulness:** established result reused / attribution checked / novelty unresolved / specific new contribution supported; also state the task it now enables and what remains blocked.

Example: “Restricted, conditional count theorem; kernel-checked arithmetic under supplied source predicates; executable consumer returns UNKNOWN without the provider; enables a future bounded registry call once the missing transport is proved.” This is useful progress without becoming a general recognizer claim.

Report the next decisive obligation for the chosen finish line, not an endless list of nearby component improvements. Preserve informative negative results and earlier accepted work even when tactics change.
