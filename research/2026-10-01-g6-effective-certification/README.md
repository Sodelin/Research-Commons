## Main answer

- There is an anytime-alpha-honest candidate-set procedure that eventually equals R(P) on one event of probability at least 1-alpha.
- Correct terminal certification with probability at least 1-alpha is attainable exactly when H is constant on R(P).
- At a conflicting robust point, every uniformly alpha-honest procedure in the stated algorithm class has probability at most alpha of issuing the correct terminal answer there.
