# G4 B: testing a universal positive-exposure extension of the COMMON theorem

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 8 October 2026.

Status: **frozen hand candidate for independent challenge**. The proposed whole G4 architecture fails under the explicit positive-linear exposure assumptions below. This is not a proof of general G4 impossibility, a fixed-target exact replica construction, or a new positive forcing theorem. The newly completed conditional COMMON result and its corrections remain intact.

## 2. Exact convex premise: the zero-bigon ordinary target is included

Fix a finite cap m>=2 and the actual natural unmarked private INDEPENDENT word language S_m. All populations and inheritance probabilities are strictly interior; one tuple supplies all arities. Let V_m=aff(S_m), including the positive ordinary-only words.

The accepted `CONVEX-ORDINARY-FINAL.md`, Section 1, states explicitly that for every tau>0,

    E_tau belongs to ri_(V_m) conv(S_(D,m)),

where D is the dimension of the capped forest algebra and S_(D,m) consists of actual words with exactly D positive bigons. Its SHA-256 is `85c74ae9db4cd5f1d0b1bc4acdce53f4bd46edc920983fb275a8782006599ed4`; Git blob `6c11de597bbc61c9fb44907b3ffeafa9c64e76ea`.

Thus the chosen target is the ordinary zero-bigon E_tau itself. The theorem does not require it to have a nonempty deterministic source presentation. Its statement is relative interior of a CONVEX HULL, not actual source interior; the latter remains unproved at general high caps. No physical external mixing operation is inferred.

Every fixed finite list of legal once-used private-box response probabilities is an affine linear readout of one sufficiently high capped forest kernel, by the original contextual compiler. Different legal exteriors and allocations simply give different fixed linear rows. The same source kernel is used in all rows.

It follows that if a finite affine combination

    A(W)=c0+sum_(e in E) c_e R_e(W)

is nonnegative for EVERY actual private INDEPENDENT word and equals zero at E_tau, then it is identically zero on every such word. Indeed it is an affine functional on V_m, nonnegative on S_m and hence its convex hull. A nonconstant affine functional cannot be nonnegative on a relative neighborhood of its zero at E_tau. All caps in this argument are finite; a finite list with smaller cap can be raised to at least two.

## 3. The universal positive representation being tested

Let X be a measurable latent space independent of the unknown word W. Suppose each actual private word has a probability measure mu_W on X, and each original legal scalar response has the form

    R_e(W)=integral f_e(x) dmu_W(x),

where f_e is a fixed measurable function, independent of W. The SAME mu_W supplies every allocation and response under discussion. This is the static positive-mixture structure used by the COMMON covariance theorem, but X need not consist of displayed trees, covariance matrices or route strings here.

No assertion is made that this representation exists. A trivial representation putting a point mass at the entire source law is not enough: the required finite support exposure must also be observable as specified next.

Fix ONE ordinary target T0=E(q0), q0 in (0,1). Its proposed finite exposure procedure may choose its coefficients, tests and sets using its responses. After running it at T0, FREEZE that one finite choice. It must give a finite sequence of measurable sets and certificates

    X=X0 superset X1 superset ... superset Xk,
    Xj={x in X_(j-1): p_j(x)=0},
    p_j(x)>=0 on X_(j-1).

At stage j, for every source whose measure is supported on X_(j-1), the expectation of p_j must be a fixed affine combination of finitely many original legal responses:

    integral p_j dmu_W = c_(j,0)+sum_e c_(j,e)R_e(W).

The coefficients are those frozen from T0; they do not change while W varies. The target expectation is zero. Finally Xk is a FINITE grid, or more generally contains only finitely many distinct latent response profiles (f_e(x))_e.

This permits a finite adaptive chain of conditional support pruning, including certificates obtained by interpolation on grids exposed earlier. It does not require every certificate to be globally nonnegative before those earlier restrictions. It does require those restrictions to arise from the stated positive support-exposure chain, rather than from an unproved source-specific condition on a target fibre.

## 4. Every such certificate would be universal

We prove inductively that EVERY actual source measure mu_W is supported on Xj.

The claim is trivial at X0. Suppose it holds at X_(j-1). The expectation of p_j is then a finite affine combination of legal responses for every actual source, is nonnegative for every source by positivity of its measure and p_j on that support, and is zero at T0. Section 2 forces that affine functional to vanish on every source. A nonnegative integrable function with zero expectation vanishes almost surely. Hence every mu_W is supported on Xj.

After the finite sequence, every source measure is supported on the SAME Xk selected by T0's frozen certificate. This is not an argument in which each ordinary E(q) chooses a different grid. The single q0 certificate, if valid under the stated assumptions, has already forced one common finite grid for all W, including every E(q).

Target-dependent scalar normalizations of a certificate cause no problem when they are fixed nonzero constants after T0 is chosen and the displayed expectation identity remains valid on X_(j-1) for all sources. A source-dependent calibration that changes the expectation identity as W varies is a different assumption and is not covered.

## 5. One common finite grid contradicts the ordinary response family

Suppose Xk has J distinct latent response profiles. Every source's entire response profile would then be a convex combination of those J fixed profiles. Thus the whole family of original response profiles would lie in a linear space of dimension at most J.

This already fails on the positive ordinary sources. Original legal crossed-cherry tomography gives, for each n, a scalar response equal to a known positive factor times the ordinary no-merger coordinate

    b_n(E(q))=q^lambda_n,   lambda_n=binom(n,2).

The known factor depends on the chosen test, not on q, so it can be divided out. Choose J+1 distinct indices n=1,...,J+1 (the n=1 coordinate is the constant probability one). A representation on J fixed latent profiles would make these J+1 functions of q linearly dependent. But the distinct monomials q^lambda_n are linearly independent on (0,1): a nonzero polynomial cannot vanish on an open interval. Contradiction.

Therefore no universal representation and finite positive-linear exposure chain satisfying Section 3 can exist for the whole actual private INDEPENDENT language. In particular, a source-faithful extension of the COMMON proof cannot merely replace the covariance atoms by another positive latent space while preserving that observable exposure mechanism.

The conclusion does not depend on a supplied word-size bound, a continuity assumption on the recovery algorithm, a finite number of latent coordinates, or a claim that the chosen ordinary target is an actual interior point. It uses only the exact convex-relative-interior provider and original linear one-box observations.

## 6. Direct control: the displayed-tree covariance law itself already fails

There is an independent, earlier accepted diagnostic against simply reusing the COMMON representation. Take the strict private source

    K=E(1/2) B_ind(1/2,1/2,1/2) E(1/2).

Its actual no-merger coordinates are b2=3/16 and b3=13/2048=26/4096, while b2^3=27/4096. Every positive probability measure on ordinary survivals a in [0,1] would satisfy E[a^3]>=(E[a])^3 by Jensen. Thus this actual INDEPENDENT source has no such positive duration-mixture representation, finite OR infinite.

Any fixed positive displayed-tree covariance law would reduce to that duration representation when only one entry is sampled. It therefore cannot universally represent natural INDEPENDENT sources. This example is reused from the accepted earlier moment countercontrol; it is not advertised as a new theorem or a fixed-target G4 counterexample.

Equal-arm INDEPENDENT cells also cannot be collapsed by the COMMON identity B_common(x,x,g)=E(x). The current-root coins do not form one common choice for all surviving roots, and the original all-copy length invariant distinguishes a nonempty strict private independent word from an ordinary one.

## 7. Original controls, interfaces and effectivity

The COMMON routing-only correction uses a static finite natural configuration set with positive weights, independent of sample allocation. That is why every included routing-only row uses the same finite joint covariance grid and only its weights must be recovered. It does not authorize resampling a shared register, independently fitting rows, changing population banks, adding a missing exterior taxon, moving the retained root, or interpreting one-output marginals as a joint multi-output law.

A universal original-master representation covering natural INDEPENDENT sources and richer interfaces must in particular restrict correctly to the unmarked private one-output natural subfamily used above. The impossibility of this exposure architecture on that subfamily already blocks its universal transfer. It does not show that the completed conditional COMMON theorem or a separately proved finite shared-register extension is false.

Nor does the argument replace exact algebraic input by a Cauchy oracle or supply an all-copy equivalence oracle. Effectivity and actual source extraction would still require their original proofs even if another representation were found.

## 8. What remains outside the obstruction

This is deliberately not a no-go for every possible G4 certificate. It leaves open:

- nonlinear functions of the observed endpoint probabilities, including products of separate response probabilities;
- certificates nonnegative only on an exact target fibre, rather than on every source remaining after the stated support-pruning steps;
- source-dependent calibrations or transforms for which latent expectations are not fixed affine readouts of original responses;
- a positive latent law with infinite effective support at the ordinary target, for which the finite-atomic exposure step simply does not apply;
- an actual fixed-target construction of exact positive rivals at every finite prefix.

The original legal menu is never enlarged. A nonlinear computed quantity is not automatically the expectation of a latent polynomial under the same fixed positive law. Repeated independent loci do not silently supply shared latent draws, and the once-used source compiler does not authorize hidden route cloning.

## 9. Terminal outcome of the attempted full extension

The strengthened COMMON provider genuinely closes its conditional natural/routing-only branch by joint recovery and actual compression. The proposed universal INDEPENDENT extension by positive linear support exposure is impossible for the precise reasons in Sections 2-5. Replacing finite atoms by the actual hierarchy-tail mixture instead loses the finite-support premise; a different exposure or a source-bound argument would still be needed.

No terminating original G4 procedure or true fixed-target full-menu negation has been obtained. The surviving whole-problem choices require new source mathematics outside this blocked architecture. The result remains a candidate until independent challenge. Historical novelty and formal verification are not claimed.
