    P=sum_i w_i(1-q^lambda_i) product_(j!=i) f_j,

The strict biological domain remains 0<p,q<1, where every f_i>0. Weights w_i are the actual differential-annihilator covector coordinates, not arbitrary Bernoulli multiplicities or source probabilities. Independent routing, untyped closure mixtures and hidden new source controls are not inserted.

Write alpha,beta in {0,1} for the active drift/killing constraints respectively:

Eliminate alpha+beta coordinates using these rational linear equations. The remaining free weights u_1,...,u_(d-alpha-beta) define a POLYNOMIAL ring A=Q[q,u_1,...,u_(d-alpha-beta)]. Each w_i is a nonzero rational linear form in u. If alpha=1 define Qred=Q/(1-p); otherwise Qred=Q.

## 2. Exact degree reductions from actual source equations

Set D_i=1-q^lambda_i. The coefficient of p^(d-1) in P is

    (-1)^(d-1)(product_i D_i) sum_i w_i.

Hence active killing implies deg_p P<=d-2, while generally deg_p P<=d-1.

Evaluating the ACTUAL Q at p=1 yields

    Q(1,q)=q^(sum_i lambda_i-1) sum_i lambda_i w_i.

Active drift therefore makes Q divisible by 1-p over A[p]. The quotient is still linear in the weights and has degree at most d-2. Generally Q has degree at most d-1. ## 3. Vanishing one genuine weight forces an actual common source factor

## 4. Why the product, rather than only individual coordinates, divides

The active row space is generated by the all-ones vector when beta=1 and the positive distinct lambda vector when alpha=1. The constrained weight space is its kernel.

Each coordinate form w_i is nonzero on that kernel. Otherwise its ambient coordinate vector e_i would lie in the active row span. With both rows active, at least two distinct coordinates k!=i would require a+b lambda_k=0, hence a=b=0, a contradiction. With one row active, any other coordinate similarly forces its scalar multiplier to zero. No-row case is immediate.

For i!=j, two restricted coordinate forms could be associates only if e_i-t e_j lay in the active row span for some nonzero rational t. With both rows active, d>=4 leaves at least TWO other coordinates with distinct lambda values. They again force a=b=0, impossible. With one active row, any other coordinate forces its scalar to zero; no-row case is immediate.

Thus all w_i are nonzero pairwise nonassociate linear primes in the polynomial UFD A. Since each divides the determinant, their PRODUCT divides it. The coordinate argument is uniform over all four flag choices; it does not assume generic nonzero numerical weights.
