## Three full G3 routes

### A. Input-derived biological compression

Aim: prove every realizable finite algebraic response tuple has a realization within a computable budget derived from that tuple, including graph size and strict parameter margins when compact slabs are used.

Decisive lemma: an original-source-preserving reduction replacing an arbitrarily long/large hidden region by a bounded admitted network while preserving ALL supplied original rows jointly. Apply it to a canonical decomposition until bounded complexity remains.

Why it is general: the reduction acts on arbitrary admitted graphs and the full joint response tuple. It is not a serial-chain factor bound.

First test: determine whether arbitrary finite atomic clock mixtures or finite tensor interfaces can be compiled into bounded ADMITTED galled/cut-child networks. Abstract Caratheodory compression is not enough; original population/coalescent/routing grammar and strictly positive lengths must be preserved.

Known obstructions: exact COMMON factor counts are unbounded at a fixed cap; low-dimensional latent stochastic models need not be biological; independent rows may have incompatible hidden assignments; raw Kingman consecutive-copy Hankel rank is infinite even for one edge. These rule out particular shortcuts, not all input-derived compression.

Stop/redirect criterion: produce a source-preserving compression theorem and exact admission proof, or a source-realizable family refuting the proposed compression invariant. Another local Jacobian or higher-cap count is not that result.

### B. Complete observable consistency / finite rejection certificates

Aim: characterize precisely which finite profiles are in the finite strict original-source image, then derive checkable witnesses on both sides.

Decisive lemma: every unrealizable algebraic profile admits a finite certificate sound against ALL admitted graphs; dovetail that certificate enumeration with the existing graph/QE YES enumeration.

Candidate mechanisms: a typed finite forbidden-pattern theorem, a complete algebraic invariant/certificate basis, or a constructive coherent-interface realization theorem whose hypotheses are themselves decidable from the original observations.

Why it is general: certificates quantify over the whole original source grammar and preserve common source/parameter/ID assignment across rows.

Known obstructions: a coherent/projective stochastic family does not imply one finite biological source; positive-limit nonattainment defeats ordinary closed separating-polynomial certificates; Hilbert/Noetherian stabilization for a supplied finite presentation does not bound unknown presentation size. An invariant proving membership only in closure is insufficient.

Stop/redirect criterion: prove completeness of one finite certificate language or locate a concrete unattained boundary point that the language cannot distinguish. Sound examples alone are insufficient.

### C. Logical/hardness dichotomy

Aim: decide whether the desired universal algorithm can exist at all rather than indefinitely adding special cases.

Decisive negative theorem: a faithful reduction of an undecidable unbounded computation/word problem to finite algebraic original-response realization, with legal strict positive network realization iff the computation terminates. A positive alternative would encode the entire admitted source image in a genuinely decidable theory with a computable finite witness bound.

Why it is general: the reduction or decision theorem must use the original network grammar and finite input encoding, not an unrelated arbitrary matrix semigroup.

Known obstructions: positive rates and probabilities, graph admission, fixed taxon/ID constraints and one-source row compatibility can destroy generic universality encodings. No such reduction has been proved. Decidability of general real exponentiation remains conditional in relevant primary literature, but no reduction from that open problem to G3 is claimed.

Stop/redirect criterion: establish an actual source-level equivalence/reduction or prove a proposed encoding cannot satisfy admission. Similar-looking equations do not establish hardness.

## Three full G4 routes

### A. Intrinsic finite forcing / fingerprint

Aim: recover a target-specific forcing invariant from legal original responses; its exact fiber should contain only all-context-equivalent admitted rivals.

Decisive lemma: for every supplied target, a computable finite original menu forces equivalence uniformly over all merely-positive finite rival sizes.

Possible mechanisms: finite atomic support forcing, a compiled graphon/tensor forcing theorem, or a source-positive sum-of-squares witness forbidding hidden refinements. Every required statistic must be synthesized from authorized observations.

Known obstructions: arbitrarily weak positive sites, inaccessible latent IDs and missing graphon moments. Finite rank observed only under an assumed hidden-size bound is circular. Exact observational equality cannot be replaced by a statistical margin.

Stop/redirect criterion: exhibit a true rival-wide forcing certificate for arbitrary targets, or a fixed target that defeats the proposed invariant at every finite resolution.

### B. Target-derived detection deadline / finite contextual basis

Aim: use a typed composition algebra for original genealogical contexts to derive a finite target-dependent basis or complexity bound. Then a finite exact tester terminates.

Decisive lemma: matching that finite legal basis implies all-context equality against EVERY admitted rival, irrespective of its hidden size. Equivalently derive a target-only rival bound adequate for the existing bounded-source tester.

Prior mechanisms: Myhill–Nerode-style minimal behavior, finite algebra/module generation, ideal-chain termination and positive no-hidden-refinement properties. The actual nonlinear Kingman-indexed polynomial representation must be retained.

Known obstructions: fixed-presentation ideal termination does not give a uniform basis against unknown rivals; the ordinary consecutive-copy sequence has no eventual constant-coefficient recurrence. An externally supplied contraction envelope is not a target-derived deadline.

Stop/redirect criterion: prove a uniform-over-rivals finite basis/refinement exclusion. A supplied-pair comparison procedure is already known and is not this gate.

### C. Exact fixed-target camouflage after every prefix

Aim: refute target-adaptive finite stopping with ONE fixed admitted finite positive target and, for EVERY finite original-response prefix, a finite positive inequivalent rival matching it exactly.

Decisive lemma: a positivity-preserving exact prefix-lifting construction/induction, or a theorem placing that same target in the appropriate image at every finite cap with an effective exact source witness.

Why it is general: one legal subclass counterexample refutes the broader universal stopping statement. In this negative route a pendant private independent-chain subclass is sufficient if every wrapper/control/response obligation is kept.

First induction candidate: show ordinary E(a), with fixed a in (0,1), lies in the positive independent-chain image at every finite forest cap through nontrivial chains. Cap-four interior is accepted, but no induction to cap five or arbitrary caps exists. Finite-dimensional controllability may help only if it returns to the SAME ordinary target with positive source parameters.

Known obstructions: a full-rank source map somewhere does not put the fixed target in its image; weak-cell approximation gives no equality; a sequence of changing targets gives no fixed-target impossibility; shared or actuated controls cannot be inserted into private passive routing.

Stop/redirect criterion: obtain the actual all-prefix induction, or prove an intrinsic finite obstruction for the fixed proposed target. Do not perform an endless ladder of higher-cap fits and call that a general theorem.

## Primary-source boundary checks

Existing source work records the absence of a fixed finite linear compiler for non-clique graphon moments on a bare bigon; nonlinear synthesis is separate.

Supplied-presentation polynomial equivalence: Lorenzo Clemente, Commutative Algebras of Series, https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.LICS.2026.29 . Its finite presentation is an actual premise, not a bound on unknown biological realizations.

Logical boundary: Wilkie, Schanuel's Conjecture and the Decidability of the Real Exponential Field, https://doi.org/10.1007/978-94-015-8923-9_11 , and Berarducci/Gallinaro, https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08365 . These give conditional decisibility frameworks, not a reduction or decision theorem for G3.

No route in this map claims historical novelty. The exact master question/source contract must be fixed before counting a mathematical advance as general closure.
