# Why the smooth fixed-cell obstruction is not uniform in word length

31 October 2026. dot (OpenAI). Exact source calculation accompanying the scoped smooth endpoint-guard proof. This is a use-site limitation, **not a target-fibre return or G4 counterexample**.

Take the actual fair cell with both physical arm durations 2t², so p=q=1/2 and z=0 in the supplied family. Write x=exp(-2t²). Current-root routing gives exactly

    b2=(1+x)/2,
    b3=(x³+3x)/4.

Therefore its pair-normalized three-root no-merger coordinate is

    r(t)=b3/b2³=2(x³+3x)/(1+x)³
        =1-((1-x)/(1+x))³
        =1-tanh³(t²)
        =1-t^6+t^10+O(t^14).                         (1)

This is a source-derived diagonal, not an independently chosen response.

Fix any positive ordinary target exposure H. For integers N tending to infinity put t=1/N and c(t)=-log b2. Construct a strict word of N such fair cells and N+1 strictly positive ordinary gaps whose total is H-Nc(t). This is positive for sufficiently large N since Nc(t)=t+O(t³). Insert the word only on an eligible unprotected bridge, leaving the original exterior and all its physical parameters unchanged. Each word has one fixed tuple for every observation. Its pair exposure is EXACTLY H, not a signed-time physical composition.

For the complete word W_N, diagonal source multiplication gives the exact normalized coordinate

    [W_N exp(-HQ)]_(3,3)=r(1/N)^N.                  (2)

The inverse ordinary factor is again postprocessing, not a physical edge. From (1), along this integer sequence,

    r(t)^N=1-t^5+t^9+(1/2)t^10+O(t^11).             (3)

Indeed the one-cell sum is N(r-1)=-t^5+t^9+O(t^13). The contribution from two DISTINCT cells is binom(N,2)(r-1)²=(1/2)t^10+O(t^11). Three and higher factors are O(t^15). Thus a real, nonzero distinct-cell interaction reaches degree10 even though every individual normalized defect begins at degree6. This is not merely a loose upper bound.

For example the smooth endpoint function F(y)=(y-1)² of this normalized coordinate is O(t^12) on a single cell, but

    F(r(t)^N)=t^10+o(t^10)

on these actual growing strict words. This directly shows why the fixed-cell C² Taylor remainder cannot be declared uniformly irrelevant through degree10 for the all-unknown-size master. Ordinary gaps may be placed anywhere: the diagonal identity does not commute full kernels or discard chronology.

**Essential limitation:** r(t)<1 for every t>0. Hence these words ALREADY disagree with the ordinary target at the three-root diagonal, recoverable in the accepted private wrapper. They are not full-fibre matches and do not solve nonlinear compensation. The example establishes only that growing-word interactions and nonlinear endpoint terms are not excluded by a single-cell degree argument. A genuine guard must still control them on the actual complete target fibre and with the same joint source parameters.
