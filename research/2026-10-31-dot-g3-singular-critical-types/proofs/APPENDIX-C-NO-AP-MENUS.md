# AppendixC: a general sufficient no-progression criterion

This is a mathematical generalization of the normalized factor map, not a claim that arbitrary exponent menus are original programme observations.

Let Λ be ANY finite set of m≥2 distinct positive INTEGER exponents, let S=Σ_{λ∈Λ}λ, and assume Λ has no three distinct terms a<k<b with a+b=2k. For every nonzero real c with Σλc_λ=0, the strict critical set of

 Σ_{λ∈Λ}c_λ log(1−p+p q^λ),  0<p,q<1,

has no complex common critical component through a strict point and has at most

             (2m−3)S−m+1

distinct points. Accordingly, a rank-deficient FREE normalized m-output factor map has at most this many distinct strict normalized cell types. Integer multiplicities are not bounded.

Proof. The complete Puiseux argument in Section3 of the main note uses only distinct positive integer exponents, the normal constraint, and absence of a three-term progression in the active subset. It applies verbatim. The derivative polynomials satisfy

 deg_pP≤m−1, deg_qP≤S;
 deg_pQ≤m−2, deg_qQ≤S−1,

where Q=Q0/(1−p) and Q0(1,q)=q^(S−1)Σλc_λ=0. The nonzero-polynomial pole arguments also apply to any such exponent set. Every strict solution is isolated in the COMPLEX common zero set by that proof. Apply the arbitrary-coefficient isolated-root form of multihomogeneous Bézout cited in the main note, with variable blocks{p},{q}. The coefficient is

 (m−2)S+(m−1)(S−1)=(2m−3)S−m+1.

Degree drops, degenerate isolated roots and common components elsewhere do not invalidate that inequality. This gives the stated bound. The common-covector bridge for the free map is unchanged.

For m=6,S=56 the formula is9·56−5=499. The positivity/integer/strict-domain and normal assumptions are essential to this particular proof. No-AP is SUFFICIENT, not asserted necessary. For example the three-exponent menu{1,2,3} contains a progression, but its critical normal fibre is empty: the same double-root argument in AppendixA would give four positive zeros for a polynomial with at most four monomials.

The triangular menu enlarged to include36 contains6,21,36. This corollary cannot be applied to it, and no claim of a curve's existence there is made. Larger-menu original source coverage and all protected-field/rank-acquisition/multiplicity limitations remain as stated in the main note.
