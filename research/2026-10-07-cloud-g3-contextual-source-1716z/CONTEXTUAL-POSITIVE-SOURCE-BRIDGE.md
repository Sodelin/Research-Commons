## 3. Exact aggregate baseline allocation

    H_(j,2)=(I+(z_j²/2) K_(r_j²))/(1+z_j²/2).

These are algebraic bare factors with strict Bernoulli probabilities and strict ratios; alone they contain an identity arm. Enumerate them as H_i=(1−p_i)I+p_i K_(q_i), i=1,...,k. Only their AGGREGATE multiplied by K_A is now physically realized.

Set

    c=1−(1−A)/(4k+1),     A_res=A/c^(2k).

Then 0<c<1 and Bernoulli's inequality gives

    c^(2k)≥1−2k(1−A)/(4k+1)>A,

so 0<A_res<1. Use one leading ordinary edge with survival A_res. For factor i use a binary bigon whose arms have survivals c and c q_i, with one common natural bit of probability p_i; follow it by one connector of survival c. The whole bigon-plus-connector forest kernel is exactly

    K_c [(1−p_i)I+p_i K_(q_i)] K_c

    K_(A_res) ∏_(i=1)^k [K_(c²) H_i]
      =K_A ∏_(i=1)^k H_i.                         (7)

The allocation is made once from the SAME A and k, before selecting any bank row or entering arity. All p_i, q_i, c and A_res remain fixed throughout the bank. The sum of coalescent hazards on the deterministic pieces is preserved; individual new rates need not equal exterior rates.

## 7. A real obstruction to strengthened fixed-rate or exposed-bank demands

The original source's freedom to choose a positive constant rate on EACH new physical edge is essential. Suppose a stronger model fixes one common rate ρ on all replacement edges while insisting on the same endpoint gap Δ. Every chosen COMMON path between those endpoints has deterministic total coalescent duration ρΔ. Its kernel is always K_(exp(−ρΔ)), regardless of the number of common bigon coins.

For a nontrivial target K_A exp[u(K_r−I)], its no-merger moments satisfy

    m_3/m_1³=exp[u(r³−3r+2)]
             =exp[u(1−r)²(r+2)]>1,

whereas every deterministic ordinary duration has m_3=m_1³. Thus that stronger fixed-rate realization fails already at cap three. Original G6 explicitly permits freely variable edge-specific constant rates, so this counterexample is a boundary check and not a refutation of its theorem.

Likewise, if a bank observes or actuates new private hybrid coins, or applies different transformations inside the replaced run without a proved common covariance identity, rowwise marginal reconstruction does not establish one physical joint bank. Such a bank violates the unchanged-run condition stated in §1 and requires an additional source-specific theorem. The present proof neither redraws retained old registers nor silently relaxes those controls.
