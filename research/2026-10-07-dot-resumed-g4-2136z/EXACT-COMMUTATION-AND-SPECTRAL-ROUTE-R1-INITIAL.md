## 3. Every actual word has distinct positive diagonal blocks

For any actual natural projective factor, the event of no merger among r current roots implies no merger after restricting to r-1 labels. Therefore b_r<=b_(r-1). All these probabilities are positive for finite strict source factors.

A legal word has a strictly positive leading ordinary edge E(z), 0<z<1. Its diagonals multiply under composition. The leading factor has

    z^lambda_r < z^lambda_(r-1),  r>=2.

Multiplying by the nonincreasing positive diagonal factors of the rest of the word gives

    1=b_1>b_2>...>b_m>0.                                    (1)

The second value b_0=1 is in the isolated empty block and causes no off-diagonal degeneracy.

## 4. Exact source-group conjugacy to the diagonal

Write the transformed actual word matrix as K=D(b)+N, N in u_m. Seek C=I+X with X in u_m and

    C K = D(b) C.                                           (2)

For block indices 1<=r<k<=m, equation (2) is

    X_(k,r) =
      [N_(k,r)+sum_(r<s<k) X_(k,s)N_(s,r)]/(b_k-b_r).        (3)

All denominators are nonzero by (1). Solve in increasing block distance k-r. Every X_(k,s) on the right has smaller block distance. The numerator lies in the allowed u_(k,r) subspace: N does, and each product does because the inherited u_m is associative and decomposes into its actual block subspaces. Thus recursion (3) constructs X in u_m exactly.

Nilpotence makes C=I+X invertible, with inverse a finite polynomial in X, and C belongs to the actual algebraic unipotent source group 1+u_m. Equation (2) gives

    C K C^(-1)=D(b).                                       (4)

No inference says that C or C^(-1) is an admitted positive source. They are used solely to identify conjugacy classes. Labels are retained in K itself.

This proves that any two actual finite-cap kernels with the same no-merger diagonals are conjugate in the algebraic source group. They need not be equal as labelled forest kernels or observationally equivalent under all completions.

## 5. Exact finite-cap characterization of the ordinary response

Fix any a in (0,1), independently of the unknown source, and put A=E(a). In the same fixed basis, its diagonal blocks a^lambda_r are distinct for r=1,...,m. Therefore

    [K,A]_(k,r)=(a^lambda_r-a^lambda_k)N_(k,r).

The empty block remains isolated. It follows that

    K A = A K  if and only if  N=0.                         (5)

Consequently, for any actual capped word and any fixed q in (0,1),

    b_r(K)=q^lambda_r for 2<=r<=m AND K E(a)=E(a) K.        (6)

This is equality in the COMPLETE faithful capped forest algebra. No selected spectral band, finite Taylor truncation, hidden route measurement or unsupported chart is substituted for the other response coordinates.

## 6. A fixed-target obstruction to every spectral/class-function stopping route

## 7. What the all-copy normal form does and does not prove about commutation

There is genuine all-copy rigidity for each supplied finite word. If W has L>=1 bigons with positive normalized ordinary connectors z_0,...,z_L, then E(a)W and W E(a) have the same cell list but different leading connectors a z_0 and z_0, and different trailing connectors z_L and a z_L. The accepted all-copy ordered normal form cannot identify them when a<1. Thus each such W fails commutation at SOME finite cap.

This quantifier is

    for every supplied finite nonempty W, there exists a detecting cap.

It does not provide

    one cap detecting every finite nonempty W of unknown size.

Nor does it establish that a word commuting at a chosen cap must already commute at every later cap. With a supplied finite length bound, the established fixed-shape/known-locus QE procedure supplies a finite detection bound; that is an existing stronger-input result, not a new unknown-size certificate.

For an ordinary-target negative construction, the still-missing source statement is therefore exact and concise: for EVERY finite cap, find a nonempty positive actual word which has the ordinary diagonals AND commutes with E(a) at that cap, while its all-copy noncommutation supplies later inequivalence. The diagonal half already exists. The commutation half retains every remaining labelled forest equation and is not solved by the new fifth-order exclusions.

For a positive ordinary-target proof, one would instead need a finite-cap commutation/diagonal rigidity theorem against unbounded positive words, with the original observation/effectivity transfer. That would still require care before extending from this private ordinary target to all original G4 targets, cores and interfaces.
