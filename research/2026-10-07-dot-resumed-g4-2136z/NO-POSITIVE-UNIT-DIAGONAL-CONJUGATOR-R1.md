## Pair survival one forces the entire identity

For any actual source and n<=m, a nonidentity output forest merges at least one pair of original entering tokens. Every such selected pair has the same no-merger probability b2 by natural selected-label projectivity. Therefore

    Pr(any merger among n entering tokens)
       <= binom(n,2)(1-b2).

The same inequality holds in the source closure by continuity. If a closure kernel has b2=1, every row n<=m is concentrated on the all-singleton identity forest. Its complete kernel is thus the identity, including the empty/singleton rows. Opaque pre-existing genealogies are preserved because an entering subtree is one token and no two such tokens merge.

## Consequence for the new structural reduction

In EXACT-COMMUTATION-AND-SPECTRAL-ROUTE-R1.md the algebraic unipotent conjugator is C=I+X. Every diagonal b_n(C), including b2(C), equals one. Its inverse has the same unit diagonal.

If the actual word K is not already its diagonal D(b), the uniquely constructed C is nonidentity. Neither C nor its inverse can then belong to closure(S_m). In particular no sequence of genuine positive source words can approximate either complete conjugator at this fixed cap.

Hence a proposed construction cannot take an actual diagonal-matching word and simply invoke algebraic source-group generation, positive-source closure or interior absorption to replace this conjugation by actual positive source factors. The necessary conjugating elements lie outside that positive closure. Group membership is strictly insufficient.

This conclusion excludes ONLY the direct realization/approximation of the unipotent conjugator itself. It does not exclude a separately proved positive padded realization with a changed intermediate pair survival, a jointly designed time-budget construction, or another actual source identity. Such a construction must account for all physical parameters, positivity and the fixed target anew; it is not supplied by abstract conjugacy.
