## 1. One fixed physical-membership premise

    H = B_INDEP(1/2,1/2,1/2) E(1/2),

K* is an admitted finite strict private word. H is its B-first factor kernel, not an asserted standalone word with a positive leading population.

Let S_m be the complete cap-m image of finite strict natural private INDEPENDENT words with a positive leading edge and positive connectors, one physical tuple shared across all rows. The needed premise is

A10 establishes only stochastic feasibility of related residuals, not (G). Convex mixtures, closure, signed-time saturation and nearby source-interior points also do not establish (G).

## 2. Conditional actual-rival construction

Assume (G), and choose an actual positive-leading word W_m with (W_m)_m=H_m. Put

    R_m = E(1/2) W_m.

Merging adjacent positive ordinary passages gives an actual admitted finite strict word, and (R_m)_m=(K*)_m exactly. If W_m has a bare cell, the normalized leading ordinary duration of R_m is strictly greater than log 2. If it has no bare cell, R_m is ordinary while K* has one cell. In either case the accepted supplied-chain normal form makes their full private laws different. No source inverse is physically used.

The pair budget is automatic: b2(H)=3/8, so every W_m has pair survival 3/8 and every R_m has pair survival 3/16, equal to K*. Thus (G) would already give a uniform finite total pair hazard; no extra cap-uniform inverse radius or external clock reservoir is required. Positivity still has to hold in the actual realizations required by (G).

## 4. What fixed-length exclusion really says

For every supplied finite length bound B, the inherited finite-ring argument supplies a finite cap M_B at which H has NO strict positive-leading private realization of length at most B.

Indeed, H has an interior two-root cohort-mass atom. Every strict private word has a positive ordinary leading population and hence a nonatomic two-root mass law, by the accepted leading-prefix argument used in A10. So H cannot equal such a word at all arities. For any one fixed length L, the differences between its forest polynomials and the rational coordinates of H lie in one finite polynomial ring. Hilbert finite generation puts all equality constraints into a finite original prefix. That prefix therefore has no solution in the strict parameter domain. Taking the finite union over L<=B gives M_B.

Under the original exact polynomial compiler and algebraic encodings, the inherited known-empty-locus QE search can find such a cap: enumerate prefixes and test the fixed finite shapes until their strict domains are excluded. No such search was run. This is an application of the old fixed-shape finite-generation principle, with its all-copy nonmembership premise supplied by the old atom lemma, not a new arbitrary-length decision theorem.

Consequently, IF (G) holds, any realizing family must have lengths tending to infinity in the precise sense that every sufficiently large cap excludes each fixed length bound. That conclusion neither proves (G) nor disproves its possible realizations.
