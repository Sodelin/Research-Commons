## 3. G1's exact mathematical endpoint

The admitted sources are finite binary rooted LSA partners of the canonical outer-labeled planar cut-child galled multigraph class, with arbitrary finite levels and blob counts, parallel arcs, positive finite population lengths and interior natural inheritance. Independent and common inheritance are separate models.

The bridge preserves the complete rooted UNRANKED forest kernel through each removable nonroot two-port chain. A forest records every merger subtree formed inside the component and every surviving ancestor. Previously formed genealogies are grafted into its input tokens. Replacement is contextual: it works inside every admitted causal exterior, including root history and exposed/shared latent choices. Common coins must be retained and reused by the exterior, rather than independently redrawn after replacement.

The cap is m, the TOTAL sampled gene copies. It equals n only in the one-copy-per-taxon experiment.

Nontrivial nonroot two-port components are exactly bigons under this source contract. The root-containing component is retained. The resulting underlying decorated core has

- r ≤ 2n−2
- V ≤ 6n−5
- E ≤ 8n−8

The original head acceptance says these retained-root bounds are sharp for every n ≥ 4. The accessible baseline [joint-law PROOFS, Theorems 1/2](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/eb284f41d13fff2602de4e98411fb15e5891f9e8/research/2026-09-30-astra-joint-law-1744z/PROOFS.md) supplies contextual full-topology replacement and the bounds. Its printed cap is one-copy n; the accepted clarification and later [source-realizability checkpoint](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/eb284f41d13fff2602de4e98411fb15e5891f9e8/research/2026-09-30-astra-source-realizability-1908z/CHECKPOINT-1.md) supply the multiple-copy interpretation. The baseline is not the complete later accepted proof.

There is a separate displayed-tree/Q/S preservation argument: erasing the switching-neutral two-port chain leaves the displayed target unchanged. It is not inferred by equating gene-tree support with displayed-tree support.

In plain language: G1 lets us replace a potentially very long hidden portion with an exact record of everything that can matter to the declared unranked genealogy experiment, while retaining a bounded structural scaffold. It is a representation theorem with a faithful biological origin.

### What G1 does not say

The edge labels are richer source-derived stochastic kernels, not ordinary scalar population lengths. An arbitrary stochastic edge label need not be realizable by an admitted source. A bounded decorated core therefore does not imply a bounded ordinary biological graph realizing the complete law.

It does not preserve complete calendar merger times by this unranked interface. The same [joint-law packet, Theorem 7](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/eb284f41d13fff2602de4e98411fb15e5891f9e8/research/2026-09-30-astra-joint-law-1744z/PROOFS.md) gives a four-taxon constant-edge-rate metric-law size obstruction. There is no contradiction: structural compression and complete-calendar demographic replacement are different conclusions.

Nor does G1 automatically preserve original intervention identities after compression, produce an unknown-source inverse, classify source images, or establish all-cap tester stopping. A full-kernel moment converse is not an observed-law tester converse.
