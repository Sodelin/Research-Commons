# Portable certificate boundary

This compact packet is an explicit projection of the local execution records. It retains logical target names, scientific counts, exact original receipt/audit/import-inventory hashes, compiler/source/configuration pins, kernel flags, resource limits, failures and freshness qualifications. Operational filesystem locations, host details and verbose loader traces are not published here. Original records and all failed attempts remain preserved locally; their hashes do not imply that their complete bytes are included in this packet. Public file hashes are listed separately in EVIDENCE-MANIFEST.json.

No mathematical source, theorem statement, proof body or full audit has been edited by this projection. This compact packet does not itself contain the full sources or audit bodies; their exact identities are pinned for the later modular delivery.
