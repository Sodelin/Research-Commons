## 2. Joint marker decoder on one fixed graph

For i=0,...,d let X_i be an actual private coagulation component, entered
by k_i fresh current tokens labelled by a disjoint set A_i. It has only
mergers, current-token exchangeability, literal deletion projectivity and
opaque-subtree grafting. Any unused natural register has already been
marginalized according to its actual one-bank law; a register exposed
outside a component would require a different, joint-register statement.

Place X_i on taxon A_i's pendant branch and introduce a distinct marker
taxon B_i with one copy. Above the component, A_i and B_i join on a fixed
positive ordinary branch. Join these paired branches by a fixed binary
tree. All remaining finite populations have specified rational survivals
strictly between zero and one. Complete above the retained root by the
usual unbounded ancestral population. Use enough paired branches that
there are at least four taxa.

The known positive entry/exit populations, if present, are included in
X_i for this lemma. No zero-duration physical edge is introduced.

Let f=(f_0,...,f_d) be the component output forests. For each tuple h of
such forests, form a final rooted labelled unranked topology T_h:
within pair i, put the components of h_i successively along a B_i-spine,
in their fixed least-token-label order, and join the resulting pair
trees along the chosen binary backbone.

Let O[f,h] be the probability of T_h from the actual exterior when its
entering component forests are f. This is a rational number: its source
is finite, its ordinary survivals are specified rational numbers, and
ancestral Kingman completion has rational coefficients. It is calculated
with the full exterior state, including all cross-group ancestral mergers.

If O[f,h] is nonzero, each pre-existing tree in f_i must be an intact
clade of T_h with leaves in A_i. Every such clade lies inside one component
of h_i. Indeed, a clade joining different h_i components along the marker
spine contains B_i, and a higher backbone clade contains a marker or a
different group's labels. Consequently f_i refines h_i, and
|f_i| >= |h_i| for every i. If the total component counts are equal, all
these inequalities are equalities and f=h.

Moreover O[h,h]>0. There is positive probability that on each paired
branch the roots of h_i and B_i merge in the selected spine order before
leaving its positive population. There is then positive probability of
the selected binary backbone topology in the known exterior/ancestral
process. Positivity refers to that actual event; the exterior has not
been frozen.

Order tuples by total component count. The square matrix O is triangular,
with no off-diagonal entries at equal count and with strictly positive
diagonal. It is invertible over the rationals. Thus the probabilities
of the actual final topologies T_h determine the ENTIRE joint output
distribution of the physically distinct X_i, by one fixed rational linear
map on final topology probabilities.

This is a decoder, not a declaration that the forest basis states or
signed inverse entries are observed or separately realized. The same
fixed graph and copy allocation suffice for its full finite matrix.

### One largest row suffices at a coherent private component

For a projective opaque-root component entered by M singleton tokens,
its output forest marginal on the first r tokens is its fresh r-root row.
Its action on an arbitrary r-root opaque input is obtained by the same
literal grafting map. Hence every entry of its capped operator through
M is a fixed LINEAR function of its fresh M-root output distribution.
In particular, left composition does not require an additional copy
allocation or another occurrence of the component.

This step uses the already proved current-token deletion/graft law of
actual private sources. It does not hold merely because an arbitrary
finite stochastic matrix has nonnegative entries.

## 3. An actual auxiliary coefficient bank

Fix M>=4 and D=2*binom(M,2). Fix an unknown static x in (0,1). Introduce
D physically distinct protected ordinary components E(x), each entered
by two current roots, on D of the paired branches in Section 2. Their
survival parameters are tied to x by finitely many supplied original
physical parameter equalities. The number D is fixed before any unknown
word is chosen. These are not ties on an unbounded set of fresh cells.

After known pads have been removed arithmetically, the all-singleton
coefficient of auxiliary j is x, and its merged coefficient is 1-x.
The auxiliaries evolve on different physical populations. For e<=D,
the joint coefficient that the first e auxiliaries have no merger,
with all other auxiliary outcomes summed, is exactly x^e.

Known pads cause no problem: the complete two-root operator of each
auxiliary is E(z_j)E(x)E(u_j). The rational linear inverse of its known
ordinary factors recovers E(x). Applying these inverses to the decoded
joint tensor remains a rational linear operation. No inverse population
is inserted into the source.

Let Y be the largest fresh-root row of one other physical private
component. Its complete capped operator entries are linear functions
of Y by Section 2. Therefore, for every rational polynomial P of degree
at most D and every linear functional L of that capped operator,
P(x)*L(Y) is a rational linear functional of the decoded JOINT tensor
of Y and the D auxiliaries. Explicitly expand P=sum p_e x^e and use
the first-e no-merger coefficients above. Unused components are summed
out by their normalization.

The resulting functional is a finite rational linear combination of
actual final topology probabilities on the fixed graph. Its coefficients
depend on the known exterior and on P, not on the unknown value of x.
Unknown x is carried by actual auxiliary populations.

## 4. Turning a finite contrast into a legitimate data row

For completeness, fix a finite rational-control programme with public
configuration J in {1,...,r}, chosen uniformly ONCE for the locus. Its
settings act coherently on the original named sites for all lineages.
In configuration j, let p_j(t) be the probability of final topology t.
The public observation channel may depend on the known programmed
configuration and final topology; it reads no natural source bit.

For a rational contrast L=sum_(j,t) a_(j,t) p_j(t), choose a positive
rational eta so that |eta*a_(j,t)|<=1/2 for every j,t. Set

    Pr(output 1 | J=j, topology=t)=1/2+eta*a_(j,t).

Then the observed probability is exactly 1/2+(eta/r)*L. Requiring its
rational data value to be 1/2 is equivalent to L=0. Negative coefficients
occur only inside this bounded channel, never as source probabilities.
Constants can be represented by row normalization.

This is the declared classical channel for a finite original randomized
programme. Its joint distribution is (1/r)*p_j(t), followed by the stated
channel and erasure of J. It does not authorize deterministic summands as
additional data rows. If a particular menu forbids configuration-aware
postprocessing, this lemma does not enlarge that menu: the comparison
requires the explicitly declared programme/channel described here.

All applications below use one fixed copy allocation, configurations at
two finite named reference sites, and this finite observation channel.
There is no change of exterior graph between configurations.

## 6. Exact corrected comparison on that ONE graph

Use independent mode and two configurations:
A leaves the lower reference natural and forces the upper reference;
B forces the lower reference and leaves the upper reference natural.
The main component kernels including known pads are exactly

    P_A=E(z) R_theta K_I O_theta E(u),
    P_B=E(z) O_theta K_I R_theta E(u).

The bare equality P_A=P_B is the old rejected intertwining condition.
Instead require every capped coordinate of

    O_theta P_A - P_B O_theta

to vanish. Since ordinary kernels commute with the known ordinary pads,
this difference is exactly

    E(z) O_theta (R_theta K_I-K_I R_theta) O_theta E(u).   (1)

All ordinary factors in (1) have positive diagonal and are algebraically
invertible on the finite capped carrier. Thus all these zero coordinates
are EQUIVALENT to [K_I,R_theta]=0 through M. Cancellation is an arithmetic
proof step; no inverse population is physically applied.

Every coordinate of E(theta) is a rational polynomial in theta of degree
at most binom(M,2), hence in x of degree at most D. Sections 2 and 3
therefore realize every coordinate of the left-hand side as a rational
linear contrast of JOINT final topology probabilities in configurations
A and B of this same graph. Section 4 converts each contrast into one
declared Bernoulli channel with rational target 1/2.

Only one physical K is present. Its largest fresh row is used once as
a component tensor. Smaller input rows, opaque inputs and left products
are linear reconstructions from that row. The auxiliary tensor contains
physically distinct ordinary populations, not extra copies of K.

### COMMON calibration on the same tuple

Use a third configuration, forcing both references in COMMON mode.
Write its main component as

    P_C=E(z) O_theta K_C O_theta E(u).

The all-singleton two- and three-root coefficients, recovered by deletion
from the same largest fresh row, obey

    b2(P_C)=(z*u)*theta^2*b2(K_C),
    b3(P_C)=(z*u)^3*theta^6*b3(K_C).

Using the same auxiliary bank, Sections 2–4 impose the two contrasts

    b2(P_C) - (z*u)*theta^2/2 = 0,
    b3(P_C) - (z*u)^3*theta^6/8 = 0.                    (2)

The largest auxiliary degree required is x^12, and D>=12 because M>=4.
Their target data values are again the fixed rational 1/2. Equation (2)
is exactly b2(K_C)=1/2 and b3(K_C)=1/8.

The old strict COMMON Jensen argument now forces x_j=y_j at EVERY
actual cell of this finite K. Indeed each ratio

    [g_j*x_j^3+(1-g_j)*y_j^3]
       /[g_j*x_j+(1-g_j)*y_j]^3

is at least one and equals one only at equal arms. The product ratio is
one by (2); strict ordinary factors contribute ratio one. Thus every
ratio is one. The resulting complete COMMON component is E(1/2).
No input relation tied an unbounded set of fresh arms, and no individual
natural bank bit was observed or refreshed.

We have therefore proved a source-derived fixed-graph finite data menu

    K has equal arms;  K_C=E(1/2);
    [K_I,R_theta]=0 through M.                          (3)
