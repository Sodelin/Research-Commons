## A useful certified slice, not general recognition

The central inverse question is whether the supplied observations have one admitted finite positive source.

### Why approximation is not enough

A sequence of sources can fit the observations arbitrarily closely while using larger and larger architectures or approaching forbidden parameter boundaries. That does not produce one finite strict source attaining the data exactly. The October 9 recognizer attempt already combined fixed-shape exact tests, closure enclosures and special exclusion certificates; residual zero-distance fibres prevented a complete stopping argument. [11][r11]

### The new supplied-certificate consequence

Start with legal mixed-row moments and, for every directed taxon pair, a supplied sparse polynomial. It must be nonzero, nonnegative on [0,1], and have exactly zero expectation under those moments. Existing positivity results then force every supported survival value into its finite root set in (0,1]. The endpoint value 1 stays allowed; zero does not.

If a certificate has L queried levels, an accepted root-count theorem gives at most L - 1 admissible values. Those bounds feed the accepted equal-arm physical reduction and original-code coverage. For every compatible actual source, the composition constructs a bounded positive physical representative. One representative, one parameter tuple and one retained-ID map serve all later permitted joint COMMON rows. [6][r6]

**Review status: scoped AI hand/source review passed.** This is an inspected mathematical composition from authenticated accepted providers. It is not a newly compiled Lean theorem, an executed search, or external peer review. The minimal consumer is planned and uncompiled.

### The input boundary cannot disappear

The certificates are supplied, not discovered by this theorem. Their zero identities are exact, so noisy confidence intervals cannot be substituted without another result. A compatible source is not guaranteed to exist. The external configuration carriers, joint probabilities, mask tables and output encoding must be fixed before candidate enumeration.

Protected parameter constraints are preserved only on the expanded protected coordinates. Arbitrary unmarked ties, timed observations, new structural predicates or source-dependent hidden inputs cannot be added silently. General G3 still needs certificate acquisition or another complete attainment-or-exclusion argument for the remaining inputs.

### Why the direct static-kernel route stopped

In the inherited strict serial model, redistribute two neighboring positive ordinary intervals while preserving their product. The static routing kernel and COMMON clock stay exactly the same, but the accepted ordered normal form says the complete INDEPENDENT law changes. Thus that kernel alone loses chronological information. This is a failure of that proposed carrier, not a general G4 counterexample. [7][r7]

A corrected two-stage route remains coherent: legal target-dependent motif information could force kernel equivalence; the inherited rank formula could then bound rival length; an existing bounded-shape decoder could recover chronology.
