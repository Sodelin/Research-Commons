# Fixed-input obstruction to a same-graph height upgrade

25 October 2026. dot (OpenAI-assisted research), prepared for Nolan.

Status: hand consequence with bounded coordinator manual acceptance, 25 October 2026. The review checked this explicit protected-core, one-experiment subclass, its arithmetic, whole-map rank argument and finite-palette contraposition, inheriting the original compiler/source hypotheses. It is not a fresh transitive proof audit. No source evaluation, symbolic computation, QE, compiler or formal verification was run. This tests a strengthening of the completed regular-source specialization theorem; it is not a counterexample to General G3 or to a source-changing one-witness selector. No historical novelty claim.

## Result first

There is one fixed rational original INDEPENDENT input D and, for every positive integer N, an admitted N-cell private pendant-word presentation of D such that:

1. The presentation can have an entirely rational parameter bank and locally constant response rank one on the fixed protected-core chart.
2. Its selected source pivot can remain uniformly nonsingular.
3. For every fixed degree bound d and logarithmic height bound h, sufficiently long graphs in this family have NO realizing bank whose fresh arm survivals all obey those bounds. This excludes every such bank on those graphs, not merely the displayed poorly selected bank.
4. The same D has a zero-bigon ordinary presentation. A selector allowed to change the source can choose that short witness.

Thus the regular theorem cannot be upgraded to an input-only degree-and-height theorem while preserving its same-graph conclusion. Source-changing compression or another whole-fibre argument is indispensable. This is not a failure of the completed regular degree theorem.

The count exclusion is a direct contraposition of the already completed finite-palette consumer. The purpose of this note is to supply an exact fixed-input regular family to which it applies, and thereby test the quantifiers of the proposed upgrade. It is a failed-upgrade consequence of reused mathematics, not a claim of a new count principle.

## 1. Original legal input and fixed carrier

Choose an admitted positive four-taxon ordinary wrapper with a private pendant eligible bridge to taxon A. All finitely many retained wrapper survivals are rational and strictly between zero and one. Use the original finite protected/read-only parameter contract to fix this retained core; no relation is imposed on the unbounded fresh positions. Take one natural INDEPENDENT experiment with two labelled copies of A and one copy of each other taxon. Supply its complete rooted unranked topology law. There is one source and one tuple, with the actual original source reconstruction and chronological private-word compiler.

Only two selected current roots can enter this private pendant slot. Its complete forest kernel therefore has exactly one nontrivial scalar, its two-root survival d_2: either the two roots remain separate or they have merged into their one possible rooted pair tree. Equality of d_2 is equality of this complete two-root kernel, not merely one diagonal approximation. The fixed actual wrapper consequently gives the same complete supplied law whenever this slot kernel agrees.

Let c=1/4 and take D from the ordinary slot E(c). All data are rational by the original finite polynomial source compiler and ordinary ancestral completion. Original parameter equalities here concern only finitely many protected core sites. They are permitted static relations, not invented observations of a latent forest or synchronization of fresh cells.

The pendant slot initialization and every inserted bigon are unmarked and unprotected. Only the outside retained wrapper sites are fixed. Therefore the ordinary replacement is an admitted source with the same original protected IDs, static relations and complete observation menu, not a deletion of a named or cross-used cell.

Let E_A be the final-tree event that the two A copies do not form an A-only clade. The other sampled taxa provide outside labels, so the event is nonempty. Strict source support and ordinary ancestral completion give its supplied probability delta>0. It is rational and fixed with D. The completed observed-cohort theorem implies d_2(W)>=delta for every matching presentation on any of these eligible pendant-word graphs. In this fixed wrapper one can also write P(E_A)=kappa*d_2(W) with fixed kappa>0: a merger in the private segment makes the event impossible, while conditional on no merger the two-root exit state and future wrapper are unchanged. The weaker inequality alone suffices below.

Also delta<1: the event's complement contains rooted topologies with the A copies forming a clade, and strict full support assigns them positive probability. Thus log(1/delta) below is finite and strictly positive.

## 2. Rational N-cell realizations of the SAME input

For N>=1 put

    q_N = 1 - 1/(4N),
    x_i = y_i = 2q_N - 1 = 1 - 1/(2N),
    g_i = 1/2,
    a_i = q_N,
    z_0 = c / q_N^(2N).

Use the actual chronological word

    E(z_0) product_(i=1)^N [B_I(x_i,y_i,g_i) E(a_i)].

Every parameter is rational. Bernoulli's inequality gives q_N^(2N)>1/2, hence 0<z_0<1/2; both arms, all connectors and every coin are strict. The fixed source grammar reconstructs an actual finite positive source for every N. No boundary tuple is used.

For a two-root natural I cell the exact formula is

    b_2(x,y,g)=g^2*x+(1-g)^2*y+2g(1-g).

For the displayed equal-arm fair cell, b_2=q_N. Consequently

    d_2(W)=z_0 product_i[b_2(x_i,y_i,g_i)*a_i]
          =z_0*q_N^(2N)=c.

Its complete two-root kernel equals E(c), so its full original supplied topology law is D. These are exact finite witnesses, not limits or approximate matching.

## 3. Regularity is not the obstruction

On each fixed protected-core chart, all the open fresh parameters and z_0 feed the readout solely through d_2. The output vector is affine in that scalar. Its direction is nonzero because the E_A probability has coefficient kappa>0. Also

    partial_(z_0) d_2 = product_i(b_2*a_i)>0.

The response therefore has locally constant rank one throughout the strict chart. At the displayed point the derivative equals q_N^(2N)>1/2. The corresponding nonzero pivot in the E_A event readout exceeds kappa/2, uniformly in N. This is a fixed legal linear combination of supplied topology coordinates, not a claim that an individual raw topology coordinate has that same bound. Since the entire fixed output vector is affine in d_2 with a nonzero direction V, some fixed raw coordinate has V_j!=0 and has derivative magnitude greater than |V_j|/2; that also supplies a uniformly nonsingular raw-coordinate pivot, possibly with a different constant.

Thus the obstruction persists on regular charts with a uniform nonzero selected pivot and fixed positive observed event mass. It cannot be blamed solely on singular fibres or vanishing Jacobian determinants. The free coordinates' approach to their strict boundary as the word length grows remains uncontrolled.

## 4. No bounded-height bank works on sufficiently long graphs

First suppose that every fresh arm survival is rational with reduced denominator at most Q. Since it is strictly less than one,

    1-x_i >= 1/Q,   1-y_i >= 1/Q.

The inheritance probabilities may be arbitrary interior reals. Nevertheless,

    1-b_2 = g_i^2(1-x_i)+(1-g_i)^2(1-y_i)
          >= [g_i^2+(1-g_i)^2]/Q >= 1/(2Q).

All ordinary factors are at most one. Any matching N-cell source therefore satisfies

    delta <= d_2(W) <= (1-1/(2Q))^N.

In particular Q >= N/[2 log(1/delta)]. The exact power inequality is enough to establish divergence without using any logarithmic equality oracle. This lower bound applies to EVERY matching bank on the graph, not just the explicit equal-arm one, and does not require bounded denominators for the coins or connectors.

For algebraic arm survivals of degree at most d and logarithmic Weil height at most h, use the finite set Lambda(d,h) of all such real algebraic numbers in (0,1). This is the same elementary Northcott/finite-polynomial enumeration already used in Section 5 of the observed-cohort supplier. If it is empty, no positive-cell bank exists. Otherwise set

    epsilon = min_(x in Lambda(d,h)) (1-x)>0.

Exactly the preceding calculation gives b_2<=1-epsilon/2 for every allowed cell, regardless of its interior coin. Thus

    delta <= (1-epsilon/2)^N,

which is impossible for sufficiently large N. Hence no input-only simultaneous degree/height bound can retain every regular realizing graph, even for this single fixed rational D.

The example does NOT say that degree alone must grow: the exhibited banks are rational for every N. It does NOT say that a height bound alone with unbounded degree has this consequence. It excludes simultaneous degree and height control on the unchanged graph, precisely the proposed addition to the regular degree theorem.

## 5. What failed, and the remaining full-source step

Attempted route: use the completed regular-source specialization and a quantitative implicit-function/rational-grid selection to obtain a total input-only finite parameter bank, then apply completed cohort survival to bound count.

The proposed same-graph upgrade is false. Even the present well-conditioned rank-one family eventually has no bounded-bank representative on that graph. Choosing different rational free coordinates in the same graph cannot repair it. A successful full selector must first change source architecture, or prove that at least one input-controlled architecture admits the required arithmetic bounds, while preserving every actual joint row, original ID, strict condition and chronological constraint.

For this example architecture change is easy: replace the entire two-root private word by E(c). The master obstruction is proving an analogous input-computable one-witness operation when higher-root forest structure, protected/coupled interfaces, and critical fibres are all retained. Replacing a word by an ordinary clock merely because its pair survival agrees is not valid there. No general compression theorem, critical-fibre selector or terminating NO follows from this example.

The required selector still must be total on NO inputs. Searching until an algebraic witness appears supplies no such stopping signal. Existing fixed-source algebraic descent, bounded degree on regular charts, and canonical YES enumeration remain completed and unchanged.
