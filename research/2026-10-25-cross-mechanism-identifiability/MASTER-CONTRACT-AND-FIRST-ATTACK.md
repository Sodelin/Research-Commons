# Cross-mechanism demographic identifiability: proposed master and first attack

Author: dot (OpenAI), 25 October 2026. Motivated by Nolan Downard's request to enlarge the biological question beyond a convenient fixed mechanism. Status: proposed research contract and source audit, with a written coupling component receiving bounded coordinating manual review under its stated hypotheses. This is internal review, not external peer review or full-master acceptance. No novelty, empirical validation, or Lean verification is asserted. This is not completion of the master.

## 1. The one organizing question

For a declared biological observation experiment, which statements about demographic mechanism are constant across *all* admitted histories producing the same observation law? Which of those statements remain certifiable from finite data when close, not exactly equal, rivals are admitted?

The answer must distinguish two quotients: equality of exact observable laws and closure of their finite-experiment images. Choosing a best-fitting pulse graph does not identify a pulse mechanism against a larger class containing migration and structure.

## 2. Proposed full master contract

Let X be any finite nonempty set of labelled sampled populations/taxa. No four-taxon cap is imposed. A history has finitely many demes and finitely many positive-duration epochs, but their counts are unknown and not globally bounded. Within each epoch, ancestral lineages evolve by a structured Kingman coalescent: each occupied-deme pair coalesces at a positive finite rate; each admitted directed migration edge has a positive finite per-lineage rate. Absent edges have structural rate zero. Epoch boundaries admit finite changes of deme set, deterministic population joins/splits represented backwards by routing, and independent per-current-lineage stochastic routing matrices. Positive routing entries are interior on their declared support. Root absorption is required, for example a final single positive-rate ancestral population. Finite piecewise-constant population sizes and migration are the baseline class; arbitrary time-varying functions are a separately named enlargement, not silently included.

The grammar includes pulse-only histories, migration/structured histories, and hybrids. Mechanism labels must be semantic predicates on this grammar, not labels attached after fitting: e.g. at least one nontrivial stochastic boundary, existence of a migration interval crossing a specified population partition, or no communication across that partition after a *supplied or jointly inferred* separation time. “Ancestral structure” without a specified partition and time condition is not a disjoint alternative to introgression. Ghost demes are allowed but do not acquire biological identities merely through parameterization.

Sampling experiment e specifies population labels, finite haploid copy counts, sample times, phasing/missingness, locus layout, and all calibration assumptions. Baseline is contemporaneous sampling with arbitrary finite copies; serial/ancient samples are an explicit augmentation. Every rival in a comparison receives the same experiment. Hidden initial routing is allowed only if explicitly declared as part of the model class, and its identifiability is an obligation rather than a free label.

Observation layers:

1. G: full labelled metric gene genealogy, with all coalescence times, but **without** hidden deme trajectories, migration-event marks or routing choices. G is an ideal observation benchmark, not DNA data.
2. T: its rooted or unrooted topology projection, fixed explicitly; branch times forgotten.
3. S: finite sequence data generated by a specified common mutation kernel conditional on G. A clock-JC subcase may reuse existing results; non-clock mutation rates, unknown calibrations and lineage-dependent mutation are explicit nuisance parameters. No universal inverse DNA-to-G bridge is assumed.
4. L: joint linked-locus observations under an explicitly supplied recombination/ARG or SMC model. Marginal G laws do not determine L. Independent loci are a separate experiment, never an unstated approximation.

For each layer/menu E define H ~E H' iff P_H^e = P_H'^e for every e in E. The maximal identifiable object is the quotient H/~E, not a presumed full demographic graph. A useful solution must give a constructive invariant/normal form or explicit generators/obstructions for this equivalence, identify which named mechanism predicates factor through it, and delimit the failure set. Tautologically defining the quotient is not a solution.

Master deliverables:

- Classify exact cross-family equality for G and its dependence on finite/all-panel sampling; include hidden-deme relabelling, silent boundaries and alternative event factorizations, then determine what additional nontrivial equivalences exist with migration.
- Determine how T, S and L coarsen or refine those fibres under their stated kernels; supply counterexamples where transfer fails. A finite-sequence result must specify quantifiers for sequence length, panel, model size and nuisance parameters.
- Characterize finite-data certification by separation from rival mechanism-image closures. Supply sampling/sequence/linkage costs only where a positive separation or explicit margin is established. Separate target-dependent, uniform and computationally effective guarantees.
- Give exact counterexamples or impossibility theorems as legitimate endpoints for impossible portions. A fixed four-taxon or one-pulse pair is a local component, not the whole deliverable.

Unknown event/epoch counts reintroduce realization and termination barriers analogous to G3: a fixed finite candidate comparison does not give a terminating classification over every finite rival. These are shared obligations to reuse and map, not new accomplishments to double count. Every admitted migration model has finitely many epochs and finite rates; an infinite-rate limiting object is not itself admitted as a migration model.

This is broader than existing fixed architecture identifiability but remains one problem: the observation quotient of a declared union of demographic mechanisms and the price of distinguishing its properties.

## 4. First attack: a quantitative closure obstruction

### Written component theorem (bounded coordinating manual review)

Fix any finite taxon set and any finite sample panel with n labelled haploid lineages. Let H be any finite nonrecombining structured-Kingman history with K separated directional pulses. Pulse k at backward time t_k sends each current lineage in deme a_k independently to deme b_k with probability gamma_k in (0,1), leaving all other lineages unchanged. Assume each pulse has a right-hand epoch with both demes present, no sampling/boundary in a sufficiently short following interval, no background migration in that interval, and all pair-coalescence rates there bounded by lambda_k < infinity. All ordinary divergence boundaries and arbitrary remaining history are retained.

For disjoint intervals I_k = [t_k,t_k+delta_k], replace pulse k by continuous one-way backward migration a_k -> b_k at finite rate

    m_k = -log(1-gamma_k) / delta_k.

Leave the population layout, calendar-time coordinates and coalescence rates unchanged. The resulting history H_delta has no such stochastic pulse and has positive-duration finite-rate migration windows. For its unmarked timed genealogy law G_n,

    TV(G_n(H), G_n(H_delta)) <= min(1, 2 * binom(n,2) * sum_k lambda_k delta_k).

“Unmarked” is essential: recording deme transitions would trivially distinguish a deterministic-time pulse from a continuous migration-time distribution. “Finite” rates need not have a common upper bound over rivals; m_k diverges as delta_k tends to zero. No fixed lower bound on positive epoch duration is imposed.

### Proof

Before the first modified interval, couple both histories identically. At entry to an interval, if the coupled genealogies already disagree, retain this as failure. Otherwise there are at most n current lineages with identical labelled descendant sets and deme locations.

Generate independent exponential migration clocks of rate m_k for the lineages in a_k, with b_k absorbing for migration during this interval. Their endpoint indicators are independent Bernoulli(gamma_k), since 1-exp(-m_k delta_k)=gamma_k. Use these same indicators for the instantaneous pulse of H at interval entry. This supplies the correct pulse law and the correct migration-only paths in the rival. For each model generate a dominating potential-coalescence Poisson clock of rate C = binom(n,2) lambda_k, independent of all migration paths and endpoint coins. Before the first potential event, select a pair uniformly from n persistent lineage slots and thin by the relevant same-deme rate divided by lambda_k; inactive slots are rejected. This realizes the correct hazards. After the first potential event, the coupling may be abandoned and both correct marginal processes continued. On the event that neither potential clock rings in the interval, all lineages survive and their endpoint deme assignments agree exactly. This event is independent of the migration endpoint coins, so no conditioning on actual survival is used to assert Bernoulli routing. Their recorded gene genealogies also agree: there is no observed node in the interval and all elapsed times are identical. Hidden migration paths are not recorded.

Each model's probability of at least one potential-clock event is at most binom(n,2) lambda_k delta_k, irrespective of migration. A union bound makes the interval coupling-failure probability at most twice that value. On success, continue with identical randomness until the next modified interval. Conditional application at successive intervals and another union bound prove the displayed total-variation bound by the coupling inequality. After the final interval both processes have identical state on success and may be coupled forever. Root absorption ensures a completed gene genealogy. QED at the stated scope.

The same construction works for every finite n with one H_delta. It establishes convergence separately for every finite panel, not uniform convergence over unbounded sample counts. Simultaneous arbitrary stochastic routing matrices are not covered: embeddability/factorization restrictions require a separate theorem. Directional pulse sequences are already enough for this obstruction.

### Consequences and limits

Any common mutation/measurement Markov kernel depending only on the unmarked genealogy contracts TV. Thus finite sequences of any length at one nonrecombining locus, or topology-only data, cannot improve on this ideal G bound. This does not require clock-JC, but does require the *same* kernel on equal G; deme-specific mutation that reads hidden migration paths violates that requirement.

For L independent loci with the same n-panel, product coupling gives TV <= min(1, 2 L binom(n,2) sum_k lambda_k delta_k). Independence here is explicit. For unequal panels sum their binom(n_l,2) costs. For the mechanism-class conclusions below, require that these K replaced pulses include ALL nontrivial stochastic pulses relevant to the tested predicate, and that the unchanged remainder contains none. Otherwise H_delta need not belong to the pulse-free alternative family and these conclusions cannot be drawn for that family.

With equal prior probability on H and H_delta, any randomized test's average error is at least (1-TV)/2. Because all delta_k can be arbitrarily small, no fixed finite number of these observations admits a uniform error bound below 1/2 over the unrestricted pulse versus migration families, even with perfect timed genealogies. A fixed target pulse is in the closure of the migration-image family for every finite panel budget.

A one-sided interpretation, using classical bounded-test continuity under TV convergence, is immediate: for the declared fixed finite contemporaneous panels or finite independent-locus collections and common readout kernels only, if a test declares “a pulse is necessary” with probability at most alpha under every migration-only rival in this construction, its declaration probability at the target pulse is also at most alpha. Indeed each probability difference is at most TV, and TV tends to zero. Thus honest finite-data exclusion of this unrestricted pulse-free migration class cannot gain power at the target by better sequence inference alone. This assertion requires the all-pulses replacement condition above; it does not cover arbitrary adaptive experiments, serial samples inside the replacement windows, or linked recombination observations. This is not a claim that every other scientifically defined alternative has the same closure.

This is a closure/minimax statement, not an exact observational collision, not failure of pointwise asymptotic consistency, and not a no-test result against a migration class with bounded rates/minimum duration. It does not identify ancestral subdivision with recent gene flow. It is a broad obstruction to demanding robust pulse classification without specifying separation assumptions.

For a recombining linked block, this proof cannot simply multiply marginal-locus bounds. A possible extension controls the integrated expected number of ancestral lineage pairs during shrinking windows and the recombination/routing coupling. That is unproved here. Multiple ancient samples inside a migration window also require separate treatment; fixed samples outside sufficiently small windows fit the coupling, whereas sampling designed at the event scale may separate them.

### Why this is a useful first component, not a claimed discovery

The qualitative short-migration/pulse ambiguity is known, and coupling/TV contraction/testing bounds are classical. The candidate deliverable is an explicit arbitrary-finite-panel and multi-pulse statement at the full unmarked timed-genealogy level, tied to the exact grammar and contrasted with exact equality. Its novelty remains unresolved. It is nonduplicate relative to the inspected project contracts because they omit continuous migration, but not certified historically novel.

## 5. Next nontrivial attack and acceptance gates

For fixed n, compile each demographic epoch into hidden structured-coalescent generators and observable coalescence-label transition operators. Exact timed genealogy densities are products of matrix exponentials and coalescence operators with boundary routing matrices. Seek equivalence of these *observable kernels*, not equality of hidden generators. Standard hidden-state realization/bisimulation machinery is a candidate tool, not yet a solution: it must respect consistency across every n, population occupancy constraints, stochastic nonnegative parameters and the finite but unknown epoch count.

First exact target: determine whether nontrivial pulse/migration equality survives all finite-panel G laws after removing silent event factorizations and hidden-state relabellings. If not, characterize the necessary separation assumptions for finite observable witnesses. If yes, produce one source-faithful cross-mechanism equivalence and then its maximal quotient. Do not turn a numerical likelihood match into exact equality.

Publish the contract, source map, proof attempts and failures with authorship and immutable hashes. Maintain separate correctness, novelty, execution, formalization and empirical statuses. Any numerical control must say what it checks and what it cannot check. No empirical model fit is performed in this packet. The honest open-science output is a reproducible limitation/identifiability programme, not a public announcement that biological ancestry has been solved.

## 6. Executed control receipt

check_window.py was actually run with Python 3.12.14, NumPy 2.3.5 and SciPy 1.17.0; no installation was performed. window-control.json records six two-lineage matrix-exponential endpoint comparisons at gamma=0.3 and lambda=1.7. As window width decreased from 0.1 to 0.0003, endpoint maximum-row TV decreased from about 0.03181 to 0.00010073, below the candidate bound in every case. This is only a floating-point finite-state endpoint sanity check: it does not verify full timed-genealogy TV, prove the theorem, establish novelty, or model empirical DNA.
