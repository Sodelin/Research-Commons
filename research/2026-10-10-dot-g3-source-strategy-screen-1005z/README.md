## 1. Positive realization can succeed on an actual original NO

For the natural unexposed COMMON subsystem, let Lambda={1,3,6,10,15,21}. Actual finite words have

    m_lambda = A^lambda product_i(1-p_i+p_i q_i^lambda),
    0<A,p_i,q_i<1.

The accepted [eight-row calibration](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f884f1e9d520f7cbf33bfbe289d4fa88c9975d7c/research/2026-10-06-dot-g3-calibrated-original-recognition-1422z/WORKING-PROOF-R2.md) maps m to Phi(m)=(F(m),2/3,25/48), with F invertible rational affine. For that menu, original realization across all admitted competing cores is equivalent to actual strict COMMON word realization. This transfer is inherited; arbitrary controlled/register or INDEPENDENT fibres are not diagonalized by this checkpoint.

The inherited sufficiently small pure-Poisson signatures

    m_lambda = exp[-a lambda-w R_lambda(r)],
    R_lambda(r)=sum_(j=0)^(lambda-1) r^j,

lie in ordinary sparse-moment interior and actual-source closure, but are not finite strict COMMON words. Rational examples follow by taking r=1/q, L=q^20 and a=w=-L log(t), with rational t sufficiently close to one: every m_lambda=t^[L(lambda+R_lambda(r))] is rational. See the prior [rational construction](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f884f1e9d520f7cbf33bfbe289d4fa88c9975d7c/research/2026-10-06-dot-g3-calibrated-original-recognition-1422z/NO-FIXED-SEMIALGEBRAIC-RATIONAL-CLASSIFIER.md) and [exact nonattainment certificate](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f884f1e9d520f7cbf33bfbe289d4fa88c9975d7c/research/2026-10-01-sol61-g3-boundary-resume-2124z/SMALL-LOSS-POISSON-NONATTAINMENT.md).

**Hand derivation.** Every rational interior point m of conv{(x^lambda)_lambda:0<=x<=1} is a convex combination of at most seven rational nodes x_j in (0,1), with positive rational weights w_j. Indeed, finitely many open-interval curve nodes have a convex hull containing a neighborhood of m; approximate these nodes rationally while retaining that property. The resulting rational linear feasibility system has a rational basic feasible solution with at most seven nonzero weights.

Set D=diag(x_j), b=1 and c=(w_j). Then D,b,c are rational nonnegative, rho(D)<1, and c^T D^lambda b=m_lambda simultaneously for every lambda in Lambda. Thus

    H(z)=c^T(zI-D)^(-1)b=sum_j w_j/(z-x_j)

has a stable positive realization of dimension at most seven. Also (c^T,D) is a proper discrete phase-type survival realization, with absorption probabilities 1-x_j>0. Yet Phi(m) is an original all-core COMMON NO by the inherited source theorem.

This rejects the direct transfer through finite positive moment realization, even with a common rational diagonal model for all coordinates. The absent implication is a strict original Bernoulli-product factorization of the same finite data. No claim is made against every possible encoding into a realization problem.

## 2. What finite survival lattices do and do not provide

A universal single survival-log base is already excluded by the accepted [cap-eight two-factor rigidity](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f884f1e9d520f7cbf33bfbe289d4fa88c9975d7c/research/2026-10-08-codex-g3-g4-full-shot-1253z/g3-witness-bound/attempt2/PRIVATE-ALL-COPY-RIGIDITY-ADDENDUM.md): the specified rational attained-boundary fibre forces unequal ratios 1/2 and 1/3, which cannot both be rational powers of one positive base. The [review](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f884f1e9d520f7cbf33bfbe289d4fa88c9975d7c/research/2026-10-08-codex-g3-g4-full-shot-1253z/integration/ROOT-PRIVATE-ALL-COPY-RIGIDITY-REVIEW.md) retains its private COMMON and calibrated A/B-restricted scope; it does not transfer full C/D correlations or exposed/reused registers.

The inherited [rational unbounded-minimum-count sequence](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f884f1e9d520f7cbf33bfbe289d4fa88c9975d7c/research/2026-10-06-dot-g3-calibrated-original-recognition-1422z/RATIONAL-INPUT-CONSEQUENCES.md) has m_lambda^(N)=b^lambda(1-p_N+p_N 2^(-lambda))^N for one fixed rational b and strict rational p_N tending to zero. Allocate c_N=b^(1/(2N+1)) to the N arm scales and N+1 ordinary pieces. Every population survival in this private word is c_N or c_N/2, in the same fixed rational-power group generated by b and 2; outside calibrated populations may use 1/2, still in that group. The minimum exact source count nevertheless diverges. Its [all-core count transfer](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f884f1e9d520f7cbf33bfbe289d4fa88c9975d7c/research/2026-10-06-dot-g3-calibrated-original-recognition-1422z/CALIBRATED-WITNESS-SIZE-COROLLARY.md) is inherited. This excludes a uniform count ceiling based on cap and lattice rank; it does not exclude an input-dependent bound.

**Separate hand check.** Hold physical survivals at powers of 1/2, with upper/lower arm survivals 1/2 and 1/4. Put q=1/2 and p=2/P for an odd prime P. Ordinary baseline cancels from

    m_3/m_1^3 = P^2(4P-7)/(4(P-1)^3).

Its P-adic valuation is 2 when P!=7. Every fixed finite rational-power group generated by positive algebraic numbers has rational valuations supported on finitely many rational primes: put the generators in one number field and use the finite support of their principal fractional ideals. Choosing P outside that support shows that even a rank-one survival lattice does not yield a fixed finite-rank group of complete source letters when inheritance varies.

This does not exclude input-dependent bases or recognition by another method. The fixed survivals in this particular example actually give a positive per-cell loss floor and easy bounded search; the calculation is a screen of an arithmetic implication, not hardness.

## 3. Source-faithful fixed-q reformulation

Fix q in (0,1), an ordinary baseline A in (0,1), and the finite exponent set Lambda. Write z_lambda=q^lambda and let h be the degree-at-most-|Lambda| interpolant satisfying h(1)=1 and h(z_lambda)=m_lambda/A^lambda. Set

    P(z)=(z-1) product_lambda(z-z_lambda).

A finite strict fixed-q Bernoulli word exists exactly when there is a polynomial f congruent to h modulo P, normalized by f(1)=1, all of whose roots are strictly negative; the constant polynomial 1 represents no unequal factors. Each root -r gives the strict factor (r+z)/(r+1)=1-p+pz with p=1/(r+1). For degree N, put c=A^(1/(2N+1)): N arm pairs cq,c and N+1 positive ordinary passages with survival c realize that same normalization physically.

Equivalently, [h] belongs to the literal finite-product semigroup of [1-p+pz], 0<p<1, in R[z]/(P). Its multiplication matrices are simultaneously diagonalizable and lie on one open segment I-p(I-M_z). This is a faithful reformulation for supplied q,A, not an unbounded-degree decision theorem. Fixed-degree real-rootedness tests, arbitrary convex mixtures and entire Laguerre–Pólya limits do not decide the required finite polynomial existence. The baseline must still be chosen coherently in the original fibre.

Equal-weight designs also retain a changing count: a source target is a sum of N log-generator values, while a probability design matches the average h/N. The prior [A2 source calculation, Section 4](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f884f1e9d520f7cbf33bfbe289d4fa88c9975d7c/research/2026-10-08-dot-g3-whole-proof-attempt-a2-1348z/WHOLE-PROOF-ATTEMPT-A2-MINIMAL-WORDS.md), already gives K(N)>=N-1 and blocks the directly applied design bound. No new exact strong-head/weak-tail theorem was obtained here.

## 4. External theorems and the missing transfer

- [Nagy–Matolcsi positive realization](https://real.mtak.hu/7970/1/algorithm%20for.pdf), sections II–III, takes an entire supplied rational transfer function and constructs a nonnegative realization after its pole/cyclic checks. It does not impose original source factorization; Section 1 gives a concrete false-positive transfer.
- [Babai–Beals–Cai–Ivanyos–Luks commuting matrix equations](https://www.researchwithrutgers.org/en/publications/multiplicative-equations-over-commuting-matrices/) take a supplied finite algebraic matrix alphabet and integer powers. Fresh continuous physical parameters at every source append are not such an alphabet. No blanket polynomial complexity claim is imported.
- [Ouaknine–Pouly–Sousa-Pinto–Worrell, Theorem 4.4](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/matrix-exponential17.pdf), decides the commuting case for finitely many supplied algebraic rate matrices and nonnegative durations. Bernoulli-mixture cells are not those fixed flows, and logarithms of algebraic cell entries need not be algebraic rates.
- [Constructible semigroup Zariski-closure algorithms](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/zariski18.pdf) supply identities/closure, which include the inherited Poisson NO points.

For supplied finitely many complete strict source letters with pair survivals s_i in (0,1), rho=max_i s_i<1. A required private pair survival t>0 forces t<=rho^n, even with extra positive ordinary passage. Exact algebraic comparisons find N with rho^(N+1)<t; enumerate actual words through length N and solve the remaining fixed finite equations. This is the inherited faithful fixed-alphabet/loss-floor boundary. A whole unknown-core fibre must itself supply the needed uniform positive lower bound; an unrelated observed coordinate cannot replace t.

The remaining hypothesis is effective, input-derived source-complete factor/alphabet extraction or exact finite-product membership for the continuous original alphabet, including terminal NO coverage on in-closure inputs. Finite log rank, positive realization and closure do not supply it. The [accepted exact-tail compression](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f884f1e9d520f7cbf33bfbe289d4fa88c9975d7c/research/2026-10-01-sol61-g3-boundary-resume-2124z/EXACT-TAIL-COMPRESSION.md) already removes countably retained analytic tails; it does not provide this input-effective bound. Known [richer logarithmic NO certificates](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f884f1e9d520f7cbf33bfbe289d4fa88c9975d7c/research/2026-10-07-dot-g3-logarithmic-certificate-classes-0428z/README.md) are also retained, without a claim of complete coverage.
