## 2. Direct alternative tested: subdivide and refresh the body

A concrete attempt to avoid the all-order lift is to replace a finite split by arbitrarily many strict short splits, with an ordinary connector after each, and retune the ordinary pads. This is a genuine admitted family, so its entire arbitrary-length behavior can be tested directly.

For any positive integer N, put

    B_N = B(2^(-1/N), 2^(-1/N), 2/3),
    D_N = E((3/4)^(1/N)),
    W_N(a,r) = E(a) [B_N D_N]^N E(r),  0<a,r<1.

All connectors and arms are positive and finite; all inheritance values are interior. Parameters are shared across every row. The pads a,r may depend arbitrarily on N or the requested observation prefix. No route inside a merged genealogy is resampled at the leaf level.

### 2.1 Full capped operator calculation

Fix any finite cap m and let Q_m be the ordinary pair-rate-one Kingman generator on complete labelled forests. Conditional on any entering forest, each pair of current roots is routed together into arm one with probability g^2 and into arm two with probability (1-g)^2. For arm durations alpha/N and beta/N, this gives

    B(exp(-alpha/N),exp(-beta/N),g)
       = I + (g^2 alpha+(1-g)^2 beta) Q_m/N + O_m(N^(-2)).

This is a finite-state operator expansion on opaque rooted forests, not a no-merger-only identity. Two or more mergers contribute only to the remainder. For the displayed family, alpha=beta=log 2 and g=2/3, so

    kappa=(5/9) log 2,  delta=log(4/3),
    B_N D_N = I + (kappa+delta) Q_m/N + O_m(N^(-2)).

Compare one step with E(exp(-(kappa+delta)/N)). Both are stochastic operators. Telescoping N products and using contraction of row total variation under stochastic composition gives a finite constant C_m such that, for sufficiently large N,

    || [B_N D_N]^N - E(exp(-(kappa+delta))) ||_TV <= C_m/N.

Multiplication by the two actual ordinary pads cannot increase this bound. Uniformly for ALL strict a,r,

    || W_N(a,r) - E(a r exp(-(kappa+delta))) ||_TV <= C_m/N.    (A)

Thus arbitrarily many refreshed short splits approach an ordinary population family. They do not approach the original retained split. The underlying reason is physical: persistent arm membership within one bigon is not the same operation as a fresh routing choice at every new bigon.

### 2.3 This rejects the whole subdivision family, not just a chosen sample size

All members of this family that match the cap-four target rows have N<=N_0. There are then only finitely many graphical architectures. The inherited bounded-shape finite-determination result supplies a finite further topology certificate excluding every inequivalent member of that finite union.

Hence the displayed arbitrary-N subdivision family cannot furnish the required exact all-prefix negative construction. This conclusion allows arbitrary strict pads; it is not a numerical check at one N. No effective value of N_0 or original observation-only stopping algorithm is claimed.

## 3. Why that rejection does not prove G4

The original class is much larger. Rivals can retain a persistent nonordinary body and add arbitrarily many weak cells; vary arm durations and coins heterogeneously; couple both eligible regions; or change other admitted parts of the source. Equation (A) does not reduce these sources to the displayed subdivision family. In particular, the inherited exact cap-five libraries already prevent promoting a small-cap diagnostic into a universal source-count bound.

The retained-body route still has to solve H_m=0 for every cap with actual strict source words. A positive route must instead prove finite legal forcing against ALL original rivals. Neither obligation was discharged in this pass.
