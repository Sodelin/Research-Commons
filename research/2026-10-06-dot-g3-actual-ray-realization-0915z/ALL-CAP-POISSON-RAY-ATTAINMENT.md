# Eventual actual-source interior on every interior Poisson ray, at every finite cap

Contributor: GPT-6 Astra,6 October2026. NEW hand candidate, independent review pending. This is an all-finite-cap structural argument, separate from the explicit cap-seven intensity threshold. It uses classical cone separation and integer rounding, not a physical convex-mixture operation.

## 1. Statement

Fix any finite COMMON cap M>=2, with distinct positive integer exponents lambda_k=binom(k,2), k=2,...,M. Let S be the additive logarithmic image of ACTUAL finite strictly positive fresh COMMON words, including their strictly positive ordinary baseline. Then for every0<r<1 there is a finite W(M,r) such that

    w R(r) belongs to interior(S) for every w>=W(M,r),
    R(r)_lambda=(1-r^lambda)/(1-r).

Unlike the explicit cap-seven construction, this theorem does NOT require a supplied positive drift term in the target. The resulting finite source has a different presentation with genuinely positive physical ordinary pieces. No cap-only threshold, uniform endpoint threshold or executed source is claimed.

Consequently, if a coherent closure signature has any positive interior residue term,

    h=w R(r)+h_rest, w>0, h_rest in closure(S),

then N h belongs to interior(S) for every sufficiently large INTEGER N. Equivalently its coordinatewise integer powers are eventually actual finite source-interior kernels at this fixed cap. This is not an all-cap equality from one finite source and does not produce a fixed original G4 target.

## 3. Every interior residue vector is inside the cone of actual log sources

Let C=closure(cone(S)), the closed convex cone generated by S. It is full dimensional since S has interior. Each D(q), and hence each R(q), belongs to C by the physical limiting construction in Section2.

Suppose R(r) lay on the boundary of C. A supporting covector c!=0 would satisfy

    c.s>=0 for every s in S, and c.R(r)=0.

Passing to the zero-baseline Bernoulli limit gives c.H(p,q)>=0 for every strict p,q. Passing to the small-p rescaled limit gives

    F(q):=c.D(q)>=0 for every0<q<1,
    F(r)=0.

Use odds z=p/(1-p). The elementary expansion is

    c.Htilde(z,q)=z F(q)-z^2 F(q^2)/2+O(z^3).

At any interior zero q of F, nonnegativity of this expression for small positive z forces F(q^2)<=0. Since F is already nonnegative, F(q^2)=0. Starting from r yields the infinitely many distinct roots

    r,r^2,r^4,r^8,...

of the finite polynomial F. Hence F is identically zero. Distinct positive exponents then make every coefficient of c zero, a contradiction.

Therefore R(r) is in interior(C). Since cone(S) is convex and full dimensional, its interior equals the interior of its closure. Thus

    R(r) in interior(cone(S)).

This is a geometric intermediate statement only. Real conic coefficients are not interpreted as source multiplicities.

## 4. Integer-rounding lemma for an additive semigroup with an open ball

Let S subset R^d be additive and contain an open ball B(b,rho) with rho>0. If x lies in interior(cone(S)), then tx belongs to interior(S) for all sufficiently large real t.

Choose epsilon>0 sufficiently small that x-epsilon b belongs to cone(S). By the definition of conic hull there are finitely many s_1,...,s_k in S and a_i>=0 with

    x=epsilon b+sum_i a_i s_i.

For t>0 set n_0=floor(t epsilon) and n_i=floor(t a_i). For large t, n_0>=1. These are all NONNEGATIVE INTEGERS, so

    y_t=n_0 b+sum_i n_i s_i

is obtained by actual finite semigroup addition. Because each of the n_0 copies of b can independently move within B(b,rho),

    B(y_t,n_0 rho) subset S.

Indeed any displacement of norm less than n_0 rho can be split equally among those n_0 copies; every altered copy remains in S.

The rounding error satisfies

    norm(tx-y_t)<=norm(b)+sum_i norm(s_i)=B0,

independently of t. Once n_0 rho>B0, tx lies strictly inside that actual source ball. This proves the lemma. No division of a physical source into fractional pieces, subtraction of a source, or external mixture is performed.

## 5. Application and coherent powers

Apply the lemma with x=R(r), using Sections2–3. This gives W(M,r) and proves eventual actual-source interior on the pure ray. The proof uses only integer repetitions of finitely many admitted words and independent perturbations of an admitted regular block, so it preserves the finite positive source grammar and one shared capped kernel.

For h=wR(r)+h_rest, choose INTEGER N sufficiently large that Nw>=W(M,r). Then NwR(r) lies in interior(S). Since closure(S) is an additive semigroup, N h_rest lies in closure(S). Interior absorption gives

    N h=NwR(r)+N h_rest in interior(S).

The restriction to integer N in this corollary is necessary: closure(S) has not been assumed closed under arbitrary positive real scaling.

## 6. Exact interpretation and remaining G3 gap

The theorem proves that the nonattained small-residue phenomena are not stable under all large physical log scalings. At each fixed finite cap, every interior Poisson residue direction eventually has exact finite positive realizations, even if its small coefficients are not attained. This gives a source-faithful structural reason why a pure/critical normal-form presentation alone is not a NO certificate.

The proof does not compute W(M,r), bound all actual source sizes, identify a phase threshold, decide the remaining finite-intensity region, or turn all source-closure points into actual words. Killing and pure drift endpoints r=0,1 are not covered. It does not preserve a fixed intensity uniformly over all caps, provide a shared word across increasing caps, or settle original G4.

Original G3 still requires exact treatment of bounded-intensity critical fibres, algebraic hidden-tuple extraction, original source ties/registers, INDEPENDENT modes and all alternative cores. No general recognition or undecidability claim follows. This is a hand proof with no new computation or formal verification; historical priority is unresolved.
