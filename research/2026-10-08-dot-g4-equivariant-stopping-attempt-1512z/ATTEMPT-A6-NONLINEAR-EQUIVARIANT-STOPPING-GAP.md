# G4 whole-proof attempt A6: nonlinear equations and effective equivariant stopping

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 8 October 2026.

**Terminal outcome:** the proposed full stopping argument fails. The tested effective Noetherian theorems concern algebraic image closures under additional functorial symmetries; they do not certify the exact positive source fibre against unknown-length rivals. This record adds an applicability analysis, not a new source-obstruction theorem. The full-forest ideal/radical result used below is recovered accepted prior work.

## 1. Proposed complete positive argument

Fix one actual finite positive target T. Query a finite legal prefix and form arbitrary polynomial comparisons of its exact response values. Products and other nonlinear computations of already obtained responses are allowed computations; they are not assumed to be affine one-box readouts or additional experiments.

The attempted proof was:

1. Express equality to T as polynomial equations on actual source parameters, with the same tuple used in every row.
2. Prove that the full target fibre is defined by finitely many such equations uniformly over all finite rival lengths.
3. Use an effective stabilization theorem to recognize when enough equations have been collected.
4. Extract an actual source and certify every later original response, retaining both inheritance modes, original interfaces and the effective-data promises.

Step 2 must concern the union of all finite rival architectures. Hilbert's basis theorem on a supplied finite rival shape only bounds the equations in that fixed finite parameter ring. It does not remove the moving rival-length quantifier. A witnessed plateau between two prefixes is not a stopping certificate.

Even supplying T in advance would not repair the uniformity problem below. Reconstructing an unknown target from observations would add, rather than remove, an extraction obligation.

For the complete response algebra of actual private INDEPENDENT words, the full finite-cap target ideals strictly increase, including after algebraic or real radical. The proof allows coefficients involving arbitrarily high but finitely many response coordinates. Formal source-group perturbations fix every lower forest coordinate and change a later diagonal. At any fixed larger cap they can retain positive normalized forest entries and the ordinary structural identities.

Those witnesses are not asserted to be actual positive words. ## 3. Testing the strongest equivariant finiteness theorem

**Primary statement.** Draisma, [*Topological Noetherianity of polynomial functors*, arXiv:1705.01419v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.01419v4), JAMS 32 (2019), 691–707, Sections 1.1–1.4, Theorem 1 and Corollary 3: a polynomial functor of uniformly finite degree over an infinite field is Noetherian for functor-stable closed subsets. Its inverse-limit formulation gives finitely many GL-infinity orbits of equations for a stable closed subset. The bounded degree and functorial stability are explicit premises.

**Actual-source test.** No such bounded-degree functor with the necessary target-fibre stability has been supplied for arbitrary ordered source compositions and all copy arities. Natural relabelling of sampled labels preserves arity. It does not turn one low-arity no-merger coordinate into every higher-arity coordinate. In the exchangeable private quotient, relabelled versions of the same scalar coordinate agree; these symmetries do not remove the strict target-ideal chain above.

Using source positions as functor variables does not solve this automatically either. A word is a chronological product. Deleting or linearly mixing parameter positions has no established compatible linear action on its endpoint kernel, and permuting positions is not generally an endpoint symmetry. Enlarging the encoding to include unobserved subword products would require a new observation and source-reconstruction argument.

Therefore the theorem cannot be applied merely because every supplied finite source map is polynomial. Finite polynomial degree at each separately fixed architecture is weaker than one uniformly finite-degree functor across architectures. No impossibility of every conceivable alternative encoding is claimed; the encoding and admissible finite-data reduction needed for this proof are absent.

## 4. The effective theorem does not bridge actual membership

**Primary statement.** Blatter, Draisma and Ventura, [*Implicitisation and Parameterisation in Polynomial Functors*, arXiv:2206.01555v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.01555v1), Sections 2.1 and 4.1–4.3, Theorem 4.1.1, give a terminating implicitisation algorithm over a computable characteristic-zero field with polynomial factorization. Its output defines an algebraic image closure by finitely many equations pulled back along all relevant linear maps. Section 1.6 explains the additional certification machinery needed beyond observing a plateau. Example 5.1.1 explicitly distinguishes image membership from membership in its closure using cubic rank and border rank.

**Actual-source test.** This is a real effective theorem, so dismissing it as merely nonconstructive would be incorrect. Its effectivity solves the stated closure problem. It does not convert closure points into one finite strictly positive chronological word with the original shared parameters. Nor does a finite list of equation-orbit types automatically become a finite original experiment prefix: evaluating every required pullback needs its own source-faithful data reduction.

Freezing the target's parameters as a finite-dimensional base does not add the missing source functor, positive-image characterization or legal pullback action. Allowing all complex algebraic points or signed source-group elements would change the rival class. The accepted formal witnesses in Section 2 show why that relaxation cannot certify the desired complete target fibre.

The nearby phylogenetic theorem of Draisma and Eggermont, [*Finiteness results for Abelian tree models*, arXiv:1207.1282v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.1282v2), JEMS 17 (2015), 711–738, Theorem 1.1 and the model definition in Section 1, likewise fixes a finite state alphabet and treats Zariski closures of the specified equivariant Markov tree models. An unbounded-copy coalescent forest process is not supplied with that fixed-alphabet representation. The title's phylogenetic setting does not identify its model with the original source grammar.

## 5. Successful nonlinear target-fibre control must be retained

The newly accepted [COMMON attained-boundary review](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/e694c6a3cf6468319f19733e42e039d3d2fcd7fc/research/2026-10-08-codex-g3-g4-full-shot-1253z/integration/ROOT-ATTAINED-BOUNDARY-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md) and [private all-copy rigidity review](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/e694c6a3cf6468319f19733e42e039d3d2fcd7fc/research/2026-10-08-codex-g3-g4-full-shot-1253z/integration/ROOT-PRIVATE-ALL-COPY-RIGIDITY-REVIEW.md) are an important positive control.

For the stated rational two-cell COMMON family sufficiently near its endpoint, a whole-word nonlinear chart guard bounds a nonnegative total tail cost uniformly over arbitrary finite rival lengths. At exact cap-eight equality the tail cost is zero; strict unequal tail factors disappear, and the local inverse fixes the two persistent factors' entire Bernoulli survival law. Thus complete private all-copy equality follows in that family.

This is precisely the extra source mathematics missing from the general algebraic transfer: persistence extraction, a count-uniform physical cost and its rigid equality case. It must not be erased by saying that nonlinear certificates are unavailable. It also must not be extended beyond its proved scope. The calibrated nine-row A/B interpretation uses at most eleven copies; unrestricted retained C/D correlations, original interventions, shared registers and INDEPENDENT routing remain separate. The near-endpoint interval has no certified numerical cutoff here, so the result alone is not an observation-only effective stopping implementation.

The prior full-ring obstruction is a private INDEPENDENT result. The successful COMMON guard concerns a different mechanism and uses actual-source inequalities, not only identities on its algebraic envelope. There is no contradiction.

## 6. Full-attempt failure and remaining quantifiers

The proposed proof fails at Step 2's uniform actual-fibre stabilization and Step 3's use of a closure algorithm as a physical stopping certificate. Stronger equivariant algebraic effectivity does not remove those failures under the checked hypotheses. Neither a bounded-rival result nor the finite parameter dimension of T supplies a bound on every inequivalent rival.

No complete original rich-menu finite-forcing algorithm, full dichotomy or fixed-target negative construction has been obtained. A successful positive proof would still need a source-specific equality mechanism covering the unresolved actual fibres, with an effective stopping certificate and the original mode/interface transfer. The special COMMON guard is evidence that this kind of mechanism can work; it is not a general coverage theorem.

This is the terminal record of this whole attempt. It does not promote a new restricted parameter test or finite-cap ladder as progress toward a completed master proof. Historical novelty and formal verification are not claimed.
