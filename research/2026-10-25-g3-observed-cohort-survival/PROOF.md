## 2. Source/observer theorem, with the whole history retained

Let d_j(W) be the no-merger probability of the SAME actual I word on j
fresh current-root tokens, after its genuine two-exit pooling. Then

    d_r(W)>=delta.                                      (4)

Proof. Any merger joining two selected cohort roots before or during W
creates an internal genealogical node whose selected descendant labels
form a subset B of A with at least two labels. Before the closed segment
ends, there are no exterior selected labels in that cohort. In particular
the merger cannot already contain a label in L\A.

Later coalescent mergers graft whole current trees. They never insert an
exterior leaf into an already formed subtree, split it, or reroute its old
leaves separately. The node therefore remains an A-only clade in the final
rooted tree. Pruning unobserved copies cannot remove this selected clade:
both children of the relevant selected merger contain selected labels.
Thus E_A implies no merger among its selected cohort ancestors in W.
It also excludes any entering opaque tree with two selected A labels.

Condition now on each actual entering history/register state. If selected
cohort labels have already merged, E_A has conditional probability zero.
Otherwise their selected input is r current roots at the actual bridge
node, possibly carrying unobserved opaque material. The actual selected
source law and grafting consistency give no-merger probability d_r(W),
independent of those opaque shapes. I choices are by current root with the
same physical g; no unmarked private bit is read/reused by the menu.

Conditioning on the subsequent genealogy or freezing the exterior is not
needed. Given the entering state, the probability of E_A is at most d_r(W),
because its future/readout is an event of probability at most one after
this necessary no-merger condition. Integrate the SAME entering history/
register mixture once. This proves P(E_A)<=d_r(W), hence (4). QED.

The statement also applies when the supplied joint experiment genuinely
provides this unconditional I marginal. It does not substitute a product
of independently fitted marginal laws or a conditional probability with
an unprovided normalization. Full population/register kernel equality is
not inferred from this event bound.

## 4. Every closed subword is spectrally conditioned without a COMMON query

The accepted private-word law gives diagonal multiplication:

    d_j(W)=z0^lambda_j product_i
             [b_j(x_i,y_i,g_i)*a_i^lambda_j],
    lambda_j=j(j-1)/2,
    b_j(x,y,g)=sum_(k=0)^j binom(j,k) g^k(1-g)^(j-k)
                         x^lambda_k y^lambda_(j-k).     (6)

These are the OLD actual two-arm current-root formulas. They use the same
x,y,g and original parent order in every coordinate; old leaves are opaque.
Ordinary factors and pooled cells are stochastic, so every factor diagonal
is in (0,1]. Therefore for every CONTIGUOUS SUBWORD U whose boundaries are
closed factor boundaries,

    d_r(U)>=d_r(W)>=delta.

Deletion projectivity gives d_j(U)>=d_r(U) for 1<=j<=r. Hence

    d_j(U)>=delta,                  1<=j<=r.             (7)

The OLD full-forest projective spectral theorem bounds each spectral
projector of U by r!, including coincident diagonals.
There are at most r distinct eigenvalues in [delta,1]. Its spectral
calculus consequently gives, for the COMPLETE labelled r-token forest
operator of each such U,

    ||log U||_row <= r*r!*log(1/delta),
    ||U^(-1)||_row <= r*r!/delta.                        (8)

These are arithmetic log/inverse bounds, not physical inverse operations.
They are not bounds on full Code/history/population matrices with same-count
state changes, nor on partial unpooled arm programs or larger caps j>r.
Most importantly, they are not bounds on physical durations or cell count.

No COMMON datum or equal-arm clock is assumed in (4)--(8). In a source/
path search, the polynomial conditions d_j(prefix)>=delta can trim this
slot's fitting paths soundly: EVERY path fitting the supplied event obeys
them. They are data-derived backward restrictions, not invariant claims
for every untrimmed initialization. Compact spectral bounds alone do not
prove finite physical attainability, a finite source bound or complete NO.

    rho_r=max {b_r(x,y,g): x,y,g in Lambda(D)}.           (9)

## 6. A precise full-panel coverage corollary

If a supplied natural I experiment includes every original taxon at least
TWICE and its complete rooted tree law is given, then every proper nonempty
descendant-taxon cohort has r>=2 and an outside label. For each such cohort
E_A is nonempty: start a rooted caterpillar with an outside label and attach
the cohort labels successively, so every internal node containing two of
them also contains that outside label. Attach remaining labels afterward.

Every fixed rooted labelled topology has positive probability under a
finite strict original source with ordinary ancestral completion: the event
of no merger before the ancestor has positive probability, and ordinary
Kingman completion gives every labelled merger history positive probability.
Thus a fitting full tree law gives P(E_A)>0. If the supplied full law gives
zero to such a nonempty event, it is already incompatible with that actual
natural-source full-support property.

The existing grammar uses eligible BRIDGE slots and retains root-LSA. A
bridge below root cannot have every taxon as a descendant: its lower endpoint
would be a stable ancestor of all taxa below the purported LSA root. Its
cohort is therefore proper. Consequently this ALREADY SUPPLIED two-per-
taxon full I row covers all eligible unmarked words of every template.
No extra panel is queried or assigned target probabilities by this proof.

Combining this coverage with the total finite-bank selection hypothesis
of Section5 yields a conditional full recognizer for inputs containing
that row, while retaining ALL other original joint rows and constraints.
It does not decide original arbitrary/coarsened menus without coverage,
and it does not supply the bank selection hypothesis. Merely renaming that
missing height/selection theorem would not be a new full G3 attack.
