## B1. Transfer undecidable discrete matrix/submonoid membership into original G3

**Intended complete conclusion:** no total original finite-algebraic-input G3 recognizer exists.

**Whole architecture:** choose an established recursively undecidable finite matrix/group language; map each input to one finite legal algebraic original observation instance; implement every simulated transition by actual strict source words; make legal observations force all accepting realizations to use the encoded language; and prove both directions across every alternative original core. The input map must be effective, the observation menu finite and original, and no infinite or zero-time source may be returned.

**Attempt made:** use nilpotent source-group blocks and the exact signed-ordinary-time saturation theorem as an entry point for a discrete nilpotent submonoid encoding. The relevant primary undecidability theorem is Roman'kov's large-direct-power Heisenberg result; smaller nilpotent groups can have decidable membership, so generic nilpotence is not a hardness theorem. See B1-DISCRETE-ENCODING-ATTEMPT.md.

**Actual result:** the available source statements do not implement that reduction. A literal group embedding cannot land in the strict physical source semigroup. A fixed finite strict generator alphabet with calibrated positive pair survival has a computable length bound. Coarsening away that calibration leaves a separate exact finite-alphabet enforcement problem; a nonnegative affine endpoint selector cannot solve it on the freely parameterized private INDEPENDENT source language. No legal nonlinear/stateful selector or all-core converse was constructed. This rejects the proposed proof architecture as currently instantiated, not the possibility of some different source-faithful undecidability proof.

**Status:** failed whole-proof attempt; not a G3 impossibility result.

**Status:** failed whole-proof architecture at the hidden-algebraicity and whole-fibre completeness steps; no global effective classification established.
