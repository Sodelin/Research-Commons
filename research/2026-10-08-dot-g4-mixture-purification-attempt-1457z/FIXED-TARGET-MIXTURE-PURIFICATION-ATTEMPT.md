The new architecture tested here is **finite convex purification**:

Steps 1 and 2 can be completed. The proposed realizations in step 3 fail. This is a global construction attempt, rather than a new finite-cap return or an assertion that its unresolved purification premise holds.

## 2. Exact finite mixtures at one algebraic target

For any m>=2 let A_m be the full capped labelled-forest algebra and D=dim_R A_m. The accepted theorem in [CONVEX-ORDINARY-FINAL.md](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/562e4ac58f3d67529ab1d3b739322e0b159bf6ed/research/2026-10-04-dot-g3-convex-ordinary-2307z/CONVEX-ORDINARY-FINAL.md) states

    E_H is in ri_V conv(S_D),

where S_D consists of actual strict private INDEPENDENT words with exactly D bigons and V includes positive ordinary-only sources. Thus there are finitely many words W_1,...,W_J in S_D and weights p_j>0, with sum p_j=1, such that

    sum_j p_j K_m(W_j) = K_m(E_H).                    (1)

Caratheodory gives J<=dim(V)+1. The same word parameters supply every coordinate of (1), rather than a separate fit at each arity.

These mixture witnesses can be chosen algebraic in survival coordinates. Fix one successful finite J and the D-cell shapes. Equations (1), sum p_j=1, and strict inequalities for all weights, survivals and coins form a finite rational-coefficient semialgebraic system, because q=1/2 and the forest source map is polynomial. A nonempty rational semialgebraic set has a point over the real algebraic numbers. Alternatively, enumerate J up to the stated finite bound and use exact real-closed-field feasibility; at least one call returns a witness. This is a mathematical effective selection in the EXTERNAL-mixture relaxation, not execution of that selection or original G3 recognition.

For any finite original prefix choose m large enough for its entering-root caps. A once-used private-box response is linear in K_m. Therefore the fixed external law which chooses j once per fresh locus and then uses the complete word W_j has exactly the target response on EVERY row of that prefix. The weights and word tuples are fixed across those rows. This describes a precise enlarged stochastic model; it is not an assertion that an original source may redraw its population parameters.

## 3. Later inequivalence of every finite positive nonempty-word mixture

The inherited [factor-count theorem](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/5e01a8e727478a2f22686d31f71304126e345116/research/2026-10-01-sol61-g4-allcopy-2237z/CHAIN-COUNT-AND-STOPPING.md) proves, for each fixed strict independent word W_j with L_j>=1,

    log b_n(W_j) = -A_j n^2 + B_j n - (L_j/2) log n + O_j(1). (2)

The bounded remainder need not converge; the theorem supplies positive upper and lower bounds uniform in n for each fixed word. No uniformity over changing source parameters is needed below.

For any FINITE positive mixture define

    A_* = min_j A_j,
    B_* = max{B_j : A_j=A_*},
    L_* = min{L_j : A_j=A_*, B_j=B_*}.

All terms are positive. Terms with A_j>A_* are exponentially smaller at quadratic order; among the remainder, terms with B_j<B_* are exponentially smaller at linear order; terms with the same A_*,B_* and L_j>L_* are polynomially smaller. The finitely many dominant terms have positive fixed weights and bounded positive rescaled factors. It follows directly that

    log(sum_j p_j b_n(W_j))
       = -A_* n^2 + B_* n - (L_*/2) log n + O(1).      (3)

In (1) every L_j=D, so L_*=D>=1. An ordinary target has

    log b_n(E_H) = -(H/2)n^2 + (H/2)n.

Equality at every n would successively force A_*=H/2 and B_*=H/2, after which the strictly negative logarithmic term in (3) is impossible. Hence EVERY such finite mixture is later inequivalent to E_H. Since (1) already matches through m, a discrepancy occurs beyond the matched cap.

This discrepancy has an original observed-law witness in the enlarged mixture model: the inherited crossed-cherry completion has probability a fixed positive known factor times b_n of the private box. Linearity preserves that identity under the external mixture. It uses no new hidden routing or clock readout. The later experiment is legal for each ordinary word component and the target; what is missing is an admitted SINGLE source for the mixture.

Thus the full fixed-target/prefix/inequivalence quantifiers would all be satisfied if an appropriate exact original-source purification of these mixtures existed. No change of target and no limiting source is being hidden in this conditional conclusion.

## 4. Why a COMMON selector around a whole word is not the required source

The first proposed purification was a locus-wide selector whose two arms contain alternative complete independent words, with later pooling back to one output. Even granting a COMMON selector in an otherwise independent construction does not make this graph admissible.

**Graph check.** Put a nonempty serial word on one arm of an outer selector/rejoining gall. Choose any hybrid of the inner word and its child edge. Removing that edge does not disconnect the underlying graph: its two endpoints remain connected by the route from the inner hybrid back along its arm to the outer split, then along the other outer arm to the outer rejoining point, then back along the remaining selected-arm segment. Thus the inner hybrid child edge is not a cut edge.

This is exactly the forbidden insertion of a bigon on a nonbridge cycle edge in the original admitted-testers proof, Section 4.1. A larger binary selector tree still contains an outer bypass around every selected nonempty branch; it does not repair the issue. Avoiding the rejoin would change the two-port interface and the original root/LSA contract.

By contrast, a COMMON gall with only ordinary populations on its arms is an admitted finite ordinary-duration mixture. It does not select arbitrary independent word kernels. Its conditional arm kernels are ordinary, and its finite natural route configurations cannot be substituted for the word-valued mixture in (1).

The declared inheritance mechanism is also part of the original experiment. An undeclared change from natural independent-current-root routing to COMMON routing is already unavailable. The graph obstruction shows that granting that extra local selector alone would still not complete the construction.

## 5. Why serial choices and fixed original controls do not implement the selector

Natural choices at different hybrids are independent. Placing independent selector stages in series therefore gives the product of their averaged kernels, with the associated cross terms. It does not give a sum of whole-word products sharing one index j. This is a failure of the proposed operation identity, not a theorem that accidental endpoint coincidences are impossible.

A persistent common index across all stages would require a shared selector register with the appropriate fixed uses. The original contract preserves such a register WHEN it was actually declared; it does not create one from an external convex decomposition. Here the target bridge is private and unmarked, with no selector input. Introducing that register changes its public type. Introducing a new family of named controlled sites changes the original-ID map.

More generally, the original core proof protects each original actuator hybrid and edge occurrence. Its unbounded erased words lie on eligible UNMARKED bridge slots. Copying an existing actuator name onto arbitrarily many new cells is not the same as reusing the same physical source parameters across response rows. The accepted register-preserving compiler retains required original sites and their joint program; it supplies no unbounded fresh-use exception.

Even a hypothetical legal operation realizing some capped mixture as a private independent word would still need a direct exact source theorem. Treating that operation as available because a finite convex decomposition exists is precisely the invalid implication. The present graph and control checks reject the proposed wrappers; they do not prove that every other source realization of E_H is impossible.

## 6. Why fine serial refinement and cap-five complements do not finish purification

The accepted weak-factor estimate gives, for a fixed full cap m and a product of actual source factors with total pair hazard H and maximum factor pair loss eta,

    d_m(product, E_H) <= D_m H sqrt(eta).              (4)

Thus fine positive serial refinement is an approximation mechanism. It does not turn the expectation identity (1) into exact equality. It also cannot uniformly emulate an arbitrary fixed nonordinary component W_j by arbitrarily fine factors at the same total hazard: if its capped distance from its pair-matched ordinary kernel is e>0, (4) requires eta>=(e/(D_m H))^2. A persistent nonordinary component cannot simply be replaced by an infinitely refined source path.

For the ordinary target itself, (4) remains compatible with hypothetical exact nonempty returns. It neither supplies nor excludes them. The accepted compact-fair energy restrictions, the later rare-face restrictions, and failures of particular retuned bodies retain their stated scopes; none is promoted to an arbitrary-word prohibition here.

Actual cap-five ordinary interior supplies positive left complements at that cap by the accepted cofinality argument. At a general cap, that argument still requires an actual ordinary-centered source chart. Neither the external convex polytope in (1) nor a nearby regular source patch supplies such a chart. Even obtaining separate finite-cap complements would leave the total ordinary time unbounded unless a further uniform estimate were proved.

The inherited exact return thresholds make the remaining fixed-target obligation explicit: if R_m=(tau_m,infinity) is the set of ordinary times admitting a nonempty actual strict return at cap m, one fixed ordinary target with all-prefix rivals exists exactly when sup_m tau_m is finite. This attempt produces no bound on those ACTUAL thresholds. External-mixture attainment is not membership in R_m, since pair hazard is a multiplicative character of serial source products, not of external convex combinations.
