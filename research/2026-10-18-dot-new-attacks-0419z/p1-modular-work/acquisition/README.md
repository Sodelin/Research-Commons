## Result and scope

The [conditional modular-work test](../README.md) has a small observation budget once actual source-Hamiltonian spectral instruments are supplied. This packet examines whether existing copy-only methods supply those instruments or the same complete observation law at an improved uniform total-copy cost.

A precise restriction is established by applying O'Donnell and Wright's *Quantum Spectrum Testing*, Section 4.2, Theorem 4.2 and Lemma 2.22: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.05028 . For the admitted one-local pair `I/d` versus `(I+a Z_1)/d`, with `d=2^N` and `0<a<=tanh(beta)`, a protocol whose effective decision commutes with every global `U^(tensor T)` needs

    T >= sqrt(log(13/9)) d/a^2

to have both errors at most one third. All unknown-source copies, including program copies, count. The first source is identical and maximally mixed in both instances, so free copies of that source allow a rigorous reduction of joint adaptive two-source protocols to the same invariant measurement bound.

This is an **application of an established spectrum-testing theorem**, not a new unrestricted P1 lower bound. The pair is easy with its locality-aware `Z_1` measurement. Full Schur transforms retaining quantum representation registers, followed by operations that exploit the known qubit tensor structure, are not excluded. The mathematical relative-modular law is exact, but its coherent inputs and controlled operations are not free mixed-copy resources.
