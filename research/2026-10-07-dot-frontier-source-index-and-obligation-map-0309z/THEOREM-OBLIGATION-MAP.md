# Prioritized theorem-to-obligation map

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 7 October 2026. Reviewable source-level integration. Source code presence, hand correctness, independent acceptance, formal checking and executed algorithms are distinct throughout.

Classifications: **advance** means a potentially stronger matched theoretical conclusion, conditional until independently reviewed; **modified tactic** means an existing proof or verification method with a new source-specific bridge required; **excluded shortcut** means the proposed immediate transfer fails its hypotheses or quantifiers; **proposed broader scope** means a genuinely changed problem, not an adopted programme. The priorities rank mathematical value as well as near-term use.

## P1. Exact real two-block complexity → finite certificates and bounded design

**Source/date/version.** OpenAI family 141, *Existential-universal real sentences in the counting hierarchy*, 4 October 2026; release adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a. [Main theorem and scope](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Existential-universal-real-sentences-in-the-counting-hierarchy-October-4-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex).

**Hypotheses.** Explicit Boolean combinations of zero/strict-positive tests on division-free integer circuits; one existential real block followed by one universal real block; binary integer constants; finite dimensions and actual encoded size. Specified algebraic constants can be isolated exactly. Arbitrary computable reals, variable exponential relations and projected formulas with hidden later existential witnesses are not covered by that syntax automatically.

**Status.** Selected introduction, counting, existential, two-block and bound sections read in the preserved screening; no independent complete proof audit, listed family Lean theorem or local replay. The bound uses counting-hierarchy oracle access. The displayed level26 belongs to a pure existential subroutine, not the general two-block result.

**Predecessors.** Tarski decision method; Collins CAD; Canny's existential PSPACE theorem; Basu–Pollack–Roy real algebraic algorithms; Renegar's quantifier-complexity work. Recent primary predecessors: [Andrews–Garg–Schost,20Feb2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17904v1) for algebraic-closure feasibility/counting, and [Balaji–Shirmohammadi–Tavenas–Worrell,30 Sep 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00644v1) for Zariski closure APS. Their closures/domains differ from strict positive real-source attainment.

**Original obligations.** G3 finite invariant templates; whole-joint CG4 rejection; bounded G7/MG1/MG2 design. General G3 and G4 remain outside the proved reduction.

**Missing bridge/receiver.** G3 owner: actual all-state auxiliary updates or an explicit quantifier-free projection with honest description-size accounting. Practical/G7 owner: finite rational-affine SAME_BACKEND source/menu instance. Current JC rate/time exponentials do not qualify. Reviewer: independently accept the exact reduction and separately audit the external complexity theorem. BOUNDED-DESIGN-BRIDGE.md supplies the new reviewable design contract.

**Classification.** Advance, conditionally, in complexity classification; excluded shortcut to an ordinary polynomial-time solver, universal template completeness, unknown-word bound, or full-menu finite forcing.

## P2. Noncommutative formula hitting → ordered operator identity checking

**Source/date/version.** Family116, *One Rational Matrix Hitting Point for Noncommutative Formulas* and *Polynomial Hitting Lists for Noncommutative Rational Formulas*,24 Sep 2026, same release pin. [Polynomial theorem](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/One-Rational-Matrix-Hitting-Point-for-Noncommutative-Formulas-September-24-2026/build/introduction.tex); [Lean scope](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/lean/docs/116.md).

**Hypotheses.** n, s≥1; characteristic-zero coefficient field; rooted division-free noncommutative formula of ≤s gates including leaves, multiplication ordered. One rational matrix tuple of dimension≤2ns² hits every nonzero such formula. The manuscript's polynomial bit bound concerns generator construction; arbitrary-field evaluation is an exact oracle. Rational formulas over Q additionally require a nonempty positive-dimensional rational matrix domain and every intermediate inverse to exist; the list hits an invertible output. General shared circuits are not thereby covered.

**Status.** Selected solution interface OAI.NCHitting.universal_hitting has been source-read; its listed scope does not separately claim the manuscript's construction/dimension bounds. The rational MainStatement includes an explicit machine and unary n, s resource contract. Challenge placeholders specify targets rather than successful checks. No local dependency audit/build.

**Predecessors.** Nisan 1991 ranks/formula-to-path representation; Raz–Shpilka2005 polynomial-time WHITE-BOX identity testing; Bogdanov–Wee2005 randomized black-box testing; Forbes–Shpilka2013 quasipolynomial hitting; Arvind–Chatterjee–Mukhopadhyay2022/2026 rational black-box testing. Chen iterated integrals, hyperlogarithm independence and linear-system multiplicity are further cited foundations. New contribution must be located in the black-box representation/resource regime, not claimed as first efficient identity testing.

**Original obligations/receiver.** Fixed source compiler/chronological matrix identities, G2 finite operator interface or a bounded practical verification task. Receiver must give a genuine free formula or admitted path representation and size. A nonzero arbitrary-matrix value is not a legal biological discrepancy; an identity can hold on the constrained physical family without being a free identity. No G3/G4 word bound or source realization follows.

**Classification.** Modified tactic; theoretical advance within its advertised black-box model remains subject to proof review. No automatic solver replacement.

## P4. BIO-1 prior audit → preserve the real statistical and sparse-query gap

**Source/date/version.** Dai–Molloy, WABI2026.1,27 Aug 2026, [official paper](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.1). Practical owner inspected Theorems2–3, Corollary1, Section5; no new independent paper proof audit is claimed here.

**Hypotheses/conclusion.** For a positive displayed-vs-nondisplayed concordance-factor gap π, their bound k≥(2/π²)log(6|Q4|/epsilon) controls pivot-block and circular-order errors given the stated hybrid/pivot/tree-of-blobs inputs and independent true gene trees. Dependence between quartets from the same gene is not assumed away: the union bound does not require quartet independence. Fast mode has O(n log n) quarnet queries.

**Original obligation.** Section5's sparse/averaged four-taxon access for NANUQ+ and a faithful biological-data-to-oracle guarantee. The accepted Commons NANUQ53 work concerns actual displayed-resolution support/means and geometry components; it is not an NMSC weight or raw-DNA statistical bridge.

**Missing bridge/receiver.** Query coverage for the exact structural input; inferred tree-of-blobs/order errors; estimated gene-tree error and missingness; lawful raw-sequence sampling assumptions. Practical owner retains these gates. No new BIO project or experiment was started.

**Classification.** Excluded novelty claim for generic Hoeffding; modified tactic only for an exact unmet source admission. Family175/186 random-graph thresholds cannot substitute for a proved coverage hypergraph with matching symmetry/measure assumptions.

## P5. E8/RNA/PRISM → exact class masses and selection-valid confidence

Existing accepted generic confidence components are reused rather than counted as new.

**Missing bridge/receiver.** Original PRISM versus supplied-engine admission, grammar/scaffold/physical weights, RNG and certified numerics, adaptive discovery and ties/abstention/missing mass. No separate RNA implementation is adopted here; no full E8 execution or validity is inferred. Families114/115 sample unrelated combinatorial carriers;119 assumes uniform independent binary inputs;139 assumes strongly log-concave smooth potentials. None is an automatic RNA sampler/confidence theorem.

**Classification.** Modified classical method plus excluded carrier substitutions. No E8-lattice interpretation.
