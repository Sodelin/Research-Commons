## 4. Joint t-cubed split and nonlinear response

For odds z define

    H(z,r)_l=log(1+z)-log(1+z r^l).

For each slot use correction coordinates v_s=(alpha_s,beta_s,zeta_s,omega_s,xi_s) and set

    a_s(t)=a_s+t^3 alpha_s,
    b_s(t)=b_s-t+t^3 beta_s,
    r_s(t)=r_s+t^3 zeta_s,
    w_s(t)=t^2(1/2+t omega_s),
    q_s(t)=r_s^2+t xi_s.

Let u(t)=u0+t^3 eta, and put

    h_s(t,v)=a_s(t)Lambda+b_s(t)D(r_s(t))
               +H(t,r_s)+H(w_s(t),q_s(t)),
    P(t,v)=F(u(t),exp[-h_1(t,v)],...,exp[-h_q(t,v)]).

The unmodified first new factor uses node r_s, as in the inherited split. The two new factors are strict when t>0 is sufficiently small. Every expansion is joint in the bounded correction variables.

The inherited cancellation gives

    h_s(t,v)-h_s(0)
      =t^3[alpha_s Lambda+beta_s D(r_s)+b_s zeta_s D'(r_s)
             +omega_s D(r_s^2)+(xi_s/2)D'(r_s^2)
             +D(r_s^3)/3]+O(t^4).

Terms of orders t and t^2 cancel identically. Since every slot change and u-change start at order t^3, polynomial nonlinearity and cross-slot products in F contribute no extra term at order t^3; their quadratic contribution begins at order t^6. Thus

    G(t,v)=(P(t,v)-y)/t^3

extends analytically to t=0, with

    G(0,v)=D_uF eta
       -sum_s D_(m_s)F diag(m_s)
          [alpha_s Lambda+beta_s D(r_s)+b_s zeta_s D'(r_s)
             +omega_s D(r_s^2)+(xi_s/2)D'(r_s^2)
             +D(r_s^3)/3].                              (L)

All derivatives in (L) are at the acquired centre. By (J), its linear part is onto. Set all unselected correction coordinates to zero and solve the square selected system G(0,v0)=0. This produces a finite computable v0 and an invertible selected Jacobian J0. No unknown realizer is used.

This is the precise change from the prior affine projection theorem: one actual nonlinear F and the feasible core directions are included before solving the joint equations. It is not independent application of an affine theorem to each slot or each row.
