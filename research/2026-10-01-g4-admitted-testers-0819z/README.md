## Main results

### 1. Every legal completion at a declared finite copy cap

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md), Theorem 5, constructs a finite determining family of **actual positive admitted biological completions** for the declared finite source/box type, control menu and topology observation contract. Agreement on that family is equivalent to agreement in every legal completion at the cap.

Let F_k be the labelled rooted binary forest set, J_m=sum_(k=0)^m |F_k|, and h the number of protected edge/hybrid IDs and supplied box incidences. Set

    R=2n-2+2h,  E=8n-8+6h.

The determining completions need no more than

    R + E(d-1) reticulations,

where d=J_m for one mechanism or d=2J_m for a paired common/independent comparison with the SAME exterior parameters. This is a conservative existence/algorithmic witness bound, not an optimal query or intervention-cost claim.

The proof derives the legal language from the root-retained core and serial bigons on eligible BRIDGES. It certifies closure under the complete polynomial generator family. Signed span coefficients are proof operations, not biological probabilities or a G3 positive-source realization theorem.

### 2. An adaptive all-cap result for unknown-size common chains

[ALL-CAP.md](ALL-CAP.md), Theorem D2, gives a terminating adaptive exact procedure for a promised finite positive common-inheritance two-port chain whose size is unknown. The input is exact algebraic rooted A-clade response access. Successive sparse duration moments eventually expose the finite atomic survival law; a nonnegative-polynomial certificate detects when it is uniquely determined. If the unknown law has s atoms, a certificate exists by interface cap 2s+1, without requiring s in advance.

A finite authorized menu of common-chain marginal program rows can be handled the same way. This does not reconstruct arbitrary hidden networks, validate arbitrary multirow source realizability, or authorize unobserved joint counterfactual responses. The general quantified certificate finder is proved to terminate but has not been implemented in this packet. One- and two-atom certificates were executed exactly.

### 3. Why one taxon-only cutoff cannot finish the whole all-copy language

[ALL-CAP.md](ALL-CAP.md), Theorems A and B, constructs exact finite-cap collisions separately for common and independent inheritance. They refute a fixed taxon-only copy cutoff, not every source-dependent adaptive algorithm. The common-chain adaptive theorem above demonstrates why that distinction matters.
