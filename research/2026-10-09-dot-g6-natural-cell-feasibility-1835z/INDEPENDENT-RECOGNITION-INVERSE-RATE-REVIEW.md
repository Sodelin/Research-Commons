## Proof and source checks

1. **One physical rate bank.** Introducing one positive u_e=1/rho_e per original physical population gives a bijection of real feasible banks. A crossing cut or another menu row reuses that variable; it does not introduce an independent epoch rate. Distinct edges remain free, including parallel arms sharing node ages. A single ancestral rate is reused in all its finite post-root exposures. The final infinite completion is not replaced by a finite hazard constraint.

2. **Exact affine grammar.** Multiplication by positive u_e sends every finite hazard lower/upper bound to an affine inequality with rational coefficients. Strictness and equality are preserved. A saturated interval contributes only its lower constraint. Missing bounds add nothing. Shared gamma bounds are already affine. In a complete weak-order chart no unresolved min/max is being disguised as an affine endpoint.

3. **Strict original source admission.** Parent-child ages remain strictly ordered independently of the hazard rows. A tied epoch may have duration zero without allowing a physical original edge to have zero duration. Zero hazard in such an epoch is consistent; a positive lower hazard is rejected. Positivity of every u_e gives a finite strictly positive recovered rate. Unused rates may be chosen freely positive. The graph remains the supplied original graph, with its original root, IDs and target.

4. **Rational witnesses.** Every row is rational affine, so the strict Fourier–Motzkin proof is sufficient: pair each lower with each upper, retaining strictness when either side is strict. At a projected solution, finite extrema give either a nonempty open interval, a weak singleton, a one-sided interval or the whole line. The stated midpoint/extreme-plus-or-minus-one choices preserve every strict row. Recursive rational back-substitution yields rational ages, inverse rates and gammas. Reciprocal positive rationals remain rational. No positive margin needs to be supplied in advance.

5. **Rate ties and fixed rates.** Equality of two physical rates translates to equality of their inverse rates. Fixed positive rational rates and rational proportionality constraints are also affine after reciprocal substitution, if explicitly supplied. They are not required by the original free-rate natural grammar and are not all implemented as fields of the present Chart class. Arbitrary nonlinear ties are outside the theorem. A fixed irrational algebraic rate would require an algebraic-coefficient/output contract and would defeat the claim of an entirely rational rate witness; the manuscript correctly does not include that case.

6. **Effectivity and finite-prefix scope.** The input chart and finite profile may have any finite size. Elimination terminates mathematically but has no claimed practical complexity bound. Complete finite graph/order/grid enumeration belongs to the surrounding G6 construction; a single chart failure does not reject other charts. Resource ceilings and malformed inputs are not mathematical NO answers.

7. **Original source witness versus proxy.** A returned bank satisfies all original multiplicative hazard constraints simultaneously. This is the actual source-cell witness needed by G6. It is not the independently rounded hazard proxy, and its existence does not by itself prove the source/proxy probability error budget or either global Hausdorff direction.
