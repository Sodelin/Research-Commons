Every rational hazard bound on rho_e times an original age difference becomes a rational affine bound on that age difference and u_e. One bank is shared across every epoch and row; one ancestral rate is shared across all finite ancestral exposures. Strict edge ages, tied unrelated ages/cuts, interior inheritance, singleton and saturated cells are retained exactly.

It removes general real-algebraic feasibility from this particular natural-cell gate. The reciprocal substitution and elimination method are classical; no historical novelty claim is made.

## Precise boundaries

The Lean substitution theorem permits arbitrary index types I and C, so its logical equivalence also covers infinite families. The finite terminating rational algorithm requires **finite supplied** chart and row lists. Formal Fourier–Motzkin termination/completeness and formal rational-witness extraction are not yet proved in this Lean module.

Graph admission, complete weak-order enumeration and correct active-exposure mapping are premises of the supplied chart. The Python prototype checks the compiled affine constraints and returned multiplicative hazards; it does not establish those graph/chronology premises. A failed chart does not reject other charts.

There are no arbitrary nonlinear parameter restrictions in the theorem. Fixed irrational rates, optional general marked controls or exact terminal probability equations need different coefficient/semantic contracts. Source/proxy error bounds, positive contextual source replacement, a complete bounded graph/chart producer, both global Hausdorff directions and full G6 statistical assembly remain separate obligations. No exact G3 recognizer or unknown-word-length bound follows.
