Its premise is the existence of five positive algebraic b_n of multiplicative rank five with

    log b_n = w[n−(1+r+...+r^(n−1))],
    n in {3,6,10,15,21}, w>0, 0<r<1.

No such example is known in the preserved work. The accepted arithmetic dichotomy forces r transcendental IF the premise holds. The barrier theorem then constructs an explicit algebraic sequence which is eventually composed of actual-word closure points that are negative for every finite word and admit no semialgebraic inductive separator, even with real coefficients. Its first valid sequence index was not made effective in that statement.

The separator contract is all positive ordinary initializations and every strict physical COMMON append, exactly the single-slot contract used by the audit. The audit's map s^λ(1−p+p q^λ) is the normalized positive physical append: arbitrary positive arms are sorted and their common maximum and connector absorbed into s; equal arms give an ordinary update. Ordinary updates can either be included explicitly or obtained by a strict equal-arm physical cell. To avoid a hidden mismatch, for this comparison take the invariant transition menu to include x_λ←s^λ x_λ as well as the strict q<1 updates. This adds one polynomial update family and changes none of the audit's RCF or reachability arguments, since ordinary factors can already be absorbed into the baseline.

An invariant defined on all positive coordinates restricts to the provider's external moment carrier. This carrier is append-invariant because multiplying its survival variable by an independent positive Bernoulli survival and an ordinary scale gives another such survival law. Thus a separator satisfying the enlarged transition menu would contradict the accepted barrier at an eventual target.

Consequently, universal SC even for single-slot algebraic singleton targets, with the complete physical append menu, would imply that the displayed normalized rank-five branch does not exist. This is a one-way necessary condition on this certificate program. It is neither a proof that SC fails nor evidence that arbitrary G3 recognition is equivalent to, or as hard as, a transcendence conjecture. A different NO-certificate family could avoid this implication. The previous small-loss and forced-boundary providers retain their established attribution.

The explicit ordinary update is a useful exact formulation correction for the certificate specification: q was strictly below one in the audit, and an arbitrary nonclosed invariant need not inherit the q=1 case merely by taking a limit. No such continuity inference is used here. Finite reachability and the conditional source-decision program are unchanged.
