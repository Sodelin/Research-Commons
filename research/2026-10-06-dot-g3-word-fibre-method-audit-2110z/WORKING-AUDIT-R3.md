## 1. The problem kept fixed

The accepted cut-cover theorem is a sufficient first branch: strong directed calibration cover removes all eligible unmarked words and gives the actual total-hybrid bound 2n−2+2h. Its programmed extension requires the same joint program in the paired calibration rows. When cover is incomplete, its partial elimination leaves uncovered words in the SAME coupled fibre.

For the residual COMMON interfaces which the existing compiler represents by fresh independent word slots, write a single core's question as

    Does there exist (θ,x_1,...,x_e) in T_y with every x_j in S_M?

Here y is the complete supplied algebraic observation vector, θ retains every static core/marked/register parameter and constraint, and T_y is the complete polynomial or semialgebraic joint fibre over the real algebraic numbers. All rows, shared programs and parameters occur together. The actual word image is

    S_M = { (A^λ ∏_(i=1)^N [1−p_i+p_i q_i^λ])_λ :
             N is a finite nonnegative integer; 0<A,p_i,q_i<1 },
    λ=binom(j,2), j=2,...,M.

Constant spectral coordinate x_0=1 is understood. The finite baseline can be allocated among positive connectors/arms, as in the accepted source normalization. Equal-arm unmarked factors are absorbed into A. This does not change protected original IDs.

This is a genuine unresolved part of the original master, already present for one slot and the cap-seven singleton embedded by the accepted calibrated eight-row COMMON reduction. It is not asserted to encode arbitrary INDEPENDENT inheritance, a new microscopic parameter tie, or an interface for which the compiler has not supplied this product constraint. Such cases retain their original source obligations. Independent fits to projections of T_y do not answer the displayed question.

For any prescribed finite word-length tuple, substitution yields an ordinary finite RCF sentence. Thus actual YES has an effective search: enumerate lengths and solve each strict sentence. If a finite real witness exists, RCF transfer/sampling supplies an algebraic witness for that same finite architecture. No algebraicity of arbitrary hidden closure kernels follows.

## 2. What transfers exactly: whole-fibre inductive certificates

This is the classical inductive-invariant method, specialized to the existing compiler; it is not a claimed new general synthesis theorem. Let z=(θ,x_1,...,x_e), and let Θ be the semialgebraic domain of legal static parameters. Take C=Θ×(0,1)^(e(M−1)); every joint condition involving word coordinates remains in T_y, rather than being silently imposed as a transition guard. Use an initialization relation I consisting of all θ in Θ and all ordinary word tuples x_(j,λ)=A_j^λ with 0<A_j<1. Quantifying A_j is an RCF definition of I. All coefficients and monomial exponents are finite algebraic/integer input data.

For each slot j, allow the polynomial update, with 0<s,p,q<1,

    U_j(z;s,p,q): x_(j,λ) ← s^λ(1−p+p q^λ)x_(j,λ),

leaving θ and every other slot fixed. Also include the ordinary/equal-arm update V_j(z;s): x_(j,λ) ← s^λ x_(j,λ), with 0<s<1. Both families preserve C. This explicit update matters for nonclosed invariants: its invariance cannot be inferred by taking q→1. The extra s is intentional: this is a positive physical cell, not an unbudgeted zero-length arm or a fractional source power. Conversely, any displayed S_M representation with N factors is reachable: split A into N+1 positive factors, assign one to initialization and one to each s. Hence finite reachability from I is exactly the compiled product-word state set, with θ retained. The claim applies only where the cited compiler already licenses these independent fresh slots.

Choose any finite Boolean formula P_c(z) built from polynomial equalities/inequalities, with a finite vector c of unspecified coefficients. Its certificate conditions are

    I ⊆ P_c;
    for every j, z in C, and 0<s,p,q<1:
        P_c(z) implies P_c(U_j(z;s,p,q));
    for every j, z in C, and 0<s<1:
        P_c(z) implies P_c(V_j(z;s));
    P_c ∩ C ∩ T_y is empty.

We may replace P_c by P_c∩C. Invariance is required at EVERY auxiliary point of the invariant, not just at already realized sources. This is the same essential domain correction in the accepted calibrated separator transfer. The formula may couple all θ and all x_j; it need not factor by slot.

**Finite-template fact.** Existence of coefficients c satisfying these conditions is an effectively decidable RCF sentence. All update maps, carrier, initialization and final joint equations have algebraic coefficients. The quantifiers over c, states and source parameters are finite. If arbitrary real coefficients work for a chosen formula shape/degree, real-closed-field transfer gives algebraic coefficients for that same template, and exact sampling can recover them. This is a mathematical algorithm, not an execution report.

Every returned invariant is a sound NO certificate for that entire core fibre: induction contains every finite word tuple, while exclusion rules out every target tuple. Enumerating all finite Boolean polynomial templates is therefore relatively complete for the class of semialgebraic inductive certificates. For a finite core catalogue, a certificate is needed for every surviving core; successful independent slot separators are not a substitute for joint exclusion.

**Missing completeness statement (SC).** Every negative algebraic compiled joint fibre admits at least one such finite semialgebraic invariant.

SC has not been proved. If it were proved for all relevant cores/interfaces, dovetailing certificate search with finite-source YES search would give a terminating recognizer on that class. A counterexample to SC would reject this certificate class, not prove that G3 is undecidable. No bound on template size is required for this conditional dovetailing; conversely, checking every fixed template does not prove SC.

Closed invariants cannot suffice. The accepted positive algebraic small-loss Poisson NO targets lie in the closure of actual words. Any closed set containing all those words contains such a target. Nonclosed semialgebraic invariants are therefore essential even for the existing singleton examples. The accepted unbounded degree obstruction and fixed rational-input classifier obstruction do not exclude an input-dependent sequence of increasingly complicated templates.

A stronger prior conditional warning applies: the accepted rank-five barrier ffd1e14a593590dae08f0c59576522e58aad69a0e9245e06af362ab0128b8c55, review 5b295a77cf9ddc4ef6d2f94bdaa92d15ae7d94534a2d1a2ad0bf2837b6143c5f, proves that universal semialgebraic separator completeness for algebraic singleton negatives would exclude the unresolved normalized rank-five branch. Our full append menu matches its physical contract, including equal-arm cells. An invariant on C restricts to its append-invariant external moment carrier. No rank-five example is supplied; this is neither an established counterexample to SC nor a hardness claim about other recognition methods. See the separately preserved CONDITIONAL-COMPLETENESS-CAUTION.md for the exact premise and source link.

## 3. Primary-source applicability map

### A. Fixed linear orbits and invariant synthesis

Fijalkow, Ohlmann, Ouaknine, Pouly and Worrell, *Complete Semialgebraic Invariant Synthesis for the Kannan–Lipton Orbit Problem*, Definition in Section 2 and Theorem 1, decide invariant existence for one fixed rational matrix and two points (with the stated algebraic extension). Closed invariant existence is equivalent to exclusion from that orbit's closure; arbitrary nonreachability need not have a semialgebraic invariant. Their theorem does not cover a new continuously varying cell at each step or a joint target fibre. The earlier Colón–Sankaranarayanan–Sipma constraint approach supplies classical attribution for coefficient-based invariant synthesis; its CAV 2003 completeness is for linear invariant templates of linear transition systems. Section 2 above uses general RCF directly, not an unsupported extension of either completeness theorem.

Primary texts: [Fijalkow et al., Sections 2–3](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/complete-semialgebraic-invariants19.pdf); [Colón et al., CAV 2003](https://theory.stanford.edu/~sipma/papers/cav03.pdf).

Almagor, Ouaknine and Worrell's *The Semialgebraic Orbit Problem* allows semialgebraic source and target sets but still one fixed rational matrix; its main decision result is dimension at most three. Thus allowing a target set in that paper does not discharge our varying-generator, generally higher-dimensional coupled problem. This is a hypothesis mismatch, not a hardness reduction.

Primary text: [STACS 2019, abstract and Section 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.11023).

### B. Commuting exponentials and effective linear logarithms

Ouaknine, Pouly, Sousa-Pinto and Worrell's Definition 1.1/Theorem 4.4 use a finite fixed list of algebraic generating matrices and arbitrary nonnegative continuous times. Their Theorem 2.7/Proposition 2.8 decide signs of algebraic-coefficient linear forms in logarithms of algebraic numbers. These are genuine effective primitives at their stated syntax. Bernoulli cells form a variable continuous alphabet and require finite integer multiplicities; commutativity alone supplies neither fixed exponential modes nor algebraic-linear logarithmic equations.

Primary text: [JACM paper, Sections 1, 2.2 and 4](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/matrix-exponential17.pdf).

The already accepted nondivisibility audit gives a stronger direct obstruction to identifying the actual word semigroup with unrestricted commuting exponential dynamics: an attained algebraic kernel has a unique positive algebraic integer root which is not attained. A homomorphic image of a divisible commuting exponential semigroup is divisible. This rules out that source-image-preserving replacement, not arbitrary constrained or nonhomomorphic reductions.

All our logs are real logs of strictly positive quantities. For positive algebraic b_i, integer multiplicative relations are exactly integer linear relations among log b_i, without a complex branch term. The effective relation lattice is supplied, for example, by **Tao Zheng and Bican Xia**, *An Effective Framework for Constructing Exponent Lattice Basis of Nonzero Algebraic Numbers*, Sections 3–4. Both authors must be credited; one frozen early draft omitted Xia.

Primary algorithm: [Zheng and Xia, 2019 version](https://arxiv.org/html/1808.02712v3).

The accepted pure cap-seven arithmetic dichotomy leaves either rational residue/log-rank one, or transcendental residue/log-rank five. In the latter, coefficients involving the unknown residue are not algebraic; Baker cannot be applied to them. Unknown retained factors are an additional obstruction to treating all quotient coordinates as algebraic. The input-only algebraic-residue critical census is already effective at its exact stated promise. Neither its empty list nor a surviving entry is a general source-membership answer. Schanuel remains an unproved possible route, with no reduction establishing that G3 has conjectural hardness.

### C. Bounded Pfaffian/exponential equations

Gabrielov–Vorobjov's bounded-cube cylindrical-decomposition algorithm explicitly assumes a Pfaffian feasibility oracle (computational model, printed page 4). Vorobjov's cited unconditional special case has polynomial expressions in one common exp(h(x)), with a single polynomial h. The simultaneous residual functions exp[−u(1−r^λ)] have distinct polynomial exponents in shared variables r,u; no conversion to that special syntax has been established. Replacing them by independent exponential variables loses a required relation.

Primary texts: [Gabrielov–Vorobjov](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~gabriea/GabVor2.pdf); [Vorobjov 1992, publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0747717108800880).

Binyamini–Vorobjov provide effective geometric format/degree bounds for restricted sub-Pfaffian sets. This audit has found no exact feasibility primitive in that result which eliminates the preceding oracle for the present equations. A finite geometric bound, compact residue interval or retained-count bound is not by itself a zero decision.

Primary record: [Binyamini–Vorobjov, arXiv:2004.06411](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.06411). The earlier local Pfaffian audit remains the detailed source record.

Even a supplied exact exponential-feasibility oracle would only decide the fixed finite presentation encoded in its input. To turn this into G3 recognition one would still need a complete finite description of every remaining negative joint fibre and a valid exclusion of ALL alternative actual words. A paired-critical presentation is not a NO certificate: the accepted large-intensity and two-copy saddle theorems give actual YES points with such presentations.

### D. Delta-complete numerical decision

Gao, Avigad and Clarke, *Delta-Complete Decision Procedures for Satisfiability over the Reals*, Definitions 3.1–3.2 and Theorem 3.1, provide a genuine algorithm for finite bounded existential (Σ1) formulas in Type-2-computable functions: it returns either exact unsatisfiability or satisfiability after a chosen positive tolerance weakening. Where these answers overlap, either is permitted. Exponentials meet its computability requirement on compact boxes. Thus this can certify individual bounded residual exclusions, or help locate candidates; a tolerance-satisfiable answer is not exact existence.

Primary text: [IJCAR 2012 paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1204.3513).

Letting the tolerance decrease does not make this a terminating exact membership test at closure-only NO points. Actual finite words approximate those targets arbitrarily closely, with their lengths unbounded; there is no positive distance certificate for the union. At a fixed finite box, robust unsatisfiability can be detected, but neither that fixed presentation nor any fixed source length is the original union. No dReal or other delta solver was run here.

## 4. Strongest next obligation

The most direct remaining certificate question is SC for the full joint polynomial fibre and exact physical append dynamics in Section 2. The primary sources just checked supply template verification and several restricted decision primitives; none supplies its universal existence premise. A useful next proof must either establish SC (or another effective, sound and complete NO family) for the uncovered source-faithful fibres, or exhibit a negative algebraic fibre with no certificate in the proposed class. More sampled critical points cannot settle this quantifier.

The alternative of an input-computable actual-source witness bound is equivalent in decision strength here, not an already weaker solved task. Precisely, for an effectively enumerated finite-architecture class with decidable bounded tests, a total computable B(y) guaranteeing a witness of size at most B(y) whenever y is YES gives a recognizer by bounded search. Conversely, a recognizer yields such a total bound: output zero on NO; on YES run the terminating witness enumeration and output its size. This elementary equivalence asserts no complexity bound or practical implementation.

Therefore the audit's outcome is a **failure map with a precise conditional certificate program**, not a new completion theorem. It distinguishes (i) finite exact testing, which is available, (ii) analytic equality testing on a proposed critical stratum, partly available, and (iii) source-complete whole-fibre rejection, still missing. The accepted cut-cover sufficient branch stays before this unresolved search. COMMON-only statements do not reject mechanism-unspecified or INDEPENDENT rivals, and the rare-parent counterexample still prevents automatic controlled-closure transfer.
