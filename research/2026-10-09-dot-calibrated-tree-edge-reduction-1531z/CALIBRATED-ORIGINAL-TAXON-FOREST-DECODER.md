# Finite original-taxon decoding of calibrated exclusive forest kernels

Status: HAND candidate for independent review. General G4 remains OPEN.

## 1. Exact conditional theorem

Work in the original finite positive rooted-LSA, binary, outer-labelled planar, cut-child source class, with interior natural coins and the declared natural BOTH topology menu of ONE physical graph/parameter tuple. Retain the ordinary unbounded ancestral completion. No controls, exported state, hidden observations or new taxa are introduced.

Assume the symmetric COMMON directed-path calibration (J) of the accepted paired ordinary-tree certificate holds for all ordered original taxon pairs:

    18 d_(i|j) = [3 c_(i|j)]^3.

Here c is the original three-label crossed-cherry probability and d the original four-label comb probability in COMMON mode. For any distinct original taxa i,j, the accepted source theorem then gives one fixed physical meeting vertex v across all route choices and ACTUAL disjoint exclusive ancestry words K_i,K_j below v, each containing ordinary paths and fully traversed equal-arm original cells. Their parameters and the upper source remain coupled in the same graph.

**Conditional decoder theorem.** For each finite m, finitely many original natural INDEPENDENT topology laws, with at most m+1 selected i/j labels and all other original taxa optionally retained once, determine the complete private forest kernels of K_i and K_j through m entering roots. They also determine the completed upper source law on each r<=m+1 current roots at v. The decoder uses only exact finite arithmetic and divisions by positive source probabilities.

For m=1 the kernel is trivial; for m=2 use the three-label pair laws. For m>=2 the total original-copy allocation is at most n+m−1. Taking all positive i/j copy allocations of total at most m+1 is one finite, nonoptimized legal menu.

This is conditional SOURCE-FAITHFUL tomography, not a universal private-slot oracle for arbitrary uncalibrated graphs. It does not prove full finite forcing or add COMMON to a menu that lacks it.

## 2. Source factorization needed by the decoder

Let b_r^i and b_r^j be the no-merger probabilities along the two exclusive words. They are positive. Below v, an i root cannot meet a j root: every possible individual route pair first meets at v. The two selected populations therefore use disjoint coalescent populations and natural root-choice drivers until that vertex. Under the natural INDEPENDENT model their no-merger events are independent, giving joint probability b_a^i b_b^j for a i roots and b j roots.

This independence is conditional on the ONE fixed physical tuple, not on independently fitted kernels. The inherited cut-child/symmetric-meeting result puts shared hybrids above the meeting. There is no reused exterior random register. After no pre-meeting merger, all a+b singleton selected roots arrive at v, and the upper continuation is independent of the earlier drivers and permutation-invariant in the current roots.

Denote its probability of a specified rooted binary labelled tree T on R roots by Q_R(T). All labels have the same natural routing rule. Q_R is invariant under label permutations, so its value depends only on the unlabelled rooted shape. Every Q_R(T) is strictly positive: the finite upper source permits no merger with positive probability, and ordinary ancestral completion then realizes T with positive probability.

These statements reuse the actual ancestry/meeting theorem in [P]; they would fail for a general exported-register or origin-dependent routing interface and are not asserted there.

## 3. Mixed-colour trees annihilate every earlier merger

Colour selected i labels red and selected j labels blue. If a final rooted tree T has no monochromatic clade containing two or more labels, ANY pre-meeting merger is incompatible with T: it would create an opaque monochromatic clade, preserved by later grafting. Conversely, on no pre-meeting merger the upper law is exactly Q_R. Therefore

    Pr_original(T) = b_a^i b_b^j Q_(a+b)(T)          (1)

for every such T, where a,b are its red and blue leaf counts.

A rooted comb whose deepest cherry has one red and one blue leaf has this property, regardless of the colours of its remaining leaves. Every nontrivial clade of that comb contains the deepest mixed cherry.

More generally EVERY rooted binary tree shape on R>=2 leaves admits a balanced two-colouring with this property: its leaf cherries are disjoint pairs. Colour the two leaves of each cherry differently, and distribute the remaining leaves to obtain colour counts ceil(R/2), floor(R/2). Every nontrivial clade contains a leaf cherry, hence both colours. This elementary construction uses a chosen observed labelled topology; it does not control biological routing.

## 4. Recursive recovery of all exclusive no-merger coordinates

At one root b_1^i=b_1^j=1. At two roots, the inherited original crossed-cherry identity gives

    b_2^i=3u_(i|j),   b_2^j=3u_(j|i).

It uses the universal first-pair probability 1/3 for three exchangeable roots; no universal four-root comb constant is assumed.

Suppose all b_r^i,b_r^j are known for r<n, with n>=3. Put R=n+1 and choose a balanced mixed comb on a=ceil(R/2), b=floor(R/2) labels. Both a,b<=n−1. Equation (1) therefore recovers its upper labelled-comb probability

    k_R = Pr_original(balanced mixed comb)/(b_a^i b_b^j).

Upper exchangeability makes the same k_R apply to the comb with n i labels and one j label, whose deepest cherry is mixed. Hence

    b_n^i=Pr_original(n-i/one-j mixed comb)/k_R.

Reverse the colours to obtain b_n^j, using the SAME physical upper law. All denominators are positive. This inductively determines b_r^i,b_r^j through any requested finite m.

At n=3 the balanced mixed comb is simply (((i1,j1),i2),j2). This is the shortest direct repair of the four-root observer obstruction. Its probability divided by b_2^i b_2^j gives the otherwise unknown k_4. No assumption that k_4=1/18 is made.

## 5. Recover the complete upper completion laws

For each R<=m+1 and each unlabelled rooted binary shape, choose the balanced mixed colouring from Section 3. Formula (1) recovers Q_R for that labelled tree from known b_a^i,b_b^j. For R>=4 these indices are at most m and in fact at most m−1 when R=m+1 and m>=3. For R=2 the sole tree has probability one; for R=3 each labelled tree has probability 1/3 by exchangeability.

Relabelling gives every entry of Q_R. This is the upper COMPLETED topology law on R current roots, not direct access to a retained upper forest state. The distinction is sufficient for the next step: opacity says that the same law grafts arbitrary prebuilt rooted subtrees at its labelled roots.

## 6. Triangular inversion recovers the whole exclusive forest kernel

Fix k<=m. Observe the natural topology law on k i labels and one j label. The single j root has no selected pre-meeting merger. Let p(F) be the unknown output probability of each rooted labelled forest F on the k i labels at v.

For each F, order its r component trees by their least leaf label and form T_F by attaching them successively to a comb spine starting at j. These are distinct observed trees: the branches off the j-to-root spine recover F.

If an entering forest F' contributes to T_F, each component of F' must be a clade of T_F avoiding j. It must therefore lie inside a component of F. Thus F' refines F. If F' and F have the same number of components, F'=F including its internal topology. Ordering by decreasing component count makes the finite system

    Pr_original(T_F) = sum_F' p(F') A(F,F')

triangular. For F' compatible with T_F, collapse its components in T_F; the remaining labelled root tree has probability Q_(r'+1) of Section 5. This is exactly A(F,F'), by opaque grafting. Incompatible entries are zero. The diagonal is k_(r+1)>0, the upper probability of the specified comb on the r component roots and j.

All coefficients are thus known OBSERVED upper probabilities, even when the upper source has hybrids. Finite back-substitution determines every p(F). Repeating for k<=m recovers the complete exclusive kernels from singleton inputs. The original opacity/projectivity theorem extends these to the full capped action on prior opaque subtrees. Reversing i,j recovers the other exclusive kernel.

This corrects the earlier tempting restriction to ordinary upper completion: ordinary completion makes these matrix coefficients rational, but known positive exchangeable Q values suffice. No stochastic inverse is inserted as a biological source; inversion is only arithmetic on a finite system of observed probabilities.

## 7. Consequences and exact remaining gate

Any source-valid finite guard on an actual calibrated private exclusive word can now be evaluated from original topology laws on the same whole graph. In particular the reviewed nonlinear one-fair-cell guard needs only b_2,b_3 and can be applied even when BOTH exclusive sides have hybrids. This does not by itself localize all hybrids or prove that an arbitrary target has such a guard.

The original full positive problem still includes uncalibrated nonordinary COMMON targets, INDEPENDENT-only menus, general registers/controls and private words with several arbitrary cells. The decoder neither supplies missing COMMON identities nor turns finite-cap equality into private all-copy equivalence. It is a lawful conditional observation adapter, not full G4 closure.
