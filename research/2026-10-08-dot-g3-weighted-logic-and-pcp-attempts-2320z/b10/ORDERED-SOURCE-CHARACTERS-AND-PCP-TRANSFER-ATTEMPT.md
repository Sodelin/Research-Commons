# Whole G3 attempt B10: paired-string computation and exact source characters

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 8 October 2026. Frozen hand candidate for independent challenge. The original general G3 recognition problem remains open. This attempt tests a complete negative reduction; it does not infer undecidability from a generic matrix theorem.

## 1. Complete proposed reduction and its actual obligations

Given a Post correspondence instance P, the proposed route would compute one finite exact rational/effectively algebraic original G3 input y(P). A solution word would produce an actual finite strict source satisfying every original row through one graph and shared physical tuple. Conversely, EVERY admitted source satisfying y(P), across all retained cores, fresh parameters and permitted words, would decode to a nonempty solution of P. The declared COMMON, INDEPENDENT or BOTH interface would have to be preserved exactly.

The strongest literal matrix route examined here has four steps:

1. Encode the two strings of each tile in a finite rational affine/polynomial matrix action, with a scalar test recognizing their concatenated equality.
2. Realize that action by a fixed family of actual private source macroletters, through one common linear invariant subquotient of the finite current-root carrier. Permit a common positive scalar normalization for each tile.
3. Realize the acceptance test as finitely many original exact observations, including any physical slack needed to turn a cutpoint comparison into an equality.
4. Prove an all-core converse enforcing the intended unbounded tile grammar, rather than merely constructing sources from chosen words.

The primary digit construction supplies Step 1. Section 4 shows that Step 2 is impossible for its mixed-length paired characters. The usual positive stochastic normalization does not repair this mismatch. However, tensor/multislot representations escape this particular ordering obstruction, as the explicit ACTUAL-source example in Section 5 demonstrates. Their source-valid readout, grammar enforcement and all-core converse remain unproved. Thus neither the literal route nor its proposed enriched repair gives a complete G3 reduction.

This is a different check from the earlier Jordan-block argument. The relevant paired affine contractions may be individually diagonalizable. The obstruction below is a COMMON ordering of characters across a whole alphabet, and does not assume its letters commute.

## 2. Inherited source filtration and a common-subquotient consequence

Fix any finite cap m. Use the finite CURRENT-FOREST linear carrier: completed subtrees are opaque, and states are graded by their current number j of roots. Every actual private natural COMMON or INDEPENDENT kernel K preserves the root-count filtration. Within grade j its block is b_j(K) times the identity. A routing event without merger does not change a forest or permute its labels. Every nontrivial off-grade transition lowers the current root count.

The independently accepted B4 source argument already gives, for a strict word K containing an ordinary E(z),

    1=b_1(K)>b_2(K)>...>b_m(K)>0,
    b_(j+1)(K)<=z^j b_j(K),  0<z<1.

This uses projective monotonicity of the no-merger coordinates and their multiplicativity. The isolated empty-forest block, when included, is another constant-one character. This filtration and ordering are inherited; they are not newly asserted for arbitrary retained multiport cores, exposed registers or tensor representations.

Let Sigma be a finite alphabet and assign each symbol s ANY finite strict private source macroletter K_s. Parameters and macroletter lengths may differ between symbols. Write

    beta_j(w)=product_(s in w) b_j(K_s).

These are positive multiplicative characters on the free monoid. For every symbol, and therefore every nonempty word, their pointwise order is the same. If j<k, beta_j(s)>beta_k(s), apart from the duplicated constant-one grade.

**Common-subquotient lemma.** Every common invariant subquotient U/W of this source representation has a filtration with scalar quotient characters chosen, with multiplicity, from the beta_j. In particular it cannot have two composition characters chi,psi for which

    chi(s)>psi(s),   chi(t)<psi(t)                         (1)

for two symbols s,t.

**Proof.** Intersect U with each root-count filtration space F_j, then take its image in U/W. On the j-th successive quotient, K_s acts by b_j(K_s), since K_s-b_j(K_s)I lowers F_j into F_(j-1). Refine every nonzero scalar quotient by any ordinary vector-space flag. This gives a common composition flag with the stated one-dimensional characters, without requiring commuting K_s or a common eigenbasis.

For completeness, the multiset of characters does not depend on the chosen common triangular flag. Its sum is the trace function w -> tr(K_w) on every word. Distinct multiplicative characters are linearly independent as functions on a monoid: from a shortest nontrivial relation, choose two differing characters and a symbol or word a on which they differ; evaluate at aw and subtract one character's value at a times the original relation. This removes at least one term and leaves a nontrivial shorter relation, a contradiction. Comparing trace sums therefore identifies the character multiset, with multiplicities. Two crossing characters (1) cannot be a submultiset of the pointwise ordered beta_j. QED.

The same conclusion applies after any fixed common similarity transformation, restriction and quotient, or addition of other invariant coordinates followed by selection of this subquotient. It applies to one actual private current-root action. It is NOT a theorem about tensor products, products of independent slots, arbitrary nonlinear coordinates or an input-dependent family of exterior maps.

## 3. The exact primary paired-string model

Rote's 2025 paper supplies a particularly close primary construction: positive stochastic automata obtained from paired-string affine matrices, rather than Jordan arithmetic counters. In Section 2.1, the six coordinates are the degree-at-most-two monomials

    1, x, x^2, y, y^2, xy.

For tile (v_s,w_s), put alpha_s=10^(-|v_s|), gamma_s=10^(-|w_s|). Its two affine substitutions have constant terms 0.v_s and 0.w_s. The matrix is triangular with characters

    1, alpha, alpha^2, gamma, gamma^2, alpha*gamma.        (2)

Concatenation of tiles multiplies these matrices and concatenates both strings. The scalar expression in Section 2.1 detects their equality; the later steps produce positive stochastic matrices. This primary construction concerns a supplied finite alphabet and cutpoint tests, not original G3 observations.

Primary source: Gunter Rote, *Probabilistic Finite Automaton Emptiness Is Undecidable for a Fixed Automaton*, MFCS 2025, Sections 2.1–2.7, DOI 10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2025.86. The characters (2) also follow directly by expanding the stated affine substitutions; no source computation or external matrix proof replay is needed to read their diagonal. They are characters of the whole word representation, not a separate choice of eigenvalue order for each tile.

## 4. A literal private source lift fails even after common damping

Suppose the tile alphabet has symbols s,t with opposite length imbalances:

    |v_s|<|w_s|,     |v_t|>|w_t|.                         (3)

Then alpha(s)>gamma(s), whereas alpha(t)<gamma(t). These two composition characters of (2) cross. The common-subquotient lemma therefore excludes ANY realization of that complete six-coordinate paired-string action as a common linear invariant subquotient of one actual private natural source alphabet, at ANY finite cap and with ANY strict finite physical macroletters.

Allow a positive scalar rho_s for each symbol, so the proposed coded matrices are rho_s A_s. Their corresponding characters are rho*alpha and rho*gamma. The same rho_s cancels in each comparison, so (3) still gives a crossing. Common letterwise scalar damping cannot repair the obstruction. The same reasoning allows a nonconstant multiplicative normalization rho(w)=product rho_s; it does not concern an arbitrary nonmultiplicative rescaling of each whole word.

The positive-matrix transformation in the primary paper also retains these characters. Its similarities preserve (2). Adding the extra stochastic coordinate preserves the original representation as an invariant block and adds a constant character. The resulting row-and-column-sum-one action splits into the common uniform line and its common zero-sum complement. Replacing C_s by (1-delta)J+delta C_s, with fixed delta>0 and J the uniform projection, acts as the identity on the uniform line and as delta C_s on that complement. The alpha and gamma characters consequently survive with the SAME multiplier delta. Positivity and stochasticity alone do not make this family a private root-graded source action.

Condition (3) is not an artificial feature needed only by an irrelevant example. For ordinary unconstrained PCP, if every tile has |v_s|>=|w_s|, a solution can use only zero-imbalance tiles. Their block boundaries align, so equality is possible exactly when an equal tile is present; the reverse one-sided case is the same. Those one-sided alphabets are elementary decidable cases. This observation does not assert a new PCP theorem for arbitrary prescribed prefixes, rational languages or the separately optimized fixed-automaton variants in the primary paper. The transfer failure stated above is for every actual tile alphabet satisfying (3).

No assertion here says that the scalar acceptance series always needs the complete six-dimensional module. If a proposed reduction preserves only a scalar language, uses a nonfaithful realization or changes the arithmetic representation, it must be analyzed separately. Nor does this show that EVERY positive stochastic automaton family fails every source encoding.

## 5. An actual tensor escape prevents a false global conclusion

The ordering lemma is not closed under tensor/monomial lifting. There is a concrete strict source control, already at cap three.

Let K_A=E(1/2). Then

    b_2(K_A)^2=1/4 > b_3(K_A)=1/8.

For the second source take the literal rational COMMON word

    c=199/200,  z=39600/39601,  p=1/4,
    K_B=E(z) B_COMMON(c/2,c,p) E(c).

Every population survival and coin is strictly interior. Its normalized baseline is A=z*c^2=99/100 and arm ratio is 1/2. Hence

    b_2(K_B)=A*(7/8),    b_3(K_B)=A^3*(25/32),
    b_3(K_B)-b_2(K_B)^2
      =A^2[(99/100)*(25/32)-49/64]
      =A^2/128 >0.                                     (4)

Thus the characters b_2^2 and b_1*b_3 in the mathematical tensor action K tensor K have opposite order on two ACTUAL strict sources. There is no contradiction: their tensor filtration has incomparable grade pairs (2,2) and (1,3), rather than the single ordered current-root grading.

This tensor action is an algebraic representation. It is not asserted to be the biological joint kernel of hidden-route clones, an additional allowed observation or a physical source operation. Polynomial lifting of exact response equations may introduce such monomials as mathematics without granting a new experiment. Multiple original rows or retained slots may likewise require an enlarged representation. Equation (4) therefore prevents extrapolating the private-linear obstruction to those richer routes. It does not itself give a source-valid computational encoding.

## 6. Why the enriched route still has no complete reduction

After allowing tensor or multislot actions, the crossing test no longer blocks the entire representation class. The complete original-source obligations then reappear:

- **Actual letters and synchronization.** One must construct the desired tensor/slot arithmetic from actual finite positive source parameters. Two arbitrarily chosen independent channel words do not enforce the SAME tile sequence. Finite protected original IDs cannot be declared anew on every unbounded fresh position.
- **Legal exact observation.** A cutpoint comparison is not a supplied exact probability. An algebraic slack identity or a polynomial of latent coordinates is not automatically a permitted original observation. Its full graph/interface realization, parameter range and coupling with every other row must be proved.
- **All-core reverse implication.** Constructing a solution from selected letters establishes only the forward direction. Every source with other fresh parameters, word shapes or retained cores must either decode to a solution or be excluded by the original finite input. The old affine/selecting-polynomial failures remain in force. No nonlinear all-core tile selector is supplied by the primary automaton theorem.

The old fixed-positive-alphabet loss bound, Jordan obstruction and commuting-affine decision test are not replayed as new theorems. Variable small-hazard letters and noncommuting nonlinear encodings remain possible. The new private-linear character test and its tensor countercontrol specify exactly which proposed repair has and has not been ruled out.

## 7. Terminal full-attempt outcome

The literal paired-string matrix reduction fails its necessary source-representation gate for mixed-length alphabets, even with common positive stochastic damping and noncommuting physical macroletters. This is a rigorous obstruction to that specified transfer, derived from an inherited exact source filtration. It is not a source-faithful undecidability result.

But no source-admitted effective input map y(P), legal exact acceptance test and all-core converse have been established for them. Therefore the attempted full reduction remains incomplete, and original G3 remains open.
