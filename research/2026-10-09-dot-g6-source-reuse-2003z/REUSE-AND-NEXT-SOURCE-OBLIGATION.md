## Useful simplification

For the original natural **finite rational cell** grammar, the proved inverse-rate compiler and rational affine feasibility theorem give a rational witness inside every feasible cell. One shared vector contains all vertex ages, all original edge/ancestral inverse rates, and all original-site inheritance probabilities. Positive rational inverse rates reconstruct positive rational physical rates. Strict inequalities, equality faces, shared occurrences and supplied chronology constraints remain exact.

Thus the natural-cell consumer can bypass the older intermediate step “algebraic cell witness, then nearby rational source, potentially outside the cell.” It can evaluate a rational source witness in the cell itself. This is a corollary of existing proofs, not a new density principle. It requires finite supplied legal charts with rational coefficients; it does not cover arbitrary nonlinear controls or fixed irrational rates.

The executable Boolean decider is proved. Rational witness existence is proved. Enumerating rational vectors and checking their finite rational rows terminates after a YES answer, by that existence theorem. No witness-returning Lean function or executable end-to-end cloud constructor is asserted here.

5. **Within-cell and full source-image coverage.** Reuse the accepted physical hazard comparison: unsaturated coordinates cost at most C times their hazard difference; comparing two saturated coordinates costs at most 2C exp(-H).
