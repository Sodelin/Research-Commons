Write r=r_N,h=h_N. Since r-h=rh/(2L) and r/h tends to 1,

C(h)-C(r)=(r²-h²)R/3+O_H(h^4)=h³R/(3L)+O_H(h^4).

The error uses the derivative of the analytic O(r^3) remainder, not two unrelated O(h^3) errors. On a bounded matrix neighborhood, the derivative of exp at an argument O(h) is identity plus O(h). Therefore

S_h-exp(hC(r))=h^4 R/(3L)+O_H(h^5).

## What this answers and what remains

This family is NOT an o(h^4) counterexample to the proposed generic critical-scale fair stability inequality. Its earlier O(h^4) upper bound is sharp on any fixed closed full carrier containing this actual four-root coordinate, in particular the full cap12 carrier. A three-root restriction alone misses the noncommutation and cannot establish this sharpness.

The calculation still proves neither the generic h^4 stability inequality nor absence of other o(h^4) half-integer countersequences. Such a theorem would require a uniform quantitative lower bound over all paired fair words with arbitrary counts, positive gaps and chronology, or a different strict finite family violating it. Fixed-count compactness, exact fair rigidity and one reference family do not supply that quantifier.

No phase bound, AG-Pair, total-G3 recognizer/impossibility, effective stopping theorem or original G4 conclusion follows. Source restrictions and one shared physical tuple remain unchanged.
