## Hand proof of global old-frontier incidence

Use three standard facts about a finite graph embedded in the plane: an edge's interior has constant incident faces; a bridge has the same face on both sides; a point in a bounded complementary region is enclosed by a simple embedded cycle. Finite planar vertex stars have their embedding-induced cyclic order. These are classical topological statements, not arbitrary added rotation data. Parallel IDs are retained; bigons are Jordan cycles, or can be temporarily subdivided for the standard simple-graph topological proof without changing their geometric traces.

**1. Every bridge is exterior.** Let e be an original bridge. If its incident face were bounded, choose an interior point of e and a nearby point in that face. A simple embedded graph cycle encloses the bridge interior (the bridge cannot itself occur in that cycle). Such a cycle is wholly on one side of G−e. The opposite bridge-side component lies strictly inside it: the bridge interior enters that side, and neither that component nor its connecting edges can cross the enclosing cycle. The exact GraphCuts theorem supplies a labelled taxon in this opposite component. A point strictly inside the cycle cannot lie in the closure of the global unbounded complementary component. This contradicts TaxonOuterFace. Hence e's incident face is unbounded and every point on its trace is on the old global exterior frontier.

This argument does not assume every vertex is outer and does not replace the network by a switching. The enclosing-cycle fact can be phrased directly for a bridge interior using a small local arc neighborhood; finite plane-graph topology justifies the perturbation without moving the graph.

**2. Both incoming arcs of every hybrid are exterior.** A hybrid has exactly two incoming original edges and one outgoing edge. GalledDetour makes the latter a bridge. Around its trivalent embedded star, both sides of that bridge belong to the unbounded face by step1. These are precisely the two sectors between the child edge and the two parent edges. Consequently each incoming-edge germ borders that face. Constant incidence along an edge interior propagates this to the entire incoming arc. In particular any chosen strict interior cut parameter is on the old global exterior frontier. Distinct parallel parent IDs cause no exception.

**3. Transfer to the actual cap.** Once the prescribed cap drawing is constructed from retained original internal arcs and original bridge subarcs, its point set is a subset of the original drawing. The original unbounded component is contained in the cap's unbounded component. Closure inclusion transfers the original bridge/interior-parent points into the cap exterior frontier. This supplies exterior incidence for its newly tagged port leaves/fresh root and for the later parent cut points. It does not identify local port order with global C.

## Stronger useful face certificate (hand level)

For the actual rooted binary cap, let h be its number of hybrids. Summing indegrees gives E=V−1+h. Connected plane Euler therefore gives exactly h bounded faces, counting parallel edges normally. Pendant port edges and the inserted-root bridge path create no bounded faces.

Each bounded face has a cycle boundary: the raw blob is two-connected in the ≥3 case, its bigon case is a genuine cycle, and all cap bridges are exterior. Orient that boundary by the original directed edges. A finite acyclic orientation of a cycle has a sink on the cycle. Two cycle edges enter that vertex, so it must be a hybrid (root indegree0, ordinary vertex/leaf indegree1). Conversely, at a hybrid the two parent edges border the exterior on the child side and can border at most one bounded face on their other side. Thus distinct bounded faces cannot use the same hybrid sink. Since there are h bounded faces and h hybrids, each bounded face has exactly one hybrid, and every hybrid belongs to its corresponding bounded face.

This is a source-bound facial certificate, not a new abstract graph-theory novelty claim. It can organize opening: each hybrid opens its own bounded face into the exterior. Its interior-side facial traversal has no other hybrid and no port-leaf excursion (those leaves are exterior). That is the correct candidate mechanism for a consecutive pair of new tips. Turning this mechanism into the exact opened-tree boundary walk, paired adjacency, and collapse recovering the original local/global taxon orders still requires the actual facial-walk/surgery transport proof. Euler counting alone does not supply that transport.

## Invalid counterexamples and exact limits

A tempting drawing nests a downstream bigon around an upstream one and puts all taxa downstream outside. It would hide upstream parent edges globally, but it is NOT admitted: the downstream entry is a nonroot dominator of every taxon, violating least_stable and the bridge-both-sides taxon theorem. This preliminary candidate was rejected, not retained as a counterexample. Likewise a hybrid with internal nonbridge child is excluded by GalledDetour and is not a valid test case. Dropping these admission facts can make the stronger property false.
