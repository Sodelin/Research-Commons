# G7 — Exact-law experimental design under a finite original registry

**Status.** Accepted complete hand characterization for the contract below; selected source/compiler/game/rank components are formally checked. Full native policy extraction and source-to-QE formal assembly are not claimed.

**Input and definitions.** Supply finite n≥2, a **complete registry of exactly r original binary hybrid IDs and parent labels**, finitely many contemporaneous sampling multiplicities and finite unranked topology readouts. Sources are positive finite binary rooted LSA outer-labelled planar cut-child DAGs, with parallel arcs allowed and interior natural inheritance. Fix COMMON/INDEPENDENT or a tagged finite union; one graph and parameter assignment must explain every response of one source. Targets are Q/S or another declared finite computable topology function.

Actions are effectively semialgebraic affine randomized exact-law programmes over algebraic constants, built from the finite original-ID row alphabet. Legality and integer costs are encoded in a finite resource state. Reset/deletion closure is required. Programme cost counts executed calls along a trajectory; sites/configurations count unions of positive-support rows along that trajectory. The computational model allows exact real signs and algebraic selection, not noisy frequencies or arbitrary computable-real zero tests.

**Conclusion.** Uniformly for every such finite input, a terminating mathematical procedure returns the feasible integer-resource Pareto frontier/minimum and a semialgebraic identifying policy, or a response-consistent admitted-source lower/impossibility certificate.

**Proof outline.**
1. Binary degree counting gives V=2n+2r−1 and E=2n+3r−2. Stub enumeration, following a topological order, exhausts every original graph and ordered parent registry. Exact admission filters check reachability, LSA, child bridges and the outer-labelled embedding; canonicalization preserves original IDs.
2. The accepted whole-forest compiler uses xₑ=exp(−tₑ). Strictly descending lineage counts give rational polynomial edge transitions; root completion has rational pair-order probabilities. Joint-frontier induction enumerates common/independent routing and original-ID forcing on the same source. It retains correlations and compiles all rows with shared variables.
3. Concatenate finite base responses into z∈ℝᴰ. Each allowed response is B(a)z+b(a). A rank-redundant response is reconstructible from the history. Simulate it virtually and delete its physical action; reset/deletion closure preserves legality and budgets. Every retained call raises rank, hence at most D informative calls suffice for any pointwise-total identifying policy.
4. Let H mean every source consistent with the whole history has the same target. Define W₀=H and W_d=H or “there exists an affordable legal action such that every response leads to W_(d−1).” Actual-source consistency is one parameter tuple across all equations. Real quantifier elimination decides these finite formulas; cylindrical algebraic decomposition selects actions within winning fibres. Enumerating finite budgets gives the frontier. A losing continuation retains consistent sources with different targets; it need not be one fixed pair defeating all strategies.

**Use and limits.** G1/G2 supply faithful shared-source semantics; declared G5 targets can be optimized. This finite complete-registry census neither solves unbounded G3 recognition nor uses a G4 equality oracle. G6, not rank deletion, handles noisy repetition. Trajectory budgets are not a bound on a globally installed programme library. Unknown extra IDs, irreversible actions, metric/DNA channels, new clock constraints and optimal unbounded expected stopping costs are separate contracts. Classical CAD/QE, affine rank and decision games are reused; novelty remains unresolved.
