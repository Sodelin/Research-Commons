# G5 — What three-tip timed laws identify

**Status.** Accepted all-size hand theorem; the exact binary M3 endpoint also has a selected 319-module Lean certificate. Broader bounded-indegree generalization remains hand-proved, not fully formalized. This card is a source-linked digest, not a new proof or novelty claim.

**Input and definitions.** Fix an original taxon set X, |X|≥3. Each rival source is a finite binary rooted temporal LSA directed multigraph, with positive edge durations and original/ancestral pair rates, strictly interior inheritance, contemporaneous tips at the same calendar age, and every hybrid-child edge an actual undirected bridge. Parallel edge occurrences matter. There is no network-level or hybrid-count bound and no planarity assumption. Each source uses its own once-drawn natural original register and COMMON or current-live-owner INDEPENDENT mechanism. Parameters, graph carriers and mechanisms may differ between rivals, but each source's panels share that one source and parameter assignment.

M3 means the rooted, labelled **full timed genealogy law on every three-element original-tip subset**; population and register IDs are hidden. The target is the union of normalized displayed rooted clusters (including singletons and X) and the union of nontrivial displayed unordered splits (both sides at least two labels).

**Conclusion and quantifiers.** For every two admitted sources, equality of every M3 law implies equality of those two unions. It does not identify which clusters occur together in one tree.

**Proof outline.**
1. The accepted frozen-germ/positive-posterior support bridge extracts no-selected-merger population-partition supports from the observed timed laws. Ordinary restriction gives one- and two-tip supports; positive survival preserves projective feasible support.
2. The original bridge-component route lemma bounds each current tip's possible populations at a fixed age by two: inside its current component it chooses at most one binary parent route; previous choices have pooled at fixed entries.
3. Pair supports identify sure exact blocks and their first attained times. Before such a time, the accepted child-bridge/safe-selector chronology prevents two current representatives from using the same hybrid. Thus feasible route support is Cartesian, without asserting posterior-weight independence.
4. The size-two-set intersection lemma reconstructs every possible exact block using only tests on at most three labels: inside intersections have Helly number three; outside singleton obstructions are witnessed by one inside and at most two outside labels.
5. Merge sure blocks, retain original-tip representatives, and lift recovered blocks through their original groups. Accepted safe-past/deletion transport preserves the invariant; at most |X|−1 deletions occur. Original switching pruning identifies exactly the displayed cluster union; cluster complementation and root suppression give the split union.
