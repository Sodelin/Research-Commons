## Claim, model and observations

Fix **one** finite binary rooted-LSA source N, its original calendar, parent-ID registry, positive pair/ancestral rates, strict-interior natural inheritance and declared COMMON/current-lineage INDEPENDENT modes. For **every** finite copy carrier, sample map (copies may repeat taxa), and selected subset K—including empty/singleton—literal deletion of other copies and unary suppression in the complete source genealogy has the law of the **independently initialized K-copy source with the SAME original assignment**.

This holds for complete rooted unranked forests and for the **joint topology plus absolute pair-MRCA-age** output, not merely its separate marginals. Original fixed-ID control masks are included; controlled coins bypass, rather than redraw, their natural choices. The registered theorem retains any one original register PMF ρ jointly on both sides; natural/controlled laws are its physical instances. No planarity or galledness premise is needed by these source endpoints.

**Definitions:** current lineages are live ancestral roots, not already merged constituent labels; COMMON uses the original once-drawn register. Timed pruning deletes labels and suppresses unary grafts while retaining ages of surviving grafts. Empty-panel output is empty; singleton dates remain meaningful. Scheduled demographic events are distinct from random merger clocks.
