# G3 — exact recognition of an unknown finite physical source

**Status:** original master OPEN; accepted restricted hand results retained. Prepared by dot (OpenAI), 31 October 2026. No new proof, Lean execution or novelty claim.

## Original target

Given every permitted finite rational/effectively algebraic joint observation input, decide whether ONE admitted finite, strictly positive original physical source realizes it. Its architecture/word length is unknown and unbounded. YES must supply one actual source, its original-ID interpretation and one shared parameter tuple/register semantics reproducing ALL rows exactly. NO must exclude the entire admitted class, and the procedure must terminate on every permitted input. Independent row fits, abstract stochastic kernels, approximate limits or a bound on one chosen architecture do not suffice. See [original master](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/0c0dc21eed1046c405e86a92673a6d473a934b6d/research/2026-10-05-dot-original-g-master-priority-1913z/MASTER-STATEMENTS.md).

## Shortest accepted argument chain and its stopping point

1. **Fix a legal finite architecture and original-ID map.** The finite labelled-forest recursion compiles the applicable topology/control rows to exact polynomial constraints in survival and inheritance variables, retaining strict positivity and the same tuple across rows. Within the stated effective algebraic language, real-closed-field decision settles that architecture. Enumerating all admitted finite architectures semidecides YES; it does not terminate on all NO inputs. The [exact source criterion](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/eb284f41d13fff2602de4e98411fb15e5891f9e8/research/2026-09-30-g3-exact-source/EXACT-CRITERION.md) carefully separates this finite-input claim from its broader computable-profile characterization.

2. **A uniform compact budget prevents escape.** For full profiles, the criterion is “there exists one finite budget B such that every finite accuracy test is feasible inside B.” Compactness and finitely many graph/ID choices produce one exact positive source. Reversing the quantifiers permits growing sources or boundary limits and proves no actual realization. This characterization does not compute B from arbitrary input.

3. **A supplied exposing COMMON certificate can give a bounded representative.** A nonzero nonnegative sparse polynomial with exact zero expectation restricts every supported directed survival to finitely many positive roots. One supported forcing configuration exposes all original natural banks. Accepted physical collapse/count and protected-provenance suppliers then bound a representative while preserving the same joint COMMON laws and protected IDs/parameters. This is the [independently accepted hand composition](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/e9e31baf3767593ae6805924463c0bcb4fc08f0f/research/2026-10-13-dot-certificate-bounded-witness-0223z/INDEPENDENT-BOUNDED-WITNESS-REVIEW.md), not a general recognizer.

4. **The input encoding matters.** The [mandatory clarification](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/e9e31baf3767593ae6805924463c0bcb4fc08f0f/research/2026-10-13-dot-certificate-bounded-witness-0223z/INPUT-ENCODING-CLARIFICATION.md) fixes external configuration carriers, correlated PMFs, masks, readouts and every protected tie before source search. It does not cover arbitrary timed observations, unmarked ties or new structural constraints. [Quadratic acquisition](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/4a082a70ef5d604ffb2db89f988d88b6159f569d/research/2026-10-14-dot-g3-quadratic-acquisition-review-2313z/INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md) supplies certificates only under its actual receiver/chart/rank/curvature hypotheses.
