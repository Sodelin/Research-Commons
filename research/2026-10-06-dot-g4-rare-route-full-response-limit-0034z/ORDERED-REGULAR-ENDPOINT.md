## 1. The actual limiting cell map is polynomial

Fix the cap and the finite filtration/basis from GRADED-FULL-RESPONSE-LIMIT.md. Let q=exp(-sigma), so q lies in (1/10,1). Each un-conjugated coefficient X_d is polynomial in rho,z: the ordinary forest kernels are finite polynomials in survival, the exact source coefficient formula is finite at each order, the normalization has b(0)=1, and a matrix-log coefficient uses finitely many products of lower coefficients.

The conjugation is polynomial in q as well. In current-root spectral blocks,

Ad(E_sigma) X = sum_(i,j) q^(lambda_i-lambda_j) P_i X P_j.

Every source coefficient is root-decreasing. The ordinary projectors preserve that filtration, and P_i X P_j vanishes when i<j. Thus only nonnegative integer weights occur. Negative powers in the inverse ordinary factor cancel in this block expression. The equal zero eigenvalue at root counts zero and one creates no Jordan term: no merger takes one root to zero, and the source coefficients vanish there. This is the same finite-cap diagonalizable ordinary operator used by the accepted spectral providers.

The limiting logarithmic coordinate Y_0(rho,z,s,q) is a finite sum of its homogeneous s-degree pieces, with degrees among the finite basis weights. It is therefore a polynomial map on the strict domain

0<rho<1, z>0, s>0, 1/10<q<1.

The nilpotent group N_0 in exponential coordinates has polynomial BCH multiplication. Treat Y_0 as the corresponding group element in those coordinates.

## 2. Its generated algebraic subgroup is the whole envelope

For fixed rho,z,q, varying positive s and using finite Vandermonde inversion separates every homogeneous degree piece of Y_0. Across all strict parameters these pieces are the classes of the actual X_d in V_d/V_(d-1). Those classes generate the entire associated-graded Lie algebra by the definition of V_d. Consequently the Lie algebra generated by the limiting logarithmic image is all gr h.

Let V be the real Zariski closure of the polynomial cell image in N_0. It is irreducible, and it contains the identity because s tends to zero. The algebraic subgroup generated by V has Lie algebra containing every logarithm Y_0. One way to see this last point in a unipotent group is that the integer powers of any element of the subgroup lie in it; their Zariski closure contains its whole polynomial one-parameter subgroup exp(t log g). Thus the generated subgroup has the full Lie algebra and equals the connected unipotent group N_0.

## 3. Positive product dimension growth, then strict chronology

Put V_n=closure(V^n), including V_0={identity}. These are irreducible closed varieties and form an increasing sequence because the identity belongs to V. If V_n and V_(n+1) have equal dimension, they are equal. For each v in V, right translation gives the closed irreducible variety V_n v contained in V_(n+1)=V_n, with the same dimension, hence V_n v=V_n. Inverses preserve it as well. Therefore V_n is invariant under the subgroup generated by V. Since it contains the identity and that subgroup is all N_0, V_n=N_0.

For D=dim h>0, every step before reaching N_0 strictly increases dimension, so at most D factors suffice for a dominant product map. The original strict real parameter domain is Zariski dense in its affine parameter space; using it rather than boundary points does not alter that dominance. In characteristic zero a dominant polynomial map to this D-dimensional group has a nonzero D-column Jacobian minor.

The strict chronological chamber

is a nonempty Euclidean open subset of the full physical parameter domain. A nonzero polynomial minor cannot vanish on that entire open set. Hence SOME strictly ordered finite physical leading tuple gives a full-rank limiting endpoint map. All source parameters are shared exactly as in the actual product. No inverse factor is used in this realizing tuple; inverses appeared only in the algebraic subgroup proof.

If D=0, use one strict cell and a vacuous rank condition. The nonempty architecture is not said to have at most zero factors.

## 4. Exact consequence and exact limit

The accepted analytic rescaling implies that a full-rank limiting endpoint remains a full-rank physical endpoint for sufficiently small positive epsilon. Its kernel approaches the ordinary target as epsilon tends to zero, and the positive-gap construction remains valid. This establishes regular endpoints near the target in the same finite architecture.

It does NOT show that the full-rank endpoint is the identity in N_0, that the image ball contains the identity, or that the target is an interior attainable point. The unscaled endpoint error tends to zero while the inverse-coordinate/Jacobian scales may degenerate; unscaled closeness alone cannot correct the error to an exact return. The remaining sufficient premise is still a regular zero of the COMPLETE ordered limiting response, or an alternative exact fixed-architecture formal solution.

This consequence removes lack of any ordered regular endpoint as a possible generic excuse. It does not settle the actual identity fibre, the source character cone, F_m or original G4. No finite-cap source calculation, effective grading computation or new execution occurred.
