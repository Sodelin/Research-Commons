# A faithful finite limiting system for the rare-route ansatz

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 6 October 2026. Hand-proof candidate for independent review. This uses the classical associated-graded construction for a filtered finite-dimensional Lie algebra. The source-specific inputs are the exact complete forest expansion and physical chronology. It does not prove a limiting return or the original G4 master.

## 1. Exact input and finite ambient representation

Fix any finite cap m and tau=log(10). Use a faithful real matrix representation of the complete capped forest graft algebra, for example its left regular representation. Equality in this representation is equality of every forest coordinate; no diagonal projection is used. All statements below retain the same physical parameters across arities.

The reviewed literal source system WORKING-EXACT-SYSTEM.md gives the rare cell with x=rho, y=exp(-epsilon s z), g=epsilon s, where 0<rho<1 and s,z>0. Right-normalize by its exact two-token survival and denote the resulting matrix by C_epsilon(rho,z,s). It satisfies C=I+O(epsilon^3) at every cap and is jointly real analytic near epsilon=0 and every fixed strict parameter point.

Let E_sigma=exp(sigma Q). For 0<sigma<tau put

L_epsilon(p)=log(Ad(E_sigma) C_epsilon(rho,z,s)),
p=(rho,z,s,sigma).

The ordinary matrix logarithm is well defined locally because C tends to I. Write

L_epsilon(p)=sum_(d>=3) epsilon^d s^d X_d(rho,z,sigma).          (1)

The scale identity follows from replacing epsilon by epsilon s in the actual source. The X_d are complete source coefficient matrices, not independent control variables. Formula (1) is locally jointly convergent in epsilon and physical parameters. Algebraic log, inverse and bracket operations below describe a chart; none are added to the legal source grammar.

## 2. A finite positive filtration from the actual coefficients

Let h be the real Lie subalgebra generated by all X_d(rho,z,sigma), for all d>=3 and strict parameters. It is finite dimensional because the ambient matrix space is finite dimensional.

Define V_k to be the real span of all Lie monomials in these matrices whose total assigned degree is at most k; X_d has assigned degree d. Thus

V_0=V_1=V_2=0,
V_k subset V_(k+1),
[V_i,V_j] subset V_(i+j).                                    (2)

Every element of h is a finite linear combination of finite Lie monomials. Select a finite basis of h and finite such expressions for its basis vectors. The largest total degree in those finitely many expressions supplies an L with V_L=h. Hence this filtration eventually exhausts h. This is an existence argument; no effective bound on L, dimension or architecture is claimed. A temporary plateau V_k=V_(k+1) alone is not a stopping rule.

Choose a basis e_1,...,e_D adapted to this filtration, with positive weights w_i>=3, such that V_k is spanned by the e_i with w_i<=k. Every w_i<=L. Define the invertible linear map for epsilon nonzero

D_epsilon(e_i)=epsilon^(w_i)e_i.

This is a change of coordinates, not a source operation.

The deformed brackets

[u,v]_epsilon=D_epsilon^(-1)[D_epsilon u,D_epsilon v]

extend polynomially to epsilon=0. Indeed, a coefficient of e_k in [e_i,e_j] can occur only if w_k<=w_i+w_j by (2), so its multiplier is epsilon^(w_i+w_j-w_k), with nonnegative exponent. At epsilon=0 this is the usual associated-graded bracket. Its positive grading makes it nilpotent: a bracket with q inputs has degree at least 3q and vanishes when 3q>L. Denote the resulting simply connected nilpotent group, in exponential/BCH coordinates, by N_0.

## 3. The entire physical response extends, without missing equations

Define the rescaled cell coordinate

Y_epsilon(p)=D_epsilon^(-1)L_epsilon(p).

It extends jointly real analytically to epsilon=0. To prove this, use fixed linear coordinate functionals for the e_i basis. The coefficient X_d lies in V_d, so its i-coordinate is zero whenever d<w_i. Dividing that coordinate by epsilon^(w_i) therefore removes only coefficients that vanish IDENTICALLY as functions of the physical parameters. The remaining Taylor series is convergent and joint analyticity is preserved. No pointwise-only divisibility or unproved parameter-uniform limit is invoked.

All sufficiently small log C values lie in h: their convergent coefficient series lies in that finite-dimensional closed real subspace. Likewise h is closed under the local BCH product. Thus coordinates in h give a faithful Lie-envelope chart for these actual source products. The envelope is not asserted to be the exact physically attainable source-image manifold; requiring a regular zero in every h coordinate can be stronger than necessary.

For bounded u,v near any fixed finite values define

P_epsilon(u,v)=D_epsilon^(-1)
  log(exp(D_epsilon u) exp(D_epsilon v)).                       (3)

This map also extends jointly analytically to epsilon=0. Here is the finite-loss argument. Each D_epsilon u is ambient O(epsilon^3). The ordinary matrix product/log is jointly analytic for small epsilon. Its BCH Taylor coefficients are finite sums of brackets of basis vectors, and (2) puts a coefficient of total epsilon degree d in V_d. Hence every i-coordinate below order w_i vanishes identically. Division by epsilon^(w_i), with at most the fixed finite loss L, leaves a convergent analytic function. Equivalently, truncate through degree L; the analytic ambient remainder is O(epsilon^(L+1)), and after division it still tends to zero. This avoids any assumption that a convergent-series module is complete.

At epsilon=0 equation (3) is exactly the finite BCH law of N_0: a bracket contribution survives precisely when its output weight equals the sum of its input weights. Higher bracket lengths vanish by the positive grading. The same argument applies to any fixed finite product.

## 4. A precise sufficient physical lift

Suppose for this cap there exist a FINITE N and strict, ordered physical leading parameters p* such that

F_0(p*)=0 and rank D_p F_0(p*)=D.                              (6)

The derivative must be of the actual ordered product in (4), with its shared parameter tuple. It is not the derivative of freely assigned coefficient matrices or an unordered sum.

If D=0, the coordinate system is empty and every normalized cell is the identity near epsilon=0; choose any nonempty finite strict architecture with ordered interior positions. The rank condition is vacuous, and the same positive-gap construction below gives the return. For D>0, choose an invertible D-column minor, fixing all other source parameters. Condition (6) is presently UNPROVED. It is sufficient, not necessary: an exact fixed-architecture formal solution can still transfer by the accepted RCF argument even when no regular limiting zero has been established.

## 5. A precise obstruction, and the remaining actual cone question

Let ell be a nonzero real character of the limiting Lie algebra, meaning ell annihilates its commutator subspace. Then

ell(F_0)=sum_j ell(Y_0(p_j)).                                  (7)

If ell(Y_0(p))>0 for every strict source parameter p in this ansatz, no nonempty finite leading architecture can return to the identity, irrespective of later coherent parameter corrections. A weak nonnegative guard leaves its zero-face problem open. This concerns the actual limiting source image, not arbitrary elements of the Lie envelope.

Conversely, the absence of such a character is NOT asserted to supply a chronologically ordered regular zero. In particular an unordered semigroup theorem, or a return allowing repeated positions without a proved perturbation/correction argument, cannot replace (6). A Lie bracket computed with inverses is not itself a physical operation. The complete cone and ordered-product issue remain the substantive source question.

This construction removes the need to assume a faithful finite limiting chart: it constructs a faithful Lie-envelope limiting system abstractly from the exact rare-route response, retaining every forest equation and all chronological terms. It does not identify that envelope with the actual attainable image manifold. It does not calculate the filtration, decide its character cone, produce (6), or prove F_m. The next mathematical work must address that actual limiting image or give a decisive structural obstruction, rather than repeat diagonal rank or an isolated finite-cap coefficient test.
