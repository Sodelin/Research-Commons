## Actual source endpoint

`Nanuq.RawBlock.binary_blob_delete_vertex_connected` takes the original `SourceNetwork.RootedBinary N`, any original `blobOf` fibre b, and original fibre vertices a,c different from v. It proves a retained-edge walk from a to c after deleting v from the **unswitched original blob**. No bridgelessness or degree certificate is an extra premise: these are derived from the original source definitions and binary degree fields.

`BlobVertex` is the original `blobOf` fibre. `BlobEdge` is a subtype of original nonbridge EDGE IDs whose source lies in that fibre. Source and target are the original endpoints. Parallel edges are never identified. In particular, the selected-switching blob tree is not substituted for the raw block.

## Proof chain

1. **Crossing IDs:** a walk preserves any side predicate preserved by its edges. A nonbridge crossing edge has a second distinct original crossing edge, obtained from its edge-deletion detour.
2. **Degree-three gate:** two distinct hypothetical components after deleting v would each have two incident crossing IDs. The pairs are disjoint, so v would have at least four incident original IDs. This contradicts degree at most three.
3. **Original bridge retraction:** collapse the opposite side of an original bridge onto its boundary endpoint. A walk step is either the unchanged original edge or reflexive; no new edge is introduced. Finite bridge removal preserves every already-excluded edge. Hence an original nonbridge detour can be retained wholly among original nonbridge edges while still excluding its own ID.
4. **Raw subtype join:** exact original walks lift into the original blob fibre, establishing connectivity and bridgelessness of this UNSWITCHED restriction. Incidence counting uses an injective map back to original IDs.
5. **Binary source join:** root/leaf/internal degree cases give in-degree plus out-degree at most three; incident-ID union cardinal is bounded by that sum. The final theorem invokes the preceding generic result directly on the original source.

The generic cut/count facts do not require looplessness. This does not discard original DAG/no-loop assumptions: the direct endpoint retains the entire original RootedBinary structure. Later drawing/cycle consumers must retain their own exact assumptions.
