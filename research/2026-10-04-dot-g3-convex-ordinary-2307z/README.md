## What is proved

For each finite full labelled-forest cap m>=2 and every ordinary duration tau>0, the ordinary kernel E_tau lies in the relative interior of the convex hull of kernels of words with exactly D positive independent-routing bigons, where D is the dimension of the capped forest algebra. All constituent words obey the private natural source grammar and use the same physical parameters across their arities.

A separate conditional corollary fixes one admitted core and one retained parameter tuple. If its actual joint compiler is multi-affine in genuinely independent natural private slots, the corresponding ordinary-slot profile lies in the relative interior of the convex hull of complete joint profiles with D_s positive bigons in each nontrivial slot. Fixed affine coarsenings and exterior controls retain their actual scope.

This rules out nonconstant AFFINE full-forest or admitted joint-output supporting guards at these ordinary targets. The finite mixtures are mathematical averages of DIFFERENT source parameter choices. They are not introduced as an allowed physical source operation.

## What remains open

The result gives neither a deterministic nonempty ordinary-return word at arbitrary cap nor ordinary-target interior in the actual positive semigroup. Nonlinear constraints may still obstruct strict attainment. A faithful shared-source realizing witness, an input-effective bound and the full coupled G3 recognizer remain open, including genuine ties, core parameter faces, declared coarsenings and singular hidden limits. The distinct original fixed-target/full-legal-menu G4 problem remains open.

Internal forcing at the private cell, independent fitting of rows, paired-mode substitutions and repeated-slot nonlinear or multi-locus compilers are not covered by the stated corollary. The proof uses classical stochastic support and convex geometry with the exact accepted source identity; it makes no new general diffusion or control-theory claim.

No code execution or finite numerical screen is presented as the source of the uniform proof. This package contains mathematical exposition and its exact independent reviews only.
