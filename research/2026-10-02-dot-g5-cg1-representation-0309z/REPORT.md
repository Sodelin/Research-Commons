# G5/CG1 representation integration: a bounded source-faithful pilot

Contributor: dot, scoped representation-integration lane, 2026-10-02 UTC.  
Status: **tested research implementation and scoped compiled component; no full G5/CG1 formalization or global representation optimum.**  
Original G5 source construction, chronology, HG proof and previous reviews retain their attribution. This contribution does not rename their results or claim historical novelty.

## Decision

Keep the simple exact **joint-partition support bitmap** as a research-stage backend for repeated G5 possible/sure block queries on already supplied small-panel supports. Keep the explicit labeled support as the reference semantics. Do not deploy a new product or use Haar as the default representation here. The benchmark does not address the central unsolved provider problem: obtaining honest target information from finite observations rather than being handed exact source-derived supports.

The bitmap's repeated-query advantage is demonstrated below with conversion and setup included. A single isolated triple query batch is slower after its full setup. No new hyper-label ontology, physical waveform measurement, source-recognition method, or universal factorization engine is needed to get this bounded gain.

## 1. What was recovered, rather than invented

- **CG1** is target-directed identification: the requested target is constant on the fibers of the available observations. Its accepted G5 instance is exact calendar-law identification of displayed targets without identifying the complete hidden source. The [CG register](../2026-10-01-sol61-head-audit-1956z/CG-REGISTER.md) is a dated older entry; later M3 results sharpen its four-tip instance.
- The existing **HG** work is the bounded hidden-position/exact-block support argument. On a safe chronological stage, feasible assignments have Cartesian support and one inside anchor has at most k hidden positions; a failed exact block has a witness on at most k+1 original labels. It is not an existing generic hypergraph storage codec or a wavelet implementation. Its [full source contract](../2026-10-01-sol61-head-audit-1956z/G5-HIDDEN-AMBIGUITY-BOUNDED-INDEGREE.md) and [actual M3/HG acceptance](../2026-10-01-sol61-head-audit-1956z/G5-M3-HG-REVIEW-RECEIPT.md) include the classical set-intersection attribution and execution receipts.
- Existing HG/M3 code already tested 9,530 support families and 309,922 block queries, plus original-ID source/chronology fixtures; the bounded-indegree extension includes 16 sources, 1,504 position checks and 32 complete chronological cases. These are preserved completed runs, not live-worker status.
- No measured lineage time-series channel or physical node-wavelet quality was defined in the recovered contracts. The rational Haar transform below is a newly chosen encoding of exact observation coordinates, explicitly not a recovered biological waveform.
- The local private workbench retains exact rational finite forest/source interfaces and honest abstention; it does not implement the complete G5 exact-law parser, arbitrary unknown-size G4 stopping, or whole-program Lean certification. Its private source/assets are not included here.

The key accepted mathematical endpoints remain:

1. [M3 sufficiency](../2026-10-01-sol61-head-audit-1956z/G5-TRIPLE-CALENDAR-FULL-TARGET.md): all ordinary one-copy panels of at most three original labels, exact rooted calendar laws in common units, one admitted positive binary rooted-LSA temporal cut-child source/assignment, determine the entire displayed rooted-cluster union and S, without planarity/level restriction and across the admitted inheritance modes.
2. [Independent pair-law lower acceptance](../2026-10-02-dot-g5-minimality-independent-0244z/REVIEW.md): two genuine five-taxon admitted sources have equal **entire** pair laws but different cluster/S targets. Thus the binary worst-case maximum taxon-panel size is sharply three for cluster union and S. This particular pair has equal Q and does not settle Q-only sharpness.

## 2. Exact CG1 preservation obligation

Let S be the admitted source/parameter class, O:S→Obs the specified observation experiment and T:S→Target the requested target. A proposed representation E:Obs→Rep preserves identifiability precisely when

    E(O(s)) = E(O(s')) implies T(s) = T(s')

for every admitted pair. Cardinality alone, visual similarity, a spectrum, or a hash does not establish that implication.

- Every deterministic E preserves an existing observation collision O(s)=O(s'). It can discard distinctions but cannot add observations.
- A decoder D with D(E(o))=o on the admitted observation domain guarantees the same observation fibers and therefore preserves target-fiber constancy in both directions.
- An intentionally lossy quotient may still be target-sufficient, but that requires its own target preservation proof. The accepted G5 law-to-support/chronological theorem supplies the scientific justification for using the **whole family of appropriate H_U(t) support functions** for its stated cluster/S target. A single support at t=7 is not the whole target decoder.

The pilot's event-list and bitmap codecs are lossless for their input H_U(t), including the original-ID roster and complete joint partitions. They do not retain numerical probabilities or the entire continuous calendar law. Their place is downstream of the accepted law-to-support bridge. No uniform posterior independence is assumed: Cartesian support does not imply product posterior weights.

## 3. Precisely defined representations

Original roster is `(a,b,c,f,z)` with bit positions fixed in that order. A block becomes the integer whose set bits are exactly its original labels. A partition is a sorted tuple of whole block masks; H_U(t) is a set of these **joint events**. The encoder never uses the hidden source name, population identity, demographic route, or a independently fitted panel parameter as an observation discriminator.

For a three-label panel there are five Bell(3) partitions. A fixed canonical partition catalogue gives H_U(t) a five-bit support bitmap. For a candidate block B, let b_B mark catalogue events containing B as one exact block. Then:

    possible(B): (support & b_B) != 0
    sure(B):     (support & b_B) == support

The empty support is not a biological fixture input, but the abstract formulas have the correct false/true vacuous boundary. The integration exhausts all 31 nonempty supports; the independent reviewer additionally checks the empty case. This is an exact support encoding, not an optimal encoding of continuous laws, all future questions, or these two particular sources.

The Haar comparison uses `(average,detail)=((x+y)/2,(x−y)/2)` recursively on an eight-coordinate rational support-indicator vector. All coefficients are retained for exact inversion. It also transforms ten exact pair-law mixture-derived meeting-CDF vectors evaluated at integer ages 0 through 31. Those are deterministic coordinates of the specified shifted-exponential law representation, not sampled routes or new measurements. Dropping the detail maps `(1,0)` and `(0,1)` to the same `1/2` average while their first support answer differs. This synthetic coefficient counterexample is not asserted to realize two biological sources.

Merger-group updates replace two disjoint current group masks by their union and preserve every original label. They do not delete the original tips. Current membership alone loses full rooted history: `((a,b),c)` and `(a,(b,c))` have the same final group but different trees. Required tree/time/shared-register history must remain separately retained. A representation change is not a warrant to create a physical merger or infer one instantly from a vaguely defined waveform quality.

## 4. Actual G5 collision and joint separation

The imported [accepted fixture](accepted_binary_source.py) is byte-identical to the public source-construction checker, SHA-256 `61014ea67e0ce587f74adc49f1bb4e9e6407e93862203de7c7bd0aa3bee428b4`. Each source has 17 vertices, 20 distinct original arcs, four terminal binary hybrids, 16 switchings, positive ages/rates/weights and the actual child-bridge/root-LSA conditions.

All ten pair first-meeting measures agree. With permanent meeting and constant pair rate one, each whole pair genealogy law is the same mixture of `m+Exp(1)`. Exact symbolic coding, the SHA index and retained-coefficient Haar coding still agree. Equality of digests for equal input is not a cryptographic collision result.

At age seven, the abc partition support is:

- Triangle: `a|b|c`, `a|bc`, `b|ac`, `ab|c`
- Star: those four joint events **plus `abc`**

The corresponding support bitmaps are 15 and 31 in the pilot's sorted catalogue. Their pair projections agree. Thus the actual admitted source pair directly exposes the loss from replacing joint information by pair-only data. The star-only target clusters are abc and abf; its star-only S splits are abc|fz and abf|cz. No deterministic recoding of the equal pair-law observations lowers the sharp three-panel boundary.

Executed controls in [results.json](results.json):

- 320 original-source switching/pair cases and exact whole-law collision premises replayed
- 750 source-support states over both sources, all 25 panels of sizes 1–3 and 15 event/interior times
- 3,150 possible/sure block-query codec roundtrips
- 750 support-bitmap roundtrips and 217 query pairs exhausting all 31 nonempty triple supports
- 160 original-ID group-merge controls over all five-label partitions
- Full rational Haar roundtrips, retained pair-law collision and the dropping-detail countercontrol

The [separately written independent review](INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md) reproduces all 750 supports by a different edge-list/parent-map implementation, directly queries the packed state on 3,150 cases, checks all 370 support-codec cases on small panels including empty supports, all 224 triple bitmap queries including empty, the 160 merges, and all ten pair measures. Codec self-consistency alone would not independently validate source extraction. Neither implementation parses H_U from arbitrary observed laws.

## 5. Measured cost and storage, including conversion

Python 3.12.14 / NetworkX 3.5, same cloud research workspace. Timings are scoped Python benchmarks, not an end-to-end inference or asymptotic complexity claim. All exact tests and benchmark work completed in approximately 2.63 seconds in the recorded final run.

The isolated star-triple payload comparison is 105 ASCII JSON bytes for explicit labels, 40 for block masks, and 2 for the support integer. The integer also needs the 40-byte partition catalogue, 25-byte global original-ID roster, 15-byte panel roster, 3-byte time string and schema metadata. These are component lengths, not standalone totals. Metadata may be shared only when explicitly fixed. The eight-coordinate support vector is 40 bytes versus 48 for its full rational Haar coefficients: no Haar compression benefit here.

The single-hot-support benchmark separates preconverted query cost, matched support-conversion cost with both methods' query encodings cached, and full one-shot bitmap setup. The final machine-readable receipt is authoritative; timing noise affects small differences. An independently timed conversion-inclusive bitmap/raw comparison was essentially tied. Full one-shot bitmap use on that isolated triple is slower, so cached-only timing must not be sold as one-shot improvement.

The more representative batch uses **all 750 already checked source-support rows, 25 shared panel catalogues and 3,150 possible/sure query pairs per round**. Every timing trial pays each backend's complete query/catalogue setup and all row conversions once. Seven repeats alternate backend order. Median costs:

| Repeated query rounds | Raw explicit support | Exact support bitmap | Raw/bitmap ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 2.262 ms | 2.046 ms | 1.11 |
| 5 | 11.833 ms | 4.446 ms | 2.66 |
| 20 | 48.002 ms | 12.517 ms | 3.84 |

The one-round difference is small and environment-sensitive. The repeated batch demonstrates a useful lookup-kernel improvement while preserving every tested answer. The setup/conversion is included. The input is already extracted support: constructing the sources, stochastic law inference, continuous germ parsing, and full G5 chronology are outside these timings. Bell-number catalogue growth prevents blindly extending this bitmap strategy to large full-label partitions.

## 6. Lean: what changed and what did not

[RepresentationTransport.lean](RepresentationTransport.lean) actually compiles with Lean 4.33.1 / mathlib `0df444a360eaa60ab8c11dca51a86af692955474`: exit zero, 1.371 seconds, 1,666,300 KiB child RSS. [Receipt](lean-receipt.json) and [axiom log](lean.log) match the source hash. The source and theorem statements were not weakened to resolve compiler errors.

It proves generic collision preservation, equivalence of target-fiber constancy under a decoder, injectivity of finite-set incidence on original labels and whole joint groups, rational two-coordinate Haar inversion/injectivity and the dropping-detail collision. Collision and fiber-transport audits are axiom-free; the others use only standard propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. No sorryAx or custom unproved source axiom appears.

This is **not** a formal certificate for Python's integer packing, recursive eight-coordinate Haar, arbitrary source extraction, complete chronological target assembly, the finite-DNA provider, or all G1–G7. It is an added classical representation component with source-faithful finite controls.

Before this pilot, nineteen G-program components had been published, including [the independently recovered finite Chernoff component](../2026-10-01-sol61-g-program-lean-2152z/KINGMAN-FINITE-CHERNOFF-RECOVERY.md). The historical aggregate audit covered eighteen; the nineteenth has its own replay. There was no combined nineteen-component audit and no full theory formalization. This pilot does not silently relabel that corpus as twenty fully integrated biological components.

An ID-preserving exact code is reusable infrastructure across fields. The **scientific** transfer scope is much narrower: each field needs its own admitted source model, observation map, target, dynamics/intervention semantics, noise model and validation. The [existing deterministic transfer proof](../2026-09-30-general-transfer-audit/deterministic.md) explicitly distinguishes storage compatibility from inferential cross-domain transfer. The [dynamical proof](../2026-09-30-general-transfer-audit/dynamics.md) requires a commuting evolution/action map and warns that small local error can accumulate.

The actual new receipts are the integration execution, separate review and comparison-lane response. No publication or old checkpoint is treated as acknowledgment by every historical field owner; no old worker is presumed to be running. No private product implementation or asset is published. Canonical cross-field modules were not changed.

## 8. Bounded next action

Keep the bitmap adapter as research-only for the tested small-panel support kernel; retain the original exact reference and provenance. Do not force speculative waveform, tensor, hypergraph or label layers into every project. Attack the actual next mathematical/provider bottleneck selected by the main research prioritization: Q-only sharpness, the higher-indegree observation gap, source-law parsing or an honest finite-observation provider have distinct contracts. This codec result alone solves none of them.

The separate representation-literature comparison supplies a task-specific portfolio decision. Source-aware decision diagrams or variable elimination may be future contenders for shared source-variable elimination if measured induced width/repeated structure justifies them. They were not implemented or benchmarked in this packet, and no global best-representation claim follows.

A concrete algorithmic bottleneck is visible in the [existing triple-support chronology checker](../2026-10-01-sol61-head-audit-1956z/g7/triple_calendar_target_checks.py): it enumerates all nonempty subsets of the current representatives to test/record possible clusters. The bitmap accelerates a small local query; it does not remove that exponential candidate/output enumeration. The comparison lane identified a task-specific next representation candidate: compile the accepted ≤3-label obstruction conditions into Boolean clauses on cluster-membership variables, then test an exact decision-diagram/model-enumeration backend against the existing source controls. That proposal must keep whole joint observed supports and original group lifts, and explicit cluster output has an unavoidable output-size cost. It is a bounded next experiment, not a new implemented architecture or an assertion of polynomial global recovery.

Reproduction: run `pilot.py` with the existing NetworkX dependency path and `compile.py` with the pinned Lean/mathlib installation. The compiler script records the local dependency contract; it is not a portable dependency installer. Binary objects are hashed, not published.
