## 2. The embedded source class, without changing preparation or stopping

Fix H>0, 0<ell<a0<r<infinity, and I=(ell,r). Fix a positive hidden initial state z0 in an open box V whose compact closure lies inside the positive orthant. Each comparison uses the **same full open box U=I times V and the same deterministic full initial state (a0,z0)**. The hidden initial law is the fixed point mass at z0, independent of the visible driving Brownian motion; no random or controlled preparation is added.

The visible reaction is fixed:

    A -> 2A, rate 1,

so before stopping,

    dA_t = A_t dt + sqrt(A_t) dW_t.

Let Z be an arbitrary admitted finite mass-action diffusion network on the hidden species, with reactions involving only Z. Its reaction Brownian motions are independent of W. This is the continuous chemical Langevin diffusion model, not the discrete-state reaction jump process. Rates, finite reaction lists and source monomial degrees may vary. No hidden reaction is discarded because its projected covariance weight is zero.

Write tau_A for the first exit of A from I and zeta_Z for the first exit of Z from V. The actual full-state stop is

    tau = min(tau_A,zeta_Z),

and the observation is the continuous held path

    Y_t = A_(t wedge tau),     0 <= t <= H.

This is exactly coordinate observation of the process held after its first full-state exit. No independent killing clock is substituted for zeta_Z. The hidden exit is generated by the admitted hidden chemical source itself.

The stopped laws are required to be well defined, as in the original source contract. For the polynomial mass-action convention on a compact positive interior region, the drift and square-root reaction coefficients are locally Lipschitz, giving the usual unique stopped construction. The proof below needs only these stopped laws, the stated independence and bounded visible coefficients.

For each fixed hidden species space this is a subclass already admitted by the master. The reduction applies for every finite choice of that space. It neither places a hidden-dimension bound on the general rival class nor asserts that all general rivals have this independent-block form.

## 3. Exact observation-to-survival reduction

Let O_Z denote the law of Y on C([0,H],R), and put

    S_Z(t) = P_(z0)(zeta_Z > t),     0 <= t < H.

**Proposition.** For any two admitted hidden blocks Z and Z' in this comparison contract,

    O_Z = O_Z'
        if and only if
    S_Z(t) = S_Z'(t) for every 0 <= t < H.

Equivalently, their hidden exit times have the same law after censoring at H. This does not distinguish an exit at H from survival beyond H.

### 3.1 A common measurable observation statistic

The visible held semimartingale has decomposition

    Y_t = a0 + D_t + M_t,
    D_t = integral_0^t 1_(s<tau) A_s ds,
    [M]_t = integral_0^t 1_(s<tau) A_s ds.

Until tau, ell<A_s<r. The drift density and quadratic-variation density are bounded by r, independently of the hidden network.

For each t in a fixed countable dense subset of [0,H] together with H, form squared-increment sums along the dyadic subdivisions of [0,t]. Let Q_t(y) be the finite limit when it exists, and zero otherwise. This is a Borel function of the observed continuous path, defined by countably many continuous functions, and is the same function for every rival.

For the martingale part, Ito's formula and isometry give

    E[|sum_j (M_(t_(j+1))-M_(t_j))^2 - [M]_t|^2]
        <= 2 r^2 t^2 2^(-n).

Indeed the error is twice the sum of integrals of (M_s-M_left) dM_s, and the quadratic-variation density is at most r. Chebyshev and Borel–Cantelli give almost-sure convergence on the dyadic sequence. The finite-variation increment sum is at most r^2 t^2 2^(-n); its cross sum with M tends to zero by Cauchy–Schwarz. Intersecting over the countable set of t yields, under each admitted law,

    Q_t(Y) = integral_0^t 1_(s<tau) A_s ds

simultaneously at those times. No common null set across an uncountable model class is needed: the statistic is common, and its almost-sure identity holds under each law separately.

This is the same bounded-coefficient common-version argument used in [the reviewed likelihood audit, Section 5](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/ac54cf01fe2da11612f765435a3d43b11d4cbd21/research/2026-10-22-dot-reaction-partial-observation/STOPPED-LIKELIHOOD-ROUTE-AUDIT.md). Its use here is on the **observed A path**, unlike the hidden-coordinate singularity statistic in that audit.

For each such t<H,

    Q_H(Y) > Q_t(Y)    if and only if    tau > t

almost surely. If tau<=t, both variations have already stopped. If tau>t, their difference is at least ell times (min(tau,H)-t)>0.

For an explicit single stopping statistic, let D be the chosen countable dense time set and define

    T(y) = inf( { t in D, t<H : Q_t(y)=Q_H(y) } union {H} ).

This is Borel measurable because it is an infimum specified by countably many Borel events. On the almost-sure event above, variation increases strictly until the actual stop and is constant afterwards, so T(Y)=min(tau,H). Thus the observed path itself determines the censored full stop, and in particular P(tau>t) for every t<H. No separate exit-time measurement is supplied. This is a retrospective full-horizon statistic, not a claim of an online estimator.

### 3.2 Necessity

The visible process and hidden block are independent before their separate exits. Hence

    P(tau>t) = P(tau_A>t) S_Z(t).

The known visible survival S_A(t)=P(tau_A>t) is strictly positive at every finite t. One elementary justification is the Lamperti coordinate X=2 sqrt(A): on the transformed bounded interior interval,

    dX_t = (X_t/2 - 1/(2X_t)) dt + dW_t.

The drift is bounded there. A bounded-drift change of measure on a finite stopped horizon is equivalent to stopped Brownian motion, which has strictly positive probability of remaining in the interval for that time. Alternatively, use the positive regular-interval killed density already specified in [the reviewed two-time proof](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/ac54cf01fe2da11612f765435a3d43b11d4cbd21/research/2026-10-22-dot-reaction-partial-observation/TWO-TIME-SAMPLED-EXTENSION.md).

Consequently the observed law determines

    S_Z(t) = P_(O_Z)(Q_H>Q_t) / S_A(t)

on the dense time set. Survival functions t -> P(zeta>t) are right-continuous, so this determines them at every t<H. Equality of observed laws gives equality of these hidden survival functions.

### 3.3 Sufficiency

Let W be the visible process already held after tau_A, through H, and put S=min(zeta_Z,H). W and S are independent, and

    Y_t = W_(t wedge S).

The map (w,s) -> (t -> w(t wedge s)) is continuous in the uniform path topology and the ordinary topology on [0,H]. Equality of S_Z(t) for all t<H gives equality of the laws of S, including the entire censored mass P(zeta_Z>=H) at H. An atom at the exact time H and survival beyond H are indistinguishable in this record. Mixing the same fixed law of W against that same law of S therefore gives the same law of Y.

This proves the equivalence without assuming that Y itself is Markov, without requiring a density between the two full hidden path laws, and without replacing the full-state stop by tau_A alone. QED.

## 4. The actual unresolved recognition supplier

Let K_t^Z be the killed hidden semigroup in V. The response in the proposition is

    S_Z(t) = [K_t^Z 1](z0),     0 <= t < H.

Therefore the general master already contains this precise inverse problem:

> Given one finite hidden mass-action target, characterize every admitted finite hidden reaction network and rate vector with the same fixed-start killed-semigroup survival response throughout the observed horizon.

This is not asking to reconstruct every hidden coordinate. Different hidden sources with this same response belong to the observation fibre in the embedded experiment and must be retained. Full-generator-equivalent sources are among them, but no assertion is made that they exhaust the fibre.

The proposition supplies a complete observation adapter for this response, **not** a classification of its chemical realizations. In particular:

- a single scalar function of time is not a finite atomic positive moment sequence merely because survival is nonnegative;
- no finite-rank hypothesis, finite exponential-mixture representation, known bound on hidden reaction support, or effective exact comparison oracle has been supplied;
- full-state weak uniqueness does not imply uniqueness of the hidden source from this response;
- a scalar/neutral/catalytic family reconstruction cannot restrict arbitrary hidden rivals after the target is fixed;
- the theorem does not justify replacing all positive times by finitely many samples.

The named inspected providers in the [nonlocal audit](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/ac54cf01fe2da11612f765435a3d43b11d4cbd21/research/2026-10-22-dot-reaction-partial-observation/NONLOCAL-REALIZATION-ATTACK.md) and [prior comparison](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/ac54cf01fe2da11612f765435a3d43b11d4cbd21/research/2026-10-22-dot-reaction-partial-observation/STOCHASTIC-OBSERVABILITY-PRIOR-COMPARISON.md) do not supply this realization classification. Predictive Markovization represents an already given law; finite-rank OOM methods require their rank assumption; fixed-family moment/bracket methods do not describe every hidden first-exit realization; the likelihood identity compares already supplied measures rather than deriving this fibre from chemical source data.

**Route status:** the reduction is justified by the hand proof above. No end-to-end general recognition route through the resulting hidden-survival realization problem is currently justified by the inspected corpus. The precise missing supplier is a complete nonlocal inverse killed-semigroup-response theorem for the admitted finite chemical sources, or another full-master method that bypasses that inverse problem while still correctly deciding the same fibres. This is a mathematical blocker, not a tool-access or execution failure, and not a universal absence or impossibility claim.
