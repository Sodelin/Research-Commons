For an actually available exhaustive menu, the corresponding nested-profile condition is needed. Under the declared effective-image and sampling premises, the reviewed result permits uniformly honest finite certification where H is constant on that robust fibre and forbids high-success uniformly honest finite certification where it is not. This is a conditional proof interface, not a demonstrated materials transfer.

A particularly important scope check is the error model. The reviewed finite-alphabet result for a known computable closed total-variation ball of radius beta has the strict boundary

for success uniformly over every allowed corruption of the specified clean source p. Equality belongs to the negative side. This formula cannot be used without the actual image family, admitted TV-ball model and calibrated beta. A contamination model is a different image construction. Missing uncertainty data do not justify assuming either class, and do not prove the real experimental hypotheses indistinguishable.
