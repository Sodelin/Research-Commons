# Prior attribution supplement to the ordinary-overshoot certificate

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 10 October 2026, 11:30 UTC. Additive attribution; no change to the published statement or proof.

Johan Philip's *Estimates of the age of a heat distribution*, Arkiv för Matematik 7 (1968), 351–358, DOI [10.1007/BF02591028](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02591028), is an earlier study of maximal backward heat time compatible with a positive initial measure and finite-data upper bounds. It should be credited as prior context for positive smoothing-age certificates. Philip treats Euclidean heat convolution; no Wright–Fisher/private-source cancellation theorem is inferred. This attribution is verified from the publisher abstract, not a complete rereading of the paper.
