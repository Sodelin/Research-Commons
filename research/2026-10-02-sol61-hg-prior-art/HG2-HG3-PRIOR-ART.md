# HG2/HG3 prior-art subsumption attack

Date: 2026-10-02 UTC. Scope: a bounded primary-source review and hand-proof attack design. At original preparation, no biological computation, simulation, implementation, new numerical controls, uploads, or publication had occurred. Historical disclosure status: public release was then on hold; that hold was superseded by the authorized public-research/private-product separation before this 2026-10-02 publication.

## Decision first

- **HG2, stated as “what an observable summary preserves, and minimal augmentation restoring a target,” is too vague to be a theorem and is classical once interpreted as sufficiency or observational identifiability.** Its useful residue is a source-constrained, costed observation problem. The existing G4 pad-placement collision supplies such a problem; it does not make the generic theory new.
- **HG3, stated as “minimal lawful stochastic interfaces for contextual replacement,” is likewise classical at the abstract kernel/lumpability level.** G1 already gives a source-specific sufficient interface. Minimality still requires a precisely fixed admissible exterior/tester language. Importing arbitrary transition testers, readable latent registers, or process-copy operations would change the genealogy contract.
- The warranted new proof attack is narrowly on **G4 placement for the already known fixed bigon B\***. A spectral-centralizer argument can upgrade its isolated five-root separator to uniform placement identifiability at exactly root cap five, with B\* and total ordinary duration known. This is an application of classical finite-dimensional algebra, not a new generalization theory.
- For **G1/G2**, prioritize source-faithful formalization of the already scoped conditional-kernel/grafting and projection proofs. A generic “minimal interface” paper currently would not advance their scientific endpoints.

## 1. Primary theorems actually inspected

### A. Sufficiency and minimality are already developed at the kernel level

Tobias Fritz, *A synthetic approach to Markov kernels, conditional independence and theorems on sufficient statistics* (2020), [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.07021v8).

Theorem 14.5, under strict positivity of the Markov category, characterizes sufficiency of a deterministic statistic s for a model p by a reconstruction kernel alpha satisfying alpha s p=p and s alpha=id almost surely under sp. Example 14.6 explicitly recovers finite Fisher–Neyman factorization. Theorem 16.3 says that a complete sufficient statistic is minimal **assuming the model already has a minimal sufficient statistic**; it is not an unconditional existence theorem. Definition 2.1 and Example 2.5 already provide stochastic composition, tensor product, copying, and discarding. Section 10 makes clear that stochastic maps generally do not commute with copying.

This subsumes an HG formulated as lossless probabilistic compression with reconstruction. It does **not** automatically solve recovery of a structural target from a collection of exact population-law coordinates. That is an identifiability/fiber question, often weaker than statistical sufficiency.

### B. Universal transition-preserving aggregation has an exact classical criterion

John G. Kemeny and J. Laurie Snell, *Finite Markov Chains*, §6.3, [primary chapter scan](https://math.pku.edu.cn/teachers/yaoy/Fall2011/Kemeny-Snell_Chapter6.3-4.pdf).

Definition 6.3.1 requires the projected chain to be Markov for **every initial distribution**, with the same transition matrix independent of that distribution. Theorem 6.3.2 gives a necessary and sufficient condition: transition mass from a state into each destination block must be constant over every source block. These block totals form the reduced transition matrix. This is strong lumpability, not a conclusion from equality of one initial law or one endpoint observation.

The G2 generator and jump intertwining criterion is a source-specific instance of this universal transition-preservation principle. The biological proofs that selected-label projection preserves actual merger rates and correct routing coins remain essential.

### C. Test-equivalence requires a specified test language

Kim G. Larsen and Arne Skou, *Bisimulation Through Probabilistic Testing*, **1989 preliminary report**, [primary scanned report](https://homes.cs.aau.dk/~srba/courses/FS-07/probabilistic_bisimulation.pdf). The final 1991 journal article is [publisher-verified](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0890540191900306), but the numbered statements below are from the directly inspected preliminary report.

Definition 6.3 requires equality of action-indexed transition probabilities into equivalence classes. Theorem 6.4 equates probabilistic bisimilarity with agreement on all probabilistic modal formulas, under the report's minimum-probability premise. Theorem 6.5 equates it with equality of observation distributions for every test in its language, under the same premise; the preliminary report states this theorem without proof. The language permits taking multiple process copies during a test.

This defeats an unqualified claim to have invented “all-context probabilistic equivalence.” It does **not** prove that a restricted genealogy observation menu is complete, and copying an internal process state is not licensed by having multiple sampled taxa/copies.

### D. Finite automaton observability already has finite linear bounds

Stefan Kiefer, Andrzej S. Murawski, Joël Ouaknine, Björn Wachter, and James Worrell, *On the Complexity of Equivalence and Minimisation for Q-Weighted Automata* (2013), [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1302.2818).

Proposition 3.1 states, for an n-state weighted automaton over any field, that reachable row spans are generated by words of length below n; a nonzero automaton has a distinguishing word of length at most n−1. Applying the difference construction gives the usual dimension-dependent finite equivalence test. Tzeng's [1992 original publisher abstract](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0221017) independently verifies a polynomial-time probabilistic-automaton equivalence algorithm, but its full theorem proof was not inspected here.

Word length in a **fixed finite representation** is not entering-root count in G4. A genealogical state space grows with the root cap. Without a uniform bounded representation or a source-family rank bound, this does not yield all-copy stopping.

### E. Candidates not promoted to certified subsumption claims

Blackwell's 1953 *Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments* and Rogers–Pitman's 1981 *Markov Functions* were bibliographically located at their original publisher, but attempted original full-text retrieval returned bot/interstitial HTML. No exact theorem scope from those originals is asserted as independently checked in this review. Fritz supplies the directly inspected reconstruction-sufficiency result needed here; Kemeny–Snell supplies the directly inspected all-initial-state aggregation criterion. Merely invoking “Blackwell” or “intertwining” would add no verified source-specific conclusion.

## 2. HG2: distinguish the generic wrapper from the source question

Let O(theta) be the chosen vector of observable laws and tau(theta) the target. Saying O identifies tau exactly means tau is constant on O's fibers. That formulation is useful bookkeeping but is not a substantive new theorem. “Minimal additional observables” also lacks a meaning until one fixes the allowed menu and its cost: number of scalar coordinates, maximum entering-root cap, total sampled copies, intervention count, or experimental/statistical cost give different minima. Arbitrary encodings can trivialize coordinate-count minimality.

The actual G4 source is much more precise. In `g4-allcopy-2237z/FOUR-ROOT-PLACEMENT.md`, §§6–7:

1. For K=E(a) B E(b), the four-root coordinates give b=C(K)/(C(K)+H(K)) whenever C(B) is nonzero. This is already an exact source-specific recovery result.
2. For B\*=B_ind(2/3,1/3,1/2), C(B\*)=H(B\*)=0. With ab fixed, **every** no-merger diagonal and every complete labelled forest kernel through four roots is unchanged by redistributing the ordinary padding.
3. The exhibited positive pad swap E(1/3) B\* E(1/2) versus E(1/2) B\* E(1/3) differs at five. One labelled-forest difference is −207959/37027067535360; an admitted positive completion gives an actual rooted-topology difference. Public source provenance: commit `3d755ef391066069d33ff4490da65f00742b7ea7`.

These facts show that all-copy no-merger information fails to preserve placement, even where it preserves total ordinary duration; cap-four full forest information also fails on this stratum. They do not establish a generic sufficiency theorem, arbitrary-chain stopping, or recovery with unknown B.

### The next proof attack: exact root-cap-five placement for fixed B\*

Fix the source B\* and total ordinary coalescent duration T>0. Parameterize strictly positive leading and trailing padding by u in (0,T). On the finite space of complete rooted forests with a fixed set of at most five labels, let Q be the ordinary Kingman forest generator and B the graft-lifted B\* operator. Then

K_u=exp(uQ) B exp((T−u)Q).

Attack the following statement: complete labelled forest data through five entering roots identifies u, and no root cap below five does.

The hand proof route is short and requires no new enumeration:

- Order states by number of live roots r. Q has diagonal block −binom(r,2) I and only strictly root-count-decreasing off-diagonal jumps. Its distinct diagonal-block values imply a square-free minimal polynomial, hence diagonalizability, despite within-block multiplicities.
- If K_u=K_v, ordinary-semigroup invertibility gives exp((u−v)Q) B=B exp((u−v)Q). This is algebraic cancellation, not a negative-duration biological experiment.
- For u≠v, all real spectral values exp(−(u−v)binom(r,2)) are distinct. Polynomial interpolation recovers Q from exp((u−v)Q), so that commutation would imply QB=BQ.
- Equality of fresh-input forest kernels at all k≤5 extends to equality of the full forest operators by the already admitted subtree graft-substitution rule.
- The existing five-root positive pad-swap witness therefore certifies QB≠BQ at cap five. All distinct u must differ there. The four-root quotient proves uniform blindness below five.

The independent G4 worker checked the centralizer argument and graft-extension premise. Before marking it accepted, write and review the complete finite-forest operator lemma and attach the existing pinned source witness. This advances a narrow G4 inverse problem. It does not identify a minimal single observable, unknown bigon parameters, arbitrary same-L products, or G3 boundary completeness.

## 3. HG3: what remains after the classical attack

The fresh G1/G2 review in `sol61-head-audit/checkpoint/G1-G2-FRESH-SOURCE-REVIEW.md` already supplies a sufficient conditional boundary state: carried rooted subtrees/live tokens, relevant exterior/root history, and every shared/exposed register retained with its conditional kernel. Equality of those conditional kernels is preserved by stochastic composition. This is ordinary conditional-law composition once the correct state is present.

Two local compiled controls diagnose why weakening the state is unsound:

- `G1SharedRegisterStress.lean`: a retained fair register with conditional survival 1/2 and 1/4 has joint event mass 1/4; independently replacing its conditional kernel by the marginal gives 3/16. The generic discrepancy is p(1−p)(x0−x1). This is an exposed-register experiment, outside passive latent-register gene-tree observation.
- `G2LiveLineageRouting.lean`: actual original hybrid/parent IDs and the live-ancestor map are retained. A pulse factors through current live ancestors; coalesced copies cannot split. Independently pulsing original copy labels splits one merged ancestor with probability 2p(1−p), rather than zero.

Both controls enforce already accepted G1/G2 premises. Neither refutes the accepted conditional-register replacement or live-lineage compiler.

**The narrow remaining interface question** is: under exactly the admitted positive biological exterior/completion and original-intervention menu, which reachable boundary states have indistinguishable continuation laws? Is the complete unranked forest plus the currently declared shared state minimal, or does that restricted menu admit a smaller quotient? Answering this requires separating contexts that are themselves admissible sources. Abstract indicator tests, readable hidden state, process cloning, and arbitrary transition controllers cannot establish necessity unless explicitly in the contract.

This could strengthen G1 by a genuinely source-specific minimality theorem, but presently no such separating-context theorem is available from the checked citations. It has no demonstrated effect on G3 global closure or G4 stopping. The justified near-term G1/G2 proof lane is therefore to formalize the original source transition state, the conditional grafting bridge, and selected-label generator/jump identities while retaining root semantics, routing mode, shared registers, and original intervention IDs. Calling this a new “lawful interface calculus” would overstate the advance.
