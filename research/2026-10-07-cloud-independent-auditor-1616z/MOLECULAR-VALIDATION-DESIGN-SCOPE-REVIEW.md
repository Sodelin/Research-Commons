One precision point: PSI and usage **levels** lie in[0,1]; their signed changes can lie in[-1,1]. The plan/protocol's phrase “MAE of delta PSI on[0,1]” must mean differences measured in original fraction units, without truncating negative changes or taking unsigned effects. This is a documentation clarification, not an observed implementation defect. Root was notified before this receipt.
