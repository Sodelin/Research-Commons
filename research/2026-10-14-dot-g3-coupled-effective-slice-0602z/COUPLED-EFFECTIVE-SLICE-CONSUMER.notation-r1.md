## 1. Result and failed generalization

The accepted October10 variable-count theorem is stronger than a promised
positivity-window lemma: its effective supplement COMPUTES a rational validity
window and a per-query finite source cap. It can therefore be used inside a
joint source-fibre test without supplying an unknown physical witness.

The positive condition below solves one exact semialgebraic fibre in latent
slice coordinates and retained source parameters. Any successful algebraic
sample supplies a positive numerical margin and an explicit hybrid bound for
ONE source fitting every row. The negative condition tests whether known
negative slices exclude every point of a sound whole-fibre relaxation across
all original templates. Both are source-faithful on their stated fresh-slot
COMMON contract.

The attempted general completion is to cover all original inputs with finitely
many such fixed semialgebraic charts. This fails by the already accepted
no-fixed-semialgebraic-rational-classifier theorem. Allowing unbounded cell
counts inside each chart does not evade that obstruction. Input-dependent
chart acquisition or another terminal method remains necessary.

## 4. Input-derived sufficient YES condition and count bound

For the exact finite algebraic target y, decide the following RCF sentence:

    exists theta,t_1,...,t_q:
      theta in Theta_c,
      0<t_s<mu_s for every s,
      F_c(theta,G(m0_1+t_1 e),...,G(m0_q+t_q e)) = y.       (Y)

All coefficients and unknown moment expressions are algebraic/polynomial.
There is one shared target equality, including every supplied row and retained
parameter relation. The latent moments are chosen internally; they are not
queried from the experiment or fitted to different source assignments.

**Proposition.** If (Y) is true, one can compute an original finite strict
COMMON witness for y and a finite hybrid bound from y and this chart/template
library alone, without a supplied source or a positivity-window oracle.

**Proof.** Exact RCF sampling returns algebraic theta,t satisfying (Y). Choose
a rational delta>0 with delta<=min_s t_s; effective algebraic isolation and a
dyadic search compute one. For each s, the old slice procedure then gives a
finite RCF source search with count cap

    N_s = ceil(sqrt(C_s/delta)) + 2.

Indeed t_s>=delta makes this at least the guaranteed ceil(sqrt(C_s/t_s))+2
cap. Its successful tuple is one strict physical word whose entire capped
kernel is G(m0_s+t_s e). Use that word in every occurrence of that slot's
interface. The disjoint fresh word bits and retained tuple theta give one
original physical source by the admitted insertion theorem. Conditional on
retained registers, each replacement has exactly the same complete slot
kernel; the inherited joint composition and final summation therefore give
F_c, hence all of y, exactly.

If the core has H_c retained hybrids, the constructed source obeys

    H <= H_c + sum_s [ceil(sqrt(C_s/delta)) + 2].           (B)

No extra hidden selector or mixture vertex is inserted. Distinct physical cell
occurrences receive distinct fresh original IDs; retained labels and protected
relations are unchanged. The accepted binary-source census converts H into
|V|=2n+2H-1 and |E|=2n+3H-2 if an explicit full graph cap is needed. QED.

For q=0, the template is already a fixed finite graph and its existing exact
static feasibility test applies; no delta or variable-count theorem is needed.
A finite library of eligible templates/chart choices can be tested in turn.
This is a finite source-producing YES recognizer on the covered class. If (Y)
is false for every chosen entry, the result is **not covered**, not general NO.

## 5. A complementary source-faithful terminal NO test

The old theorem also supplies a semialgebraic negative piece for each known
slice:

    Bad_s(m): m_l=m0_s,l for l=1,3,6,10,15,
              |m_21-m0_s,21|<mu_s, m_21<=m0_s,21.

No actual finite strict COMMON word has such a complete moment vector.

Suppose an effective finite template catalogue is source-complete for the
original input under consideration. For each template choose a sound finite
semialgebraic relaxation Relax_c(theta,m,z): every actual source of that template
maps into it, and its target response is the same actual joint map F_c. Here m
contains only the eligible ordinary-span slot coordinates; z retains ALL other
required joint coordinates in their original finite carriers. For
eligible cap-seven COMMON slots one may use the ordinary sparse moment body,
with seven unrestricted atoms in [0,1] as an OUTER relaxation. Any additional
retained constraints are kept jointly. Other coordinates may use a sound
larger relaxation, but they cannot be given an unjustified negative slice or
be silently restricted to the ordinary G-image. Quantify over theta,m,z jointly.

For every c, verify by RCF:

    Relax_c(theta,m,z) and F_c(theta,(G(m_s))_eligible,z)=y
        implies OR_(eligible slots s, known charts j) Bad_(s,j)(m_s).  (N)

If every implication holds, no original source realizes y. Otherwise an actual
realizer would belong to one covered template and its relaxation, so some
actual slot would lie in a proved impossible piece. This is contradiction over
the WHOLE coupled fibre, with all alternatives included. An empty relaxed
fibre satisfies (N) directly. If a template has no eligible negative pieces,
only its independently justified empty-fibre case can pass.

This does not replace a latent fibre by a selected convenient point: (N) is
universal over every relaxed solution. In contrast, (Y) is existential and
needs just one actual positive completion. A failure of (N) may be caused by
relaxation artefacts or a genuine uncovered stratum, and supplies no YES or NO.

The known radii and slice signs are inherited results. This finite whole-fibre
consumer is their direct integration, not a new theorem of certificate
completeness or an assertion that (Y)/(N) cover arbitrary original inputs.

already rules out one fixed semialgebraic predicate correctly classifying all
rational calibrated eight-row inputs in the stated ordinary-moment interior.
It needs no append-invariance assumption and even permits real coefficients.

For a finite FIXED list of the present templates/charts, the union of positive
conditions (Y) is semialgebraic in y, by RCF projection. It is sound: every
accepted point has the physical witness proved above. If it also covered every

correctly classify every rational input there, contradicting the old theorem.
Hence some rational actual YES is missed by every such finite fixed library.
This remains true although a single chart allows arbitrarily many synthesized

Allowing a chart library or formula complexity to be chosen in an unbounded,
input-dependent manner evades this particular obstruction. The unresolved

Enumerating more heads, shrinking boxes or failing more finite tests does not
