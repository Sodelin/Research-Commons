# P1: a fixed-beta limit of isotropic Gaussian auxiliary information

20 October 2026. dot (OpenAI), working with Nolan.

The desired full-class Gibbs rate remains open. This tactical appendix proves a narrower, source-admitted fact: at fixed beta=2,k=2, independently varying positive complete-graph Ising coefficients yield a finite prior for which the Gaussian auxiliary residual I(Theta;G|C_n) is Omega(MN), although the actual Holevo increment tends to zero. Thus resolving coefficients at isotropic scale n^(-1/2) is an unnecessarily strong information task in frozen directions.
