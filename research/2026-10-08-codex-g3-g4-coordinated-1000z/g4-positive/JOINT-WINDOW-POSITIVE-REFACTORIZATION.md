## 2. The clock-redistribution identity

Fix a finite signed expression in G_m,

    W = E_(t0) B1 E_(t1) ... BL E_(tL),      L>=1,
    T = sum_(j=0)^L t_j > 0,
    tau_i = sum_(j=0)^(i-1) t_j.

The B_i are genuine strict bare INDEPENDENT cells; the t_j may have either sign. A signed expression is not itself an admitted source.

Choose numbers p_i,q_i>0 for i=1,...,L and u_j>0 for j=0,...,L, with

    sum_i(p_i+q_i) + sum_j u_j = T.                         (1)

For example set all 3L+1 numbers equal to T/(3L+1). Define the effective ordinary gaps

    s0 = u0+p1,
    s_j = q_j+u_j+p_(j+1),       1<=j<L,
    s_L = q_L+u_L,
    sigma_i = sum_(j=0)^(i-1) s_j,
    a_i = tau_i-sigma_i,
    C_i = E_(a_i) B_i E_(-a_i),
    P_i = E_(p_i) C_i E_(q_i).                             (2)

Then, exactly in the original graft/source group,

    W = E_(u0) P1 E_(u1) ... PL E_(uL).                    (3)

**Proof.** In any group the ordinary subgroup gives

    E_(t0) B1 ... BL E_(tL)
       = product_(i=1)^L [E_(tau_i) B_i E_(-tau_i)] E_T,   (4)

where cell order is retained. The right side of (3), with (2) expanded, has the same form with effective clock positions sigma_i and cells C_i. Since

    E_(sigma_i) C_i E_(-sigma_i)
       = E_(tau_i) B_i E_(-tau_i)

and sum s_j=T by (1), the two products are equal. This proof also fixes the conjugation orientation: these are **prefix** ordinary clocks, not the suffix normalization used for a projective residual. No ordinary factor is commuted through an unchanged bigon. QED.
