# Complete-joint source attainment by a boundary degree certificate

Contributor: Codex correspondence lane, 8 October 2026. Hand-derived extension of COUPLED-FINITE-PIVOT; independent review pending. Brouwer degree, homotopy invariance and Poincaré–Miranda are classical reused results. This does not close original G3.

## 1. Same original source presentation

Fix one admitted retained core, original IDs/parent labels, control/program/register tuple, mode and finite declared response profile. Take finitely many legal physical pivot coordinates u in R^q and an open bounded box B whose closed box is wholly inside their strict admitted domain. Original parameter ties must be implemented by the actual legal coordinates; they are never independently freed.

Freeze all other actual source parameters and intervening slot pieces in a **source-faithful closure presentation**, as in the inherited finite-pivot theorem. Let G on the closed box be its complete joint response. Let G_N replace every frozen closure piece by a finite strict physical word and every permitted core-face parameter by a coherent strict approximant. All occurrences of a shared piece/parameter/register use the SAME replacement. Each G_N is the response of one fixed finite positive admitted source shape at one parameter tuple, simultaneously across every row. No independent response-row approximants or externally mixed sources are admitted.

The inherited polynomial compiler is multilinear in finitely many frozen kernel blocks and has bounded degree in these fixed pivot coordinates. Convergence of those original blocks therefore gives uniform C0 convergence G_N to G on the closed box. This uses no inverse of a kernel and no regularity of a frozen block. It is the same source approximation contract as the prior finite-pivot theorem, with a weaker convergence requirement.

## 2. Degree theorem

**Claim.** If p is absent from G(boundary B) and deg(G,B,p) is nonzero, then p has one finite strict admitted source realization with the complete supplied response.

**Proof.** The compact boundary has positive separation delta from p. For all sufficiently large N, sup_closedB ||G_N−G|| is less than delta. The straight homotopy H_t=(1−t)G+tG_N avoids p on the entire boundary by the triangle inequality. Hence deg(G_N,B,p)=deg(G,B,p) is nonzero. The degree existence property gives u_N in B with G_N(u_N)=p. By construction this is one actual finite strict source, with every original tie and all response rows retained. QED.

For rational/effectively algebraic p and a finite source with supplied effectively algebraic fixed coefficients, its finite polynomial equalities/strict inequalities have a real solution and therefore an effectively real-algebraic solution by the inherited RCF transfer. An exact finite-source RCF solver can recover that witness. This does not create an arbitrary-real equality oracle or bound graph size without an effective approximation/margin premise.

The theorem need not assume DG onto, or even a nonzero derivative at the target. Section 2 of the inherited provider follows by its nonsingular local degree; this extension also admits odd higher-order source singularities. Section 3's redundant-equation remedy remains mandatory: if response coordinates have exact identities, choose a proved smooth local image variety with an injective chart, and prove G and every G_N stay in it. Apply degree in that chart and restore **every** response equality through its injectivity. A numerical rank drop or an unproved choice of coordinates cannot discard equations.

## 3. Finite algebraic face certificate and conditional word bound

A directly checkable sufficient certificate is a rational/algebraic strict box with, after an allowed orientation of each coordinate,

    G_i(u)−p_i < −delta  on the lower i-face,
    G_i(u)−p_i >  delta  on the upper i-face,

for one positive rational delta and every i. Poincaré–Miranda gives nonzero degree (equivalently the map is homotopic on the boundary to an oriented box map). Quantification is over the entire faces, not a finite vertex sample. When G is an actual polynomial finite-source map, exact real-algebraic methods can check all these inequalities and strict source admission. The resulting certificate proves an actual finite YES without Jacobian inversion.

When G is only a closure presentation, a supplied coherent finite-source approximation procedure with a proved uniform error e(N)→0 supplies a conditional input-dependent finite bound: choose any computable N0 with e(N0)<delta; retain its one finite shape and solve the full polynomial target system in B. The number of hybrids in that shape is a valid bound for ONE witness of p. No property of a dense net alone supplies this e(N), a source-faithful pivot presentation, the full joint face signs, or delta from an arbitrary G3 input.

For general nonzero degree without Miranda face signs, the same existence proof holds. A finite exact algebraic degree computation or another checked boundary homotopy is an additional certificate obligation. No such general degree algorithm is implemented here.

## 4. Master implication and failure boundary

This is an exact positive whole-response criterion. It can replace the regular finite-pivot step when the actual response has odd singular local degree, as demonstrated by SOURCE-INSTANCE-AND-GLOBAL-GATE. It is neither a source count theorem nor a necessary condition for realization: that note also gives an actual algebraic YES source with degree zero on its chart.

A proposed global algorithm would have to find an admitted nonzero-degree presentation for every relevant positive fibre **or** provide a separate complete exclusion criterion for every remaining NO fibre, considering all admitted cores. No such coverage is proved. Outer semialgebraic invariant/closure contact cannot supply it, and local degree zero cannot remove an alternative source. The unresolved original coupled word problem is therefore unchanged outside the newly certified positive case.
