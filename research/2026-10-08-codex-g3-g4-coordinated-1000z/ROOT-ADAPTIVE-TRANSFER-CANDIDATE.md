## Source and query assumptions

Fix an admitted target `T` and the declared private-box interface. For each integer `m>=2`, suppose an admitted positive finite `W_m` has one physical parameter assignment supplying the complete labelled capped kernel of `T` for all entering-root counts at most `m`, and is not all-copy equivalent to `T`. Substitution leaves the same positive exterior wrapper, original labels, root/LSA, allowed IDs and controls. There are no newly observable controls on the private cells and no private random register crossing the box boundary.

Every admitted row in this interface has a finite entering-root budget `b(e)`. For a row sampling finitely many initial copies, their number is a valid conservative budget: chronological mergers and current-root routing do not duplicate an existing genealogy. A legal finite randomized control program is evaluated with its entire joint configuration distribution. Conditioning on an external configuration for the proof is not permission to observe a deterministic summand.

The observer uses the same source-independent policy on every source. At each round it chooses an admitted complete row before a fresh independent locus, or requests an exact legal response; the latter returns a deterministic response value. All its randomization and finite response/policy kernels are measurable in the declared spaces. No hidden routing history or source-description oracle is available. Its query may choose freely varying legal exterior parameters; the source kernel equality holds for **every** such legal row with `b(e)<=m`, not merely finitely many preselected values.

## Lemma 1: uniform legal-row transfer

Under the source assumptions, `T` and `W_m` give the same response law for every admitted row whose entering-root budget is at most `m`.

Proof. Condition in the calculation on the complete entering labelled genealogy forest and on any external configuration used by the actual joint source factorization. Treat each currently surviving subtree as one opaque token. The private INDEPENDENT component uses its independent driving randomness and acts by its capped forest kernel. The two kernels agree for every possible entering token count up to `m`. Joining the unchanged exterior tensor and summing shared external variables therefore gives the same final authorized topology/channel law. All summands use the same source tuple; no per-coordinate refit occurs. This argument applies pointwise to legal exterior parameters. It does not condition on a private register shared with the exterior, because that is explicitly outside this private interface. QED.

## Lemma 2: finite adaptive truncation

For integers `N,m`, modify the policy to suspend immediately before any row with `b(e)>m`, and after `N` query rounds. Then every finite transcript law of this suspended policy is identical under `T` and `W_m`.

Proof. At round zero, the same internal randomization law is used. Given the same finite past, the policy selects its next action by the same stochastic kernel. For an over-budget or terminal action, the rule is identical on both sources. For an admitted in-budget row, Lemma 1 supplies the same next-response kernel, including a point-mass exact response when that is the chosen input contract. Integrating these common kernels proves the induction. Continuous randomization over legal exterior parameters needs no finite set of branches: Lemma 1 is uniform over every in-budget row. Neither infinite transcript measures nor a target-description oracle enter this finite induction. QED.

## Conditional soundness obstruction

Let `A_(N,m)` be the event that the original policy has declared all-copy equivalence to `T` by at most `N` query rounds, before any row with budget greater than `m`. This event can be evaluated by the suspended policy. Suppose a proposed certifier is uniformly `alpha`-sound on every admitted source not equivalent to `T`: its probability of making that declaration in any such finite event is at most `alpha`, where `0<=alpha<1`.

For each `N,m`, choose the one source `W_m` supplied by the source premise. Lemma 2 gives

    P_T(A_(N,m)) = P_(W_m)(A_(N,m)) <= alpha.

Consequently the operational finite-declaration probability satisfies

    sup_(N,m) P_T(A_(N,m)) <= alpha.

It cannot simultaneously exceed `alpha` at the target. In particular, uniformly zero-error certification cannot make a positive-probability finite declaration at `T` under the stated premise. If an actual infinite execution probability space and the relevant measurable terminal event are supplied, every finite terminating execution uses finitely many finite-budget rows. Its declaration event is the increasing countable union of `A_(j,j)` for sufficiently large `j`; continuity from below then gives the same bound for the genuine terminal probability. This last interpretation is conditional on that probability space, rather than a claim that this note constructed it.

The rivals may vary with `m`. Uniform soundness supplies the same upper bound before taking the supremum; no single infinite rival or cap-dependent target is used. This targets full response-law equivalence. The existing private-chain replicas have the same displayed species-tree target and do **not** thereby obstruct a task that asks only for that weaker biological target.

## Remaining master implication and failure boundaries

The substantive missing premise is still an actual fixed positive `T` with complete capped rivals at every `m` and later legal inequivalence. Signed-time words, diagonal replicas, approximate matches or exterior padding alone do not meet it. The argument does not prove that this premise holds, does not give a witness bound or inverse radius, and does not settle general G3 or original G4.

A positive finite-determination result restricted to private chains would still require the original all-rival and effectivity bridges. A negative construction in an admitted private subcase can refute the universal master only after its full declared interface embedding is checked; it cannot silently omit available internal original-ID interventions. The present lemmas preserve the already declared private interface and do not expand its observation menu.
