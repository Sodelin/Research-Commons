## Fixed public type, observation, target and exterior

Use a one-output pendant component containing the original leaf A. It has no lower input port. Its hidden A stem is the already justified unmarked private serial chain type from the original tester sections 3 and 4: positive ordinary passages and independent-current-root bigons, including their positive connectors. There is no exposed internal hybrid actuator, protected internal parameter or shared register. The public leaf set below the sole output cut is exactly `{A}`. The public interface is obtained by attaching the leaf A to the child end of the original private two-port type and sealing that child port; it does not expose the hidden word as a new fixed finite internal template.

A legal exterior connects the sole output to an original cut bridge and contains the other original leaves B,C,D, so the completed source has at least four taxa. It is accepted only when both compared substitutions satisfy the original finite binary, rooted-LSA, outer-labelled planar and cut-child checks. The exterior may have arbitrary permitted finite complexity, positive original parameters, original exterior controls and finite joint randomized forcing programs. It contains the component once. No exterior lineage can enter the sealed component from another leaf: its only connection is the output cut, and backward ancestry follows directed parent arcs toward that output. The child-side A labels cannot be replaced by an arbitrary mixed-label entering forest. This last typing condition is essential.

For an allocation with k A copies and at least k B copies, label the selected copies `A_1,...,A_k,B_1,...,B_k`. Delete the other sampled labels from the final rooted labelled unranked genealogy and suppress unary vertices. Let `T_k` be a fixed binary comb of the k designated cherries `(A_i,B_i)`. The declared output is one bit:

```
success iff the restricted genealogy equals T_k;
failure otherwise.
```

If fewer than k B copies are sampled, output failure. For k=0 choose a fixed constant output. This is one fixed rule on the observable final genealogy at every finite allocation. It is a deterministic finite coarsening with no hidden forest, routing bit, metric time, independently sampled counterfactual or signed observable coefficient. The rule and original label ordering do not depend on the unknown word, the tested copy cap, or a fitted source parameter. No other topology outcome or coarsening is authorized in this channel-specific experiment.

Fix once `q=1/2` and the target A stem `K*=E(q)`. Put it in the fixed positive four-taxon tree `((A,B),(C,D))`, with every other natural survival, for example, `1/2`, and the standard unbounded ordinary completion above its retained root. This is a finite zero-bigon target with positive finite populations. Other legal exteriors are experiments on the same fixed hidden component, not a change of that component's graph or physical parameter.

## Actual all-context factorization

For any admitted private stem K, write `b_k(K)` for its no-merger probability on the k fresh A copies. For every legal exterior/control row e with the indicated allocation there is a nonnegative constant `c(e,k)`, independent of K, such that

```
P_K(success under e) = c(e,k) b_k(K).                 (1)
```

Proof. Every genealogy subtree formed inside the sealed component contains only A labels. If any two of its current A roots merge, an A-only clade containing at least two selected A labels is formed. Opaque grafting retains that clade in the final genealogy. Deleting the other taxon labels does not remove its two A daughter subtrees, so it remains after restriction/unary suppression. It is incompatible with `T_k`, whose every A leaf has its designated single B leaf as sibling. The final success event therefore implies no component merger.

On that event the output forest of the component is exactly the k labelled singleton A roots, with the same port/calendar boundary as specified by the completion. Its probability is `b_k(K)`. All internal routing outcomes that preserve these singletons are summed; their private bits are neither observed nor joined to an exterior register. Conditional on that output the exterior law is the same for every K. Its probability of `T_k` is `c(e,k)`, proving (1). This is conditioning in the proof of a final-topology event, not a separately authorized hidden preparation or readout.

Exterior joint forcing programs retain their whole configuration distribution. Conditional on each configuration, the same factorization holds because none of its controls acts inside this private component. Averaging yields (1) with the averaged exterior constant. If a finite randomized row also chooses from finitely many allocations, its success law is the corresponding finite sum of `c(e,k)b_k(K)`; one shared K supplies every term. If the original registry instead exposes internal component IDs or a common crossing register, this proof does not apply and those rows cannot be deleted.

For k>=1 and sufficient B copies, `c(e,k)>0` for every ordinary admitted complete exterior row. There is a positive-probability history with no exterior coalescence on any finite population edge and any one allowed finite routing/control configuration of positive mass. All selected roots reach the retained root and then have a positive ordinary probability of `T_k`. Lineage conservation, finite strictly positive edge lengths, and the stipulated unbounded ordinary ancestral completion are used here. In any event, only one exterior with positive c is needed for the later separator.

In the fixed tree exterior, restrict to A,B. Until their meeting, the B pendant has survival `r=1/2`. Conditional on no B-only merger, ordinary completion on 2k distinct roots gives

```
c(e0,k)=r^C(k,2) kappa_k > 0,
kappa_k = 2^(3k-2) (k-1)! / ((2k)! (2k-1)!).          (2)
```

To derive the second formula, a comb of k labelled cherries has `2^(k-1)(k-1)!` compatible merger rankings. Each has probability `1/product_(j=2)^(2k) C(j,2)=2^(2k-1)/((2k)!(2k-1)!)` under the uniform pair-merger chain. All pre-meeting A mergers are already excluded by the same event. Other original leaves are removed by selected-label projectivity. This is the original crossed-cherry observation bridge with an explicit fixed topology choice.

## One fixed target, exact rivals for every finite prefix

For each finite m>=3, the accepted [uniform-time diagonal theorem](../../2026-10-04-dot-g3-structure-followons-2200z/uniform-diagonal/UNIFORM-TIME-DIAGONAL-FINAL.md), sections 1 and 4–6, supplies an actual nonempty finite strict independent private word `W_m` with

```
b_k(W_m)=q^C(k,2)=b_k(K*)    for 0<=k<=m.             (3)
```

The newer [fixed-interior-coin short-arm theorem](../../2026-10-08-dot-g4-fixed-coin-short-arms-1040z/FIXED-COIN-SHORT-ARM-DIAGONALS-AND-STRONG-SATURATION-CANDIDATE.md), sections 1 and 5, permits every new cell coin to be exactly `1/2`. Its cell count is finite and may depend on m. The pre-padding family has total pair hazard tending to zero; choose a strictly positive member with hazard below `-log q`, then add a strictly positive ordinary pad for the missing hazard. Thus q is the same fixed target at every cap, not a cap-dependent number tending to zero. Every realizing arm, leading edge and connector remains strictly positive and finite. No realizing graph uses an endpoint coin or a zero/infinite population.

Place `W_m` on the same A stem inside the sealed component. Inserting its finite positive parallel bigons on that cut bridge preserves all original binary degrees, the cut-child property, the outer-labelled embedding and the existing LSA root. The sole boundary, leaf A, wrapper and public original exterior ID map remain unchanged. If boundary calendar ages are fixed as part of the type, subdivide their positive span into the finitely many private epochs and choose finite positive pair rates to give the specified natural coalescent lengths; no metric observation is added. One fixed parameter tuple of `W_m` supplies every arity and row.

Let P be ANY finite prefix of legal responses in this declared channel menu. It contains finitely many finite allocations/configuration terms, hence a finite maximum k. Choose `m>=max(3,k)` and the one stem `W_m`. Equation (1) and (3) give exact equality of the whole observed bit distribution on every row in P, for every listed legal exterior. No independent source fit is made per completion or deterministic program summand. This covers the entire finite prefix, not selected fixed-wrapper probes alone.

The accepted [finite-chain count invariant](../../2026-10-01-sol61-g4-allcopy-2237z/CHAIN-COUNT-AND-STOPPING.md) gives for a fixed finite strict independent word with N genuine bigons

```
log b_k = -A k^2 + B k - (N/2) log k + O(1).
```

`N(W_m)>=1`, whereas `K*` has N=0. Therefore `W_m` differs from `K*` at some later finite no-merger coordinate k. Equation (2) converts that difference into a nonzero observed bit-probability difference in the fixed legal four-taxon exterior. The later experiment uses the same fixed declared channel, not a newly added hidden diagnostic. Natural real-algebraic survival/coin witnesses can be selected from the inherited finite polynomial existence theorem at rational q if exact encodings are wanted; their positive natural durations need not themselves be algebraic.

Hence after EVERY finite legal prefix of this channel experiment, an admitted finite strictly positive rival agrees exactly on the entire prefix and differs on a later legal experiment. The rival varies with the prefix; the target and observation rule do not.

## Adaptive/effective distinction and exact limit

A deterministic exact-response algorithm that halts affirmatively at the target after finitely many adaptive queries has a finite transcript P. Its queries at each stage are determined by preceding exact responses. The same rival `W_m` produces the same responses, so induction gives the same queries and stopping decision, despite the later legal separator. Such a stop cannot certify all-future equality in this channel menu.

There is also a uniform randomized exact-oracle obstruction, without choosing a rival separately for a probability-zero seed. Give the algorithm the same source-independent random seed distribution on every source. Let `E_m` be the event that it answers "all-future equivalent" at the target after finitely many queries, every one with at most m entering A copies. These events increase and their union is the entire finite affirmative-halting event. On **every** seed in `E_m`, the fixed rival `W_m` gives exactly the same responses to every queried exterior, so induction follows the same transcript and gives the same affirmative answer. Hence the probability of a false affirmative answer under `W_m` is at least the target probability of `E_m`.

If finite affirmative-halting probability at the target is at least `1-alpha`, and false affirmative probability is required to be at most alpha for EVERY inequivalent rival, where `2alpha<1`, continuity from below supplies a finite m with `P_target(E_m)>alpha`. The corresponding one actual rival violates soundness. This covers zero-error almost-sure halting as alpha=0. It uses exact response access, the same seed law, ordinary measurability of the algorithm and finite queried copy counts; it does not invoke noisy samples, a changed observation channel or a hidden-source seed.

This is a complete negative branch for the **explicitly declared channel and sealed leaf type**, conditional on its source/type admission and the inherited accepted theorems. The previous restricted diagonal/cross-cherry result was already established; the new claim is its all-exterior transfer for this sealed component. It disproves a universal finite-determination claim across every allowed declared coarsening if that is the claim being considered. It leaves the original **full rooted-topology/forest menu** unresolved, because that menu may observe other outcomes at the same allocation. Section 1.2 permitting a channel does not authorize narrowing a registry that already permits the full topology. No main-master closure is asserted.
