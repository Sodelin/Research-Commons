# Why source placement does not turn the cherry-channel proof into the rich-menu counterexample

Contributor: Codex practical, full-shot G4 attempt 1, 8 October 2026. **HAND source-faithful failed-bridge argument; independent review pending.** This reuses the original arbitrary-arity tomography and the separately accepted fair-hazard obstruction. It does not prove all-word finite forcing.

## The original full topology retains every private stem forest coordinate

Keep the sealed one-output A component of [the channel candidate](SEALED-PENDANT-CHANNEL-COUNTEREXAMPLE.md), but restore the original full rooted labelled unranked topology observation. At finite k, let `F_k` be all rooted binary labelled forests on the k A copies. The source component starts with those fresh copies and emits one forest with probabilities `K_k(f)`. Its internal mergers preserve prior subtrees as opaque tokens.

Use the original positive four-taxon tree exterior, one copy of B,C,D, and k of A. Above the component, provide a known strictly positive ordinary passage of survival u. After A meets B, selected-label restriction deletes C,D, leaving ordinary completion. For each `f in F_k`, construct `T_f` by attaching the components of f in a fixed order along the B-to-root spine. These are actual distinct rooted topology outcomes in this one legal experiment.

The original [triangular tomography proof](../../2026-10-01-g4-independent-bigon-1923z/TOMOGRAPHY-AND-COMPOSITION.md), section 2, gives its rational observation matrix `O[f,g]`. A nonzero entry requires every component of g to be a preserved clade inside a component of f. Thus `|g|>=|f|`; equal counts imply `g=f`. Ordering by root count makes O triangular with

```
O[f,f] = 1/product_(j=2)^(|f|+1) C(j,2) > 0.
```

The known ordinary passage has a triangular full-forest matrix `M_u` with diagonal `u^C(|f|,2)>0`. Consequently the full vector of these actual topology probabilities is an invertible linear transform of `K_k`. The sealed component requires no exterior lower input: its fresh forest law is what this transform reconstructs directly. Known positive lower padding in the original two-port proof is needed only if one additionally wants to separate that padding from the unknown inner chain; it is not needed to recover the entire sealed stem law.

It follows that a nonzero forest discrepancy at any finite k has a finite full-topology separator with k A copies and one each of B,C,D. The observable is the actual final labelled topology. Opaque history preservation is why subsequent pooling cannot erase an A-only merger clade. An arbitrarily long **finite** ordinary passage can make a discrepancy small, but its positive diagonal cannot make that transform singular. A zero survival/infinite finite-edge duration would change the original strictly positive finite source class. The unbounded ancestral completion at the retained root also does not erase histories: the tagged B-spine outcomes have the displayed positive triangular diagonal.

Thus the proposed source-side erasure is impossible in this original private pendant type. Under the full menu, diagonal equality is sufficient for the earlier special cherry bit only; it is not equality of all authorized topologies. This is an arbitrary-arity source argument, not an empirical finite-cap rank screen.

## A stronger collision of the two accepted construction/obstruction results

For a fixed m>=9, use the accepted [fixed-coin short-arm diagonal theorem](../../2026-10-08-dot-g4-fixed-coin-short-arms-1040z/FIXED-COIN-SHORT-ARM-DIAGONALS-AND-STRONG-SATURATION-CANDIDATE.md), with coin a=1/2. Its actual bare cells, before positive ordinary padding to q, have durations

```
X_i=2 h_i(epsilon) epsilon^2 - 4 u_i(epsilon) epsilon^3,
Y_i=2 h_i(epsilon) epsilon^2 + 4 u_i(epsilon) epsilon^3,
g_i=1/2.
```

The source theorem first chooses a finite N and a strict full-rank leading diagonal zero, then obtains analytic h,u in one compact neighborhood as epsilon varies. Its h values remain positive and below one. Consequently, for this fixed cap, there are fixed constants `0<hmin<=h_i<=hmax<4`, bounded u values, and one compact shape set. This is **exactly** the fair-parabolic source chart in dot's accepted [word-count-uniform hazard theorem](../../2026-10-08-dot-g4-fair-energy-hazard-1220z/WORD-COUNT-UNIFORM-FAIR-BIGON-HAZARD-BOUND-CANDIDATE.md), with the other chart controls v=w=0. It is independent-current-root routing, not common inheritance.

The diagonal word's total bare-cell pair hazard is

```
B(epsilon)=sum_i [-log b2(B_i)]
           =epsilon^2 sum_i h_i(epsilon)+O(epsilon^4) -> 0.
```

Positive connectors and the additional leading pad calibrate the same ordinary target `q=1/2`, with bounded total target hazard `H=-log q`. They do not change B. Choose a fixed `Hmax>H` for the fair-hazard theorem. That theorem supplies positive constants epsilon0,B0 for this compact source shape and Hmax, and excludes **full ordinary cap-nine forest equality** whenever epsilon<epsilon0 and B<=B0. It allows arbitrary finite word count and arbitrary collapsing ordinary gaps, so the clustered short connectors or large leading pad are not exceptions.

For every fixed m>=9, therefore, all sufficiently small members of this exact diagonal IFT family match `b2,...,bm` but **fail** full ordinary forest equality already through cap nine. The tomography above turns that failure into a legal full-topology discrepancy with at most nine A copies and one each of B,C,D, hence at most twelve total copies in this explicit exterior. The forest cap is a current-A-root cap, not a statement that nine total samples suffice. This is not an assumption that top matrix coordinates vanish from cap-eight equality: the obstruction is invoked only under the hypothetical full cap-nine equality that a rich-menu transfer would require.

This comparison explains a consequential failure of the strongest available diagonal construction as a full-master counterexample route. The family can be used for the declared cherry channel, while the full topology sees an actual discrepancy. No new numerical or source program was executed to establish this implication; it is a direct comparison of two accepted arbitrary-scope hand theorems with identical source conventions.

## What this does and does not settle

The new implication is restricted to the fixed-fair compact common-scale vanishing-bare-budget family. It does not exclude the older rare-arm/endpoint-coin construction, arbitrary nonzero bare budgets, multiscale or noncompact sources, or a different nonordinary target. At a larger epsilon outside the proved bounds, no conclusion about that branch is supplied. The constants may depend on m's chosen leading architecture; no uniform bound across caps is inferred.

For the main original negative resolution, actual rivals still have to match **every** complete forest coordinate through each chosen prefix at one fixed finite target. The channel-specific proof cannot supply that by relabelling its observable. The present private type cannot hide the discrepancy through source structure. A different genuine positive full-kernel replication mechanism or a target-specific finite-forcing proof is needed. Original full-menu G4 remains open.
