## 3. Strict algebraic construction

Let r_N be the unique positive algebraic root r_N^N=R, and put

    x_N = 1/(2r_N-1),
    u_N = a/x_N^N,
    s_N = u_N^(1/(2N)),
    z_N = sqrt(u_N).

Since r_N>1, we have 0<x_N<1. The identity

    r_N^2-(2r_N-1)=(r_N-1)^2>=0

gives x_N^N>=R^(-2)>a. Hence 0<u_N<1 and both s_N,z_N lie strictly in (0,1). Every coordinate is effectively real algebraic from the rational R,a and integer N. The coins are 1/2, so natural inheritance is interior. There is no zero-duration arm, connector or leading edge.

Take W_N=E(z_N)[B(x_N,x_N,1/2)E(s_N)]^N. Its COMMON coordinate is

    z_N s_N^N x_N^N = u_N x_N^N = a.

Moreover

    ((1+x_N)/2)/x_N = r_N,

so its INDEPENDENT coordinate is a r_N^N=aR. Because a<R^(-2), also 0<aR<1. The entire paired cap-two terminal is identical for all N.

For a literal rational terminal choose R=2,a=1/8. Then the paired ordinary survivals are 1/8 and 1/4. At N=1 the physical parameters are x=1/3, u=3/8, s=z=sqrt(3/8), g=1/2. This is a hand substitution, not a computer evaluation.
