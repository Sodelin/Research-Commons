## Exact rational-readout lemma

Let K be a fixed finite real diagonalizable matrix with positive eigenvalues. Let f be a rational function of its matrix entries, with real coefficients fixed independently of n and denominator nonzero for every sufficiently large integer n at K^n. Then there do not exist beta>0 and a nonconstant real polynomial P such that

    f(K^n)=P(n) beta^n

for every sufficiently large integer n.

Proof. Diagonalizing K gives each entry of K^n as a finite linear combination of positive-base exponentials b_j^n. Therefore every fixed polynomial in those entries has the form

    sum_r a_r rho_r^n,  rho_r>0,

after collecting equal bases. Write the numerator and denominator in this form. Cross-multiplying the proposed equality gives a finite identity

    sum_gamma Q_gamma(n) gamma^n=0,                      (1)

where gamma ranges over distinct positive bases, and each Q_gamma is a constant numerator coefficient minus P(n) times a denominator coefficient (with its base multiplied by beta).

Distinct positive-base exponential-polynomial sequences are linearly independent: if any Q is nonzero, choose the largest base with nonzero coefficient polynomial. Divide (1) by that base to the power n and by its leading polynomial power. Its nonzero leading coefficient remains, while every smaller-base term tends to zero exponentially. This contradicts (1) along all sufficiently large integers. Thus every Q_gamma is identically zero. Since P is nonconstant, each corresponding denominator coefficient must vanish. The denominator sequence is identically zero, contrary to the stated domain of f. QED.

The same proof works if the desired equality holds along an unbounded set of integer n: the dominance contradiction still applies. It does not require the eigenvalues or coefficients to be algebraic.

## Actual coupled source scope

A finite original source compiler applies fixed polynomial operations to capped kernel entries and static protected parameters. If the same fixed physical word is repeated n times while those parameters remain fixed, its finite programmed response is covered by the lemma. Allowing a defined rational normalization of observed kernel coordinates only enlarges that class. A tensor product, dual or rational algebraic group representation evaluated at K^n has entries rational in K^n; a fixed rational readout of that representation is also covered. Character clock normalization cannot manufacture a nonidentity unipotent counter through these operations.

This includes the literal scaled integer sequence n beta^n that supplies Bell's displayed Jordan gadget. A nonzero polynomial-times-exponential repetition signal cannot be recovered by these fixed nonlinear rational operations, even if the pair survival is left unobserved.

For a fixed bounded-language presentation K1^n1 ... Kr^nr, where each Ki is a fixed strict source word, matrix multiplication gives finite sums of pure multivariate exponentials; the different Ki need not commute or share eigenbases. A fixed rational readout cannot equal a nonconstant polynomial in the integers n1,...,nr times a fixed product of positive exponentials for all sufficiently large independent integer choices. Fix the other variables at values for which the polynomial depends nontrivially on one ni, and apply the one-variable lemma. Hence literal polynomial-value gadgets are not restored merely by finitely many protected blocks and coupled polynomial response operations.

No arbitrary tie among fresh positions is added to the original grammar. These conclusions concern a proposed simulation using fixed letters and a fixed finite bank, not the entire freely varying source fibre.
