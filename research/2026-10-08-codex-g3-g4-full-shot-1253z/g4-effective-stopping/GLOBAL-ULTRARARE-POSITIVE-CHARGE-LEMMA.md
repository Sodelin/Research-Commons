## 2. A uniform rare-coin sign, with an explicit margin

Proof. We can use the weaker safe bounds `g<=1/16`, `q>=15/16`, `u<=g<=d/16`. Since `v=(d-gu)/q`,

    d-g^2 <=v<=d/q<=16d/15,
    v-u >=d-d^2/256-d/16 >=239d/256 >7d/8.
