The component contains no public original actuator ID or shared register; inserted private hybrids remain unmarked. The declared mechanism is natural INDEPENDENT current-root inheritance throughout. Both the ordinary target component and each serial bigon rival must satisfy this same type, including root/LSA, degree, cut-child and outer-face admission after substitution.

The absence of a lower input is essential. It forbids an exterior from placing B lineages inside the component before its output. Likewise an exposed internal control/register, a full calendar/time channel, or a prescribed cross-boundary parameter tie is a different type and needs a separate proof.
