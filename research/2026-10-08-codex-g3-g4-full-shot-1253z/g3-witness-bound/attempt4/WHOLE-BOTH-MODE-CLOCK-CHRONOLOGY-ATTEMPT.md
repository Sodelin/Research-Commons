## 2. The actual source distinction

    b2_INDEP(x,x,g) = (g²+(1−g)²)x + 2g(1−g)

This is a clock budget, however, not a lower bound on the duration of any cell. Every natural edge remains strictly positive, but their durations may tend to zero as the word grows.

## 4. Full CURRENT-forest chronological limit

The obstruction is not confined to no-merger coordinates. Fix any finite total-copy cap m. Use the finite stochastic matrix on labelled CURRENT forests, with existing descendant trees grafted opaquely. Let Q_m be the ordinary Kingman generator on that carrier, so E(e^(−t))=exp(t Q_m).

For a fair equal-arm INDEPENDENT cell of duration t, assign one fair colour to each current root and coalesce within each colour for t. A selected current pair has the same colour with probability 1/2 and, at first order, merges at rate one when it does. Thus every ordinary graft-merger off-diagonal rate is halved. The diagonal is minus their sum. At most m roots are present, so the probability of two or more mergers is bounded by a cap-dependent constant times t², uniformly over the finitely many initial forests. Therefore the ACTUAL compiler matrix obeys

    B_INDEP(e^(−t),e^(−t),1/2)
          = I + (t/2) Q_m + O_m(t²).                   (4)

No colour is redrawn inside that cell. The O(t²) proof can be obtained by the finite colour-augmented continuous-time chain, whose total exit rate is at most binom(m,2); averaging its one-event term gives (4). In the next physical cell roots receive fresh independent colours, exactly as the original law specifies.

    exp(s_n Q_m)
      [ B_INDEP(e^(−t_n),e^(−t_n),1/2)

## 5. What this limit can and cannot realize

The paired limit (7) is not a same-source ordinary population: any ordinary source passage has the SAME duration in the two experiments. More strongly, no finite strictly positive PRIVATE serial word realizes (7) at all entering arities.

To see this, COMMON b3=b2³ forces every bigon's arms equal. For one common bigon, strict convexity gives

    g x³+(1−g)y³ >= [g x+(1−g)y]³,

with equality iff x=y. The b3/b2³ ratios multiply through serial words, and ordinary factors have ratio one. Hence a finite word whose COMMON law is E(C) has only equal-arm bigons after ordinary normalization.

The accepted source-authentic chain-count theorem gives, for any finite strict private INDEPENDENT serial word with L bigons,

    log b_r = −a r² + b r −(L/2)log r + O(1),

with source-dependent constants and genuine positive arms/coins/connectors. An ordinary E(D) law has no log r term. All-copy equality would therefore force L=0. With no bigon the two modes have the same ordinary survival, contradicting C!=D. This is an inherited all-copy count invariant used for a new paired-clock limit, not a new asymptotic theorem.

The conclusion is PRIVATE serial all-copy nonattainment. It excludes neither other original retained cores in an unmarked outer-source fibre nor a finite word matching only the GIVEN cap. It proves no finite rational/algebraic G3 NO instance. The master may have a small, differently retuned representative of each finite paired profile. ## 7. Occupation-flow alternative and the exact missing quantity

Dot's independently reviewed exact occupation-flow theorem retains the entire original coupled source state and the actual strict one-append edge relation. A finite positive edge measure mu satisfying the FULL Borel balance equation yields one finite actual path, with shortest length at most mu(E). The quantity mu(E) is total EDGE COUNT mass; for the atomic flow of W_n it is n (up to the declared leading initialization convention).

A clock budget instead controls a hazard-weighted quantity, such as sum t_j=T in (1). It gives no bound on the number of edges, because t_j=T/n. Replacing the finite-count-mass requirement by finite clock cost would need a new theorem.
