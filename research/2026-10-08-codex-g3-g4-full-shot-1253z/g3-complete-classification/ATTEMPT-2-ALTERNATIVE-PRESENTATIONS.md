## 2. Accepted interior theorem gives a regular alternative, not just an endpoint

**Strengthened absorption statement.** Every positive-coordinate m∈int(C) has a finite strict COMMON realization with row-rank-d moment Jacobian. If m is effectively real-algebraic, there is such a realization with real-algebraic survival/inheritance coordinates, and strict fixed-shape RCF enumeration with the rank condition eventually finds one.

**Proof.** Work in additive logarithmic coordinates hλ=−log mλ. Actual finite words form a semigroup Slog; their positive-coordinate closure is Clog locally. The inherited Cauchy construction supplies finite strict word maps ψ near zero whose derivative has rank d. Concretely, choose a fixed strict q and d distinct sufficiently small interior pi. The pi derivative matrix has entries

    (1−q^λj)/(1−pi(1−q^λj))
      = 1/((1−q^λj)^−1−pi).

Its Cauchy determinant is nonzero. Positive short arms/connectors and a sufficiently small positive ordinary duration give actual words arbitrarily near the identity. The fixed physical map has a d-variable submap with invertible derivative at one such point u. By the inverse-function theorem there is an open neighbourhood W of u consisting of values of this same submap, with the derivative rank persisting. This retains the physical submersion block; it does not merely assert W⊂Slog.

Given h∈int(Clog), choose u sufficiently small that h−u∈Clog. Choose an actual finite v∈Slog approximating h−u closely enough that h−v∈W. The fixed submap therefore has a strict parameter point θ with ψ(θ)=h−v. Concatenate the actual word for v with the fixed word for ψ(θ). Their total is exactly h. Varying just θ gives derivative rank d at this concatenated physical source. The proof never applies a negative ordinary duration as a source operation: h−v is in the positive word image W, and v is itself an actual word. Additive subtraction is used only to select that positive factorization.

Componentwise exponentiation has invertible diagonal derivative at the positive tuple, so the moment map also has row rank d. On the fixed resulting finite graph, its moment coordinates are rational-coefficient polynomials in actual survival and inheritance coordinates. The finite formula requiring the exact algebraic input, all strict domain inequalities and one nonzero d×d derivative minor has a real solution. Real-closed-field transfer gives a real-algebraic solution of the same formula. Enumerating admissible finite word graphs and solving these finite strict formulas terminates under the stated existence premise. It yields the same physical graph/tuple across the full capped COMMON kernel, not separate row fits. No bound on the first successful graph is obtained. QED.

Conversely, a row-rank-d physical presentation gives m∈int(S) by submersion. A positive-degree physical d-dimensional chart also puts m∈int(S): its boundary avoids m, and the nonzero degree persists for a neighbourhood of m. Restricting a full-rank map to an invertible d-variable minor gives a sufficiently small local chart of degree +1 or −1. Thus the full-rank and positive-degree alternatives in (U) are equivalent at the **global existence** level here, although a chosen chart can have zero degree or singular derivative.
