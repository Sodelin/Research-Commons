## Results and exact limits

1. **Source-semigroup relative-interior realization.** The actual capped forest algebra has a faithful left action with entering-arity diagonal blocks. Positive polynomial word families stabilize in Zariski closure by the algebra dimension and have identity-near real open patches. Every nonsingular relative-interior point of the source closure is a finite positive word. This does not decide which coupled observed fibres meet that interior or a boundary.
2. **All-size polynomial identity tests.** With the complete protected-core grammar, genuinely independent private slots, one fixed mechanism and one parameter map across all rows, an R+ED test-shape bound suffices for polynomial equalities. A violated universally verified identity is a sound NO certificate. R+ED is not a realizing-witness budget; a passing identity test is not an existence decision.
3. **Independent weak factors and divisibility.** The inherited full-forest weak-bigon estimate yields word-uniform distance from an ordinary match. In the nonsingular source-group closure, ordinary kernels are exactly those with arbitrarily fine exact factorizations or unbounded integer root orders. Quantitative strong-factor/root bounds do not bound the total number of arbitrary weak factors.
4. **Three-lineage survival control.** A conservative explicit power of b_3 lower-bounds every higher declared no-merger diagonal of the same physical independent kernel. A positive pair floor alone does not do so. Observation-derived floors require an actually supplied positive full rooted-topology event under the stated descendant/outside-copy conditions.
5. **Conditional second-order strict attainment.** A classical stable-openness criterion applies to coherent jointly legal finite C² pivot charts and approximants preserving every response equation. A nonattained presentation then admits one covector with the stated finite-support negative-index bounds. This conditional criterion is not an exhaustive G3 reduction or a sufficient NO certificate; the full nonlinear compiler Hessian remains explicit.
6. **Ordered independent closure.** Every fixed-cap independent source limit with positive pair survival has an ordered countable closed-bigon presentation with an ordinary residual and a uniform ranked-tail error. Conversely every such presentation is a source limit. Nonsingularity is equivalent to nonsingularity of every retained jump. Closed parameters, zero gaps and countably many jumps are boundary data; this representation does not give finite strict attainment.
