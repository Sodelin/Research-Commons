# Why the private face bound does not yet give the original input bound

Authored 2026-10-21, additive scope clarification to the immutable
`G3-CAP3-FACE-ALL-LENGTH-BOUND.md`. The HAND theorem and its proof are unchanged.

In a larger recognition problem, `c` can be an existential INTERNAL kernel
coordinate rather than a supplied observation. The proved bound
`M(c)=max(4,K+2)`, with `(4/3)^K>=1/c`, then varies as `c` tends to zero.
It does not supply a target-derived positive lower bound on that coordinate,
or a bound uniform over every fitting chart bank.

Even in a cap-three face-only reduction, an input-effective one-witness
choice/lower-bound supplier would be needed before this becomes an original
input bound `H(p)`. It suffices to bound the internal `c` values for ONE
fitting bank, not all banks. Source reconstruction and finite-code admission
alone do not provide this implication. Higher caps and the off-face paired
word problem retain additional independent gaps.
