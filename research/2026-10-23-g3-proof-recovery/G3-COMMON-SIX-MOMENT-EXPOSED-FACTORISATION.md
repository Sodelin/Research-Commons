For r=2, survivals u<v with positive weights p,1-p always have a strict
one-cell realization. Choose A=(1+v)/2, arms u/A,v/A and coin p. Its
private minimum is1.

For r=3 write the positive weights as w0,w1,w2 on u<v<w. A physical word
exists EXACTLY when

    v^2=u w,                  w1^2>=4 w0 w2.                 (1)

Its private minimum is2. To prove necessity, at least two nondegenerate
Bernoulli factors are needed to produce three values, and the support
bound allows at most two. Their two middle products must coincide; thus
the factors have the same arm ratio and the three values are geometric.
The probability generating polynomial must factor as

    w0+w1 z+w2 z^2=(p0+(1-p0)z)(p1+(1-p1)z),

so its discriminant is (p0-p1)^2=w1^2-4w0w2>=0.

Conversely assume(1). Set h=1+w0-w2 and

    p0,p1=(h +/- sqrt(w1^2-4w0w2))/2.

and (1-p0)(1-p1)=w2>0. Choose A=(1+w)/2, and give BOTH fresh cells arms
