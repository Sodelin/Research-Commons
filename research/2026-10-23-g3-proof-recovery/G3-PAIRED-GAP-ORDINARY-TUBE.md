## Polynomial construction of L_m

For a bare cell put h=1-p and

    c=px+hy,  q=p^2 x+h^2 y+2ph,
    Delta=q-c=ph(2-x-y).

For each mode form the MATRIX polynomial

    F_mu(x,y,p)=B_mu,m(x,y,p)-E_m(d_mu(B)).

At p=0 both actual modes are E_m(y); at p=1 both are E_m(x).
Every entry of F_mu is therefore divisible by p(1-p) in Q[x,y,p].
Write F_mu=p(1-p)G_mu. At x=y=1 the actual cell and its pair-matched
ordinary kernel are both identity. Hence G_mu(1,1,p)=0 as a polynomial.
There are computable matrix polynomials H_mu,x,H_mu,y with

    G_mu=(1-x)H_mu,x+(1-y)H_mu,y.                           (3)

For example subtract G_mu(1,y,p) before division by1-x, and divide
G_mu(1,y,p)-G_mu(1,1,p) by1-y. These are exact polynomial divisions.
They do not fit an asymptotic normalized source direction.

For a rational polynomial H let |H|_coef be its absolute coefficient sum.
On the CLOSED unit cube, |H|<=|H|_coef. Choose L_m>=1 above the maximum of
the coefficient row-sum bounds, separately for each H matrix in each mode.
Then(3) gives

    ||B_mu,m-E_m(d_mu(B))|| <= L_m ph(2-x-y)=L_m Delta.     (4)

All boundary evaluations here are polynomial identities used for bounds;
endpoint inheritance or zero exposure is not admitted as a strict source.
Finite polynomial kernel generation, exact divisions and rational coefficient
arithmetic make L_m total computable. No RCF oracle or word bound is assumed.

## A directly inductive semialgebraic invariant

The tube(2), together with stochasticity, is closed under multiplying two
paired kernels that each satisfy it. Indeed stochastic telescoping and the
ordinary semigroup identity bound each product's deviation by the sum of
the two deviations. For pair coordinates(C,Q) and(c,q), multiplication by
Cc leaves the upper bound

    L_m[c(Q-C)+C(q-c)] <= L_m(Qq-Cc),

because the difference is L_m(Q-C)(q-c)>=0. This argument also works when
C or c is zero; its left side then vanishes where appropriate.
Each physical cell satisfies the tube by(4), since its c<=1, and ordinary
initializations satisfy it with zero error. Thus(2) is an explicit member
of the original reduction's semialgebraic invariant language. It can be
used in an actual finite core/word state without storing an unknown length.
No claim that this certificate language is complete is made.
Here C,Q are tracked multiplicative state coordinates; on actual source

not assert multiplicativity for an arbitrary synthetic stochastic matrix's

## Why absolute pair gap without a positive anchor is insufficient

At C=Q=0 the polynomial tube intentionally gives no restriction.
There is a concrete physical closure guard. Take z=1/2,p=1/2 and let both
arm survivals x=y=e and the connector a=e tend to zero. Every e in(0,1)
gives a strict one-cell word. Its pair coordinates are

    C=z e^2,   Q=z e(1+e)/2,

so Q-C->0 and both tend to zero. COMMON tends to ordinary completion E(0).
For four singleton tokens the limiting I cell completes each routed colour
group, then its two groups merge in the upper ordinary completion.
Six of the sixteen equally likely routings split2+2 and produce a balanced
tree; two put all four in one group, with ordinary balanced probability1/3;
the other eight give a comb. Its balanced probability is therefore5/12,
against1/3 for ordinary completion. After the leading E(z), the difference
is z^6/12=1/768: on all smaller current-root states the completed first-pair
extension is already the ordinary one, and the four-root no-merger mass is z^6.

Thus a uniform bound tending to zero with Q-C alone would be false.
The ratio in(1), or the positive C anchor for its closed-face consequence,
is essential. This boundary guard is not an admitted zero-exposure target
or a full original-source compiler execution.
