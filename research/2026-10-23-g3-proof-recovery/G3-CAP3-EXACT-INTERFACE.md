## Exact membership, with one auxiliary variable

For a fresh natural INDEPENDENT private bridge with at most three CURRENT
entering roots, the proposed full kernel image is the open strip S in that
proof. Its membership test requires only polynomial real-algebraic predicates.
Let q,r be the proposed pair/triple no-merger coordinates, and define

    D(v)=2+3v-2v^2-2v^3,
    P(v)=3+6v-2v^2-8v^3,
    Aq(v)=q D(v)^2-2(1-v^2)(1+2v)^2,
    Br(v)=r D(v)^3-2v(1+v)^2 P(v).

The exact criterion is the conjunction of:

1. `0<q<1` and `r>0`.
2. `r-3q+2<0 OR (r-3q+2)^2<4(1-q)^3`.
3. `q<=1/2 OR exists v: 0<v, 2v^2<1, Aq(v)=0, Br(v)>0`.

The third condition compares r with the lower boundary using its unique
v parameter; no logarithmic equality or numerical gap is introduced. The
second condition is exactly r<U(q), with its sign branch retained before
squaring. Endpoint equality is excluded because every physical passage
and natural inheritance parameter must stay strict.

This can serve as an internal finite-template solver interface. Each eligible
cap-three slot uses the SAME q,r across all rows, with the full forest matrix
M(q,r) including empty, single, pair and opaque subtree inputs. The retained
core compiler still carries every population and original shared register.
After solving the joint topology equations, reconstruct each slot's strict
word and then one shared physical bank. Slot coordinates are internal
variables, not additional measured outcomes. Templates with an unbounded
higher-cap slot remain outside this branch; failure in the cap-three subset
does not exclude such templates.
