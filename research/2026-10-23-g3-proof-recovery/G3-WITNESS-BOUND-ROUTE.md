## A conditional bound with the correct quantifiers

For an actual fresh natural private word, let d(K) be the probability that
two entering current roots leave its full forest kernel without merging.
Mergers cannot be reversed, so concatenation gives

    d(K_1 ... K_N) = product_i d(K_i).

This is an internal proof coordinate; it is not automatically an observed
topology probability. For a strict bigon with arm survivals x,y, inheritance
g and following connector survival a, its pair diagonal is

    d_COMMON = a*[g*x + (1-g)*y],
    d_INDEPENDENT = a*[g^2*x + (1-g)^2*y + 2g*(1-g)].

Both lie strictly between zero and one for strict natural parameters.
These formulas retain the two different mechanisms. In the independent case
the split-arm event contributes no merger before the connector. Freshness
and actual current-lineage semantics are required; an exposed or shared
register must remain in the joint conditional kernel rather than being
silently made independent.

Suppose an effective supplier provides rational 0<epsilon,rho<1 and
guarantees the following ONE-witness assertion:

    if input p is YES, there is one admitted realizing source in which
    each relevant private word has pair diagonal >=epsilon and every
    counted physical unit has pair diagonal <=rho.

Any such word of length N satisfies epsilon<=rho^N. Find an integer k by
exact rational multiplication until rho^k<epsilon. Then N<k. The search
terminates without an exact logarithm comparison. Ordinary leading factors
of diagonal <=1 do not invalidate the inequality. Together with the
inherited finite core catalogue, this bounds one physical witness and makes
the fixed-size compiler usable for a total recognition procedure.

This is a sufficient interface, not a supplier for it. Its constants must
be computed on NO inputs too; the implication is vacuous there but the
procedure must terminate. It need not bound every presentation or every
realization of a YES input.

For a GIVEN finite catalogue of strict algebraic units, rho is acquired:
take a rational strictly between the largest pair diagonal and one. This
does not acquire the catalogue from the original observation input. In the
original fresh-parameter grammar the connector may approach survival one
and routing may become arbitrarily rare, so strictness alone supplies no
common rho below one. A floor obtained only after selecting a particular
finite architecture leaves the original count-before-height order intact.

## Why a legal coarsening does not automatically provide epsilon

Take the strict COMMON physical word

    K = E(1/2) B_COMMON(1/4,1/2,1/2) E(1/2).

It has pair diagonal (1/2)*(3/8)*(1/2)=3/32. Put K^N in one eligible
pendant-A slot of a fixed positive four-taxon exterior. Each copy is a
literal finite cell with its own legal private site; adjacent ordinary
factors can be combined legally. Every source is finite, strict and uses
one shared parameter tuple across its declared rows.

Use the permitted finite coarsening that maps every final rooted labelled
unranked topology to one outcome. Its exact observed probability is one
for EVERY N, whereas the internal pair diagonal is (3/32)^N and tends to
zero. All four sampled taxa and the original completion can be retained;
the coarsening simply discards their final distinction.

Thus these observation values do not give a positive lower pair bound
valid over all realizing sources. This is a deliberate degenerate legal
channel, not a new observation model. A simple ordinary source also realizes
it. The example therefore DOES NOT refute the one-witness supplier above,
a shortest-source bound, or G3 decidability. It rules out using a uniform
all-presentations epsilon as an automatic consequence of arbitrary original
coarsened data. Acquiring a suitable representative is the missing work.

The COMMON arithmetic was checked exactly with Fraction. The same chosen
parameters give INDEPENDENT diagonal 11/64, also strictly between zero and
one; no equality of the two mechanisms or original compiler execution is
claimed by that arithmetic check.

## Fixed-matrix theorems are not a source catalogue or an output adapter

Cai--Lipton--Zalcstein's [The Complexity of the A B C Problem](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0097539794276853)
states a polynomial-time membership result for two supplied commuting
matrices over an algebraic number field. Its publisher abstract was read

a full-paper proof review.
The original input supplies neither two fixed generators for all fresh
physical units nor a full target matrix. It supplies a finite response fibre
under a declared observation/compiler map. Membership in a supplied matrix
semigroup and intersection of that semigroup with an observation fibre are
different tasks. A source-faithful finite catalogue and a matching output
adapter must be proved before transferring that result.

Likewise, a theorem about existence of a nonnegative product does not decide
the prescribed exact source-response equations. The targeted search did
not locate an applicable theorem for the actual variable-length,
fresh-parameter, joint-source contract; that is a limited search outcome,
not a claim that every possible theorem has been ruled out.

## Full-source global optimization attempt

The following construction uses the ENTIRE original source catalogue and
joint input, not a smaller observation menu. It gives a canonical attained
score but does not give a decision procedure. It is a hand-derived route
audit; its RCF optimizations have not been executed.

Use the inherited effective enumeration A_0,A_1,... of finite admitted
architecture templates, including protected original IDs and joint registers.
For a fixed template, natural survivals and inheritance coordinates are in
(0,1); fixed legal intervention endpoints are not falsely made strict.
The original polynomial input contract is essential. No additional calendar
or exponential model is introduced here.

Replace strict inequalities by weak inequalities to obtain a compact
algebraic-parameter relaxation in the closed coordinate cube. Multiply one
normalized slack factor for every strict admission condition, including
theta*(1-theta) for each strict natural coordinate. Polynomial slacks can
be bounded on the cube by a computable positive coefficient bound. The
result is a nonnegative continuous semialgebraic quality Q_i in [0,1],
positive precisely at genuinely admitted tuples and zero on any relaxation
point violating original strictness. Keep all original joint response
equations F_i(theta)=p, parameter equalities and weak constraints.

If the relaxed fibre is empty, define M_i(p)=0. Otherwise let M_i(p) be
the maximum of Q_i on it. Compactness gives attainment. RCF optimization
computes its exact algebraic value and an algebraic optimizer when positive.
Most importantly,

    M_i(p)>0 iff A_i has an actual strict joint source for p.

Define

    S(p) = sup_i 2^(-i-1) M_i(p),
    S_N(p) = max(0, 2^(-i-1) M_i(p) for i<N).

The exact finite S_N values are computable algebraic numbers. Since
0<=M_i<=1, the uniform tail estimate is

    0 <= S(p)-S_N(p) <= 2^(-N-1).                            (T)

Thus S is a uniformly computable real from the EXACT encoded algebraic
input. This is not a Type-2 continuity claim for approximate response oracles;
the finite optimizations use exact algebraic comparisons.

S(p)>0 exactly on the full original YES set, and S(p)=0 on the full NO set.
If S is positive, any single positive term gives a positive lower bound l.
Choose N with 2^(-N-1)<l. No omitted template can improve that term, so the
global maximum is attained in this finite prefix and is itself algebraic.
If p is NO, S=0 is algebraic as well. Consequently S(p) is algebraic for
EVERY admitted encoded input, and its finite-prefix sequence stabilizes
exactly after finitely many terms on EVERY input.

This does not finish the height argument. Neither the finite stabilization
index nor an exact algebraic-number code for S has been acquired uniformly.
On a YES input, enumeration eventually finds a positive lower bound and can
then compute the exact maximum and its source optimizer. On a NO input,
every finite prefix is zero and (T) only bounds how small a later positive
term could be. Algebraicity, attainment and a known approximation modulus
do not turn those bounds into a zero certificate.

### Why those properties alone cannot imply zero decidability

For any program e, let t be its first halting step, if it halts. Consider
the uniformly computable finite-step terms

    h_i(e) = 2^(-i-1) if e first halts at step i, otherwise 0.

Each term is rational and computable by a bounded simulation. Their supremum
is rational: zero if e never halts, or 2^(-t-1) if it does. The same bound (T)
holds for their finite maxima, the supremum is attained whenever positive,
and the finite-max sequence is eventually constant for every e. A uniform
zero test based only on these properties would decide nonhalting, which is
impossible by the classical halting argument.

This is a computability countermodel to the proposed inference, NOT an
embedding of programs into legal G3 sources and NOT a G3 undecidability
proof. The original G3 inputs retain finite exact algebraic descriptions;
a Cauchy name for an algebraic output is not automatically such a description.
Any successful use of S needs additional source-specific information giving
a total effective positive gap or an independently verifiable zero test.
That remains the original unresolved recognition obligation.

## A narrower exponential decision theorem has a specific interface

Read [Barbagallo--Jeronimo--Sabia, arXiv:1905.10882v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.10882),
Theorem 18, Proposition 19 and the reduction in Section 5.2. The oracle-free
case uses polynomial expressions sharing ONE exponential e^h. It introduces
z=h(x), eliminates the other algebraic variables by RCF, then decides the
univariate e^z formula. The general order-one Pfaffian result uses an
algebraic-point sign oracle. Section 5.3 warns that its zero encodings do not
automatically support field arithmetic, and gives an addition-closure
obstruction. These particular statements and reductions were read; no
algorithm was implemented here.

In the inherited G3 slice, writing x_n=log b_n gives several constraints
e^(x_n)=b_n plus a nonlinear polynomial constraint on different x_n.
The pure parametrization instead uses e^(w D_n(r)) for several distinct
polynomials D_n. Neither presentation has the common single e^h interface
just stated. Combining their equations or treating separate encoded roots
as elements of an effective algebraic field requires a new adapter; none
was supplied by this read. Rationally commensurate exponents are an easier
case already covered by the inherited arithmetic branches. No impossibility
of every conceivable adapter or general Pfaffian method is claimed.

## A real-geometric check on an algebraic exceptional-set shortcut

This is a hand lemma about the real promised cone family, not a theorem
that exhibits an algebraic input in its unresolved pure branch. Fix w0>0
and any nonempty open interval J contained in (0,1). Put

    D_n(r)=n-(1+r+...+r^(n-1)),
    b_n(r)=exp(w0*D_n(r)),  n=3,6,10,15,21.

The curve r in J is Zariski dense in R^5: no nonzero real polynomial in
the five b coordinates vanishes on the entire curve. Indeed, substitute
b(r) into a polynomial sum_alpha c_alpha b^alpha. Its monomials become

    c_alpha exp(P_alpha(r)),
    P_alpha(r)=w0 sum_n alpha_n D_n(r).

For different multi-indices, P_alpha-P_beta is nonconstant. The D_n have
different degrees 2,5,9,14,20, so they are linearly independent, and every
D_n vanishes at r=1, excluding a nonzero constant combination. If the
substitution vanished on J, real analyticity would make it vanish on all
of R. Among the finitely many distinct exponent polynomials there is a
unique eventually largest one as r tends to positive infinity. Divide by
its exponential. Every other term tends to zero, leaving its nonzero
coefficient equal to zero, a contradiction. Evaluating the analytic formula
outside J is only this identity proof; it is not a physical observation or
an admitted parameter extension.

Consequently the curve cannot lie in a finite union of proper algebraic
exceptional sets. It is also not semialgebraic: in any finite polynomial
sign description, choose a curve point where all nonzero polynomials in
the description are nonzero. Such a point exists by the just-proved
analytic nonvanishing and finiteness of the description. Their signs are
constant in an ambient ball, so membership would include that whole ball.
But b_3 is strictly monotone on J and determines r, so the curve has empty
ambient interior. This is a contradiction.

This screens a proposed REAL-geometry argument that would put the entire
pure exception into finitely many proper algebraic strata. It does not
refute a recursive decision procedure on the encoded algebraic points,
prove nonsemialgebraicity of the full original YES/NO set, or exclude a
different stratification using additional analytic information. In
particular the arithmetic question of which, if any, rank-five algebraic
tuples lie on the pure family remains unexhibited and unexcluded.
