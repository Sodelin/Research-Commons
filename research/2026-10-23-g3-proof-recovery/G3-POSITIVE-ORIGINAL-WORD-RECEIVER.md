## Calendar/rate construction

Let the original slot be u->v with D=C(u)-C(v)>0. For N bigons, put

    C(H_j)=C(v)+(2j-1)D/(2N+1),
    C(U_j)=C(v)+2jD/(2N+1),             j=1,...,N.

The literal rootward sequence is the positive lower connector, first bigon,
positive connector, next bigon, ..., positive final connector to old u. Every
connector and each arm interval has duration d=D/(2N+1)>0. Parallel arms share
the SAME actual interval. Every old age stays unchanged, including the retained
root and arbitrarily serial/negative old tip ages if the supplied calendar has
them. Each new survival z in(0,1) is realized exactly by

    rate(z)=-log(z)/d > 0,
    exp(-rate(z)*d)=z.

All new rates are finite and positive. The N=0 receiver keeps one ordinary slot
with its old duration D and the requested strict new survival. No zero passage,
infinite branch, identity limit, unequal arm dates or per-mode refit is used.
For natural gamma, the retained tester's p is P(parent0); native originalGamma
is P(parent1)=1-p. Both modes use these same physical edges/rates/coin values.

3log(2)/D, its arm rates are-3log(35/44)/D and-3log(29/44)/D, and gamma=1/2.

Every new U_j has indegree1/outdegree2 and every H_j indegree2/outdegree1;
all original degrees are retained. Strict assigned ages prove acyclicity.
The new hybrid-child connectors are cut edges in the original bridge strip.
Any old cut-child edge outside it remains a cut edge: a cycle cannot enter and
leave a substituted bridge strip through another exterior attachment.

least stable ancestor of ALL taxa. Every new vertex dominates only leaves on
that proper side; no new common dominator of all taxa is introduced. All old
