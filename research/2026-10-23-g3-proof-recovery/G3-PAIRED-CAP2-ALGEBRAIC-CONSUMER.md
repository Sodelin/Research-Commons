Authored2026-10-22UTC. Implements the HAND strict-image theorem in

Load `Cap3PrivateWordCertificate.wl` and then
`PairedCap2WordCertificate.wl`. The first supplies the reused exact algebraic
input and closed real-decision helpers; no independent model is substituted.

`Cap2PairedWordMember[C,Q]` decides `0<C<=Q<1` for exact real-algebraic
inputs. Inexact, transcendental and complex inputs return Failure. A genuine
unresolved decision also remains Failure. An outside exact target has no
finite strict fresh natural private cap-two word, by the HAND theorem.

`Cap2PairedWordWitness[C,Q]` returns a compact certificate. `C=Q` uses no
bigon and ordinary bank `C`. Otherwise it chooses `A=(1+Q)/2` and doubles
`N=1,2,4,...` until the positive algebraic roots of `C/A,Q/A` enter the
strict bare-cell image. The proof supplies an explicit finite sufficient
threshold; no supplied stopping oracle or count promise is needed. The
piecewise bare constructor produces ONE arm0/arm1/parent0 tuple. Its numeric
parameters are repeated at `N` DISTINCT fresh sites; their natural bits are
not tied or reused.

The certificate retains `Targets`, `OrdinaryBank`, `CellCount`,
`RepeatedCell`, `PassageCount` and `DistinctFreshSites`.
`Cap2PairedWordVerify` directly checks strict algebraic parameters and BOTH
physical products against the targets, normalizing algebraic differences.
It does not infer verification from a solver's status string.
`Cap2PairedWordPassage[certificate,j]` gives the telescoping strict ordinary
factor; `Cap2PairedWordExpand` returns the explicit passage/cell lists after
verification. Exact output can be large; the compact certificate is a
representation of a finite word, not an assumed continuous power of a
source signature.
