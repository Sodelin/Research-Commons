## Positive polynomial certificate and product proof

For a bare cell put h=1-p and

    d=p x^3+h y^3,
    q=p^2 x+h^2 y+2ph,
    r=p^3 x^3+h^3 y^3+3ph(p x+h y).

The exact identity is

    r-dq = p^3 x^3(1-x)+h^3 y^3(1-y)
      +ph[p x(3-2x^2-y^3)+h y(3-2y^2-x^3)].                 (2)

All summands are positive in the strict physical domain 0<x,y,p<1. In
particular 2x^2+y^3<3 and 2y^2+x^3<3. Hence each cell has r>dq.

    DQ=A^4 product(d_i q_i).

is simply A^-1>1. This proves(1) for EVERY finite length, including ordinary
words, without a word-length bound or a closure argument. Equality is also
