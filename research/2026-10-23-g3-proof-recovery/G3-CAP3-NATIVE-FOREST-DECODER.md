## Why the resulting native capped kernel is M(Q,R)

Condition on the whole entering case, including its actual stored register.
After identifying three current-root IDs with {a,b,c}, the valid unranked
forests partition into exactly seven states:

    U: three separate singleton roots;
    P_ab,P_ac,P_bc: one pair cherry plus the remaining singleton;
    T_ab,T_ac,T_bc: one completed tree, indexed by its first cherry.

This enumeration follows from binary source mergers, disjoint leaf fibers
and no splitting of an entering genealogy. It does not expose survivor IDs,
ordered graft orientation, private population paths or merger times.

Let R be the ACTUAL no-loss probability for three entering current roots and Q
the ACTUAL no-loss probability for two entering current roots on the same
original word and fixed register. For a supplied OriginalSpan, the uncompiled
G3ActualOriginalSpanNoLoss.lean candidate derives these from original ordinary
and bigon factors, without a FourRootArmLaw or desired row-constant premise.

Write b_ab,b_ac,b_bc for the three partial-forest masses. Pruning to the pair
{a,b} leaves it unmerged exactly in U,P_ac,P_bc. Actual cross-carrier program
projectivity therefore gives

    Q=R+b_ac+b_bc,
    Q=R+b_ab+b_bc,
    Q=R+b_ab+b_ac.

All pair restrictions use the same two-current-root source law after copy
renaming; their previous taxon assignments do not enter the primitive pair
rates or current-root coin weights. Solving gives b_ab=b_ac=b_bc=(Q-R)/2.
Permutation of the three current-root labels sends the three completed cherry
types transitively into each other. The copy-bijection law therefore makes
their probabilities equal. Normalization makes each (2-3Q+R)/6.
