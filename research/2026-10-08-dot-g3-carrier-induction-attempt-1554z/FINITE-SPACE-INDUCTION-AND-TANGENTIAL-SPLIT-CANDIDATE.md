## 4. A valid tangential-criticality replacement on fixed carriers

This is the main direct deduction for this attempted recursion.

Write F=product_i f_i and use the accepted cleared derivative convention

    P_c=sum_i c_i(1-q^lambda_i) product_(j!=i) f_j,
    Q_c=sum_i c_i lambda_i q^(lambda_i-1) product_(j!=i) f_j.

Then

    c H_p=P_c/F,       c H_q=−p Q_c/F.                    (1)

Put D=sum_i lambda_i+d−1, so deg(P_c)<=D and deg(Q_c)<=D−1. Let G(p,q)=0 be ONE fixed absolutely irreducible carrier of degree delta, defined over the real algebraic numbers and containing a strict real analytic arc whose closure reaches a neutral point. Let V_G be its complete neutral log-value space from the accepted divisor theorem.

At a nonsingular point, (G_q,−G_p) is a nonzero tangent vector. Therefore tangential stationarity of c H is exactly

    T_(c,G)=G_q P_c + p G_p Q_c = 0.                     (2)

The PLUS sign follows from the minus sign in c H_q in (1). Its total degree is at most delta+D−1.

There are two exhaustive alternatives:

**Constant-value alternative.** If G divides T_(c,G), the tangential differential of c H is identically zero on the carrier. On a neutral real arc c H is consequently constant, and that constant is zero because f_i tend to one. Thus c annihilates V_G. Conversely, if c annihilates V_G, its restriction to the neutral real arc is identically zero, so T_(c,G) vanishes there and G divides it. Equivalently this alternative is the finite linear condition V_G subset ker(c), not a new full-square differential criticality assertion.

**Isolated-point alternative.** If G does not divide T_(c,G), they have no common curve component, since G is absolutely irreducible. Projective Bezout bounds their distinct intersection points, counting affine points among them, by

    B_G=delta(delta+D−1).                               (3)

Every strict nonsingular tangential stationary point belongs to this finite intersection. All singular points of G are also included automatically, because both G_p and G_q vanish there, making (2) zero. Thus permitting singular parameter values cannot produce an uncounted family. If T is a nonzero constant the intersection is empty, as expected. Restrict to the actual strict real carrier pieces afterwards.

For a supplied algebraic c, these finite actual candidate points can be isolated by RCF/algebraic elimination. The bound (3) holds also for real c, but does not claim algebraic output coordinates when c is transcendental. As c varies, the isolated point locations generally vary; a uniform number of point TYPES is not a fixed finite normal-independent set of source parameters.

No bound on occurrence multiplicities follows from Bezout. Nor is T=0 replaced by P=Q=0: the latter would incorrectly demand free variations transverse to the restricted alphabet.
