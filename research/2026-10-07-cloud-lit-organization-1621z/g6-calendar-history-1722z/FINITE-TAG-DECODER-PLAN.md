## Concrete new decoder plan

Let `bin : ℝ → Tag`. Finiteness of `Tag` is needed for the eventual finite carrier, but the algebraic decoder statement does not need it.

1. Define proposed `TagDecoration t` by the same recursion: `PUnit` at a leaf and `Tag × TagDecoration a × TagDecoration b` at a graft. Define proposed `mapBinDecoration bin` by applying `bin` to every internal real age. Leaf sampling dates are not internal-node tags.
2. Define proposed `decodeTags B t`: at a graft read `B (witness a) (witness b)` and decode both children using the same `B`. Prove, by the inherited `decode_of_pair_agreement` induction, that leaf-pair agreement with the binned real `pairAge` implies `decodeTags B t = mapBinDecoration bin d`. In particular, take `B x y = bin (pairAge leafAge t d x y)`.
3. At each graft, `witness_mem` and child disjointness prove the two chosen witnesses are distinct and genuine cross-child leaves. `pairAge_cross` says their age is exactly that node's age. `pairAge_left/right` reduce all descendant comparisons to the child tree. This proves coverage of **every** internal node and independence from which cross pair was chosen. It uses neither chronological sorting nor a unique bin per node.
4. Define a tagged decorated-tree equivalence generated by the inherited child swaps/congruence, carrying the root tag and swapping the entire child decorations. Prove compatibility of tag decoding from actual pair-age agreement on age-decorated representatives related by the corresponding swaps. Symmetry supplies the selected-entry equality when reversing one fixed root's two witnesses, but does **not** suffice for arbitrary nested swaps: a child swap can change the ancestor's witness. The later G3 bridge below supplies an explicit symmetric-matrix counterexample and the stronger actual decorated transport. `UnorderedEquiv` alone does not prove the missing matrix consistency.
5. Descend the decoded tagged object to the original unranked topology quotient, or encode each node by its original descendant clade with the corresponding tag. Prove both directions of observation equivalence: topology plus all internal-node tags determines each observed off-diagonal MRCA tag; topology plus the pair-tag matrix determines every node's tag. Recursive swaps, never sorting by time/bin, identify the outputs.
6. Apply this pointwise result on the intersection of `actual_original_complete_timed_support` and `actual_original_faithful_output`. For selected subtrees, use the existing pruning and same-matrix decoder theorems. A finite measurable bin map then supplies a measurable observation quotient of the original law. It still needs its actual statement and compiler evidence.

The simpler first compiler target is step 2 on a fixed actual tree. Step 4 is the essential unordered gate; an ordered tuple equality cannot be asserted on a quotient without it. ## Empty, singleton and non-node coordinates

- **Empty selected panel:** represent no component by an empty forest/`none` in the pruning carrier. There is no `Genealogy` witness to construct and no internal tag. `witness` never requires an ambient `Nonempty Copy`; a supplied tree provides its own label.
- **Singleton:** its topology is the labelled leaf and its decoration is `PUnit`. There is no internal age to decode. Its pair-matrix diagonal is a fixed leaf sampling date, proved by [ChronologicalPruning](../../2026-10-07-dot-g2-timed-endpoint-ordinary-integration-0941z/G2ChronologicalPruning.lean) `pair_age_self` and the faithful output's `timed_matrix_diagonal`.
- **Diagonal and unselected entries:** [JointTimedObservation](../../2026-10-06-dot-timed-g2-partial-preservation-and-priority-0714z/sources/G2JointTimedObservation.lean) retains explicit present/absent flags; an absent zero payload is not an age. Under the original contemporaneous-tip G6 contract, diagonal tags are determined data and add no internal-node observation. Keep that instantiation explicit; do not accidentally add arbitrary leaf dates to the menu.
- **Forest intermediates:** decode each component only on its own leaf pairs. Unresolved cross-component entries are not represented by internal nodes and must retain the proper unresolved/absent convention. Final coalescence/support comes from ancestral completion, not the tree decoder. Do not infer an arbitrary matrix's biological admission from successful decoding.
- **Repeated tags:** two ancestor/descendant nodes may lie in the same bin. Retain both nodes and their rooted ancestry. Quotienting ranks unrelated to the original observation never contracts that edge or associates the binary grafts.
