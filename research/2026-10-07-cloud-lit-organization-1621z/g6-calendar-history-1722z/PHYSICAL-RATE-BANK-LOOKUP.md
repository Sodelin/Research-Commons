## Existing hand estimates have narrower meanings

The governing [G6 Theorem 5](../../../research/2026-10-01-g6-effective-certification/PROOF.md) already bounds a finite ordinary population hazard change by `binom(M,2)*delta` for every entering forest and sums operation errors. Its independently quantized/clipped hazard proxy need not obey one physical constant rate across successive epochs and is explicitly not admitted as an exact source. Joint real-algebraic feasibility supplies an actual source witness; it does not convert the proxy itself into a physical bank. This inherited hand argument must keep that distinction.

G3's [supplied-effective constructor](../../../research/2026-10-07-cloud-g3-contextual-source-1716z/EFFECTIVE-ONE-BANK-CONSTRUCTOR.md), [independently accepted at hand level](../../../research/2026-10-07-cloud-independent-auditor-1616z/G3-EFFECTIVE-SUPPLIED-BANK-REVIEW.md), couples one COMMON bit and an ordinary exceptional exposure, giving `|Delta p|+binom(M,2)|Delta b|`. It retains the exact positive baseline and outside bank. G3 confirmed this is a contextual architecture estimate, not an accepted arbitrary-source physical-bank theorem. No executable constructor or global input oracle was provided.

## Proposed full-record coupling, not an accepted provider

Fix the same graph, original arc IDs, sample/copy carrier, entering admitted `Code s`, durations/cuts, original boundary/register rules and supplied old decoration. Change only the positive bank `r` to `rhat`. For one current coordinate put `a=pairRate(r,i)/2` and `b=pairRate(rhat,i)/2`.

For `a≠b`, the proposed elementary coupling draws independent `U~Exp(min(a,b))` and `V~Exp(|a−b|)`: the slower clock is `U`, the faster is `min(U,V)`. For `a=b`, use one common clock and no zero-rate exponential. The intended marginals are `Exp(a)` and `Exp(b)` and the disagreement probability is

    P(clocks differ)=|a−b|/max(a,b)
                    =|r_i−rhat_i|/max(r_i,rhat_i).

Independently couple the finite coordinates of `Choice N s`. If every rate ratio discrepancy is at most `eta`, a union bound would give a **single joint clock-vector** disagreement bound `card(Choice N s)*eta`. A conservative all-entering-state bound is `(card E+1)*m^2*eta`, where `m=card Copy`: each population's off-diagonal pair set embeds in `Copy×Copy`. The sharp bound from disjoint live populations is unnecessary for this proposal. Empty catalogues have disagreement zero; empty/singleton carriers require no division by their cardinality. Constants are for one supplied finite graph/copy bank, not a universal cap on the original unbounded source class.

On agreement of the complete vectors, the identical deterministic compiler gives agreement of **the entire** actual marked record: merger ages, destinations, old subtrees, flag and padding. Joint measurability then allows a coupling/TV pushforward bound for any measurable joint readout. This is not a sum over output coordinates and does not assert independence of same-locus panels. It also applies in principle to `completeAncestralTrace`, because both sides use `clockCover` of their respective coupled vector; on equality those covers and all records coincide. No deterministic truncation of the unbounded ancestral ages is needed for that proposed comparison.

For a finite calendar, matched entering states/pasts would receive the same unchanged boundary draw and a coupled current clock vector for each interval. The proposed induction charges at most one such bound per actual clock-product occurrence, plus the ancestral attachment. It must be proved against `actualCalendarTraceLaw`/`completeCalendarTraceLaw` and their unnormalized terminal fibres. Conditioning on an endpoint or success would change the argument. When inputs/pasts already differ, their own discrepancy budget must be charged.

The narrow next missing provider is therefore **a source-pinned two-positive-rate exponential coupling lemma with correct marginals, followed by a finite-product and measurable full-record pushforward bound**. Existing exponential-race/tail algebra is useful, but no such quantitative provider was found in the 27-module search. Finite-calendar fibre composition and actual physical age/bin/tree readout are the next consumers. A compiler check of the current single-bank law is not a check of these new statements.

Rational bank selection would still need supplied effective positive enclosures or a certified algebraic source cell. Local positive lower endpoints can certify relative accuracy; no global hidden rate floor is introduced. Arbitrary positive real source parameters remain unrestricted in the existence contract and do not become computable names. Perturbing demographic ages or inheritance probabilities is outside this rate-only proposal.
