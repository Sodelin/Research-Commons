## How history crosses a boundary and a read cut

There are three different histories. The underlying finite snapshot keeps old **genealogical structure**. The marked-record vector keeps actual active mergers and their **clock ages**. The decoration matrix keeps the **MRCA ages of old tree nodes**, independent of the emptied `State.history` list. They are related by named source theorems, not interchangeable by a cast.

An interval updates the matrix only where two different old ancestors become equal at its actual destination. A demographic boundary routes populations and preserves genealogies, matrix and shared register. `calendarMatrix` passes both the endpoint and old matrix to the next segment, with the interval duration added to the offset. `completeMatrix` passes that result into the ancestral tail. This directly addresses old subtrees: their nodes and previous ages survive every later graft.

A read cut inside an interval is implemented by `cutEndpoint`: active flags plus `age ≤ cut` choose the last actual endpoint; inactive records never fabricate a merger. `actual_history_cut_readout` ties it to the same clock compiler at the earlier horizon. The retained residual construction and `actual_epoch_history_source_law` derive the future transition law jointly with the whole past. Terminal-fibre products remain unnormalized; no unique pointwise conditional is selected on null histories.

## Proposed finite-bin quotient and remaining proofs

Two source-faithful routes are plausible. Neither is an available G6 timed provider merely because these components exist.

1. **Finite endpoint vector:** refine the actual demographic schedule at the requested cuts, retain the joint snapshot vector at these reads and complete terminal topology, then extract each pair's first coalescence bin from ancestry equality at the cuts. Old trees in snapshots and the terminal tree supply all merger nodes. The existing finite-history law is directly useful here; final-state-only `actual_source_joint_readout_tv` is insufficient for the whole vector.
2. **Finite carried decoration:** retain `Code × (Copy → Copy → Option (Fin (J+1)))` or an equivalent well-labelled tree decoration. The sentinel means a pair has not yet coalesced. Each genuine cross-operand merger writes the current epoch's bin; boundaries and self/holding steps leave old tags unchanged. Derive this as a quotient of the actual real-age fold. A freely chosen finite tag matrix is not source admission.

Exact obligations for either route:

- Fix bin conventions at cuts and prove the bin map is measurable with finite range. Instantiate the G6 contemporaneous-tip contract; handle diagonal/absent flags and empty panels explicitly.
- Prove the finite quotient equals the original **topology plus every internal-node bin** observation almost surely. For a pair-MRCA encoding, show each actual internal node has cross-child leaf witnesses and its bin is represented consistently; erase internal operand order and unrelated within-bin ranks. This equivalence has not been formalized in this note.
- Use the actual threshold/decoration certificates and `actual_complete_avoids_fixed_age` for the finite cut set. The generic rational infimum reader alone does not prove an actual threshold. Strict versus inclusive cut conventions agree only after this source nullity gate.
- Prove insertion/refinement of calendar **read cuts** preserves the actual joint trace law and original constant rate on every crossing physical edge. `actual_epoch_history_source_law` supplies same-clock epoch refinement ingredients; demographic boundaries, offsets and the final ancestral interval still need their complete composition. No new degree-two biological vertex or independently fitted rate is permitted.
- Establish either quotient transition laws or the finite joint-history PMF for the refined schedule, uniformly over every entering source snapshot and old carried bin decoration. Use actual `sourceIteration` and boundary kernels; do not assume the desired history/net theorem as an interface field.
- Lift the existing conditioned Poisson-prefix and per-operation budgets to that **joint** finite history, and then its observation quotient. Charge the sum over the retained primitive operations; preserve one joint record per locus and one rate/inheritance assignment across every menu row.
- Connect single-bin positive chain replacement to this quotient while grafting all old tagged subtrees back. [G6 Lemmas2–3](../../../research/2026-10-01-g6-effective-certification/PROOF.md) protect cut-crossing connectors, retain at most2J bigons per slot and retime positive replacement words between the same real endpoints. [Prior G4 CLOCK §§2–4](../../../research/2026-10-01-g4-admitted-testers-0819z/CLOCK.md) supplies the attributed cut/shared-rate construction. Different bin resolutions do not imply full metric-law TV approximation.

The source admission/core/target-preservation, all-size replacement bound and jointly feasible shared-parameter hazard-cell obligations remain the original G6 obligations. A successful finite-bin adapter would advance them; it would not by itself close the effective cloud, confidence, termination or negative endpoint consumers.
