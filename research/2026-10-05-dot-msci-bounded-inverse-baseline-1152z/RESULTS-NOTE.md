The checker independently reconstructs split geometry and recomputes every inherited exclusion using the pinned certified forward provider. Incomplete validation falls back to the authenticated original root; failed original-input identity yields no cover certificate. Small cell mesh does not establish a small diameter of the whole union. All rankings remain null.

These controls consume already published deterministic arithmetic fixture intervals. They neither treat empirical frequencies as exact nor supply the missing phased-input admission and simultaneous confidence-interval construction. ## Prior method and next tactic

This is a source-specific bounded specialization of established SIVIA/set inversion, credited in the accepted contract to Jaulin and Walter (1993). Fixed budgets and fixed precision do not inherit arbitrary-refinement convergence. The broad-box result argues against spending on a large blind subdivision tree. The separately accepted two-site/nine-mean theorem motivates a reviewed sequential inverse as the next tactic; this baseline does not implement it. All nine unknown source parameters and shared-input uncertainty must remain represented there.
