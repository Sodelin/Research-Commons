# G4: weighted automata and observable-algebra applicability

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 16 October 2026, 15:45 UTC. Bounded prior-method audit. No new master theorem, execution or novelty claim. General G3 and General G4 remain open.

## Result

The natural copy-append weighted-automaton route fails its finite-rank hypothesis even at an admitted finite ordinary target. The physical-cell-append interpretation has finite matrices for a fixed copy cap, but its words index different physical sources, not additional legal observations of the same unknown source. Its finite-dimensional linear span therefore does not give the required finite-I forcing theorem. Fixed-chart polynomial Noetherianity remains valid and useful, but does not establish one panel for all unknown architectures.

## 1. Exact prior and required interface

Carlyle and Paz, [Realizations by stochastic finite automata (1971)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-0000(71)80005-3), gives the classical finite-rank realization characterization (publisher abstract inspected). Balle and Mohri, [Spectral Learning of General Weighted Automata via Constrained Matrix Completion (2012), §2.2, Theorem 1](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2012/file/700fdb2ba62d4554dc268c65add4b16e-Paper.pdf), explicitly states that a real series on words has a finite weighted-automaton realization exactly when its concatenation Hankel matrix has finite rank; that rank is the minimum state dimension. Its §2.2 statement and surrounding definitions were read. Its learning results do not supply finite rank for a new physical observation process.

A proposed adapter here must specify which operation concatenation represents, show every matrix entry is an actual legal response or a known finite linear combination thereof, and bind all entries to one source and its shared tuple. Neither source size nor a finite capped compiler automatically establishes the required rank premise for arbitrarily many sampled copies.

## 2. Legal copy append: an exact failed premise

Reuse the reviewed [legal anchored Gram construction, §3](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/7356a7c5807dff20a6576c608f03a168a36712d7/research/2026-10-16-dot-g3-g4-direct-continuation-0532z/G4-LEGAL-ANCHORED-GRAM-ATTEMPT-0816.md). Fix an ordinary admitted target, two A anchors a1,a2, and add A copies z1,...,zk, with all other required taxa physically sampled and projectively summed out. Let h_k be the probability that each restricted triple is ((a1,zj),a2). This is one finite sum of completed-topology probabilities from a single legal union panel. There is no hidden forest readout or fresh-locus multiplication.

The inherited ordinary merger rule and projectivity give

    h_k = 2/((k+1)(k+2)) = integral_0^1 2(1-p)p^k dp.

Thus, for every r and every nonzero real vector c=(c0,...,cr),

    sum_(i,j=0)^r c_i c_j h_(i+j)
      = integral_0^1 2(1-p)(sum_i c_i p^i)^2 dp > 0.

Every (r+1)-square Hankel block has rank r+1. If a finite D-dimensional linear state updated by one fixed copy-append operator A represented this series, h_k=alpha^T A^k beta, its Hankel rank would be at most D, contradiction at r=D. The same obstruction applies to a finite-alphabet linear realization that preserves this unary sublanguage and its concatenation operation.

This is an explicit failure for this precise legal-observation encoding, reusing an already reviewed classical calculation. It does not exclude arbitrary nonlinear states, an alternative encoding without this compositional contract, or a target-specific finite certificate that need not reconstruct all future responses. In particular, it is not a G4 disproof. Infinite Hankel rank also does not imply that an algebra of scalar functions is not finitely generated.

## 3. Physical cell append: finite dimension, wrong equivalence question

The source-faithful [G3 finite-dimensional reachability reduction, §§2–4](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/9d21c0727564ae0ba101039d97b07c614d17f2e1/research/2026-10-06-dot-g3-global-source-reachability-0019z/REDUCTION.md) really does supply finite full-forest/register carriers for any fixed finite input cap. A fresh physical cell with tuple u acts by a matrix K(u), and a physical word acts by their product, with every required component using that same u.

For a fixed carrier, the span of matrix products is finite dimensional. This is genuine linear algebra, not a source-size bound. A linear dependence among products expresses an operator as a signed real linear combination of other operators. The source grammar admits chronological products of strict physical kernels, not arbitrary signed combinations. The dependence therefore does not supply a shorter physical representative, a finite positive witness, or exact membership in the physical semigroup.

Furthermore, these appended cells modify the source. Observations in G4 sample more copies or change an admitted observation row of the same fixed source; they do not insert chosen physical cells into an unknown rival. A word-equivalence algorithm for two supplied linear representations consequently answers a different question. The source reduction explicitly leaves unbounded append count and exact reachability unresolved even though its state dimension is fixed. Passing to a larger copy cap also changes that carrier. This route stops at that precise adapter/membership mismatch.

## 4. What finite generation would actually prove

Let S be the whole admitted source class and let A_I be the real algebra of functions on S generated by all legal I response coordinates. Suppose, as an additional theorem, A_I were generated by finitely many elements a1,...,ad. Each aj is itself a polynomial in finitely many legal coordinates; take their finite union J. Equality on J then implies equality of all of A_I, hence of the entire I law. The accepted entire-I-to-C supplier would give equality of C_T for every target. This would prove a stronger global finite-panel statement than the currently required target-relative claim.

No inspected supplier establishes this global finite generation. Section 2 does not refute it: it concerns rank under concatenation at one fixed target, whereas A_I concerns scalar functions varying over sources. Conflating these two algebras would be invalid.

On a fixed finite architecture with the inherited natural polynomial survival/coin compiler, there is a valid weaker statement. For fixed T, form the ideal in the finite polynomial parameter ring generated by all differences I_o(z)-I_o(T). Noetherianity supplies a finite subset of these differences generating the same ideal. Equality on that subset forces every I difference to vanish, including on the strict domain. Entire-I-to-C then gives C_T equality on that chart. For rational coordinates, denominators must be nonzero on the strict chart and can be cleared or localized. This argument does not require compactness or assumptions about boundary roots once the common finite polynomial-ring premise holds.

However, the selected subset can depend on rival architecture. The source catalogue in G4 contains arbitrarily many cells and hence no single supplied finite parameter ring for all-copy observations. A finite union of charts allows a finite union of panels; the actual unbounded union does not. An input-independent bounded core alone leaves unbounded private words and does not repair this point. Thus fixed-chart Noetherianity recovers prior progress but does not prove the needed quantifier interchange.

## 5. Remaining exact obligation and stopping decision

The current [finite-I bridge equivalence](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/9d21c0727564ae0ba101039d97b07c614d17f2e1/research/2026-10-13-codex-g4-renewed/whole-source/continuation-2110z/FINITE-INDEPENDENT-BRIDGE-EXACT-EQUIVALENCE.md) asks, for each fixed strict T, for ONE finite legal J_T such that EVERY finite unknown-size rival agreeing on J_T agrees on C_T. Whole-source generation or a target-relative radical/zero-set implication uniform over architectures would suffice. None of the inspected weighted-automata results establishes that premise. Conversely, no exact all-prefix physical rival family was obtained.

The [reviewed anchor-budget correction](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/7356a7c5807dff20a6576c608f03a168a36712d7/research/2026-10-16-dot-g3-g4-direct-continuation-0532z/G4-INDEPENDENT-ANCHOR-BUDGET-CORRECTION.md) preserves useful exposure and squared-coin bounds but does not bound word length or provide this uniform observable algebra. Its single-cell inequality is not preserved by ordinary continuation. Neither gap should be filled by an assumed linear realization.

Decision: stop this finite-rank import route. Preserve the exact negative applicability result and fixed-chart reuse. This checkpoint preserves the failed import as a coordination reference; it does not add a new biological theorem or resolve either master.
