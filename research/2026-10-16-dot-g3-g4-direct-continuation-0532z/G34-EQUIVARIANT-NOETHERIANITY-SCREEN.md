## Second obstacle: finite equation orbits need not give a finite panel

Even if an equivariant ideal were supplied, its conclusion needs a separate observation adapter. For example, I=(x_1,x_2,...) is generated by one orbit under finite permutations. On the set of all finite-support real sequences, its zero locus is the zero sequence. Nevertheless any finite set of coordinate observations is matched by a nonzero finite-support sequence supported beyond that set. Finite orbit generation therefore does not alone imply finite coordinate determination against unknown finite support.

This is an elementary logical countercontrol, not an encoding of biological rivals or a G4 disproof. In the actual model, copy-label permutations within one allocation can be redundant by exchangeability, but they preserve total copy count. Physical-parameter transformations do not become extra observed values of the SAME source. A proof would need to establish exactly which entire equation orbits are implied by a finite legal panel.
