# What the hemlock integration establishes and what comes next

Codex G6 scientific integration, 8 October 2026. This note describes the confirmed Linda Raubeson/Holman hemlock question. It supplies no professor endorsement or contact, new empirical capture verdict, live API call, or completed G6 biological admission.

The immediate result is a reproducible, small **actual-sequence comparison**: the nuclear 4CL1 marker groups chinensis with caroliniana, while the plastid rbcL marker groups chinensis with Japanese sieboldii. The respective descriptive column-bootstrap supports are 100/100 and 93/100. This reproduces the direction of the reported disagreement on a deliberately smaller dataset. It does not discriminate chloroplast capture from the other processes that can produce gene-tree disagreement.

## What Holman and colleagues already established

The governing publication is *A New Species and Introgression in Eastern Asian Hemlocks (Pinaceae: Tsuga)*, Systematic Botany 42, 733–746 (2017), DOI [10.1600/036364417X696474](https://doi.org/10.1600/036364417X696474). The [USDA-hosted complete paper](https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/55598.pdf) was actually read; its SHA256 is `81d19502fe93b40f68a8a673060670fad7e0588bb9bd186018004c372dae26ab`. The extracted text inspected here has SHA256 `9c0059d6ffcbebcf0d45c0e1bfbefd4f1473383324ede16f46cb6738936ce24a`. Methods/results are on pp.734–738; discussion on pp.738–741; specimen/accession and sequencing metrics are in Appendices1–2, including the ulleungensis treatment's specimen list.

| Published evidence | Actual reported scope and method |
|---|---|
| Nuclear sequence sampling | Tissue from 14 hemlock individuals was collected for DNA work. New 4CL sampling is reported as three diversifolia, two each of caroliniana, chinensis, Japanese sieboldii and Ulleungdo hemlocks, and one canadensis. PCR amplicons were cloned; 10–16 colonies per accession were sequenced to assess intra-individual variation. Previously published nuclear sequences supplied additional outgroup information. These are not 10–16 independently sampled genomic loci. |
| Two nuclear gene copies | Two distinct 4CL paralogs, aligned to 1,049 bp each. An ILD test found no significant difference between their phylogenetic signals, and they were concatenated: 2,098 bp, 46 parsimony-informative characters, selected HKY+I model. A nonsignificant test does not establish independent ancestry or identical genealogies. |
| Plastid sequence sampling | Nearly complete plastomes from one individual each of canadensis, caroliniana, chinensis, diversifolia, heterophylla, Japanese sieboldii and Ulleungdo hemlocks, plus Nothotsuga longibracteata. The plastid alignment is 122,525 bp, with 1,574 parsimony-informative characters; selected GTR+Γ with four rate categories. A large linked plastome is not thousands of independent ancestry blocks. |
| Original tree inference | Geneious9.0.5 sequence processing, MAFFT alignment, PAUP*4.0b10 maximum parsimony with TBR and 1,000 random-addition searches; jModelTest2.1.4/AIC model selection; GARLI2.0 maximum likelihood with 1,000 bootstrap replicates; Sumtrees3.3.1 consensus; visual and ILD incongruence comparisons. No posterior capture probability or calibrated sequence-to-G6-law channel is supplied by these procedures. |
| Rooted relationships | The reported nuclear phylogram uses canadensis as outgroup: caroliniana/chinensis is sister to the oceanic diversifolia/sieboldii/Ulleungdo clade. The plastid phylogram uses Nothotsuga as outgroup: caroliniana is sister to diversifolia/Ulleungdo (80% MP/77% ML bootstrap), and that group is sister to chinensis/Japanese sieboldii. All other plastid branches are reported with 100% MP and ML support. |
| Independent phenotypic question | Quantitative morphology used 78 wild-collected herbarium specimens; the broader common-garden morphology/phenology study included 18 individuals. Six of eleven leaf characters were retained after significance screening. Phenology was observed in 2010–2012. These support species-delimitation discussion, not a direct estimate of capture probability. |

The authors explicitly say the divergent topologies **suggest** capture of a chinensis chloroplast by Japanese sieboldii and refer to the capture as **putative**. They also discuss ancestral polymorphism/non-coalescence in conifers and possible ancient hybridization involving caroliniana. The described analysis compares gene trees and morphology; it does not fit and discriminate a declared capture model against an incomplete-lineage-sorting model. Strong support for a marker tree is not the probability of its proposed historical cause. The capture suggestion is already the authors' result; rediscovering the discordance is not a new biological finding.

## Actual recoverable data and our addition

The [Dryad dataset](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.2r12j), version21609, has CC0 metadata and four inventoried files: separate 4CL alignments, concatenated 4CL alignments, whole-plastid alignments, and a needle-morphology CSV. [Metadata](DRYAD-DATASET-METADATA.json) and [file inventory](DRYAD-FILE-INVENTORY.json) were retrieved; original download attempts returned401/403, which remain saved. No successful full-alignment retrieval or full-paper replication is claimed. The inventory alone does not provide raw sequencing reads or clone chromatograms.

An alternative standard anonymous GenBank retrieval returned 15 versioned records: plastid/4CL1/4CL2 for five paper-listed species. The tiny pilot uses eight of those records, cpDNA plus 4CL1 for the **same four paper-listed specimens** of chinensis, caroliniana, Japanese sieboldii and diversifolia. [The exact source and processing manifest](REAL-BASELINE/MANIFEST.json) retains each accession, voucher join, annotation, raw hash and recipe. NCBI's no-restrictions-on-reuse policy is recorded separately from depositor IP rights; GenBank deposits are not relabelled CC0 or Apache. chinensis rbcL has no deposited gene annotation: its candidate region is explicitly homology-inferred using 33 consistent 40-base exact anchors and coding-boundary checks.

Our actual additions are source/specimen traceability, an accessible offline comparison, exact preserved preprocessing and original software receipt replay, an explicit uncertainty/admission boundary, and executable controls for the original mathematical source solver. The pilot uses one 1,048-site nuclear marker and one 1,428-site plastid marker, unique reference-centered global alignments, uncorrected-distance neighbor joining and 100 fixed-seed column-bootstrap draws. It omits ulleungensis, population replication and outgroups, and therefore does not reproduce the paper's rooted relationships or identify capture direction. Marker choice before tree outcomes is author-reported; no immutable independent preregistration pin exists.

Two separate **synthetic exact-law controls** demonstrate that the original source producer/checker can distinguish different displayed-split targets within its complete declared 15-source, zero-hybrid registry. The real Tsuga intake instead produces a checked UNKNOWN/NOT_ADMITTED result. These are different evidential steps. Our established mathematics supplies conditional target/source contracts and shared-source requirements; it does not turn the estimated marker trees into exact calendar genealogy laws, infer every hidden edge, or prove an empirical capture history.

## Concrete milestones toward a useful scientific analysis

1. **Complete the published-data replay.** Obtain the original permitted NEXUS/CSV bytes through a working public route and authenticate their recorded deposit hashes. Preserve every specimen, allele/clone and paralog ID; reproduce the separate and combined 4CL and whole-plastid analyses with the original roots/model settings, then show method and alignment sensitivity. Done means published inputs and reported relationships are auditable, including disagreements; a replacement alignment must remain labelled a reconstruction.
2. **Define biological observation units.** Audit the two 4CL copies' orthology/paralogy and clone/allele assignments; establish which nuclear regions are genuinely independent, and retain whole-plastid linkage. Justify Tsuga's compartment-specific transmission and any recombination/partition model from appropriate evidence. Add justified multi-individual nuclear sampling or additional existing public independent loci where available. Done means a model has justified units and inheritance premises, rather than counting aligned sites or clones as iid loci.
3. **Compare explicit competing explanations.** Predeclare a bifurcating species history with incomplete lineage sorting; a history with plastid introgression/capture; and, if justified, alternatives involving older caroliniana introgression or an unsampled ancestral lineage. Separately test sensitivity to gene-tree error, substitution-model misspecification and paralog/phase assignment. Fit these using an established, suitable coalescent/network implementation and the same admitted observations; use model adequacy and calibrated simulation or predictive comparison. Posterior or likelihood preferences must state priors, sampling/model assumptions and unresolved equivalent histories. Our existing fixed-six-copy clock-JC/pulse numerical solver has a different input contract and cannot silently fit these hemlock matrices.
4. **Build the sequence-to-source bridge.** Propagate alignment, tree, phase and sampling uncertainty into a declared joint observation law/confidence object with a justified error calibration. Use one original source and shared parameter bank where the model requires them; do not pool linked plastid genes or separate compartments as independent genealogies by default. The current G6 fixed-calendar bank gate covers a narrow exact component; the general all-source effective image/certification interface and empirical channel remain open. Done means the practical solver receives a valid biological contract, or a precise refusal reports the remaining premise.
5. **Return an interpretable comparison.** The application should show baseline relationships, which declared history/target classes remain compatible, uncertainty and sensitivity, source receipts and reproducible commands on the same data. State separately whether reticulation is supported, whether plastid capture is distinguishable from alternatives, and whether direction/timing are identifiable. A useful result may exclude a model or show that several histories remain possible. No method guarantees a definitive answer from these two nuclear paralogs and one plastid history; extra independent evidence may be necessary.

This path needs no AlphaGenome call: hemlock tree/history analysis is a separate plant scientific lane, and current AlphaGenome/molecular work has human/mouse contracts. The implemented pilot, source controls and public workbench all run offline. An API is a delivery interface, not a substitute for additional independent observations or the missing model/channel justification. Root owns any broader application/API choice.
