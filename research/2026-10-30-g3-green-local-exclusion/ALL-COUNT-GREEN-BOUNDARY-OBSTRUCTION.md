## 5. Universal finite-sequence Green identity, including boundaries

For arbitrary 0<rho<sigma and finite real p_0,...,p_N satisfying P_p(rho)=P_p(sigma)=0, define

    Tquad(rho,sigma)=sum_(i<j) p_i p_j rho^i sigma^j
                        +(1/2)sum_i p_i^2(rho sigma)^i.

Put w=sqrt(rho sigma), c=(1/2)log(sigma/rho)>0, q_i=w^i p_i, and extend q by zero outside0..N. The two moments become sum q_i exp(±ci)=0. Define for every integer i

    f_i=sum_j sinh(c|i-j|)q_j.

Both moment identities show f_i=0 outside0..N, and also f_0=f_N=0. Thus f has finite support: no boundary tail or endpoint term is discarded. With Lf_i=f_(i-1)-2cosh(c)f_i+f_(i+1), the exact fundamental-solution identity gives

    Lf=2sinh(c)q.

Finite summation by parts gives

    sum_i f_i Lf_i
      =-sum_i(f_(i+1)-f_i)^2-2(cosh(c)-1)sum_i f_i^2.

On the other hand P_p(rho)P_p(sigma)=0 implies Tquad(rho,sigma)+Tquad(sigma,rho)=0, and direct symmetrization yields Tquad=(1/2)sum_i q_i f_i. Therefore

    Tquad(rho,sigma)
      =-[sum_i(f_(i+1)-f_i)^2
              +2(cosh(c)-1)sum_i f_i^2]/[4sinh(c)].          (4)

If p!=0, then q!=0 and f!=0 by Lf=2sinh(c)q. Hence (4) is STRICTLY negative. This identity applies to every finite N and every real signed p satisfying the two moments; there is no sampling or positivity assumption on the retiming coefficients.
