## Coefficient representation

All coefficients of target data, residuals, base, h and parameter jets share one fixed real embedding. The embedding consists of a rational squarefree polynomial and a rational interval isolating exactly one real root. Polynomial coefficient lists are ascending powers of that root. Scalars are exact integer/fraction strings, never floating point. Every coefficient is interpreted at that root across the entire packet.

An implementation may require an irreducible minimal polynomial instead, but must prove that restriction preserves the representation. This checker permits reducible squarefree polynomials; it uses root-specific gcd tests and DOES NOT claim the quotient is a field. Never infer element nonzero just because its remainder modulo a reducible defining polynomial is nonzero.

The generator is a constant coefficient encoding, not an extra source variable or independent per-row knob. Treating it as an added physical variable changes the polynomial-map dimension and its error-bound duty.

## Checkable stages and output meanings

1. Parse the exact root embedding; reject malformed, ambiguous, nonreal or repeated-root encodings.
2. Check a fixed base and positive leading terms for p_i and 1-p_i.
3. Compute d from supplied F and N from physical variables; compute R=d(6d-3)^(N-1).
4. Compose EVERY supplied residual with the SAME jets and verify all coefficients below q vanish.
5. Check h's first nonzero coefficient at order s and q>R max(r,s).
6. Return the scoped polynomial verdict and byte hashes.

CERTIFICATE_NOT_VERIFIED means this supplied certificate did not verify. It is not a proof that the polynomial fibre is empty. INCONCLUSIVE_RESOURCE means the configured prototype budget was reached. It is not a mathematical rejection or an unknown-size stopping certificate. The prototype's finite input caps are operational limits, not assumptions in G4. An unbounded theoretical enumeration over certificate lengths is not a terminating negative decision algorithm.

The hand theorem yields existence of nearby exact strict zeros. The checker does not compute a certified correction radius, display corrected source coordinates, or construct a specific exact zero. A downstream real-algebraic witness extractor or separately verified isolator remains necessary when actual source coordinates are required.
