## Status vocabulary

- **Compiled component:** exact source bytes passed the matching compiler,
  with a source hash and printed axiom audit. Its precise hypotheses apply
- **Source bridge pending:** a real graph/process/observation connection is
  not Lean proved; a generic interface alone does not discharge it
- **Hand-only accepted:** the declared mathematical result has a reviewed
  hand proof, but no complete matching Lean theorem
- **Open:** the mathematical endpoint itself is unresolved
- **Resource blocked:** an existing finite certificate was not completed
  under the recorded bounded resource attempt
- **Implementation tested:** executable checks exist without a full Lean
  specification-to-code refinement or all-execution proof
