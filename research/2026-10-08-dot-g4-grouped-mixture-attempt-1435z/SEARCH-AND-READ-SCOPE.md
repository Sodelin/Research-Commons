The 2015 predecessor is subsumed by this paper and is not counted as independent evidence. A later generalized-mixture result appeared in discovery but is not used for a substantive claim.
- **Lovász–Szegedy, 0901.0929v2:** Definition 1.2; Section 2.3, including Theorem 2.2, Proposition 2.3 and Claim 2.4; Lemma 3.2; Theorem 7.1 and Corollaries 7.2–7.3; introductory step-graphon forcing context. The separate original step-function forcing reference was not reread in this attempt and is not treated as a newly authenticated theorem here.
- **Forman–Haulk–Pitman, 1101.5619v4:** definitions of independently-generated exchangeable hierarchies, Theorem 2, and the opening of its proof. This is a representation input, not a finite-support or finite-forcing result.
- **Bertoin–Le Gall, Flow1.pdf:** Sections 2.2–2.3 and Example 1. These contain the paintbox and conditional Kingman-frequency facts used by the new deduction.
- **Benvenuti–Farina technical survey:** Section II and Theorem 2 of the author manuscript. An institutional record for Anderson–Deistler–Farina–Benvenuti's earlier original realization paper was found, but its full text was not obtained. No technical statement is attributed to that unread paper.
- **Bhaskar–Song, 1309.5056v3:** Theorems 5–6 and 11, Corollary 7 and its proof. The distinction between target complexity and complexity of both compared models was checked directly.

No claim is made that every page of these papers was read. arXiv version metadata and available publisher/author identities were checked.
