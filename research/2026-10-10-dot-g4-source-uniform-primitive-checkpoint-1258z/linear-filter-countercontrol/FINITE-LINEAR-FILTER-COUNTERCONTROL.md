## Exact non-source countercontrol

Let P be the exchangeable paintbox partition kernel obtained by assigning each current root independently to one of three equally likely colours and joining all roots of a common colour. On root counts,

    P(n,j)=(3)_j S(n,j)/3^n,

where (3)_j is the falling factorial and S(n,j) is a Stirling number of the second kind. Set P(0,0)=1. This defines one consistent partition law at all sample caps; its projectivity is literal deletion of labels from the same IID colour assignment. Let E be ordinary Kingman coalescence with q=exp(-t)=1/2. Form the mathematical mixture

    K=(P+E)/2.

The single mixing variable is shared across all selected roots. Therefore K is again exchangeable and projectively consistent. Every admissible count entry K(n,j), 1<=j<=n, is strictly positive because the ordinary component is. The zero-root state remains isolated. None of this introduces a legal shared-selector operation into the original source grammar: this mixture is only a diagnostic outside that grammar.

Thus even strict positivity of all admissible count entries, exchangeability and projectivity do not imply the source filter. The original two-arm dynamics and their single physical routing operation are essential. This does not refute the filter on actual sources, and K is not claimed to match any supplied target.
