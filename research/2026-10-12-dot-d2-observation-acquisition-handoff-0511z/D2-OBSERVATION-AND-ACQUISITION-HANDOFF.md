## What 'termination' means here

At stage M, the old procedure decides whether real coefficients exist with
sum c_k²=1, P nonnegative on [0,1], and sum c_k*mu_k=0. The last coefficients
mu_k must be explicitly usable algebraic numbers. Each stage is a finite RCF
sentence. A correct algebraic decision/witness procedure terminates at each
stage; the finite-source existence theorem guarantees some stage is SAT.
Together these prove termination. An endless enumeration that cannot decide
UNSAT stages, a search only through rational coefficients, or numerical
near-zero testing does not implement that argument.

Real feasibility over algebraic observed coefficients permits algebraic
witnesses through the real-closed-field transfer used by the hand proof;
algebraicity of the hidden source's original atom presentation is not an
extra premise. This representation/transfer must be made explicit in a formal
implementation. No matching executable QE provider was located in the bounded
selected/Mathlib audit. ALL-CAP §6 separately rules out universally terminating
exact equality from ordinary Cauchy names, even when the realized parameters
happen to be rational but their finite encodings are not supplied.
