# Infinite-persistent-array attempt: unresolved full implication

Contributor: dot, 9 October 2026, 16:40 UTC. Working failure record, not an independently reviewed new theorem or a G4 completion. Do not treat the supporting condition below as a global localization result.

The original task remains to exclude every unknown-size positive rival of one fixed finite target by a finite original certificate, or construct exact whole-prefix positive rivals for that target at every depth. This pass tested the infinite persistent-cell escape left by the separately reviewed local insertion theorem and the finite-persistent companion. It did not repair it.

## 1. Full-forest obstruction to the old peeling proof

A bounded total COMMON clock does not by itself supply a first persistent cell in the limiting chronological order. Cells can accumulate immediately after the initial ordinary interval. Each fixed positive-time cell can then see a finite entering root population. The accepted finite/fixed-omega proof cannot simply extract the first bare deterministic cohort in this setting.

A possible full repair would prove that a positive ordinary left factor of the all-copy endpoint must correspond to a genuinely ordinary initial interval even for such accumulation sources. No such ordinary-factor rigidity theorem was proved. Cancelling an algebraic ordinary operator does not establish that its residual is an actual chronological positive source.

## 2. The linear asymptotic interchange is valid; the logarithmic one is not supplied

Suppose, conditionally, a countable diagonal representation has been obtained:

    phi_target(n)=sum_i phi_n(t_i,p_i)+beta*n*(n-1),

with beta>=0, sum_i t_i<infinity, phi_n=log R_n, t_i>0, and 0<p_i<=1/4. This displayed representation is a hypothesis here, not a proved compactification of every original full-response array.

Set h=log(g/(1-g)), b=-log(2 sqrt(p))=log cosh(h/2), and

    psi_n=t*n^2/4-phi_n >=0.

The inequality follows because k(n-k)<=n^2/4 in every positive binomial summand. Dominated convergence at quadratic scale gives t_target=sum_i t_i+4 beta. Fatou at linear scale then gives sum_i b_i+beta<=b_target.

The forcing lane supplied the important correction to an initially proposed linear-mass escape. For S=Bin(n,1/2)-n/2, binomial symmetry gives exactly

    psi_n=n*b-log E[exp(-t*S^2) cosh(h*S)].

Since cosh>=1 and Jensen gives -log E exp(-t*S^2)<=t*n/4,

    0<=psi_n/n<=b+t/4.

The preceding Fatou bound and finite clock make this summable over cells. Thus dominated convergence does apply at linear scale, and

    b_target=sum_i b_i+beta.

The attempted claim of possible lost linear mass is withdrawn. This correction is attributed to the forcing lane.

At logarithmic scale put rho_n=psi_n-n*b. Completing the square gives rho_n>=-h^2/(4t). For each fixed strict cell the inherited finite-cell saddle asymptotic gives rho_n/log n -> 1/2. Consequently, IF sum_i h_i^2/t_i is finite, the summably shifted nonnegative terms and Fatou bound the number of persistent cells by the finite target's cell count. This is a conditional supporting observation only.

Neither finite clock nor summable b forces this extra energy summability. For example rational survivals q_i=1-4^(-i) and rational coins g_i=1/2+2^(-i-2), i>=1, have finite sum -log q_i and finite sum b_i, while h_i^2/(-log q_i) stays bounded below by a positive constant for large i. Thus the energy sum diverges. These cells do not match a claimed fixed target and are not a G4 counterexample. Deriving an energy bound from the COMPLETE exact target equations remains unproved.

## 3. A tested augmented-resource inequality fails

A proposed alternative was to use the exact asymptotic resources t and b in a global target-relative score. Write x=exp(t)>1 and y=4p in (0,1]. Their totals under addition are X=product x_i and Y=product y_i. A resource-matched single cell has

    R2_target=1+Y*(X-1)/2,
    R3_target=1+(3/4)*Y*(X^2-1).

For two cells, exact R2 equality gives R3_product<R3_target; a direct symbolic substitution verified the algebra. That sign does NOT induct to arbitrary words. The supplied exact Fraction checker constructs two strict three-cell examples with the same two resource totals and exact R2 agreement, but opposite R3 signs:

- x=(5,6,11/10), y=(49/50,9/10,8620/13631): R3 residual 1324783593/27262000 >0.
- x=(2,2,2), y=(9/10,9/10,882/1427): R3 residual -12691161/4566400 <0.

All y correspond to interior coins and all x to strictly positive arm durations. A common ordinary clock below 1/X can be supplied by strict pads to both compared words. The target is different between these two examples. Neither example matches R3, the rest of the diagonals, or the complete forest kernel. They refute only the proposed universal low-coordinate sign; they are not exact all-prefix rivals or a new bounded forcing theorem.

The discovery screen used 20,000 floating-point samples for each of four word lengths and is not a proof. The rational checker was subsequently actually executed and verifies the two displayed counterchecks exactly. No full source simulation, global optimizer, source factorization, QE or Lean build ran.

## 4. Exact remaining obligation

This attempt does not show that target equality bounds the bias-over-duration energy, does not classify initially accumulating persistent cells, and supplies no legal exact-prefix construction. The finite-persistent/local results must not be extended over that gap.
