## 7. Finite lawful tomography after the source reduction

A single full topology law with four copies A1,...,A4, one copy j, and one of every remaining original taxon suffices to recover the COMPLETE forest distribution K_4 on A's edge. Here is an explicit finite triangular decoder, so no extra private-box interface is assumed.

For each rooted binary labelled forest F on the four A labels, order its component trees by their least leaf label. For any entering forest F' to yield T_F after ordinary completion, every component of F' must be a clade of T_F avoiding j. It is therefore contained within a component of F: F' refines F. If F' has the same number of components as F, this forces F'=F, including its internal labelled topology.

Thus the matrix from entering forest probabilities to the probabilities of the selected final trees T_F is triangular in decreasing number of components. Its diagonal at a forest with r components is the strictly positive ordinary probability

    kappa_r=product_(k=2)^(r+1) 1/binom(k,2),
