## Why the proposed descent did not close that coverage

Word length is well founded on each supplied finite word, but the original problem supplies no maximum initial length. Unrolling one last-cell split at a time therefore gives the already available fixed-length family. A finite induction summary must handle the whole unknown suffix language through one shared source tuple.

Strict positive clock decrease also does not itself give a well-founded discrete rank. Summably weak actual positive cells can keep a positive total reserve. The accepted strong/weak source analysis bounds the strong head and approximates the weak remainder; exact boundary membership is not supplied by the approximation. A proof that treats every weak remainder as an ordinary proxy would lose SAME-source paired clocks and chronology, as the fifth Codex cycle explicitly checks.

A5's value-space dimension drops only on its justified constant-carrier branch. Normal-dependent isolated points with arbitrary integer repetitions remain at its terminal stage. The newly accepted global-support example goes further: every ambient global support covector can ignore an active INDEPENDENT direction on an attained original BOTH face. Consequently applying a COMMON dimension-drop rule to that zero pullback does not decide the relative coupled problem. Neither fact proves that a different source-specific rank is impossible; neither supplies one.

The primary WSTS theorem also needs the missing structure. Finkel–Schnoebelen, *Well-structured transition systems everywhere!*, Definition 3.2, Proposition 3.5 and Theorem 3.6, require a compatible well-quasi-order, a decidable comparison and an effective finite predecessor-basis operation for upward-closed goals. The original exact terminal fibre has not been proved upward-closed for a source-valid order with those operations. Their theorem concerns coverability under those hypotheses; it does not manufacture them from finite matrix dimension or positive entries. See the [primary paper](https://www.cmi.ac.in/~madhavan/courses/concurrency2017/literature/finkel-schnoebelen-wsts.pdf). This is an applicability check, not a source-faithful undecidability reduction.

Replacing individual states by parameterized regions is a possible way around a pointwise order obstruction, as the ordinary summary above demonstrates. But exact predecessor acceleration for those regions needs precisely the missing unbounded-source summary or terminal theorem. Allowing arbitrary finite region syntax removes a fixed format restriction; it does not ensure that the exact saturation has a finite effectively discoverable summary.
