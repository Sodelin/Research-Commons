## 1. Source, norm, and inherited premises

Use the original natural private unmarked INDEPENDENT bridge-word grammar, with every population survival and inheritance parameter strictly in (0,1), finite length, current-root routing, opaque old subtrees, and one physical parameter assignment across all root arities. Let S_n be its COMPLETE labelled rooted-forest endpoint image through entering-root cap n. Positive ordinary edges are included; the algebraic identity may be used as an empty multiplier in statements below, but is not called a strict positive population.

Write E_h=E(exp(-h)), h>=0, and lambda_n=binom(n,2). For an actual kernel K put h(K)=-log b2(K). Source composition multiplies all no-merger diagonals and adds h. Restriction pi_(n-1) discards the fresh n-input row and retains EVERY complete lower row. It is an algebra homomorphism because composition at k roots uses only rows of arity at most k. No chosen diagonal or merger-count quotient replaces those full rows.

The original forest convolution is

    (A*B)_k(w)=sum_(u in F_k) A_k(u)
                  sum_(v in F_|u|) B_|u|(v) 1{graft(u,v)=w}. (1)

This is the inherited current-root graft law; each whole component of u becomes one input token of B. It applies linearly to signed proof elements as well as to physical kernels.

Use the faithful original RIGHT-graft matrix M_K on all prebuilt labelled forests through n. Its singleton-input rows recover every fresh source row. An actual M_K is stochastic and M_(KL)=M_K M_L. Define

    d_n(A,B)=(1/2)||M_A-M_B||_infinity,

where the induced infinity norm is maximum absolute row sum. This equals maximum fresh-row total variation: every prebuilt row is an opaque graft of a fresh row on its current roots, so its norm cannot exceed that fresh-row norm, and all fresh rows themselves occur among matrix rows. This is a norm of COMPLETE forest differences in the faithful original action, not of the ordinary-diagonalizing signed basis.

The accepted weak-factor provider supplies, for every actual K at cap n,

    d_n(K,E_(h(K))) <= D_n h(K) sqrt(1-exp(-h(K))),
    D_n=2 binom(n,2) [(3/2)binom(n,3)+27binom(n,4)].     (2)

Its physical telescoping proof also gives

    d_n(K,E_(h(K))) <= D_n h(K) sqrt(eta)               (3)

if every genuine bare bigon in the chosen physical word has pair loss at most eta. Ordinary factors create no matching error. These bounds are uniform in finite word length, but D_n depends on the cap. The relevant sources are the accepted weak-factor reconstruction and its original G6 full-forest estimate, already pinned in the earlier threshold packet.

## 2. No positive source lies in a nontrivial projective identity fibre

For n>=3, let A be an actual positive source, or a projective stochastic source-closure kernel through n. If pi_(n-1)(A)=I, then b2(A)=1. Selected-label projectivity and the pair union bound give

    Pr(any merger among k roots)<=binom(k,2)(1-b2(A))=0

for every k<=n. Thus A=I on its COMPLETE cap-n action. Old subtrees are unchanged when no current roots merge.

In particular, if K is nonsingular and an actual appended A satisfies

    pi_(n-1)(K A)=pi_(n-1)(K),

then algebraic cancellation in the lower capped group gives pi_(n-1)(A)=I, and hence A=I through n. The same holds for prepending A. A strict positive word has pair survival less than one, so it cannot be such a nontrivial correction. The statement also covers source-closure multipliers, where the identity itself is allowed.

Therefore the nontrivial kernel of an AMBIENT projective group map is not an available physical correction space. A serial lift preserving the same lower ordinary duration must spend additional hazard and compensate elsewhere, or retune the existing physical word. This is an application of the accepted pair-one theorem, not a newly attributed pair-union argument.

## 3. The genuinely new arity is an exact square-zero layer

In the signed capped graft algebra define

    J_n={v : v_k=0 for k<n, and v_n(singletons)=0}.       (4)

Any additional actual affine/projective identities can be imposed on J_n; they do not change the following identities. They are proved directly from (1), with no ambient-matrix substitution.

For v,w in J_n, the only potentially nonzero row of v*w is row n. For w_|u| to be nonzero, |u| must equal n. The only n-root forest on n tokens is the singleton forest, whose v coefficient is zero. Hence

    J_n*J_n=0.                                          (5)

For every t>=0,

    E_t*v=exp(-lambda_n t)v,        v*E_t in J_n.         (6)

The first identity follows because the left ordinary factor must leave all n roots separate to call v's only nonzero row. The second follows from the lower zero rows and the zero singleton coefficient. These identities also extend algebraically to negative ordinary times as proof operations.

Now suppose K is an actual source with h=h(K) and BOTH

    pi_(n-1)(K)=pi_(n-1)(E_h),
    b_n(K)=exp(-lambda_n h).                             (7)

The first line is complete lower-forest equality, not lower diagonal rank. The second is the additional highest no-merger equation, not a consequence of the first. Then K-E_h lies in J_n. Define its normalized full new-row error

    v(K)=exp(lambda_n h)(K-E_h) in J_n,
    K=E_h*(I+v(K)).                                     (8)

All allowed labelled n-forest coordinates remain in v. Their inherited projectivity and graft identities are retained. No coordinate of v can be independently chosen as a source control.

Define the genuine ordinary transport on this layer by

    T_t(v)=E_t^(-1)*v*E_t=exp(lambda_n t)(v*E_t).         (9)

It preserves J_n and T_(s+t)=T_s T_t. If K,L both satisfy (7), at respective hazards h,k, then their actual product also satisfies (7), and (5),(6) give the EXACT composition law

    v(KL)=T_k(v(K))+v(L).                                (10)

There is no approximation and no omitted multi-insertion term: the cross term is zero by (5). With chronological factors K1,...,KL and h_i=h(K_i),

    v(K1...KL)=sum_i T_(h_(i+1)+...+h_L)(v(K_i)).         (11)

Positive ordinary gaps have v=0 but contribute their true hazard to the suffix transport. Thus older residuals are transported by the duration AFTER them. Reversing this orientation would give the wrong equation.

In the ordinary RIGHT spectral decomposition of the n-input row, the highest nonzero root count is at most n-1, and T_t multiplies its root-count-r eigenspace by exp((lambda_n-lambda_r)t). This spectral interpretation is only algebraic postprocessing of the entire signed row. It is not permission to drop a forest shape or to replace the true physical suffix by a fictitious common clock before (7) has been proved for each block.

Consequently, after (7) is genuinely achieved for the blocks, exact full cap-n ordinary return is equivalent to the WHOLE vector sum in (11) being zero. This is an all-n structural reduction of one projective step; it supplies no sign-rich actual blocks, no zero of their reachable residual image, and no hazard bound by itself.

## 4. Explicit ordinary-inverse norm

The ordinary right-graft generator Q has, on a forest with r current roots, diagonal entry -lambda_r and total nonnegative outgoing merger rate lambda_r. Therefore

    ||Q||_infinity<=2lambda_n.

The ordinary inverse is a mathematical matrix inverse, M_(E_t)^(-1)=exp(-tQ). The elementary induced-norm exponential bound gives

    ||M_(E_t)^(-1)||_infinity<=exp(2lambda_n t).          (12)

This is valid on the complete finite labelled-forest state space, including old subtrees and absorbing one-root rows. The stronger exact inverse norm F_n(t) is already supplied by the accepted padded-conjugator budget proof; (12) is a deliberately simpler explicit bound. No inverse ordinary edge is inserted physically.

## 5. Every actual two-sided repair of a fixed defect has a positive cost

Let K be any actual cap-n kernel with h=h(K), and set

    e=d_n(K,E_h).

Let P,R be actual source words, with either algebraic identity multiplier optionally allowed, and put p=h(P), r=h(R), delta=p+r. Suppose their ACTUAL positive composition makes a complete ordinary return:

    P*K*R=E_(h+delta).                                  (13)

Pair multiplication forces precisely this target duration. Let B=E_p*K*E_r. Stochastic telescoping and (2) give

    d_n(B,P K R)
       <=d_n(P,E_p)+d_n(R,E_r)
       <=D_n delta sqrt(1-exp(-delta)).                 (14)

To recover K's original defect, cancel only the known ordinary matrices:

    M_K-M_(E_h)
       =M_(E_p)^(-1) [M_B-M_(E_(h+delta))] M_(E_r)^(-1).

Combining (12)--(14) proves the uniform-in-length necessary bound

    e <= D_n exp(2lambda_n delta)
                    delta sqrt(1-exp(-delta))
      <= D_n exp(2lambda_n delta) delta^(3/2).            (15)

In particular, if delta<=H for a specified finite reserve H and e>0, then n>=3, D_n>0, and

    delta >= [e/(D_n exp(2lambda_n H))]^(2/3)>0.          (16)

The conclusion permits arbitrary finite numbers of genuine cells in BOTH repair factors. It does not assume their diagonal equations separately, any regular source parametrization, or a common small-parameter ansatz. All factors are actual words, not just positive matrices.

If every genuine bigon added in P and R has pair loss at most eta, (3) instead gives

    e <= D_n exp(2lambda_n delta) delta sqrt(eta).        (17)

Thus a fixed nonordinary K cannot be repaired by arbitrarily pair-fine added words under a fixed finite reserve H. More precisely, any such repair has

    eta >= [e/(D_n exp(2lambda_n H)H)]^2

when H>0. When H=0, (13) forces e=0. This is a two-sided repair application of the accepted weak-factor theorem, not a claim that infinitesimal source controls span the full endpoint group.

## 6. Exact implications and limits for a projective lift

If K already matches ordinary passage through n-1 but has a nonzero full cap-n defect, (16) is a necessary reserve cost for repairing that SAME fixed physical K by actual outer factors. The accepted cap-five family has a nonzero sixth-diagonal defect at each sufficiently small fixed positive scale, so its cap-six full defect e is positive. It cannot be repaired by arbitrarily small-hazard added source factors while keeping that seed unchanged.

This does NOT prohibit retuning the seed as the cap changes. The accepted weak-source families can make finite-cap defects tend to zero. Constants D_n and exp(2lambda_n H) also depend strongly on n. Therefore (15) alone gives no divergence of the exact thresholds tau_n and no obstruction uniform over all m-dependent actual rivals. It does not replace the stronger source-membership gap in the prescribed-conjugator construction.

The exact single-layer law (11) applies only after full lower equality AND the new diagonal equation have been met by each block. The accepted cap-five seed's negative sixth diagonal means it is not yet an admissible n=6 block for that law. Cancelling just its selected topology coordinates, or assigning a compensating scalar independently of its physical source bank, does not cross this gate.
