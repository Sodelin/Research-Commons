# CWU connection: albumin–gold affinity and measurement-model dependence

## Executive summary

This study offers a concrete distinction between measuring an assay response and inferring a binding mechanism. A useful mathematical audit could determine which conclusions survive changing the admissible response model. It would not replace new structural or conformational measurements.

## Proposed mathematical interface

Specify θ=(surface preparation, binding parameters, conformation/response nuisance variables) and a joint forward law Φ(θ) for fluorescence plus selected independent measurements. Choose a target L, for example an affinity ordering under a fixed physical definition, or a declared mechanistic class. Different operational definitions of affinity cannot be silently treated as estimates of one invariant quantity.

**Decisive proposed proposition:** determine whether L is constant on the fibres of Φ for an explicitly calibrated model class; where it is not, produce an admissible indistinguishable pair and assess one independent assay that separates it. A positive G6 certification theorem further needs effective forward-image closure enclosures and a nonzero robustness margin. Neither proof is supplied here. G7's exact affine finite-census contract needs extension before selecting noisy nonlinear assays.

Missing: binding stoichiometry and heterogeneity assumptions, response-model alternatives, instrumental noise and calibration, admissible protein states, parameter bounds and data sufficient to validate those choices. Larger fitting runs cannot cure exact observational equivalence; better observables may. An observed method discrepancy alone is not a proof of mathematical nonidentifiability or experimental error.

## Gap classification and benefit

**Experimental:** conformational characterization and independent measurement validation. **Mathematical:** whether the declared target is identifiable under a validated observation model. **Computation:** inference/simulation after that model is fixed; no quantified capacity shortfall established. Benefit to biological inference is making target definitions, nuisance variables and assay dependence explicit.
