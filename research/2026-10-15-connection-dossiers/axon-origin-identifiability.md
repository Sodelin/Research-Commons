# CWU connection: testing the proposed evolutionary origin of axons

## Executive summary

The authors explicitly ask for gene phylogenetics combined with gene perturbation to test an axon-origin hypothesis. Our plausible contribution is to determine which competing evolutionary explanations those observations can actually distinguish. The required evolutionary observation model is not yet supplied.

## Primary source and author-stated need

K. E. Miller, F. Oprea, S. Alam, A. Grodsky and E. M. Craig, **Did axons evolve by activating cytokinesis during interphase? A hypothesis on the origin of neurons**, *Molecular Biology of the Cell* 36(9), ar110 (2025). Online 30 July; issue 1 September. DOI [10.1091/mbc.E24-12-0585](https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.E24-12-0585); [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12415613/). Version: published hypothesis/review; full text retrieved, targeted discussion inspected.

The abstract and concluding discussion propose systematic phylogenetic analysis of essential genes paired with disruption of critical genes. The paper proposes cytokinesis-derived axogenesis; it does not establish that evolutionary history. This is an author-posed biological testing need, not a formal mathematical conjecture.

## Known boundary and later work

[Ferraioli et al., Science Advances 12, eaea8399 (2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12959406/), DOI 10.1126/sciadv.aea8399, published online 4 March / issue 6 March, supplies newer comparative evidence. Selected introduction, comparative discussion and Figure 5F were inspected. Sensory-system homology and convergence remain contested in that discussion. This does not certify that every version of the 2025 hypothesis is unresolved. Importantly, genomic absence, presence without relevant expression, and unreported expression are different observations.

## Candidate mathematical interface

Let Θ encode explicit histories of gene duplication/loss, co-option and cellular mechanisms; let L distinguish the scientific alternatives; let Φ record gene-family data and specified perturbation responses. G3/G4 suggest asking whether a finite panel separates a target from every admitted rival. G6 could distinguish residual model ambiguity from sampling uncertainty. G7 would require a noisy intervention model with temporal specificity and confounding controls.

**Decisive proposed proposition:** for a declared finite panel E, either prove that Φ_E(θ)=Φ_E(θ′) implies L(θ)=L(θ′) over the complete admitted source class, or construct two admitted differently labelled histories with the same observation law. A statistical extension requires quantitative separation, not just exact injectivity. Neither statement has been proved here.

Missing premises: orthology/paralogy, dependence between genes, duplication/loss model, annotation error, ancestral loss versus present absence, perturbation-to-history semantics and a tractable complete rival class. A species branching order alone does not identify cellular homology. No biological source-count theorem applies merely because the data are phylogenetic.

## Gap classification and benefit

The authors' gap is primarily **experimental/evolutionary validation**. Our proposed gap is **mathematical identifiability after model admission**. More sequence data or computation need not distinguish observationally equivalent histories; conversely, a formal distinction does not establish historical truth. No novel theorem is claimed. Benefit back to biology: a concrete test of all-rival separation and observation-model admission, directly relevant to the still-open G3/G4 obligations.

## Mathematical contract and verification boundary

The proposed connection is our operationalization, not a theorem claimed by the cited authors. G6 requires a declared source class, finite target labels, effective bounds on closures of forward observation images, and robust label separation for finite honest stopping. See the [G6 proof account](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/1f2e49a9e95b79f0d20dfb0da29e59f638676d22/research/2026-10-01-g6-effective-certification/PROOF.md).
