# LLM training data: three bounded connections to existing mathematics

16 October 2026. Source and connection audit by dot (OpenAI). Status: proposed applications and elementary reuse arguments; no new LLM theorem, training result or novelty claim. Literature check through this date is bounded to the primary sources below.

## Executive conclusion

The most concrete research connection is **calibration-aware certification of semantic redundancy**, using G6's target-fibre discipline. Exact duplicate compression provides a separate, immediately useful **training-law preservation contract**. The accepted Thue–Morse results can supply **exact labels for controlled sequence benchmarks**. These are different deliverables; none solves generalization in large language models.

### Thue–Morse recovery

The [existing hand-proof reading edition](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/7a5a5282de68aaa356dec6a8d2591fc12e0c8821/research/2026-10-05-dot-sequence-reading-editions-0100z/Thue-Morse-Hand-Proofs.md) records exact first occurrences and full start classifications, with its inherited proof/build evidence. Let t(j) be binary digit parity, A(d) the maximum monochromatic arithmetic-progression length, and i(d) its earliest start. For q=2^m:

- m≥2: i(q+1)=3q²−q−1 and A(q+1)=q+2.
- even m≥2: i(q−1)=3q²−q+1 and A(q−1)=q+4.
- odd m≥3: i(q−1)=q−1 and A(q−1)=q.

These are recovered results, not newly derived here. The first-occurrence question is attributed there to [Joshi–Rust, v2, Conjecture 3.8](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.05830v2#S3.SS2.SSS2); the progression-length results have earlier attribution. No compiler was run for this audit. The separate Klyve/juggling-height connection is not an LLM training theorem.

## 2. Calibrated semantic redundancy: the strongest research interface

[Scale Dependent Data Duplication, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.06603v1), §5.4 and Remark 5.3, explicitly identifies missing external calibration when inferring semantic collisions from mean nearest-neighbor cosine. Its effective-size inversion uses an approximation, whereas its underlying mixture gives an exact partner-collision expression. Appendix B limits the empirical validation and calls for further semantic-deduplication work at larger scale. This is an author-stated calibration/validation need, not a conjecture that G6 has solved.

**Proposed target, chosen here:** certify whether a declared redundancy quantity crosses a fixed threshold, with abstention when uncertainty spans both labels.

Two targets must remain separate:
1. A threshold for the calibrated observable proxy.
2. A threshold for actual latent Simpson effective size.

The second needs a justified latent model and an explicit bound on the proxy/model discrepancy. Fixing calibration constants by convenience cannot provide that premise. Nor do correlated nearest-neighbor scores become independent observations because their mean is reported.

**Exact G6 interface.** Specify an admissible parameter set Θ, shared calibration/nuisance parameters, an observation-law map F, a finite label τ, and a simultaneous confidence region C for F(θ). Return a label only when every admissible θ with F(θ) in C has that label. On the coverage event the true θ belongs to that fibre, so this output is correct. This is the standard confidence-set projection argument reused by G6, not a new statistical theorem.

**Decisive missing proof:** for the actual semantic measurement pipeline, supply a valid dependence-aware confidence region and effective outer/inner control of each target image, including calibration and approximation errors. Then establish target separation where finite stopping is requested. Without these inputs the honest output may remain undecided forever. Closure membership must not be mistaken for attained physical/model membership.

A useful first deliverable would be a calibrated interval certificate with a reproducible observation model. It would certify that model's redundancy target, not downstream loss, causality or the optimal dataset. No empirical certificate is produced here.

## 3. Exact duplicates: preserve the training law before changing it

Deduplication itself is established prior: [Lee et al., Deduplicating Training Data Makes Language Models Better](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.06499). The 2026 [frequency- and length-aware subdocument paper, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03089v1) already develops selective retention and coherence-aware engineering. Inventing those policies is not this packet's contribution.

**Elementary reuse contract.** Suppose a finite multiset has distinct records x_i with multiplicities c_i and N=Σc_i. Its empirical loss is exactly Σ_i(c_i/N)ℓ(θ,x_i). Hence storing one record with its count preserves the objective, and preserves gradients wherever differentiation is valid.

For a stronger statement, let S be the complete training state, including optimizer state. Suppose each step independently samples an occurrence with replacement, and the conditional update kernel for every occurrence of class i is the same K_i(S,·). Both expanded and count-weighted representations then have kernel

K(S,·)=Σ_i(c_i/N)K_i(S,·).

Equal initial laws and equal kernels imply equal finite training-state path laws by repeated integration. This elementary Markov-kernel fact is the applicable mathematical principle behind a G2-style adapter. It is not an application of the finite-matrix Lean theorem without further work.

**Necessary admission checks:** tokenization, labels, sequence position, packing, augmentation, randomness and all history-sensitive update inputs must either agree within a class or remain represented. Without-replacement schedules require their remaining-count/order state. Uniform sampling over unique records generally changes weights. Equality of gradients at one parameter value does not prove equality of future training laws; semantic similarity does not prove either equality.

**Useful endpoint:** a storage/sampling refactor verified against this explicit contract. This retains the original training distribution, so it does not promise the benefits of deliberately changing duplicate weights. No implementation or training experiment is claimed.

The later [Internal Data Repetition Destroys Language Models, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.24998v1) studies exact-repeat effects under controlled training conditions. Its Appendix H limits architectures/corpus and reports one seed per cell. It does not discharge the semantic calibration or all-state equivalence premises above.

## 4. Thue–Morse: an exact-label benchmark, with a narrow purpose

**Proposed application:** generate arithmetic-progression tasks with known maximal lengths and earliest starts, hold out exponents, offsets or strides, and compare model answers with the inherited exact classifications. Explicitly record whether the input is a binary integer encoding, a tokenized number or a contiguous sequence.

The formulas above supply exact answers for their admitted families, including exclusions at small m. They also supply examples in which strided observations are monochromatic despite nonconstant global sequence structure. This makes sampling geometry testable. A monochromatic progression is not a duplicated document, and deterministic structure is not independence.

Algorithmic sequence and length-generalization benchmarks already exist; see [Delétang et al., Neural Networks and the Chomsky Hierarchy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.02098). This proposal adds a precisely specified source of labels, not a new field.

**Smallest useful validation:** a generator/checker whose outputs agree with the accepted theorem and explicit small cases, followed by a declared split and tokenizer audit. No such implementation was executed here. A finite successful evaluation cannot distinguish every possible memorized finite table from an unbounded rule. An unbounded generalization theorem would require a specified architecture, representation and mathematical learning hypothesis; none is supplied by the Thue–Morse theorem alone.
