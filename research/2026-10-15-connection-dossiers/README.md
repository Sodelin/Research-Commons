## How to read the claims
- Author-stated open question: a named source explicitly poses an unresolved question, with a bounded later-work check. This is not proof that no solution exists anywhere.
- Experimental or measurement need: new data, calibration or physical validation are requested; that is not automatically an open mathematical conjecture.
- Proposed connection: our suggested mathematical interface, whose missing assumptions and decisive proposition are stated.
- Scoped result: a particular hand-reviewed result, retaining its exact domain and verification limits.
- Computational capacity: efficiency can be the actual unresolved question, but no dossier infers a hardware bottleneck without evidence.

The remaining work is justified individually. G3 and G4 biological masters remain open. No current physics transfer is labelled Lean-verified. The empty G1 second, G3 second and both G5 cells in the earlier matrix are not filled by assertion; no additional connection is claimed there.
