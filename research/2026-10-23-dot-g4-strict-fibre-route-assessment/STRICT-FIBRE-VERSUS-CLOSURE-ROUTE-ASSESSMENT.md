## 3. The finite COMMON certificate separates the limit

The legal crossed-cherry COMMON event chi with two copies of the pendant taxon and one outside taxon has probability p_T > 0 and satisfies

    C_chi(T^c) = exp(-c/2) p_T != p_T = C_chi(T).

Hence C(T^c) != C(T). Since C_T is a determining COMMON certificate,

    y_c := C_{C_T}(T^c) != y_T := C_{C_T}(T).

This follows by the contrapositive of the accepted certificate property. The crossed-cherry event need not itself have been selected in C_T.

For an arbitrary finite legal I panel J, set x_T = I_J(T) and define the actual joint finite-response image

    S_J = { (I_J(N), C_{C_T}(N)) :
            N is an admitted finite strict original source }.

The SAME N and SAME tuple produce both coordinates. The inherited sequence yields

    (I_J(N_r), C_{C_T}(N_r)) -> (x_T,y_c).

Consequently, for EVERY finite J,

    (x_T,y_c) belongs to closure(S_J),
    y_c != y_T.                                             (1)

The topology here is ordinary Euclidean topology on the finite response vector. There is no interchange of a limit with a hidden query.

## 4. Exactly which positive step fails

A tempting positive argument would replace the unknown-size actual response image by a closed observational relaxation F_J containing S_J, then try to prove

    F_J intersect ({x_T} x R^{|C_T|}) = {(x_T,y_T)}.          (2)

Equation (1) makes (2) impossible for EVERY such closed F_J. This includes any closed semialgebraic outer approximation or algebraic zero set that contains the entire actual S_J. For instance, any family of polynomial equations valid on all S_J also vanishes at (x_T,y_c), by continuity.

This is the exact limit of the corollary. It DOES NOT rule out all semialgebraic elimination, all polynomial guards, or all exact algebraic proofs:

- Strict semialgebraic predicates can exclude nonattained boundary points.
- One can first impose I_J=x_T on the actual strict source set, and only then analyze its image or closure.
- A source-level guard may retain finite graph structure, strict parameters, and induction information not present in a closed finite observational relaxation.

The invalid interchange is

    closure(S_J) intersect {x=x_T}
       versus
    closure(S_J intersect {x=x_T}).

The fair-cloud sequence has x_r -> x_T; it is not known to have x_r=x_T for a general finite J. Thus it supports membership in the first set only. It does not force membership in the second set.

The required exact bridge remains the strictly weaker assertion

    S_J intersect ({x_T} x R^{|C_T|}) = {(x_T,y_T)}.          (3)

Equation (1) neither proves nor disproves (3).

## 5. What is, and is not, attained

The construction does not establish an exact admitted preimage of (x_T,y_c) for an arbitrary finite J. It also does not prove such a preimage impossible. If one could produce such a preimage for every finite J at this SAME target, it would give the exact fixed-target negative alternative.

At the level of the entire infinite response, the mixed profile (I(T),C(T^c)) has no admitted finite source preimage: entire I equality would force C(T), contrary to C(T^c)!=C(T). This full-law exclusion must not be confused with exclusion at a finite panel.

A positive proof must establish the exact strict-source fibre condition (3), or another equivalent finite bridge, without relaxing to the larger closed fibre in (2). No unknown-size bound, common positive parameter margin, rival budget, finite atomic mixing law, or observation of hidden forests has been added.
