# A queried-support receiver for one successful trace step

The new generic row component has three actual hosted selected-type/axiom
checks with zero errors and zero warnings. It supplies the trace-local
alternative identified in the selected-key word use-site review. The native
provider can retain its bounded Result/Unknown interface: its actual successful
queries still need certificates; no row is invented on a refusal.

For a finite entering list, `fibre_bind` sums all entering-weight times
conditional-fibre products. `queried_bind_matrix` requires the conditional
identity ONLY for members of that entering list, and for EVERY output key.
`compressed_step` combines it with a prefix invariant and exact complete-fibre
compression. The resulting coefficient is the ordinary finite matrix update.
Duplicates sum and omitted destinations have zero coefficients. This holds
for arbitrary semiring coefficients and finite key carriers; the literal
state carrier need not be enumerated or bijective with the selected views.

The premises retain actual work. Exact native decoding must supply the key
and polynomial coefficients; each successful queried table must prove all
destination fibres; native accumulation must prove compression equality.
The component does not prove a Rust BTreeMap operation or turn a result
dictionary into such a certificate. A whole finite trace additionally needs
its actual initialization and per-iteration instances. This is state-fibre
aggregation, with no deduplication of different target-labelled sources.
