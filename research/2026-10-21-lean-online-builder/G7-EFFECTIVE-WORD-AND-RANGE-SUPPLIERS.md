contains the six graph/registry arrays; sourceOfValid constructs N from its

snapshot on that fixed N and sample. Graph admission alone does not establish
the forest/ancestor/population invariant of a runtime snapshot.

genuine state Code directly into SourceFiniteProjection.SelectedIndex. The latter
