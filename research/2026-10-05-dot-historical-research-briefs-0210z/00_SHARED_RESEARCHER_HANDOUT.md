## The mathematical question

Several hidden ancestry networks can be compatible with the same observations. This programme asks which features can nevertheless be identified, what can safely be simplified, and when a finite observation can justify a reliable conclusion.

The current models are restricted branching-and-merging networks with positive coalescence rates and explicit inheritance rules. Important results use exact probability laws of gene genealogies, including merger times in shared calendar units. Real DNA data do not supply those laws directly. A scientific application needs an additional observation and calibration model.
