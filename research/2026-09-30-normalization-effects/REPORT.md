## 1. Abstract

We separate topology normalization, stochastic normalization and experiment optimization. A switching-wise quotient erases controls in blobs with at most three ports, while an exact coordinate-dependence test can erase further neutral controls. A facial interaction graph is outerplanar; its three-coloring yields three partial-control environments covering every two-choice split witness. Four binary color rows give certain occurrence, with matching admitted lower bounds. Effective edge responses characterize control-aware two-port CF reduction under common inheritance. Passive equivalence does not preserve all interventions; a three-port common-inheritance example even prevents general passive reduction to a fixed tree star.

## 4. Findings and proofs

### 4.1 Switching-wise support normalization and exact coordinate erasure

Take an actual admitted source and its tree of blobs. Each cut has a nonempty taxon set on both sides. In any global switching, the pruned local tree joining two ports is a path; joining three ports gives the unique unrooted tripod. Replacing <=3-port blobs by those pieces preserves EACH switching's unrooted tree topology, hence all quartet and split outputs. The original outside incidences and embedding persist. A nonroot tripod is oriented from its unique entry; a root in a replaced piece can be put on a replacement edge with two taxon-bearing sides. No new cycles are introduced. This inherits the same path, capping and rooting premises audited in the existing actual-source normalization.

Retain the original IDs in blobs with >=4 ports, numbering them s. All erased switches may remain naturally random. If a sufficient old certificate contains erased literals, delete them: complete them to the old values, apply sufficiency, then use switching-wise independence from erased coordinates. Thus a menu on retained coordinates lifts by forcing those SAME original IDs; no passive hidden-network selector is used.

Further exact erasure is available for the declared coordinate-projection task. A hybrid is necessary iff some two global switchings differing only at that hybrid have different unrooted tree outputs. To test this without enumerating 2^r global switches, inspect each four-port set in its capped blob. Only the sampled ports' own hybrid choices matter after tree restriction, so at most four bits need enumeration. For every hybrid h, enumerate the other three ports and their at most eight choices; compare the two h choices. A changed local quartet lifts by choosing one taxon in each nonempty port component. Conversely every changed global quartet either is resolved by a fixed cut or occupies four ports of one central blob. Therefore this test detects exactly every coordinate dependency.

Iteratively flipping individually irrelevant bits proves factorization through the relevant coordinate set. If a removed bit has a dependency witness, any coordinate projection deleting it identifies two states with different required outputs. Hence this is a necessary-and-sufficient, computable minimum subset for COORDINATE ERASURE. It is not the minimum network realization or every possible nonlinear encoding. Tree-quartet preprocessing makes the number of local tests O(sum_B h_B p_B^3), with a fixed factor at most eight; graph reading and embedding/ID acquisition still have their own cost.

### 4.2 Sharp counts and a stronger finite enumeration cap

Each hybrid in a capped blob has a distinct outgoing child port. A nonroot p-port blob has an ordinary rootward entry, so h<=p-1; at most one root-containing blob can have h=p. If t counts ordinary trivalent attachment vertices, the blob tree identity is sum_B(p_B-2)+t=n-2.

After two-port deletion all p>=3, giving r<=2n-3. After <=3-port deletion all retained p>=4, giving s<=n-1+floor((n-2)/2)=floor(3n/2)-2. If no retained blob contains the root, each bound improves by one. The independent count argument and exterior-cycle attaining constructions are in normalization_count_argument.md. Thirty-four finite extremizers, n=4,...,20, passed degree, DAG, root-LSA dominator, bridge, port and count checks. Sharpness is for the specified port compression, not minimum equivalent stochastic realization.

For a rooted binary network, V=2n+2s-1 and E=2n+3s-2. Thus this topology quotient supplies an actual source representative with at most 5n-5 rooted vertices and 5n-6 after root suppression (one smaller for odd n). This strengthens the existing 8n-14 unrooted bound for discrete profile enumeration. It changes neither the profile set nor a certified Q*(n); it only narrows a safe actual-graph census cap. The faster root-aware paired-tip census may already avoid that naive graph search, so a wall-clock improvement is not presumed.

### 4.3 Three environments are sufficient

For every retained capped blob let D be its simple weak dual: vertices are bounded faces/hybrids, and adjacent faces give an edge. A duplicated hybrid pair straddles a skeleton edge exactly when its bounded-face tree path contains that edge. Two straddlers are therefore the two incident bounded faces and form an edge of D. A two-choice certificate can consequently be chosen on an edge of D. Singleton and zero-choice certificates need no edge.

Each bounded face has its hybrid boundary edge incident to the exterior face. Deleting the exterior vertex in the plane dual puts every remaining vertex on one face. Simplifying loops/parallel edges gives an outerplanar D, which is three-colorable. Reuse colors between blobs. Permute each blob's colors so its largest class C3 is passive.

| Environment | C1 | C2 | C3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | force 0 | force 0 | natural |
| 2 | force 1 | natural | natural |
| 3 | natural | force 1 | natural |

Every two distinct colors cover all four signed assignments with no conflict and at least one correct force. A certificate edge has distinct colors, so its occurrence is >=g in one row. A singleton in the passive class still occurs naturally with probability >=g; the other singleton colors have compatible rows. A zero-choice target is certain. Global splits localize in one blob; a displayed quartet can inherit a witness from a full split realizing it. This proves the whole-class upper bound.

The maximum controls in a row are <=floor(2s/3)<=n-2. Total forced assignments across the rows are <=2floor(2s/3)<=2n-4. These are upper bounds, not matching actuator-cost optima. An edgeless D allows a passive baseline; a bipartite D allows two rows, forcing both values on one color class.

### 4.4 An admitted graph proves three necessary

The lower fixture is a plane binary copy tree

    (((11,0),(1,6)),(((7,2),(3,8)),((9,4),(5,10)))).

Its contour is 11,0,1,6,7,2,3,8,9,4,5,10. Taxa 0,...,5 are ordinary. Pair copies (6,7), (8,9), (10,11) become hybrids H0,H1,H2 and taxa 6,7,8. Join each adjacent pair in its disjoint exterior wedge; all taxa remain exterior and every hybrid has a pendant cut child edge. Orient the remaining tree from a binary root inserted at taxon0's edge. That direct taxon branch makes the root the LSA; the graph is binary, acyclic and galled. Positive coalescent lengths and interior parent probabilities can be assigned freely.

There are three branch stems, each containing a different anchored ordinary pair {0,1}, {2,3}, {4,5}. Each has exactly two boundary hybrids. Its four distinct target splits correspond EXACTLY to the four assignments of those two choices. No other edge can realize that same bipartition: an edge within a branch separates its anchored ordinary pair, while an edge in another branch includes/excludes another ordinary pair. The three stem pairs are the three edges of a hybrid triangle. Thus twelve actual target events are the twelve signed pair cylinders, not artificial coverage demands.

For any missing signed-pair coverage, choose both required parent probabilities equal to g. Every conflicting row gives zero, and any nonconflicting row fixing neither gives g^2<g. Hence a fixed parameter-oblivious menu must cover all twelve assignments by at least one correct force. A row can cover at most two assignments of a given pair, and does so only when exactly one pair coordinate is fixed. Two rows covering all four patterns would each have to fix exactly one coordinate of EVERY triangle edge. This is impossible: for three fixation indicators x_i in {0,1}, the three equations x_i+x_j=1 sum to 2 sum_i x_i=3. Therefore at least three rows are necessary for any 0<g<=1/2 under this contract.

three_environment_lower.py independently constructs the actual graph, checks degree/DAG/LSA, recomputes eight switched split sets, verifies all twelve exact cylinder events, and examines ALL 378 two-row menus from the 27 possible partial rows at the fair state g=1/2. None succeeds. The three-row construction passes 432 actual probability comparisons at declared corner parameters. Thus the worst-case graph-informed partial-control minimum is exactly THREE. This is not a claim every individual network needs three, nor a parameter-aware minimum for every fixed small g.

### 4.5 Four environments give certain occurrence and are optimal in the worst case

On the three colors use rows 000, 011, 101, 110. Every color pair realizes all four assignments with both coordinates fixed. Force only retained original IDs; leave erased IDs natural. Every full split certificate then holds surely in one row, and topology-neutral randomness does not change it. A singleton also sees both choices. Thus four environments suffice for occurrence one, with <=s simultaneous controls each.

The same lower fixture contains four distinct exact signed-pair targets on one actual pair. Under positive natural probabilities, a target has probability one in a row only when BOTH pair coordinates are fixed correctly. One row certifies at most one of its four assignments, so at least four rows are necessary. The exact script also checks every three-row partial menu. Independently, the Halin receipt contains an eight-taxon graph with sixteen supported nontrivial splits. Each selected tree has n-3=5, so three arbitrary environmental distributions can have at most fifteen distinct probability-one split targets; this rules out three even beyond product partial rows.

## 7. Reconstructive analysis: common-inheritance response edges

Consider a genuine two-port blob with all sampled attachments outside it. For local switching s, let L(s)>=0 be its pruned unrooted species-tree path length between ports, including exactly the chosen component region. For partial local row c under NMSCcom define

    phi(c)=E_c[exp(-L(S))],    t(c)=-log(phi(c)).

A quartet exposing the cut as 2|2 has internal length a_q(outside)+L(S). Its tree-MSC CF is linear in exp(-internal length); independence of local and outside switches therefore permits replacing the blob by an edge t(c). Other allocations place the local path on a pendant quartet part, so it has no CF contribution. Unroot tree species switchings first to include a root-containing two-port blob; tree quartet CF root invariance gives the same proof. This argument is common inheritance only.

If erased IDs remain natural in every used row, phi is constant, and one fixed edge preserves all quartet CFs under arbitrary controls on the retained IDs. If original controls inside the deleted blob are allowed, an exposed cut admits one fixed edge for all those controls iff phi(c) is constant. With every local switching fully forceable, this is iff every L(s) agrees. A terminal one-taxon side does not expose a 2|2 quartet and is a separate unobservable case.

The executed serial-bigon fixture uses route lengths log2 and log4. Passive contraction and all nine retained rows preserve all five quartet CFs. Forcing the removed bit changes the required effective survival from 3/32 to 1/8 or 1/16, disproving one fixed all-control normal form. Read normalization-effects-checks.json for exact vectors. This is not a substitute for the biological owner's independent-lineage root/interface theorem.

## 8. Middle-out synthesis: the boundary at three ports

Topology-only three-port erasure is safe; a fixed stochastic tree-star replacement is not generally safe even under common inheritance. An explicit triangle has U->V, U->H, V->H; V leads to a two-taxon clade A, H to a single taxon B, and the rootward side to a two-taxon clade C. All switches display the same unrooted five-taxon topology. With natural inheritance 1/2 and every original edge length log2, put a=x=log2 and c=2log2 (the two root-side edges). Its two local switched arm pairs are (L_A,L_C)=(a+x,c) or (a,c+x).

Then E exp(-L_A)=3/8 and E exp(-L_C)=3/16, but E exp(-(L_A+L_C))=1/16, rather than their product 9/128. Quartets 2A|B|C and 2C|A|B pin those two effective arm survivals. Quartet 2A|2C requires their product to be 1/16, an impossibility for a fixed metric star. All original lengths are positive, and rootability/galled/exterior admission hold. three_port_cf_check.py evaluates both actual switched trees with exact Fraction arithmetic and verifies this discrepancy. This is a hand-derived and exact-tested counterexample, not a stochastic simulation. It shows why the universal stochastic operation cannot be extended from two to three ports by topology reasoning alone.

This does not prove the globally minimum CF-equivalent reticulation count or rule out other retained-network models. The full passive CF-fiber classification, NMSCind simultaneous normalization, unknown-control-map recovery and practical intervention remain open.

## 12. Robustness and counterfactuals

Verdict: exact within the fixed parameter-oblivious, ideal graph-informed contract. Three and four have matching actual-source lower bounds; confidence bounds remain conditional.
