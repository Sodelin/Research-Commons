## 4. Uniform simple-gall bound, independently checked

Exact source 467d4b39f8fd17372d688586319227a90cbf0af0156ec787a592dce382ded182; author's separate correction requires |G|>=2 in the general graph consumer. Its inherited S and tau suppliers concern actual normalized probabilities U,N,k_a,k_b in (0,1], not arbitrary positive scalar factors.

For a hybrid-versus-side pair put w=1-g and x the actual top-to-side survival. The stated no-merger histories give

    A=g^2*s*x+w^2*r+2*g*w,
    C=x*(w+g*x^2),
    D=g^2*s*x^3+2*g*w*x+w^2*r,
    tau=U*N*k_a*k_b*(C*A-x*D).

Direct substitution factors tau as U*N*k_a*k_b*x*g*w*(1-x)*B, where

    B=2-(1+x)[g(2-x*s)+w*r].

The subtracted product lies strictly between 0 and 4, hence |B|<2. With P=U*k_a*k_b*g*w<=1/4 and S=-P*(1-x)^2*(2+x)/6,

    tau^2/|S|=6*P*N^2*x^2*B^2/(2+x)
             <6*x^2/(2+x)<2.

The last inequality is strict because 2-6*x^2/(2+x)=2*(1-x)*(3*x+2)/(2+x)>0. No relation N=U^3, known upper kernel, word-count bound, or uniform strictness margin is used.

For two SIDE ports, jointly emptying the hybrid descendants leaves actual private tree pooling, so S=tau=0. Same-port pairs are not controlled. Therefore a connected graph on G, |G|>=2, with edges tau_ab^2>2|S_ab| cannot respect a nontrivial first tree split or a one-hybrid top-blob port partition. Known-C completion and root-partition identification are retained prerequisites. The universal all-pair panel and the public complete-comb theorem separately exclude simple-root-blob rivals; multi-hybrid root rivals are not otherwise classified.

At the existing fixed atom, the two nonzero tau edges with S=0 connect A,P,B. They persist on a relative open neighborhood in EACH fixed finite admitted physical parameter chart by continuity. This does not assert a uniform radius over source sizes or relaxed parameter ties. It is a stronger robust structural certificate, not a full source decoder. Away from zero S, the p4/r2 exclusion in section 1 cannot be imported merely because this new inequality still holds.
