# Source-admitted automatic adaptive policy assembly

Attributed implementation and checks by dot (OpenAI), 4 October 2026.

This connected workflow accepts a supported source provider, a fixed mechanism
and a PATH budget. The caller supplies no action word or leaf decoder. It
selects an affordable first original forcing, chooses and certifies one
observation-only section of the inherited full-support winning region, generates the
continuation, derives every source-realizable leaf region, and rechecks the
entire policy. `python3 run_workflow.py` runs synthesis, actual-source execution
and negative coverage/admission controls after the pinned dependencies are
installed. Independent review remains a separate gate.

The supported provider is the already accepted complete four-taxon/one-original-
hybrid two-forcing image: nine joint images covering all 546 original sources
under its documented admission/readout contract. Its topology observations are
full unrooted, original-tip-labelled quartet laws. The same graph, edges and
natural gamma are shared throughout a history. Forcing the two original parent
bits hides gamma without resampling/refitting it. COMMON and INDEPENDENT are
specified as separate fixed mechanisms; no mode is refit after a response.

No new pair-separation theorem is asserted. The inherited 33-pair positive
polynomial certificates already exclude equal next responses for different
targets at every interior two-row mixture. Their exact Fraction checker is
reused, with its source-image coverage supplied separately by the pinned
provider. Adding source-consistent history cannot create a collision excluded
without it. Thus every legal action in the CHOSEN full support[0,1] wins:
0<w<1. This is not the entire history-conditioned winning-action fibre of the
whole menu. After first forcing0, the endpoint w=0 (pure forcing1) also wins by
the inherited two-forcing decoder; its support is[1]. The constructor here
chooses the full-support branch and does not exclude that endpoint from the
mathematical menu.

Quantifier order is preserved: choose a legal first action, then handle EVERY
source-generated first response; choose an observation-only action section at
that history, then handle EVERY exact next response. Each candidate retains
ONE original parameter assignment across ALL observations. Two rival
candidates may have different assignments. The section must be one global
function of observable history, not a function secretly chosen per source.

The assembler uses a finite two-call constructor and a finite section candidate
library: first-response coordinates, then an interior constant. It certifies
each candidate against every actual source-image model. It selects h0_0 in the
tested instance, producing first forcing0 followed by the genuinely response-
dependent mixture [h0_0,1-h0_0]. The accepted strict sign-cell affine projector
derives all six target-region families from the complete accumulated history,
including the hidden nuisance gamma. The whole-policy verifier checks legal
actions, target correctness and coverage over every actual source point with
the cumulative call/configuration/original-site costs. Generic proof trust
remains SAME_BACKEND; the reused positive identities retain their independent
arithmetic scope.

The actual-source replay selects each graph/positive edge/gamma assignment
BEFORE its two calls. It interprets the GENERATED policy and recompiles both
rows with the same assignment. Different first responses choose different
second actions. It checks both fixed mechanisms separately and the actual
displayed-Q target at PATH[2,2,1]. Those source points are controls for the
symbolic whole-policy coverage proof, not an expanded source census or
empirical fit.

This is automatic assembly of an existing admissible strategy, not a new
optimum or mathematical G7 closure. The earlier one-call interior-mixture
optimum for this fixture remains preserved. The construction deliberately
provides an adaptive witness; it does not compute the full Pareto frontier.
Unsupported providers, requests, resource failures, failed section extraction
or failed whole-policy checks return UNKNOWN, never a losing-budget theorem.
In particular a budget too small for this chosen adaptive witness is not
declared impossible.

The complete finite-registry G7 hand characterization is prior accepted
mathematics, including source-pair terminal reduction, exists-action/forall-
response recursion and constructive CAD selection. Its exact primary review
is delivered under inherited-contract. General executable CAD history
partitioning, arbitrary section forms, nonlinear source projection and full
recursive policy synthesis are still open. A finite candidate library is not
their completeness theorem. Unknown-size G3/G4 recognition, source-word
realization bounds, Lean certification and empirical DNA admission remain
distinct; they are not silently required to validate this fixed-registry
instance, and they are not solved by it.
