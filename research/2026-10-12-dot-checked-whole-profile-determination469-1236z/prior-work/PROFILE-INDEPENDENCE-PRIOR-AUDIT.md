## Applicable classical method

A linear transform, a compatible total ordering, and vanishing ratios of later transforms relative to an earlier one imply identifiability of all finite mixing distributions. This primary paper's applications concern named distribution families, not MSC trees. The original 1963/1977 full texts were not directly obtained in this audit; do not describe this as their complete original-paper replay.

No single v or global ordering is required to separate the entire continuum of trees at once. The source-specific bounds, positive denominator and legal event remain required; citing a general mixture theorem cannot supply them.
