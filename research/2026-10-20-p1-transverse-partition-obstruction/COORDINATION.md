# P1: narrow consequence for the ongoing task

The greedy partition that covers a finite prior by forward-relative-entropy balls D(rho_i||rho_center)<=M/n cannot uniformly supply H(G|C_n)=O_(beta,k)(M). The stronger proposed actual-group root-fidelity overlap bound exp(O_(beta,k)(M)) also fails for its cells. Retire these two universal assertions for this construction.

Do not retire the original physical-state problem, arbitrary state-sensitive groupings, soft or quantum kernels, or direct testing. No replacement full-class supplier is proved here. The admitted transverse family itself has chi_(2n)=O(M) and a fixed-reference O(M/n) Bures bound, so it is not a lower-bound obstruction to the target.

Retain fixed known beta, coefficientwise bounded k-local Hamiltonians, unrestricted degree, full trace-norm separation, collective/adaptive measurements, and all physical copies from both unknown sources. Uniform physical estimation/increment supply and the prior-independent testing transfer remain open. Accepted upper13 and lower32 are unchanged. This is a tactical correction, not a scope rewrite.
