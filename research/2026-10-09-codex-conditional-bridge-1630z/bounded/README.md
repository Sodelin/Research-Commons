to create a second solver or substitute the Rust diagnostic for it.

The reviewed mathematical source is the frozen zero-score criterion and
INDEPENDENT-survival addendum at `cf6c1b32`. The formal lane uses the same exact
rational b, epsilon, sigma and M interface. Python computes
`delta=min(epsilon,sigma/(2*(M+1)))` and
`ceil(2*(1+delta)/(b*delta))`, with b/epsilon/sigma positive and M nonnegative.
The exact test `b=3/4,epsilon=1/3,sigma=1/4,M=2` gives delta=1/24,
rational bound 200/3 and ceiling 67. The example gives delta=1/2 and ceiling24.
Generic positive b arithmetic does not establish that an actual source can
have the proposed floor.

not prove analytic sign, source coverage or a survival floor. All count

with valid formatting. `UNKNOWN_CORE_CATALOGUE_COMPILER_UNAVAILABLE` remains
explicit. BOTH additionally requires the real same-physical-source paired

not a BOTH experiment. No conditional slot bound becomes an extra-hybrid
