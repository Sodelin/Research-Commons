Projection supplies a base-state certificate. A terminating rational/RCF search selects its constants. No such search, numerical cutoff, quantifier elimination or executable certificate was run. This proves certificate existence for an old NO family, not a new general NO classification.

Three structural consequences constrain how that certificate route might extend:

- The old forced-boundary obstruction survives any positive closure prefix, including unknown retained factors, and a source-faithful joint section. A suitable transcendental-residue contact cannot be excluded by a semialgebraic invariant. No negative algebraic joint fibre meeting that premise is exhibited.
- For independently updated word slots, the pointwise intersection of all semialgebraic inductive invariants equals the product of the one-slot intersections, with static parameters retained. This intersection is set-theoretic; pointwise separation of every target point does not automatically supply one finite separator for the whole fibre.
- That one-slot invariant intersection is not definable, even with real parameters, in the real exponential field or any o-minimal expansion containing exp. Rational residues have some points excluded by the new certificates, while all transcendental-residue positive Poisson points remain in the intersection. A hypothetical definition would project to an infinite countable definable residue set. This is a representation obstruction for the certificate hull, not for the actual source image or arbitrary algorithms on algebraic inputs.
