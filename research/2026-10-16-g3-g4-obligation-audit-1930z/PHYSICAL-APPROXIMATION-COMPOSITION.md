## Plain-language answer

Yes: we already proved genuine approximation by actual positive biological sources, uniformly over unknown original size. That is stronger than the particular fair-cell example. It is a relevant input to the correction approach. What has not been supplied is the quantitative guarantee that correcting the residual stays inside the physical parameter domain and preserves the desired distinction. The approximation theorem allows the replacement architecture to grow as accuracy increases; the correction theorem works in one fixed chart and charges for its dimension, degree and distance to forbidden parameter boundaries. Therefore the two results do not yet compose automatically.

## What the stronger approximation actually guarantees

For each finite cap M and tolerance eta, G6 replaces ANY actual serial chain by an actual positive chain with at most B_mode(M,eta) bigons and contextual full-forest TV error at most eta. All entering forests and lower copy counts are covered simultaneously. Calendar cut guards and freely variable positive rates give realization between prescribed positive endpoint ages. Joint experiment rows use one source tuple. Independent and COMMON modes are treated separately; this is not an automatic same-tuple BOTH approximation theorem or arbitrary-control preservation theorem.

For INDEPENDENT, put C=binom(M,2), W=1+ceil(log2(6C/eta)), D_M=2C max(1,3 binom(M,3)/2+27 binom(M,4)). The construction uses

    delta=min(1/2,[eta/(3D_M W)]^2),
    B_ind=ceil(W/delta).

Weak cells are replaced by pair-matched ordinary edges with error at most D_M q^(3/2) per cell; strong cells retain their original order. Thus representative size can grow approximately eta^(-2) times logarithmic factors. Retained parameters have no uniform strictness floor.

COMMON approximation instead compresses weak GENERATORS, then reconstructs them by positive Bernoulli Euler factors, with error at most r_i^2/L_i and L_i increasing with inverse tolerance. Its positive baseline repair is exact for the repaired deterministic factor, but the overall law remains approximate. This generator compression is not exact source-factor compression.

These are substantial accepted approximation capabilities. Their goal is effective law-image closure and robust finite-data certification; the proof expressly avoids deciding exact boundary attainment.

## The decisive quantitative test

Fix one actual polynomial source chart F, where F contains every required equality row for the SAME target and tuple. Let z be a physical approximate tuple, rho=dist(z,complement of the strict domain), e=||F(z)||, and let h be the polynomial later-law distinction. On a suitable fixed compact neighborhood, suppose

    dist(z,F^(-1)(0)) <= C_F e^(1/R),
    R=d(6d-3)^(N-1),

and h has Lipschitz constant L_h. A sufficient correction guard is

    C_F e^(1/R) < rho,
    L_h C_F e^(1/R) < |h(z)|.

Include any local branch or extra physical-admission margin as well. The corrected nearest zero then remains strict, satisfies ALL rows, and has nonzero h. With parameter t, e=O(t^q), rho>=c t^r and |h|>=c' t^s, the reviewed sufficient inequality is

    q > R max(r,s).

Small residual alone is insufficient. For a sequence of different charts, C_F, R, rho and the distinction margin all change. G6's eta can tend to zero while the theorem supplies no comparison with these changing quantities. The upper bound's failure to establish the guard does not prove correction impossible; it identifies an unproved composition premise.

The fair weak-cell family has a useful stronger feature: all fixed I rows approach one target at O(N^-2), while a legal COMMON coordinate remains separated. However the number of cells grows, weak-cell margins shrink and the full-chart error exponent changes. Its convergence rate is not a high-order jet in a fixed finite chart. Conversely G6's general approximation does not promise preservation of that COMMON separation when replacing an I-chain.

## What would finish this bridge

Produce actual approximants for every needed cap with verified versions of the two inequalities above and one cap-independent physical clock allowance; or construct fixed-chart jets meeting q>R max(r,s) and retain the all-cap/common-clock requirement separately. The algebraic theorem also requires algebraic fixed chart data for its algebraic-coefficient converse. Calendar endpoint preservation alone does not bound total coalescent clock, because the permitted rates vary.

No stronger inspected approximation supplier establishes these extra comparisons. This conclusion is limited to the cited recovered sources, not an exhaustive absence claim. Missing comparison is not source NO, a G4 disproof, or a reason to discard physical approximation. No computation, new physical theorem or publication was performed in this comparison.
