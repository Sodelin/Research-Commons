## A uniform local budget with cutoffs chosen first

For one profile row let m be its full copy count, I its finite refined interval occurrences, H its original hybrid registry, and B_ind its INDEPENDENT boundary occurrences. Choose a coarse bounded neighborhood of theta within the fixed chronology face, with positive rational rate lower bounds, finite rate/age upper bounds, and inheritance bounds away from zero/one. These bounds are LOCAL proof devices, not floors on the admitted class. Each finite interval has a rational upper bound A_i on its original mean a_i=G_m(r)t_i throughout this neighborhood.

Write B_i=max(1,A_i). For any b_i<=B_i and cutoff K_i, the existing residual estimate satisfies

    delta(b_i,K_i) <= 2*B_i^(K_i+1)/(K_i+1)!,

since its Taylor denominator is at least one. Choose K_i ONCE large enough that 2B_i<=K_i+2 and this rational factorial bound is below a chosen small allocation. The factorial bound tends to zero, so this terminates; it is an ordinary rational inequality search, not a zero test for an exponential. For a zero-duration interval b_i=0 its deficit and mean error are zero, including the zero-count edge case. Cutoffs are fixed before the finer local boxes are selected, so K_i cannot diverge while rate-box error is being shrunk.

Now enclose theta and theta_hat in arbitrarily narrow shared rational boxes inside that coarse neighborhood. Their duration boxes are derived from the same affine age enclosure, retaining exact equality constraints; interval lower bounds may be zero and upper/lower extrema may be conservatively enclosed. Let

    q=max_population(upper_rate/lower_rate), alpha=q^(-2),
    beta_h=min(lower_gamma_h/upper_gamma_h,
               (1-upper_gamma_h)/(1-lower_gamma_h)),
    b_i=(1+m^2)*(1+sum_population upper_rate)*upper_duration_i,
    eta_i=b_i-(1+m^2)*(1+sum_population lower_rate)*lower_duration_i.

Both banks belong to each supplied box. As those boxes narrow at theta, q->1, alpha->1, beta_h->1 and eta_i->0. Coarse bounds ensure b_i<=B_i. This limit is valid also at exact cut/node equalities because the equalities are retained, and at removed zero epochs because no duration is introduced.

Choose the boxes so narrow that the following finite rational bound is less than epsilon:

    2*sum_i(eta_i+delta(b_i,K_i))
      +sum_i K_i*(1-alpha)
      +sum_h(1-beta_h)
      +m*sum_{occurrence in B_ind}(1-beta_h(occurrence)).    (E)

There are finitely many occurrences and the K_i are fixed; the deficit allocation was chosen sufficiently small first. Apply the same construction simultaneously to all finitely many rows, using their own copy counts/cutoffs and ONE shared age/rate/inheritance bank. If there are no finite intervals or no hybrids the corresponding sums are empty; the argument does not divide by those counts.
