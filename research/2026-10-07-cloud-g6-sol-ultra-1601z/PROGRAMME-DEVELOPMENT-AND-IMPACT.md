source state, actual clock and boundary kernels, source transport and timed
readouts, then finite numerical certificates. The final inference consumers

## What changed in the goals

The programme expanded from graph structure and ideal exact observations into
source-faithful timed histories, joint observations, finite computation and
honest uncertainty. G6 gained a RAW NONPLANAR extension without silently
changing G1's original planar theorem. COMMON versus INDEPENDENT inheritance,
same-locus dependence and one shared parameter bank became explicit.

We also separated questions that should never have been merged: exact
attainment versus approximation; target recovery versus reconstruction of
every hidden detail; sufficiency versus optimality; accepted hand proof versus
complete Lean; a mathematical identification scale versus useful solver
precision. The [completion standard](../../docs/RESEARCH-COMPLETION-STANDARD.md)
keeps each original promised endpoint controlling. Finding a harder bridge
does not permit narrowing that promise without naming the changed scope.

the full JC solver. Its variable exponentials need a justified representation
before a polynomial decision theorem applies. Arbitrary matrix discrepancies
need not be legal biological observations.

The screen has also improved attribution and excluded misleading transfers:
graphon or iid-bit results do not automatically apply to finite positive
genealogical sources. That protects the real question and avoids spending
proof/compute effort on an invalid substitution. New transfers should be
measured by an actual discharged obligation, not catalogue size.

## Impact and timing

**Already useful:** preserved proofs/failed receipts, readable corpus
navigation and source-specific status reduce lost work and let another reader
resume without reconstructing chats. Completed scoped formal results can be
inspected and reused now; their relevance depends on matching assumptions.

**Next research impact:** a coherent reproducible package connecting source,
observations, identification, approximation, uncertainty and experiment cost.
Its negative results are useful too: they identify where observations cannot
justify a conclusion and where a solver must abstain. External specialist
review and accurate attribution will determine the credibility and novelty
of the combined mathematical contribution.

**Potential biological impact:** certified inference for the declared source
and data model. This depends on the genealogy/sequence observation bridge,
admitted datasets/model adequacy, numerical/sample cost and useful parameter
accuracy. Generic mathematical closure alone supplies none of those.

The [October roadmap](../../timelines/2026-10-october-research-roadmap.md) targets
31 October deliverables; its dates are planning targets, not breakthrough
guarantees, and its older status counts are superseded by current receipts.
Scoped methods/documentation can be useful before every original master
closes. There is no evidence-based date for full G3/G4 closure, independent
uptake or empirical impact. Prioritize connected proofs and usable certificates
because those determine which established mathematics can support real use.
