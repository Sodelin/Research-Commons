## Why this matters under stringent standards

declared physical source to the actual observation, numerical error and
eventual certificate. It is becoming harder for a hidden assumption or
an endpoint-only surrogate to pass as the original theorem.

Useful impact is already present in reusable proof components,
reproducible receipts and research that another contributor can inspect.
Broader impact requires the remaining full-source assembly, external
expert review and useful algorithms. Empirical scientific use additionally
requires the relevant data and model assumptions to hold. There is no
justified breakthrough date or completion percentage from module counts.
