## 2. Projector proof in the fixed S(h) frame

On the complete fresh r-root carrier let P_j^S be the spectral projector of S(h) for s_j. It is a polynomial in the actual S(h). If R deletes one original token, projectivity gives R S_r=S_(r-1) R (with the convention adjusted if kernels act on row vectors). Consequently deleting after P_r^S gives zero, since s_r is absent from the smaller carrier's spectrum. This statement is independent of row-versus-column notation: the s_r eigenspace has zero image under deletion.

For any actual F, move deletion through the right spectral projector and F using their natural projective compatibility. It then meets the left P_r^S and gives zero. Thus the spectral block P_r^S F P_(r-2)^S restricts to zero under deletion. The right projector on the smaller carrier is the compatible polynomial spectral projector: its defining polynomial has the same values on every remaining eigenvalue. The right projector makes every output root count at most r-2. Its top output row can involve exactly two merger patterns: a rooted triple or two disjoint cherries; root exchangeability makes each per-labelled pattern have a single coefficient t_r or d_r.

After deletion, inspect one specified retained cherry and singleton roots, with output root count r-2. A rooted triple extends it in exactly three ways. A disjoint new cherry can use any of the r-3 retained singleton roots. Lower-root output rows cannot contribute to this highest retained count. Thus exactly

    3 t_r+(r-3)d_r=0.

For r>=4 this leaves at most one dimension. Opaque grafting reconstructs the whole r-to-(r-2) block on arbitrary existing forests from this fresh-root top row; no independent shape freedom remains.

To connect to the chosen block-unitriangular spectral coordinates, write S=V D V^-1 and F'=V^-1 F V. Then P_r^S F P_b^S=V E_r F' E_b V^-1. At input root count r and output root count b, both exterior diagonal blocks are identities. Its top block is exactly F'_rb. All these maps preserve the actual fresh-root/graft interpretation inherited above; no different frame is used for different factors.

Therefore there is a fixed lifted matrix H_r with F'_(r,r-2)=c_r(F) H_r for every such F. The same conclusion holds for U=V^-1 QV, by differentiating the actual projective ordinary family at zero. The accepted ell(AB)=1 guarantees both U12,10 and U10,8 are nonzero. Hence their shapes span the respective one-dimensional spaces. Right-diagonal normalization and prefix conjugation are scalar on these blocks and preserve proportionality.

### Spectrum compatibility clarification

Projector polynomials need not have identical coefficients across carrier sizes: they need only agree on the actual smaller carrier spectrum. A source spectral projector defined on a larger complete leaf carrier, when restricted by opaque grafting to a forest with at most r current roots, acts on the same spectrum s_1,...,s_r. Thus grafting to larger leaf carriers does not introduce additional spectral values into this fresh-current-root reconstruction. This is a source/grafting compatibility claim, not arbitrary similarity-transform invariance.
