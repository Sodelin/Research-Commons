Checked 30 October 2026; bounded applicability review, not exhaustive novelty audit.

Its main substitution model mixes displayed trees rather than modelling incomplete lineage sorting. Section 6 points to a coalescent extension through its combinatorial Lemma 5.6 and the A-3blob proof in Allman et al. (2025).

This does not supply the present master gate: pendant 2-blobs remain outside that separation, while our hard private serial cells live precisely in such concealed structures. The stated results do not provide shared-register natural-I to COMMON forcing, finite positive witness extraction, or an unknown-word stopping bound. No transfer to the full original source class is asserted. This useful recent paper is not a replacement for the exact physical source-profile supplier.
