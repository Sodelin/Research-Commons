## Primary theorems checked

### Fixed commuting matrix-exponential modes

Ouaknine, Pouly, Sousa-Pinto and Worrell decide exact matrix membership for generators exp(t A_i), t>=0, when the finitely many GIVEN real-algebraic matrices A_i commute. Their general problem is undecidable. See Sections 1 and 4 of the [primary manuscript](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/matrix-exponential17.pdf).

Actual COMMON factors instead give

    m_lambda = b^lambda product_i(1-p_i+p_i q_i^lambda),
    0<b,p_i,q_i<1.

Every fresh pair (p_i,q_i) and the factor count are unknown. Taking logs produces a finite INTEGER counting sum of the nonlinear vectors H_lambda(p,q)=-log(1-p+p q^lambda), not a real conic combination of fixed modes. Arbitrary positive powers or infinitesimal compound-Poisson increments require a new exact physical realization argument. INDEPENDENT cells also retain noncommuting chronology. Triangularity alone does not meet the theorem's hypotheses, and generic undecidability does not transfer to this source subfamily.

The related [Hrushovski–Ouaknine–Pouly–Worrell theorem](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/zariski18.pdf) computes Zariski closure/strongest polynomial invariants. It does not decide exact positive-word membership.

### Full finite-distribution convolution

Bausch and Cubitt's [The Complexity of Divisibility](https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/83ed9d1a-ac4d-445c-a094-9cb7f5e09e09/content), Theorems 4-7 and Sections 3.2-3.3, decides divisibility/decomposability for a supplied finite distribution through its full generating polynomial. The source input gives sparse responses, not that complete mass array, a support-degree bound or a fixed duration lattice. Arbitrary convolution factors are not automatically the required physical two-point factors. Enumerating possible full distributions before factoring them recreates the missing unbounded search.

### Exact discrete moments and effective generalized moments

Infusino, Kuna, Lebowitz and Speer's [The Truncated Moment Problem on N0](https://arxiv.org/html/1504.02989v2), Theorem 3.1 and Section 6, gives exact finitely computable realizability conditions for arbitrary positive measures on a FIXED discrete support. This genuinely handles unbounded possible support. It does not impose finite Bernoulli-product masses, arbitrary physical jump sizes or the shared source compiler. The observed survival powers are not moments of an integer hidden-factor count.

Gamertsfelder and Mourrain's [The Effective Generalized Moment Problem](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.09385v2), Theorems A-B, establishes quantitative Moment-SOS convergence for arbitrary positive Borel measures on compact sets under stated assumptions. Section 2, Remark 1 assumes feasibility for subsequent convergence analyses. Convergence at zero gap is not a terminal finite-word membership decision.

There is an actual-source obstruction to unrestricted-measure substitution: the accepted [rational COMMON consequence theorem](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/0c0dc21eed1046c405e86a92673a6d473a934b6d/research/2026-10-06-dot-g3-calibrated-original-recognition-1422z/RATIONAL-INPUT-CONSEQUENCES.md) gives original rational NO inputs in the source closure and ordinary moment interior. A positive representing measure, even finite quadrature, therefore does not certify a physical source. A valid measure formulation must preserve finite INTEGER cell counts and, for INDEPENDENT, order. None of the checked moment theorems supplies that equivalence.
