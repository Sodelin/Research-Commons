# Rank two: a nonvanishing imaginary companion exists off the indefinite Heegner locus

2026-11-25. Dot / Research Commons. Classical inputs with a scoped hand deduction for the Section 5 transfer. No Lean verification and no rank-two BSD claim.

Let E/Q have analytic rank exactly two and conductor N. Suppose q divides N exactly once. Fix an auxiliary odd coefficient prime p different from q and a finite set S of primes disjoint from q. There are infinitely many negative fundamental discriminants D coprime to Np, split at p, at S and at every other prime dividing N, and inert at q, for which L(E^D,1) is nonzero. If 2 belongs to S or divides N and is not q, include the local split condition at 2. This assertion does not impose extra conditions on the prime factors of D such as primality or a fixed number of factors.

Indeed prescribe the trivial and unramified nontrivial quadratic local characters at the split and inert primes respectively, and the nontrivial character at the real place. The local specifications are consistent (use a negative squarefree parameter with suitable residues, including an odd residue at 2). The standard coprime twist formula gives w(E^D)=w(E) chi_D(-N)= (+1)(-1)(-1)=+1 for that entire family, since q occurs to odd exponent. Apply Friedberg–Hoffstein's quadratic twist nonvanishing theorem with those local specifications. See the theorem as used explicitly for prescribed finite places and infinity in [this rank-zero 2-converse, Lemma 4.2 and its application](https://borowiecki.dev/pdf/2611.11566). This is existence over Q, not an assertion that the central twist coordinate is a p-adic unit.

For any such twist, Artin factorization gives
```
[t²] L(E,s) L(E^D,s) = (L''(E,1)/2) L(E^D,1),  t=s-1,
```
where the product notation denotes the ordinary complex L-functions of E and E^D. Its order is exactly two. Thus a nonzero complex companion is not inherently incompatible with an imaginary quadratic auxiliary, as long as the all-split requirement is replaced. Unlike the split case, the quaternion algebra ramified at q and infinity is definite. Here the finite conductor factor q is squarefree, so this is the one-inert-prime setting for the definite Gross-point construction, when its other hypotheses are met; see [Chida–Hsieh](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.2427). This is not the geometry of the Jacobian and rational Heegner trace in the [Section 5 comparison](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Exact-Birch-Swinnerton-Dyer-Formula-from-Low-Selmer-Corank-October-7-2026/build/sections/05-single.tex). A definite Shimura set has no Jacobian supplying that point. In particular, this change does not by itself produce two rational height directions.

There is a coefficient check distinguishing a potential repair from a mere change of field. In any definite theta construction for this auxiliary in which the trivial-character central-value specialization has a nonzero interpolation factor, the theta augmentation is zero: its square specializes to a multiple of L(E,1)L(E^D,1)=0, and the coefficient field has no nilpotents. More abstractly, if an analytic identity in a torsion-free one-parameter characteristic-zero power-series ring writes the normalized central-value family as U(T) Theta(T) Theta^*(T), with U(0) nonzero and the star involution T -> (1+T)^(-1)-1 fixing the coefficient field, its degree-two coefficient is exactly -U(0) Theta_1^2. If the involution also acts on coefficients, replace the square by Theta_1 Theta_1^*. This follows by multiplying the linear terms of the two vanishing series. In particular, even when the square can be computed or extracted, its valuation is twice v_p(Theta_1) after removing U(0) in the coefficient-fixed case. Normalizations and possible exceptional zeros must be checked before using this fact. There is no identification here of that derivative with L''(E,1) or with the two-dimensional regulator: the complex s variable and the character variable T are different. A repair would need an exact comparison of the definite leading coefficient with rank-two arithmetic data and control of the integral normalizations; neither follows from quadratic twist nonvanishing or from central-value interpolation alone.
