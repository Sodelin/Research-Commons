# Practical genealogy solver release candidate

Authors: Codex coordinator and four direct application, contracts, biology and
validation roles, for Nolan. Date: 9 October 2026. Evidence is scoped to named
inputs and source identities. This report is a research application report,
not a claim of general G3/G4 closure or a biological accuracy certificate.

## Plain-language executive summary

The application answers a useful question: given observations that meet a
precisely specified genealogy model, which combinations of its nine parameters
remain possible after a bounded, independently checked computation? It exports
the entire retained solution cover, its uncertainty, the computation journal
and a readable explanation. A researcher can choose an included example,
run the existing numerical solver, and recheck its journal separately.

The strongest included numerical example starts with very narrow supplied
arithmetic observation bands. The inherited solver localized all nine
parameters to normalized widths below 1/20, with maximum approximately
0.00377180445. This is a useful numerical result under those input conditions.
It does not measure accuracy on real biological observations.

The archived example with 1,024 synthetic loci has a different outcome. Its
bands admit two source points separated by normalized rA=3/55, exceeding the
requested 1/20 width. The application therefore explains UNKNOWN and preserves
the wide cover. Re-running or improving inversion of those unchanged bands
cannot remove that particular ambiguity soundly. An unsupported biological
model is refused before the solver is invoked.

The Rust receiver is an optional diagnostic after checking. Its inherited
39/39 exact native/reference comparisons concern signed parameter-pair
geometry and refusal, not producer performance, observed accuracy or universal
equivalence. No producer acceleration, world-first status or whole-application
Lean proof is claimed. General G3/G4 proof work remains separate and open.

## Problem and scientific motivation

Gene trees and sequence patterns can disagree because of several mechanisms,
including incomplete lineage sorting and reticulation. Inference requires a
single coherent source and uncertainty model before such disagreement can be
turned into a history claim. Fitting separate summaries or finding a plausible
parameter point does not establish that all observations share one source.

This solver keeps one shared nine-parameter assignment across its feature
rows. It returns an outer cover rather than selecting one attractive fit.
Checking every contraction and every retained branch helps prevent a numerical
shortcut from silently discarding a compatible source. UNKNOWN and refusal
are research results: they separate insufficient information, bounded
computation and a scientifically unsupported model.

The original practical master is useful admitted inference over the complete
original domain with every normalized physical union width at most 1/20.
The released application supplies the reproducible computation and evidence
workflow; useful empirical all-nine accuracy remains an open scientific goal.

## Capabilities, assumptions and input contract

The original model uses six labelled phased copies A1,A2,B1,B2,C1,C2. Each locus
contains two sites sharing the same genealogy. The architecture fixes a
backward B-to-C pulse with its original pulse/rate ties and stationary
homogeneous clock-Jukes–Cantor observation law. The nine parameters are
h,u,v,rA,rB,rC,rAB,rR,g. Domain normalization uses the original physical
coordinate ranges; g retains its actual backward routing interpretation.

The application accepts the declared original-model arithmetic request and
its exact rational feature bands. The phased copy labels, whole-locus unit,
domain, features and target coordinates are part of admission, not labels a
user can replace casually. Mean conversion is exact and once-only:
Y=(1+parity)/2 implies E[parity]=2E[Y]−1. Within-locus terms are dependent;
prospective independent-locus confidence premises require separate evidence.

The producer contracts/splits the original domain with exact rational or
justified outward interval arithmetic. The separate whole-journal checker
replays numerical steps and authenticates a complete retained cover. The
union extrema, rather than one selected box, determine reported widths.
Small outer widths do not alone prove that a physical source witness exists.
Resource exhaustion or an invalid journal must preserve conservative
uncertainty rather than manufacture a certificate.

The optional native/reference stage measures pair diagnostics on checked
cells. It cannot narrow the checker cover or promote scientific admission.
The offline molecular adapter compares matched reference, alternative and
combined-haplotype contexts and labels expression, splicing and
polyadenylation-related outputs separately. Such predictions are neither
experimental validation nor automatically admissible ancestry observations.

The earlier one-stage control localized rR to 1/256 and left the other eight
normalized widths one. It was a deliberately restricted control, not the
strongest inherited backend. The archived bounded full-domain run checked
23 stages and 50 frames, retained two cells and returned all nine widths one.

The explicit inherited full-domain mean separation certificate Delta=2^-512
at normalized 1/20 resolution is valuable theory. Its conservative sampling
implication is unusable here and its denominator exceeds the current numerical
input cap. Injectivity, compact inverse continuity and an explicit separation
bound do not establish affordable empirical precision. The source-admitted
near-collision and confidence-region obstructions are equally worth preserving.

Fresh execution records, exact commands, source hashes and resource receipts
are collected in the release evidence and independent validation artifacts.
The new application reproduced the exact archived requests byte-for-byte.
Its informative producer/checker ran in 3.425/3.476 seconds, while its
finite-data run took 1.719/1.519 seconds in this environment. These are single
run observations, not an improvement estimate. The finite-data optional
native diagnostic matched all four ordered pairs of its two retained cells.
The coordinator freshly rebuilt the pinned Rust component and reproduced

The report's claim tiers distinguish hand derivation, source/static review,

## Prior methods reused and comparison

The numerical producer, AB-profile contractors, independent whole-journal
checker, exact arithmetic conventions, signed receiver and Python reference
are inherited work. Their immutable pins and previous failed attempts remain
available. The integration adds an approachable common workflow, source
authentication, explicit examples, provenance and exports, rather than a new
inverse method.

Identified sets, interval branch-and-bound, rational confidence calculations,
Hoeffding/union bounds and posterior restriction are established techniques.
Variance-aware betting and confidence-sequence reasoning also have established
literature; a new implementation requires its own precise applicability proof.
The [comparison](validation/COMPARISON.md) distinguishes a matched inherited
backend comparison from tools using different experiments or metrics.

NANUQ, QNet, SNaQ/PhyloNetworks and Bayesian MSC/MSci tools address related
network or genealogy questions under their own source and observation models.
An estimated topology, posterior interval or likelihood estimate is not the
same output as this complete checked nine-parameter outer cover. Comparing
accuracy or speed requires matched assumptions, data, parameter domain and
metrics; no unmatched benchmark ranking or historical novelty claim is made.
Novelty remains unresolved without a credible broader prior-work census.

from subsequent offline Cargo commands.

## Biological applicability and AlphaGenome

The finite record is a public synthetic panel; confidence eligibility,
finite-generator-law certification and biological accuracy remain false.
The same-data joint/covariance-aware receivers also retain the near-collision
pair on the archived record, at their own confidence-region scopes. Altered
statistics need a justified joint locus observation law and a fresh coverage
proof; altered sampling needs predeclared, independent complete loci.

The inherited prospective 69,134-locus bound concerns a fixed-AA1 midpoint
test between two stated source points. It is a sufficient two-point risk
guarantee, not a required count or a whole-domain design. Better use of
observations is discussed with exact proposal status in the
[biology packet](biology/BIOLOGICAL-INPUTS.md).

The hemlock demonstration is descriptive. The recovered 4CL1 and rbcL markers,
specimen/accession joins, homology decisions, orthology/paralogy uncertainty
and linkage assumptions retain their recorded limits. Marker disagreement
alone does not prove chloroplast capture. This release does not relabel those
records as six-copy two-site clock-JC observations.

Optional AlphaGenome work remains offline. Current official SDK/API capabilities
and terms must be inspected and recorded before live setup. Account/service
permission and applicable usage terms, a secure environment or secret-manager
credential source, allowed data, output use and resource budget are required
before activation. Keys must never be sent in chat or stored in code, logs,
reports or Commons. Official capabilities are human/mouse regulatory prediction,
not an admitted hemlock genealogy channel. Absent access or missing output
types are reported explicitly and do not block the core application.

The dated [official-source audit](biology/ALPHAGENOME-OFFICIAL-SOURCES.json)
pins the current SDK and documents human/mouse support, four accepted sequence
lengths and separate RNA/splicing output types. The API has no direct PAS
enum; this demonstration's PAS quantity is a derived two-window proxy.
Official README usage restrictions are recorded as sourced documentation.
The current terms page returned a shell/sign-in page, so substantive legal
terms and service eligibility remain unresolved; no live setup or acceptance
was performed.

## Independent validation and limitations

The [independent review](validation/INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md) records exact reviewed
source hashes, commands, actual receipts, failures and corrections. Validation
covers regression, malformed input, incompatible models, ambiguity, boundaries,
journal/request tampering and bounded resources. Reviewer-authored tests and
comparison notes have a separate coordinator review and are not self-certified.

The original G3/G4 masters, full application formal correctness, empirical
source admission, retrospective finite-sampling confidence and useful biological
all-nine localization remain unverified. A checked nonempty outer cover is not
a verified source witness. A conditional width certificate is not measured
accuracy. A failure of one fixed band procedure is not universal impossibility.
No service, deployment, professor contact or persistent credential access is
part of this release.
