## The actual observation contract

The source model is `fixed-six-copy-clock-jc-nine-v1`: six **labelled phased**
copies A1,A2,B1,B2,C1,C2, two complete preselected sites per locus on **one
shared genealogy**, one fixed backward B-to-C pulse, original population-rate
ties, and stationary homogeneous clock-JC sequence evolution. One assignment
of h,u,v,rA,rB,rC,rAB,rR,g explains every feature. Original h,u,v ranges are
[1/32,1/8], all five rates [1/2,6], and g [1/6,2/3]. Here g is the probability
that the current backward B block routes to C; labels do not authorize a
generic gamma-complement transformation.

The pulse is at age h, AB merger at h+u and root merger at h+u+v, in one
calendar/clock scale. B has the same rate before and after the pulse; C has
the same rate in its tip and stem segments. Pulse decisions apply to current
surviving lineages: descendants already coalesced into one lineage route
together. The ideal BPP source compiler uses pair rate r=2/theta; unrestricted
BPP/MSci inference does not automatically impose these rate ties. The
[source-channel audit](../../2026-10-05-dot-msci-generated-phased-model-check-1854z/generator/GENERATOR-CHANNEL-AUDIT.md)
pins this distinction and the forward C-to-B/backward B-to-C convention.

The statistical sampling unit is the **whole locus**. Genuine independent
loci or the stated conditional mean law need scientific justification.
Conditional site independence on one genealogy does not make the two sites,
copy pairs or nine features marginally independent. Distinct locus IDs do
not demonstrate independent ancestry blocks. Missing calls, pooled/unphased
genotypes, ambiguity letters and independently chosen site genealogies are
outside this intake. Column selection must be fixed and digest-bound; choosing
sites after their outcomes changes the experiment.

The inherited exact extractor assigns chi(A)=chi(C)=1 and chi(G)=chi(T)=-1.
For each specified copy pair, `Y=(1+product(chi))/2`, using the first site for
order-1 and both sites for order-2. Its feature order is AC1,AC2,CC1,BC1,BC2,
AB1,AB2,AA1,BB1. The input quantity is a **shifted Bernoulli mean**. Under this
source law its raw character/Laplace moment is `2*mean-1`, with clock-JC
Laplace argument 8k/3. Convert **once**, intersect with the nonnegative model
moment interval [0,1], and reject an empty intersection. Feeding raw moments
under the shifted quantity tag changes the requested problem.

## Better observations: feasible proposals and existing limits

Three coherent paths should be evaluated against the same source and data,
with each changed statistic or design explicitly registered:

1. **Retain the complete nine-vector per locus.** Existing
   [joint betting and covariance receivers](../../2026-10-07-cloud-practical-1619z/README.md)
   already use within-locus dependence and predictable stakes. They retain
   the same pair on this archived record at their respective scopes; the
   covariance method uses its own future delta=1/20 event. Its approximately
   2.167/1.957 capital maxima do not cross threshold 20. Reusing their
   source-law/conditional-mean proof and exact conservative arithmetic is
   preferable to silently intersecting independently budgeted events. A
   future combined procedure needs a valid joint error budget and a complete
   checked source cover. These saved executions are reviewed inherited
   results, not freshly rerun by this role.
2. **Use more of each original complete panel under a new statistic.**
   [Existing same-data analysis](../../2026-10-07-dot-same-data-jc-statistics-boundary-1011z/README.md)
   averages JC contrasts, both sites and exchangeable cross-population copy
   pairs while preserving nine expected means. The whole locus remains one
   unit. These averages lie in [1/3,1], with established variance bounds, but
   they are not literal Bernoulli observations under the old receipt. The
   documented common range-aware Hoeffding and specified fixed-variance
   Bernstein upgrades still fail uniform all-width success through 100,000
   loci at the stated near-collision boundary. Other data-adaptive/joint
   methods and full joint two-site likelihood remain proposals requiring an
   actual forward observation law, calibrated coverage and matched-method
   comparisons. No retrospective confidence gain is released here.
3. **Predeclare genuinely fresh complete independent loci.** The
   [existing exact two-point design](../../2026-10-07-cloud-practical-1619z/TWO-POINT-PROSPECTIVE-BUDGET.md)
   gives 69,134 loci sufficient for a fixed-AA1 midpoint test between the two
   specified points, under admitted iid sampling, with worst-source error
   below 1/20. This is an established sufficient two-point bound, neither a
   necessary count nor a uniform nine-parameter design. Useful all-domain
   precision remains open. Additional sites, different phased panels or
   compartment-specific measurements require new source/observation contracts
   and should be justified as changed experiments before collection.
