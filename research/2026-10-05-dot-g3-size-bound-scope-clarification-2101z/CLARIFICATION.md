# Original G3: graph size is the missing witness bound

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 5 October 2026. Scope clarification by reuse of the original exact compiler; no new source theorem or novelty claim.

## Finite exact-input statement

Use the original G3 contract: finitely many admitted rational or effectively real-algebraic joint response data, one finite positive original source, one legal original-ID map and one shared parameter assignment. The [original exact-source criterion and compiler](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/eb284f41d13fff2602de4e98411fb15e5891f9e8/research/2026-09-30-g3-exact-source/EXACT-CRITERION.md) and the [current master statement](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/bc864455aaa705dbfd71d3e63429051420c0767d/research/2026-10-05-dot-original-g-master-priority-1913z/MASTER-STATEMENTS.md) remain controlling.

A total input-computable bound R with the following promise would suffice:

> If the complete supplied joint fibre has any finite strictly positive admitted realization, it has at least one whose graph has at most R reticulations (or an equivalent bound on vertices/edges).

No additional, separately assumed uniform lower bound on its positive parameters is needed for the recognition algorithm.

## Why the original compiler already handles the margin

With graph size bounded, enumerate the finite admitted graph shapes and legal maps for the finite original-ID register. For each shape, use the original polynomial compiler with the SAME variables in every response row. Retain every declared algebraic tie, natural strict inequality, joint randomized-control weight and readout constraint. Exact real-closed-field decision determines whether that finite conjunction is satisfiable.

If every bounded shape is unsatisfiable, the promised size bound makes this a complete NO over the original source class. If one is satisfiable, effective real-algebraic sampling produces a strictly interior algebraic parameter tuple for that graph. This does not convert a zero-length, infinite-length or endpoint-inheritance limit into a positive witness: its original strict inequalities must actually hold.

If the parameter-coordinate set is empty, simply choose any integer budget covering the finite graph. Otherwise, for the finitely many survival and inheritance coordinates v in (0,1), compute the positive algebraic number min_v{v,1-v}. Algebraic comparisons then give an integer B large enough to bound the graph and place every such coordinate in [1/B,1-1/B]. Thus a compact positivity budget can be computed AFTER finding the bounded-size witness. All other admitted algebraic constraints remain in the same sampled tuple.

Here it is the survival coordinate x=exp(-t) that can be chosen algebraic. The physical coalescent duration t=-log(x) is positive and computable, but need not be algebraic. No algebraicity of that logarithm is claimed or required by this compiler argument.

## What this does not remove

The unavailable part is R: a computable graph/private-word compression bound for at least ONE witness of the whole joint input. The decorated-core bound does not bound private-word lengths. A local rank certificate, a single-slot point-membership test, a closure test or a bound covering only one chosen presentation does not supply R for the complete coupled fibre. Any proof via limits still has to cover genuine ties, critical/zero-gap cases, alternative cores and the original source rules.

This clarification concerns finite exact input only. A uniformly computable infinite all-cap profile is not a finite real-closed-field conjunction. Its original nonescape criterion remains the joint size-and-margin statement exists B, for every m, T(B,m). No arbitrary-real equality oracle, decision procedure for that infinite input, or collapse of those quantifiers follows.

The immediate research target is therefore the actual graph/word-size reduction or a complete source-faithful exclusion argument. Positivity-margin bookkeeping should not be mistaken for an additional independent hypothesis needed once such a finite-input size bound is available. Original G3 remains open.
