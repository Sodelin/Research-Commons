## Same-age events and the stated source

Tomáš Flouri, Xiyun Jiao, Bruce Rannala and Ziheng Yang, “[A Bayesian Implementation of the Multispecies Coalescent Model with Introgression for Phylogenomic Analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7086182/),” *Molecular Biology and Evolution* 37(4), 1211–1223 (2020), describes hybrid nodes whose parents can have the same age as the node; its Figure 1 caption identifies model B as an introgression case with equal event ages. The paper also describes tracing a lineage through a hybrid node, the `4Nμ` population convention, one shared genealogy within a locus and independent genealogies among loci under fixed model parameters. These are source-attributed descriptions. The selected [contract](MODEL-CONVENTIONS-COMPARISON.md) separately fixes this manuscript's direction, six samples, two distinct population-rate ties and JC channel. Reading these passages does not prove equality of the full stochastic laws or that an arbitrary positive-edge graph grammar admits the zero-duration convention.

The [2022 correction notice](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/39/11/msac245/6827898), *Molecular Biology and Evolution* 39(11), msac245, says the originally posted supplementary material was incorrect and omitted two tables; it supplies a replacement supplement. ## Independent, common and correlated routing

John Fogg, Elizabeth S. Allman and Cécile Ané, “[PhyloCoalSimulations: A Simulator for Network Multispecies Coalescent Models, Including a New Extension for the Inheritance of Gene Flow](https://academic.oup.com/sysbio/article/72/5/1171/7187011),” *Systematic Biology* 72(5), 1171–1179 (2023), distinguishes independent inheritance across lineages at a reticulation from common inheritance and develops a correlated alternative. The indexed source text states that the latter can interpolate between inheritance assumptions. This manuscript fixes independent choices for the **current** B blocks. It does not treat independence across distinct blocks as independence among descendants already combined in one block.

For two distinct B blocks present at the pulse, the stated independent-Bernoulli rule gives `g²` for both moving, `(1-g)²` for both staying, and `2g(1-g)` for a split. A single shared Bernoulli choice with the same marginal `g` would give `g`, `1-g` and `0`, respectively. These are elementary conditional routing probabilities, not observed sequence probabilities, a simulation result, or a proof about another model's identifiability. The contrast explains why one cannot silently substitute a common coin in the manuscript's BB formula. The original source theorem does not assess whether independent or correlated routing fits a biological dataset.
