## Why it is related

The paper compares outgroup-ghost, inflow and outflow hypotheses on a known `AB|C` backbone; its Figure 1 caption describes inflow as forward `C → B`. That is the same coarse direction as the present source's backward `B → C` pulse. The common backbone and arrow do **not** by themselves establish identical parameterizations, population-size ties, sampled data or probability laws. The present manuscript fixes the direction and uses two labelled copies per species, a known normalized JC clock and the joint law of two sites sharing a genealogy.

The paper's introduction describes its theoretical analysis as using one sequence per species. Its theory compares gene-tree topology probabilities, selected pooled biallelic site-pattern summaries and pair-coalescence-time distributions across three scenarios. It reports that the particular topology and site-pattern methods studied cannot distinguish these scenarios in the stated simple setup, while the pair-time distributions give different scenario features. Its full-likelihood discussion concerns the joint gene-tree topology and time distribution; it is not a claim that gene-tree times are observed without error in a sequence alignment.

The paper reports separate simulations: gene trees and 1,000-base-pair sequences under HKY were generated, and a restricted BPP comparison used specified parameter settings. These are the authors' reported analyses, not a test performed here and not an independent proof of the present theorem. Their cross-scenario problem differs from identification of nine numerical parameters **within** the present fixed family from its exact two-site sequence law. In particular, this comparison does not claim that either result implies a useful two-site sample size, identifies arbitrary network direction, or establishes biological fit.

## Explicit source questions

- The introduction describes one sampled sequence per species, and the listed rooted topologies use the three labels `a,b,c`. A later sentence in Theory instead says that there are three sequences from *each* species at a locus. The separately described simulation also has a one-sequence-per-species condition and a distinct multi-sequence experiment. Both passages appear in the extracted advance-PDF text and in the extracted typeset-PDF text as well as in the HTML presentations; those dependent sources do not resolve the wording. The analysis scope above follows the explicit introduction and its three-leaf topology description, while retaining the Theory sentence as an unresolved inconsistency rather than silently correcting it.
- The pair-time discussion says that in the inflow scenario the lower endpoint for the `bc` coalescence time is the introgression time. Separately, the BPP simulation discussion says that its parameter `τ_H` cannot be inferred in that analysis. The Figure 1 caption uses `τ_S`, `τ_T` and `τ_R` for its displayed reference times. The mapping between these labels, the precise BPP parameterization and the applicable observed-data laws has not been established here. This is a question for model-specific reconciliation, not an asserted contradiction with the present six-copy result or a correction to the authors' work.

## Conditional comparison of the inflow pair formulas

The displayed inflow densities in Supplementary Note 2, equation (5), have an `AB` contribution on the T branch and a root contribution; the `BC` expression has an introgressed S-branch contribution and a root contribution. Their S- and T-branch survival factors are displayed at the start of the note. In the **visually inspected original**, the last line of equation (5) repeats the left-hand label `f(t_bc)`, while its exponent and domain use `t_ac`. This is an apparent notation inconsistency. The following comparison explicitly treats that last right-hand side as the `AC` density; the source is not silently amended, and an authoritative correction or confirmation has not been located.

For this algebraic comparison only, suppose the two models' time units and population assignments agree, `τ_S=h`, `τ_T=t1`, `τ_R=t0`, `l_S=t0-h`, `l_T=t0-t1`, `2/θ_S=rC`, `2/θ_T=rAB`, `2/θ_R=rR`, and `γ=g`. These identifications are hypotheses, not a verified mapping of the article's full BPP/MSci network. Put

\[
W_z(b,e,r)=e^{-zb}\frac{r}{r+z}\left(1-e^{-(r+z)(e-b)}\right),\qquad
V_z(e,R)=e^{-ze}\frac{R}{R+z}.
\]

Direct integration of each displayed positive-rate branch against `exp(-z t)` gives the following conditional comparison with the retained [forward formulas](FORWARD-SOURCE-CORRESPONDENCE.md):

| Pair | Integral under the stated identifications | Draft branch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AB` | `(1-g) W_z(t1,t0,rAB) + [g+(1-g) exp(-rAB*(t0-t1))] V_z(t0,rR)` | `AB` |
| `BC` | `g W_z(h,t0,rC) + [1-g+g exp(-rC*(t0-h))] V_z(t0,rR)` | `BC` |
| `AC` | `V_z(t0,rR)` | `AC`, **conditional on the relabeling above** |

At `z=0`, the finite-branch masses and root-tail weights in the first two rows each sum to one; the third root density also has mass one. This elementary integration is a source-to-formula comparison, not a formal proof, a new stochastic-process construction, or evidence that a six-sample process has these pair marginals. In particular, it says nothing about the within-species `AA`, `BB` or `CC` pairs, shared two-site genealogies, full nine-parameter identification, or empirical performance.

The paper's equation (5) uses `τ_S` as the lower endpoint of its inflow `BC` branch; the main article separately discusses `τ_H` as the introgression time in its BPP analysis. Equation (5) does not explicitly display `τ_H`, but its branch-length parameters may depend on the model's timing convention. The figure and BPP parameterization needed to decide that relationship were not visually checked here. The hypothesis `τ_S=h` therefore remains a conditional comparison, and the paper's discussion of `τ_H` is not resolved or contradicted by it.

For context, the current BPP project's [manual at repository commit `c442d58c15e5967c9d1e93b2132773e429a9ece4`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bpp/bpp-manual/c442d58c15e5967c9d1e93b2132773e429a9ece4/bpp-4-manual.md) describes generic MSC-I examples with either `τ_S > τ_H` (its model A) or `τ_S = τ_H` (its model B1), depending on parent-time settings. The same manual records a change in the convention for the introgression probability beginning with BPP 4.4. These examples explain why graph, parameter ties and software version are material to a crosswalk; they do **not** identify the particular model or BPP version used by Pang and Zhang. In particular, matching the pair-density onset to this manuscript's `h` does not by itself prove that their separately named `τ_H` equals `h` or that either model's full sequence law is the other's. The manual's source was read at the pinned commit; no BPP program, model or simulation was run.
