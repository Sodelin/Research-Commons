# Bounded prior-work comparison: Brits and colleagues, 2026

## Source and scope

On 31 October 2026 UTC, this pass read the abstract, introduction, model description and the statement of Theorem 4.9 in Jari Brits, Niels Holtgrefe, Leo van Iersel and Samuel Martin, [*On Tree-Network Distinguishability and Full Identifiability of Phylogenetic Networks*, arXiv:2607.12919v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12919v3), dated 25 August 2026. It also compared the abstract and introduction with [v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12919v1), dated 14 July 2026. This was a selected primary-text reading, not an independent proof audit; no complete source bytes were retained or hashed. The arXiv record identifies v3 as the latest version displayed at the time of this pass, not a journal-review or acceptance record.

## Relationship to the manuscript

The v3 abstract and Theorem 4.9 report full **topology** identification, up to the stated triangle ambiguity, for level-1 semi-directed networks under JC, K2P or K3P on the paper's restricted parameter space. “Full” there distinguishes its stated all-parameter-point topology result from a generic result. It does not mean that all numerical branch or population parameters have been recovered. The paper uses a displayed-tree substitution mixture; its introduction says those substitution models do not incorporate incomplete lineage sorting, while discussing some consequences for coalescent-based models separately.

The present manuscript instead fixes its three-taxon directed backbone and asks whether nine specified **numerical parameters** are identified under a multispecies coalescent with an introgression pulse, six labelled samples and a shared genealogy across two sites. The 2026 paper is relevant context but does not by itself prove or refute that exact statement. A rigorous model-to-model transfer, wider prior-art assessment and independent reviews remain open; no priority claim follows from this comparison.

## Version-sensitive distinction

The v1 abstract describes its tree-versus-network distinction under **JC and K2P**, whereas the v3 abstract states the corresponding result under **JC**. This observation concerns those stated versions and passages; it does not establish why the wording changed or independently adjudicate either proof. The separate level-1 topology result in v3 is stated for JC, K2P and K3P. Do not import the broader v1 abstract wording into the v3 result or conflate the two results.
