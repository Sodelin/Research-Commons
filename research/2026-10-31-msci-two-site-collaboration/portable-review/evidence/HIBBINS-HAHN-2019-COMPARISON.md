# Hibbins and Hahn 2019: timing, direction and pair divergence

Mark S. Hibbins and Matthew W. Hahn, “[The Timing and Direction of Introgression Under the Multispecies Network Coalescent](https://doi.org/10.1534/genetics.118.301831),” *Genetics* 211(3), 1059–1073 (2019), examines related three-taxon pulse scenarios. The [publisher record](https://academic.oup.com/genetics/article/211/3/1059/5931156) gives the article date as 22 January 2019 and the issue date as March 2019; the [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30670542/) also gives PMID 30670542 and PMCID PMC6404246.

The cached excerpts are one article, not independent copies or a complete reading; no original publisher response bytes were retained, no figures or supplement were inspected, and no article equation or proof was visually checked.

The authors use the backbone `((A,B),C)` in a network-coalescent setting and consider several scenarios, including forward gene flow from `C` into `B`. They describe one sampled lineage per extant species, generally with an outgroup. Their `D1` and `D2` are hypothesis-testing statistics based on expected coalescence times or sequence divergence associated with alternative gene-tree topologies. The stated aims are, respectively, to inform relative timing and direction in conjunction with other information. Their simplified analytic expectations have conditions; the discussion notes sensitivity to ancestry proportions and population sizes. The article also reports simulations and an empirical application, which were not independently assessed here.

For the forward `C`-to-`B` scenario, the selected `D2` discussion says the expected A–C coalescence time is unchanged between its two parent histories. That is qualitatively consistent with the retained manuscript's A–C pair being unable to meet before the root. This is not a completed time-unit, population-size or model mapping, nor does equality of a mean establish equality of distributions. The paper's other direction and comparisons are outside the manuscript's fixed backward `B`-to-`C` family.

The present two-site argument instead uses six labelled samples and within-locus JC character moments to distinguish parameter vectors *inside* a stipulated direction and model. It does not infer the direction or implement the earlier tests. The selected passages do not establish that the 2019 statistics prove or contradict this nine-parameter claim. This bounded comparison does not determine priority, prove the source pair laws or replace mathematical and biological review; full-text, figure, supplement and detailed model comparisons remain open.
