# Source conventions and foundational model references

This is a bounded source and attribution check for the manuscript's model description. It is not a proof audit, historical-priority finding, certification of the biological model, or complete literature search.

## The selected source contract

The complete retained contract was read from guide commit `501730afc601aa196ff4c7b84e96105a63199668`, path `recovery/msci/source/f5837e3f1f1604333f5dc6f4c6a23eeca5f95f74.txt`, SHA-256 `dee9fb1a751553e8fdd27d50c81f2cb5456f9d76ad60259f10ddd1d7a1b75d83`. Its sections 1–2 specify the fixed mutation scale, the diploid convention `theta_P = 4 N_P mu`, the **per-current-pair** coalescence rate `2/theta_P`, a single backward B-to-C pulse with independent current-lineage routing, fixed event ages and a root continuing until all sampled lineages coalesce. The pulse corresponds to forward C-to-B introgression; existing C lineages do not move at that pulse. The contract ties `theta_B` to itself across the pulse and separately ties `theta_C` to itself across the pulse; it does not require `theta_B = theta_C`, so `rB` and `rC` may differ.

The contract gives each locus one sampled genealogy, independent JC site evolution conditional on that genealogy, independently uniform root states, and independent repetitions across loci at the same parameters. It adds no missing-data, sequencing-error, ascertainment, alignment-uncertainty, selection or duplication/loss channel. The manuscript's statement that it models no within-locus genealogy switch is an inference from this one-genealogy channel; it is not a claim that all empirical recombination is absent. The contract does not assert that the model fits a dataset.

The retained [55-site theorem](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/df6705e55a830869b8c89e7603edb09cb629d07b/research/2026-10-05-dot-msci-55-site-separation-0508z/THEOREM.md), section 1, writes `r_P=2/theta_P` and states the pair projection on which the later formulas depend. Those retained source assertions and the later scoped hand result are not independently re-proved by this comparison.

## Foundational references and limits

- Jukes and Cantor, [*Evolution of Protein Molecules*](https://evoluscope.fr/phylographe/biblio/JukesCantor1969.pdf), chapter 24, pp. 21–132. The accessible third-party reproduction's extracted text at printed pp. 99–100 discusses the approximation of equal single-base changes and a correction for multiple changes. The 1969 date, volume and page range are also given in [Jukes's later bibliographic account](https://garfield.library.upenn.edu/classics1990/A1990CZ67100002.pdf). The manuscript's normalized generator, independently uniform roots and shared-genealogy product are the **selected contract's** stated conventions; this check does not attribute the complete MSci observation model to the 1969 chapter.
- Kingman, [*The Coalescent*](https://www.ccg.unam.mx/~vinuesa/tlem/pdfs/Kingman_1982_Coalescent.pdf), *Stochastic Processes and their Applications* 13 (1982), pp. 235–248. The university-hosted reproduction's extracted text at printed pp. 235–236 defines the finite partition process with unit rate for each transition that combines two current equivalence classes and gives total rate `k(k-1)/2` with `k` classes. The manuscript's population-dependent rate `r_P`, pulse, joins and diploid parameter convention come from the selected contract; Kingman's one-population result alone is not the present source process or a proof of its pair projection.

The later [pair-projection comparison](PAIR-PROJECTION-LUMPABILITY.md) records the same paper's natural-restriction passages on printed pp. 239 and 245, its reliance on Kingman's separate 1982 genealogy paper, and a finite-state Markov projection criterion reported in that paper's §5. It distinguishes the classical single-population result from the model-specific phase and pulse obligations. The additional genealogy-paper body was read as a third-party transcription with degraded formulas, not as an authenticated publisher edition.

The two public PDF bodies were inspected through extracted text only. Screenshot requests returned references but no inspectable image payload in this session. Publisher DOI fetches were inaccessible through this interface, and neither mirror's bytes were authenticated against a publisher edition or retained as a complete original. The bibliographic links provide reader access; the local source and these references do not independently verify the complete theorem or its formalization.
