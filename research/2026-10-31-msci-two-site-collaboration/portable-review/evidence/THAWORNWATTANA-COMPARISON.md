# Prior-work comparison: Thawornwattana and colleagues

## Source and scope

Yuttapong Thawornwattana, Jun Huang, Tomáš Flouri, James Mallet and Ziheng Yang, [*Inferring the Direction of Introgression Using Genomic Sequence Data*](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msad178), *Molecular Biology and Evolution* 40(8), msad178 (2023), is named in the retained identification proof as related pair-law work. On 31 October 2026 UTC, this pass read selected text of the [published article hosted by PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10439365/): the abstract, model setup and Figure 1 caption, the two-species pairwise-coalescence discussion, and selected discussion of identifiability. No complete publisher-response bytes were saved or hashed, and the entire article and supplements were not independently reviewed.

## What is comparable

The article uses an MSC-with-introgression model and examines distributions of coalescence times for within- and between-species pairs in its two-species analysis. It reports that, in its stated two-species setting, a between-species pair alone cannot distinguish its opposite-direction models, while within-species pair information from multiple sampled sequences can distinguish them. It cites earlier work by [Yang and Flouri (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msac083) for related identifiability results; that earlier paper was not independently read in this pass. The 2023 study also uses simulations for three or four species and analyzes a butterfly dataset. Those separate analyses do not validate the present manuscript's fixed model or its biological applicability.

The article's Figure 1 caption explicitly says its introgression arrows point **forward in time**. This manuscript states its pulse as **backward B-to-C**. Population labels and arrow conventions therefore need an explicit mapping before any formula or direction claim is transferred. The article measures time in expected mutations per site and states a pairwise coalescence rate `2/theta`; agreement of notation alone is not a complete equivalence of its models with this manuscript's nine-parameter source.

## Boundary for the manuscript

The published work supports citing pairwise-coalescence analysis as a relevant precedent. It does not by itself prove this manuscript's fixed three-species, six-sample identification statement, determine its pulse direction, provide a required sample size, or establish a complete Lean proof. The exact source-to-source model correspondence, the cited 2022 prior work, broader prior art and independent scientific/biological review remain open. This pass records the authors' claims and definitions; it does not independently prove their mathematical results or rerun their simulations.
