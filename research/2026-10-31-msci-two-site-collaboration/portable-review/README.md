# Portable MSci manuscript: local review draft

Start with [MANUSCRIPT.md](MANUSCRIPT.md). This folder contains the full
internal reading edition and 22 directly linked evidence notes. It is a
portable review draft, not a Zenodo deposit or a final publication package.
Human authorship, contribution statements, rights and license remain open.

The source manuscript and notes were read from local Git commit `eac66d66775556a264dc43124799b0ecd1a41c2a`.
The proof and historical review appendices in the manuscript are byte-for-byte
unchanged from that source. Links in the principal text and evidence notes were
adapted to this folder; 15 deeper internal supporting files are not
included and their links are explicitly labeled in the affected evidence notes.
External links require internet access. [MANIFEST.json](MANIFEST.json) records
the source and output hashes and the omitted source paths. [PACKAGES.json](PACKAGES.json)
is the original source-join manifest; its own paths describe the original guide
and are not a promise that those guide files are included here.

The full Lean proof, independent expert review and biological validation remain
open. The compiler owner's reported two-file root-component result is scoped
and does not verify the rest of the theorem. Reproduction and approval of the
selected files and rights are needed before an archival deposit.
