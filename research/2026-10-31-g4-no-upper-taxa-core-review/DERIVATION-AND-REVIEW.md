## 1. Source class, public interface and quantifiers

Each source is finite, strict and admitted in the original binary rooted-LSA, outer-labelled, hybrid-cut-child class, on the same n≥4 original taxa. Population lengths are positive and finite, and inheritance coins lie strictly inside (0,1). Natural INDEPENDENT inheritance draws fresh parental choices for CURRENT surviving lineages; an already merged subtree is opaque and routes once. Above the root rho is the ordinary unbounded Kingman completion.

Both child edges of rho are cut edges. Fix an original taxon C. Its root-child cut component has a nonempty taxon set C0. In the OTHER root-child component, an eligible bridge slot S_up, which is an admitted positive finite serial word, leads to the top v of a simple nonroot one-hybrid gall. Its arms are directed ordinary paths from v to H, with at least one intermediate side vertex in total. Every intermediate side vertex has one off-cycle child cut component. H's child cut component has nonempty leaf set A0. There are **no upper-region taxa**: every taxon outside C0 lies in A0 or in one of those side components.

All descendant cut-component interiors may be arbitrary admitted finite sources, including nested blobs and positive bridge words. They have one upward cut port and private natural randomness, not a shared stochastic register across components. Their size is unbounded. This is a selected simple main gall with arbitrary component interiors, not a claim that the entire original graph has only one hybrid. Cycle edges themselves are ordinary, not independently inserted serial slots.

The rival N may have a DIFFERENT A0, C0, side partition, arm allocation, order or cycle length. It has its own arbitrary physical tuple, fixed across all its rows; the target T has its own fixed tuple. Tuples are NOT equated beforehand.

This is the **unmarked natural-I full rooted labelled-topology interface**: original taxon/copy labels and the stated root-completion convention are retained. No actuator settings or externally supplied internal vertex/parent-edge labels are inferred or erased. Arm naming may be exchanged only in this unmarked interface. A source comparison with a marked or supplied internal-ID interface needs the corresponding explicit matching theorem; this result does not authorize silent renaming under the original compiler contract.

The assumptions that BOTH roots have two cut children and that their selected main galls lie on the side opposite C are source-class restrictions, not conclusions against arbitrary original rivals.

## 2. Legal finite panel and conclusion

For each original taxon i select a row R_i with THREE copies of i and one of every other taxon. For each unordered pair {i,j}, select R_ij with TWO copies of each of i,j and one of every other taxon. There are

    n + n(n−1)/2 = n(n+1)/2

physical rows, each with n+2 total copies. Every original taxon receives a positive allocation. All smaller laws used below are exact fixed-label marginals of these full laws, not zero-copy physical experiments or hidden-state probes.

**Theorem.** If N and T are in the class above and match this panel exactly, then their root taxon partitions coincide. Their main H-descendant blocks coincide, as do their side-port taxon partition, split between arms up to naming, order along each arm and cycle length. They have the same oriented main coin and every ordinary main-cycle survival. For a chosen common A in A0, they also have the same two-root no-merger coordinate of its actual cut-exit kernel; every selected side taxon's cap3 cut-exit kernel and the cap3 composite S_up kernel agree. Same-port two-taxon no-merger coordinates agree as well.

No component interior, serial word, higher-cap kernel or C-side source is thereby recovered. This is semantic finite forcing within the class, not an exact-real comparison algorithm or effective stopping theorem.

## 4. Root taxon partition from crossing triples

If a,b are in one root cut component and c in the other, let d be the probability that a,b remain separate on reaching rho. An actual positive top edge gives d<1. Conditional on separation, the three roots first merge uniformly. Hence

    Pr(((a,b),c))=1−2d/3,
    Pr(((a,c),b))=Pr(((b,c),a))=d/3.

The within-component pair is strictly preferred. Suppose two different nontrivial root bipartitions were compatible with the same crossing-triple laws. Some a,b in one block of the first partition must lie in different blocks of the second; otherwise the two nonempty partitions would coincide. Choose c in the other first block. The first partition strictly prefers ab, while the second strictly prefers ac or bc, a contradiction. This also covers singleton root blocks.

Therefore the all-one-copy marginal pins the root partition against two-cut-root rivals. In particular C0 is common. A root blob is outside this argument.

## 5. Fixed common A and B: side ports and the A-block test

Temporarily suppose A is below H and B at a side port in BOTH sources. The [public W3 theorem](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/1b5e86b30f4ea222f6f5efdb67b7bd91f92c8e93/research/2026-10-31-g4-three-cycle-wrapper-review/DERIVATION-AND-REVIEW.md), with the [family's actual pruning maps](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/f35e1d5c9c5d213af92d558ab826d2cb91a58afa/research/2026-10-31-g4-fixed-port-gall-review/DERIVATION-AND-REVIEW.md), recovers from R_B the coin g toward B's arm, its prefix x and upper pair survival U>0. Other side branches have no retained inputs, so only consecutive ordinary cycle edges are multiplied. No word is commuted past a hybrid.

For distinct taxa i,j outside C0, let D_ij=3 Pr(((i,C),j)). It is their actual root separation. Write p_ij=D_ij/U. Set

    d=1−g+gx,        M=1−g²(1−x).

For t different from A,B outside C0,

    t is below H  iff  p_Bt=d and p_At<M.                       (1)

Indeed, if t is below H, p_Bt=d. If z is the JOINT A/t separation at H entry, and L,R are the two total arm survivals, then

    p_At=z[g²L+(1−g)²R+2g(1−g)]<M,

because L<x, R<1 and 0≤z≤1. A merged pair routes once. Conversely a side t on the opposite arm gives p_Bt=1>d; at B's port it gives x z_Bt<x<d; farther along B's arm it gives x<d. At a nearer port with prefix y, equality p_Bt=d forces y=d, but then p_At=1−g+gd=M. This exhausts the no-upper-taxa class.

Once A0 is known, apply the single-A W3 inverse to EVERY side taxon b to obtain its prefix x_b and the same U. For distinct side taxa b,c,

    D_bc/U = 1                          on opposite arms,
             max(x_b,x_c)               at distinct same-arm ports,
             x_b z_bc,  0<z_bc<1         at the same port.

Strict edges give distinct prefixes at distinct vertices on one arm. Thus D_bc=U identifies opposite arms; within an arm, equal prefixes identify the port, and decreasing prefixes give the order away from v. Take the same-arm relation reflexively. Fair g and equal total arm survivals introduce no exception. Same-port z_bc is recovered as D_bc/(Ux_b).

One two-A marginal from R_A gives both total arm survivals and d_A. The public family theorem then recovers every edge by positive successive-prefix ratios, treating a portless arm as its single total edge. It recovers the stated cap3 kernels, not their physical words.

## 6. Remove the common SIDE anchor while keeping a common A

Suppose A lies below both main hybrids, but the side sets S_T,S_N were disjoint. Choose b in S_T and t in S_N. In T, A,t are below H and b is a side taxon; in N, A,b are below H and t is a side taxon. Pair marginals give

    D_Ab=D_bt=D_At.

Within EACH source the three pair separation probabilities at v are therefore equal, although its U need not equal the other source's U. If P3 is the total probability of one three-leaf tree at v, the cherry mass is C_ij|k=1−sigma_ij−P3, so the three cherry masses are equal too.

For a specified exit triple, the upper kernel contributes

    E_((i,j),k)=S_v t_U+C_ij|k(1−U)+T_((i,j),k).

Thus triple differences cancel both common upper terms. In T the actual history count yields

    E_((A,t),b)−E_((A,b),t)
      =g(1−x)[(1−z)+zg(1−y)]>0,
    E_((A,b),t)=E_((b,t),A),

where y is the suffix on b's arm and z is A/t separation at H entry. The first contribution is a pair already merged below H, routing ONCE; the second is a separate pair routing together and merging on the suffix. All remaining prefix triple histories are equal. In N the exchanged roles give E_((A,t),b)=E_((b,t),A), contradicting the two T relations on the SAME decoded exit law.

Hence some side taxon is common. Its preselected triplicate row supplies (1), forcing the entire A0 block to agree; section5 then applies. A common side taxon may be selected after considering a rival because ALL candidate rows were selected before the rival. No row-specific source refitting occurs.

## 7. Mixed-copy orientation sign removes the common H anchor

Let alpha lie below H and beta at a side port. Retain two copies of each. Let ka,kb>0 be their actual separate-exit probabilities. Orient g toward beta's arm, let x be the prefix and s its suffix. Define the labelled two-root forests

    X={((alpha1,beta1),alpha2),beta2},
    Y={((alpha1,beta1),beta2),alpha2}.

Only three input types at v can refine X: X itself, the three-root forest {(alpha1,beta1),alpha2,beta2}, and four singletons. The analogous list for Y has the SAME latter two inputs. Opaque grafting cannot undo any other cherry or tree. Exchangeability of the actual upper kernel gives identical coefficients on those two common inputs; its two-root no-merger coefficient is U. Hence

    E[X]−E[Y]=U(P_v(X)−P_v(Y)).

For X, both alpha roots must choose beta's arm and survive the suffix separately, with the beta roots separate at their port. If h4_x denotes the specified four-root prefix-history probability, this contributes ka kb g² s h4_x. The SAME term contributes to Y. In addition, Y can arise when alpha1 takes beta's arm, alpha2 takes the direct arm, and alpha1,beta1,beta2 complete the specified triple on the prefix. Its probability is ka kb g(1−g)t_x, where

    t_x=(1−x)²(2+x)/6>0.

There is no opposite extra history for X: a beta root cannot escape its own arm. Previously merged alpha or beta pairs have the wrong first cherry. Consequently

    E[X]−E[Y]=−U ka kb g(1−g)t_x<0.                            (2)

This is a PER-LABELLED formula. Each orbit has multiplicity four; an orbit-summed formula multiplies both sides by four, not just one side.

If the rival puts beta below H and alpha at the side, the SAME labelled difference is positive. The known ordinary completion decodes both from the legal (2,2,C) marginal, so they cannot match.

If T's and N's H-descendant blocks were disjoint, choose alpha in the former and beta in the latter. Each lies at a side port in the other source because there are no upper taxa. Jointly prune all other material. The public family pruning map produces exactly these opposite W3 marginals, with arbitrary actual finite cut kernels and strict products. Thus (2) excludes EVERY disjoint-block case, including multiple-taxon blocks and additional shared side taxa. It does not assume the kernels are arbitrary positive matrices or literally pendant words.

The H blocks therefore intersect. Choose a common A in the intersection; section6 forces equality of the whole blocks and section5 completes the claimed recovery. All needed rows were in the preselected finite panel. This proves the theorem.
