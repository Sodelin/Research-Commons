## Scientific scope

The source is one strictly positive independent-inheritance bigon between two strictly positive ordinary Kingman pads, with exact rational survival/weight parameters. Full labelled forests, root-count observations and targets preserve one shared source and original identities. The runnable cap is one through five entering interface roots; it is not a universal determining cap or a rule equating specimens with interface roots.

The finite-catalogue sorter provides exact compatibility and conditional time-uniform fresh-locus filtering under its declared source/channel/sampling assumptions. It reports target agreement within that catalogue or abstains. It also implements its cited fixed-shape all-copy parameter relation and a nondegenerate four-root trailing-survival branch. Read [the input contract](package/docs/INPUT-CONTRACT.md) and [confidence proof](package/docs/CONFIDENCE.md).

The separate four-taxon sequence estimator is exploratory and does not supply a calibrated DNA-to-interface channel. This package does not implement the later continuous nine-parameter six-copy phased-JC inverse, general G3 recognition, unknown-size G4 stopping or whole-program Lean certification.

Recovered existing capability is useful reuse, not mathematical novelty: source coherence, confidence accounting, retained alternatives, identity checks and abstention need not be rebuilt. A future typed continuous-cover adapter must preserve its different source/channel, whole-domain coverage and numerical-validation obligations. No such new adapter is claimed here.
