This is an executable consequence of the existing source model, not a new
mathematical recognition theorem. It supplies a sound NO rule, not a complete
G3 stopping algorithm.

Fix ONE finite admitted positive rooted-LSA binary outer-labelled planar
cut-child graph, with any finite number of unmarked hidden vertices/hybrids.

identities. At a protected original hybrid H, COMMON inheritance uses one
Bernoulli bit B_H shared by every current root at that site. The original source
register law is a product across original sites; the bit is independent of all
other source randomness. Its natural parameter satisfies0<gamma_H<1. The fixed
original-tip sampling/readout plan does not condition on the realized hidden bit.

For any chosen original-tip sample panel and any fixed background programme at
other sites, let Lnat,L0,L1 be the exact unranked genealogy readout laws when H
is natural, forced to its original parent0, or forced to its original parent1.
All remaining original source parameters, source randomness laws and readout
coordinates are the SAME across this triple.

Then, as probability measures and hence coordinatewise,

    Lnat = gamma_H L0 + (1-gamma_H) L1.

Proof: condition on B_H. Under B_H=b, the source's one COMMON operation at H
sends every current root to original parent b. This is exactly the forced-b
operation, with the other original randomness/parameters unchanged. Independence
of B_H from those sources of randomness makes the conditional law the forced
law. Apply the law of total probability. The same argument applies for every
sample panel and background programme, with the SAME gamma_H. Coalescence before
or after H, an empty current cohort, arbitrary graph level/hidden size and the
actual above-root continuation do not change the conditional source identity.
