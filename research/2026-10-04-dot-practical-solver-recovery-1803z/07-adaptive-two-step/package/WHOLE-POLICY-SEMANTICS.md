# A complete source-admitted response-dependent two-step policy

This is an executable instance of the inherited G7 contract. The actual source
class and its nine joint images are inherited unchanged from the independently
accepted complete 546-source four-taxon/one-hybrid certificate. The original
source is a positive binary rooted, LSA, outer-labelled, planar cut-child DAG,
with parallel edges allowed, one named original hybrid H0, free positive
edge-specific coalescent lengths, interior natural inheritance parameter, and
one common or independent mechanism fixed throughout the experiment. The
observed law is the full unrooted, original-tip-labelled quartet law from one original copy per taxon.
Neither latent routing nor a displayed-target oracle is observed.

For each ordered forcing-target pair (a,b), one original source assignment has
two survival monomials (s,t) in the joint OPEN unit square. Its forcing-0 law is
f_a(s), and its forcing-1 law is f_b(t), where f_a(s) has coordinate a equal to
1-2s/3 and the other two coordinates equal to s/3. The actual displayed quartet
target is the set {a,b}. Completeness and surjectivity of these JOINT images,
including all shared original edges, are a separately pinned accepted provider.
They are not inferred from two independently fitted marginal catalogues.

The policy is:

1. Call the original forcing-0 configuration and retain the entire response h.
2. Choose the real weight w=h[0], then call the pooled programme with weight w
   on forcing 0 and weight 1-w on forcing 1. Retain the entire new response z.
3. Return exactly the quartet coordinates strictly above min(z).

This action changes on actual reachable responses. For example the a=0 source
with s=1/3 chooses w=7/9, while s=2/3 chooses w=5/9. There is no response
discretization and no rational-only restriction in the mathematical policy.
The first response alone is ambiguous: actual (a,b)=(0,0) and (0,1) sources with
the same s have identical first laws and different displayed Q. The shipped
source replay supplies both original graphs and positive shared assignments.

The PATH cost is two exact calls, the union of two original configurations, and
one original controlled site: [2,2,1]. The first call's singleton support has
weight 1; the second call's two-point support has both weights strictly positive.
For a=0, w=1-2s/3 is in (1/3,1); for a other than 0, w=s/3 is in (0,1/3).
Thus the action is legal on EVERY reachable first response, including responses
requiring irrational/algebraic action weights.

The whole reachable second response, using the SAME original assignment from
the first call, is z=w f_a(s)+(1-w)f_b(t). Put
B=(w s+(1-w)t)/3. Coordinate i then equals

    B + [i=a] w(1-s) + [i=b] (1-w)(1-t).

Every target coordinate has a nonempty sum of positive factors. Every other
coordinate equals B. Because {a,b} has at most two elements, at least one
coordinate equals B, so min(z)=B and the returned target is exactly {a,b}.
The independent Fraction checker reconstructs these identities for all nine
images, their normalized complete rows and the actual response-dependent
actions. The elementary strict inequalities prove the leaf guards and action
legality for every REAL point in each joint open square. This is full reachable
response coverage for this fixed policy; testing a finite list of responses is
not the coverage argument.

Accumulated-history admission is explicit. From h the executor recovers its
unique forced target a and s=3 min(h). It derives w from that SAME h, then
recovers the other forced law as g=(z-w h)/(1-w). It requires g=f_b(t) with
0<t<1. It uses the accepted joint surjection representative to construct ONE
admitted original graph and ONE positive edge/gamma assignment, and recompiles
both actual history rows together. Malformed or impossible histories are
UNKNOWN and do not acquire a target by a vacuous homogeneous-fibre argument.

The supplied command-line executor accepts exact rational response vectors and
reconstructs algebraic original edge values where needed. This encoding limit
does not restrict the complete-real policy theorem above; other response
encodings receive UNKNOWN until an adapter is provided. Observed frequencies,
DNA, unsupported clocks, missing registry coverage and all nonempty empirical
admission payloads are rejected. Scientific admission remains a separate gate.

This policy is not claimed optimal. The earlier accepted fixed interior mix
already identifies this restricted target in one call. Genuine response
