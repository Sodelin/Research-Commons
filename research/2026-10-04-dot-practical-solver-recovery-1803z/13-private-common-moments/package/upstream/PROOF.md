## 0. Endpoint and distinction from the existing ingredients

For the registered positive, finite, binary, rooted-LSA, outer-labelled planar, cut-child galled source class, this manuscript constructs a **finite determining family of actual admitted completion tests at every specified finite total-copy cap**. The source's original reticulation level, number of blobs and length of unmarked two-port chains are unrestricted. The construction uses one source and parameter assignment per physical test, retains the root, labelled genealogy histories, shared coins and original control IDs, and projects to the authorized observation before declaring equivalence.

The result is not an arbitrary-field tree-automaton theorem with an assumed biological grammar. The finite grammar is obtained from the actual source's bridge/bigon decomposition. Signed linear combinations occur only in the proof that the physical tests span the legal tests. They are not asserted to be stochastic source realizations.

The exact operational result is for a **declared finite cap and finite explicit control/observation interface**. ALL-CAP.md proves that no taxon-only copy cutoff can cover the unbounded-copy language, separately for independent and common inheritance. It also gives a supplied-common-chain all-cap decision and separates a nonconstructive fixed-shape existence theorem from an executable stopping rule. CLOCK.md treats finite calendar resolution and explains why complete metric laws cannot use the same universal finite-dimensional interface.

## 1. Exact contract

### 1.1 Source

A source is a finite rooted binary partner in the registered semi-directed class. Its root has degree (0,2), ordinary internal vertices (1,2), hybrids (2,1), and labelled leaves (1,0). Parallel arcs are allowed. Every hybrid's child edge is a cut edge. All taxon leaves admit a common outer-face embedding. The root is the lowest stable ancestor of all sampled taxa. There are n >= 4 taxa. Natural population coalescent lengths are positive and finite; the survival coordinate x_e = exp(-t_e) lies in (0,1). Natural inheritance weights lie in (0,1).

At one locus, independent inheritance assigns a fresh parental choice to each **currently surviving lineage** at a hybrid. Common inheritance uses one parental bit for all surviving lineages at that hybrid. Natural choices at different hybrids and fresh loci are independent. Previously merged subtrees do not become independent leaves again. There is one unbounded ancestral population above the root.

The total number m of sampled gene copies, not just the number n of taxa, is bounded in the experiment. Sampling allocations are part of the test. The class-wide theorem is quantified over every finite n and m; it does not impose a fixed reticulation or blob bound on the original sources.

### 1.2 Public type, controls and observations

The public interface specifies leaf/copy labels, graphical ports and orientations, root placement, original actuator IDs and incoming-edge labels, and the allowed experiment rows. A compared component occurs once as a typed box. A supplied finite graphical template, or an already justified two-port-chain type, supplies the admission information for gluing. For a general box, keep the finite joint boundary tensor, not an untyped marginal kernel.

A deterministic original-ID setting fixes a parent for every lineage at that site throughout the locus. A finite randomized program is evaluated with its **joint configuration distribution**; a shared forcing bit is not resampled at different sites or copies. If only the randomized program is authorized, its deterministic summands are internal calculations, not additional available experimental rows. Natural source parameters stay fixed across all rows. Missing or unmatched original IDs are not silently renamed.

The basic final observation is a rooted labelled unranked gene topology or a declared finite coarsening/channel thereof. Only probabilities of the permitted final outcomes count as tests. Hidden routing states, forest coordinates, root histories and hypothetical separately sampled coins are implementation states, not automatically observable probes. Full gene coalescence times and unbounded-copy testing are separate contracts treated in the companion files.

The algorithm may choose fresh exterior source parameters freely within the stated positive source class. It does not assert completeness for an arbitrary additional, unprovided constraint language tying parameters on distinct fresh edges. Fixed/read-only original parameters and finite shared control registers are retained explicitly. No biological availability or cost of an ideal actuator is established here.

### 1.3 What a completion means

A completion is an actual finite source diagram, with one occurrence of the declared box, such that substituting either compared graphical realization produces an admitted source. The graph/type check is performed before a completed diagram is called a test. This includes direction, degree, cut-child, labelled embedding, root/LSA and ID constraints.

Open tensors may retain several ports and a finite shared-coin register. Marginalizing internal variables of the source factorization gives a tensor on the boundary indices. Shared variables are summed only after all their uses have been joined. An arbitrary noncausal stochastic matrix is not accepted as a source-derived box merely because its entries are nonnegative.

## 2. The finite labelled forest algebra

Let F_k be the set of rooted binary unranked forests on k distinct entering tokens. Trees in a forest partition the token labels; a token may itself carry an already constructed genealogy. Write f_k = |F_k|, with f_0 = 1. Selecting the block containing token 1 gives

    f_k = sum_{j=1}^k binom(k-1,j-1) (2j-3)!! f_(k-j).

Thus f_1,...,f_5 = 1,2,7,37,266. Define

    J_m = sum_{k=0}^m f_k,
    A_m = direct_sum_{k=0}^m R^(F_k).

For K,L in A_m, define graft convolution by

    (K * L)_k(w)
      = sum_{u in F_k} K_k(u)
          sum_{v in F_|u|} L_|u|(v) 1{graft(u,v)=w}.

Order the roots of each forest by the least original token label in the root's subtree. This makes token substitution canonical. The unit has mass one on the all-singleton forest at every k.

**Lemma 1.** This is an associative, bilinear, finite-dimensional unital algebra. Actual two-port source kernels compose by this product.

**Proof.** Substituting prior subtrees into token leaves and then substituting the resulting roots is the same as doing the latter substitution inside the intermediate forest first. The least-label ordering agrees after substitution, since the minimum of a union is the minimum of its block minima. Expanding either parenthesization of three kernels therefore sums the same products over the same triples of forests. Bilinearity and the unit are immediate. For actual sources, condition on the complete labelled intermediate forest; the independent driving randomness in the next serial component gives exactly the displayed convolution. QED.

This algebra contains signed vectors for linear reasoning. Only the explicitly constructed positive words will be used as physical witnesses. The larger ambient tuple is convenient: it does not assume that arbitrary tuples satisfy sampling consistency or are source-realizable.

## 3. Genuine positive generators and their finite span

Let E(x) be the ordinary Kingman edge kernel at pair survival x. Put lambda_j = binom(j,2). For 1 <= r <= k,

    P_(k,r)(x)
      = (product_{j=r+1}^k lambda_j)
        sum_{j=r}^k x^lambda_j / product_{l=r,l!=j}^k (lambda_l-lambda_j).

The rates lambda_1,...,lambda_k are distinct. Conditional on the surviving-root count r, the embedded sequence of mergers chooses each current pair uniformly. Consequently every labelled forest coordinate of E(x) is a rational polynomial in x, with degree at most lambda_m. The empty-input coordinate is one.

For a parallel-arm bigon B(x,y,g), common inheritance gives

    B_common,k = g E_k(x) + (1-g) E_k(y).

Independent inheritance instead sums over every assignment S of the k current roots to the first parent:

    g^|S| (1-g)^(k-|S|) E_|S|(x) tensor E_(k-|S|)(y),

relabelled and joined as a forest. This is a **joint** distribution, not the product of separately averaged marginal route kernels. Its multidegree is at most lambda_m in x and y, and m in g. Common inheritance has degree at most one in g.

A legal source chain is not an arbitrary word of naked bigons. It has the form

    E(z) * [B(x_1,y_1,g_1) * E(a_1)] * ... * [B(x_L,y_L,g_L) * E(a_L)],

with every parameter strictly inside (0,1). The leading edge and all connectors are positive. L=0 means one positive ordinary edge, not a zero-duration population. Define the positive cell

    C(x,y,g,a) = B(x,y,g) * E(a).

Each coordinate is a rational polynomial of coordinatewise degrees at most

    (lambda_m, lambda_m, m, lambda_m).

**Lemma 2 (positive interpolation).** The span of all such cells is generated by cells with x,y,a in any fixed lambda_m+1 distinct points of (0,1), and g in any fixed m+1 distinct points of (0,1).

**Proof.** Apply univariate Lagrange interpolation in each variable, coordinate by coordinate. The coefficient attached to each grid point is the same across all forest coordinates. Each grid evaluation is an actual positive cell. The interpolation coefficients may have either sign; this is a span identity, not a stochastic mixing interpretation. QED.

For topology computations, choose survival points j/(lambda_m+2) and inheritance points j/(m+2). Original fixed parameters are not replaced by this grid. The grid concerns fresh freely chosen exterior parameters.

### 3.1 A stopping rule that checks the whole generator family

Start with the unit as a basis word. Append a generator to each current basis word; retain a new word exactly when its vector is linearly independent of the existing span. Keep the actual parameter tuple and word provenance for every retained vector. Continue until every generator times every basis word reduces to the existing span.

**Lemma 3.** This algorithm terminates, and its output spans every finite word of positive source cells. Its basis words contain at most J_m-1 bigons.

**Proof.** The unit is nonzero, and at most J_m independent vectors can enter A_m. Each new word is formed from a previously retained word and one generator, so its length is at most the number of preceding strict dimension increases plus one. At termination the span contains the unit and is invariant under right multiplication by every grid generator. Induction on word length covers every grid word. Lemma 2 then covers arbitrary positive cell parameters, since products distribute over linear combinations. This verifies closure under the entire generator family, not just the points sampled before a plateau. QED.

A finite positive leading-edge grid gives a determining family of actual full chains E(z_j)*W. Every chain in that family is an admitted positive chain, including when W is the algebraic unit.

The implementation can accelerate generator selection by calculating the exact polynomial coefficient span, then retaining positive grid evaluations until that known rank is reached. It verifies that every polynomial coefficient lies in the retained evaluation span. Coefficient vectors are not treated as biological generators.

### 3.2 Comparing inheritance mechanisms without changing the experiment

For a same-syntax comparison across two inheritance mechanisms, use the paired algebra A_m direct_sum A_m and the paired generator

    (C_common(x,y,g,a), C_independent(x,y,g,a))

with the SAME x,y,g,a. Multiplication is componentwise. The identical saturation proof gives a bound 2J_m-1. Independent alphabets for the two components would incorrectly permit different exterior experiments and are not used. For a single mechanism, use the smaller bound J_m-1.

## 4. The source grammar, not an assumed automaton grammar

### 4.1 Root-retained core

In the registered cut-child source, each nonroot p-port blob has one ordinary entry port. Each hybrid consumes a distinct descendant cut port, so it has at most p-1 hybrids. A root blob has at most p. In particular, a nontrivial nonroot two-port blob has exactly one hybrid; the binary degrees force it to be a parallel-arm bigon. Both its external port edges are bridges.

Remove all nonroot two-port blobs while keeping their exact chain kernels, and retain the root-containing blob. In the remaining blob tree, the sum of p-2 over branching vertices is n-2 and there are at most n-2 branching vertices. A branching root gives r <= 2n-3; a two-port root blob contributes at most two hybrids and gives the uniform bound

    r_core <= 2n-2,
    V_core <= 6n-5,
    E_core <= 8n-8.

The binary identities are V=2n+2r-1 and E=2n+3r-2. This is the G1 structural ingredient, not a new claim that its stochastic labels have bounded positive-source realizations.

**Eligibility matters:** an erased chain sits on a bridge of the reduced core. Inserting a cut-child bigon along a nonbridge cycle edge would make its child edge noncut. Such an insertion is illegal and is excluded. The implementation has an explicit negative control for this mistake.

### 4.2 Original marks and supplied boxes

Protect every original actuator hybrid, every fixed original edge ID, every edge of a supplied box's finite graphical template, and every edge incident to its boundary. Let h bound the total number of protected edge/hybrid IDs in the compared expansions. A marked edge can be incident to at most two nonroot two-port bigons, and a marked hybrid belongs to at most one. Retain those incident bigons as well.

At most 2h extra bigons are retained beyond the unmarked core. Hence a safe bound is

    R = 2n-2+2h,
    E = 8n-8+6h.

Marking both compared templates and taking their combined count is a conservative way to obtain a common bound. Protected IDs are not merged into a chain label. The root is never silently moved or replaced by an unlabelled scalar summary.

Every remaining unbounded portion is a serial chain on an eligible unmarked bridge slot. Conversely, inserting any finite positive word into such a slot preserves binary degree, cut-child status, acyclicity, root/LSA and the labelled embedding. The inserted bigon is a local parallel replacement on a bridge, with no new taxon or alternative displayed split.

### 4.3 Effective finite graphical catalogue

There are finitely many rooted directed multigraphs with the bounded numbers of vertices and binary degrees, and finitely many placements of the supplied marks, ports and leaf labels. Enumerate them, retain precisely those whose two substituted expansions satisfy the source/type tests, and declare only their eligible bridge slots variable.

These are finite combinatorial checks. Acyclicity and dominance test root/LSA; removing each hybrid child edge tests the cut condition; finite rotation systems, or a planar embedding with an added apex adjacent to all labelled leaves, test whether all labelled leaves can lie on one face. Parallel arcs are retained as distinct original edges. The catalogue may contain duplicates; deduplication is optional for correctness.

This yields an explicit finite family of core templates followed by serial positive-chain words. No arbitrary graph gluing, hidden-state readout, matrix inverse, generic graphon tester or unconstrained scheduler has been admitted by analogy.

## 5. Faithful compilation of every core and observation

Use a **joint** state whose entries are labelled rooted forests assigned to the current population boundaries, together with every shared original coin/configuration register that crosses the chosen tensor boundary. There are at most m current ancestral roots. The graph and control register are finite, so this state set is finite.

Process the graph in reverse topological order. On an ordinary population edge apply E(x) to the current roots and graft their prior subtrees. At an ordinary branching vertex pool its two child populations deterministically. At a hybrid, either enumerate the independent choices of the current roots or use one shared common bit for the entire entering forest. A fixed intervention overrides that bit for the whole locus. A synchronized program uses its one joint configuration draw, retained through all its uses. At the root, complete the full labelled genealogy in the unbounded ancestral population.

Crucially, maintain the joint distribution of all live populations. Separately averaging different branches and then multiplying would destroy correlations inherited from a hybrid or a control register. The compiler's root-containing two-hybrid fixtures test this issue directly.

For a general supplied box, leave its finite boundary indices unsummed. Internal factor summation gives its joint boundary tensor. Keep a shared coin as one variable with one prior factor; do not replace it by independently averaged copies. Joining a box with an exterior and summing their common boundary indices is linear in the box tensor.

**Lemma 4 (fidelity and multilinearity).** For every enumerated legal core, the compiler returns its actual authorized response probabilities. Each response is multilinear in the distinct variable bridge-chain kernels and linear in a singly occurring box tensor. In fresh ordinary source parameters its degrees are at most lambda_m per edge survival and m per hybrid inheritance weight.

**Proof.** Expand the joint finite-state computation into its possible routing/forest transitions. Each term is the probability of that whole history, with each shared draw weighted once, each physical edge transition used once on the entire entering forest, and prior subtrees grafted. All histories compatible with the final genealogy are summed. This is exactly conditional probability in the source process. A fixed chain slot contributes one kernel coordinate per history, including its empty-input coordinate when no lineage visits it. Thus different slots are multilinear variables, even though the full genealogy law is generally not a product of marginal laws. The edge and routing polynomial bounds follow from Section 3. An original variable that is shared across response rows is one variable, not independently chosen parameters per row. QED.

Now apply the declared observation map and the declared whole randomized program. **Projection precedes observable-span formation.** Equality of a stronger, unobserved forest law is sufficient for many purposes but is not substituted for necessary-and-sufficient equivalence under a weaker authorized observation.

## 6. Main finite-cap theorem

**Theorem 5 (source-complete finite determining tests).** Fix the public type, finite total-copy cap m, finite explicit legal menu and finite topology observation map above. For two supplied source-derived boxes, there is an effectively constructible finite family T of actual positive admitted completions such that

    every authorized response agrees in every admitted finite completion
    if and only if
    every response agrees on T.

The original sources and exteriors may have arbitrary finite reticulation levels, blob counts and chain lengths. One can choose the determining completions with at most

    R + E(d-1) reticulations,

where R=2n-2+2h, E=8n-8+6h, and d=J_m for one inheritance mechanism or d=2J_m for a paired comparison. This is a deliberately conservative witness bound, not an optimal query or actuator-cost result.

With rational/algebraic source coefficients and exact field equality, the procedure terminates and returns either an equality certificate or an actual legal separating completion. For arbitrary real coefficients it remains an exact mathematical determining-family statement; ordinary numerical access is not an exact equality oracle.

**Proof.** Take an arbitrary legal completion. Protect the declared IDs and supplied box templates, retain its root, and reduce its exterior to one of the finite core templates in Section 4. The exact labelled kernels on the remaining eligible bridge slots retain its full unranked genealogical effect.

By Lemmas 2 and 3, expand each arbitrary positive bridge chain as a finite signed linear combination of positive leading-edge/basis-word chains. Lemma 4 permits this expansion independently in each physical slot, without factorizing correlated lineage marginals. Expand fresh retained-core parameters by positive polynomial interpolation as well. The result expresses each final authorized response functional of the arbitrary completion as a linear combination of response functionals of the bounded, positive, legal completed graphs in T. Each summand is one source with one parameter assignment and the same original-ID menu; no source mixture is asserted to realize the original box or chain.

The finite catalogue and grid include every sampling allocation and authorized final outcome in the given finite experiment contract. Therefore agreement on T implies agreement on every legal completion. The reverse implication is immediate because T consists of legal completions. Each of at most E slots uses at most d-1 additional bigons, which proves the bound.

Every loop in the construction is finite except the algebraic saturation, whose termination and closure certificate are proved in Lemma 3. Equality of the finitely many resulting exact coefficients is decidable over the stated field. If a difference is found, its stored graph, word, parameter tuple, row and observed outcome form a physical test witness. No current incomplete prefix is treated as a stopping certificate. QED.

### 6.1 Exact observable quotient

Let U be the finite joint tensor space of the box and let W be the span of the authorized test functionals produced above. For same-type boxes K,L,

    K equivalent_to L  iff  K-L belongs to W^perp.

One may reduce T to a basis of W while retaining actual test provenance. Do this with exact coefficients at the specified parameter values, or retain the complete determining menu. A generic symbolic rank calculation with denominators does not authorize discarding exceptional positive parameter values. The unreduced finite menu is valid on those degeneracies as well.

This is a computed source-admitted observable span, not an assumed access to arbitrary forest coordinates. It does not solve the positive source-image question for an arbitrary tensor and does not imply that the displayed target is constant on a given passive observational fiber.

### 6.2 Adaptation and repeated experiments

If the legal protocol chooses the next complete row before each fresh independent locus, equality of all row response laws is equivalent to equality of every finite adaptive transcript law. Induct on the transcript length: the same past has the same probability and the same policy kernel, and each chosen row has the same next-outcome law. Randomized policies are handled by conditioning on their randomization. The converse is a one-row policy.

The same source parameters are retained throughout; no new parameter fit is chosen per row or per locus. This argument does not cover undeclared within-locus feedback, persistent cross-locus biological latent states, or a policy that can secretly observe a source's hidden routing history. It is not a noisy finite-data certification theorem.

## 7. Scope ledger

Established here, at submitted hand-proof status:

- Complete positive graphical completion coverage at every declared finite copy cap, not a fixed-level special case.
- A terminating, source-generated algebra saturation with actual positive witness words.
- Original-ID, shared-coin, root-history, joint-population and observation-projection safeguards.
- An explicit finite witness-size bound and exact-field equality procedure for the stated finite experiment contract.

Not established by this theorem:

- A positive bounded-source realization of an arbitrary kernel (G3).
- Passive target identification or every Q/S fiber (G5).
- A finite noisy confidence guarantee or an optimal physical intervention cost (G6/G7).
- A taxon-only all-copy cutoff, which ALL-CAP.md disproves for both mechanisms.
- A general effective all-copy stopping algorithm for arbitrary supplied independent-inheritance sources. The nonconstructive fixed-shape result in ALL-CAP.md does not supply that algorithm.
- A finite universal scalar-test basis for complete calendar genealogy laws, which CLOCK.md obstructs.

Thus this manuscript supplies the missing **finite-cap legal-grammar and stopping bridge**. It must not be relabelled as every stronger uncapped algorithmic endpoint without an explicit scope decision and independent review.
