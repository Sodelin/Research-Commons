## Recursive meaning and source reuse

All parameters stay strictly inside the unit interval. The exported actual graph/mode models and complete original registry are preserved; model variables are not independently refitted at each response.

The zero-call predicate is the absence of a jointly history-consistent pair with different targets. A recursive branch is an existential legal action followed by a universal exact response, then the recursively quantified continuation. Thus later actions may depend on earlier responses; the current action cannot depend on its own unseen response. Unreachable responses are harmless because their consistent source set is empty and hence homogeneous. The external entry point separately checks that a supplied initial history has an actual source before assigning an identification verdict.

The last-call reduction is equivalent to eliminating the universal response through pairwise response collision. Legal-action constraints inside a quantified source collision are equivalent under the outer legal-action guard. Source-only QE leaves action/history variables free. If a legal action excludes a pair without any history, adding history cannot recreate it. Incomplete QE is retained as a residual formula or UNKNOWN, never replaced by False.

Support weights are strictly positive on the specified support, zero elsewhere, and normalized by the final weight; singleton supports are exact constants. Configuration and original-site costs are cumulative unions along each path. Calls decrease by one; homogeneous histories may stop. Reusing a completed lower-call win preserves the same row/site caps.
