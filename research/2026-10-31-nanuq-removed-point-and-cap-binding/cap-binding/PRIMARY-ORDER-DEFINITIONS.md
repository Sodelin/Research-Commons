# Primary-source definition check (31 October 2026)

## Holtgrefe et al., Bulletin of Mathematical Biology (2025)

[Published source](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4), Definition2.3(v): “the network has a planar embedding with the leaves on the unbounded face”. The paragraph immediately before Proposition2.9 defines an induced circular order by an outer-labelled planar representation inducing that order on leaves. Circular orders are taken modulo reversal and cyclic permutation (before Definition2.6). No disk or Jordan support-curve datum is included in these definitions. Proposition2.9 relates induced orders to displayed splits; it is not a definition permitting arbitrary C.

## Rhodes, Baños, Xu, Ané, arXiv2402.11693v2 (21 July2024)

[Primary preprint, §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.11693v2#S4), Definitions7–8: the frontier is formed by edges/nodes on the “topological boundary of the unbounded face”; outer-labelled means the designated vertices lie there. Theorem4.1 proves biconnected-blob frontier orders agree across such embeddings; its proof uses that each frontier is a cycle. Definition9's more general network congruence is through an outer-labelled displaying supernetwork with a full-taxon blob. That is not the manuscript's fixed original drawing. These definitions do not posit a Jordan curve through all taxon leaves bounding the entire drawing.

## Consequence for our formal interface

The manuscript TheoremA fixes an actual outer-labelled embedding and its induced C. Its A.4 proof uses the outer-face boundary traversal and bridge excursions. A Jordan support-curve encoding remains a possible equivalent representation requiring an explicit equivalence theorem; it is NOT literally an already-present primary premise. The existing TaxonOuterFace matches the face-incidence portion but does not formalize traversal/order. No disk-input scope substitution is made. The full raw-network order gate remains open.
