## Explicit same-drawing cap construction to formalize next

With original bridge parametrization from source to target: for every outgoing port, use the original inner/source endpoint and choose the new tip at t=1/2, retaining the prefix [0,1/2]. For the unique incoming port of a nonroot blob, place the fresh root at t=2/3 and the port tip at t=1/3. The root-to-port arc traverses [1/3,2/3] backwards; the root-to-entry arc traverses [2/3,1] forwards. All original internal arcs remain identical. These new vertices/arcs are precisely the existing RootedCapVertex/RootedCapArc tags. The two incoming-port cap arcs meet only at their shared fresh root; all other bridge interiors are disjoint by the original curve incidence axioms. This determines a PlanarCurves candidate and a drawing-subset proof without arbitrary redraw or new physical edge IDs. It is NOT yet implemented or compiled and does not prove port outside/order.

After such a cap drawing is built, opening each incoming hybrid-parent arc at t=1/2 retains exactly its prefix. Any chosen t in (1/2,1), e.g.3/4, is then absent from that opened drawing by original arc injectivity, edge-interior disjointness and vertex-incidence. Such a point is a usable removed point for RemovedPointMerge only after BOTH actual old-face closure incidences are supplied; the absence proof does not supply those incidences.
