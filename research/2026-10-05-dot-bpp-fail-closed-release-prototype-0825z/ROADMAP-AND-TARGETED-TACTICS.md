## Recommendation now

Ship the fail-closed decision contract first. The current data-to-engine-to-authenticated-diagnostics path works for its admitted research fixtures. A trustworthy application must also decline to recommend histories when its numerical gates remain unresolved. The new release screen does exactly that for the frozen records; it is not a general-purpose sequence admission engine or a completed inference application.

## What the evidence supports about the next tactic

1. **Do not equate high theta ESS with joint mixing.** In the matched pair, extant-theta/root means agree while likelihood means differ. That narrows the diagnostic question but does not identify a phase algorithm error, genealogy trapping or implementation defect. Locus height/length summaries also do not characterize the full genealogy distribution. The verified phase-expanded pattern inventory is workload metadata, not a cause test.
2. **Read existing genealogy evidence before rerunning.** A prospective read-only analysis could compare genealogy summaries invariant under within-individual allele-label swaps, while preserving population and individual identities, and time-block occupancy between chains. Any parser must authenticate all rows and account for equivalent allele-label swaps within unphased diploid individuals; naive differences between labelled trees can count symmetry rather than substantive modes. Coarser population-level summaries may also help, but would be intentionally lossy rather than proven equivalent-state quotients. Such summaries would localize a question, not establish the cause of likelihood differences. No new topology-summary implementation or result is claimed here.
3. **The obvious tuning controls are already active.** Both matched runs underwent four auto-tuning rounds. Their final burn-in Gspr acceptance was 0.25733/0.25780, with the same 0.00014 step size; Gage acceptance was 0.65705/0.65416, with step size 99. A simple explanation that SPR acceptance was essentially zero is unsupported. Acceptance is not an exploration certificate. These facts come from the independently reproduced COMPLETED-PAIR.json.
4. **Potential changes require a discriminating comparison.** The official manual documents keyed Gage/Gspr/mix step sizes and automatic versus fixed tuning. Changing proposal scale can preserve the target but has no established benefit here. The earlier mg_gamma versus mg_invg possibility likewise remains a proposal, not a demonstrated remedy. The manual recommends independent runs and inspecting traces; its troubleshooting typo about finetune=0 does not override the option reference/source showing auto-tuning at1. [Official manual](https://bpp.github.io/bpp-manual/bpp-4-manual/).
5. **Do not rediscover an existing algorithm.** The VDRoP paper describes conditional-parameter proposals and gene-tree SPR/coalescent-time updates already implemented in BPP. It explains why sampling genealogies is a substantial computational task. Those advances do not guarantee exploration on this particular target. Changing gamma to inverse-gamma merely to analytically integrate theta changes the prior; it is not a same-target numerical repair. [VDRoP primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71057-z).
6. **No hidden initialization or experimental-feature shortcut.** The prior pinned-source audit found ordinary no-date tau initialization overwrites Newick ages. No supported arbitrary root/theta start control was established. In the pinned source, method.c explicitly labels rev_gspr and randomized gene-node order experimental; they are not endorsed as a mature drop-in remedy. No checkpoint was recorded in the completed attempts, so their rounded scalar/Newick logs cannot be presented as exact resumable state. Future documented checkpoints could improve resource recovery, not generate independent chains by themselves. [Pinned method source](https://github.com/bpp/bpp/blob/da8caf3aa00cf275cc9a044e0d806e9bbb0e1460/src/method.c), [pinned genealogy source](https://github.com/bpp/bpp/blob/da8caf3aa00cf275cc9a044e0d806e9bbb0e1460/src/gtree.c).

## Mature alternatives: exact-target matching is the gate

- **StarBEAST3** is an existing MSC implementation with efficient genealogy inference. Its tutorial's ploidy setting scales population size; that alone does not establish equivalence to BPP's integration over unphased, paired diploid genotypes across linked sites. A candidate cross-engine comparison must explicitly match that observation likelihood, population-size/time units, JC69/strict-clock restrictions, fixed versus inferred topology, and gamma-prior/joint species-time density. This audit does not establish such an exact match. [Official BEAST announcement](https://www.beast2.org/2022/03/31/starbeast3.html), [official tutorial repository](https://github.com/taming-the-beast/StarBeast3-Tutorial).
- **RevBayes** provides coalescent distributions and coordinated species/gene-tree moves, so it is a credible configuration-audit candidate, not an already validated replacement. Even its basic coalescent documentation distinguishes its population-size parameter from BPP's mutation-scaled theta. The same genotype/phase, prior and units checklist must pass before calling it an independent replication. [Coalescent parameter documentation](https://revbayes.github.io/documentation/dnCoalescent.html), [coordinated move documentation](https://revbayes.github.io/documentation/mvSpeciesSubtreeScale.html).
- **SNAPP** targets unlinked biallelic markers. Replacing these linked multi-site loci with SNPs or inferred gene trees changes the statistical target. Gene-tree summary tools, network pseudolikelihood methods and the separate exact-law solver likewise cannot be silently substituted for the current direct-sequence posterior. [SNAPP primary method](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.4193).

No alternative is installed, run or claimed to validate this dataset by this assessment. No third-party contact is initiated.

## Concrete application milestones and stopping rules

1. **Admission:** implement a general schema for alignment/map identities, positive dimensions, pairing/phase symbols, locus independence/orthology assumptions, licensing, missingness and requested biological equivalence class. The current actual adapters validate only pinned fixtures. Unknown profiles currently return NOT_ADMITTED. Raubeson remains unadmitted.
2. **Execution:** retain immutable attempts, exact model/control/engine/dependency hashes, bounded process cleanup, resource receipts and separated timeout/recovery history. This part is demonstrated for the current fixtures. Queue semantics and arbitrary new inputs are not yet implemented.
3. **Numerical assessment:** integrate an established rank-normalized R-hat/bulk-tail ESS implementation under a reviewed dependency pin, plus topology/clade-indicator precision and time-block/replicate checks. At least four genuinely independent supported starts are a prospective screen; the two completed matched chains are insufficient. R-hat<1.01 and ESS>=100 times the chain count are screening thresholds, not convergence proofs. Worst-case effective indicator sample size2500 gives MCSE0.01; multimodality/nonstationarity can invalidate that precision calculation. No numerical release can be earned by favorable burn trimming or a single selected parameter's ESS. These previously reviewed prospective thresholds and their primary sources are preserved in the [release-gate note](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/24d1819231cdac3a363fc87ed64dea3d9e179fc5/research/2026-10-05-dot-bpp-instrumented-mixing-diagnostic-0608z/RELEASE-GATES.md).
4. **Validation:** predeclare repeated simulation, truth distribution, recovery/coverage/loss targets and binomial Monte Carlo uncertainty before choosing a simulation budget. A fixed-truth single realization and selected interval inclusion do not calibrate the method. Keep empirical posterior-predictive and model/prior sensitivity checks separate.
5. **Reporting:** only after the applicable gates pass should a future reviewed release path expose ranked histories with model-conditioned uncertainty, identifiability qualifications and Monte Carlo precision. The current implementation has no approved success/ranking path. It emits an honest refusal with the evidence needed to understand it.

The immediate deliverable is therefore an auditable integration with a working refusal boundary and an explicit remaining roadmap. It is progress toward the full application, not a claim that the underlying inferential problem is solved.
