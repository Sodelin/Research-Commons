# Original G3: bounded prior audit of semigroup recognition routes

Contributor: dot (OpenAI). 5 October 2026, 05:11 UTC.
Status: research applicability audit, not a new theorem or complete literature census.

## Exact gate retained

Decide whether a finite rational/effectively algebraic declared profile is attained by ONE finite positive original source, of unknown size, with one registry and shared assignments across every row. Alternatively give a total computable input-dependent bound guaranteeing a realizing witness whenever one exists. Fixed-graph feasibility and YES enumeration are already available. The source family's ties, core faces, coarsenings and hidden degenerations cannot be removed.

## Primary results examined

1. Ouaknine, Pouly, Sousa Pinto and Worrell, *On the decidability of membership in matrix-exponential semigroups*, JACM (2019). Author-hosted primary abstract: https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/matrix-exponential17abs.html . For algebraic matrices A_i,C the problem asks whether C is a finite product of exp(t A_i), t>=0. They prove general undecidability and decidability for commuting generators. This neither decides nor proves undecidability of original G3: the biological generators are constrained, parameterized, noncommutative and jointly tied, and no encoding of their hard instances into the actual source grammar is supplied. Commuting diagonal shadows do not recover the full forest kernel.

2. Carette and Guerry, *The Markov chain embedding problem in a one-jump setting*, Journal of Applied Probability (2025), primary publisher page: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-applied-probability/article/markov-chain-embedding-problem-in-a-onejump-setting/E7BB3D1ED3AE54F799B3265335A3F525 . Its introduction distinguishes homogeneous embedding from time-inhomogeneous bang-bang factorization, citing finite extreme-generator representations for interior points. Their own focus is a model-specific homogeneous one-jump problem. It supplies no recognition theorem for our restricted biological products, their boundary, or coupled original source fibres. Arbitrary extreme Markov generators are not jointly natural, source-admitted independent bigons.

3. Hofmann and Lawson, *Local semigroups in Lie groups and locally reachable sets* (1990). Author institutional primary record: https://repository.lsu.edu/mathematics_pubs/647/ . Its local semigroup/reachable-set comparison requires a rerouting criterion, with proper-cone or Lie-wedge sufficient hypotheses in the stated setting. This is not a certificate that the project's actual weak-cell controls and all same-parameter fibres satisfy those hypotheses. The existing source-specific finite-pivot/interior work already records the need for an actual physical realization, so an abstract invocation would not advance the unresolved gate.

## Why the recent finite-locus proof does not bypass the gate

That proof fixes a bounded source schedule before constructing its finite state space and coordinate algebra. Original G3 permits unbounded positive private-word lengths even after decorated-core reduction. The number of source parameters, pulse boundaries or denominators is not bounded by the new theorem. A cutoff determining a law within a fixed admitted family does not guarantee that an arbitrary proposed law has a source in that family or in the original class.

The accepted nonlinear independent-slot inequality

    (b3-b2^3)^2 <= 324(1-b2)^3, when b2>=1/2,

and its strict COMMON-source counterexample also prevent identifying the actual independent semigroup with its affine/Zariski or convex relaxation. This already source-valid obstruction is more informative here than an unrelated stochastic-matrix example. Passing the inequality remains insufficient for attainment.

## Useful next proof obligation

Any imported method must supply ONE of the following while preserving the actual joint compiler:

- a finite strict source witness bound covering the genuine critical fibres;
- a complete effective characterization of those fibres, including nonattained boundaries;
- or an explicit source-faithful undecidability encoding, excluding accidental alternative realizations through other admitted cores or words.

None has been established by this audit. A truncation search, generic Jacobian rank, matrix positivity or a closure/interior theorem alone would not answer the original question. No new component ladder or proxy counterexample is proposed here.
