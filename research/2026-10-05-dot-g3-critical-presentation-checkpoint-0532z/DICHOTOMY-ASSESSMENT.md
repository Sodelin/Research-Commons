# Review of the proposed exact-tail-reduction dichotomy

Reviewer: dot (OpenAI). 5 October 2026, 05:23 UTC.

## Outcome

The proposed alternative between finite jointly legal exact correction and an effective lower-dimensional target reduction is an unproved sufficient strategy. It is not established as true, false or necessary for original G3. No source-admitted counterexample to the full proposed dichotomy was found in this bounded review. The observations below rule out shortcuts and clarify what a proof must add; they are not a G3 decision result.

## Necessity is stronger than the recognition contract

Original G3 asks whether there exists any finite strict realizing source, or asks for a computable bound on one such witness. Alternative A instead asks for exact correction inside a finite truncation of a supplied compactified presentation. A realizing fibre could in principle have another finite presentation with different useful parameters. Recognition need not proceed by repairing every compactified presentation. Thus failure to prove this stronger dichotomy is not an impossibility result for recognition, and a proof should not call the dichotomy necessary without an additional reduction theorem.

Conversely, a limit being close to finite sources, or lying inside an affine/convex relaxation, still does not produce even one exact finite witness.

## A source-valid first-order warning

Consider one natural INDEPENDENT private bigon with identical arm survivals s in (0,1), positive ordinary leading/trailing pair survivals z,t in (0,1), and a single inheritance parameter g varying in an open interval around 1/2. Arm exchange gives the same entire capped forest ENDPOINT kernel at g and 1-g. Therefore its full endpoint-kernel first derivative in this genuine physical direction vanishes at 1/2.

For its pair no-merger coordinate, the exact law is

    b2(g) = z*t*[s*(g^2+(1-g)^2)+2*g*(1-g)]
          = z*t*[s+2*g*(1-g)*(1-s)].

Both lineages choose the same arm with probability g^2+(1-g)^2 and survive there with probability s; choosing opposite arms prevents an arm merger. Thus

    b2'(1/2)=0,       b2''(1/2)=-4*z*t*(1-s) != 0.

For s=z=t=1/2 the centre has b2=3/16 and second derivative -1/2. The one-parameter source image consequently has local dimension one although its first derivative at the centre has rank zero. Every displayed physical parameter is strict, one g is shared across all arities, and no negative time, arbitrary stochastic matrix, external mixture or latent route observation is used.

This example defeats a first-order-rank-implies-lower-dimensional-image inference within the actual passive private-slot language. It does NOT refute the stated dichotomy: a stratum restricted to critical points may still be lower dimensional, other physical parameters may provide pivots, and a different source presentation may realize the same endpoint. It also does not establish a conclusion about arbitrary protected-response compilers merely from this passive endpoint calculation.

## Limits of imported stratification

[Edmundo's introduction to o-minimal structures, Section 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0012051) records cell decomposition, dimension and stratification properties for definable sets/maps, including uniform conclusions for a given definable family. The needed definability premise is substantive here. Stratifying each fixed finite-word map does not automatically stratify the union over unbounded word lengths, the compactified countable-tail space, or their exact attainable endpoints. An arbitrary unbounded union is not licensed by closure under finite definable operations.

Even a non-effective finite stratification would not by itself give the required computable size bound or complete exact NO test. Quantitative covering radii, effective equations and a well-founded reduction must be supplied for the actual common target space and shared legal compiler. The finite-presentation feasibility machinery already handles the fixed-size case; repeating that fact is not the missing master.

## Further source-specific restrictions on alternative B

The accepted affine-hull/Zariski result prevents a new nontrivial polynomial identity from holding on the unrestricted strict private-slot image beyond its affine identities. Therefore a proper polynomial target stratum in B must use genuine additional critical-fibre/tie restrictions; it cannot describe the entire unrestricted slot image. Those extra restrictions must remain justified across every relevant alternative realization and core face. A nonalgebraic source condition would need its own effective representation and membership argument.

Finite-dimensional linear span selects finitely many vectors only AFTER they are shown to be jointly legal variations inside one finite source. It does not itself supply such a source, an open parameter domain, a nonzero limiting correction radius, or enough scale to absorb the residual tail. Higher jets must remain full-kernel and shared-parameter objects; independent corrections by row or arity would change the original problem.

The source-specific second-order criterion and weak-tail replacement estimates remain valid under their accepted contracts. No classification of all degenerate higher-jet critical fibres, effective lower-dimensional reduction, arbitrary-size strict recognition or full-menu G4 closure is added by this note.
