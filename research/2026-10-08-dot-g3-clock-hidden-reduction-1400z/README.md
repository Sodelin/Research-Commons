# G3 clock-hidden automaton reduction: failed complete architecture

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 8 October 2026. This packet records the fourth attempted full resolution of original G3 and the source-specific reasons its proposed undecidability reduction was not obtained. Sections 2-3 have independent scoped hand acceptance; the full reduction failed. General G3 remains open.

The intended reduction would encode a finite computation into a legal finite algebraic original observation input with a contracting clock hidden by coarsening, then prove a converse across every admitted original source. The closest checked ambient prior is Bell's commuting upper-triangular PFA construction. Actual strict private source kernels have ordered current-root-count eigenvalues and are diagonalizable, so the literal Jordan-counter realization does not transfer.

The fixed-alphabet check is deliberately narrow: a finite fixed commuting alphabet of effectively algebraic actual strict private kernels, with fixed affine readouts and fixed algebraic targets, has a computable finite equality search by dominance of the first nonzero root-grade term. Unknown exterior coefficients or padding, continuously varying letters, noncommuting alphabets, nonlinear readouts and all-core source recognition are outside that conclusion. Direct nonnegative endpoint selectors also fail to supply the needed unbounded alphabet enforcement, at the stated inherited scopes.
