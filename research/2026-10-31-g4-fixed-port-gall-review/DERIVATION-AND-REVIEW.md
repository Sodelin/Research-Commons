## 1. Exact class and quantifiers

Let T be a strict finite admitted original source, with n≥4 original taxa, binary rooted-LSA structure, outer-labelled embedding, hybrid cut-child condition, positive finite population lengths and interior natural coins. Use natural INDEPENDENT inheritance at CURRENT lineages, retaining previously merged subtrees as opaque units. Observations are the full rooted labelled topology laws at the stated allocations.

The root rho has two cut-child sides. One contains a chosen taxon C. The other leads through an eligible bridge slot S_up to a simple nonroot one-hybrid gall with top v and one hybrid H. **S_up is an admitted finite positive serial word**, not an arbitrary unknown ancestral region. Above rho is the ordinary unbounded completion.

The gall's directed arms are

    v=p0→p1→...→pr→H,       v=q0→q1→...→qs→H,

with ordinary edge survivals l0,...,lr and d0,...,ds, respectively. The coin g is the probability of choosing H's left parent. Every survival and g lies in (0,1). Let r,s≥0, r+s≥1, and k=r+s+2 be the number of cycle vertices. Thus this theorem excludes the side-port-free two-cycle case.

H's child cut edge leads to a descendant component X_A containing a chosen taxon A. Every intermediate cycle vertex u has one off-cycle child cut edge leading to a nonempty descendant component X_u, with one chosen original taxon B_u. These components are disjoint and have only their stated upward cut port. They may have arbitrary finite admitted internal complexity and arbitrary allowed positive bridge words. The C-side component is arbitrary within the same private single-cut source conditions. Cycle edges themselves are ordinary, not serial-word slots.

A rival N has the SAME ordered main cycle, arm designations, original core IDs, and named partition of taxa into X_A, the X_u and the C side. Internal descendant sources, S_up and all parameters may differ. T and N have **separate** arbitrary physical tuples; each tuple is fixed across all rows of that source. No rival internal-size or word-length bound is assumed. No stochastic register is shared across the cut components.

## 2. Finite physical panel and conclusion

For each of the k−2 side vertices u, use one physical row R_u with THREE copies of B_u and one copy of EVERY other original taxon. Also use one row R_A with TWO copies of A and one copy of every other original taxon.

There are exactly k−1 physical rows, with at most n+2 total sampled copies (n+1 in R_A). All original taxa are allocated positively. Pruning to a selected subset below is an exact finite sum of the permitted full labelled outcome probabilities, not a zero-copy physical query or a hidden forest observation.

**Theorem.** If N and T match these full natural-I laws, then they have the same g and all k cycle-edge survivals. They also have the same two-root no-merger coordinate of the actual A cut-exit kernel, the same cap3 S_up kernel, and the same cap3 selected-taxon cut-exit kernel at every side port.

This does not determine component interiors, slot words, nonselected-taxon kernels, higher capped kernels or the C-side source.

## 3. Actual finite cut-exit kernels, with their tails retained

The source-interface construction follows the original [joint finite-state compiler, §5](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/5e01a8e727478a2f22686d31f71304126e345116/research/2026-10-01-g4-admitted-testers-0819z/PROOF.md), Git blob `b41fdf706e4dfcb5d14ffdbc88631012674ef1f4`.

In a descendant component with one upward cut, retain m labelled copies of its chosen taxon and prune all other material in the JOINT source calculation. Run the actual component computation through the cut edge, leaving the boundary forest unsummed before it joins the core. Call this kernel K. At a side vertex stop before pooling with the arm roots. At H stop **before H's parental routing**, so the main coin g stays outside K_A. The child/port edge's finite ordinary tail and any word on it are INCLUDED; the ordinary cycle edges are not double counted.

This is an actual source-tensor contraction, not an abstract stochastic substitute or a claim of serial normal form:

* Each finite edge and routing operation is stochastic, and pooling is deterministic. No event is conditioned on; the resulting exit law is nonnegative and normalized.
* Copy labels are passive, giving exchangeability. Ordinary Kingman restriction and fresh independent choices of current retained lineages give projectivity before the boundary. A merge with subsequently deleted material does not change the retained block's population or future fresh routing distribution. Branch marginals are not independently averaged in place of this joint restriction.
* Previously formed subtrees remain opaque under routing and merger. Zero or one retained root is unchanged as a topology.
* For each fixed m, choose one finite ancestral route from the chosen leaf to the cut, require all roots to follow it and prohibit mergers. The finitely many relevant coin and no-merger factors are strictly positive, so K's all-separate probability is positive. This is not a uniform lower bound over arbitrary component size.

The different descendant components have disjoint populations and private natural randomness. Given the fixed physical tuple their exit kernels can therefore be combined independently at the core. This step would not be valid for a cross-cut shared random register or a second upward port; both are explicitly excluded.

Consequently K_A and every K_Bu satisfy the exact low-cap hypotheses needed below. A theorem about an ALL-COMPLETED single-taxon law that absorbs the final ordinary tail cannot replace this finite-boundary construction.

## 5. Exact pruning maps

Define the positive prefix and total products

    P_i=product_(a=0..i-1) l_a,     L=product_(a=0..r) l_a,
    Q_j=product_(a=0..j-1) d_a,     R=product_(a=0..s) d_a.

Retain only A, B at the left side vertex p_i, and C. Every other side component receives no retained roots. The intervening cycle vertices have no remaining input or routing; their consecutive ordinary kernels combine by E(a)E(b)=E(ab). The retained source law is exactly

    W3(g, P_i, L/P_i, R; K_A,K_Bpi,K_up).

For a right side vertex q_j, the corresponding law is

    W3(1-g, Q_j, R/Q_j, L; K_A,K_Bqj,K_up).

The reflection is necessary: the W3 coin always points toward the retained side taxon. At the main hybrid every current lineage leaving K_A routes once, including a lineage carrying a merged A pair. No ordinary factor is commuted through a hybrid word; only consecutive original ordinary cycle edges are combined.

All products and suffixes lie in (0,1). The same contractions apply to N with its own tuple, and with the same selected port labels by hypothesis. These W3 laws are mathematical marginals, not a claim that the original source physically has a three-cycle.

## 6. Recover totals once, then all edges by strict ratios

For each u, R_u supplies the five single-A laws. The W3 theorem recovers g or 1−g according to the fixed arm designation, the associated prefix P_i or Q_j, K_Bu through cap3 and the common K_up through cap3. Repeated recoveries of g and K_up are consistency conditions on the SAME source, not independently refitted parameters.

Since r+s≥1, choose one existing side port u*. The R_A marginal on A²/B_u*/C, together with its already recovered prefix and K_up, gives d_A and both total arm products: at a left representative it gives L/P_i and R, hence L; at a right one it gives R/Q_j and L, hence R.

If r≥1, the left edges follow from

    l0=P_1,
    l_i=P_(i+1)/P_i       (1≤i<r),
    l_r=L/P_r.

If r=0, its sole edge is l0=L. The right formulas are identical with Q,R,d and s; if s=0 its sole edge is d0=R. Thus no prefix at a nonexistent side vertex is used.

The W3 divisors are positive on every strict finite target because d_A, each side d_B,n_B and U,n_U are positive finite no-merger probabilities. All prefix divisions above are also positive. There is no generic-rank exception at g=1/2 or when the two arm totals agree. These pointwise facts imply neither uniform conditioning at source boundaries nor a word-count bound.

There are k ordinary cycle survivals and one coin, so k+1 intrinsic core parameters are recovered, together with the stated capped kernels. This proves the theorem.
