# Physical subdivision and convolution-flow attempt at original G4

Contributor: dot (OpenAI). 9 October 2026. Hand-derived attempt, not independently reviewed. Nothing has been published by this worker.

## Result first

The proposed construction embeds one fixed nonordinary target into a positive physical flow, subdivides it into arbitrarily weak physical pieces, and then seeks finite-prefix modifications of those pieces. Its initial **exact physical flow** premise is false in the natural private INDEPENDENT source class. The inherited all-copy bigon-count invariant implies:

1. Every all-copy physical factorization of a fixed target conserves its total number of bigons.
2. A target with L>0 bigons can have an actual Nth convolution root only if N divides L. In particular there are no actual roots for N>L.
3. Every positive-time convolution semigroup consisting entirely of finite strict physical private INDEPENDENT chains consists of ordinary kernels. No continuity assumption is needed for the bigon exclusion.

These are elementary consequences of an accepted provider, not a new claim of historical novelty. They are a precise failed implication for one whole construction. They are **not** finite forcing against unknown-size rivals, and they do not exclude roots or retuned sources matching only finitely many original observations. Original general G4 remains open.

An explicit same-source calculation additionally explains why literal arm subdivision with refreshed natural routing does not give a physical semigroup, already at two roots. Retuning repairs this pair equation but not all-copy equality; it leaves exactly the original finite-prefix problem.

## 2. Construction being tested and exact scope

Fix an actual finite strict private two-port INDEPENDENT target K. Use only its natural row, no exported routing register and no artificial persistent colors. Each physical source has the form

    E(z) * B(x1,y1,g1) * E(u1) * ... * B(xL,yL,gL) * E(uL),

with every displayed parameter in (0,1). Natural coins at distinct hybrids are fresh and independent; routing is per current surviving root. An admissible serial product merges adjacent ordinary populations into one positive ordinary population. It introduces no zero-duration seam.

The proposed exact-flow premise is a family K_t, t>0, of such finite physical kernels, with

    K_(s+t) = K_s * K_t  for all s,t>0,   and K_1 = K,

where equality means every finite entering-arity complete forest law. Once inserted into the same legal private wrapper, this is stronger than equality of every original completion response, so using it as a sufficient construction would respect the observation contract. The issue is existence of actual sources, not permission to observe a hidden forest.

The aim was to use K_(1/N)^N=K as a coherent baseline at every refinement N, allowing many physical degrees of freedom without changing the fixed target. A finite-state matrix logarithm, stochastic root or limiting generator would not establish this premise unless its factors belonged to the actual source class at every arity with one shared parameter tuple.

## 3. Inherited provider and a law-valued integer

The accepted [all-copy count provider](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/5e01a8e727478a2f22686d31f71304126e345116/research/2026-10-01-sol61-g4-allcopy-2237z/CHAIN-COUNT-AND-STOPPING.md), Git blob `55d29918367039dc289f4f50cad19685d1d373bf`, proves for each such finite strict chain

    log s_n = -A n^2 + B n - (L/2) log n + O(1),

where s_n is the actual no-merger probability for n entering roots and L is the number of physical bigons. Constants and the O(1) bound are source dependent. The lattice oscillation need not converge. No uniform parameter margin is assumed.

Consequently all-copy equality forces equality of L, even when all physical parameters and chronological placements differ. Write ell(K)=L for this law-valued nonnegative integer. It is well defined on the natural private all-copy kernel classes, rather than merely on chosen descriptions.

For actual K and H, grafting gives s_n(K*H)=s_n(K)s_n(H). Equivalently, their physical serial concatenation contains L(K)+L(H) bigons and remains admitted after positive seam merging. Therefore

    ell(K*H)=ell(K)+ell(H).                         (3.1)

This uses the original source multiplication, not multiplication of separately averaged lineage marginals. In the no-merger event exactly n current roots enter every factor. The proof does not assert observability of ell from a finite prefix.

## 4. Exact factorization and root obstruction

Suppose a fixed actual target K has an all-copy factorization into actual sources

    K = H1 * ... * Hr.

Applying (3.1),

    ell(K) = sum_j ell(Hj).

All summands are nonnegative integers. At most ell(K) factors can be nonordinary. This remains true when every factor is freely retuned and has an arbitrary finite number of cells. Arbitrarily many ordinary subdivisions are allowed; no total factor-count bound is claimed.

If K=H^N for an actual source H and integer N>=1, then

    ell(K)=N ell(H).

Thus N divides ell(K). If ell(K)>0 and N>ell(K), no such actual root exists. If ell(K)=0, every physical root also has zero bigons and is ordinary. This is a necessary condition only: divisibility does not prove root existence, and chronological equations may prohibit roots even when the integer condition holds.

For the proposed convolution family, fix t>0 and any positive integer N. Its law gives K_t=(K_(t/N))^N and therefore ell(K_t)=N ell(K_(t/N)). Choosing N>ell(K_t) forces ell(K_t)=0. Every positive-time member is ordinary. There is no nonordinary target at t=1.

This conclusion uses no continuity, bounded source size across t, measurability or differentiability. Allowing source lengths to become arbitrarily large as t tends to zero does not bypass the integer argument, provided each K_t remains one finite physical source and the equalities hold at every arity.

One may further write K_t=E(q_t), where 0<q_t<1 and q_(s+t)=q_s q_t. Positive ordinary durations T(t)=-log q_t are additive and positive on t>0, hence monotone and necessarily T(t)=ct for some c>0. Thus the only such positive-time physical semigroups are the ordinary coalescent time changes. This last identification is elementary: rational additivity and monotonicity squeeze any real t between rational sequences.

## 5. Literal subdivision fails because natural coins refresh

Consider one genuine bigon with equal arm hazard h>0 and an interior inheritance g. Put

    r=2g(1-g),   c=g^2+(1-g)^2=1-r.

For two entering roots, opposite arms have probability r and cannot merge inside that bigon; the same arm has probability c and survival exp(-h). The actual pair survival is

    f(h)=r+c exp(-h).

Now replace the bigon by N serial bigons with arm hazard h/N and the same g, refreshing natural routing at every original site. On the no-merger event the two current roots remain separate and the fresh factors multiply, so their pair survival is

    f(h/N)^N.

Since r,c>0,

    d^2/dh^2 log f(h) = r c exp(-h) / f(h)^2 > 0.

Strict convexity and log f(0)=0 imply, for every N>1,

    N log f(h/N) < log f(h),

and hence

    f(h/N)^N < f(h).                               (5.1)

This is an exact strict discrepancy, not a small-error claim. Positive leading and trailing ordinary hazards can be divided among the N leading/connector pieces so their total is unchanged; they multiply both sides by the same positive pair-survival factor and preserve (5.1). All arm and connector lengths stay positive. Thus the failed comparison can be implemented with legal sources, without relying on a naked zero-connector bigon.

For example, g=1/2, exp(-h)=1/4 and N=2 give f(h)=5/8 but f(h/2)^2=(3/4)^2=9/16. These are hand evaluations of the exact pair formula, not executed experiments.

If parental colors were preserved across all subdivisions, the N subintervals in the same original arm would combine to hazard h. That operation is merely subdividing an original ordinary arm and retains the same original hybrid, or it requires an additional persistent lineage-color interface. Pooling to a two-port source and then refreshing natural routing does not preserve those colors. A shared external register is not silently available, and a locus-wide shared bit would not reproduce per-current-root persistent assignments.

## 6. Retuning escapes the pair obstruction, but not the exact-flow obstruction

The preceding discrepancy is not claimed for arbitrary retuning. In the equal-arm example let p=r+cx with x in (0,1). For any N>=1 define

    x_N = (p^(1/N)-r)/c.

Because r<p<=p^(1/N)<1, x_N lies in (0,1), and N fresh bigons with arm survival x_N have exactly the same pair survival p. Positive ordinary budgets can be kept equal as above. Thus ordinary pair calibration cannot exclude this retuned construction.

However the original target has one bigon and the retuned N-cell source has N bigons. For N>1, the inherited all-copy invariant proves their full kernels differ at some finite arity. The original cross-cherry observer in the count provider, or accepted private tomography, converts that difference into an authorized topology discrepancy in the same fixed positive wrapper. No hidden state has become a new readout.

The separating arity may depend on N and the target. This is deliberately **not** promoted to one uniform finite prefix excluding all N. Indeed the issue is exactly the difference between

    for every N there exists a separating cap m(N)

and

    there exists one cap m excluding every unknown-size rival.

The first is enough to reject an all-copy convolution flow but not to prove G4 finite forcing. Conversely the pair-matching formula does not solve the full capped forest equations at any requested larger cap. It supplies no negative master construction.

### 8.2 Fully strict construction with one shared parameter tuple

Write K=E(exp(-T))*W, where T>0 is its original leading ordinary hazard and W is the unchanged remaining actual word. Choose R,S>0 with R+S=T. Set p=exp(-S). For N>=2 define

    g_N=1/(N+1),
    r_N=2 g_N (1-g_N),   c_N=1-r_N,
    x_N=(p^(1/N)-r_N)/c_N,
    h_N=-log x_N,
    a_N=exp(-R/(N+1)).

For sufficiently large N, 0<x_N<1: p^(1/N) tends to one, r_N tends to zero, and p^(1/N)<1. All other parameters are already strictly interior. Define the actual replacement word

    V_N=E(a_N)*[B_INDEP(x_N,x_N,g_N)*E(a_N)]^N,
    K_N=V_N*W.

The N+1 ordinary segments each have positive hazard R/(N+1). There are N genuine positive equal-arm bigons with interior fresh natural coins. The source is finite, strict and legal for every sufficiently large N; source parameters are shared across all arities. Adjacent ordinary pieces at W's interface merge legally if necessary. Every original target factor in W is preserved.

Pair survival is exactly

    s_2(V_N)=exp(-R)*(r_N+c_N x_N)^N
            =exp(-R)*p=exp(-T).

Thus s_2(K_N)=s_2(K). The physical count is L+N, hence the all-copy count provider ensures that K_N is later inequivalent to K for every N>=2. This later inequivalence does NOT supply exact finite-prefix matches.

### 8.3 Complete forest convergence, rather than only scalar convergence

Fix any finite cap m. Work on its finite labelled forest state space; carried subtrees stay opaque roots. Let Q_m be the ordinary Kingman merger generator. For a same-arm hazard h and natural inheritance g, the original routing formula gives

    B_m(exp(-h),exp(-h),g)
      = I + c(g) h Q_m + O_m(h^2),
    c(g)=g^2+(1-g)^2.

To verify the first derivative, each pair of distinct current roots is sent to the same arm with probability c(g). Conditional on that event it merges at rate one. Two or more mergers have probability O_m(h^2). Every labelled merger has the ordinary current-root pair-choice semantics; no reset of merged descendants occurs. The finite source formula is jointly smooth in h and polynomial in g on a neighborhood of h=0 and g in [0,1]. Its second derivatives are bounded on a compact neighborhood, so the O_m(h^2) bound is uniform in g, including the limit g_N to zero.

The ordinary kernel likewise has E_m(exp(-c(g)h))=I+c(g)h Q_m+O_m(h^2). Thus their operator difference in a finite stochastic-kernel norm is bounded by C_m h^2 for small h. Telescoping products of stochastic kernels contract the corresponding norm. Replacing each of the N bigons in V_N by its ordinary comparison therefore changes the complete cap-m kernel by at most

    N C_m h_N^2.

The remaining ordinary factors compose exactly to the ordinary hazard R+N c_N h_N. From exp(-S/N)=1-S/N+O(N^-2), c_N to one, and c_N bounded away from zero,

    h_N=S/(N c_N)+O(N^-2),
    N h_N^2 -> 0,     N c_N h_N -> S.

It follows that V_N converges to E(exp(-T)) in the COMPLETE cap-m forest kernel. Composition with the same W preserves convergence. This is one sequence of strict physical sources working at every fixed cap, not a separate chosen abstract stochastic kernel at each cap.

For COMMON, equal-arm B_COMMON(x_N,x_N,g_N)=E(x_N) exactly. Hence its replacement kernel is exactly E(exp(-(R+N h_N))), which tends to E(exp(-T)) because c_N tends to one. This establishes simultaneous convergence in the two natural modes from ONE source tuple; it does not make their finite-N pair survivals equal.

The original source-complete completion theorem and grafting semantics transfer fixed-cap kernel convergence through any fixed admitted once-used private completion. Finite collections of such natural tests have one maximum entering cap, so their complete observed distributions converge jointly. No persistent exported register or unidentified multiport interface is added. Transfer to arbitrary additional controls requires the original insertion/tie contract and is not asserted by analogy.

### 8.4 Consequences and the exact limitation

Equip the source-law space with the topology of convergence on finite original natural completion observations. The construction proves that ell is nowhere locally bounded above at any actual private target. This remains true within its EXACT INDEPENDENT pair-survival fibre.

Therefore none of the following can extract an upper count bound near a fixed target:

- a continuous real-valued finite-prefix formula bounding count;
- a locally bounded finite-prefix estimator bounding count;
- a finite collection of strict open inequalities satisfied by the target and asserted to bound all matching-neighborhood source counts;
- a uniform asymptotic remainder or uniform convergence claim that would yield any of those robust bounds from a finite prefix.

Indeed any such finite neighborhood contains K_N with arbitrarily large L+N. This is an exact contradiction within the actual physical source class, not a generic compactness analogy.

What it does NOT contradict is a finite collection of exact response equalities defining a thin fibre. The approximants have only exact pair agreement, and generally differ elsewhere at every finite N. In particular the accepted natural BOTH ordinary-tree certificate uses an equality face that these sources approach without attaining. That existing theorem is a concrete control against the false inference “unbounded nearby counts implies failure of exact finite forcing.”

Thus the all-copy count invariant cannot be converted to general G4 stopping merely by increasing the asymptotic sample arity until the count seems stable, or by assigning a positive error tolerance to the inferred integer. A successful exact count route still requires a TARGET-SPECIFIC finite equality certificate that bounds the count on the exact full-response fibre, despite its unbounded-count neighborhoods. The inherited asymptotic theorem supplies no such implication.

This second attempt stops at that precise missing equality-fibre step. It provides neither an exact fixed-target every-prefix rival family nor a proof that exact count extraction is impossible. The positive master remains open, including discontinuous Boolean or algebraic equality guards.
