## Result first

For the inherited ordinary-topology polynomial source contract, with algebraic fixed interface data and semialgebraic same-source admission constraints, every actual finite source whose complete legal response profile is algebraic has a strictly positive, all-law-equivalent realization with algebraic SURVIVAL/COIN coordinates on the SAME graph. This is an all-profile existence statement, not only the familiar finite-data algebraic-witness fact. Its proof uses one fixed finite-variable parameter ring, not the non-Noetherian all-source response algebra.

Consequently, under exact effectively algebraic response access, the full observation-only G4 stopping problem is equivalent to obtaining a uniform terminating finite-forcing procedure for every supplied algebraic source, with soundness against ALL original real-parameter rivals of unknown finite size. No separate promised enumeration of all target descriptions, no source-size oracle and no algorithm locating a Hilbert generating prefix is needed for this reduction.

**The universal forcing procedure/certificate coverage is not proved. General G4 remains open.** The complete attempted route below reaches that gate and checks why the inherited all-copy count invariant and generic Noetherian argument do not discharge it. No every-prefix physical rival family is claimed. These are applications of classical algebra/logic and enumerative identification, not historical novelty claims.

## 3. Same-graph all-profile descent

**Theorem 1.** Under Section 1, let theta in D_N(R) be one actual finite source. Suppose a_i=f_(N,i)(theta) belongs to A for EVERY legal coordinate i. Then there is eta in D_N(A) such that

    f_(N,i)(eta)=a_i for EVERY i.

The graph, root, original IDs, shared register interpretation, number of physical edges/hybrids and source constraints are unchanged. Only the permitted physical parameter assignment is replaced.

**Proof.** Work in the ONE finite-variable ring A[X_1,...,X_d]. Let

    I = ideal generated by { f_(N,i)(X)-a_i : i legal }.

This ring is Noetherian by Hilbert's basis theorem [HB]. Therefore finitely many members of this original generating family generate I. To justify the last sentence, choose any finite generators of I, write each as a finite combination of original generators, and take the union of the finitely many original indices used. Call that finite set J.

The finite first-order ordered-field formula

    exists X: D_N(X) and f_(N,j)(X)=a_j for every j in J

is true in R, witnessed by theta. All its coefficients belong to the real closed field A. Quantifier elimination for real closed fields [RCF] makes the embedding A into R elementary, so the formula is true in A. Choose its witness eta.

For every i, the polynomial f_(N,i)-a_i belongs to the ideal generated by the J differences, hence vanishes at eta. All original strict inequalities and ties in D_N also hold at eta by the same witness formula. This gives one strict physical source for the ENTIRE legal profile. QED.

No limit of sources, boundary population, compactification or separately selected tuple for each prefix is used. The proof does not say how to find J or eta from an oracle. It does not require finite determination against other source shapes. There are finitely many parameter variables even though there are infinitely many observation equations.

The same argument works over any real closed coefficient subfield K of R when all admission and response coefficients and all target response values lie in K. This is mathematical field descent; it creates no effective presentation of K.

## 4. Algebraic rivals suffice to falsify a finite forcing claim

**Theorem 2.** Let S be an admitted algebraic-coordinate source and F a finite set of original legal coordinates. The following are equivalent:

(a) Every admitted real-parameter rival agreeing with S on F agrees on every legal coordinate.

(b) Every admitted algebraic-coordinate rival agreeing with S on F agrees on every legal coordinate.

**Proof.** (a) implies (b). If (a) fails, choose the actual finite rival graph N, its strict real tuple theta, and ONE legal later coordinate k where its response differs from S. The finite formula

    D_N(X),
    f_(N,i)(X)=f_(S,i) for every i in F,
    f_(N,k)(X) != f_(S,k)

has coefficients in A and is true in R. Real-closed-field transfer gives an A-valued witness on the SAME N, preserving all F equalities, the later inequality and every admission constraint. This contradicts (b). QED.

This theorem uses only finite-data transfer, credited as prior machinery; it is included to make the universal rival quantifier precise. It does not assert that checking (b) terminates. Algebraic descriptions are countable but unbounded.

## 5. Exact reduction of observation-only stopping to supplied algebraic-source forcing

Fix one effective algebraic interface satisfying Section 1. Define:

**O:** There is one algorithm that, from exact algebraic original-response access to ANY promised finite strict real source in this interface, halts with a sound finite determining legal transcript. No source description or size bound is given.

**D:** There is one algorithm that, from ANY supplied admitted finite algebraic-coordinate source S in this interface, halts with a finite legal determining set F(S), sound against ALL admitted finite real-parameter rivals of unknown size.

**Theorem 3. O and D are equivalent.**

**O implies D.** Simulate O with the exact algebraic forward-evaluation oracle of S. Every query answer is computable from the finite source/compiler. The terminating transcript is the required F(S). Any rival agreeing on that transcript follows the same deterministic query path and is covered by the algorithm's forcing guarantee. This argument concerns exact deterministic certificate algorithms; an unverified randomized stopping rule is not substituted.

**D implies O.** Enumerate every finite admitted graph/ID presentation and every finite tuple of real algebraic coordinates. Check its finite admission predicate and keep admitted descriptions S. Such enumeration preserves positive coordinates and all finite algebraic ties. Run D for the candidates, dovetailing if desired. For every returned determining set F(S), query the unknown target on F(S) and compare those finitely many algebraic responses exactly with S. Halt at a complete match, returning that finite transcript and S as an all-law-equivalent representative.

Soundness: the actual target is an admitted real rival matching S on F(S), so D's guarantee forces equality at every legal coordinate. Every other source matching that same transcript also agrees with S, and hence with the target.

Termination: let T be the promised target. All responses returned by its oracle are algebraic. Theorem 1 gives one admitted algebraic-coordinate S_star with the SAME complete law as T, on T's own finite graph. Its finite description appears in the enumeration. D halts on it and every queried response matches, so O halts no later than this successful comparison. The algorithm never has to recognize S_star in advance, find T's graph, locate J, or decide general all-copy equality between arbitrary candidates. QED.

Thus all-profile candidate coverage is not a separate promise on this exact algebraic-response branch. Theorem 3 DOES NOT construct D. It reduces the original observation-only task to a still-unproved unknown-rival forcing task, not to the already solved comparison of TWO supplied descriptions.

An equivalent useful sufficient form is an effectively enumerable certificate relation V(S,c) that is sound against every original rival and covers every algebraic source S. Verification must terminate, and a verified certificate must provide its finite legal query set. Enumerating (S,c) and applying the same finite comparison implements O. Coverage may be by an all-law-equivalent algebraic representative rather than every redundant presentation. Any such relaxation must be stated explicitly; it does not permit independently fitted rows.

## 6. The genuinely global attempted step and where it fails

The intended completion was:

    all-profile descent
      -> enumerate algebraic targets
      -> use source count/ordered equality to recognize bad rivals
      -> enumerate exhaustive sound source guards
      -> find a forcing guard for every candidate

The first implication is now justified under Section 1. The third/fourth implication remains the decisive gate.

### 6.1 Count asymptotics do not certify an unknown-size prefix fibre

For a supplied private INDEPENDENT word, [C] proves

    log s_n = -A n^2 + B n - (L/2) log n + O(1),

where the constants and the validity of an asymptotic bound depend on that physical word. It distinguishes different fixed finite L at the all-copy level. It does not make L an exactly observable finite-prefix function uniformly over arbitrarily weak cells, rare coins, varying connector lengths or alternative original cores.

After Theorem 1, both target and any finite-profile counterexample can be represented algebraically. Their algebraic degrees/heights, field extensions and cell counts remain unrestricted. Real-closed-field transfer adds no height bound or source margin. Consequently it does not make the O(1) estimate uniform over rivals. Replacing “there exists a later discrepancy for this rival” by “one finite prefix excludes every different-count rival” is precisely the still-unproved exchange

    for every rival W there exists k(W)
        versus
    there exists k for every rival W.

The original all-cap ordinary-DIAGONAL return theorem [DIA] is a direct source control: for every finite diagonal cap and algebraic ordinary target q there is a nonempty strict matching word, with algebraic survival/coin coordinates obtainable at that finite cap by finite-shape RCF transfer. Its all-copy count is nonzero. Thus descent plus the count invariant cannot force count from only those diagonal prefixes. This reuses an existing all-cap theorem, not a new cap calculation and not a claim of full-topology nonforcing.

The missing information could still come from the full original topology rows. No argument here shows those rows cannot force a count, chronological normal form, or equivalent target class.

### 6.2 Exhaustive guard syntax does not establish guard coverage

The already reviewed private frontend [V] retains a target-match index, the current physical kernel and pending ordinary products, and checks initialization, inductiveness and terminal exclusion by RCF. Passing certificates are sound. All finite algebraic-coefficient semialgebraic guards can be enumerated and checked.

What is missing is the theorem

    every finite algebraic target admits such a passing guard
    at some finite original prefix,

or a broader equally sound certificate language with proved coverage. Ordinary finite forcing, if true, does not by itself supply a semialgebraic inductive separator for an unbounded source reachability system. Conversely, algebraic source enumeration does not certify the absence of an unenumerated longer rival. The frontend also still needs original general-core, multiport, protected-tie and shared-register transport; Theorem 1 does not supply that transport.

The source-independent exact “good” set could be defined by all future response equalities, but writing that infinite set as a guard merely restates G4. The known full response algebra is non-Noetherian [N], and the full-target ideal records distinguish formal algebraic fibres from actual positive-source fibres. No global finite guard follows from that notation.

### 6.3 A brief generic effectivity countercontrol

Even fixed finite-variable polynomiality plus strict semialgebraic source admission is insufficient to compute a valid forcing prefix UNIFORMLY for arbitrary effectively presented observation families.

Given a program e, take x in (0,1), target x=1/2, and let f_(e,n)(x)=0 if e has not halted within n steps, and f_(e,n)(x)=x otherwise. This is a uniformly computable rational polynomial family with one shared strict variable. Every target is finitely determined within this family: if e never halts all profiles are identical; if e halts at h, coordinate h determines x. If a uniform procedure computed a valid determining prefix m from this compiler and target, simulating e through m would decide halting. If it has not halted by m but halts later, x=1/3 matches the claimed prefix and disagrees later, contradicting validity.

This is only a diagnostic against the proposed GENERIC Hilbert/finite-variable effectivity implication. These f_(e,n) are NOT asserted to be the original biological topology compiler. They change the observation family, so this gives neither source-faithful G4 impossibility nor original every-prefix physical rivals. The original source grammar may possess a special closure theorem absent from this generic example; that theorem remains to be proved.

## 7. Outcome and exact remaining obligation

Established as hand candidates:

1. One fixed finite physical graph has an algebraic-coordinate realization of its entire algebraic legal profile, with strict source constraints preserved under the stated semialgebraic admission contract.
2. A false finite forcing claim against a supplied algebraic source already has an actual algebraic-coordinate counterexample on the real counterexample's graph.
3. On the effective algebraic topology interface, observation-only stopping is equivalent to a uniform supplied-algebraic-source all-size forcing algorithm. Candidate coverage and complete-source extraction need not be separately assumed if that forcing algorithm exists.

Not established:

- A finite determining certificate for every general original target.
- A complete source-admitted finite guard language or a count/shape bound for bad rivals.
- An algorithm for computing Hilbert generators or recognizing a stable ideal prefix.
- A full-prefix actual rival construction at one fixed target.
- Extension to arbitrary-real Cauchy access, nonalgebraic immutable interface coefficients, general nonsemialgebraic rate/clock restrictions, or undeclared new observation channels.

The global attempt stops at universal certificate coverage, not at a supposed lack of enumerable algebraic representatives in the covered interface. A successful next proof must supply that genuinely source-specific coverage or a finite observable count/chronology guard; it cannot reuse two-supplied-source equality as if it already quantified over every unknown-size rival.

No numerical source search, source compiler execution, QE run, proof-assistant build or publication was performed. File copies/hashes are preservation checks, not scientific verification. Independent review is requested specifically for strict source admission, the all-profile versus finite-profile distinction, the same-source shared tuple, and Theorem 3's non-effective-existence/effective-enumeration separation.
