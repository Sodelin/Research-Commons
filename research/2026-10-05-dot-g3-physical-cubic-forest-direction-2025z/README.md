## Exact mathematical scope

For one fixed interior inheritance probability p and real d, take arm DURATIONS

    t/p + (1-p)d t^(3/2)/p,
    t/(1-p) - p d t^(3/2)/(1-p).

They are both strictly positive for sufficiently small t>0. With a(t)=-log b2(t), the full capped kernel equals

    E_a(t) + t^3 [1-3p(1-p)d^2] T + O_m(t^(7/2)).

The signed coefficient T is defined on every arity in PROOF.md. The remainder is uniform on compact interior p ranges and bounded d ranges at each fixed cap. The same source supplies all rows; there are no hidden routing observations, negative physical times or external source mixtures. The frozen proof uses the name a(t) both for calibrated ordinary duration and, explicitly distinguished in Section 4, a temporary arm coefficient; those definitions must not be interchanged.

Finite leading cancellation leaves a remainder. The remaining transfer problem must handle higher grades, repeated spectral weights, chronological order, positivity and the fixed clock budget. The original G3 core/tie/singular/zero-residual branches are not covered by this leading-order calculation.
