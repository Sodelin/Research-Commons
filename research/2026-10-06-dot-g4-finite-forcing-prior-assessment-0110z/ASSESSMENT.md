# Original-observable finite forcing: prior-led assessment

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 6 October 2026. Read-only primary-source and admitted-source assessment for independent review. No new numerical run or master theorem is claimed.

## Recommendation

Do not promote a generic moment/rank or published network-identifiability method into a full original-G4 attack yet. The strongest applicable *kind* of theorem is positive moment forcing, which can exclude arbitrary larger rivals without a size bound. But the natural observable sequence in our INDEPENDENT source class fails its positive-mixture premise, and even a one-edge target has infinite copy-count Hankel rank. The newest network-identifiability result does not cover our unbounded words: its graph definition excludes parallel edges, and its own model retains a 2-blob exception.

Finite forcing remains a legitimate alternative to the rare-route construction. It merits a full attack only after identifying a genuinely nonlinear relation in ORIGINAL finite observations whose equality case controls every longer positive private word, or an original-observable positive representation with a proved whole-fibre rigidity theorem. No such starting identity was found in this audit. This is a recommendation about the available routes, not an impossibility theorem for finite forcing.

## 1. Exact target, menu and quantifiers

The controlling [original master](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/bc864455aaa705dbfd71d3e63429051420c0767d/research/2026-10-05-dot-original-g-master-priority-1913z/MASTER-STATEMENTS.md) fixes one admitted strictly positive target, original IDs/shared parameters, and the complete legal response experiment. Rivals may have any finite admitted size. Tests observe final rooted labelled unranked gene topologies or the declared finite coarsening, with actual once-used legal completions. Hidden routing labels, times and repeated independent occurrences of the box are not extra observations.

The input supplies exact response values without a supplied complete source description or rival-size bound. A positive result must give a target-derived finite certificate against the whole rival class, with its stopping/effectivity promises. Pairwise identifiability, generic parameter recovery, a known-size comparison and eventual identification are different statements.

The ordinary pendant bridge with fixed survival q=1/10 in the fixed positive four-tip tree remains a useful stress target. The rare-route exact-zero gap is a gap in one sufficient counterexample construction; it is not necessary for G4 itself. A universal positive resolution must also handle the other original targets and declared mechanisms.

## 2. What the closest published methods actually provide

### Polynomial-family finite determination

[Belkin–Sinha, Polynomial Learning of Distribution Families](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~ksinha/papers/PLDF_FOCS_10.pdf), Section II, uses polynomial moments in a fixed finite-dimensional parameter family and a finite-generation argument. This is directly useful once a source shape/complexity class is fixed. It does not supply a common parameter ring or a uniform determining family for words of unbounded length.

For our fixed-shape compiler, the logical output is “for each rival shape, some finite determining set.” G4 requires one target-derived finite set working for every rival shape. Interchanging those quantifiers is the missing argument. The [supplied-chain stopping boundary](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/5e01a8e727478a2f22686d31f71304126e345116/research/2026-10-01-sol61-g4-allcopy-2237z/UNKNOWN-SIZE-STOPPING-BOUNDARY.md) already records this distinction.

### Finitary processes and hidden Markov rank

[Schönhuth, Generic Identification of Binary-Valued Hidden Markov Processes](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1101.3712), Section 6.1, states the length-2d−1 determination result within the class of process functions of rank at most d. Its finite identification problem fixes d; generic HMM parameter identification has additional qualifications. A finite observed Hankel block does not establish the global rank bound against all larger processes.

Moreover, source graph size does not even yield the required copy-count rank promise here: Section 3 proves infinite Hankel rank for an ordinary single-edge source. This does not exclude some other carefully proved finite-state representation; it rejects the direct import.

### Positive moments and flat extension

[Laurent–Mourrain, A generalized flat extension theorem for moment matrices](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~monique/files/archivlm.pdf), generalizing Curto–Fialkow, concerns genuine moment/Hankel structures with specified index multiplication and flat-extension conditions. Positivity plus an annihilating polynomial is the key mechanism that can exclude unknown larger support.

An elementary instance appears in [Lovász–Szegedy, Finitely forcible graphons](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.0929), Claim 2.4: a k-valued function is forced by its first 2k moments against unrestricted competing functions, by integrating a nonnegative polynomial vanishing exactly on its values. Thus target-dependent finite forcing can genuinely control an unbounded rival class; it is not merely a rank-bounded identification theorem.

The transfer needs observable moments of one positive representing object for EVERY admitted rival, together with a theorem that its recovered object determines the original responses. Those premises are not supplied by demographic finiteness. Section 4 gives an explicit positive INDEPENDENT source that contradicts the natural ordinary-mixture representation. The valid COMMON moment branch remains separate.

### Network identifiability, including the strongest recent lead

[Brits–Holtgrefe–van Iersel–Martin, On Tree-Network Distinguishability and Full Identifiability of Phylogenetic Networks](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12919v1), Corollary 5.8 and Section 6, gives a substantial all-parameter distinguishability result under JC/K2P leaf-pattern models, modulo 2-blobs. Its Definition 2.1 excludes parallel edges, which our original grammar retains. Its displayed-tree substitution mixture is distinct from the current-root coalescent law; the coalescent discussion uses a qualified nontrivial-3-blob argument. The 2-blob exception is a relevant analogous boundary, not a claim that all our private words belong to that paper’s graph class. No arbitrary parallel-chain recovery or original-topology transfer follows.

[Allman–Ané–Baños–Rhodes, Beyond Level-1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01545-8), Definition 7 and Theorems 4.3–4.6, treats reduced galled tree-child classes with cycle/attachment restrictions and explicit data assumptions. Parallel private bigons are not brought under those hypotheses. Recovering a reduced core therefore does not certify the unbounded passive tail.

The exclusion is substantive, rather than a small technical change: our hard rivals can insert arbitrary positive private bigons without changing the reduced external core. Neither theorem claims to resolve that fibre under the original observation contract.

## 3. Exact source obstruction to the naive finite-rank transfer

Let b_n be the no-merger probability of a private bridge on n entering tokens, with b_0=b_1=1. These quantities are recoverable from original legal topology probabilities, not added hidden observations: the fixed crossed-cherry wrapper in [ALL-CAP Section 2.3](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/5e01a8e727478a2f22686d31f71304126e345116/research/2026-10-01-g4-admitted-testers-0819z/ALL-CAP.md) multiplies b_n by a known positive exterior factor.

For an ordinary bridge of survival 0<q<1,

b_n=q^(n(n−1)/2).

For i,j=0,...,r−1, its copy-count Hankel matrix factors as

H_ij=b_(i+j)=q^(i(i−1)/2) (q^i)^j q^(j(j−1)/2).

Thus

det H = (product_i q^(i(i−1))) product_(i<j)(q^j−q^i),

which is nonzero for every r. Even this finite one-edge source has infinite copy-count Hankel rank. For r=2 the determinant is q−1<0, so this direct sequence is not a positive ordinary Hankel moment sequence either. No computation or new source assumption is used.

This rules out equating “finite demographic graph” with “finite-rank process on the copy-count alphabet.” It does not rule out other nonlinear observation transformations whose required properties are actually proved.

## 4. Exact source obstruction to the COMMON-mixture transfer

Suppose one tries to represent every INDEPENDENT private bridge by a probability measure mu on ordinary survivals x in [0,1], with

b_n=integral x^(binom(n,2)) dmu(x).

Then b_2=E[X], b_3=E[X^3], so Jensen's inequality requires b_3>=b_2^3.

A strict actual INDEPENDENT bigon with equal arm survivals x=y=a in (0,1) and g=1/2 instead gives

b_2=(1+a)/2,
b_3=(a^3+3a)/4,
b_3−b_2^3=(a−1)^3/8<0.

Positive leading/following ordinary populations with combined survival z multiply b_2 by z and b_3 by z^3, so the difference becomes z^3(a−1)^3/8 and stays negative. This is fully inside the original positive private-word class. It decisively refutes that proposed universal positive-mixture representation. It does not refute another moment representation or the valid COMMON mechanism theorem.

More generally, the accepted [uniform-time diagonal return theorem](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/608f690fd76e216036ed2cd282c236fd0aa4ac46/research/2026-10-04-dot-g3-structure-followons-2200z/uniform-diagonal/UNIFORM-TIME-DIAGONAL-FINAL.md) already gives exact replicas of any fixed ordinary q after every finite diagonal prefix. Therefore NO function of finitely many of these diagonal values, linear or nonlinear, can force that ordinary target against all positive independent words. A successful original-menu forcing argument must use further topology/forest information through actual legal tests.

## 5. Why standard positivity/gluing shortcuts also need a source proof

The accepted [affine PSD corollary](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/ce9e2d748031e1823f5b95b7afd22c607e4d9eac/research/2026-10-05-dot-g4-affine-psd-route-check-2004z/PSD-RANK-COROLLARY.md) says that an affine source-positive matrix has the same kernel at every strict target in this same freely parameterized private family. Thus an affine moment/connection matrix cannot produce a new target-specific rank defect there. This is reused evidence, not a new universal impossibility claim.

Graphon forcing suggests genuine nonlinear equality-case methods, but its observation multiplication/gluing must not be assumed here. Four lineages in one ordinary population have no-merger probability q^6; two independent two-lineage repetitions would give q^2. The original once-used box does not turn those independent repetitions into a four-lineage no-merger event. Multiplying known probabilities algebraically is legitimate, but treating that product as another affine legal response requires a separate identity. Hidden moment products and multiple copies of the source cannot be silently introduced.

Nonlinear matrices or inequalities in actual original probabilities remain possible. So do target-specific source arguments not covered by the affine convex-interior premise. This audit does not exclude them.

## 6. Concrete go/no-go criterion for a full finite-forcing attack

A candidate worth promoting must provide:

1. A finite collection of ORIGINAL legal probability expressions, with the same target, controls, source assignment and once-used completion rules.
2. A theorem valid for EVERY finite positive rival, including arbitrary private-chain length, that matching these expressions forces all later original responses. A reduced-core result is insufficient.
3. A checkable target-derived certificate under the original exact-response input. Existence of a forcing set with a supplied target description is not automatically a detectable stopping algorithm.
4. An explicit source proof of positivity, multiplicativity, recurrence closure or equality-case rigidity used in step 2. A finite fitted rank or a hidden representation is insufficient.

The most defensible future finite-forcing target is therefore a source-positive NONLINEAR full-topology identity with a whole-word equality characterization, rather than diagonal Hankel flatness, supplied-size algebraic identification or network-core reconstruction. At present this is a named missing ingredient, not a concrete proved invariant. I recommend retaining finite forcing as an alternative research direction, but not starting a full computational programme under it until such a source identity is proposed and reviewed.

The rare-route ordered-zero gap remains separately preserved. Neither its necessity nor its sufficiency for every possible G4 resolution is asserted by this assessment.
