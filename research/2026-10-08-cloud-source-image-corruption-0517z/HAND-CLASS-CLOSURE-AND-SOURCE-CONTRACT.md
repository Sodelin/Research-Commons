## Class separation and closure

For a class W of PMFs on the SAME finite alphabet, write

    E_beta(W) = {z : some q in W has TV(q,z)<=beta}.

Every allowed corruption at p avoids E_beta(W) exactly when

    for EVERY q in W, TV(p,q)>2beta.

Proof: if an allowed z lies in E_beta(W), triangle gives TV(p,q)<=2beta.
Conversely, such a q has midpoint z=(p+q)/2 within beta of both. This is
precisely the previously source-reviewed pairwise argument, quantified over
the class; it does not assume a supplied desired law.

The draft defines TVClosure(W) by finite-TV neighborhoods and proves inclusion,
monotonicity, idempotence and the empty-class case. The original-source consumer
instantiates the class to the actually derived wrong-answer native image, and
then explicitly to its closure. It does not collapse the latter to raw sources.

## Compact boundary and observed-image closure: hand argument

Assume a faithful finite biological experiment has identified I_wrong, and let
W=closure(I_wrong) in the finite probability simplex. If W is empty, every
allowed observation is separated from wrong images; no fictitious minimizer is
introduced. Otherwise, finite-simplex compactness and continuity give a closest
wrong law q_star in W. This law need not itself be a positive biological source.
Thus pointwise class separation is equivalent to

    d_TV(p,W)=TV(p,q_star)>2beta.

At equality the midpoint overlaps. The current Lean draft has an explicit
closest-law premise only in a generic auxiliary lemma; it does NOT prove or
store compact attainment as a biological field. Formal compactness/attainment
and its numerical distance interpretation remain future obligations.

For beta>=0, the closure of E_beta(I_wrong) is E_beta(W). The forward inclusion
uses compactness: for z_j->z and q_j in I_wrong with TV(q_j,z_j)<=beta, take a
subsequence q_j->q_star in W and pass to the limit. For reverse inclusion, fix
z with TV(q_star,z)<=beta and positive-source laws q_j->q_star. Put
d_j=TV(q_j,z). If d_j<=beta, choose z_j=z. Otherwise d_j>beta and set

    z_j = (1-beta/d_j) q_j + (beta/d_j) z.

This is an allowed OUTPUT PMF for source q_j. TV(q_j,z_j)=beta and
TV(z_j,z)=d_j-beta<=TV(q_j,q_star), so z_j->z. The construction also handles
beta=0: on the second branch z_j=q_j; on the first d_j=0. It never replaces a
biological source with a midpoint or convex mixture. No zero denominator is
used on the first branch. Negative beta is outside the stated error model.
