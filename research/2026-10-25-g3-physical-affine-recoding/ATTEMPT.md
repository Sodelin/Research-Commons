## 2. The already accepted coding channel and its limit

In the inherited cap-four quotient, F=C/b2 and chi=b4/b2 obey

    F(KL)=F(K)+chi(K)F(L),   chi(KL)=chi(K)chi(L).

The second established coordinate T=C+H obeys

    T(KL)=T(K)+b4(K)T(L).

Actual strict physical letters can distinguish every binary word using the first channel. The attempted PCP prescription chi=beta^|u|, b4=beta^|v| fails because b4/chi=b2<1 forces |v|>|u| for every tile. This conclusion concerns those characters, not every possible pair of characters.

## 3. An alternative ratio really escapes that simple ordering test

For a bare INDEPENDENT cell with inheritance g=1/2 and arm survivals x,y in (0,1), the no-merger probabilities are

    b3=(x^3+y^3)/8+3(x+y)/8,
    b4=(x^6+y^6)/16+(x^3+y^3)/4+3xy/8.

These follow by summing over the binomial numbers routed down each arm, using ordinary survival x^{choose(k,2)}y^{choose(4-k,2)} for four roots, and the analogous three-root expression.

Two exact examples:

* x=y=1/2: b3=13/32, b4=81/512, hence b4-b3^2=-7/1024.
* x=1/4,y=3/4: b3=55/128, b4=6253/32768, hence b4-b3^2=203/32768.

To make the surrounding ordinary passages strictly positive, place E(z_left) and E(z_right), both survivals strictly between zero and one, around either cell. Writing z=z_left*z_right, the diagonals become z^3 b3 and z^6 b4; therefore the sign of b4-b3^2 is unchanged. These are genuine strict padded words, not zero-duration boundary gadgets.

Consequently the candidate dilations b4/b3 and b3 have a ratio b4/b3^2 that is not uniformly above or below one. The previously accepted simple length-order contradiction does not automatically reject these alternative characters. This is not evidence that their required affine translations are realizable.

## 4. Sampling consistency removes the proposed digit

Let K be any unexposed natural exchangeable, restriction-consistent complete forest kernel generated by an admitted private word, and let b_r(K) be its probability of no merger starting with r current labelled roots. Let q_r(K) be the probability of ending with exactly one specified pair merged and all other roots separate. Exchangeability makes this independent of the specified pair.

Delete one designated root and ask that the remaining r-1 roots all remain separate. This can happen in exactly these cases:

1. no merger occurred among the r roots;
2. the deleted root merged with exactly one of the r-1 retained roots, and nothing else merged.

A larger cluster or a merger of two retained roots cannot restrict to r-1 separate roots. Thus restriction consistency gives the exact identity

    b_(r-1)(K)=b_r(K)+(r-1)q_r(K),
    q_r(K)=[b_(r-1)(K)-b_r(K)]/(r-1).             (1)

This is a structural identity, not a bound or asymptotic expansion. It holds for arbitrary finite word length. It uses only the natural private kernel; it does not assert that arbitrary exposed registers have the same quotient.

For chronological KL, the transition to one specified merged-pair state satisfies

    q_r(KL)=q_r(K)b_(r-1)(L)+b_r(K)q_r(L).

Define chi_r=b_r/b_(r-1), F_r=q_r/b_(r-1). Strict survival makes these divisions legitimate. Then

    F_r(KL)=F_r(K)+chi_r(K)F_r(L),
    F_r(K)=(1-chi_r(K))/(r-1).                   (2)

For any word K1...Kn, its accumulated affine translation is therefore

    sum_i [product_(j<i)chi_r(Kj)] F_r(Ki)
       =[1-product_i chi_r(Ki)]/(r-1).          (3)

The apparent digit channel is only a telescoping function of its dilation. Its affine maps share one fixed point and commute. In particular, the 4-to-3 channel cannot encode an independent string, despite the diagonal flexibility demonstrated in Section 3. Two letters with the same dilation have exactly the same translation in this channel.

This excludes the explicit adjacent-root channel repair, not all higher-cap coordinates, alternate encodings, nonlinear observations or undecidability reductions. The old C/b2 order-sensitive channel skips a root level and is not contradicted by (1).

## 5. Connection to actual primary hardness constructions

Bell's ICALP 2019 Theorem 1 establishes undecidable non-strict cutpoint emptiness for a commutative polynomially ambiguous PFA over a letter-monotonic language. Section 3.1 starts with powers of a Jordan matrix carrying an integer in its superdiagonal, then uses tensor/direct sums and stochastic normalization. Its matrices need not be physical coalescent kernels.

Reading those gadgets does not repair the source adapter. Fixed-letter polynomial counters are already screened by the accepted finite-fibre obstruction, and a cutpoint predicate is not automatically an admitted exact observation bank. The present calculation instead directly tests an actual physical affine-channel replacement. It fails by the exact restriction identity (1), not by a claim that every triangular stochastic semigroup is easy.

## 6. Remaining full-master obligations

A successful hardness reduction would still need BOTH:

* actual physical channels carrying the required synchronized word comparison, with the same source tuple and admissible observation readout;
* a finite original input forcing every matching source, including alternate cores and unrestricted fresh parameters, to satisfy that encoded language.

A finite chosen alphabet of actual strict words is insufficient. If a positive pair-survival target also exposes its cumulative contraction, the inherited finite-palette count bound applies. Hiding that coordinate avoids that particular bound but does not enforce the alphabet or the shared index word.

No total decision algorithm or undecidability reduction results. The concrete outcome is that one plausible alternate-character construction survives the old diagonal-order screen but fails at its translation/readout identity. No new component ladder or generic impossibility claim is proposed.
