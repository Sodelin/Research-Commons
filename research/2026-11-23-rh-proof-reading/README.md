# What the RH proof contains beyond its headline

**First edition · November 23, 2026 · Research Commons reading index.** Proof by Levent Alpöge with Claude; this is an attributed reading and verification map, not a new RH result. [Original announcement](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf) · [Lean community verification](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/) · [Independent logs](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/) · [Pinned original source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/tree/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005).

## Two investigations

- **[The denominator bound](rh_denominators/README.md)** asks what reusable theorem is already inside the proof. The arithmetic result is surprising because it does not mention zeros or prime counting. Extracting its actual domain and identifying the slack downstream separates a theorem one could reuse from an analogy to Vandiver. Checkpoint: exact statement, a hand-checked finite-product consequence, and exact rational replay for a source-local weakening of the adapter. Pending: isolated transitive-axiom and kernel checks for the side theorem, and comparison to precise prior class-group estimates.
- **[The nonabelian benchmark](rh_nonabelian/README.md)** asks whether the engine can be transplanted. The splitting field of x³−2 is small enough to write out every local factor yet already introduces one irreducible degree-two factor. Checkpoint: hand reduction and a proof that simply regrouping finitely many Dirichlet factors cannot reproduce the missing factor. Pending: the actual analytic estimate for split primes, or another method that supplies its zero-free half-plane. No extended RH case has been proved here.

| 5. A sphere of digits controls an arithmetic tower | Integers encoded by equal-norm vectors avoid short affine relations: no-carry arithmetic reduces the relation to three distinct collinear points on a sphere. This yields a large set with restricted orbit intersections. | [Pinned ThickExists](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab/src/Formalism/FrobeniusOrbits/FrobeniusOrbitsProofs/ThickExists.lean); [reported named replay and example](https://nasqret.github.io/riemann-rev/book/supply.html#the-behrend-set-in-the-tower). | 6. A small determinant produces a divisibility gain | In a ring quotient, two transformed rows become proportional in the sense of both being multiples of the same row. | 7. Sparse windows can rule out zeros globally | A one-sided bound on suitable smoothed prime sums in arbitrarily late power windows rules out zeros to the right of the designated line in the relevant positive family. | [Statement, source links and reported independent replay](https://nasqret.github.io/riemann-rev/book/engine.html). It is a conditional analytic transfer; transferring it to the two-dimensional factor requires checking the pole and growth hypotheses, then producing applicable windows. |
| 8. Arithmetic output with no zeta in the statement | Robin and Lagarias divisor-sum inequalities follow from RH; the team has combined independent formalizations and checked the resulting declarations. | [MIT verification, arithmetic consequences](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/#arithmetic-consequences). ## Who is publicly working on it?

At least three identifiable public projects discuss the denominator branch: [Rubinstein/Waterloo](https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~mrubinst/alpoge_claude_grh/), [Jaswal](https://manvirjaswal.github.io/papers/rh-dirichlet-after-alpoge/), and [Naskręcki's atlas](https://nasqret.github.io/riemann-rev/book/supply.html). They provide reading and verification, not evidence of three teams independently improving the theorem. The author signaled the broader extension. The targeted search did not identify a public project applying this new proof to the exact x³−2 benchmark. That is not a census of private or unpublished work and not a novelty claim. The case has substantial classical literature and computations.

## How to extend this list

## Second pass · November 23, 2026

- [Denominator and class-group follow-up](rh_denominators_v2/README.md): Jaswal already published the rational specialization and a single-prime consequence; it was not unreported. This note isolates the finite-layer comparison and packages a weighted rank budget. It contrasts Soulé's explicit cutoff and Kurihara's stronger result in one twist. An exact-signature audit is specified below; no new Lean compile is claimed.
- [Compact-window nonabelian reduction](rh_nonabelian_v2/README.md): build a finite-range prime-power statistic that automatically cancels the linear term and whose Mellin multiplier cannot cancel a zero inside the critical strip. An eventual one-sided bound on this statistic would suffice. The necessary estimate remains unproved; the original `IsChiField` cyclotomic hypothesis prevents direct reuse of the cohomology-window provider. Exact rational identities replayed; the reduction is a hand proof.
- [Two transferable pieces beyond RH](math-extractions/EXTRACTIONS.md): a quantitative normalized moment-recovery bound in the equally spaced exponent case, motivated by a Navier–Stokes lemma, and a noise allowance for streaming greedy selection inspired by the SAT proof. Exact finite examples replayed; not new Lean proofs or claims of priority.
