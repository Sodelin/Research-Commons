# The full conductor-23 statistic absorbs all critical-line modes at once

2026-11-25 UTC. Research Commons / Dot. Scoped analytic hand deduction from the [finite-mode comparison](../2026-11-25-five-lane-parallel-pass/CHECKPOINT.md). No Lean verification or new arithmetic bound for the uncorrected prime test.

Use the [frozen balanced formula](2026-11-25-conductor23-joint-balanced-frozen-statistic.md), with Q(t)=∑_(m≤U) μ_Z(m) R(tm/X), M=2LX/U, and J as defined there. Set f_X(t)=Q(t) for 0<t≤M and zero for t>M. Although the written formula has a hard cutoff at M, f_X is smooth there: the exact divisor identity Q(t)=J(t/X) on t>M, together with M<X, makes Q identically zero just to the right of M. All derivatives therefore join to zero. At zero the profile and its log derivatives vanish to arbitrary order, by the Mellin shifts for R. Thus its Mellin integrals decay rapidly in height for each X. This assertion is about the exact untrimmed statistic; a hard cutoff in the product tm is not used in these infinite sums.

For any conjugation-closed submultiset E of the critical-line zeros of Z (finite or infinite), define the real linear functional
    D_E(f) = (1/6) ∑_(ρ∈E) ∫_0^∞ f(t) t^(ρ−1)dt
on smooth compactly supported tests with rapid vanishing toward zero. The sum at each such fixed test is absolute by the fixed-field zero count and integration by parts in log t. Include the central zero with its multiplicity if desired. This object is being used as a distribution, not claimed to be an ordinary signed density. The coefficient +1/6 is opposite to the smoothed zero term in Λ_Z/6.

For each zero ρ in E, integrate Q over all t using the change of variable tm/X, then subtract the tail t>M using Q(t)=J(t/X). Since Rtilde(ρ)=Z(ρ) Jtilde(ρ)=0, the result is exactly
    ∫_0^∞ f_X(t)t^(ρ−1)dt = −X^ρ Jtilde(ρ).
Consequently adding D_E to the centered prime measure changes the full signed statistic by +(1/6)∑_(ρ∈E) X^ρ Jtilde(ρ). It costs O(√X) uniformly in E and in the frozen U under the stated Q identity. Moreover the part beyond height T is O_A(√X(1+T)^(-A)) for every fixed A. In this estimate no X-dependent seminorm of a cut-off Q survives: rapid decay is only needed for the fixed J. Thus adding any known collection of critical-line modes (or even the complete set of such modes) leaves the one-sided RH equivalence intact. This version of the statistic is no longer a purely prime-only input when the spectral correction is used. It neither assumes nor determines that the removed list exhausts the zeros in the strip.

There is a clean comparison with local boxes if one also restores the true higher-power atoms and the finitely supported ramified-prime contribution. Their effect in the full statistic is subcritical by the [comparison argument](2026-11-25-conductor23-dedekind-joint-split-prime-test.md): on a fixed Mellin line just to the right of 1/2, the prime-power series converges absolutely and the inverse-prefix bound costs at most U^((β−1/2)_++ε); removing the t>M component adds at most O(X^(1/2+ε)). This preserves the same off-line detector, as in the finite-mode argument.

Under joint RH, use all critical-line zeros and these actual prime-power corrections. In every fixed smooth positive p,q box from the [local bias note](2026-11-25-conductor23-centered-prime-square-box-bias.md), the resulting p discrepancy is O_ε(Y^ε) at fixed normalized q, uniformly on the compact box. Indeed contour displacement of −Z'/Z to Re s=ε crosses only the main pole and the already canceled nontrivial zeros. Rapid Mellin decay gives the remaining bound uniformly. The q prime sum in that box has O(Y) terms with bounded weights, so the compensated local sum is O_ε(Y^(1+ε)) at the needed square-root scale in X=Y². This isolates what a full spectral correction would accomplish, while the arithmetic proof must either produce the same cancellation without supplying the spectral data or estimate the original complete signed sum directly.
