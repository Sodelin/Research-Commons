## Required physical suppliers
1. Admitted device class, shared nuisance parameters, and precise target.
2. Actual joint laws of legal observations, including dependence. Mean conductance alone does not specify detector noise.
3. Effective approximations to physical target-labelled observation-law images.
4. Calibrated observation channels, model/systematic discrepancy, and simultaneous confidence control. Fresh independent observations or a separately justified dependence-valid concentration theorem are required.
5. Target separation sufficient for the requested guarantee, or explicit ambiguity and abstention.
6. For design optimization: legal actions, response representation, costs, and reset/repetition rules.

Finite target labels can encode sign or promised-margin classes. Exact recovery of a continuum-valued determinant requires a separately stated target/error formulation. TV-law balls, contamination models, and deterministic conductance-error bounds are distinct contracts.
