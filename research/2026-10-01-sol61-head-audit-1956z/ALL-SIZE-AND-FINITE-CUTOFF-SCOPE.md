# All-size coverage and finite-cutoff inventory

Attribution: Sol6.1 head audit, coordinated by dot. Date: 2026-10-02. This source-scope note answers which dimensions are bounded, which have uniform sufficient observations, and which retain genuine open recognition obligations. It adds no new mandatory G gates.

## Four different finite claims

1. **Pairwise finite separation:** any unequal pair of infinite finite-input law families differs somewhere at a finite input. This alone gives no algorithm detecting equality or a bound over the source class.
2. **Source-dependent sufficient cap:** a particular source or pair has a finite determining prefix, whose size may vary without a known effective bound.
3. **Uniform cap:** one fixed cap works for EVERY member of a specified source/target/measurement family. An exact threshold requires both the matching upper theorem and a full-menu lower example.
4. **Effective stopping:** a terminating procedure returns a certified determining prefix, even if its universal numerical value was never computed. A known all-copy equality locus can supply this; observing stable numerical ranks or successive ideals does not.

Full infinite-family injectivity proves that all finite laws together identify a target. It need not by itself provide any of the last three claims. An epsilon approximation bound is also distinct from exact finite determination.

## What compression actually preserves

The separately attributed [symmetry interface](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/d15b8a79688225192c6724986e4633cb3266ab14/research/2026-10-01-sol61-g4-allcopy-2237z/SYMMETRY-SUFFICIENT-INTERFACE.md) formalizes this source argument. For a private exchangeable two-port source at a FIXED entering-root cap n, the probabilities of unlabelled rooted forest shapes recover every labelled fresh n-root probability by dividing orbit mass by orbit size. Selected-label projectivity then supplies all lower fresh rows, and opaque subtree grafting supplies every prebuilt-forest action. At n=6, 20 orbit probabilities (19 after normalization) suffice for 2431 labelled forests; at n=4 the six-orbit quotient has five normalized coordinates. This is classical exchangeability plus source projectivity, not a proof of minimal dimension or minimum physical tomography cost. Port colors, label-sensitive actuators and exposed registers require their appropriate joint interface/stabilizer. It does not cap arbitrary entering n.

An ordinary bottleneck of KNOWN duration t>0 gives the classical bound Pr(N_n(t)>M)<=2/(tM), uniformly in finite n. Replacing only that high-current-root branch by a normalized kernel on the SAME labelled forest boundary type gives a coupling TV error at most this bound. A genuine cutset crossed by all relevant lineages permits a sum of such bounds without independence. Retained subtrees and joint registers/history remain intact; low root count is not a finite scalar-count representation. Known lower bounds on durations yield uniform epsilon budgets; strict positivity alone does not. This is approximate source-interface compression, not exact stopping.

The root/G4 follow-up gives a sharper classical bound at pair rate one:

    Pr(N_n(t)>M) <= min{1, 2/(tM), exp(M+1-t M(M+1)/4)}.

Indeed the time to reach M is the sum of independent holding exponentials with rates lambda_j=j(j-1)/2, j>M. Set theta=M(M+1)/4, half the smallest such rate. Then -log(1-theta/lambda_j)<=2theta/lambda_j and sum_(j>M)1/lambda_j=2/M, so its log moment generating function is at most M+1. Chernoff's inequality gives the exponential term, uniformly over finite starting n. At t=1, M=7 gives exp(-6)<0.01; the simpler Markov bound requires M=200 for that sufficient error budget. These are proved sufficient bounds, without a minimum-cutoff, runtime or whole-Lean claim. The holding-time/coming-down framework is classical; see [Song's primary-author notes, Section 2.11](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~yss/Pub/CMPG_lecture_notes.pdf).

## Why the remaining dimensions were not silently extended

G3's compact-N-factor counterargument disproves one proposed cap-only shortcut. G4's complete forest collisions show count summaries can erase placement and order information. G5 removes hidden source-size/level restrictions for its target through a positive-support and bounded-position proof, while deliberately retaining a same-source promise. G7's finite span uses resettable exact-law algebra and original IDs. None of those arguments supplies the other's missing inverse/empirical/control premises by association.
