## Theorem

The sharp jointly attainable cost vector is:

- one actuated site
- two distinct configurations
- one executed pooled exact-law program

Every admissible procedure has worst-case costs at least these three values. One fixed nonadaptive program attains all simultaneously.

### Attaining policy for every admitted positive source

Choose force 0 and force 1 each with probability 1/2 and discard the row label. After full forcing, either inheritance mechanism is the ordinary MSC on that switched tree. For each selected tree its matching quartet probability is 1−2x/3 and its two alternatives are x/3 for some 0<x<1, the survival over the positive internal path.

Let T_0,T_1 denote the two switched quartets and x_0,x_1 their respective positive internal survivals. They may coincide and may obey arbitrary shared-source constraints; no independent parameter reassignment is used. The pooled vector has the form

p_t = b + (1/2)·1{T_0=t}(1−x_0) + (1/2)·1{T_1=t}(1−x_1),
b=(x_0+x_1)/6.

At most two different quartets occur, so at least one topology is absent and min_t p_t=b. Both positive switching weights and positive internal lengths guarantee

Q = {t : p_t > min_u p_u}.

This is the actual implementable exact-real decoder, not an existence-only policy. The original quartet split union is then S=Q. The policy uses precisely two configurations, one site and one program. It remains valid when the source has a neutral bigon/singleton Q or unequal selected internal lengths.
