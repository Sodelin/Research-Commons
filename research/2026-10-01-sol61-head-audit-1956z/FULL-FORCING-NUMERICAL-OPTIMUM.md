## 1. Exact experiment and largest statement

The source is any finite binary rooted LSA representative of the admitted outer-labeled planar cut-child class, at arbitrary finite level/blob count, with exactly r complete ORIGINAL hybrid IDs and parent-0/parent-1 labels. Its graph and target are unknown. Positive finite edge coalescent lengths/interior inheritance may vary freely. No topology-dependent mapping of IDs to leaf descendants is supplied.

Every permitted row is a FULL assignment in {0,1}^r, forcing every lineage at each original hybrid while preserving other demographic parameters. A STATIC parameter-oblivious programme selects full rows before locus randomness, with specified positive weights, optionally retaining selected-row labels. The readout is exact unrooted one-copy gene-quartet law; at general n one may retain the whole vector of taxon-quartet marginals. Fresh loci/shared-label policies and common versus independent natural inheritance are kept explicit. No partial or response-adaptive choices are included in the static lower bound.

Count D as the union of distinct full configurations with positive weight across all programmes. K counts programmes, with at most L positive configurations per programme. This is not the count of loci or the global library size of an adaptive policy.

**Theorem.** For r≥2, a static full-forcing support universally identifies Q/S iff its binary rows form a strength-two covering array. Hence

D_min(r)=C(r):=min{m≥4 : r≤binom(m−1,floor(m/2)−1)},
K_min(r,L)=ceil(C(r)/L), for integer L≥1.

These are exact worst-case costs for the entire finite-source class, already necessary on four taxa. The upper bound holds for every finite n≥4. No assertion that the same four-taxon collision remains indistinguishable under every richer fixed-n observation is made.

For r=1, D_min=2 and K_min=ceil(2/L); the [one-hybrid proof](ONE-HYBRID-OPTIMUM.md) supplies its matched witnesses. For r=0, one empty configuration/one programme suffices for the unknown positive tree target. Its site cost is zero.

Common/independent natural inheritance do not change the full-forcing theorem: complete forcing makes both conditional histories tree MSC. Allowing passive common laws or richer calendar/rooted/multicopy data would change the experiment and can invalidate this lower bound.

## 4. Necessity for numerical laws, labels and arbitrary fixed weights

If support U is not strength-two, it misses some assignment (u,v) on two IDs i,j. Use the preceding admitted paired sources for that missing cylinder.

Every selected full row lies outside it. Consequently every selected row law is equal between the two sources. A label-retaining programme has law (w_z p_z)_z, equal on both; a pooled programme has sum_z w_z p_z, also equal. ## 6. Prior bound and constructive implementation

The binary strength-two capacity is classical, not a new combinatorial discovery. [Choi, Kim and Oh (2011), introduction and Definition 2.5](https://arxiv.org/html/1111.0587v1) explicitly give maximum columns binom(m−1,floor(m/2)−1) and its standard construction; the primary text was checked in this audit.

Construct m=C(r), set the first row to all ones, and assign each column a distinct (floor(m/2)−1)-subset of rows 2,…,m as its remaining ones. Equal-size distinct subsets supply 01/10; row1 supplies 11; their union misses a row, supplying 00. The capacity theorem supplies matching combinatorial necessity.

Examples: C(2)=4, C(3)=4, C(4)=5, C(10)=6. This source/numerical-law bridge does not claim historical originality; theorem-level comparison with prior network experiment results is still needed before a novelty claim.
