Finishing G1–G7, Lean and BIO-1 would not establish universal biological or cross-science inference. The programme branches by scientific source class, observation, target and resources. “All finite levels” is not “every model,” and it does not mean infinite networks.

## 1. Central dependency map

1. Specify actual source class and observation experiment; establish source/target semantics
2. Structural all-level theorem relates displayed-quartet support Q and displayed-split union S, with compatible orders
3. An identifiable biological observation regime supplies structural answers, target confidence sets or justified abstention
4. Exact-query/BIO-1 algorithms acquire and reconstruct those answers efficiently
5. G6 certifies finite-data conclusions under the actual experiment and robust separation
6. G7 optimizes intervention/design resources under a specified accessible original-ID registry and menu
7. Independent review and Lean verify the real bridges/endpoints alongside the mathematics
8. Stronger observations, broader graph classes, different targets and empirical validation branch outward and require their own proofs

Nondependencies matter: G5 promised-source recovery does not require G3 recognition of arbitrary supplied laws. G6's latest construction approximates target-image closures without deciding exact G3 boundary membership. G4 finite-cap contextual equivalence is different from G5 fibers, G6 noisy certification and G7 cost. Exact finite query integers refine the usable asymptotically optimal algorithm rather than precede its use.

## 2. Finish declared source-realization and equivalence contracts: G3/G4

These are genuine mathematical frontiers, not a request merely to test larger examples.

- **G3:** actual-source membership at higher copy caps, especially ordinary-moment-interior points and independent-inheritance noncommuting forest matrices. A positive measure/moment representation is not automatically a finite positive biological source. Common cap-four recognition and arbitrary-cap exposed-boundary recognition do not settle interior membership. Needed: finite source/factor bound for each attained stratum or valid negative classification
- **G4:** effective source-dependent all-copy stopping for arbitrary independent serial chains/general source boxes. Positive branches include unknown-size common chains, one independent bigon up to arm exchange, and finite-cap tomography of arbitrary private-randomness two-port chains
- The full-labelled-forest tomography argument exposes kernels through actual positive rooted-topology observations. Equality at every infinitely many copy allocation still needs a stopping theorem
- Serial composition is a distinct obstruction: an ordinary coalescent edge and independent bigon commute through three roots but can differ at four. Identifiability of each factor does not prove identifiability of products. The latest seven-total-copy separator is an actual finite witness, not an all-copy classification
- Remaining multiport/shared-register/weaker-tester-menu cases need their own admitted contexts and completeness proof

Arbitrary finite internal source size at each fixed total copy cap is a meaningful universal theorem. “All copies” adds another quantifier; neither permits infinite source networks.

## 3. Effective inference and optimal design: G6/G7

**Mathematical acceptance work:**

- G6's source-preserving finite-profile approximation and robust-fiber certification are full-contract hand-proof submissions awaiting source-critical acceptance. Review cut guards, positive calendar embeddings, shared clock/rates and the distance-2β boundary
- G7's F1/F2 continuous adaptive exact-law argument is restricted to complete finite original-ID registries, finite readout, reset and deletion-closed path budgets. Its finite-depth reduction does not bound the global number of distinct real-weight programs across all policy branches
- Pointwise honest stopping, source-specific finite mean/exponential tails, uniform finite mean, worst-case finite horizon and exact optimal expected constants are separate claims
- Unknown/inaccessible hybrids create actual exact-law or honest-stopping obstructions in the supplied four-taxon unrooted one-copy readout. Additional weights do not remove these collisions. Richer observations need new source-matched analysis
- Rational-only adaptive weights, unrestricted metric/sequence readouts, physical actuator costs and unbounded expected-constant optimization are separate contracts

**Implementation/verification work:**

- G6's complete graph catalogue, hazard-cell search, calendar compiler and integrated inference engine have not been executed
- G7's census/compiler is implemented with substantial small-census replay; full biological source-to-optimal-policy execution and policy extraction remain incomplete
- Complete mathematical optimizer specifications require verified execution and measured resource use. A returned abstract Bernoulli optimum is not a biological optimum
- Match every lower bound to the same source class, observations, allowed adaptations and cost as its upper bound

## 4. BIO-1: complete biological inference pipeline

The most direct downstream programme is already BIO-1:

- Combine a selectable sparse/adaptive quartet strategy with a scientifically justified data-to-answer provider
- Preserve adaptive-choice error accounting when quartets share genes/loci
- Declare supplied versus reconstructed circular order/tree of blobs
- Handle missing taxa/quartets, gene-tree estimation error, dependence and near-degenerate parameters
- Return certified partial structure/confidence sets or abstention when complete support is not uniformly recoverable
- Count distinct queried subsets, loci, runtime, storage and confidence separately
- Compare with relevant existing NetCS/quarnet-query work under matched premises and budgets before claiming novelty

Reviewed exact-support recovery already gives matching Θ(n log n) adaptive growth under its source premises. Do not rediscover that algorithm. Exact integer Q*(n) for n>5, executed all-size optimal policies and the actual biological experiment remain separate refinements/gaps.

G5 supplies another observation route: four selected tips per exact continuous calendar-law query. Four tips is not four observed loci, and the theorem does not establish sequence-to-calendar-law access.

## 10. Recommended progression and stopping rules

First close named review/verification and genuine G3/G4/G6/G7 mathematical obligations under their contracts, while preserving accepted components. Integrate an attainable BIO-1 pipeline. Then choose one stronger-observation or broader-class branch from a demonstrated information gap.

An assigned mathematical branch stops only at its promised characterization, an admitted counterexample to the precise claim, or an honest concrete barrier with resumable proof state. An implementation branch stops at a verified actual-source run matching the promised resources/results. A scientific bridge stops at source-matched evidence rather than analogy. None requires silently solving every neighboring field.

Preserve existing ownership, few simultaneous tasks and meaningful durable checkpoints. Future selection among this full documented roadmap should follow actual mathematical readiness and Nolan's priorities rather than automatic expansion of today's allocation.
