## What is established here

**Conditional all-size result.** Given the submitted positive biological normal form, each target's limiting CF laws are exactly the polynomial images of the closed parameter cubes of the same target-labeled catalogue graphs. Retaining the original target label at zero-inheritance or collapsed-edge limits is essential. Closure membership becomes a finite existential real-polynomial problem rather than a universal epsilon test.

**Finite certification boundary.** Define the robust fiber as every target that produces, or can approach, the observed CF vector. In the declared marginal fresh-locus experiment, uniformly honest high-probability finite certification is possible exactly for properties constant on that fiber. Nested all-prefix confidence boxes give the positive procedure; finite-horizon continuity gives the negative direction. The upper confidence guarantee also applies when a full gene tree supplies dependent quartets within each independent locus. The necessity theorem does not silently extend to the richer full joint n-gene experiment.

**An independent all-size obstruction.** Every positive binary species tree on n >= 4 admits a positive one-reticulation extension with a strictly larger split target and law P_eta = (1-eta)P_tree + eta Q. This holds even for complete rooted metric genealogies and both inheritance mechanisms. The source construction extends ASTRA-OBS's existing four-taxon sequential argument to every such tree. It does not depend on the new normal form. It is an impossibility of uniformly honest finite terminal certificates over arbitrarily weak reticulation alternatives, not an impossibility of all estimation or pointwise consistency.

**Complete conditional four-taxon frontend.** The exact and robust image table is implemented using six source-justified image families. At (1/6,5/12,5/12), independent inheritance has one exact target but two robust candidates. At (1/2,1/3,1/6), either mechanism has one robust target with exact separation 1/12. A tree-shaped profile has one exact target but three robust candidates. Completeness depends on ASTRA-OBS's local image lemmas, not on enumerating six arbitrary graphs.
