## 1. Answer

The defensible connection is **the same kind of inference problem recurring in different scientific models**: available observations can identify a requested prediction or property without identifying the whole hidden mechanism. Exact coarse-graining can sometimes make that connection mathematical. This does **not** establish a literal self-similar or fractal map between a species genealogy and a psychological mechanism.

There are three distinct claims:

1. **Literal graph/fractal self-similarity.** This would need specified objects, scales, maps, and an isomorphism or scaling law. None is established by the present sources. A genealogical network, a social-observation network, and a latent learning-state system have different vertex/edge semantics.
2. **Model abstraction/coarse-graining.** A projection can preserve a chosen family of predictions, dynamics, or interventions under explicit consistency conditions. This is established prior art. [Rubenstein et al., UAI 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.00819), Definition 3, Theorem 6 and Section 4.5, require intervention-respecting transformations; [Banisch and Lima, 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.3902), Sections 2–4, derive exact Markov aggregations from weighted-network symmetries. Neither licenses causal conclusions from resemblance alone.
3. **Common inference structure.** Observation equivalence, target constancy, joint consistency, feasible interventions, noise and cost are reusable mathematical questions. The scientific work is to instantiate them with an actual source and observation process. This is the strongest justified interpretation here.

The biological program already has a concrete target-directed theorem: the accepted G5 calendar result recovers Q/S from exact four-tip calendar laws under its admitted-source promise. It need not reconstruct the hidden graph or solve arbitrary-law G3 recognition. The cultural-learning illustration below shows the same *logic* in a different source model, not a transfer of NANUQ's graph theorem.

### The “largest identifiable collection” question

For a declared source class M and obtainable experiment menu E, put m~E m' when all permitted observation laws agree. A target h can be answered exactly precisely when h is constant on these classes. Thus all such targets factor through the observational quotient M/~E. This is a classical characterization; it need not give a finite list, an effective representation, a valid scientific model, or a stable decoder.

A useful new application asks which *scientifically meaningful* targets and feasible measurements realize this principle. Finite noisy terminal certification uses robust/closure fibers, not just exact fibers. The accepted [G6 proof, 883a9315](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/883a9315f893e93211c64de344bac3dc365a3e18/research/2026-10-01-g6-effective-certification/PROOF.md), Sections 4.6–4.9, already makes that distinction for its actual computational experiments. No general claim that the complete hidden source is ambiguous at every law is made here.

## 4. Provisional meta-G questions: classical overlap and useful missing instantiations

The following labels are questions for organizing the requested combination audit. They are not newly assigned proof gates.

- **MG1: certified answers under budget.** Target identification, finite-data coverage and resource constraints must refer to one experiment. The prior art includes decision sufficiency and fixed-budget/fixed-confidence identification. [Kaufmann, Cappé and Garivier, 2016](https://jmlr.org/papers/v17/kaufman16a.html), Lemma 1 p. 7 and Theorems 4/6, relates expected action-specific sample counts and KL separation to decision error under fresh stochastic draws. G6's honesty and G7's exact-law support costs cannot simply be multiplied into a proven biological finite-budget optimum.
- **MG2: value of the next measurement.** Choosing experiments for posterior uncertainty reduction is old: [DeGroot, 1962](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177704567). The useful application-specific objective is reduction in uncertainty about the requested target, or improvement in a declared decision loss, with feasible actions. It need not coincide with entropy reduction about the whole hidden model. An optimal policy needs a source-matched law and a matched lower bound, not a generic active-learning slogan.
- **MG3: resource substitution.** [Blackwell, 1953](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032) and [Le Cam, 1964](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177700372) are classical experiment-comparison antecedents. Richer data can dominate a coarsening through a parameter-independent channel. Different resources generally produce a partial order, not a universal exchange rate. CG2 is a concrete source-specific obstruction to substituting arbitrary contemporaneous copies for fresh loci in its rare-routing family. Calendar resolution, labelled programs and actual controls may remove different ambiguities; their availability is separate.

Access limit: identities for Blackwell/DeGroot/Le Cam were verified, but direct publisher full-text readers failed. They are prior-art pointers, not newly audited full-theorem premises. The fully retrieved modern primary statements above and the existing [general-transfer boundary record](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/main/research/2026-09-30-general-transfer-closure/MASTER-BOUNDARIES.md) suffice to prevent relabeling these generic ideas as novel meta-G results.

## 5. ONE concrete cultural-learning bridge

### 5.1 Primary model before the proposal

The selected source is **Eriksson, Cownden and Strimling (2017)**, [PubMed 29230064](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29230064/), [DOI 10.1038/s41598-017-17826-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-17826-9), complete PMC body retrieved in two checked contiguous offsets. It is primary mathematical/simulation research, not a proof of a particular evolved human adaptation.

Their Equations 1–5 define a learner's current probability x of behavior B. After observing B it becomes x+g(x); after observing b it becomes x−g(1−x). Their three increment specifications are

    C: g(x)=γ x(1−x)
    N: g(x)=γ(1−x)
    A: g(x)=γ(1−x)²,             0<γ≤1

The source analyzes different population consequences and order effects despite social enhancement based on one observation at a time. The labels C/N/A describe those model specifications; they are not inferred psychological diagnoses or labels for people. Model details, including reinforcement and observation order, change the conclusions.

The sigmoid literature is also contested. [Acerbi et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27796373/), Results/Methods, simulates restricted-demonstrator and variant-preference alternatives producing sigmoid-like population summaries. [Smaldino, Aplin and Farine, 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30228351/), Results, Table 1 and Discussion, finds pooling/initialization/event-definition artifacts and restricts the demonstrator-subgroup alternative to specific conditions. [Acerbi et al.'s 2018 reply](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-30382-0) maintains a conditional-validity concern. The justified conclusion is **inspect the actual generative and measurement process**, not “all sigmoid evidence is useless” or “a sigmoid uniquely reveals a mental mechanism.”

### 5.2 Explicit illustrative contract

This is an analyst-designed observation contract added to the published update model, not a claimed reproduction of its simulation code or an established empirical task.

- **State:** a learner's latent x∈[0,1]; across learners it has an unknown distribution H. Known γ and a named C, N or A update rule are initial model promises.
- **Baseline observation:** Y∼Bernoulli(x). Each learner block can contain R repeated emissions that are conditionally independent given the same x and do not update x.
- **Intervention:** a controlled, perceived B or b demonstration applies the corresponding published map once. A word w applies its maps in order. Merely recording behavior does not substitute for a social demonstration.
- **Target:** q_w=E_H[x after w], the probability of a fresh subsequent B emission in the declared task/context.
- **Causal assumptions:** assigned demonstration rather than self-selected exposure; stable common H across randomized arms; perception/attention and stimulus semantics specified; no reinforcement, hidden social feedback or context shift between steps; measurements themselves do not teach or change x. Each participant's entire trial block is one joint outcome. Independent participants or a separately justified dependence model are required for finite-data inference.
- **Reset:** neither human participants nor animals are assumed resettable. The algebra uses fresh blocks from the same H. Any within-person reset would need evidence; otherwise use independently randomized cohorts with a common sampling population.

These conditions are falsifiable model assumptions. If response emissions update learning, repeated observations cannot be used as frozen-state moment measurements.

### 5.3 Exact “answer without the hidden distribution” illustration

Write μ=E[x] and m₂=E[x²]. A single baseline emission identifies μ but not H or m₂. Under the stated frozen-emission contract, two joint baseline emissions identify m₂ through P(Y₁=Y₂=1). Direct substitution in the source maps gives

| Rule | q_B | q_b |
|---|---|---|
| C | μ+γ(μ−m₂) | μ−γ(μ−m₂) |
| N | μ+γ(1−μ) | (1−γ)μ |
| A | μ+γ(1−2μ+m₂) | μ−γm₂ |

Thus the one-step N prediction requires only the mean; C/A predictions require two moments under this measurement menu. **None requires recovering the full latent distribution or every learner's state.** The result is an elementary consequence of the published polynomial maps and classical moment reasoning, not a new general theorem.

For an exact interior-valued counterexample, fix γ=1/2 and compare H₀ concentrated at x=1/2 with H₁ equally mixing x=1/4 and x=3/4. Both have the same complete one-emission baseline law Bernoulli(1/2). Their second moments are 1/4 and 5/16. After one B demonstration their probabilities are

    C: 5/8 versus 19/32
    N: 3/4 versus 3/4
    A: 5/8 versus 21/32

This proves ambiguity of a specified future prediction from that *restricted baseline menu* for C/A. It simultaneously shows an identified prediction for N despite ambiguity about H. It makes no assertion of ambiguity under every richer observation law.

There is a second useful distinction. Starting at the same homogeneous x=1/2 with γ=1/2, C and A give identical baseline and one-demonstration B/b response laws: 1/2, 5/8, and 3/8. Their response after **BB** differs:

    C: 95/128;        A: 89/128;        gap=3/64

So the one-step answer can be identified across this named pair while the update-rule label remains unidentified under the shorter menu. The second demonstration gives a discriminating observation. This is an exact limited-menu example; the two mechanisms do not agree under all possible protocols.

For a horizon h, composition of C/A's quadratic maps is a polynomial of degree 2^h when γ>0; its expected response is therefore determined by moments up to that degree. N remains affine (or constant at γ=1), so its responses require at most μ. This is a **sufficiency observation**, not a claim that 2^h moments are necessary under every richer menu: directly sampling the target protocol may estimate q_w much more cheaply.

The accompanying [standard-library script](illustrative_moment_probe.py) checks the exact rational examples, both one-step moment formulas, and 1,800 polynomial point identities over horizons 1–4. [Receipt](illustrative-checks.json). These checks corroborate arithmetic; they do not validate the causal/emission model or identify empirical subjects.

### 5.4 Falsifiable probe and competing explanations

The smallest illustrative discriminator for the homogeneous C/A pair is a randomized **BB** arm, compared with baseline and B/b arms. The predicted task-specific response probabilities above can be checked with fresh participant blocks. The two frozen baseline emissions serve a different purpose: testing/estimating latent heterogeneity for a named update family. Pooling them as independent participants would be incorrect.

Preregister stimulus identity, exposure order, payoff, repetition, attention, variant salience, population sampling, and whether baseline measurement changes behavior. Retain full block-level joint observations. A held-out BB prediction is meaningful only if γ and source assumptions were calibrated without using its held-out outcome, or uncertainty in those inputs is carried into the prediction set.

Competing explanations include restricted attention to demonstrators, variant/content preference, reinforcement, latent memory of order/history, context-dependent γ, heterogeneous update rules, measurement-induced learning and social desirability. A successful pairwise C/A discrimination does not exclude these alternatives. An empirical protocol must compare their predicted responses, not treat C/A as exhaustive.

[Morgan et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21795267/) is primary human experimental prior art showing that demonstrator number/consensus, subject confidence, task difficulty and information costs can affect social-information use. Only its abstract/metadata were retrieved here. It supports task-specific feasibility questions; it does not validate our frozen-emission/reset contract or establish the selected equations as the evolved mechanism.

## 6. Match to PSY-1–4 and limits of the evolutionary explanation

The comparison uses the current [psychology catalog](https://github.com/Sodelin/Cross-Scale-Causal-Formalization/blob/main/research/open-problem-catalogs-2026-09-30/PSYCHOLOGICAL.md), not a relabeling of its tasks.

- **PSY-1:** shares intervention-target identification and causal-measurement concerns. A social demonstration is not a clinical symptom intervention. This illustration does not identify centrality-based treatment rankings.
- **PSY-2:** shares information/learning concerns, but the selected source lacks endogenous approach/avoidance policy selection, exposure learning and clinical context transfer. It is not the missing active-inference extension.
- **PSY-3:** the closest connection: short trajectories can agree while later responses differ, so source-matched delayed probes matter. BB here is not delayed fear relapse or a latent-cause clinical model. PSY-3 must keep its own published task, update equations and prospective outcome.
- **PSY-4:** no specific solution. Repeated binary behavior observations are not measures of metacognitive bias/efficiency and do not establish incremental dementia prognosis.

The cultural model addresses a **proximate response/update question** and a model-level population consequence. An evolutionary explanation needs additional evidence about selection, fitness trade-offs, developmental acquisition and historical/comparative alternatives. The distinction between causation, development, function and evolution is longstanding; [Tinbergen, 1963](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1439-0310.1963.tb01161.x) is the original conceptual source (publisher metadata and abstract inspected; full body unavailable here).

No mental mechanism, adaptation, intelligence, diagnosis or individual propensity is inferred from ancestry, a trait, graph resemblance, or a fitted population curve. No literal cross-scale isomorphism has been proved. A mathematical quotient that preserves one response target does not automatically preserve all intervention outcomes, social meanings, or biological history.

## 7. One prospective question, separate from the finish line

**PROSPECTIVE-CE-1, not G8 and not a novelty claim:** after selecting and validating a concrete behavioral task, fix its obtainable frozen-emission and social-stimulus menu, the horizon and target q_w. Carry unknown γ, heterogeneity and specified attention/measurement error jointly across arms. Which future response targets are constant on the resulting source-law fibers, and which remain robustly certifiable at the available block budget?

The calibrated-polynomial fragment may already be fully handled by classical moment/design methods; that is a reason to reuse it. The worthwhile unresolved work is the **task-specific law/probe/causal validity and noise boundary**, or a matched source-specific impossibility/design result after comparison with existing model-recovery work. Do not start a full new theorem lane merely to reprove polynomial expectation or a generic separating-query principle.
