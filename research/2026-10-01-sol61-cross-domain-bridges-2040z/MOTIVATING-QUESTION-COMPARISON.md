## Verdict

**Two different questions must be separated.** Later work supplies substantial positive answers to the broad 2024 unknown-blob-topology question for specific source/data regimes. It does not establish universal full topology recovery. The exact NANUQ distance/support conjectures recorded as the parent of Samuel's all-level package were posed in a **2025** paper, not the 2024 circular-order paper.

## 1. Recover the actual source before deciding status

Samuel's [SOURCE-RESULT-LEDGER at e2502c82](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/research/nanuq-all-level-2026-09-29/SOURCE-RESULT-LEDGER.md) explicitly identifies Holtgrefe et al., [*Distinguishing Phylogenetic Level-2 Networks with Quartets and Inter-Taxon Quartet Distances*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4), Theorem 4.7 and Section 6, as its distance/support source. The arXiv record [2507.17308](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17308) starts on 23 July 2025; the journal article was published 23 October 2025. Section 6 proposes whole-network level-two circularity/support, level-three bloblet circularity, and a parametric-distance extension. The current project theorem is a separate computer-assisted mathematical package, not a result already published in that source.

The genuine 2024 paper is Rhodes, Baños, Xu and Ané, [*Identifying circular orders for blobs in phylogenetic networks*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11693), first posted 18 February 2024 and revised 21 July 2024. Its abstract states: “An open question is the identifiability of the topology of a blob of unknown level.” That is a broad motivation, distinct from a universal NANUQ-metric conjecture.

## 3. Consequence for importance and novelty

For circular-order recovery or full topology within the published C4/C5 regimes, **reuse the existing result**. An expensive reconstruction detour may be unnecessary. For the precise NANUQ coefficient/support property, topology recovery and order identification are not substitutes: the particular metric can still require its own theorem. The source paper itself distinguishes the proposed distance route from existing TINNiK/tree-of-blobs information, and does not pitch it as first biological identifiability.

It is not full-source identification, CF-only reconstruction, or a sequence-calibration theorem.

**What can currently be said:** some ideas initially framed as new are already known; the broad 2024 motivation has strong later restricted answers and explicit earlier impossibility examples; the canonical project's named 2025 structural conjectures require an exact metric/support comparison.
