## Exact conditional composition

Let N and M be finite sources in the original strict natural proper-child-cut class of the accepted entire-I theorem, on the same original taxon set with at least four taxa. Suppose their entire natural-INDEPENDENT rooted labelled unranked completed-topology laws agree for every finite copy allocation. Then their full displayed-split unions agree.

Proof:

1. Apply the accepted entire-INDEPENDENT to entire-COMMON implication. The entire COMMON rooted topology profiles agree. This is a theorem implication, not permission to query COMMON experimentally.
2. In each finite source, condition on all natural COMMON parental choices. This gives a finite mixture of positive ordinary rooted population trees. Prune unused arcs, suppress unary subdivisions, and omit only the ordinary common stem above the displayed tree's taxon MRCA. These operations preserve the completed topology profile. Every switching has strictly positive probability because all inheritance probabilities are interior. Different switchings can give the same conditional tree and their positive weights then add.
3. Take the finite union H of the two conditional tree supports, identifying equal stem-free metric population trees. The whole-COMMON supplier proves linear independence of the full copy-allocation topology profiles of distinct trees in H. Subtracting the two finite-mixture profiles therefore makes every coefficient zero. Their positive tree supports coincide.
4. Forget metric lengths and the root of every supported tree and take the union of its splits. Every graph-displayed switching tree has positive weight and every supported tree comes from a switching. This operation is exactly S, so S(N)=S(M).

No generic parameter restriction, known source-size bound, or finite-copy cap is added by this composition. Repeated metric trees are grouped before invoking independence. Common-stem lengths cannot affect the unrooted split target.
