# Biological observations to the full all-level displayed-split union

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), for Nolan. 25 October 2026.
Status: new research contract and source-led scope map, not a completed classification.

## The original target and non-negotiable class

For a labelled taxon set X with |X| >= 4, let C(X) be the full class in the adopted all-level structural theorem: finite binary semi-directed LSA phylogenetic networks, outer-labelled planar and galled. There is no bound on finite reticulation level, blob number, or source size. Parallel occurrences, small cycles, fair inheritance, repeated numerical parameters and other admitted degeneracies remain admitted. For biological observations choose a compatible rooted-LSA realization, positive finite population exposures on original arcs, interior inheritance probabilities, and ordinary infinite ancestral completion. Keep one graph and one physical parameter tuple across every observation.

The target is S(N), the UNION of splits of all displayed trees, not the intersection, network isomorphism class, tree of blobs, circular order, or a selected best-fitting network. Both trivial and nontrivial splits are included. The exact displayed-quartet-set oracle and its accepted optimal query theorem are structural suppliers, not biological measurements. No frequency is silently treated as a displayed-quartet indicator.

## Observation contracts to classify separately

A fixed allocation is a specified vector m=(m_x:x in X) of nonnegative integer sample counts, fixed before seeing the data; each sampled individual has a distinct label and known species assignment. Repeated independent loci under that allocation do NOT increase m. A finite panel is finitely many such allocations, with all laws generated by the same source/tuple. An entire allocation law supplies all finite allocations; it is not a finite biological experiment.

1. Q1: exact quartet CFs, one sampled individual per taxon, under natural independent-lineage NMSC (I). Quartet summaries discard cross-quartet dependence within loci.
2. U(m): full unrooted, labelled, unranked gene-tree topology distribution at fixed allocation m. U(1) on four taxa equals its single quartet CF vector; on larger taxon sets it is stronger than the vector of quartet marginals.
3. R(m): full ROOTED labelled unranked topology distribution. Rooting is extra observation information; it is not assumed available from arbitrary nonclock DNA.
4. U/R finite panels versus the entire finite-allocation hierarchy. Distinguish target-dependent panel existence, a uniform copy cap, and an effective procedure that finds a sufficient panel from observations.
5. Metric genealogies: declare whether measured in generations, coalescent units or substitutions; specify population-size and mutation-rate nuisance parameters. Rank and time are not provided by topology observations.
6. DNA: a separate mandatory adapter below. Identifiability under a genealogy channel is not already a DNA theorem.

Natural I means each currently surviving lineage makes its own independent parental choice; a lineage carries its opaque previously merged genealogy. Natural COMMON is a different mechanism with one choice shared by all lineages at a hybrid/locus. Use COMMON only as an explicitly named comparison or justified mathematical intermediate, never as an experimental I query.

## Full deliverable and acceptance condition

Determine, for the above explicit observation schemes over the entire original class, whether equality of observations forces equality of S. Give an actual recovery map/proof where it does; otherwise provide an actual same-observation/different-S pair and delineate precisely the failed universal claim. Classify exceptional positive-parameter cases rather than deleting them. Establish which extra information changes the answer: copies per taxon, rootedness, larger gene-tree marginals, metrics, or sequencing assumptions.

A single admitted pair refutes a universally quantified scheme, but does not classify other schemes or identify every exceptional fibre. A positive generic or fixed-level branch is a component, not full closure. Weaker recoverable targets must be separately named. The supplied explicit quartet pair is the first negative component, not a replacement for this master.

The statistical layer must give honest finite-locus error/sample guarantees only on explicitly identified/separated subdomains, plus impossibility or lower bounds when separation fails. State whether loci are iid, block-independent or satisfy a proved dependence condition; do not silently count linked sites as independent loci. Reuse G6's existing robust-fibre framework rather than republishing it as a new theorem. G7 fixed-registry design is not unknown-source experimental design.

## Required sequence adapter

For any positive DNA claim declare the substitution model (e.g. finite-state JC/GTR with its actual clock/rate assumptions), treatment of invariant sites and rate heterogeneity, locus lengths, no within-locus recombination or an explicit recombination model, between-locus independence/linkage, missing data and gene-tree reconstruction error. Prove that the induced sequence law determines the required genealogy information, or derive a direct target decoder. Conditional iid sites on a genealogy do not make gene genealogies iid. An asymptotic infinite-sites-per-locus argument is not a finite-sequence bound. Fixed-family clock-JC/two-site MSCI results already in the corpus are not universal adapters.

## Source-led novelty and duplication map

- Allman, Ané, Baños and Rhodes (2025), Theorem 5.7, already prove arbitrary-level galled tree-child identifiability. Their C4/C5 restrictions, generic parameters, and one-versus-two samples matter. Reusing that theorem is not a first beyond-level-1 result, nor all-exceptional closure. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01545-8
- Allman, Baños, Mitchell and Rhodes (2023) supply the tree-of-blobs identifiability foundation, not all internal blob splits. The generic qualifications and contracted features must remain attached. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00285-022-01838-9
- Rhodes, Baños, Xu and Ané (2025), especially §7.2, study arbitrary-level outer-labelled planar circular orders and biological limitations. Their illustrated CF-indistinguishable networks have the same two displayed trees: that example alone is NOT an S-changing counterexample. https://arxiv.org/html/2402.11693v2
- Dai and Molloy (WABI 2026), NetCS, address level-1 blob reconstruction with the tree of blobs supplied. Their sorting/statistical bounds are pertinent prior work, not full-class biological-oracle recovery. https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.1
- Cummings et al. (2026), quintet CFs, provide a general n-tet probability algorithm but investigate five-taxon level-1 topological identifiability excluding 2-cycles. Their Proposition 4.1 gives an arbitrary-m-tet cherry/3-blob obstruction; it need not change S. Their algebraic distinguishability results are not all-parameter stochastic-image classification. https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03544v1
- The anomaly phenomenon and the exact small-cycle probability formulas are prior work: Ané et al. (2024), and Allman et al. (2024), Appendix B.2. The explicit cancellation witness in the companion proof is an attributed specialization, with historical novelty unresolved. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00285-024-02050-7 ; https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-024-01339-4
