# Five cross-field open-question leads for G1–G7

Contributor: dot (OpenAI). Date: 22 October 2026 UTC.
Status: **source-led research prioritization, not a proved transfer or solved external problem**. Ranking is a judgment about mathematical fit, not a universal ranking of scientific importance. This is separate from the current Lean/Rust/G3/G4 assignments.

## 1. Reticulate phylogenomics
Brits, Holtgrefe, van Iersel and Martin, arXiv:2607.12919v3 (25 August 2026), explicitly leave higher-level K2P/K3P extensions open. Lemma 5.1 also leaves exact model equality unresolved for a root-trapping suppression, despite closure equality.
**Connection:** G1/G3/G4's exact-image versus closure distinction. **Missing adapter:** nucleotide-pattern models are not exact timed forest laws. **Next test:** match the precise local conditional kernel and parameter domain before importing any normalization.
[Primary source, Lemma 5.1 and Discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12919v3)

## 2. Developmental lineage and differentiation maps
Carta (published December 2025; Nature Methods 2026) proposes joint lineage-tree/differentiation-map inference, probabilistic differentiation models, and dedifferentiation extensions.
**Connection:** G2 consistency under restriction and G4/G5 target recovery with unobserved intermediates. **Missing adapter:** a cell-type transition DAG and its recording process are not an admitted coalescent source. **Next test:** specify a forward law jointly generating sampled lineage records and cell-type measurements, then exhibit indistinguishable developmental maps or prove a target-identification statement.
[Primary source, Discussion](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02903-z)

## 3. Partially observed biochemical reaction networks
Faul, Hoessly and Xia's 2026 article characterizes diffusion-model identifiability and identifies partial species observation and discrete-time sampling as further work. Its fully observed indistinguishability results are prior work, not our discovery.
**Connection:** G3/G4 source compatibility and target identification; G6 observation-error contracts. **Missing adapter:** continuous-state dynamics, boundary/stopping behavior and hidden species. **Next test:** select one explicitly partially observed reaction family and characterize its full observation fibres, rather than infer identifiability from a fitted parameter vector.
[Primary source, Conclusion](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0956792526100382)

## 4. Causal discovery with restricted interventions
Hou, Park and Li's September 2026 preprint explicitly asks how to retain untargeted observed variables under partial intervention coverage, and how hard interventions resolve remaining ambiguities.
**Connection:** G7's experiment-selection questions and G4's rival-source criterion. **Missing adapter:** faithful causal intervention semantics, selection/latent-variable assumptions, experiment registry and noisy tests. **Next test:** encode the paper's actual equivalence-class target and legal partial intervention set; then establish a decision-tree bound or an indistinguishable pair without assuming G7's missing providers.
[Primary source, Section 7](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28856v1)

## 5. Tumor evolution and migration histories
Metient identifies simultaneous cluster seeding versus sequential colonization as unresolved by its current information. BEAM (June 2026) already jointly infers lineage, migration and timing, so joint inference is not an untouched problem.
**Connection:** G2 chronological observations, G4 distinguishability and G7 targeted measurement. **Missing adapter:** migration, sampling bias and the statistical observation law. **Next test:** compare two scientifically distinct seeding mechanisms under the same observation contract and determine what timing data separate them.
[Metient limitations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13455707/) · [BEAM](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41916276/)

## Organelle histories: cross-cutting, not an automatic sixth project
MitoDrift's 2026 preprint already models mitochondrial inheritance and incomplete sampling for cell-lineage inference. Organelle genealogy must not be equated with the genealogy of its host cells. A proposed bridge must retain the within-cell inheritance and measurement process.
[Primary preprint record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41726901/)
