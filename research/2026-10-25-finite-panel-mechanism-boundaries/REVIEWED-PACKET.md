## 4. Complete cap2 equivalence classification inside this chart

For two A lineages that have survived separately to tau, let S denote their being in the same deme and D their being in different demes. Independent routing gives

    p=P(S)=gamma^2+(1-gamma)^2 in [1/2,1].

The no-coalescence generator is

    K(c,m)=[[-c-2m, 2m], [2m, -2m]],
    alpha=(p,1-p).

Write u(h)=alpha exp(Kh)1 for no merger within elapsed window time h. Cayley–Hamilton gives

    u''+A u'+B u=0,
    A=c+4m, B=2cm, u(0)=1, u'(0)=-d, d=cp.

Its Laplace transform in h is

    U(z)=(z+A-d)/(z^2+A z+B).

There is NO numerator/denominator cancellation anywhere on c,m>0 and 1/2<=p<=1. The cancellation residual is

    B-d(A-d)=c[2m(1-2p)-cp(1-p)]<0.

Both terms are nonpositive; at p=1/2 the second is strictly negative, at p=1 the first is strictly negative, and in the interior at least one is strictly negative. Therefore equality of u on any positive-length interval—by analyticity, equality for all h—identifies the coprime monic denominator and numerator, hence exactly (A,B,d).

Solving c^2-Ac+2B=0 leaves at most two positive choices. The complete cap2 fibre is

    (c,m,p)  <->  (4m, c/4, cp/(4m)),

WHEN the partner p'=cp/(4m) also lies in [1/2,1]. Within each p, gamma is determined up to gamma<->1-gamma, the hidden-deme swap. If c=4m the two roots coincide and the twin is the same source parameters. If the partner is inadmissible, only the original representative remains. This is a full equality-fibre description for this supplied symmetric window chart, not a generic phase-type similarity assertion.

Why it classifies the complete cap2 G laws rather than only a local summary: before tau both sources have identical paths. If the two A lineages coalesced before tau, they form one current block; that block has no further merger opportunity with other taxa before sigma, whatever its unobserved route. If two survive, equality of u gives the complete distribution of their possible sole merger time in the window, including survival to sigma. After a window merger only one A block remains and its hidden location is irrelevant until the deterministic join. At sigma all surviving A blocks enter the same backend states, with the same already-recorded labelled timed forest distribution. Couple the unchanged other taxa identically and apply the same backend. This proves equality of the full joint labelled timed genealogy for every n_A<=2 panel in the declared interface.

The argument conditions only at the G-law proof level; it does not grant an experimental conditional-state oracle. Fresh pulse routing is independent per surviving block at tau, including a block formed by an earlier merger. No stochastic routing at time zero has been introduced.

## Preserved support-barrier proof

# A source-faithful no-recent-flow exclusion witness under a supplied partition/time barrier

Author: dot (OpenAI), 25 October 2026. Candidate mathematical implication and exact bounded illustration. Classical support, Laplace-transform and concentration reasoning; no novelty, practical recommendation, or complete mechanism classification.

## 1. Define the biological alternative before testing it

Fix observed population labels A,B and a supplied backward-time barrier S>0. In EVERY null history, their initial demes lie in different declared population compartments. Up to time S, all migration and boundary routing preserve the compartment, and no deterministic population join merges compartments. Each compartment may contain arbitrarily many unobserved demes, arbitrary positive finite piecewise-constant coalescence/migration rates, and arbitrary internal subdivision. Deme/epoch counts remain finite but unbounded across rivals. At and after S, ancestral structure, migration, joins and routing are unrestricted subject to the original absorption contract.

Equivalently, there is a supplied time-consistent colouring of demes before S, with A and B in different colours and every permitted lineage transition/join preserving colour. This is the precise null meaning of no communication between the declared compartments more recently than S; it is not the undefined phrase 'ancestral structure.' Ghost demes need not be sampled. The observed labels' placements on opposite sides and the time barrier are supplied scientific assumptions, not outcomes inferred from the same data.

Observe one copy from each of A,B at each nonrecombining locus, under the same known homogeneous stationary JC clock. Loci are independent when confidence bounds are invoked. Let s=8mu/3>0 in the supplied time units, and let T be their pair coalescence time.

Every null has T>=S almost surely: differently coloured lineages cannot occupy the same coalescing deme before S. Internal ancestral structure, arbitrary hidden size, and unknown epoch counts do not evade this support condition. The converse is NOT asserted: a history may violate the compartment rule without producing early coalescence in this particular observed pair.

## 2. Exact characterization of an observable support violation by finite site moments

For integer r>=1 define phi_r=E[exp(-rsT)]. From the complete r-site pair JC law this is the expectation of the product of r copies of a fixed nontrivial pair character. Hence every null satisfies

    phi_r <= exp(-rsS) for every r.

Conversely, suppose the target has P(T<S)>0. Then some t_*<S has q=P(T<=t_*)>0. For every r,

    phi_r >= q exp(-rs t_*).

Choose any integer

    r > log(1/q) / (s(S-t_*)).

Then phi_r>exp(-rsS). Thus the exact observable equivalence is

    P(T<S)>0  <=>  there exists a finite integer r with phi_r>exp(-rsS).

This is a positive witness for a support violation, not a total finite search deciding the negative case. It is target-dependent; no universal r is claimed as q approaches zero or t_* approaches S. The proof uses the actual pair sequence statistic directly rather than assuming an inferred perfect genealogy or sampling hidden populations.

Most importantly, any such violation excludes EVERY member of the specified no-recent-flow null class, including unknown-size structured rivals. It does not merely compare two fixed architectures. It identifies the necessity of some barrier-violating process, not whether that process was a pulse, continuous migration, a misplaced population boundary, or a failure of the supplied model assumptions.

## 3. A finite-data one-sided test with declared margins

Suppose a scientifically admitted target class supplies t_*<S and q>=q0>0. Select r so that

    Delta = q0 exp(-rs t_*) - exp(-rsS) >0.

Let Y be the r-site product character, Y in {-1,+1}. Average Y over N independent loci and reject the no-recent-flow class when

    Y_bar > exp(-rsS)+Delta/2.

Under EVERY null, the upper-tail error is <=exp(-N Delta^2/8), by Hoeffding for a variable of range two. Under EVERY target satisfying the early-mass margin, the lower-tail error has the same bound. Therefore N>=ceil(8 Delta^(-2) log(1/delta)) gives both errors <=delta. If arithmetic bounds are used operationally, thresholds and Delta must be conservatively enclosed; a floating sign estimate alone is not a certificate.

The cost is N independent genealogies, r sites shared within each genealogy, and 2rN nucleotide calls. This is not an adaptive sampling/linked-ARG theorem. Extremely weak early mass or a barrier gap approaching zero makes the certificate deteriorate; this is consistent with the prior pulse-to-fast-migration closure obstruction. That obstruction concerns pulse versus unrestricted migration, whereas this null forbids BOTH cross-compartment pulse and migration before S.

## 4. Actual admitted alternative and a rational illustration

    P(T<=t_*)=gamma[1-exp(-lambda(t_*-tau))].

All rates and durations are positive and finite; the pulse occurs after a positive initial epoch, not as undeclared initial stochastic sampling. The observed B lineage provides access to the donor-side ancestry. Root absorption can be supplied by a common ancestor at S. More elaborate alternatives may imply an early-mass bound too, but it must be justified rather than assumed from the word 'introgression.'

For s=lambda=1, gamma=1/2, tau=log2, t_*=2log2 and S=3log2, the early mass is q=1/4. At r=3,

    phi_3(target)>=1/256,
