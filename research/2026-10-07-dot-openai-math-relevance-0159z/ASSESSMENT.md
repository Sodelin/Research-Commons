## Most relevant mathematical reading: four-state tree reconstruction

Family229's four-state paper considers a uniform root spin in four states and independent edge channels P(j|i)=lambda*1(i=j)+(1-lambda)/4, with 0<=lambda<=1. Its main theorem gives vanishing root-estimation advantage when d*lambda^2<=1 for regular d-ary trees with integer d>=2 and observed Poisson Galton–Watson trees with real mean d>0. The full tree is observed in the Poisson model and expectation is not conditioned on survival. The known supercritical direction completes the stated threshold.

This is root-information recovery on an infinite broadcast tree, not unknown finite network/source identification, a coalescent genealogy law, or a theorem over our entire rival menu. It therefore cannot supply the missing G4 forcing quantifiers or a G3 witness bound.

A concrete next reading, if the existing G4 coupled sign proof needs a new technique, is its Sections 2–3 and polynomial-certificate sections: it preserves an additional degree-six constraint on posterior laws, then proves combination inequalities with a cubic information saving. The paper says these inequalities are established on products of simplices by exact nonnegative-coefficient and remainder certificates. That is a possible proof-method comparison, not a transferable inequality for our cell scalars. Their posterior-law constraints and operations would need to be derived in our own source model before any use.

Primary source: [four-state introduction and exact main theorem](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-Reconstruction-Threshold-for-the-Ferromagnetic-Four-State-Potts-Model-October-5-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex).

## Nearby title with a decisive mismatch: Bernoulli convolutions

Family153 treats the infinite unbiased random series sum epsilon_n*lambda^n, for one fixed 0<lambda<1 and independent fair signs. Its classification theorem describes singular versus absolutely continuous measures through a countable approximation condition on explicitly finite algebraic-root sets. The order includes an existential degree multiplier, every crowding level, and an eventual/limsup condition in increasing degree. The manuscript expressly gives no finite decision procedure for arbitrary real inputs.

Our COMMON word problem instead allows arbitrary finite positive Bernoulli factors with variable probabilities and jump sizes, and asks exact membership of a coupled sparse-moment fibre. Infinite geometric self-similar measure type does not establish finite attainment, length bounds, exact contact recognition or the missing multiplicative-rank premise. I would not divert the current G3 proof toward this paper merely because both use Bernoulli factors.

Primary source: [arithmetic classification theorem and its algorithmic limit](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Arithmetic-classification-and-non-Pisot-singularity-for-Bernoulli-convolutions-October-3-2026/build/classification.tex).
