# Family116: a bounded ordered-identity checking candidate

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 7 October 2026, 02:07 UTC. This is a post-02:03 source-reading addendum. No import, build, checker or scientific computation was performed.

The actual theorem [OAI.NCHitting.universal_hitting](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/lean/OAI/Algebra/FormulaHitting/Hitting.lean) takes n,s>=1, a characteristic-zero field F and a division-free noncommutative formula f in n variables with size at most s. If some word coefficient is nonzero, evaluation on its explicit rational generator matrices is nonzero over F. Its direct dependencies include Multiplicity, DimensionBound, SystemOutput, SeriesFaithfulness and JetMatrix. We have read the selected interface and proof body, not audited or rebuilt that dependency chain.

The selected [family116 formalization scope](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/lean/docs/116.md) distinguishes this universal hitting property from the paper's separately stated polynomial bit-construction and O(n*s^2) dimension claims. Those additional bounds are not separately asserted by that selected theorem.

The rational-formula result [OAI.RationalHitting.main](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/lean/OAI/Computability/RationalHitting/Main.lean) has a stronger executable interface. The [exact MainStatement target](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/lean/ComparatorChallenges/RationalHitting.lean) uses rational constants, addition, multiplication and inverse gates. Evaluation of an inverse requires an actual two-sided inverse. Admissibility and nonzero status are witnessed by a positive-dimensional rational matrix evaluation. The theorem's output list must hit every admissible nonzero formula within the bound at an invertible value while retaining every intermediate inverse domain. One fixed deterministic finite-state Turing machine has polynomial bounds on common matrix dimension, full binary output and time in n+s+1, with literal unary n,s input. The selected solution builds through OutputComputation. The challenge's placeholder proof specifies the comparison target and is not itself the solution certificate.

These are concrete candidates for verifying a fully specified, bounded-size ordered operator identity, after its exact free-formula representation and size bound have been justified. Our INDEPENDENT chronological products must retain their noncommuting order; a free noncommutative test can be conceptually relevant there. But a polynomial may vanish on our restricted physical source family while being nonzero on arbitrary matrices. Conversely, an arbitrary signed rational test matrix is not a legal positive stochastic experiment or admitted source. Therefore these hitting results do not by themselves establish a physical rival, detect a legal observable discrepancy, supply an unknown-word bound or decide the original G3/G4 target fibre.

The same release-version and license boundary from the Lean interface addendum applies: Lean4.34.1, Mathlibd13f23b7 and Apache2.0, versus our pinned4.33.1/0df444a3 chain. A future actual use needs a small attributed source/dependency adaptation and separate verification. No such use is proposed until there is a concrete bounded identity obligation.
