# Master scope: characterized foundations and remaining obligations

Scope audit, 2026-09-30; reviewed the master mathematical packet and constructive continuation. Detailed proofs have separate reviews. No GitHub changes or novelty claim.

**Verdict:** the packet characterizes several strongest precisely declared mathematical problems. It does not solve an unrestricted promise to discover, implement or improve transfer between every scientific domain. “Foundation complete; application obligations remain” is defensible after proof review. “Full scientific-transfer problem solved” is not.

## Precise all-instance claims

| Admitted category | What is characterized or certified |
|---|---|
| Arbitrary sets; fixed representation and entire declared target family | Exact decoding iff every observation fiber has one joint answer. Relational transfer iff every constrained code has one answer across all admitted links. All separating questions force injectivity. |
| Metric answers with simultaneous center selection | Uniform epsilon-decoding iff each fiber’s intersection of radius-epsilon balls has a center. The optimal radius is an infimum; attainment needs its own premise. |

| Arbitrary measurable source, one probability/sigma-finite dominating measure for the whole family, nonempty standard-Borel target | Ordinary measurable kernel deficiency attains its minimum and equals the worst finite-prior, finite-action bounded Bayes-risk disadvantage. One kernel gives all-parameter bounded-risk transfer. |
| Fixed ordinary kernel with source setwise continuity, target weak continuity and a full-support prior | An almost-everywhere uniform TV bound extends to every parameter. This certifies an existing kernel; it does not construct one from a single prior test. |
| Specified consistent path conditionals, uniformly close at every history | Entire countable path-law TV is bounded by one minus the infinite product of conditional agreement budgets. This does not infer causal or adaptive admissibility of the simulator. |
| Explicit finite measurement tables | Exact adaptive query-budget recursion yields an optimal policy or budget obstruction. Arbitrary-set recursion is an existence criterion, not a terminating algorithm. |

These are conditional all-instance statements within their categories. Domination, available measurements, justified domain links and admissible interventions remain premises even when the parameter set includes every declared level.

## Stronger universal claims already refuted

Many-to-one compression cannot preserve every possible question. Unconstrained unknown cross-domain links cannot identify a nonconstant target. Equal observation laws with different answers defeat any sample size and downstream reasoning. Matching component marginals does not establish a full joint-law simulator. Similar geometry or unforced dynamics does not establish intervention transfer. Small one-step errors alone do not establish a small infinite-horizon error. Full-support priors alone can hide exceptional parameters.

Undominated generalized comparison does not universally produce an ordinary measurable kernel. The non-Borel-target example has abstract deficiency zero and ordinary deficiency one half; stronger regular-space published counterexamples are separately recorded. No total procedure decides exact transfer for every program presenting total computable observations and targets, by the bounded-halting reduction. This refutes unrestricted automation; it does not refute restricted decidable grammars.

**Four different endpoints:** abstract positive transition; ordinary measurable Markov kernel; computable sampling/decoding program; finite-data, finite-cost certificate. None should silently stand for the next. A nonconstructive kernel existence proof does not establish executable or physically available simulation.

## Specific scientific obligations genuinely remaining

Fix the application’s full source class, obtainable joint observation experiment, target answers, admissible parameter correspondence and intervention menu. Then prove the observation fibers respect those targets, or give a class-matched obstruction and the extra-information boundary. Supply identifiable regimes, uniform separation or abstention, dependence-aware finite-sample coverage, robustness to model/link uncertainty, and measurement/computation costs. Passive conditional invariance is insufficient for causal effects; transport needs identified and aligned intervention responses. Strict improvement additionally needs a baseline and loss criterion.

The biological observation-to-displayed-support bridge remains a separate application obligation. Its unresolved positive regimes are not settled by general comparison; its proved collisions should remain negative regimes rather than be renamed open questions. Other domains require equally concrete bridge statements.
