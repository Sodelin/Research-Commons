## Design/control priors beyond phylogenetic terminology

**Addanki–Kasiviswanathan–McGregor–Musco2020**, ICML, *Efficient Intervention Design for Causal Discovery with Latents*: Lemma3.1p4 characterizes necessity of strong-separating systems for ancestral-graph recovery. The cost setup/preceding discussion shows touching every observed variable necessary when unrestricted linear-cost interventions are allowed; Theorem3.3 gives a2-approximation with m≥constant·logn and stronger constrained regimes later. The system assumes causal Markov and observational/interventional faithfulness, with an exact conditional-independence oracle. Interventions are on observed variables. This is prior latent-aware design/set-system/cost machinery, not the actual original-hybrid-parent/gene-law Q/S oracle or union-of-ever-touched-site cost. [Primary PDF](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/addanki20a/addanki20a.pdf).

**Akbari–Etesami–Kiyavash2022**, ICML, *Minimum Cost Intervention Design for Causal Effect Identification*: known semi-Markovian graph, all positive compatible models, exact full observed/interventional distributions, additive per-query variable costs, and one specified causal effect. Lemma1 collapses a collection to its union in its single-c-component/avoid-target regime. Actual implementation exists. Functional target-directed optimal design, allowed-actuator restrictions, latent handling and exact optimum/obstruction are prior ideas. Unknown original phylogenetic graph, parent labels, coarsened genealogy readout, and distinct sites/configuration/programme costs are not their contract. [Published primary](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/akbari22a.html), [author PDF](https://www.sakbari.com/papers/minimum-cost-intervention-design/paper.pdf), [implementation](https://github.com/SinaAkbarii/min_cost_intervention).
