- L:Y×Y→Nat with every L(i,j)>0;

- coefficients c(i,j,k), defining p_ij(x)=sum over k in momentLevels(L(i,j)) of c(i,j,k)x^(k choose 2).

Assume for each directed pair (i,j):

1. p_ij is a nonzero polynomial;
2. p_ij(x)≥0 for every x∈[0,1];
3. sum_k c(i,j,k)y(i,j,k)=0.

These are supplied certificate premises, not assertions that a certificate can be found for every original G3 input. Coefficients may be real for the semantic theorem. Effective certificate checking requires an exact supported coefficient representation; an algebraic representation is a separate algorithmic interface.

Define S=max over all (i,j) of (L(i,j)−1), with natural-number subtraction, and

B=(2n−2+2h)+(8n−8+6h)(S−1).
