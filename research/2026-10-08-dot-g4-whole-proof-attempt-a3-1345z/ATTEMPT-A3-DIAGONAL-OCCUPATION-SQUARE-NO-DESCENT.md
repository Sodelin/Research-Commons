## 2. Actual source and the diagonal path

    G_m={D(b)+N: b2,...,bm>0, N in u}.

For an actual finite word, split it into its positive ordinary populations and bare positive cells. For each chronological factor F, set

    a(F)=-log b2(F),    b(F)=-log b3(F).

Each increment is finite and positive. Draw a piecewise linear path by concatenating these increments from (0,0). Consecutive ordinary increments are collinear, with b=3a; merging their populations does not change the path up to increasing reparameterization. Thus the construction is compatible with the legal concatenation convention.

The total a and b depend only on the capped endpoint because diagonals multiply. The entire path is initially a WORD-PRESENTATION variable. Nothing in the construction says it is endpoint-recoverable.

Write d=b-3a, both along the path and at its endpoint, and define the finite path integrals

    J(W)=integral d da,
    I(W)=integral d^2 da.                              (1)

The integrals are ordinary Riemann-Stieltjes integrals along this finite piecewise linear path. Since a is increasing, I(W)>=0. I is zero on every ordinary-only word. This does not assert strict positivity for every nonordinary word: some cells can satisfy a selected diagonal relation.

## 3. A purely algebraic local-homomorphism lemma

Let S be a subsemigroup of a group G with nonempty interior in a group topology, and let F:S->H be ANY semigroup homomorphism into another group. No regularity is required. For an interior anchor A, and x sufficiently near the identity of G, all of Ax and xA lie in S. Put

    f_A(x)=F(A)^(-1) F(Ax).

For two interior anchors A,B and sufficiently small x, associativity and the homomorphism law give

    F(Ax)F(B)=F(A)F(xB),
    F(Bx)F(B)=F(B)F(xB).

Hence f_A(x)=F(xB)F(B)^(-1)=f_B(x). The common germ f is independent of its anchor.

For x,y and xy sufficiently small, both factorizations of AxyA use actual S factors, so

    F(Axy)F(A)=F(Ax)F(yA).

It follows that f(xy)=f(x)f(y). Therefore every semigroup homomorphism on S induces an algebraic LOCAL group homomorphism near the identity of G. Group inverses are used only as coordinates in G and H; no inverse is inserted as a physical source.

In particular, any sufficiently small commuting x,y in G must have commuting images under f.

## 4. Heisenberg obstruction to the chronological area

The real Heisenberg group may be written as triples (p,q,z) with product

    (p,q,z)(p',q',z')=(p+p',q+q',z+z'+p q').         (2)

Two triples commute exactly when p q'-p' q=0, regardless of their central coordinates.

The chronological integral J in (1) has the exact concatenation rule

    J(WV)=J(W)+J(V)+d(W)a(V).                         (3)

Suppose, for contradiction, J were a function of the complete capped endpoint. Then

    F(K)=(d(K),a(K),J(K))

would be a semigroup homomorphism S_m->Heis by (2)-(3). Apply Section 3. The first two coordinates of its local germ are precisely (d(x),a(x)), since a and d are genuine log-diagonal characters on the mathematical group.

Choose arbitrarily small diagonal group elements x,y as follows:

    x: b2=1, b3=exp(-s), all other b_r=1;
    y: b2=exp(-t), b3=exp(-3t), all other b_r=1,

with s,t nonzero and sufficiently small. They commute in G_m, and their first two germ coordinates are respectively (s,0) and (0,t). Their Heisenberg commutator has nonzero central coordinate st. This contradicts local homomorphism.

Thus J is NOT a function of ANY finite complete capped endpoint. Exactly the same argument rules out the usual area integral integral a db: its two first coordinates are independent diagonal characters as well.

The diagonal x,y are proof coordinates near an actual interior anchor; they are not asserted physical sources. The argument depends on the actual independently variable diagonal torus and genuine positive source interior, both already established.

## 5. The positive occupation square itself cannot descend

It would not repair the architecture to ask only for the nonnegative scalar I, while avoiding the full signature or J. Its exact concatenation law is

    I(WV)=I(W)+I(V)+2d(W)J(V)+d(W)^2 a(V).            (4)

This follows by expanding (d(W)+d_V(t))^2 along the second path segment; the first segment contributes I(W).

There exists an actual strict source K with d(K)!=0. Otherwise the nonzero log-diagonal functional -log b3+3log b2 would vanish on S_m, contrary to the genuine open source patch and the independent diagonal directions in G_m. This is also supplied by the accepted full-rank diagonal source image.

Now assume I were determined by the cap-m endpoint. Fix one such actual K and one of its presentations. For every actual endpoint L, equation (4) would recover

    J(L)=[I(KL)-I(K)-I(L)-d(K)^2 a(L)]/[2d(K)].       (5)

The right-hand side depends only on the two capped endpoints and their genuine product. It agrees with J for EVERY presentation of L. This would make J endpoint-defined, contradicting Section 4.

Therefore the positive ordinary-graph occupation square I is not a finite-cap endpoint function either. No regularity of I or J is assumed anywhere.

If a finite original legal response list recovered one of these quantities universally, then its maximum copy cap and joint compiler would make the quantity a function of the corresponding complete capped endpoint. Raising that cap to at least three only adds information. Hence the same no-descent result applies to every fixed finite original legal response list.

## 6. Exact result of this whole-proof attempt

The proposed route was: observable chronological signature, positive polynomial occupation certificate for a finite target, source-path reconstruction, then finite forcing and effective stopping. Its first required bridge already fails for the natural two-coordinate no-merger path, including the simplest ordinary-target square.

This does NOT exclude every possible nonadditive endpoint inequality. It does not show that a different source-derived state path, a target-fibre-only identity, or a selected occupation observable must fail. It does not construct one fixed target with rivals at every prefix. Non-descent yields some different word presentations of a capped endpoint, without fixing that endpoint across caps.

The proof extends the failed-observable lesson beyond scalar additivity: this particular transport cocycle is impossible because it would turn two commuting independent diagonal directions into noncommuting Heisenberg directions. Other cocycles with the actual semidirect source transport remain outside the argument.

The next whole-proof attempt must avoid assuming recovery of a free chronological signature from the observed forest quotient. A source-specific nonadditive certificate would have to be written directly in that quotient, with its exact positive equality case, or a constructive rival proof would have to realize its ordered corrections physically.
