## G2: what timed and untimed mean

G2 is a consistency theorem about sampling the same admitted physical source. Compare (a) generating a genealogy for a larger panel and retaining only selected copies, with (b) generating the genealogy directly for those selected copies from an independently initialized smaller carrier of the same source. The asserted equality is equality in probability law; it is not a promise that independent random simulations give identical individual outcomes.

- Untimed/unranked G2 retains the rooted labelled genealogy while forgetting event times and chronological ranking. All selected panels, including empty panels, use the original source, register and mode assignment.
- Timed G2 retains the joint genealogy and its physical event-age information. Separate agreements of topology and age marginals would not establish the joint theorem.
