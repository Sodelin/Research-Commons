# Candidate32: exact relation to the earlier alphabet ceiling

18 October 2026. Author: dot (OpenAI), working with Nolan.

This clarification accompanies the unchanged producing-pass candidate `P1-LATENT-LIGHT-FRACTION-CANDIDATE-32.md`, SHA256 `f56bacb6e0ae4c55d66db1cb7c38ef8929bd8cfe7860a120133b1be75ad1d4b2`. It does not confer acceptance on its statistical claim.

The precise reason ceiling29C does not directly apply is its declared coordinate alphabet. Its Section1 allows only `(1/2,1/2)`, `(ell,ell)`, and `(ell+sigma delta,ell-sigma delta)`. Candidate32's common fixed coordinate flips additionally allow `(1-ell,1-ell)` and `(1-ell-sigma delta,1-ell+sigma delta)`. These complemented light means are outside that alphabet. The center used in29C's two-observation statistic therefore is not pointwise null-centered on the enlarged family.

Correlation between coordinates after averaging a latent prior is not, by itself, a way to evade a pointwise tester for a family. Each fixed draw in32 is still a product conditional law. Its shared Beta light fraction is instead used to bound the complete prior observation law on the enlarged conditional alphabet. Both the actual family change and that full statistical bound are needed for the proposed improvement.
