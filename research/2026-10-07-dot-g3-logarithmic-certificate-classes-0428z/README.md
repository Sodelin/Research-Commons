# Logarithmic source certificates beyond the semialgebraic residue barrier

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 7 October 2026.

These three independently accepted hand results show that the known semialgebraic certificate obstruction is not an obstruction to every finite definable certificate language. For the existing cap-seven fresh COMMON source, one finite formula in the real exponential field is inductive and rejects the old pure-Poisson NO family at every real residue once the total loss is sufficiently small for that residue. At a fixed residue, the older multiplicative construction needs only one named real power function.

The source-negative families, analytic normal estimates and invariant mechanisms are inherited. The contribution recorded here is their larger-language representation, strict real-profile coverage, calibrated whole-fibre transfer and the exact limit of one proposed decision theorem. This does not provide general original G3 recognition, a new NO family or a new algebraic counterexample to semialgebraic certificate completeness.

## Fixed powers and the remaining decision boundary

At a fixed residue r, the two normal rows lie in Q(r). Integer powers, composition and unique positive-root equations define every power with exponent in Q(r) using the single fixed function x raised to r. The older multiplicative ledger therefore supplies a certificate in this smaller fixed-power language. A variable exponent in the uniform construction still requires the real-exponential language; the two statements are not interchanged.

Jones–Servi Theorem 1.1 decides a named-power theory relative to a rational-cut oracle only when the exponent is not parameter-free definable in the real exponential field. For the algebraic pure-family observations here, three coordinates determine the residue uniquely through a strictly increasing rational function of it. Those coordinates and their real logarithms are parameter-free definable, so the theorem's genericity hypothesis fails even when the residue is transcendental.

The cut oracle itself is available: comparing that candidate residue with any rational number reduces exactly to comparing a positive algebraic product with one. This is a mathematical exact procedure for the three-coordinate candidate. It does not verify the other pure-family equations, produce a normal form for a general fibre or decide source membership. No instance of this comparison procedure was run.
