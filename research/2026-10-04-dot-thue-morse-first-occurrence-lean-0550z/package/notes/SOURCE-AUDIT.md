# Sources and priority limits

Checked 4 October 2026. This is a bounded primary-source check, not a complete citation census.

1. Joshi–Rust, v2, 11 June 2025, Definitions 2.3 and 2.5, §3.2.2, Conjecture 3.8: https://arxiv.org/html/2501.05830v2 . The latest version record remains v2 at https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05830 . It explicitly asks for all three first-occurrence formulas. Its preceding paragraph supplies m≥2 for the length statements; the displayed first-occurrence conjecture omits explicit small-index qualifications. The plus family m=1 must be excluded.
2. Aedo–Grimm–Nagai–Staynova, TCS 934 (2022), 65–80, Lemma 20, Theorem 21 and Proposition 22: https://oro.open.ac.uk/84734/15/1-s2.0-S0304397522004868-main.pdf . Full primary PDF read. Lemma 20 already supplies the two block alignments used here. The length proof uses superword recognition and diagonal/antidiagonal geometry. Our proof is closely based on these classical ideas. The extra step is retaining enough location information to classify every maximal start.
3. Parshina, *On arithmetic index in the generalized Thue–Morse word*, WORDS 2017; author preprint uploaded 9 November 2018: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.03884 . The abstract, definitions, and length/carry sections were inspected. Its “arithmetic index” concerns the least difference for a prescribed arithmetic word, not the least starting position for a fixed difference. Do not treat the shared word “index” as an identity of problems. Aedo et al. separately credit Parshina's WORDS 2015 paper for the minus-family maximal lengths; that earlier full text was not independently obtained here.
4. OEIS A342827, Jeffrey Shallit (23 March 2021): https://oeis.org/A342827 . Primary sequence page read. The d=3 example confirms i(3)=45, excluding the m=1 plus formula. It cites Aedo et al., Joshi–Rust and Parshina, and links Aedo's data through d=2048. Data are not used as proof.

Targeted follow-up queries included the exact conjecture number, author names, “Thue-Morse first occurrence”, “earliest progression 2026”, and the paper title/formulas. No solving primary sequel was located. An Emergent Mind problem page still reproduces the conjecture, but this is a secondary discovery lead and does not establish open status. The official published Joshi–Rust PDF endpoint timed out and the DOI endpoint was inaccessible through the retrieval tool; the complete accepted author v2 was read instead. The 2025 Joshi thesis was inspected for relevant updates and did not supply a resolution of these three Thue–Morse formulas.

The exact mathematical proof may be accepted independently of priority. No claim is made that no prior author had noticed these short consequences, and no author has been contacted. No Lean verification is asserted. The complete all-start classifications and their shared three-letter condition are results of this packet; their worldwide novelty is unassessed.
