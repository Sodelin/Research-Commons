## 1. Exact cancellation, retaining the full filters

Use the independent on-site Pauli baths and finite-dimensional Hamiltonian class of the [reviewed supplier04](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/44ace19b8deed339e992c4ca6a6e07ca8c152bbe/research/2026-10-16-p5-strong-noise-coarse-graining/P5-SHORT-WINDOW-CARDINALITY-SUPPLIER-04.md). Fix the on-site Hamiltonian H0 and a finite set E of interaction terms. For F subset E, let

    H_F = H0 + sum_(e in F) h_e,
    K_(i,a),F = integral_0^infinity Phi_(i,a)(s)
                 exp(-i H_F s) A_(i,a) exp(i H_F s) ds,
    R_F(O) = i[H_F,O] + sum_(i,a)
             ([A_(i,a),O] K_(i,a),F - K_(i,a),F^dagger [A_(i,a),O]),
    T_F(t) = exp(t R_F).

All integrals remain infinite. The exponential envelope makes each finite-volume filter well defined.

For every connected component C of the interaction hypergraph F, H_F factorizes over components. A coupling anchored at i in C evolves entirely inside C. Thus K_(i,a),F and its Redfield channel act only within C, R_F is a sum of commuting component generators, and each component generator annihilates its identity. Consequently T_F(t)O_X depends only on components meeting the initial support X. This is an exact algebraic statement; it uses neither complete positivity nor a norm bound on T_F.

Define the finite inclusion-exclusion increment

    D_E(t;O_X) = sum_(F subset E) (-1)^(|E|-|F|) T_F(t)O_X.

If a component of E containing an edge is disjoint from X, select one edge e in that component. In the sum above, pair each F not containing e with F union {e}. These two evolutions agree on O_X, since neither can connect that component to X. Their signs are opposite, so D_E=0.

Finite inclusion-exclusion also gives

    T_L(t)O_X = sum_(E subset L) D_E(t;O_X)

for any finite interaction set L. For a one-site X, only edge sets connected to that site survive. For a multi-site X, several disconnected components may survive provided every component meets X. Requiring the entire edge set to be connected would incorrectly discard some terms.

This establishes an exact rooted organization of the actual full-filter dynamics. It supplies no volume-uniform summability or all-time integrability of the surviving increments. Spatially correlated baths joining separate components would require a correspondingly enlarged interaction structure; they are not silently included here.

## 2. What cancellation does in the exact note22 benchmark

Retain the independent two-qubit blocks and notation of [reviewed note22](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/b740a3a1725b569a6061a1ce78bc12c0a6b79e11/research/2026-10-16-p5-strong-noise-coarse-graining/P5-REPAIRED-REFERENCE-TRANSFER-OBSTRUCTION-22.md): P_b=Z_(b,1)X_(b,2), H=J sum_b P_b, C=integral Phi(s)cos(2Js)ds, D=integral Phi(s)sin(2Js)ds, r=Re C. Its exact formula is

    R(P_b) = -lambda_R P_b + mu I,
    lambda_R = 16r,    mu = 16 Im D.

For the admitted bath with inverse temperature a, its two-sided spectrum satisfies J_bath(-2J)=exp(-2aJ)J_bath(2J). The conventions in the preserved proof give

    J_bath(2J) = 2r - 2 Im D,
    J_bath(-2J) = 2r + 2 Im D.

The explicit spectrum is strictly positive. Hence r>0 and

    mu/lambda_R = -tanh(aJ) =: z,    |z|<1.

On the commuting algebra generated by P_b,

    T_t(P_b) = exp(-lambda_R t) P_b
               + z(1-exp(-lambda_R t)) I.

This is precisely a classical two-state Markov kernel with stationary mean z. The full restriction is the product of these kernels. Write a diagonal observable as a function f(p_1,...,p_M), p_b in {-1,1}, and let osc_b(f) be the maximum difference obtained by flipping p_b. The four anticommuting Pauli axes in block b each have commutator norm osc_b(f), while the other two contribute zero. Therefore

    I(f) = 4 sum_b osc_b(f),
    I(T_t f) <= exp(-lambda_R t) I(f),
    integral_0^infinity I(T_t f)dt <= I(f)/lambda_R.

The second inequality follows because the b-coordinate difference receives exactly exp(-lambda_R t), and every other coordinate kernel is a supremum-norm contraction.

Thus the very same growing-support diagonal observables used in note22 have a uniformly bounded *actual* response ratio, despite their divergent reference-transfer ratio. This checks that a large intermediate absolute estimate can be repaired by exact resummation in that benchmark. It neither makes the full two-qubit Redfield map positive nor proves a connected noncommuting result. For fixed X, component cancellation simply leaves finitely many independent blocks.

## 3. A genuinely nonpositive prior, and its precise import boundary

Primary source: Thiago Bergamaschi, *Fast mixing of all-to-all quantum systems at high temperatures*, [arXiv:2606.26090v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26090v1), 24 June 2026; [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.26090v1), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26090v1). Checked 17 October 2026. PDF page references below count the first PDF page as page 1.

The source's non-CP auxiliary generator has imaginary-time Gibbs conjugates in its anchored channels (Eq.8, p.7). Its KMS oscillator seminorm uses the Gibbs-weighted 2-norm (Eqs.19,62). Lemma4.7 and Remark4.9 (pp.19–20, Eqs.69–75) use contraction of deleted-site evolutions in that norm. Theorem5.1 (p.23, Eq.94) compares Dirichlet forms with the specified CKG Gaussian-filtered sampler. Lemma3.4 (pp.13–15, Eqs.32,42–47) bounds each connected filter cluster by a product of exp(2|s| ||h_e||)-1; Lemma3.10 (p.16, Eq.51) controls the rooted sum only at short complex time.

**Our applicability check:** an actual one-sided bath integral K_(i,a),F is not identified with the prescribed imaginary-time Gibbs conjugate. Bath KMS alone does not supply that identity, Gibbs detailed balance of R_F, or contraction of every deleted-site evolution. Even a weighted-2-norm gap would require a further local, volume-uniform conversion to the operator-norm response against arbitrary initial states. A global finite-dimensional norm comparison is insufficient. These are generator and norm obligations, not an objection based merely on nonpositivity. This pass checked the cited definitions, statements and the contraction step; it did not independently verify every proof in the paper.

## 4. The positive cluster majorant fails even with the actual admitted bath

This calculation concerns the particular positive bound just cited, not the true signed cluster coefficients. For a cluster of r terms each of norm J, that bound is (exp(2Js)-1)^r. Merely replacing |Phi| by an upper envelope cannot establish divergence of an actual-bath-weighted integral. Here the admitted example supplies a lower tail as well.

For the already reviewed covariance

    Phi(s) = c_b/(2a) sech[pi(s+i a/2)/(2a)],
    kappa = pi/(2a),    tauB = 1/kappa,

the exact identity |cosh(x+i pi/4)|^2=cosh(2x)/2 implies, for s>=0,

    c_b/(sqrt(2)a) exp(-kappa s)
        <= |Phi(s)| <= c_b/a exp(-kappa s).

For s >= log(2)/(2J), exp(2Js)-1 >= exp(2Js)/2. Consequently

    integral_0^infinity |Phi(s)| (exp(2Js)-1)^r ds
        >= [c_b/(sqrt(2)a)] 2^(-r)
           integral_(log(2)/(2J))^infinity exp[(2Jr-kappa)s]ds
        = infinity,                  if 2Jr >= kappa.

Arbitrarily long chains admit such connected sets with fixed body size, degree and J. To check that this can coexist with the preserved strong-noise conditions, take H=J sum_j Z_j Z_(j+1). Its incidence strength is h<=2J; the commuting-neighbourhood constants can be taken m=3 and nu=3. The earlier margin becomes

    g >= 8 b0 gamma - 4J - 1152 gamma J tauB.

For fixed admitted bath parameters, choose J>0 sufficiently small to make this positive. If the explicit small gamma*tauB box is imposed as well, choose the bath amplitude to meet it first, then choose J. Neither choice depends on chain length or cluster order. The bath positive-type/KMS and noise-floor verification is inherited from the [reviewed original bath](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/43c6d81f0ae5ee1f233e9fe56acca1e9a0c4d05c/research/2026-10-16-p5-strong-noise-coarse-graining/P5-STRONG-NOISE-COMMUTING-PAULI-CANDIDATE.md), with gamma=2 sqrt(2)c_b/pi and b0=pi/(4 sqrt(2)).

This commuting chain makes the limitation especially clear. At an endpoint, the true evolved X_1 depends only on J Z_1 Z_2. All other commuting edge evolutions cancel exactly, so its actual filter stays on two sites; the corresponding larger edge-set increments vanish. Nevertheless the unrefined positive bound above diverges. Therefore the displayed infinity cannot be interpreted as divergent physical filters, failed P5, or a lower bound on the surviving rooted coefficients.

## 5. Exact stopping point

Remote-component cancellation extends rigorously to interacting connected graphs through the finite rooted expansion in Section1. The closed classical resummation in Section2 does not extend by that identity alone. Overlapping noncommuting terms produce surviving rooted increments involving the full signed filters and repeated physical-time evolution. No independent, volume-uniform bound on their integrated operator-norm influence has been obtained here.

The new source provides a valid nonpositive mechanism for a different specified generator and norm. Its direct cluster-majorant import fails as shown above. A faithful next proof must control the actual surviving rooted histories, or establish an appropriate metric and local norm conversion for the actual generator. This note supplies neither missing estimate and does not count their restatement as master progress. The original fixed-support, all-time, all-volume response and square-window comparison remain open.
