It reduces unrestricted exactly-one satisfiability to unrestricted Exact Cover by one set per literal: its variable element plus all clauses containing that literal. Set sizes are not fixed to three. Thus this theorem cannot be silently used as the hardness of our `TripleCoverInstance` domain.

The [3-dimensional-matching source](https://github.com/PierreSenellart/descriptive-complexity/blob/fc068f65d3c07d48539238f54afccd7c5da240fc/DescriptiveComplexity/Problems/ThreeDimMatching.lean) does provide `threeDimMatching_NP_complete`. Its [definitions](https://github.com/PierreSenellart/descriptive-complexity/blob/fc068f65d3c07d48539238f54afccd7c5da240fc/DescriptiveComplexity/Problems/ThreeDimMatching/Defs.lean) quantify over three marked classes and a ternary available-triple relation. A selected subrelation must cover each marked class exactly once in each coordinate. The classes need not be disjoint or exhaust the underlying structure; unrelated structure elements may remain. A classical adapter would tag the three coordinate sorts, handle unequal class sizes and uncovered elements by a no-instance, and enumerate the available triples. Equal-size covered inputs then yield the usual 3-uniform exact-cover problem. That adapter, its input encoding and its machine time have NOT been imported or formalized here.

## Why this does not close our TM2 hardness obligation

The repository's complexity classes are defined by logical characterizations on finite structures, and its reductions are first-order interpretations. Its [README limitations](https://github.com/PierreSenellart/descriptive-complexity/blob/fc068f65d3c07d48539238f54afccd7c5da240fc/README.md) explicitly says that agreement of the polynomial-class machine bridges with usual string-encoding presentations remains classical rather than formalized there. The exception it identifies is recursive enumerability, not the polynomial-time interface needed here.

The [Machines module](https://github.com/PierreSenellart/descriptive-complexity/blob/fc068f65d3c07d48539238f54afccd7c5da240fc/DescriptiveComplexity/Machines.lean) presents machines as relations over a finite universe. Time and tape positions share a sort, with a unary time bound given by its cardinality; configurations carry a total tape function. This is a concrete, meaningful internal model, but it is not definitionally our pinned `Turing.TM2ComputableInPolyTime` on all Boolean input strings. A translation theorem cannot be replaced by matching the words “Turing machine.”

The [Encoding module](https://github.com/PierreSenellart/descriptive-complexity/blob/fc068f65d3c07d48539238f54afccd7c5da240fc/DescriptiveComplexity/Encoding.lean) is unusually explicit about the remaining boundary. It requires executable Boolean-valued relations and polynomial size bounds in both directions. It also warns that these conditions alone do not restrict the encoder's computational complexity: an encoder could compute an expensive source answer and hide it in one relation while satisfying those size bounds. Its interface does not measure encoder time against a machine model. It separately warns that hardness needs an appropriate decoding/well-formedness direction, not only faithful encoding of concrete inputs.

Accordingly, the valid takeaway is an existing formally presented descriptive 3DM hardness route with clear translation obligations, NOT an encoded binary-TM2 X3C hardness certificate. No assertion is made that such infrastructure is globally unavailable elsewhere.
