## C. Precise continuation boundary

The stochastic pair permits daughter-state changes at division and does not refute identification in a daughter-inherits-mother model. The proof additionally shows that a normalized invertible linear intertwiner between positive state-preserving division operations must be a permutation. What remains is to derive such an intertwiner from the actual endpoint measurement law, declare the permitted hidden rival class, and justify the barcode/expression observation adapter. No such implication is asserted here.

Pilarski–Stadler–Seidel already supply joint lineage/rate inference for fixed cell-type configurations (DOI 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1014370). The unknown transition-graph/hidden-source question must be distinguished from that accomplished prior work. Neither checkpoint solves G3/G4 or translates arbitrary cell histories into the accepted G5 source class.
