The strongest immediate checker contract is: `M≥2`, original rational survivals
`0<z_a<1`, nonnegative rational weights `alpha_a`, output **original indices**
`atom_b`, strictly positive rational `beta_b`, `r≤M−1`, exact mass equality,
and exact weighted geometric moments for every `j=3..M`. Equality of the
`j=2` moment follows from mass; the `j=0,1` moments vanish. Rational-to-real
sum, product and power identities supply the hypotheses of
`original_population_compression` directly.

That theorem quantifies complete original destinations, carries all old
ordered genealogies and register fields, allows noncanonical admitted Code,
and requires the actual entering state to be co-located with live count at
most `M`. Co-location is over **all copies of that typed carrier**. A subset
of an outer frontier needs the existing ancestor-closed restriction/reassembly
bridge, rather than declaring the outer state co-located. Choose the
carrier-free moment certificate once and apply the source theorem to each
carrier; no per-row choice of compressed atoms is licensed.

The current prototype still assumes validated input. It does not remove
zero-weight inputs when the input is already below the size bound; production
reuse must filter them before elimination and return an empty certificate for
zero total mass. It checks origin by **survival value**, so equal-valued input
atoms are not distinguished by original index. Its bounds, pivot arithmetic,
termination and output certificate are not yet a generic Lean producer
refinement. A successful certificate can nevertheless have an independent
formal soundness consumer. Resource refusal or UNKNOWN is not source
infeasibility. Root has separately authorized a bounded private `M=2..4` Rust
reproduction; this audit does not enlarge that scope.

The oldest legal strictly positive construction is already in
[October 1 Appendix A](../../2026-10-01-g6-effective-certification/PROOF.md),
independently accepted in
[the source-critical review, §3.4](../../2026-10-01-sol61-g6-independent-review-2005z/REVIEW.md).
For COMMON, peel off a genuine positive smaller-arm baseline. Residual
factors are `(1−p)I+pK_z`. Weak-factor Poissonization, positive generator
compression, near-unit replacement and Euler factors precede reconstruction.
For a baseline survival `X∈(0,1)` and `K>0` binary factors, set

```
a = 1 − (1−X)/(4K+1)
X_res = X/a^(2K)
```

The old proof gives `X<a^(2K)<1`, hence `0<X_res<1`. Each residual factor
uses arm survivals `a` and `a*z`, its interior coin, and a following connector
of survival `a`; the initial population has survival `X_res`. The extra
ordinary survivals multiply exactly to `X`. `K=0` uses one positive ordinary
population. [The old checker](../../2026-10-01-sol61-g6-independent-review-2005z/independent_audit.py)
already checks that scalar redistribution. Thus the positive-arm mathematics
is reusable hand work, not a newly missing mathematical idea.

**B alone does not supply that positive baseline, error budget or source.**
Without near-unit replacement, `alpha/(1−z)` can be unbounded. Accepted
[A](sources/G6OriginalPopulationGeneratorBounds.lean) supplies a coarse full-row
generator remainder and the exact ordered half-rate generator. Its constant
can give a conservative replacement bound; it must not be called the older
sharper count-conditioned `R_M` or used while quoting its unchanged bound.
The full source-specific stochastic Poisson/Euler approximation remains a
composition to formalize. INDEPENDENT requires its separate multiple-merger
bound and ordered pair-matched replacement; COMMON commutation does not
transfer to it.

Rational supplied survival atoms are also a different input interface from
the private calendar's rational ages and inverse-rate bank: its survivals are
`exp(−duration/u)`, generally transcendental. Likewise converting a rational
survival back to a physical rate uses `−log(z)/duration`, generally irrational.
An exact rational extractor therefore needs supplied rational atoms, or a
separately specified certified representation/approximation contract for the
calendar application. Arbitrary-real noncomputable B does not provide one.
