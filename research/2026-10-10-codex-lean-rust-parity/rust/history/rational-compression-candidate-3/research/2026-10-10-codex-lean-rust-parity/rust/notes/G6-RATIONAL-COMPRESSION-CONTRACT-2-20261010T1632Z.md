# Reviewed private compression contract: explicit rational resource bound

Contributor: Codex Rust implementation role, 10 October 2026. Root reviewed and authorized the [first proposal](G6-RATIONAL-COMPRESSION-CONTRACT-20261010T1625Z.md) with the precise resource qualifications below. This document supersedes that proposal for the additive private implementation; the older proposal is preserved. No public command, accepted runtime or source-admission gate changes.

The exact request is now `{schema:"g6_rational_compression_request_v1",M:2..4,atoms:[{z:"p/q",alpha:"p/q"},…],limits:{atoms:0..64,matrix_cells:0..8192,rational_bits:0..65536}}`. Documented defaults are **64 atoms, 8,192 cumulative matrix cells, 4,096 rational bits**. The fixed 65,536-byte request and 4 MiB result transport bounds remain. All keys are required, unknown/duplicate fields rejected, canonical rationals/domain and full raw/semantic request binding follow proposal1. The accepted natural JSON implementation (including semantic `-0`) is reused unchanged. No hidden per-rational parser cap is added.

`rational_bits(q)` is `max(bit_length(abs(numerator)),bit_length(positive_denominator))`, with `bit_length(0)=0`. The constant `0/1` or `1/1` requires one bit. Validate the entire domain before resource decisions; first check original input array count, then the maximum input/one-bit-constant requirement. Check **every arithmetic operand and reduced result**, including input/output/certificate values, against the requested bit cap. A multiplication/division of bounded operands forms integer products of at most twice the operand cap; addition/subtraction may add one further temporary bit before reduction. This bounds local temporary multiplication size, not total BigInt runtime. External process limits remain mandatory for controlled runs.

Before each RREF matrix, precheck/charge cumulative `(M−1)*current_positive_count` matrix cells **before** generating its moment columns. The unchanged first-pivot/first-free algorithm, zero filtering, original index preservation and exact output certificate remain as proposed. Rational limit exhaustion during moment construction, RREF, update or certificate construction returns UNKNOWN, with no compressed atoms/mass/tails. Bit-bound failure never means mathematical infeasibility; a valid rational compression can exist outside the operational budget.

Every UNKNOWN has `limit:{stage:"INPUT"|"MOMENTS"|"RREF"|"UPDATE"|"CERTIFICATE",kind:"atoms"|"matrix_cells"|"rational_bits",required:nat,cap:nat}`. Atoms/input-bit limits use INPUT, matrix-cell limits MOMENTS, and arithmetic-bit limits their current stage. Required exceeds its matching request cap. Work is exactly `{elimination_steps,matrix_cells}`: completed elimination steps and precharged matrix cells, including a matrix whose generation stops on a bit bound. No partial certificate is returned. UNKNOWN exits2; COMPRESSED0; ERROR1.

The native checker checks the supplied rational mathematical certificate without RREF: original indices, positive weights/cardinality, mass and common tail moments, exact raw/semantic request binding and the reported mass/tails. Work is **informational**, even on COMPRESSED; `producer_work_verified:false` and `resource_exhaustion_verified:false` are always explicit in checker output. It may validate type/range framing but does not independently certify producer work or bit/RREF exhaustion. UNKNOWN acceptance is framing only and has `certificate_valid:false`. On COMPRESSED, `certificate_valid:true` applies only to the finite rational identities and positive original-index certificate; source/factor/arbitrary-real flags stay false.

The checker itself has bounded arithmetic/frame handling and can reject on its own resource failure; such rejection is not a proof that the rational identities are false. Mathematical certificate soundness after casting to real geometric sums is a separately scheduled proof consumer. The accepted noncomputable existence/source theorem is not an executable totality/refinement claim. Separate crate, build target and private ELF preservation follow proposal1.
