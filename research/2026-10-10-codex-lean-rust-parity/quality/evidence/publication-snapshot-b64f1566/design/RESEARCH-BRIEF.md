# Research progress at a glance

10 October 2026. New results sharpen G3/G4 and reconnect older proofs to executable Lean/Rust components. The full scientific solver remains unfinished. The [discovery map](DISCOVERY-MAP.md) gives proofs, reviews, attribution and remaining obligations.

| Lane | Concrete progress | What remains |
|---|---|---|
| **G3 — recognition** | Fair two-head sources attain positive interior residues. A separately reviewed hand proof excludes sufficiently small killing residues, across all finite word lengths. New explicit extraction tolerances identify the heads; rational valuation checks expose a limitation of zero-level log guards. | No certified numerical killing cutoff or general unknown-size source recognizer. Other heads, joint menus and routing modes need their own premises. Exact arithmetic replays support the certificates; they do not replace the analytic proofs or Lean formalization. |
| **G4 — separation** | Bounded-clock private arrays now have a countable limit representation. Atom and overshoot arguments exclude stated prefix cases. An actual source cell disproves the proposed inverse sign rule; a new norm estimate avoids that assumption. | The smaller-prefix case, positive cancellation and effective all-rival stopping remain open. These are scoped hand/source results. |
| **Lean — reusable proof baseline** | The selected [1,340-module snapshot](../lean/CURRENT-CONNECTED-CERTIFICATE.json) now has fresh component, aggregate and complete axiom-audit passes. The separately attributed 118-module full-epoch repair and 108-module population fixture context are accepted. Older G1 unranked row independence is reused. | Later published profiles have separate reproduction checks. Native parser, encoder and whole-solver correspondence remain distinct obligations. Historical failures stay preserved. |
| **Rust — exact execution** | AFFINE, CHART and LAW have actual Lean/Rust byte comparisons. Eight inherited Lean population evaluator fixtures match every literal and unordered coefficient. The internal epoch candidate passes nine independent mixed-generator cases and 117 prior protocol cases. | Lean population runs use `--run`, not a standalone ELF. Whole-epoch correspondence, parent-probability transport, source search, physical/observation admission and empirical nine-parameter accuracy remain open. |

The practical lesson is to reuse older providers under their exact source, calendar, population, register and routing premises. A new executable component should close a named gap, while retaining the original G1–G7 mission.
