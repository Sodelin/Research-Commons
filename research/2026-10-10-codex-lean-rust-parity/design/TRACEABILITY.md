## Source and observation contract

The original G programme retains one admitted finite source, literal original vertex/edge/hybrid IDs and parallel parent occurrences, one shared physical parameter assignment, and the declared COMMON/current-lineage INDEPENDENT register mechanism. Natural durations/rates remain strictly positive and natural inheritance remains interior. Legal controls and readouts are those of the original experiment; latent route labels, internal forests and metric calendars are not automatically new observed data. The original G3/G4 master class is the registered binary rooted-LSA outer-labelled planar cut-child class. A later fixed-graph G6 theorem or recovered nonplanar provider keeps its broader **own** hypotheses; it does not change that original class by renaming it.

The practical nine-parameter branch is a distinct supported instance: six phased copies, two sites on one genealogy per locus, fixed backward B→C pulse and stationary homogeneous clock-JC, with shared original parameter ties. It consumes nine shifted-character mean intervals and retains the original h,u,v, rA,rB,rC,rAB,rR,g domain and width targets. A narrow outer cover is not a source witness, calibrated confidence or biological accuracy. Marker alignments and molecular predictions have no automatic admission to either experiment.

## Extraction exclusions and gates

Classical choice, arbitrary abstract finite-type numbering, proof-only bijections, nonconstructive compactness/existence, arbitrary-real equality/sign access and existence of multivariate polynomial tables are not executable source providers. Their theorem evidence remains valid under its original hypotheses. Runtime support requires effective finite carriers/layouts, explicit coefficients, exact arithmetic and a checked selection/compiler bridge.

The new typed rational affine witness removes a specific existence-to-construction gap for **supplied** affine cells. It does not extract arbitrary real source parameters from exact nonlinear observation laws. Native positive and negative certificate checkers now have kernel-checked semantics for well-formed decoded rows, and actual native answer-versus-UNKNOWN has an exact `requiredCap` theorem. These proofs provide output-certificate soundness and the cap branch; arbitrary dynamic solver/typed witness equality, wire parsing/serialization and Rust-to-Lean implementation correspondence remain explicit obligations.

The corrected [attempt-2 receipt](evidence/parity-attempt-2/PARITY-RECEIPT.json) **supersedes acceptance with 281/281 passing** native byte/exit/original-row checks. Shape guards also prevent malformed library rows from being accepted through truncated zip/default-zero access. Neither failed evidence nor the old binaries was overwritten.

The additive [CHART replay](evidence/chart-parity-attempt-1/PARITY-RECEIPT.json) passes **78/78** actual native cases: 46 original AFFINE/COUNT/refusal fixtures and 32 new literal-chart/refusal cases. These new binary identities are separate from the accepted AFFINE baseline. The coordinator's [42/42 direct bank review](../quality/evidence/chart-bank-review-1/RECEIPT.json) checks physical-bank conditions without generating affine rows; the [249/249 graph oracle](../quality/evidence/difference-review-1/RECEIPT.json) and [250/250 exact Z3 oracle](../rust/evidence/exact-z3-oracle-RESULT.json) independently challenge affine decisions.
