## The contracts behind “complete”

**G1** covers the original finite binary rooted LSA/cut-child, outer-labelled planar multigraph class, parallel arcs included. One original-provenance decorated core is chosen before the source-law/context parameters and preserves the declared rooted unranked forest/history and displayed-target contract. The sharp bounds are on that core, not on the lengths of its decoration words or the original physical source.

**G2** retains topology and absolute merger ages from the same trace, once-drawn original registers, shared source parameters and literal pruning for every panel, including empty/singleton panels. Its source-verification certificate does not certify executable behavior of generated partial runtime companions. Older categorical extensions retain their own proof scopes.

**Binary G5** uses equality of every ordinary original three-tip **full timed rooted genealogy law**, with contemporaneous labels, positive durations/rates, interior inheritance and the stated temporal LSA/hybrid-child-bridge premises. It identifies the full normalized rooted-cluster **union** and nontrivial unordered split **union**. It does not identify the hidden graph, parameters or which clusters co-occur in a displayed tree. COMMON and current-owner INDEPENDENT remain explicit; no planarity or hybrid-count bound is needed. HG uses its separate bounded-indegree contract.

**G6** preserves one shared positive source over the whole finite observation profile, separate natural COMMON/INDEPENDENT mechanisms and unbounded original source size. It characterizes which answer maps can be finitely certified: the answer must be constant on the robust target fibre. Ambiguous regimes may never terminate; universal finite stopping and zero-distance exact attainment are not promised. Accepted written algorithms need to be formalized and implemented at those quantifiers; a fixed-cell decision is not the whole master.

**G7** assumes a complete finite original hybrid registry, supplied finite unranked readouts, semialgebraic legal actions, reset/deletion-closed trajectory costs and exact-law access with the declared exact-real selection model. Unknown registries, noisy data and different observation/cost models are separate extensions.
