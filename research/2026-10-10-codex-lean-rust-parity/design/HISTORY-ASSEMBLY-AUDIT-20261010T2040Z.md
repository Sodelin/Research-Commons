## Three strong claims checked against their premises

**The 1,487-module union is a coherent library, not a complete executable solver.** The [accepted certificate](../lean/INTEGRATED-MODULAR-UNION-CERTIFICATE.json) and [independent saved review](../quality/INTEGRATED-LIBRARY-UNION-REVIEW.json) bind 100,217 unique declarations and 91,274 theorem declarations. All 1,487 components use authenticated earlier kernel receipts; the aggregate and complete ownership audit are fresh. Two fixture `main` entrypoints retain separate accepted execution contexts after the preserved 1,489-module collision. No compiler count discharges a theorem's source/readout assumptions or refines Rust by itself.

**Rational atom compression is an exact useful consumer of old mathematics.** Oct1 `closure_bounds.py::cone_reduce` already used the correct geometric-sum moments; Sept30 `joint_kernel.py::_null_vector` already supplied Fraction RREF. Current bounded Rust certificates feed a checked rational-certificate → B consumer, preserving positive original support, total mass and all required tails. B's theorem chooses atoms once before arbitrary original source/Code/population/destination quantifiers. It is noncomputable for arbitrary real inputs. Neither the rational extractor nor this theorem constructs legal positive network factors, proves its work counters, or directly consumes transcendental calendar survivals.

**The three physical calendars are stronger than example agreement, but narrower than a generic parser theorem.** The accepted [Law source:366](lean/G7OriginalCompletedCalendarLaw.lean#L366) covers arbitrary finite nonempty samples and positive shared rates on these fixed calendars, including serial negative tip ages and distinct parallel occurrences. It consumes exact phase permutations and every-raw-root support, including zero branches. It identifies a completed genealogy/location/register PMF, not timed merger-age data. Native calendar validation already requires strict interior original gamma; there is no endpoint promotion.

## Concrete next reuse, without another optimizer

1. **Repair the bank interface first.** Old [physical_realization:364](../../2026-10-04-dot-practical-solver-recovery-1803z/01-exact-source-solver/package/solver.py#L364) returns `rate = -log(survival)/duration`; algebraic survival generally yields irrational inverse rates. Current calendar input accepts rational inverse rates and outputs symbolic exponentials. Define a source-faithful algebraic-survival/log-rate representation or a rigorously qualified approximation bridge before claiming these paths compose. Also bind original occurrence IDs and `g_old = 1 - gamma_original`: the inherited compiler weights parent0 by `g_old`, while the original Lean gamma weights parent1.
2. **Reuse existing target/policy verification at its exact-law scope.** The one-call passive quartet routine and complete n4/r1 two-forcing policy have actual source witnesses and restricted matched lower bounds. The supplied-policy verifier already preserves one source vector through history. Connect these existing producers/checkers under explicit registry/menu/history restrictions before adding new search. Do not turn candidate-verifier failure into a losing-budget certificate.
3. **Compose G6's old positive construction with A/B, rather than re-prove convexity.** Use positive baseline redistribution, guarded full-forest transport and the actual G1 spliced-source constructors, preserving target, ages, original IDs and one rate bank. COMMON and current-lineage INDEPENDENT require their respective arguments. This is the missing source-level assembly toward the old G6 master, not evidence that its hand theorem was absent.
