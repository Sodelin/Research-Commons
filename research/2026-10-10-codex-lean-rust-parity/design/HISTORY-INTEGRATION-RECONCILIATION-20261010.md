Two concrete compatibility checks govern reuse. The old law compiler assigns
`g` to incoming parent occurrence zero; the original Lean/Rust source assigns
`gamma` to parent one. Preserve incoming occurrence order and forced bits, and
use `g_old = 1 - gamma_original`; the existing
[parent-orientation contract](SOURCE-PARENT-ORIENTATION.md) already records this
transport. Also, the older fitter realizes an algebraic edge survival through
`rate = -log(survival)/duration`, generally irrational. The calendar interface
currently parses rational inverse rates. Directly copying that witness into its
DTO is therefore insufficient; an exact bank/law representation bridge is a
named integration task.
