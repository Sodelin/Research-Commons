It composes the original population, full epoch and conditional boundary carriers. The original scientific G6/G7 admission and optimal-policy gates remain separate.

One input fixes the structural original `RootedBinary` source, all original edge occurrences and ordered hybrid parent IDs, outer Copy sample assignment, an injective global-label embedding and one COMMON/INDEPENDENT flag for each original hybrid. No per-visit mechanism change is silently allowed. Original parent0 is bit false; parent1 is bit true; **original γ is the true/parent1 probability**. ## Finite carrier and initialization

Reuse `source_population::Context`, canonical live-only `Snapshot` and `source_epoch::SelectedView`. A full view retains the ancestor's complete **ordered** tree and original population for every outer Copy, and every original vertex register bit. Each row retains one validated literal Snapshot witness whose view is exactly its key. Omitted dead fields are `None`; a live representative lies in its own whole genealogy leaf fibre; all fibres partition Copy and descend to their original sampled taxa.

`Initial::Conditional` supplies one admitted canonical entering Snapshot with constant weight 1. `Initial::Natural` constructs singleton lineages at their original sampled tip vertices. Every nonhybrid register bit is false. Each original hybrid bit is drawn **once**, with weight γ for true and 1−γ for false, and is retained through the entire word. The initial full-register row has exactly 2^h destinations, even for empty Copy. Before expansion its total monomial contributions are 3^h; these differ from a single-hybrid boundary's binomial count. This is the literal [original initialization polynomial](../../2026-10-09-dot-g7-completed-calendar-polynomial-2330z/G7OriginalInitializationPolynomial.lean).

An interval invokes the unchanged whole mixed-population epoch, preserving node exterior fields and the entering register. `EXIT e` uses the original occurrence e. `ENTER v` resolves the original node operation: root entry, the unique incoming occurrence at an ordinary vertex, or the fixed declared hybrid mode and original parent pair. COMMON uses the entering stored bit with conditional weight 1. INDEPENDENT draws fresh bits for current live owners at that site. These operations never change the stored register, genealogy or ancestry.

Normalize by the full ordered selected view after each letter and retain a literal range witness. The inherited projection theorem supplies representative independence; the independent oracle also challenges it on every repeated reached fibre in its finite cases. Numeric IDs preserve every parallel occurrence. One source, sample map, label embedding and mode vector serve every stage.

## Shared bank and completion

For declared slots `0..J−1`, variables are slot-major `[("X",j,"E",e)]` for every original e ascending, then `("X",j,"A",root)`; original hybrid variables `("ORIGINAL_GAMMA",hid)` follow in ascending registry ID. An epoch's original E/ancestral exponents are embedded in that slot; boundary and initial polynomials share the final hybrid coordinates. Repeated slot IDs reuse **the same** coordinates. Keep exact reduced rationals, delete exact zero coefficients and sort full exponent vectors lexicographically.

Physical evaluation is `x[j,e]=exp(−rate[e]·duration[j])` and `x[j,A]=exp(−ancestral_rate·duration[j])`, using one positive rate bank and one nonnegative duration per slot, with the same original γ bank. Independent symbolic coordinates are formal polynomial inputs; their arbitrary numerical assignment is not a physical source-admission certificate. Slot/word dimensions have a separate cap; the earlier 32-coordinate CHART cap is unchanged.

`Terminal::FullView` returns the supported finite endpoint table. An optional separate `Terminal::AncestralComplete` consumer requires nonempty Copy and every supported lineage already in the original ancestral population. Its rational ordered row is the zero-survival coefficient of the actual ancestral kernel. [G7AncestralRationalCompletion](../../2026-10-09-dot-g7-completed-calendar-polynomial-2330z/G7AncestralRationalCompletion.lean), `actual_completion_at_zero`, derives this from the actual infinite-time limit; [G7SelectedCompletionPolynomial](../../2026-10-09-dot-g7-completed-calendar-polynomial-2330z/G7SelectedCompletionPolynomial.lean) retains the selected carrier and root-support premise. A supplied finite-time identity alone must not be evaluated at an excluded endpoint. Root completion adds no physical survival variable. If that native consumer is deferred, its absence is explicit and finite endpoints must not be labelled complete genealogy laws.

The final unranked reader canonicalizes child order by least global leaf, joins equal clade/population fibres and preserves the entire register. A topology-only reader hides the register/location **after** verified completion and explicit pushforward; it is not an intermediate transition carrier. Root-suppressed splits are a further declared pushforward, not a replacement for the full joint row.

## Limits and honest failure

Original graph validation, Copy≤4 and original graph-bank≤32 remain as in the accepted internal components. Expanded variables≤256, declared slots≤16 and word length≤32 are separate input limits. Global copied labels are injective unsigned 64-bit identifiers and do not relabel outer local tree IDs.

The wrapper's deterministic default limits are initial assignments 16, initial raw term contributions 256, logical selected-row provider queries 4,096, cumulative raw word branches 65,536, cumulative raw term products 262,144, normalized stage destinations 4,096 and normalized stage terms 65,536. Initial assignment and term requirements are checked before initial expansion. At a letter, first check cumulative queries; obtain complete conditional provider rows or propagate their `Unknown`; then check cumulative branches, cumulative term products, normalized destinations and normalized terms in that order. A provider row is normalized by its full selected key. One branch is one entering selected row × one supported conditional destination. Term products count entering nonzero monomials × conditional nonzero monomials before cancellation. All initial raw monomial contributions are recorded separately. Node/empty identity rows still require one logical query and one contribution. Each existing epoch/boundary provider keeps its separately declared internal limits; this wrapper does not conceal or relabel them.

Use `Decision::{Table, Unknown}` and distinct invalid-input/internal-error results. Unknown records the stage, limit, cap and exact required count; no partial word table is promoted. Invalid ancestry, mutated source/register keys, unsupported completion or malformed variable embedding is not a negative scientific result. External bounded-process failures are distinct from an arithmetic/provider Unknown.
