## Results that change the novelty assessment

1. **The G1 numerical core bounds already occur in primary literature.** Gunawan–DasGupta–Zhang's Corollary 1 gives r ≤ 2n−2 and V ≤ 6n−5 for simple galled networks; binary degree census then gives E ≤ 8n−8. Gunawan–Zhang's earlier Theorem 4.1 also states broad-class sharpness. A correct source/core admission is still required, but reproducing these constants is not a new general graph theorem.
2. **The G4 COMMON finite-atomic moment uniqueness ingredient is classical boundary theory for Chebyshev moment spaces.** The sparse positive monomials admit the same exposing-polynomial/unique-support argument. An effective exact-law search and the biological A-clade observation adapter are application contributions, separate from that known uniqueness principle.
3. **The G5 fair-pair intersection ingredients have close line-graph prior.** Triangle/claw ambiguity, finite-support intersections, and generalized Vandermonde counting are known mathematics. This does not identify the entire displayed target from a calendar law: the actual source, clock, safe-stage and chronological transport remain the important specific implication.
4. **No new generic E8 weighted grammar, class aggregation, stochastic traceback, confidence sequence, or modular optimization theory is established.** CParty already states the ideal grammar correctness theorem, PRISM gives the cancellation argument, and RNAshapes/RapidShapes/classified DP already compute class sums and residual certificates. Our checked modules are useful formalization and application/refinement work.
5. **Some exact source-specific statements still warrant a serious originality audit.** These include the all-positive nonplanar cut-child M3→displayed-cluster/S hand theorem, the actual original-ID control collisions/resource bounds, and full labelled/shared-register source assembly. No checked prior directly subsumes those complete contracts. This is a comparison limit, not evidence of novelty.

## G foundations

### G-A1. Graph census and decorated-core constants

The finite binary rooted degree contract gives V=2n+2r−1 and E=2n+3r−2 by ordinary handshaking. The closest exact size theorem is **Gunawan, DasGupta and Zhang**, *A decomposition theorem and two algorithms for reticulation-visible networks*, Information and Computation 252 (2017), 161–175, DOI 10.1016/j.ic.2016.11.001. Accepted manuscript printed p.8, Theorem 2, characterizes bicombining galled networks by parents in one tree-node component; printed p.9, Corollary 1, gives r≤2(n−1), V≤6n−5. Its proof explicitly requires no parallel edges; tree vertices need not be binary. [Author PDF](https://bdasgup.github.io/resume/publ/papers/Infor_Comput_IC4848_final.pdf).

**Mapping and countercontrol.** The accepted G1 hand scope already includes galled multigraphs, but the raw edge-indexed Lean class does not assume galledness or simplicity. A 4-tip binary rooted-LSA tree with 7 serial nonroot parallel bigons has r=7,V=21,E=27, exceeding 6/19/24 while every hybrid child is a bridge. This only refutes direct application to the raw graph. Removing those two-port bigons gives the expected ordinary tree core. Binary parallel arcs must consume the tail's whole outdegree and the hybrid's whole indegree; a parallel root bigon contradicts least-stable rooting. These observations support a short core-simplicity adapter; the exact suppression construction must still be admitted.

**Sharpness.** Gunawan–Zhang, *Bounding the Size of a Network Defined By Visibility Property*, arXiv:1510.00115 (2015), §4 printed p.8, Theorem 4.1 and Figure 3(a), already states a sharp 2(n−1) broad galled bound. Its simple-graph definition is §2.1. The extremal fixture's membership in our precise root/embedding/core subclass has not been independently proved here. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.00115).

**Verdict:** constants/census classical; core admission and restricted realization source-specific. Reuse the decomposition and existing degree-count library, rather than claim a new bound.

### G-A2. Exact contextual forest replacement and shared registers

At a fixed cap on CURRENT ENTERING live roots, preserve the full labelled forest kernel with arbitrary already-formed opaque input subtrees, retain root history, and condition on any shared register exposed to the exterior. The accepted contextual hand theorem has this stronger interface; the current finite original-Copy implementation does not automatically supply a uniform hidden-history or total-leaf bound. Finite stochastic-kernel composition and congruence are standard. Fritz–Liang's free Markov/gs-monoidal categories explicitly distinguish sampling once/copying a value from sampling independently twice; their universal interpretations admit typed multi-input/output diagrams. [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10485-023-09717-0).

The biological bridge must prove that the actual two-port decomposition supplies that exact kernel and independence/conditioning boundary. Marginal register resampling is unsound; the package's 1/4 versus 3/16 witness is a finite stress test of this known dependence issue. Calendar-time preservation and ordinary bounded-source realization do not follow from an unranked decorated kernel.

**Verdict:** generic congruence classical; complete source assembly/source labels application-specific. Existing conditional-kernel/PMF composition is reusable; no new general category claimed.

### G-A3. Kingman merger clocks, finite CTMCs and source projection

Independent exponential pair clocks, minimum-clock law, residual memorylessness, uniform pair choice for equal rates, CTMC generator/semigroup and Poisson uniformization are established probability. Kingman's 1982 *The coalescent* supplies the sampling-consistent ancestral baseline. [Primary record](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-4149%2882%2990011-4).

The native modules' contribution is the actual labelled forest/population catalogue, correct live-token multiplicity, pruning/merger/pulse correspondence and clock-reset realization. Mathematical generator intertwining is useful only after the represented generator equals the executed source and all boundary/routing events are admitted. Strong lumpability has long-established rate-into-block conditions; preservation of a full hidden source is stricter than terminal marginal equality. [Modern primary theorem with uncertainty extensions](https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/318778353/HKKR_Algorithmic_Minimization_of_Uncertain_Continuous_Time_Markov_Chains.pdf).

**Stronger prior extension:** deterministic varying population hazard replaces constant pair rate by accumulated hazard; times must be mapped back in the original calendar. [Griffiths–Tavaré 1994, §2](https://www.columbia.edu/cu/simontavare/STpapers-pdf/GT94b.pdf). This is an admission opportunity, not a newly proved extension of our source.

**Verdict:** probability foundations classical/formalization; actual source transport incomplete and source-specific.

### G-A4. Independent, common and intermediate inheritance

Fresh choices belong to surviving ancestors, so merged descendants reuse one parent's choice. A common locus/site choice is shared across live ancestors. These are existing network coalescent models, not new definitions. Yu–Degnan–Nakhleh already computes gene-tree probabilities in networks and explains how coalescent times add information. [2012 primary article](https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371/journal.pgen.1002660).

**Stronger prior extension already implemented:** Fogg–Allman–Ané 2023 introduces Dirichlet-process correlated inheritance, encompassing independent/common extremes, with **PhyloCoalSimulations.jl**. [Published primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/sysbio/syad030), [software](https://github.com/cecileane/PhyloCoalSimulations.jl). The current binary-mode kernels do not yet admit its latent law, shared-state semantics or copy restriction. A convenient interpolation of marginal outputs is not a lawful substitute.

**Verdict:** model baseline classical; original-ID live-lineage/pulse binding formalization/application; intermediate model prior available.
