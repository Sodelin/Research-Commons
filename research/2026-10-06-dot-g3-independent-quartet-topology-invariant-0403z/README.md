## Result

For a fresh natural INDEPENDENT private word, let a be its pair no-merger probability and let T be the probability that ordinary ancestral completion produces a balanced rooted quartet, minus the Kingman value 1/3. Every strict positive word satisfies

    T < 144(1-a)^3.

Together with b4<=a^3, this is a nonlinear semialgebraic invariant on the actual full forest-kernel state. Its proof preserves chronological order. The exact identity

    T(KL)=T(K)+b4(K)T(L)

follows by conditioning on the current roots left by K. Three-root completion is exchangeable over the three opaque roots; at two or fewer roots the quartet shape is already fixed. Previously formed subtrees are never treated as several independently routed lineages.

A direct full-routing calculation bounds the positive one-cell contrast by 144 times the cube of pair loss. Classical binomial log-concavity gives b4<=b2^3, and the cocycle then proves preservation for every abstract stochastic graft state satisfying the invariant, under every actual source append. The constant is deliberately nonoptimal.
