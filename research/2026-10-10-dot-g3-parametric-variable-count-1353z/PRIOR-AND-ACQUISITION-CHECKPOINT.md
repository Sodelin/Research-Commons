## The attempted source-invariant acquisition and its failed step

Closed source-derived envelopes can certify a strict gap from the semigroup of words avoiding a specified head neighborhood. That gives a genuine all-rival extraction certificate directly from numeric moments. Repeating this operation can locate head neighborhoods without treating one presentation as observed provenance.

The failure is terminal recognition of the unobserved weak remainder. The envelopes converge to the exact SOURCE CLOSURE. A zero-margin limit can be a finite word or a nonattained infinitesimal residue. Critical-span refinement proves actual interior when a finite full-rank block is found; it does not certify rank-deficient stabilization from increasingly accurate approximate parameters. The new infinite-retained theorem says every boundary presentation has finitely many strict heads, but does not provide a numerical bound, an effective stopping test, or a selected normal for that unknown finite list. The present variable-N certificate supplies one valid terminal argument when all its finite hypotheses are furnished. It does not prove that the refinement will reach one of them.

The [October 8 coupled algebraic-normal interface](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/a3453370e8e2f79dfee488d75ee90066c6285591/research/2026-10-08-codex-g3-g4-full-shot-1253z/g3-complete-classification/attempt-5/COUPLED-ALGEBRAIC-APPEND-NORMAL.md) selects an algebraic append separator only after an algebraic realizing tuple on a fixed finite graph has been chosen. This is legitimate, but it neither chooses that unknown graph nor supplies a closure-boundary NO presentation. A normal valid at one presentation need not constrain every alternative presentation of the same numerical target.

A narrow reuse clarification is possible for residue-free closure forms: under a promise that an algebraic target has a finite strict-head presentation with drift/killing and no interior Poisson terms, enumeration of head counts and the polynomial equations m_l=A^l B product f_l selects an algebraic such presentation. If the target is additionally promised to lie on the closure boundary, a closure version of the same cone argument selects an algebraic per-presentation normal. Active drift/killing coefficients are two-sided tangents; at a zero coefficient their directions are one-sided inequalities, not annihilation equalities. This does not certify the boundary promise or exclude a remote actual-source alternative. It is the old fixed-count/RCF principle applied to a promised polynomial closure stratum, not complete NO acquisition.

Positive interior residues retain nonpolynomial level equations. The accepted [three-log purity reduction](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/c268158f448598002d8041e7102aef0e70447d1b/research/2026-10-10-dot-g3-three-log-purity-1210z/README.md) already leaves a fixed degree-eight equality in logarithms even on a zero-head, source-faithful promised branch. ## Increment and limits

The new reusable statement permits all finite algebraic data that pass its explicit tail, source-forcing, full tangent-rank and residue-shift tests. It allows multiple controlled heads at the matching dimension and algebraic b through the already valid logarithm enclosure method. The entire tuple of head displacements is controlled; duplicate columns cannot be hidden by selecting a smaller minor. The generalized residual uses an arbitrary-dimensional head quadratic form, and allows either sign of the secondary quartic coefficient.

Certificate verification is finite and source-faithful. Certificate acquisition for every original target, repeated-head/rank-deficient terminal analysis, zero drift, general exposed/joint registers and INDEPENDENT/BOTH chronology remain open. A failed certificate test means no conclusion about original membership. No nearby-head or larger-cap example ladder has been run.
