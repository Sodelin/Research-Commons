## 2. Compact budgets are witnesses, not added biological assumptions

For each integer B>=3, let G_B contain every admitted graph with at most B reticulations and every legal placement of the supplied finite original IDs. This is finite: rooted binary degrees give V=2n+2r-1 and E=2n+3r-2. Finite directed multigraph enumeration followed by exact admission checks is complete. ## 3. Exact criterion

Suppose the proposed profile p has rational enclosures: a rational p_hat(i,m) with |p_hat(i,m)-p_i|<=2^(-m-2). Exact rational/algebraic finite profiles provide these; so do valid uniformly computable all-cap profiles. This is an input representation, not an equality oracle for arbitrary reals.

Let T(B,m) be the following FINITE predicate:

There exists N in G_B and ONE theta in K_N,B such that

    |F_N,i(theta) - p_hat(i,m)| <= 2^(-m-1)  for every i<=m.

Each T(B,m) is a finite disjunction of existential real-closed-field formulas with rational coefficients. Complete algebraic decision therefore decides this finite predicate. An implementation returning UNKNOWN has not decided it.

**Theorem (G3 non-escape equivalence).**

    p is realized by one finite admitted positive source
    if and only if
    there exists B>=3 such that for every m>=1, T(B,m) holds.

**Proof, necessity.** An actual source has finitely many vertices and finitely many strictly interior natural parameters. Choose B large enough to include its graph, marks and every coordinate in the stated slab. The same source and theta meet every finite test, since enclosure error is at most 2^(-m-2).

**Proof, sufficiency.** Fix a B for which every test is feasible. Choose a witnessing graph/ID map and parameter tuple for each m. Only finitely many graphs/maps occur; one occurs along an infinite subsequence m_j tending to infinity. Its parameter tuples lie in a single compact K_N,B, hence have a convergent subsequence with limit theta*. Every coordinate i is among the tested coordinates eventually. On that subsequence,

    |F_N,i(theta_mj) - p_i| <= 3 * 2^(-m_j-2) -> 0.

By continuity, F_N,i(theta*)=p_i for every i. The limit remains in K_N,B, so every population length is finite and strictly positive and every natural inheritance is interior. The graph, root history, original-ID mapping and parameters are now the SAME across all rows and copy caps. N is an actual admitted source, not a relaxed kernel. QED.

This covers boundary points of the actual source image without confusing them with endpoints of an individual parameter cube. It does not require a bound supplied in advance. It also does not prove a computable upper bound on the B witness from an arbitrary input profile.

## 4. Exact rejection and the computational boundary

Negating the theorem gives a second exact characterization:

    p is NOT realized
    iff for every B there exists a finite m with T(B,m) false.

For each fixed budget a finite exclusion certificate therefore exists whenever that budget contains no matching source. The missing step in turning this into a general terminal NO algorithm is the universal quantifier over B, not a failure of positivity or cross-cap parameter sharing.

Equivalently, let kappa_m be the smallest feasible B for the first m tests (or infinity). Exact source realization is equivalent to boundedness of the cumulative budget sequence max_(j<=m) kappa_j. Allowing B to increase without a uniform bound supplies no exact source certificate.

For computable all-cap probability presentations, the displayed definition is an effective Sigma-2 upper bound (existential integer B, universal integer m, decidable inner predicate). This is NOT a claim of Sigma-2 completeness for the entire untyped biological problem. A finite-cap rational/algebraic input retains the earlier exact positive semidecision by graph enumeration and strict polynomial feasibility; a general terminal negative procedure for that encoding is not proved here.

## 5. Why the order of quantifiers matters

The forbidden replacement is `for every finite test/accuracy there exists some positive source`. Source size may diverge, natural parameters may approach 0 or 1, or the witnesses for different response rows may be mutually incompatible. The theorem requires a uniform finite budget AND joint prefix fitting. Neither projective consistency nor a positive abstract stochastic realization supplies those requirements.

At the simplest two-port interface, K_x with x tending to 1 converges to the identity kernel, although every finite positive chain has strictly positive pair-merger probability. Repeating a fixed positive-duration population tends to the completely merged kernel, although each finite chain has strictly positive pair-survival probability. These two limits show why bounding only size, or only individual parameter margins, is not a general exact-realization argument.
