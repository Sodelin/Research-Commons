## 2. Exact source contraction and the bound

For a bare independent bigon,

    beta2=g^2 x+(1-g)^2 y+2g(1-g)
         =1-g^2(1-x)-(1-g)^2(1-y).                         (1)

This is the probability that two CURRENT roots either choose different arms or choose one arm and avoid its ordinary pair merger. The formula does not reuse separate coins for prior merged subtrees.

On the whole two-root no-merger event, the same two tokens exit each cell and every subsequent site has fresh private source driving. Thus ordinary/bigon no-merger factors multiply exactly:

    p2=(product_i zi)(product_(j=1)^L beta2_j) <= rho^L.     (2)

The leading and all connector survivals are positive, so p2>0. The loop terminates since rho^b tends to zero. If a rival had L>=b, then (2) would give p2<=rho^L<=rho^b<p2, a contradiction. This is a certified FINITE complexity bound from an admitted finite statistic plus a known global rival contraction promise.

No logarithm approximation is needed. The familiar bound L<=log(p2)/log(rho) is equivalent but exact multiplication/comparison is the safer algorithm for algebraic inputs.

## 3. Concrete source promises implying rho<1

### Both arms have known positive duration lower bounds

Suppose every rival arm survival satisfies x,y<=q for a supplied exact q in (0,1), equivalently each arm has coalescent duration at least -log q. Then, without ANY further inheritance-weight lower bound,

    beta2 <= q[g^2+(1-g)^2]+2g(1-g)
          =q+2g(1-g)(1-q) <= (1+q)/2.

Take rho=(1+q)/2<1. All g remain natural and strictly interior; no deterministic actuator is introduced.

### One sufficiently inherited arm per cell

A weaker admissible promise says every rival cell has at least one arm with survival <=q and that arm's inheritance probability >=delta>0, with q,delta exact and supplied. Then (1) gives

    beta2<=1-delta^2(1-q)=rho<1.

The qualifying arm can differ between sites. This Boolean per-cell promise is semialgebraic in the original source variables. No physical identification of that arm is required by the algorithm.

### Finite per-parameter encoding alphabets

A supplied finite allowed set of strictly interior algebraic bare triples also gives an effectively computable rho=max beta2<1. For example, if each arm survival is rational with denominator at most D>=2, then x,y<=1-1/D and rho=1-1/(2D) works. This bounds each parameter's resolution, not L in advance. An unbounded finite algebraic encoding promise alone does NOT supply such a rho.

Coalescent-time lower bounds require population-rate calibration; a calendar duration alone is insufficient without the appropriate pair-hazard/population bound. The class's original bare positivity x,y<1 has no known uniform margin from one and does not imply this promise over arbitrary rivals.
