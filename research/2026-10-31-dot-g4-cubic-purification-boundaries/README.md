## 1. An actual coupled shrinking-arm cubic direction

[Complete proof](cubic/PROOF.md) · [independent review](cubic/INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md).

For an actual strict current-root INDEPENDENT bigon in a positive ordinary wrapper, common-arm time t=beta p, and an **exactly preserved pair budget H**, the complete finite-forest kernel has cubic residual

    p³ kappa E_(H-r) T E_r + O(p⁴),
    kappa=(beta-(1-a))²/2-(1-a)³/6.

Both signs occur with strict source parameters. T is **twice the inherited triple channel R**, not a newly discovered operator. The proof checks all transition types, including vanishing disjoint-merger coefficient, then uses actual sampling consistency and row conservation for the rest. Existing input trees remain opaque. Constants concern each fixed finite carrier; there is no uniform-in-cap or arbitrary-growing-word remainder.

This is exact pair fitting, NOT exact triple/higher-fibre fitting. A cubic zero or cancellation of opposite leading coefficients does not eliminate higher terms. Moreover fixed positive-beta charts spend pair exposure of order p for amplitude of order p³: under fixed H, their total formal cubic amplitude vanishes as the maximum p tends to zero. Thus the two signs are not free finite signed flows for a Trotter argument. Shrinking beta and other regimes remain outside that expenditure estimate.

## 2. Stopping alone cannot turn the actual convex construction into exact-pair sources

[Complete proof](stopping/PROOF.md) · [independent review](stopping/INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md).

In the inherited bounded actual-source Wright–Fisher convex-centering construction, the pair coordinate M is a bounded martingale. A bounded adapted stopping rule inside a fixed compact interior coin interval, with positive ordinary pads, can have M_T equal to its initial target almost surely **only when T=0 almost surely**. The proof derives zero quadratic variation of M, then obtains a contradiction from the quadratic variation of its diffusion coefficient whenever T>0.

This is not a generic denial of optional stopping and not an actual-word nonattainment theorem. The expectation identity remains valid. The result blocks only this stopping-only conversion into an almost-surely exact-pair source. It does not exclude a particular sampled endpoint, legal deterministic pad adjustment, several-cell retuning, another controlled construction or exact finite ordinary returns. Future-conditioned selection is not an adapted stopping rule and does not inherit the same identity automatically.
