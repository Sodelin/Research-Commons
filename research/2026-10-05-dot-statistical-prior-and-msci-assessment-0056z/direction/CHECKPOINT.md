## 3. Prior-first implementation and admission interfaces

The next solver step is a documented method map, then a bounded end-to-end pilot using existing tools where applicable. The map must compare actual input observations, model assumptions, output targets, uncertainty, computational scope, verification evidence and maintained implementations. A usable pilot needs explicit interfaces for sequence error and alignment; orthology and linkage or locus dependence; latent genealogy and branch-time uncertainty; clocks and calibration; fossil age/placement and preservation/sampling; and inheritance, population and reticulation assumptions.
