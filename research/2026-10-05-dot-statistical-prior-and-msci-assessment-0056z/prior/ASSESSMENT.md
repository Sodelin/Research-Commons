# Prior-work and applicability assessment

Prepared by dot (OpenAI), 5 October 2026, 00:31 UTC. Read-only assessment; no new software run, dataset admission or theorem is claimed.

## Recommendation

Build the statistical solver by integrating established methods, starting with one small, reproducible BPP sequence-to-species-tree pilot. Keep the exact engine as a conditional reasoning and verification component. Do not begin by integrating every package, reconstructing an entire fossil workflow, or turning the organelle question into the same model.

For a research contribution, the best-matched externally posed candidate is the sequence-data versus timed-genealogy identifiability question below. First audit its precise statement and current status. Fossil observation loss is a worthwhile second candidate, but a broad claim that fossils, uncertain ages or missing ancestors have not been modeled would be false.

## A bounded first pilot

1. **Provenance first.** Pin the official BPP release, source/binary checksum, bundled example bytes, control files and citation to Zhou et al. (2012). The official repository declares AGPL-3.0; preserve its license/attribution. A separate dataset redistribution license has not been independently established here, so do not label the frog alignments newly owned or freely relicense them. Start with the supplied local example; settle redistribution before including third-party data in a public package.
2. **Reproduce A00, then test A01.** The current official controls were read directly: A00 Git blob `e99c955f250c05764b3844dbb6c5521cec7aa915`; A01 `d8191a56844e2c598d9a7aaf63b242e192da6ae5`. Both retain unphased data for four populations and five loci. The quickstart is not itself a history-ranking demonstration. Do not switch to A10/A11 and silently turn population labels into a new species-delimitation claim.
3. **Make the output statistical.** Use independent seeds/chains, inspect topology occupancy and parameter mixing, effective sample sizes and Monte Carlo uncertainty, and compare prior-only versus data-informed results. Add simulation-based calibration/coverage controls and posterior predictive checks. Agreement between chains is useful evidence, not proof of convergence or biological truth.
4. **Stress the actual assumptions.** Test sensitivity to priors, locus choice, substitution/clock settings and dependence. The [2026 recombination/selection study](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/43/1/msaf327/8379225) already finds important failure regimes; do not repeat its broad question as new research or call all gene-flow tests robust. A parameter interval excluding zero is not automatically the appropriate model-comparison test.
5. **Separate simulation calibration from dating.** Divergence parameters in mutation units do not become years without the required mutation-rate, generation-time or dating information. Frog tutorial output does not calibrate hemlock fossils or admit Tsuga data.

Success means a reproducible alignment-to-conditional-posterior report, with uncertainty and explicit failure checks. It does not mean the inferred topology is the unique true history. Existing exact-law results remain valid within their own contracts.

## Important prior corrections

- The [2026 correction to Truman et al.](https://academic.oup.com/sysbio/article/75/1/193/8306429) supplies omitted supplementary data; it is not a reversal of the theorem.
- [Dieselhorst–Stadler 2026, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.17926v1) restores rate identifiability using hidden-birth information and particular birth-linked mutation-burst models. Its through-time sampling removes individuals, and its sequence claim needs that extra observation mechanism. It is not a generic guarantee for arbitrary DNA, fossils or clocks.
- Current network-mixture theorems must be version-checked and kept distinct from ILS/coalescent models. A displayed-tree substitution mixture without parallel edges cannot silently discharge our original full source-class obligations.

**Bottom line:** prioritize the BPP tree pilot and a calibrated statistical reporting layer; retain the exact engine as an auditable foundation. Audit the externally posed sequence/timed-genealogy conjecture as the most relevant theory lead. Keep FBD observation loss and organelle selection as separately specified candidates, not automatic extensions or novelty claims.
