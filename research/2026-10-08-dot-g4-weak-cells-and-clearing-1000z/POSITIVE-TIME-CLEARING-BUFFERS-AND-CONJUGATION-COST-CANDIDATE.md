## Purpose and source

The accepted Lawson application proves that the entire actual capped source group is generated by strict source words and ordinary intervals with arbitrary REAL durations. It does not make internal negative durations physical. Here are two exact reasons why simply moving and buffering those intervals does not supply that missing theorem.

Fix the original natural private INDEPENDENT full-forest cap n and its faithful source algebra. All actual cells retain their labels, old subtrees and one physical parameter tuple across arities. Write E_t=E(exp(-t)); physical ordinary edges require t>0. Mathematical conjugations may use other real t, but no such edge is called physical.

## 1. What an existing ordinary buffer really permits

Take a literal actual word presentation K=E_a V E_b, with a,b>0 and its finite physical cell body V held fixed. Then

    E_s K E_(-s)=E_(a+s) V E_(b-s).                     (1)

This is an actual strict word by this SAME presentation whenever -a<s<b. It moves ordinary duration from one end to the other without changing the pair hazard. At s=-a or s=b an end duration becomes zero; beyond an endpoint it becomes negative. Equation (1) then ceases to be a strict realizing presentation. It does not prove that some different positive presentation is impossible.

Now consider a fixed signed-time expression

    W=E_(t0) K1 E_(t1) ... Kp E_(tp),
    Ki=E_(ai) Vi E_(bi), ai,bi>0.                        (2)

Absorb ordinary-only Ki into neighboring ordinary factors first, so every Vi contains at least one genuine cell, starts and ends with a cell, and retains its positive internal ordinary gaps. The signed connector times ti are arbitrary real numbers. The literal duration between the last cell of Vi and the first cell of V_(i+1) is

    gi=bi+ti+a_(i+1).                                   (3)

For any shifts si, replace Ki by E_(si) Ki E_(-si) and adjust the signed connectors to preserve the exact product:

    t0'=t0-s1,
    ti'=ti+si-s_(i+1)  (1<=i<p),
    tp'=tp+sp.

The redistributed end buffers are ai'=ai+si and bi'=bi-si. Therefore

    gi'=bi'+ti'+a_(i+1)'=bi+ti+a_(i+1)=gi.              (4)

The first and last merged durations are also unchanged by this reparameterization. Additional exterior positive padding changes only those two exterior durations, never any gi.

Consequently rebracketing, merging adjacent ordinary edges, and relocating EXISTING buffers by (1) cannot clear a genuinely nonpositive internal merged gap in the fixed cell-body list. This is an exact syntactic invariant, independent of the cap and number of cells. An actual positive-time clearing theorem must provide a DIFFERENT positive realization of a conjugated body/product, rather than obtain one by this bookkeeping alone. Finite-cap alternative presentations remain possible and are not excluded by (4).

## 2. A complete-kernel bound on backward conjugation

Use the accepted fixed rational ordinary-diagonalizing LEFT basis of the actual source group,

    K=D(b)+N,
    E_t=D(exp(-lambda_r t)), lambda_r=binom(r,2).

Choose one off-diagonal block (k,r), k>r>=1, and any fixed real linear functional L on that block. Extend it to complete capped kernels by the linear regular-action map and fixed basis change. In fresh forest coordinates it has some finite coefficient representation

    L(K)=sum_(j=0)^n sum_(f in F_j) c_(j,f) K_j(f).

Set

    M_L=sum_(j=0)^n max_(f in F_j)|c_(j,f)|.             (5)

For EVERY normalized nonnegative complete forest tuple, including source-closure tuples,

    |L(K)|<=M_L.                                        (6)

This follows row by row because each row is a probability distribution. It does not use the signed ordinary basis for a stochastic norm. Other coefficient representations may improve the bound; (5) is an explicit finite valid one. The functional need not annihilate u^2.

Fix an actual K for which L(K)!=0. Then M_L>=|L(K)|>0. Put Delta=lambda_k-lambda_r>0. Backward ordinary conjugation gives

    L(E_(-s) K E_s)=exp(Delta s)L(K), s>=0.              (7)

If this conjugated kernel were in the normalized stochastic source closure, (6) would force

    s <= log(M_L/|L(K)|)/Delta.                          (8)

Thus sufficiently large backward conjugation leaves even the stochastic closure, not merely the unknown deterministic source image. This is a theorem for a fixed nonzero block of a fixed actual kernel. A kernel commuting with ordinary passage has no such nonzero block and is outside this particular conclusion.

The opposite conjugation E_s K E_(-s) CONTRACTS this block. No claim says both directions necessarily lose stochastic positivity. In particular, when K has ordinary diagonals, that forward conjugation tends algebraically to its ordinary diagonal kernel. Membership near that limit is exactly an additional actual-source centering question, not a consequence of convergence.

## 3. Buffering an amplified factor has an unavoidable hazard cost

Let p,rho>=0 be exterior ordinary buffer durations and delta=p+rho. Consider

    X=E_p E_(-s) K E_s E_rho.

Its pair hazard, if physically realized, is h(K)+delta. The selected block scales exactly as

    L(X)=exp(Delta s-lambda_k p-lambda_r rho)L(K).        (9)

For X to be even a normalized nonnegative forest kernel, (6) requires

    Delta s <= lambda_k p+lambda_r rho
                                      +log(M_L/|L(K)|),
    delta >= max(0,Delta s-log(M_L/|L(K)|))/lambda_k.    (10)

The second line uses lambda_k>=lambda_r. If a fixed total reserve H is available, then necessarily

    s <= [lambda_k H+log(M_L/|L(K)|)]/Delta.              (11)

At r=1, lambda_r=0. Whenever such a block is nonzero, right padding alone has no effect on (9), so after the bound in (8) is exceeded NO amount of right padding can repair stochastic positivity. Sufficient left padding p>s is a genuine but costly construction, since it simply makes E_(p-s) K E_s a positive word. The necessary lower bound captures the linear growth of that cost; it does not assert sufficiency below p=s.

All statements are uniform over source word lengths, but depend on the chosen cap, kernel and block. Small source defects may make the admissible conjugation range large. No bound uniform over every cap-dependent rival is inferred.

## 4. Consequence for the Lawson factorization and fixed target

Moving a negative ordinary interval to the RIGHT across K uses the amplified conjugate:

    E_(-s) K = (E_(-s) K E_s) E_(-s).

Moving it to the LEFT uses the contracted conjugate:

    K E_(-s)=E_(-s)(E_s K E_(-s)).

The first direction is subject to (8)--(11). The second still needs actual positive membership of the contracted kernel; a signed-basis contraction does not supply a physical realization. If its diagonals are not ordinary, even its limiting diagonal operator is not automatically an admitted source. If the diagonals are ordinary, an actual neighborhood of that ordinary endpoint is precisely the missing hypothesis.

The signed-time saturation theorem supplies no bound on its internal shifts, no conjugated-cell membership theorem, and no cap-uniform buffer allowance. Pair survival fixes the final ordinary target hazard, so adding arbitrary time to clear formal factors changes that target unless an equal amount is legally removed elsewhere. The syntactic identity (4) shows why existing-buffer reallocation cannot silently perform that repair.

These results do not exclude a better signed factorization or a different actual source architecture with the required fixed budget. They establish that a general positive-time clearing claim needs substantive new source membership and budget control. Original G4 and the all-cap ordinary-return thresholds remain OPEN.
