## Accepted weak-cell strengthening

The [weak-cell proof](WEAK-CELL-CONVEX-CENTERING-AND-SIGNED-SATURATION-CANDIDATE.md), checked by its [independent review](WEAK-CELL-CONVEX-SATURATION-HAND-REVIEW.md), gives the following at each fixed finite full-forest cap:

- A positive ordinary target lies in the relative interior of the EXTERNAL convex hull of actual strict words with exactly D bigons, where D is the capped forest-algebra dimension.
- Every bare arm can be shorter than any prescribed epsilon; every coin can lie in any prescribed open interior interval; every mixture component's total pair hazard can lie within any prescribed delta of the target hazard.
- Every nonzero primitive source-block functional takes both signs on bare cells under those same short-arm and coin restrictions.
- Adjoining arbitrary REAL ordinary durations gives exact finite-product saturation even when every bare cell has pair loss below any prescribed eta.

The last assertion uses the WEAKER pair-loss restriction. Its diagonal-rank provider can have one arm bounded away from zero and a coin tending to an endpoint. Saturation under simultaneous short-arm and fixed interior-coin restrictions has not been established.

Every mixture component is a genuine finite positive same-parameter source. The external mixture itself is not a physical source operation. The conclusions give no common rescaled full-response palette after lower-forest constraints, nor any bound on the length or total signed duration of a saturation factorization.
