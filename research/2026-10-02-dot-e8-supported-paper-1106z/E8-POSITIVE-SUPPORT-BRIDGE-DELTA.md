## Exact hypothesis improvement

Before: the old unambiguous traceback transport asks for `Trace(b,d) ≃ Structure`, including every padded trace tuple. Invalid/padding tuples may carry zero weight and should not need a biological/RNA interpretation.

After: `SupportedTracePaperContract` requires only:

- an equivalence from `PositiveTrace = {t : Trace(b,d) // 0 < traceWeight(t)}` to the independently specified paper Structure;
- preservation of the imported, actual finite-model traceWeight on that support;
- current classifier commutation on that support.

It contains no desired probability-law equality. The source trace weights/probabilities are the imported model functions, not arbitrary fields named correct. The paper weight/classifier are still specification data whose relationship to actual C++ is unproved.

`trace_weight_zero_outside_positive` proves the complement has zero weight from nonnegative factors/terminal weights. `sum_restrict_positive_traces` reuses mathlib `Finset.sum_congr_set` to remove that complement, without changing total/fiber masses. Normalizer and class mass then transport by `Equiv.sum_comp`.

The final endpoint, `supported_trace_paper_class_probability`, reuses existing `supported_observable_probability`. It proves the selected class probability equals the paper class weight sum divided by its complete normalizer. Only the starting partition is positive. Child partitions may be zero; classifier labels on zero-weight invalid/padding traces are unconstrained. Shape-label type may be String, with no globally finite class assumption.

**Codomain precision.** The current equivalence is from positive source traces onto the **whole chosen paper Structure**. With pointwise weight preservation this implies every paper Structure has positive weight. It is appropriate when Structure denotes the supported finite-energy Boltzmann ensemble. It does not admit extra zero-weight paper objects on that same codomain. A general nonnegative paper carrier with such objects instead requires a bijection between positive supports on both sides and a proved zero-weight paper complement. The older full-carrier PMF bridge separately admits zero-weight outcomes. This distinction was independently checked by the strongest-statement auditor.
