## Strongest missing implication

The proposed semialgebraic route would need to show that every complete algebraic target fibre meeting the joint invariant hull over a real closed ordered extension has an **actual standard finite strict real source witness**. The latest countermodel theorem proves the equivalence to certificate completeness, not that implication. Fixed finite architectures already transfer between real closed fields; the unbounded architecture/word quantifier is the unresolved step.

Alternatively, an input-computable witness-size bound or another sound complete NO certificate family would close recognition. Neither is presently proved. Rational hidden parameters, fixed-cap exceptions, a finite template library or a new source observation menu cannot silently replace the original question.
