## Alternative exact composition route

At each epoch use a common supported state-index universe independent of σ: partition blocks and their epoch-active populations, not the entire raw code carrier. Extend impossible transitions/states with zero coefficients. Initial singleton genealogy/populations are the same for every bank; only register fields differ.

Build the complete endpoint-resolved path expansion conditioned on σ, including ALL boundary factors. Coalescent total rates, site killing and root weighted rates are determined by current blocks/populations/characters and the original fixed positive rate bank; they do not depend on unused register bits. Therefore the exponential bases and their common indexed denominator can be indexed independently of σ, even though the realized path coefficients depend on σ.

Only then apply the original PMF.bind law and finite-sum linearity to average each complete path coefficient over σ. Keep Eσ[product_j B_j^σ] exactly. Do NOT replace it by product_j Eσ[B_j^σ], and do not claim the bank-marginalized process has become Markov. Correlations induced by the once-drawn bank remain in the joint path coefficient.

If the common index/exponent construction is proved, finite bank averaging changes coefficients but need not multiply the exponential-term bound by the number of bank assignments. An unordered merger is then indexed by an unordered pair of distinct original-copy labels, from a single universe of size b=choose(n,2). Invalid pairs at a given observation receive coefficient zero. Actual owner/block bijections and accepted same-target weighted orientation equality are what justify this indexing of the ORIGINAL ordered clocks; no clock is discarded merely because its endpoint agrees.

A current observation plus such a merger-label sequence determines the successive partition/location observations without consulting bank bits. Strict live-card descent permits at most n-1 merger labels, hence at most E=sum_{k=0}^{n-1} b^k sequence indices, independently of σ. At a calendar boundary retain the next actual supported observation as an explicit index (at most S once the epoch-active encoding is proved). Its fixed-bank whole-block routing probability is part of the coefficient, zero if impossible.

For each finite epoch a path has at most n holding exponents. The retained original term bound therefore has a common prospective index shape: one final ancestral merger sequence (≤E), and for each of J finite epochs a merger sequence (≤E), one selected holding exponent (≤n), and a boundary observation (≤S).
