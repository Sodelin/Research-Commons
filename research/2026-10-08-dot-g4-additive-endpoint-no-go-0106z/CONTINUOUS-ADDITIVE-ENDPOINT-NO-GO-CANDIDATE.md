## Result first

Fix any finite entering-root cap m>=2. Every continuous real-valued additive function of the ACTUAL capped private INDEPENDENT source endpoint is a linear combination of log no-merger diagonals:

    F(K L)=F(K)+F(L) for all actual K,L
        implies
    F(K)=sum_(r=2)^m c_r log b_r(K).                       (1)

No prior extension of F to an algebraic group, conjugacy invariance, polynomiality, differentiability, Ore condition, inverse-source availability, or word-length bound is assumed. The local mathematical group is used only inside genuine open patches of the positive source semigroup.

Consequently, if such F is nonnegative on every actual capped source and vanishes at ONE strictly positive ordinary E(q), 0<q<1, then

    F(K)=0 for EVERY actual K.                            (2)

Thus an endpoint-recoverable additive entropy/energy production, nonnegative under every genuine append operation and zero at the ordinary target, cannot be a nontrivial separator at ANY finite cap. This is a failure of a defined invariant architecture, not an all-word ordinary-return or ordinary-rigidity theorem. Nonlinear, nonadditive, transport-weighted, or conditionally defined invariants remain open.

## 2. Two genuine-source facts needed for the extension argument

Write S^o=int_G(S), which is nonempty by the cited provider.

First, if a is in S^o and K is in S, then K a is in S^o. Choose an open U containing a with U subset S. Left multiplication by the nonsingular K is a homeomorphism of G; K U is open and lies in S by actual semigroup closure. The analogous right statement also holds.

Second, EVERY actual K has a continuous contraction K_t, 0<=t<=1, with

    K_1=K, K_0=I, and K_t in S for every t>0.             (4)

Take one legal presentation of K, replace every population survival z,x,y by z^t,x^t,y^t, and leave every inheritance coin fixed. For t>0 all populations remain finite and strictly positive. At t=0 all population durations are zero, so no entering roots merge; the complete kernel is I even though the private routing coins remain interior. This boundary point is a mathematical limit, not an admitted positive-duration source.

In particular, for any fixed a in S^o, the WHOLE path t->K_t a is inside S^o, including t=0: for t>0 use the first fact, and at zero it is a itself. Thus K a and a are joined within the interior, regardless of whether K itself is an interior point. We do not assume that every source is interior, or that S contains I.

## 3. Derive a common local character from F on positive sources only

For a in S^o and x in a sufficiently small group neighborhood of I, both a x and x a lie in S^o. Define

    f_a(x)=F(a x)-F(a).                                (5)

Although x itself need not be physical, every argument of F in (5) IS in S. This is a continuous local function and f_a(I)=0.

### Independence of the interior base point

For a,b in S^o and x sufficiently close to I, associativity and additivity on actual source pairs give

    F(a x)+F(b)=F(a x b)=F(a)+F(x b).

Also

    F(b x)+F(b)=F(b x b)=F(b)+F(x b),

so F(x b)=F(b x). Substitution proves f_a(x)=f_b(x). These are equal as germs at I; a common global neighborhood for all interior base points is neither claimed nor needed. Denote the common germ by f.

### Local additivity

Choose x,y and their product sufficiently close to I so that a x, y a, and a x y all lie in S^o. The two actual-source factorizations of a x y a give

    F(a x y)+F(a)=F(a x)+F(y a).

The previous base-a equality gives F(y a)=F(a y). Therefore

    f(x y)=f(x)+f(y).                                  (6)

Thus f is a continuous LOCAL group character. This conclusion follows from additivity only where its factors are actual positive kernels. We have not inserted an inverse source into S or extended F on faith.

## 4. Elementary classification of the local character

This part uses only (3), (6), ordinary conjugation in G, and the elementary continuous Cauchy equation. It avoids a general semigroup extension theorem.

### It kills each actual unipotent block

Take X in an allowed block u_(k,r), 1<=r<k<=m. A matrix supported on this one off-diagonal block satisfies X^2=0, so

    (I+sX)(I+tX)=I+(s+t)X.

The local continuous Cauchy equation gives f(I+tX)=c_X t for sufficiently small real t. Choose an ordinary a>0, a!=1, sufficiently close to 1. The matrix E(a)=D(a^lambda) and its algebraic inverse are both near I in G. All arguments below can therefore be kept in the domain of the local character by making t sufficiently small. Local additivity gives

    f(E(a)(I+tX)E(a)^(-1))=f(I+tX).

On this block the actual ordinary weights give

    E(a)(I+tX)E(a)^(-1)
         = I+a^(lambda_k-lambda_r)tX.

The nonempty arity rates are distinct; the isolated empty block supplies no exception. Hence a^(lambda_k-lambda_r)!=1, so c_X=0. Thus f vanishes on every elementary unipotent block factor.

Every 1+N with N in u sufficiently small factors into a finite product of these elementary factors. To check this within the ACTUAL u, eliminate blocks in increasing distance k-r. Multiplying by an elementary inverse I-X cancels the chosen block; all new products have strictly larger block distance and remain in u because u is associative and block decomposed. There are finitely many distances. The factors and their partial products remain near I after the initial neighborhood is reduced. Therefore

    f(I+N)=0.                                         (7)

### Its remaining local part is log-diagonal

Every K in G sufficiently close to I factors as D(b)(I+N), with N in u, because block-scalar diagonal multiplication preserves u. Equation (7) gives f(K)=f(D(b)).

The full diagonal torus in (3) has independent positive coordinates b2,...,bm. Restricting f to each one-coordinate subgroup b_r=exp(t), with all other b_j=1, gives a continuous locally additive function of real t. It equals c_r t. Multiplying these commuting one-coordinate factors gives

    f(K)=chi(K),  chi(K)=sum_(r=2)^m c_r log b_r(K),    (8)

near I. The displayed chi is already a globally defined continuous additive function on G, since genuine diagonal blocks multiply. Mathematical diagonal factors near I need not be positive sources; they occur only in the local germ already justified by actual interior translations.

## 5. Return from the local group germ to EVERY actual source

Set R=F-chi on S. For any a in S^o and x sufficiently near I, (5),(8) yield

    R(a x)-R(a)=0.

Therefore R is locally constant on S^o. Fix any actual K and an interior a. By Section 2, t->K_t a joins a to K a entirely within S^o. A locally constant function is constant along this connected path, so

    R(K a)=R(a).

But additivity on actual endpoints gives R(K a)=R(K)+R(a). Hence R(K)=0. K was arbitrary, proving (1).

This is the step that prevents an unproved discrete presentation-dependent remainder. No connectedness assumption on all of S^o and no physical factorization of K into arbitrarily weak cells is used. Scaling the parameters of one fixed word supplies a path of sources; it is NOT claimed to have positive-source increments. This is fully compatible with the accepted continuous-positive-increment obstruction.

## 6. Nonnegative ordinary-neutral additive scores vanish identically

Assume F(K)>=0 for every K in S and F(E(q))=0 for one 0<q<1. By (1),

    0=log(q) sum_(r=2)^m c_r lambda_r.                  (9)

For m>=3, the accepted [uniform-time diagonal-return theorem](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/608f690fd76e216036ed2cd282c236fd0aa4ac46/research/2026-10-04-dot-g3-structure-followons-2200z/uniform-diagonal/UNIFORM-TIME-DIAGONAL-FINAL.md) supplies an actual strict word W with the same prescribed diagonal b_r(W)=q^lambda_r and FULL diagonal differential rank m-1 at W. By (1),(9), F(W)=0. The inverse/submersion theorem makes the set of log-diagonal vectors of actual nearby words contain an open neighborhood of

    v_q=(lambda_2 log q,...,lambda_m log q).

The linear functional c dot v is nonnegative throughout that neighborhood and vanishes at its center. A nonzero linear functional takes both signs near any point of its zero hyperplane, so c=0. This proves (2).

For m=2 the source has only the pair-survival coordinate, and (9) directly gives c2=0. Thus (2) holds at every stated cap.

    F(W)=F(E(z0))+sum_i [F(B_i)+F(E(zi))]

is understood whenever bare-cell terms are defined by a compatible continuous endpoint extension, or by grouping each cell with its strict pads. Formula (1) is the safe intrinsic statement on S and needs no separate assumption that an unpadded B_i is itself in the legal word grammar. Equivalently, sums over genuine legal padded segments are ordinary additive sums.

If a proposed nonnegative local production can be summed over legal segments to give a continuous function of the COMPLETE endpoint and ordinary segments produce zero, (2) says that endpoint function must be identically zero. A positive hidden-history cost is not ruled out as a history variable; it fails to be an observable endpoint invariant if different legal word presentations give different costs. A finite tuple of continuous additive scalar endpoint scores has the same log-diagonal limitation componentwise.

This strengthens the previously accepted spectral-method warning in one precise direction.
