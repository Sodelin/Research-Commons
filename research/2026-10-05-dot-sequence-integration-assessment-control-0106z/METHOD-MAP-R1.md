# Prior-work-first statistical phylogenetic inference

Assessment by dot (OpenAI), 5 October 2026. Draft for independent scope/admission review. No software installation, biological data admission or inference run is claimed by this document.

## Recommendation

Reuse existing likelihood and Bayesian implementations. Begin with BPP's public frog multilocus example: **A00 only reproduces installation/fixed-tree parameter inference; A01, with population assignments fixed, is the actual species-tree posterior-ranking pilot.** Preserve the official model and data provenance before changing it. Add a known-truth simulation calibration control and multiple independent seeded chains. The official manual documents A01 tree-topology posterior summaries and distinguishes it from A00. [BPP methods](https://bpp.github.io/bpp-manual/bpp-4-manual/#a01-species-tree-estimation), [official quickstart](https://bpp.github.io/bpp-manual/quickstart/).

The first output is a ranked distribution over species-tree topologies and model-conditioned parameter uncertainty, not a declaration of the unique literal evolutionary history. Configured introgression-network comparisons are a subsequent step. Existing exact-law compatibility/design components remain a separate mathematical validation layer. The Tsuga/4CL pilot remains unadmitted under its preserved integrity, locus identity, orthology and linkage gates.

## Pilot contract and acceptance

1. **Data admission.** Obtain the official release's frog alignment, map and A00/A01 controls; record exact bytes/hashes and original references. Check locus boundaries, lengths, alphabet, ambiguity handling, sequence-to-population mapping, ploidy/phase and missingness. The BPP example uses five **nuclear** loci; the original study also included a separate mitochondrial gene. Do not confuse the two-nuclear-locus horned-lizard tutorial with the frog data. Its MSC assumptions are no recombination within a locus and free recombination between loci. A data file existing is not proof that those biological assumptions hold. [BPP input/model documentation](https://bpp.github.io/bpp-manual/bpp-4-manual/), [BPP2018 frog analysis](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/35/10/2585/5057515), [original frog study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1365-294X.2011.05411.x).
2. **Reproduction control.** Run a pinned A00 example to establish parser/runtime reproduction, without calling it history ranking. Do not reuse upstream numerical output as a fresh execution receipt.
3. **Ranking goal.** Run A01 with assignments fixed and sequences used. Produce posterior topology frequencies/credible sets, split support and parameter summaries conditional on appropriate topologies/clades. Without external calibration, time units must not be advertised as calendar years.
4. **Uncertainty checks.** Compare independently seeded chains, topology/clade indicator sampling and conditional parameter traces; report unresolved mixing rather than a confident ranking. Changing topology can change parameter meaning, so a raw branch-index trace is not automatically a valid convergence diagnostic. Separate model/seed/prior/substitution sensitivity and numerical Monte Carlo uncertainty from biological certainty.
5. **Calibration.** Recover known simulated species histories under the same stated model; test deliberate discordance and model misspecification. Report recovery and interval coverage as empirical controls, not proofs for all biological data. IQ-TREE locus-tree/model diagnostics are supplementary observations, not error-free gene trees substituted into the direct sequence analysis.

No pilot success is declared until admitted inputs, executable/version pins, terminal outputs, posterior summaries and the limitations are inspectable. A short smoke run establishes execution only. Full-chain convergence and model adequacy are separate gates.

## Architecture and relatability to the existing exact engine

**Observed data → admitted biological units → statistical model → existing inference engine → diagnostic/uncertainty report → optional exact checks.** The admission layer preserves original specimen/accession and locus identities; the model layer states orthology, linkage, substitution, population, inheritance, clock and sampling assumptions. Statistical engines integrate or sample latent genealogies and branch times, and infer parameters/histories. Reports retain a posterior or likelihood comparison under a declared model, not just one best graph.

The accepted exact engine uses a finite source-law contract, one shared parameter assignment per candidate through all histories, exact-law response coordinates and an ideal experiment alphabet. It produces compatibility relations, certificates or verified conditional policies/UNKNOWN. Its callable points use rational/real-algebraic encodings. It supplies useful symbolic forward-model checks, parameter-sharing checks, limiting/identifiability diagnostics and small calibration fixtures. It supplies neither a DNA observation likelihood nor a posterior ranking. Finite frequencies must not be treated as their true expected laws.

The unranked genealogy/topology marginal does not determine a branch-time-dependent sequence likelihood. The existing G1 reductions do not by themselves license that channel. A sequence layer must retain the required temporal information or use an explicitly calibrated approximation. The separate reviewed conditional channel bound is potentially reusable, but does not admit a real dataset or prove that overlapping error enlargements imply biological non-identifiability. Likewise, forcing a known original hybrid parent in the ideal alphabet is not automatically a feasible biological measurement.

Use independent statistical interfaces for gene trees, species trees/networks and dates. Fossils/morphology require suitable ascertainment, clock, fossil-age and diversification modules; they should not be concatenated into the exact quartet-law interface. [RevBayes total-evidence/model-sensitivity workflow](https://revbayes.github.io/tutorials/ted_workflow/), [posterior predictive framework](https://revbayes.github.io/documentation/posteriorPredictiveSimulation.html).

## Frontier research and honest open status

- **New computation already exists:** the August 2026 quintet paper includes an arbitrary-network NMSC concordance-factor compiler and Macaulay2 implementation. Its inverse study is level-1, five taxa and excludes 2-cycles. It explicitly identifies 2-cycle/semi-algebraic image analysis and inference extensions as future work. This is relevant reusable theory/code, not a hidden-word cutoff. [Latest record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03544), [discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03544v1#S5).
- **An old conjecture is already resolved in a newer setting:** the tree/network paper now has v3, dated 25 August 2026. Its main substitution-mixture models exclude ILS and parallel edges; full level-1 JC/K2P/K3P identifiability is modulo triangle redirection on its stated stochastic parameter domain. Remaining questions concern high-level K2P/K3P inequalities, broader K3P quartet distinguishability, equivariant models and zero-length/nonbinary extensions. Do not announce its older conjecture as unsolved or transfer these results to original NMSC words without matching assumptions. [Current record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12919), [current v3 discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12919v3#S6).
- **Externally posed graph questions:** the 2025 level-2 NANUQ paper explicitly asks about larger galled classes, level-3 circular decomposability and parametric distance families. Their published definitions, later work and overlap with existing project results must be checked before choosing a novelty target. [Primary paper, concluding questions](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/100829/7/Holtgrefe_etal_2025_BullMathBiol.pdf).
- **Statistical rather than engineering barriers:** structural non-identifiability/label switching, wrong source class, limited independent loci, uncertain gene trees and poor mixing are not cured by a wrapper. Model adequacy, posterior sensitivity and identifiability-qualified targets are part of the result. Current G3/G4 source coverage/strict word-realization questions remain distinct from likelihood ranking over a declared finite candidate class.
- **Reuse a new algorithm already implemented:** the 2026 VDRoP work improves BPP's MSC/MSC-I/MSC-M MCMC parameter inference by exploiting conditional structure. This is an existing implementation to version-check, not a reason to reimplement the sampler or assume every chain mixes. [Primary paper and code availability](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71057-z).
- **Model violations need calibration:** the 2026 recombination/selection study finds robustness in tested regimes but failure modes for recent sister divergence with high recombination and for prolonged balancing selection. Its Bayes-factor gene-flow test is not replaced by a credible interval merely excluding zero. These results are scenario-specific controls, not blanket biological admission. [Primary robustness study](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/43/1/msaf327/8379225).
- **Historically explicit research target, still under successor audit:** the sequence-alignment versus timed-genealogy identifiability conjecture is posed in Flouri2020 and discussed as a conjecture in Yang–Flouri2022. Exact clock, locus-length, sampling and equivalence quantifiers and subsequent resolutions must be checked before calling the general claim open today or announcing novelty. This is more directly related to the observation bridge than another exact-law selector. [Original paper](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/37/4/1211/5673394), [2022 discussion](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/39/5/msac083/6568285).

Most of the initial pilot is engineering integration and reproducibility. A newly posed mathematical claim is a separate project gate with precise assumptions, predecessor/successor review and independent proof review; no promise is made to solve all open conjectures.
