Status: frozen whole-proof attempt. The elementary cofinality equivalence below is a hand candidate; the all-cap extension and original G4 conclusion are unproved.

## 1. Full intended construction

Fix one nonempty finite positive private INDEPENDENT seed K. The proposed negative G4 proof would find, at every finite cap m, an actual finite positive P_m with

    P_m K = E_(H_m),     sup_m H_m < infinity.          (1)

Taking one H strictly above this supremum and adding positive ordinary padding would give exact capped rivals for the ONE target E_H. Every rival contains the fixed nonempty seed, so the accepted all-copy word-length invariant gives later inequivalence. The original finite-cap compiler supplies equality of each finite legal prefix and the admitted private-bridge construction preserves the original graph and menu. This is the same fixed-target quantifier as the master problem, not a cap-dependent target substitute.

The starting resource is actual ordinary interior through cap five. The attempt was to convert that interior into positive left-completion control, propagate it through the exact projective source extensions, and bound the accumulated clock cost. The first conversion works conditionally at a given cap. The propagation does not follow from the accepted premises.

## 2. Fixed-cap cofinality equivalence

Fix one finite cap m>=2 and use the ACTUAL source group

    G={D(b)+N: b2,...,bm>0, N in u},

with the accepted block-decomposed associative source algebra u. Let S be its finite strictly positive source-word semigroup. Ordinary E_t is diagonal with entries exp(-lambda_r t), lambda_r=binom(r,2), in the fixed ordinary-diagonalizing LEFT regular basis.

The following statements are equivalent:

1. Some positive ordinary E_H lies in int_G(S).
2. Every g in G has a positive source left complement: there are P in S and T>0 with P g=E_T.
3. Every K in S has such a positive source left complement.

The implication (2)->(3) is immediate. For (3)->(1), choose K in a genuine open source patch U, use P K=E_T, and observe that P U is an open subset of S containing E_T. Existence of an actual open patch is an inherited premise.

### Proof of (1)->(2)

Let U be a G-open neighborhood of E_H contained in S. Fix arbitrary g in G. Choose a positive integer N so large that

    D_* = D(exp(-H lambda_r - log b_r(g)/N))

lies in U. This is possible because D_* tends to E_H as N grows. D_* is an ACTUAL source endpoint because it lies in U; its original positive word need not resemble a diagonal matrix syntactically.

Set D0=D_*^N, an actual concatenation. Then g0=D0 g has the exact ordinary diagonal D(exp(-NH lambda_r)). Write

    g0=C E_(NH),    C in 1+u.

All nonempty off-diagonal ordinary weights lambda_k-lambda_r are strictly positive. Hence

    Ad_(E_s)(C^(-1)) -> I as s->infinity.

Consequently, for sufficiently large positive s,

    Q_s=E_H Ad_(E_s)(C^(-1)) lies in U subset S.

The actual source P0=Q_s E_s obeys

    P0 g0
      = E_H E_s C^(-1) E_(-s) E_s C E_(NH)
      = E_(H+s+NH).

Therefore P=P0 D0 is an actual finite positive source satisfying P g=E_T, with T=H+s+NH. The inverse and negative E_(-s) occur only in selecting the point Q_s inside an already verified actual chart; the realized source is a positive word for Q_s followed by positive ordinary padding and positive words for D0. No negative-time factor is physically executed.

This completes the equivalence.

## 3. Exact cost dependence

Choose any block maximum norm on u and let gamma be the smallest allowed positive ordinary weight. If a ball of radius rho around I satisfies E_H(I+V) in U, and M=||C^(-1)-I||, then any positive s with

    exp(-gamma s) M < rho

suffices. The diagonal correction integer N is chosen so the vector (-log b_r(g)/N) lies in the diagonal slice of U around E_H.

Thus the construction is finite at the fixed cap, but depends on the ACTUAL ordinary-chart neighborhood, all diagonal coordinates of g, and its unipotent inverse norm. It supplies no uniformity in cap. Even for an ordinary-diagonal seed, where D0 is unnecessary, the contraction time contains log(M/rho)/gamma. Neither a uniform rho nor a summable sequence of such clock costs has been established.

In particular, this argument does not contradict the earlier padding obstruction: it does not make an ordinary point appear in a chart lacking one. It starts from an actual chart already centered at E_H and uses it to realize an inverse-shaped complementary endpoint.

## 4. Exact projective extension attempt

At cap n, projection to cap n-1 preserves the same word and physical parameters. If ordinary interior is known at cap n-1, the cofinality result supplies an actual word whose product with a given projected source has an ordinary COMPLETE lower-cap endpoint. Lifting that complement word back to cap n is legitimate: it uses the same source parameters at all arities.

However, the lift carries an uncontrolled new no-merger coordinate b_n and new forest row. Even before using the square-zero new-arity residual layer, one must solve the NEW diagonal defect on the exact lower-return fibre:

    delta_n(W)=log b_n(W)-lambda_n log b2(W),
    W_(n-1)=E_h.

The accepted full-rank ordinary diagonal theorem works on the unconstrained source domain. It does not show that delta_n takes both signs after every lower forest row has been fixed. A positive lower complement may contribute a defect of either magnitude or a fixed sign on that relative fibre. Its value cannot be prescribed from its lower endpoint.

The pure-diagonal endpoints D_* used in Section 2 are available only inside a FULL cap-n ordinary chart. Applying that step with a cap-(n-1) chart and treating its lift as pure diagonal at cap n would assume the very induction conclusion being sought. This is the precise circular step in the proposed extension proof.

After the new diagonal is fixed, the accepted new-arity square-zero algebra still requires an ACTUAL sign-rich constrained source family or another exact positive correction theorem. Ambient group generation and arbitrary signed ordinary times do not provide those physical relative endpoints. The global primitive-cocycle no-go does not apply automatically to a function defined only on this constrained lower-return fibre.

## 5. Outcome of the complete attempt

The fixed-cap cofinality equivalence is a valid proposed source-group argument, with its physical realization and clock dependencies explicit. Starting from accepted cap-five ordinary interior, it yields cap-five positive left complements for arbitrary cap-five source-group points. It does not propagate ordinary interior even to the next cap without the missing constrained source theorem.

Therefore (1) remains unproved. There is no established all-cap existence, uniform target budget, or fixed-target rival construction from this architecture. A failure of the induction argument is not a source-specific proof that the extension is impossible: the unresolved alternative could be a genuine nonlinear relative-fibre inequality, or a construction that overcomes it.

This record is an incomplete whole-proof attempt, not a new cap ladder. No additional source coefficient calculation is offered as a substitute for the all-cap positive extension and bounded-cost steps.
