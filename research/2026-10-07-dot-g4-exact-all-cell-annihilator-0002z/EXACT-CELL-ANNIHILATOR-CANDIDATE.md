## 3. The conditional component-shape property

Call an exchangeable fresh-forest kernel G a Yule-component kernel when, conditional on any specified output set partition with block sizes s1,...,sr, the component trees are independent with laws Y_(s1),...,Y_(sr). Write p_n(s1,...,sr) for its EPPF: the probability of ONE specified set partition, not an aggregate over partitions of the same sizes. Then

    G_n(T1,...,Tr)=p_n(s1,...,sr) product_i H(Ti)/C_(si).   (2)

Only positive projective kernels arising below are needed; a general probability-theoretic classification is not assumed.

### Lemma 1: B is a Yule-component kernel

For an ordinary edge, every compatible ordered sequence of n-r pair mergers leading to a specified forest has the same integrated holding-time probability. The number of interleavings of the component histories depends only on their sizes. Thus conditional on the output partition, its shapes have the product Kingman law in (2).

For a bare B(x,y,g), each output block is wholly inside one routing arm. Conditional on which final blocks occupy each arm, the original labels were routed with a weight depending only on the total numbers in the two arms. The two ordinary arm kernels have the preceding shape law, independently. Summing the routing allocations preserves the same product Yule law conditional on the final partition. This uses one initial independent choice for each CURRENT token, not re-routing descendants after a merger. Therefore B has (2).

### Lemma 2: E(a)G is Yule-component whenever G is

Assume G acts on its entering roots by the original opaque-graft rule. Fix a final partition with component trees T_i on s_i original labels. If k_i roots in component i survive the leading ordinary edge, let j_i=s_i-k_i and j=sum_i j_i. The initial ordinary histories never merge different final components. For a given cut of T_i into k_i clades, take a compatible ordinary prefix of j_i pair mergers inside that component and a compatible ranked history of the contracted quotient tree on those k_i opaque roots.

Summing this product of history counts over all cuts with k_i surviving clades gives EXACTLY H(T_i): each full ranked history of T_i has one unique cut after its first j_i events. The j events across the distinct final components have j!/product_i j_i! possible interleavings, a factor depending only on s_i,k_i. Their ordinary holding-time factor depends only on n and the total surviving count k=sum_i k_i. The G partition probability is p_k(k1,...,kr); its quotient-tree factor is product_i H(T_i/cut_i)/C_(ki), independent of the sizes and shapes attached below each opaque root.

Consequently, after summing the cuts and then the k_i, the total dependence on the prescribed component shapes is exactly product_i H(T_i). Normalizing over all trees on the fixed blocks gives (2), since their history counts sum to C_(si). Hence E(a)G is Yule-component. The empty and singleton cases are immediate.

In particular, E(a)B(x,y,g) is Yule-component for every actual parameter tuple. It is projective by the original source process. Lemma 2 is only LEFT ordinary closure; arbitrary right multiplication or products of Yule-component kernels are not asserted to preserve the property.

To finish at a selected six-leaf tree T and three singleton spectators, the starting forest must be a cut of T into l rooted clades, with the same three singleton spectators. Let U be such a cut. ## 5. Exact actual-cell spectral annihilation

    ell(e9 E_9(a) B_9 P4)

The ordinary spectral expansion is exact. Since equality holds for every a in the open interval (0,1), compare polynomial coefficients. The a^36 coefficient on the right is zero, so

## 6. Consequence for arbitrary original positive private words

Let W=K1...KN be any actual finite strict private word, with each K_r an ordinary factor or a bigon followed by its positive connector. Use the inherited scalar diagonals b_j^(r)>0 and blocks U_ab^(r)=P_a K_r P_b. The one-root-drop blocks vanish by original projectivity, so the inherited exact 9-to-4 recurrence gives a single-insertion sum plus two-insertion terms through intermediate root counts 6 and 7.

Equation (1) removes EVERY projected single-insertion term, with no bound on N and no small-parameter assumption. Exactly,

    ell(e9 P9 W P4)
      = sum_(r<t) sum_(k=6,7)
          [product_(l<r) b9^(l)]
          [product_(r<l<t) bk^(l)]
          [product_(l>t) b4^(l)]
             ell(e9 U9k^(r) Uk4^(t)).                  (6)

All products are actual cell-dependent diagonal transports; none is replaced by an ordinary-clock exponential. The two blocks in each bilinear term are constrained by the same original physical parameters of their respective cells. No signed source generator or independent control of matrix entries is introduced.

An ordinary target has zero left side, so (6), together with its exact lower-band constraints, is a necessary condition for an ordinary return. It is not proved sign-definite here. Nonordinary targets have their actual left side, and the fixed one-bigon target has zero by (1) and pad scaling, but this does not establish their higher-response rigidity either.

The next direct obligation is a source-faithful factorization/sign or equality analysis of the TWO actual-cell bilinear terms in (6), under the complete coupled lower-band/diagonal target equations.
