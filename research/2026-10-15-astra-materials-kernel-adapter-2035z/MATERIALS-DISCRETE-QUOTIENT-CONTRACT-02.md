## One selected contract, pending the integration owner's agreement

**Endpoint:** prove or refute target-preserving strong lumpability of the selected descriptor for the ideal fixed-50/50 MC family, preserving all declared temperatures, swap controls, readouts and lattice dependence. A negative answer must state the exact descriptor and parameter/size regime it refutes.

1. **Published profile.** Keep all nine supported classes. The prior audit's main E_PM_best_bw_bayes fit has eight active couplings, in meV/bond: ip1=14.06319439678522, ap=23.121510844950343, Y=24.310382819733455, ip2=4.275729528405308, apd=-7.201067472252812, Yd=2.441465280303593, ip3=-3.0213174072952786, c2=1.847522750175608; ip4=0 through V.get. A different or zero-coupling model is not automatically a counterexample at these fitted coefficients. Exact decimal-rational versus binary-float interpretation must be frozen before a numerical equality claim.

2. **Geometry and sector.** Keep the periodic weighted multigraph with every offset multiplicity and its layer parity. Exact 50/50 requires even N=L*L*Lz and valid starts; the script does not validate those conditions. The chemistry requires even Lz. The published L=Lz=8,12,16 cases must all remain in scope. The prior audit's L>=5, even Lz>=6 no-alias range is a proposed extension domain, not a checked theorem or a replacement for the declared size family. Smaller admitted sizes require their literal loops/aliases and a checked swap-energy binding. A witness at an excluded tiny size does not refute the published-size assertion.

3. **Two distinct candidate maps.** Let S_c=(1/2)*sum of directed class-c spin products, with multiplicity. D_pair is the nine S_c values. D_aug adds every Fourier amplitude actually retained by make_phases at that lattice, in source order. Half-frequency modes depend on divisibility by four. All nine SRO values, including ip4, remain required. A witness against D_pair cannot be promoted to a witness against D_aug. The final choice of map must be identical in the adapter and counterexample work.

4. **Literal attempt law.** Local proposals choose i uniformly and one of NB[i,0:4] uniformly: only the four in-plane ip1 entries. Arbitrary proposals choose i,j uniformly, including i=j. The implemented law is their 50/50 mixture. Equal-spin and rejected proposals are self loops, not conditioned redraws. Acceptance uses the literal source dE expression; its equality to the Hamiltonian difference must be justified on the admitted geometry. Pure local/arbitrary kernels are component controls, not separate CLI options. A component failure must not be claimed for the implemented mixture without checking that mixture's fibre mass.

5. **Full control/temperature quantifiers.** Use a common descriptor at each admitted geometry/profile for every selected control and temperature. The proposed strongest temperature family is all finite T>0; it includes the recorded menus but is an explicitly labelled mathematical extension, not a report that all T were simulated. Preserve the 50/50 kernel and both requested component kernels separately. Actual cooling/adaptive schedules and block sampling remain ordered experiments; no replacement by independent temperature rows. A grid-only result or T=infinity example is not the all-positive-T result.

6. **Precise dynamic premise.** Set Y=image(D) and P(s,y)=1[D(s)=y]. For every selected control a, all admitted s,s' with D(s)=D(s'), and every y, require
   sum_{t:D(t)=y} K_a(s,t) = sum_{t:D(t)=y} K_a(s',t).
   With stochastic K_a this defines a stochastic B_a and gives K_a*P=P*B_a in the accepted row convention. All rejected/ordered proposal mass counts. This equality must be proved or refuted for the actual instance, not stored as an assumed scientific capability.

7. **Readout and path premise.** Require every target readout O to factor as O_bar composed with D, and transport the initial distribution by the same P. Preserve the complete joint readout path law, so the same block/temperature protocol retains energy and Fourier means, heat-capacity/Binder estimators, all SROs, q_max tie order, thresholding and undefined/NaN conventions. The source also returns the final labelled spin array. If that array is itself a required deterministic readout, its factoring forces D to be injective; if only the scientific summaries are required, that target boundary must be explicit. It must not be silently dropped.

8. **Refutation evidence.** Supply exact admitted s,s', equal selected D, and either unequal required readouts or unequal total probability into a specified destination fibre for an admitted finite T, profile and kernel. Preserve the exact coefficients and proposal multiplicities. State any interval/family analytically established and all degenerate or remaining regimes. One witness refutes its universal claim; it does not classify all sizes, prove a minimal descriptor or exclude every stronger representation.
