# The saved cap-five rejection in the fixed-reference spectral bands

Contributor: Codex Terra, 31 October 2026. This is a reuse and source-overlap
audit of the already saved three-outlier rejection. It derives one exact
whole-word consequences at five current roots and identifies their overlap
with the inherited centered sources. They are not a new candidate family,
a sufficient full-fibre estimate, a phase theorem, or a solution of G3/G4.

The actual source, both paired clocks, the unchanged reference S, opaque
grafting, the same original physical tuple, strict parameters and actual
chronology are retained throughout. All spectral projections are algebraic
postprocessing, not extra legal observations or physical inverse edges.

## 1. What the saved rejection actually measures

Put M=TS-ST for the exact three-outlier word T in
`CAP4-AND-TWO-NORMALS-OUTLIER.md`. The original checker uses the canonical
fresh-five orbit row and

    Psi_raw(M) = M[0,4]/6 + M[0,6]/6 + M[0,8].          (1)

The pinned archive helper's ordering gives:

| Index | Output forest | Current roots |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 2 | one rooted triple, two singletons | 3 |
| 3 | two cherries, one singleton | 3 |
| 4 | one four-leaf comb, one singleton | 2 |
| 5 | one balanced four-tree, one singleton | 2 |
| 6 | one rooted triple, one cherry | 2 |
| 8 | a singleton joined to a balanced four-tree | 1 |

Thus (1) is not itself a 5-to-2/drop-three functional: it includes a
one-root output. The old exact computation gives zero coefficients through
epsilon^5 and coefficient `(3/64) P5` at epsilon^6; the saved interval for
P5 is strictly negative on that branch. Its full polynomial and interval
certificate remain in the original checker and its log, not regenerated here.

Let P_r be the ordinary-generator root-count spectral projector and m be
the fresh-five row of M. Exact complete cap4 commutation makes all rows on
four or fewer current roots zero, and diagonal/adjacent-drop commutator
entries vanish. Sampling deletion and grafting then give the exact identity

    Psi_raw(M) = -2 A + (7/6) B + C,                       (2)
    A=(m P_3)[2]/8,  B=(m P_2)[4],  C=(m P_1)[8].

These are also the **fixed-S** 5-to-3, 5-to-2 and 5-to-1 spectral components.
Indeed for every original projective source the adjacent-root-drop bands
vanish, and the inherited identity `3 t_3+(3-3)d_3=0` makes its remaining
3-to-1 band vanish. Therefore S is diagonal in the ordinary spectral frame
on the carrier with at most three current roots. Its strictly positive
ordinary bookends make s1>s2>s3, so its fixed-S and ordinary projectors
coincide on that carrier. Since m is supported there, (2) is unchanged by
using the fixed-S projectors. On these components

    P5^S M Pr^S = (s_r-s_5) P5^S T Pr^S, r=1,2,3.

This fixed-S identification applies to the **commutator after cap4
commutation**. The bare-source proportionalities below are in the ordinary
Q spectral frame; they must not be asserted unchanged after separately
conjugating a bare cell into the fixed-S frame.

The saved nonzero scalar therefore proves that **at least one** of A,B,C
is nonzero on the IFT branch. It does not establish B!=0, and this audit does
not attribute the old epsilon^6 polynomial to an individual spectral band.
Individual jet coefficients can depend on derivatives of the IFT branch;
the old scalar's lower coefficients vanish identically, which is why its
own saved certificate needs no such derivatives.

## 2. Exact physical source equations in all three bands

For a bare equal-arm cell B(q,q,g), let p5(3,1,1) and p5(2,2,1) be
probabilities of one specified set partition, including all within-block
rooted shapes. Set

    A5(B)=3 p5(2,2,1)-2 p5(3,1,1),   d5(B)=A5(B)/7.

Let rho53 be the orbit mass coefficient of the two-cherries-plus-singleton
shape in the fresh row of P5 B P3, tau52 the orbit mass coefficient of the
four-comb-plus-singleton shape in P5 B P2, and gamma51 the one-root output
index 8 in P5 B P1. The inherited general
projective and bare-cell Yule identities specialize at n=5 to

    w5=0,  6 u5=-7 d5/6,
    rho53(B)=15 d5(B)=(15/7) A5(B),
    tau52(B)=60 u5(B)=-(35/3)d5(B)=-(5/3)A5(B)
             =-(7/9) rho53(B),
    gamma51(B)=(7/12)rho53(B)=(5/4)A5(B).                  (3)

Here u5,w5 are **per-labelled** spectral coefficients; the factors 60 and
15 are the actual orbit multiplicities. The first two relations in (3) use
the inherited projective/Yule source identities. The third was checked as an
exact Q[q,beta] identity against the pinned full orbit source engine.
Equivalently, in the pinned bare source orbit matrix,

    A5(B)=(B[0,3]-B[0,2])/5,
    tau52(B)=(B[0,2]-B[0,3])/3,
    rho53(B)=3(B[0,3]-B[0,2])/7,
    gamma51(B)=(B[0,3]-B[0,2])/4.                         (4)

The n=5 drop-three band is thus one-dimensional and uses an OLD d_n source;
it is not a new freely selectable bare-cell generator. Equation (3) is a
**bare-cell** relation. It must not be applied unchanged to an arbitrary
word or to a bare cell after an ordinary gap, because the three bands have
different right diagonal transports. The gamma51 check is an exact
polynomial identity over all q,beta, not a finite parameter grid, and reuses
the pinned orbit generator rather than reconstructing the whole source model.

Let K be any actual chronological word of bare B_i and ordinary factors.
Write b_r(F) for the r-current-root no-merger diagonal of factor F and set

    W_(5,r),i = product_(f before B_i) b5(f)
                 * product_(f after B_i) br(f), r=1,2,3.

Every actual factor and every ordinary gap is included. A total root drop
two or three has exactly one nonzero insertion because adjacent-drop bands
vanish. Drop four also has only one: its only potential two-insertion path
is 5-to-3-to-1, and the second block is zero on every actual source.
Consequently

    tau52(K)=-(5/3) sum_i W_(5,2),i A5(B_i),
    rho53(K)= (15/7) sum_i W_(5,3),i A5(B_i),
    gamma51(K)=(5/4) sum_i W_(5,1),i A5(B_i).             (5)

Define k_r=b_r(K), s_r=b_r(S), B_*=B(x,x,1/2), and write the reference as
S=E(t) B_* E(b) with x=t/(2-t), b=t^2(2-t). If the **complete** cap5
commutator vanishes, its three projections give, exactly,

    sum_i W_(5,2),i A5(B_i)
      = t^10 b * (k2-k5)/(s2-s5) * A5(B_*),
    sum_i W_(5,3),i A5(B_i)
      = t^10 b^3 * (k3-k5)/(s3-s5) * A5(B_*),
    sum_i W_(5,1),i A5(B_i)
      = t^10 * (1-k5)/(1-s5) * A5(B_*).                    (6)

All displayed denominators are strictly positive for these strict sources.
Equations (6) use the SAME tuple and exact unknown k_r; they do not set
k_r=s_r^nu. The positive first right-hand side is for the **A5-normalized**
receiver `-(3/5)tau52`; raw tau52(B_*) is negative. Eliminating the
reference source from the first two equations gives the exact signed moment

    sum_i W_(5,3),i (R_i-Lambda) A5(B_i)=0,                (7)
    R_i=product_(f after B_i) b2(f)/b3(f),
    Lambda=b^(-2) * (k2-k5)/(k3-k5) * (s3-s5)/(s2-s5).

More explicitly, (2)--(5) trace the **original saved raw functional** to
the three actual whole-word residuals. Define, for r=1,2,3,

    Delta_r=(s_r-s5) sum_i W_(5,r),i A5(B_i)
             -(k_r-k5)t^10 b^binom(r,2) A5(B_*),
    s1=k1=1.

After cap4 commutation, with M=KS-SK, the exact identity is

    Psi_raw(M) = (5/7)Delta_3 - (35/18)Delta_2 + (5/4)Delta_1. (7a)

This is a signed combination, not an assertion that the old negative scalar
alone identifies which individual Delta_r fails.

The R_i are positive and decrease along the actual chronology. If all A5
values have one sign, (7) puts Lambda between their extreme R_i values; the
unrestricted source has no such sign premise. In fact near u=0 the fair
source is positive, while every fixed nonfair beta>0 has a negative leading
A5 term. The equation is therefore not a global positivity certificate.

For a word with COMMON survival c=exp(-H), each actual no-merger diagonal
obeys b_r(F)>=c(F)^binom(r,2). Thus W_(5,1), W_(5,2), W_(5,3) all lie in
[exp(-10H),1], independently of cell count, coin margin and placement.
These bounds do not remove the signed cancellation in (7).

### A strictly narrower necessary positive-charge cone

The same exact Q[q,beta] source calculation factors A5 as

    A5(q,beta) = q(1-beta)(1-q)^2/40
      * [(1-q)(q^2+3q+1)-beta L(q)],
    L(q)=3q^3+6q^2+14q+7.                                 (7b)

For every strict cell, L(q)>0. The fair reference has A5(B_*)>0, so the
positive A5-normalized first endpoint in (6) forces at least one source
cell to satisfy

    beta < beta_A(q):=(1-q)(q^2+3q+1)/L(q).               (7c)

This condition is strictly stronger than the already established cap4
existence condition beta<beta_chi(q):=(1-q)/(5+q), because

    beta_chi-beta_A = 2(1-q)^3(q+1)/[(q+5)L(q)] > 0.       (7d)

In particular a single bare cell on the exact cap4 charge-zero locus has

    A5(q,beta_chi) = -q(q+1)(q+2)(1-q)^5/[10(q+5)^2] < 0. (7e)

The condition concerns the existence of a positively charged cell. It does
not place every source cell in this narrow cone, and the formal rank check
below does not show that (7c) is independent of every old full-source
inequality.

The existing whole-forest minorization also gives a quantitative source-mass
version. For the same paired original fibre put

    gamma(H)=exp(-3H)*(1-exp(-3H))/24 > 0.

The independently reviewed gap-budget argument proves
`k2-k4>=gamma(H)`, hence `k2-k5>=gamma(H)`. For 0<h<=1/4 the reference
obeys t^10 b>=exp(-3), s2-s5<=40h and
A5(B_*)>=exp(-1/4)h^3/640. Thus (6) yields

    sum_i W_(5,2),i A5(B_i)
       >= exp(-13/4) gamma(H) h^2 / 25600.                 (7f)

Writing d_i=1-q_i, (7b), q L(q)<=30, and W_(5,2),i<=1 imply

    sum_i v_i d_i^2 [beta_A(q_i)-beta_i]_+
       >= exp(-13/4) gamma(H) h^2 / 76800.                 (7g)

This forces weighted positive mass in the stricter cone, with explicit H
dependence. It does not control the negative A5 complement, all cell scales,
the phase, or an integer witness.

## 3. Exact source overlap and the leading scale

Put u=-log q, v=g(1-g), beta=(2g-1)^2, b2=q+2v(1-q),
tau_pair=-log b2 and C=exp(-tau_pair Q)B. As in the inherited sources,
J_n(C)=exp(binomial(n,2) tau_pair) A_n(B), F_n=J_n/15. The actual normalized
5-to-2 bare coefficient is

    tau52(C) = -25 F5(C).                                  (8)

Independent exact enumeration of the five-labelled-root two-merger
histories and their holding terms gives

    F5(C) = -(1-beta) beta u^2/20
            +(1-beta)(1-11 beta-30 beta^2)u^3/120
            + O(u^4).                                       (9)

For fixed bounded Z,Y with q=1-epsilon Z and beta=epsilon Y,

    F5(C)/epsilon^3 -> Z^2 (Z-6Y)/120.                     (10)

The limit is uniform on each fixed bounded scaled box. It is NOT a uniform
relative estimate on unbounded Y or a positive lower bound for microscopic
cells. A direct exact rational actual-source check at g=1/3,q=2/3 confirms
the normalization and (3)--(4); its one case does not prove all-parameter
source identities.

The completed all-n source gives the same leading polynomial for F7 and the
old F9. Its overlap is actually stronger. Let sigma,rho be the SAME
normalized transverse sources used in `CENTERED-MATCHED-ACTION.md`, and

    s(C)=v*(beta^2*u^3+beta*u^4+u^5).

The archived all-n deletion rule gives
`[u^3](J_n-J_(n-1))=-6 v beta^2`. Its fair rule gives the quintic
coefficients 1/40 for J5 and 1/4 for J9. With the old sigma/rho cubic and
fair quintic values, the entire fair-slice derivative identity
`C_beta|0=-2(C_fair)_u`, and the endpoint-v factor, one obtains for small u
and ALL strict coins, with a constant uniform through the coin endpoints,

    F5(C) = F9(C)+(4/15)sigma(C)+(14/15)rho(C)+O(u*s(C)). (11)

For the coefficient check, `J5-J9` has cubic `24 v beta^2` and fair
quintic `-9 u^5/40`; `4 sigma+14 rho` has exactly those two values.
The fair derivative identity removes the remaining mixed quartic. After
factoring v, the remaining quartic is divisible by beta^2 and the remaining
quintic by beta, giving the stated `u*s` bound. This is a hand consequence
of the cited all-n/centered suppliers, not a separate source-engine run.

There is an additional direct overlap with the old cap4 leading charge.
Let ell=log(b2/q), let C_* be the fair reference bare cell at x=1-epsilon,
and put kappa5=F5(C_*)/ell(C_*). Since ell/epsilon -> Z/2 and
kappa5/epsilon^2 -> 1/60,

    [F5(C)-kappa5 ell]/epsilon^3
       -> [Z^3-6YZ^2-Z]/120.                               (12)

For the saved fair/biased/fair cells `(Z,Y)=(A,0),(X,Y),(A,0)`, the sum of
(12)'s leading polynomials is exactly the saved cap4 G1/120. This is a
single-cell calibrated comparison, not an assertion that ell has the
whole-word 5-to-2 weights. It explains why the leading weak-scale source
alone does not add a new independent local constraint to that partial
system.

## 4. Formal overlap versus the actual unresolved implication

The exact rational audit constructs a SIGNED projective/grafted top-row
space at n=5,6,7, imposing complete cap4 zero, scalar diagonal zero and
adjacent-drop zero. Its constraint rank is 39. Adding Psi7 gives rank 40;
adding the second independent U74 coordinate gives 41; adding U64 gives 42;
adding U53 gives 43; adding U52 gives 44. Thus U52 is not a linear formal
consequence of these projected conditions and projectivity alone, even
after the entire U74 band, U64 and U53 are set to zero. This is not a
physical source, a constructed commutator, or a proof of independence under
all full-fibre/source inequalities. In particular the test does not impose
the 9-root channels of the selected R representation. The inherited R-only
phase-flexibility theorem remains at its original quotient scope; this audit
neither claims its actual words pass nor fail the new U52 condition.

After using (11), the exact 5-to-2 equation (6) has the centered-source
combination F9+(4 sigma+14 rho)/15 plus a controlled per-cell O(u*s) term,
but these quantities occur with W_(5,2),i exp(-10 tau_pair,i). The old Psi7
receiver and centered/selected-R identities have different actual prefix,
suffix and reference-jump transports. The paired clocks do not prescribe
k5 or replace these weights by the old ones. The missing step is therefore
a source-valid, full-fibre control of the **signed, differently weighted
chronological residual** together with its actual endpoint, strong enough
for the existing action/phase use-site. Neither the formal rank test, the
same leading polynomial nor a per-cell O(u*s) estimate supplies that step.
