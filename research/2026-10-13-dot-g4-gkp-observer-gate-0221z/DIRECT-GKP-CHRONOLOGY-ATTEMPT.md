## 5. Tested repairs and exact failure points

### Observe a graph by sampling its vertices

Only clique no-merger tests have the direct source interpretation above. A P3 or cycle density uses a specified interaction graph on static independently sampled types. Original coalescence does not permit selecting arbitrary pair clocks while leaving the others disabled; after a merger its current-root state also changes. A forest probability or a power of the observed word matrix therefore cannot simply be renamed as the latent cycle integral. The accepted P3 nonrecovery theorem is an exact control against a universal finite version of this substitution.

### Add clock/position markers to the kernel

An ordered, time-marked or gadget-encoded kernel could record the missing connectors and chronological order mathematically. Its new motif densities, however, are not supplied legal observations. Merely defining enough latent markers to encode the source reinstates exactly the decoding obligation. The preceding pad construction refutes the original unmarked product kernel; it does not prove that every enriched encoding fails.

### Use 1614 to bound the original INDEPENDENT rival

1614 preserves a COMMON representative under the stated protected interface and target-derived exposing-row/witness premises. It does not preserve the original INDEPENDENT law. Already in the equal-arm private class, for any C in (0,1) and every L≥1 choose

```

```

with strict coins. Every resulting word has COMMON survival C, while its static operator rank is 2^L and its INDEPENDENT all-copy length invariant is L. These are actual finite strict words; algebraic C gives algebraic survivals. An ordinary COMMON representative therefore cannot be used as a length bound on the original INDEPENDENT source. This is an inherited-model control, not a counterexample to 1614.

### Extend the all-copy inverse across a compactification

The old spectral reduction already isolates the weaker target-relative zero Z_T for one supplied biased cell. It asks whether an exact-target chronological hierarchy in the source-derived countable compactification forces a specific C4–C8 spectral square to vanish. Its rank, continuity, finite-persistent classification and local source arguments are accepted. Z_T remains open. Finite-source all-copy injectivity plus continuity along an array does not make the motif constant on a new limiting fibre. Repeating those completed operator calculations is not progress on the observer implication.

Stop this activation route here rather than adding graphon/rank/motif bookkeeping. General G4 is neither proved nor disproved.
