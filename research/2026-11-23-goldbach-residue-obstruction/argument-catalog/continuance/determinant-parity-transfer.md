# Determinant transfer for the parity-weighted long-divisor sum

This is a hand corollary of the pinned unequal-box determinant argument. It retains the original target \(N\), block, exact middle mask, \(Y\)-rough support, and cutoff \(d>D\). Write \(H=\sqrt N\), \(L=\log N\), \(P=H^{41/40}\), \(n=d\ell=N-qh\), and \(w=-\mu(h)\mu(d)\log h\log\ell\). All bounds below are \(O_A(N/L^A)\) for every fixed \(A>0\).

1. On \(\ell\le P\), the sum of \(\mu(n)w\) is negligible, with the original \(d>D\) still imposed. An additional fixed interval condition on \(d\) or \(\ell\), whose endpoints may depend on \(N\), is allowed.
2. In particular, in the remaining \(d>P\) range, the signed \(w\)-sums over \(\mu(n)=+1\) and over \(\mu(n)=-1\) differ by a negligible quantity. The original untwisted sum on that range remains open.
3. Where both \(d,\ell\le P\), each of those two parity-restricted signed sums is negligible.

## Transfer

For squarefree \(n\), \(\mu(n)\mu(d)=\mu(\ell)\). After inserting the extra factor \(\mu(n)\), relabel \(b=\ell,\ c=d\). The exact equation and weights become
\[
 qh+bc=N,\qquad -\mu(h)\mu(b)\log h\log b.
\]
Now \(b\le P\) is the divisor carrying Möbius, while \(c\) is the variable smoothed in the determinant proof. The logarithm \(\log b\) is part of the arbitrary arithmetic coefficient on \(b\). In the original determinant proof that logarithm was on the smooth variable. Moving it to \(b\) increases its coefficient norm by at most \(L\), while removing it from the smooth function preserves its derivative bounds. The sharp condition \(c>D\) is now an interval edge on the smooth variable, as are any further interval restrictions on \(c\). There is no additional cutoff requirement on \(b\). This last detail matters for large original \(d\). The pinned power budget is valid for all dyadic scales of the signed divisor up to \(P\). [Pinned unequal determinant argument](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/ec0d19f39f6024e75d4e4403b43bfe82e5c4b882/research/2026-11-23-goldbach-residue-obstruction/argument-catalog/continuance/determinant-asymmetric-range.md).

Here is the check on the changes to the proof. Trim \(n\le N/L^B\) by the same absolute endpoint bound with \(B\) large in terms of \(A\). On retained boxes \(bc\) has scale at least \(N/L^B\), so \(c\) has scale \(\gg H^{39/40}/L^B\), just as required for the smooth divisor in the pinned argument. Smooth just inside the true dyadic interval intersected with \(c>D\) and any extra interval restriction. At a dyadic scale \(C\) for \(c\) and \(U\) for \(b\), omitting a transition zone of width \(C/L^J\) costs \(O(UC/L^J+U)\) candidate \(b,c\) pairs. This includes very short interval intersections, which can be discarded in the same count. The pinned sparse-pair divisor-moment bound absorbs the cost for large fixed \(J\), since \(UC\ll N\) and \(U\le P\) up to a fixed factor. The smooth derivatives are bounded by fixed powers of \(L\), on the same scaled coordinates as before. Neither the five masks nor the coprimality condition changes: the roughness of \(c\) is expanded as for the old smooth divisor, and the roughness and allowed interval of \(b\) are in the signed coefficient. In the zero mode, the extra \(\log b\) is independent of \(h\) and can be taken outside the masked \(h\)-prefix estimate, leaving at most one more factor \(L\). Thus both modes retain the same power or arbitrary logarithmic saving. [Endpoint trim](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/ec0d19f39f6024e75d4e4403b43bfe82e5c4b882/research/2026-11-23-goldbach-residue-obstruction/argument-catalog/continuance/determinant-endpoint-trim.md).

In applying the determinant proof, the squarefreeness restriction on \(n\) can be removed from the swapped expression at negligible absolute cost. A nonsquarefree rough \(n\) is divisible by \(p^2\) for some \(p>Y\), so there are \(O(N/Y)\) such endpoints. At fixed \(n\) the absolute swapped weight is at most \(L^2\tau(n)\tau(N-n)\). Cauchy–Schwarz and fixed divisor moments give \(O(N L^{O(1)}/\sqrt Y)\). The same argument removes any exceptional nonsquarefree endpoints from the untwisted expression when comparing the parity sectors. [Pinned squarefree reduction](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/ec0d19f39f6024e75d4e4403b43bfe82e5c4b882/research/2026-11-23-goldbach-residue-obstruction/argument-catalog/continuance/determinant-fixed-box.md).

For conclusion 2, \(d>P\) forces \(\ell<N/P=H^{39/40}<P\). The transferred estimate is a bound on the difference of the two original signed parity sums. For conclusion 3, the unmodified determinant argument applies on \(d\le P\) with the extra interval \(\ell\le P\) handled by the same smooth-edge check. It bounds the sum of the parity contributions, while the transferred estimate bounds their difference. This proves the separate bounds on their common support. In the larger original \(d>P\) range, only the parity-weighted combination is controlled by this transfer.
