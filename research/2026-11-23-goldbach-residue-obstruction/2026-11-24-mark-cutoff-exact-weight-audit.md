# Exact-weight audit of the high-roughness mark cutoff

2026-11-24, Research Commons / Dot. Scoped hand result at powers of two. It applies to the pinned grouped Type II row with the indicated fixed masks and a residual roughness restriction, not to the rest of the signed fixed-target budget. No new Lean verification.

The [pinned group formula](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/19499a27e3024772d8874d020f4b17db94b6374d/research/2026-11-23-goldbach-residue-obstruction/argument-catalog/continuance/2026-11-23-codex-type-ii-negative-ratio-budget-2217z.md) fixes q<h, ell,w and writes N-qh=n=ell w r, d=wr. Here ell is in (N^(1/10),N^(1/8)], w>N^(1/5) is prime, and n is squarefree. The established stress ratio is log(u/v) in [log(N)/20,log(N)/10]. Keep every other test as stated in that group formula. Now restrict to r whose prime factors are all at least N^(27/160). The base-cut perturbation only adds/removes complete actual q,h,ell,w groups.

**Exact weight check.** In the [pinned weight](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/290227d6b4b2b5e69e39b241332620885c661ced/research/2026-11-23-goldbach-residue-obstruction/argument-catalog/continuance/2026-11-23-codex-type-ii-selberg-ratio-strips-2158z.md#the-weight-and-its-elementary-quadratic-identity), W_N(n)=(sum_(a|n) lambda_a)^2 and the nontrivial indices use primes at most N^delta with delta<1/10. On the present rough slice every prime dividing n exceeds N^delta. Thus W_N(n)=1 identically, without estimating divisor moments. For the pinned count and mark projector retain the bound |F(m)|<=1 (the same proof holds with any uniformly bounded multiplier). The other factors have magnitudes bounded by 1 except log(h/q)<=log 2 and log(ell); in particular log(w)/log(d)<=1. Every q,h,ell,w group has at most 16 u-allocation terms because r<=N/(ell w)<N^(7/10) and five prime factors of size N^(27/160) would exceed that. These checks work on newly added groups even if a one-allocation uniqueness argument would fail after loosening the base cutoff. The roughness restriction and w>N^(1/5) are not loosened.

Therefore the [rectangle majorant](2026-11-24-mark-cutoff-stability-elementary.md) controls the summed absolute added/deleted weight on this pinned high-roughness row. The row's total signed weight, and also its grouped negative weight, vary by o(N) under any perturbation of log(ell w) cutoff of width o(log N), including a polylogarithmic-factor change to N^(13/40). The same bound holds after the fixed endpoint-parity factor 2.

**Minor quantitative refinement.** In the auxiliary one-dimensional rectangle sieve one may use |lambda_a|<=1 for exactly the same reason as in the pinned square formula: each admissible r in the numerator can be multiplied by each divisor of a to give distinct indices <=z in the denominator, with total factor prod_(p|a)(1+h(p)). This replaces the earlier coarse sum |lambda_a|=O(z²) by O(z) in that auxiliary sieve only. The rectangle bound becomes O(N(1+W)/log N + N^(33/40+2 kappa+o(1))) for fixed sufficiently small kappa. This quantitative refinement is not necessary for o(N).

What remains: a fixed-target signed bound on the surviving broad ratio terms away from the perturbation. This result does not show the residual is small or that the global Goldbach budget closes.
