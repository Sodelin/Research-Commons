## 1. Exact obligation and prior comparison

The accepted finite-persistent classification assumes a fixed FINITE set of persistent cells and that the maximum remaining strength tends to zero. It does not assert that every array has such a subsequence. The October 9 failed-global record assumes a COUNTABLE diagonal representation before analyzing its quadratic, linear and logarithmic asymptotics. The present argument checks how to obtain a countable chronological representation directly from bounded-clock actual arrays. It does not reuse the finite-persistent assumption as if it were general compactness, or repair the divergent bias-over-duration energy step.

Let W_r be arbitrary finite equal-arm private natural BOTH words on the same deterministic COMMON clock [0,H], with H>0. Each cell has a literal interval [s,s+t], duration t>0, original coin g in (0,1), and p=g(1-g). Ordinary passages fill the remaining clock. Cells have disjoint interiors. Each word uses its one physical tuple in the original chronological order, for every finite input size. The source-derived calibration to this carrier and the same-bank natural BOTH menu are required; arbitrary uncalibrated networks and extra forced-program interfaces are outside this statement.

The accepted uniform full-forest estimate, in maximum row l1 norm on any fixed finite cap m, is

    ||B(t,p)-E((1-2p)t)|| <= C_(m,H) p t^2.                 (1)

Set w=p(exp(t)-1). The inherited stochastic telescoping bound gives

    sum p_i t_i^2 <= (max w_i) sum t_i <= H max w_i.        (2)

Here E_s denotes ordinary duration s, with pair survival exp(-s). Weak ordinary replacement is only an approximation of the INDEPENDENT kernel, not an exact BOTH-law replacement of a finite cell.

## 2. One nested subsequence, preserving positions and clock measure

For each r, rank the cells by nonincreasing w, breaking ties by their original source position. Pad the list with zero-duration, zero-strength dummy entries. Retain the position s_(r,j), duration t_(r,j), and the ACTUAL oriented coin g_(r,j) of rank j. Equal-arm natural kernels are arm-exchange invariant, but no independent coin is fitted to a different row.

Because exp(t)-1<=exp(H)t and p<=1/4,

    sum_j w_(r,j) <= exp(H)H/4 = L_H,
    w_(r,j) <= L_H/j.                                    (3)

All coordinates lie in compact intervals. A nested diagonal subsequence makes every ranked triple converge simultaneously. Write w_j for the limiting strengths, in nonincreasing order. The positive w_j form an initial finite segment or all positive integers; w_j tends to zero. A positive w_j implies t_j>0 and 0<g_j<1. For any finite selected set their limiting intervals have disjoint interiors, since the approximating intervals do. Hence

    sum_(w_j>0) t_j <= H.                                 (4)

The chronological order is read from these limiting intervals, not the rank order. It may have no first member and may have arbitrary countable accumulation. No omega-order assumption is introduced.

Define the measure nu_r by its density on [0,H]: it is 1 on each ordinary passage and 1-2p_(r,j) on each cell interval. Thus

    (1/2) Lebesgue <= nu_r <= Lebesgue.

On a further subsequence, nu_r converges weakly to one measure nu. A diagonal construction on rational interval integrals, or weak compactness of these uniformly bounded densities, supplies this subsequence without reference to a sample cap. The same inequalities hold for nu; in particular it has a density r(s) in [1/2,1] and no atoms.

On the interior of every persistent interval I_j=(s_j,s_j+t_j), nu has the CONSTANT density 1-2p_j. Indeed, any continuous test function supported in a compact subinterval lies in that same ranked cell for all sufficiently large r, and its original density converges to 1-2p_j. This identifies nu there. Let R be the complement of the union of the persistent open intervals. On R, the restriction of nu is the ordinary weak-residue INDEPENDENT clock; Lebesgue measure is its COMMON clock. These clocks must remain attached to the same position set.

All ranked source parameters and the clock measure have now been selected before choosing any m. This is one shared subsequence, not an independently fitted representation at every cap.

## 3. Finite-threshold products and convergence of all complete endpoint kernels

If infinitely many w_j are positive, retain the first k ranked cells. Place them in their actual limiting chronological order. In every gap between these retained intervals, place an ordinary INDEPENDENT factor whose duration is nu of that gap. Keep the retained cell B(t_j,g_j) itself. Call the resulting finite boundary-padded INDEPENDENT product A_k.

The gaps can have zero duration. They include all unretained cells' proxy clocks, including persistent ones not yet retained. This is a finite analytic product used to define the limit; it is not declared a finite strict BOTH source with COMMON duration equal to its INDEPENDENT ordinary gaps. Its coupled COMMON endpoint is E_H. The gap's COMMON expenditure remains its Lebesgue length.

Define A_(r,k) in the original array in the same way, retaining its first k ranked cells and replacing all others by E((1-2p)t). Combining adjacent ordinary pieces is exact. Equations (1)–(2) give

    ||K_m(W_r)-K_m(A_(r,k))||
       <= C_(m,H) H w_(r,k+1)
       <= C_(m,H) H L_H/(k+1).                            (5)

Every retained interval and parameter converges. Gap clock masses converge too: weak convergence controls fixed endpoints because nu has no atoms, and moving endpoints incur at most their displacement since all densities are at most one. Consequently

    K_m(A_(r,k)) -> K_m(A_k)                              (6)

for each fixed k and m.

The products A_k themselves are Cauchy. To compare A_l with A_k, l>k, replace the newly retained cells in A_l by their ordinary proxies. The nu density on every such cell interval is exactly 1-2p_j, so after combining gaps the result is EXACTLY A_k. Stochastic telescoping gives

    ||K_m(A_l)-K_m(A_k)||
       <= C_(m,H) sum_(k<j<=l) p_j t_j^2
       <= C_(m,H) H w_(k+1).                             (7)

Thus A_k converges at every finite complete forest cap to K_m^*. Let k increase in (5)–(7). Along the one already selected subsequence,

    K_m(W_r) -> K_m^*                                    (8)

for EVERY finite m. The cap-dependent constants cause no conflict: the subsequence was chosen on source parameters and measures, and each fixed m separately satisfies the bounds. Every exact projective restriction, label symmetry and opaque-graft identity survives these finite-dimensional limits. Every K_m^* is stochastic and comes from the same chronological interval/clock data. The COMMON endpoint is exactly E_H throughout the approximants and limit.

If only J strengths have positive limits, retain those J cells. Then w_(r,J+1) tends to zero, so the same proof gives a finite boundary-padded proxy product directly. This recovers precisely the finite-persistent case, including J=0. It does not assert that J must be finite.

The result is a countable source-derived generalized endpoint carrier. Its representation need not be unique or recoverable from the endpoint law; no such canonical-identifiability claim is made. It is neither a claim of a finite original graph nor a joint identification of unobserved latent arm trajectories. A coherent time-process realization can be taken from the finite-threshold products using the already reviewed bounded-intensity jump-record construction; for present purposes (8), projectivity and chronological factorization are the claims needed. No equality of hidden path laws is required or asserted.

## 4. Exact diagonal representation and clock consistency

Let lambda_n=binom(n,2), and let d_n(t_j,g_j)>0 be the actual cell no-merger probability. It obeys

    exp(-lambda_n t_j) <= d_n(t_j,g_j) <= 1.

The first inequality follows by coupling to the dominating rate lambda_n: keeping roots in two arms cannot increase their total pair-merger rate beyond the pooled ordinary rate. It also follows directly from the original binomial routing formula. Therefore all logarithmic sums below converge absolutely for each FIXED n, by (4).

Taking diagonal entries in A_k and passing to the limit yields the exact formula

    D_n^I = exp(-lambda_n nu(R)) product_j d_n(t_j,g_j),
    D_n^C = exp(-lambda_n H).

Let delta=Lebesgue(R)-nu(R)>=0 and R_n(t,p)=exp(lambda_n t)d_n(t,g). Then

    log(D_n^I/D_n^C)
      = lambda_n delta + sum_j log R_n(t_j,p_j).          (9)

This is the countable diagonal representation conditional in the failed-global record, now derived for the stated bounded-clock array domain. Its beta in the notation beta*n(n-1) is delta/2. It does not justify interchanging the n-to-infinity logarithmic asymptotic with the sum over j. In particular, no bound on sum (log(g_j/(1-g_j)))^2/t_j is supplied here.

A useful consistency check is the pair row. For a strict persistent cell,

    b2(t,p)=2p+(1-2p)exp(-t)>exp(-t).

Consequently

    log(D_2^I/D_2^C)
      = delta + sum_j [t_j+log b2(t_j,p_j)] >= 0,         (10)

with strict positivity if there is ANY persistent cell, or if delta>0. Thus a paired ordinary target cannot hide either kind of positive residual in this representation. Zero-coin approximating cells may still converge to a genuinely ordinary weak residue with density one; no macroscopic ordinary edge in the approximants is inferred. This recovers the known ordinary limiting boundary rather than constructing an exact-prefix rival.

## 5. The smaller-canonical-prefix obstruction is retained

Suppose there are persistent cells, but their start positions have an infimum sigma which is NOT attained. There is no first persistent cell. On [0,sigma] the generalized representation has only ordinary weak residue, giving a justified INDEPENDENT prefix E_c with

    c=nu([0,sigma]).

The COMMON expenditure of this interval is sigma, which need not equal c. After shifting sigma to zero, every persistent interval has positive start, and the sum of their durations is finite. Dominated convergence shows that the sum of the FULL durations of cells intersecting [0,epsilon] tends to zero as epsilon decreases. The finite-threshold products have the same uniform bound. Their ordinary pieces have rates in [1/2,1] on the common clock. Therefore the reviewed atom-exclusion theorem applies to this residual endpoint carrier. It has no deterministic proper clade-union mass, and in particular its scalar marked-frequency law has no fixed interior atom.

For an endpoint-equal finite target E_a B(t,g)K, this rules out c=a: cancel the JUSTIFIED E_a prefix from the finite moment/forest identities, and compare the first-cell atom with the residual's atomlessness. The reviewed overshoot theorem rules out c>a, since the representation supplies an actual positive consistent exchangeable residual after its prefix. The unresolved no-first-persistent case must therefore have

    c<a.

This does NOT rule it out. A strictly ordinary-smoothed target residual is itself atomless, so cancellation at c gives no contradiction. The statement also does not claim that an attained first persistent cell exhausts every classification argument; any subsequent peeling uses its separately proved source assumptions.

An exact all-prefix rival array within this calibrated private domain has an all-cap endpoint limit equal to the target, since the matched cap tends to infinity. The compactification places that limit on the common chronological carrier above. It does not turn the limiting carrier back into a finite exact rival, provide a uniform finite testing cap, or discharge the smaller-prefix rigidity/counterconstruction alternative.
