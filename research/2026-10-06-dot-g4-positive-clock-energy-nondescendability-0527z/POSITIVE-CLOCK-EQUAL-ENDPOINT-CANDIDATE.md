## Claim

## 1. Augmented algebraic group

Let H be the accepted connected split algebraic source group at cap m. Consider the algebraic semidirect product

 G=H semidirect Ga,
 (K,a)(L,b)=(KL, a+b4(K)b).

The actual augmented words (K(W),A1(W)) form a semigroup by the exact source recursion. Its Zariski closure J is a closed algebraic subgroup: the standard algebraic-semigroup closure argument used in the accepted source-group provider applies to these invertible matrices as well. One can represent the additive coordinate by the two-by-two matrix with diagonal (b4(K),1) and upper-right A1(W), alongside the faithful representation of H.

J is connected. Its generating parameter images are irreducible rational images of physical parameter cubes, and their closures contain the identity at the all-survival-one boundary; their finite products are irreducible and contain the identity. The connected algebraic subgroup they generate is therefore J. This use of the boundary proves an algebraic group property, not admission of a zero-length final source.

Projection J->H is onto: its image is an algebraic subgroup, hence closed, and contains the Zariski-dense actual capped words. Its kernel is an algebraic subgroup of Ga. In characteristic zero such a subgroup is either zero or all of Ga.

If the kernel were zero, projection would be an isomorphism of algebraic groups (a bijective algebraic-group homomorphism in characteristic zero). J would be the graph of a regular b4-cocycle on H agreeing with A1 on actual words. The rational-cocycle obstruction already proved rules this out. Hence the kernel is all Ga. Surjectivity then implies J=G: every fibre over K contains every additive coordinate.

## 2. From algebraic density to actual strict equal-endpoint words

This step uses the accepted finite-product dominance/regular-endpoint method, NOT a claim that the entire real algebraic group is physically attainable. Let Y be the irreducible closure of the augmented image of the genuine one-cell architecture E(a) B(x,y,g) E(z), with all five parameters strict. Its closure contains augmented bare cells by a,z tending to one, and augmented ordinary factors by x,y tending to one. Thus its generated group is exactly J, without adjoining a different generator family. All maps are rational with denominators nonzero on the physical open cube. Their closures contain the identity. The connected algebraic group generated by them is J=G by Section1.

Successive irreducible product closures have nondecreasing dimension. The identity lies in Y, so these product closures form an increasing chain. If X=closure(Y^n)=closure(Y^(n+1)), then X y is a closed irreducible set of the same dimension as X and is contained in X for every y in Y. Hence X y=X; right multiplication by y inverse also preserves X. Thus X is invariant under the group generated by Y. Since X contains the identity, this forces X=G. Therefore some finite product map is dominant onto G. Equivalently, some full-dimensional Jacobian minor is a nonzero rational function of that finite architecture's parameters.

The maps and minor are defined over the reals. A nonzero rational minor cannot vanish on the entire nonempty open strict physical parameter cube. At some actual strict parameter tuple the derivative is onto the real tangent space of G. The inverse/submersion theorem therefore places an open neighbourhood of its augmented endpoint (K0,a0) inside the actual image of this SAME finite strict architecture.

In that open neighbourhood, hold K=K0 fixed and vary a in a small nontrivial interval. This gives two actual strict words W1,W2 with the identical full capped kernel K0 and A1(W1)!=A1(W2). Neither their parameters nor K0 are merely closure points. No numerical selection of them or resource claim is made.

## 3. Recover a difference of E*

The two words have equal A0=1-V(K0). Let Delta1=A1(W1)-A1(W2)!=0 and Delta2=A2(W1)-A2(W2). For a fixed actual prefix P, their E* difference after prepending P is

 V(P)[R(P)^2 Delta2-2R(P)Delta1].

Choose two strict actual prefixes with distinct positive R, for example an ordinary prefix with R=1 and a positively padded balanced bigon with R<1. This quadratic expression cannot vanish at both distinct positive values unless Delta1=Delta2=0. Thus one prefix yields two actual positive words P W1 and P W2 with exactly the same capped endpoint P K0 but different E*. All positive connectors remain in place.

This proves the claim. Equality of capped kernels implies equality in every original completion at that cap where the private box is legally substituted. It does not expose the two costs as measurements; their difference is precisely what the theorem says is lost by the endpoint map.
