# Older linear and below-n-log-n results: exact resource audit

Contributor: dot (OpenAI), 11 October 2026. Bounded primary-source recovery requested by Nolan. This is an attribution and applicability checkpoint, not a new complexity theorem. No compiler, external implementation, large census, or third-party program was run for this audit.

## Finding

Several real earlier results could explain the recollection, but their variables and resources differ. The strongest literal `2n` match is the checked G1 bound of **2n−2 hybrid vertices** in one normalized decorated source. The Thue–Morse evaluator uses **O(b) fixed-size integer updates** for a b-bit starting index. Exact topology learning has **Theta(n log n) oracle-query complexity**. A separately published external Fourier result now has a retained Lean reproduction for **O(n (log n)^0.999245264)** exact complex-arithmetic operations.

None of the inspected results gives a cost-preserving reduction to improved bit complexity for multiplying arbitrary B-bit integers. This is a finding about the inspected statements, not a claim that no future transfer exists.

## 1. The precise checked G1 linear result

The current accepted source is [G1FullOriginalClassMasterAndSharpness](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/8839f3ba07a74888307c3cb6a8454eb13714c466/research/2026-10-11-dot-accepted-connected-union1581-0718z/source-store/3109b3e555c3a00799c3c9efbb882992cf194d78392d6e4af47ca3b4c82a7a35.lean), SHA-256 `3109b3e555c3a00799c3c9efbb882992cf194d78392d6e4af47ca3b4c82a7a35`. Its theorem `actual_G1_original_class_master_with_all_n_sharpness` quantifies over the original admitted finite binary LSA/CutChild, outer-labelled galled semidirected parallel-arc source class and n≥4 original taxa. It supplies one reduced decorated source with

- at most 2n−2 hybrids;
- at most 6n−5 vertices;
- at most 8n−8 edge occurrences.

It preserves literal displayed-tree/quartet/split/all-compatible-order targets and the stated original-parameter, register, current-root contextual unranked history/completion interfaces. All three bounds are attained by admitted positive/interior examples for every n≥4. This is already a checked declaration in accepted connected 1581; no new execution is claimed here.

The resource is **decorated core cardinality**. Source labels and kernels retain original computations; their description/evaluation length is not bounded by these three formulas. The theorem does not claim linear construction time, linear total bit representation, or a bounded ordinary-network inverse witness. Replacing an arbitrary arithmetic circuit by this biological core would require a separate, costed encoding and decoding theorem that is absent here.

The earlier [exact minimum specification](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/6c0817c69d729d890816f48c1cd778510ce9d0c2/research/2026-09-30-query-resumption/EXACT-MINIMUM-THEOREM.md) used at most **8n−14 unrooted vertices, plus at most one rooting vertex**, to enumerate topology profiles. Its promise preserves the topology targets, not full calibrated biological kernels. It is therefore a different normalization contract. The [three-port refinement](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/6c0817c69d729d890816f48c1cd778510ce9d0c2/research/2026-09-30-normalization-effects/REPORT.md) further gives a topology-only 5n−5 rooted-vertex bound, with a parity improvement for odd n. These hand results do not strengthen the G1 contextual-kernel census by substitution.

## 2. Exact all-level queries, linear sketches, and quadratic/exponential algorithms

The accepted hand integration [OPTIMAL-ADAPTIVE-INTEGRATION](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/6c0817c69d729d890816f48c1cd778510ce9d0c2/research/2026-09-30-query-resumption/OPTIMAL-ADAPTIVE-INTEGRATION.md) proves **Theta(n log n) complete-quartet-support oracle calls**, where n is the number of labelled taxa and the output is the full displayed split union plus any common circle. Arbitrary admitted source levels remain allowed. Its lower bound is at least ceil(log base 3 of (2n−5)!!), already on the ordinary-tree subfamily. Its reference order learner has a conservative **O(n² log n) word-operation** implementation, excluding the oracle's own computation. Compact output and fully expanded split lists have different costs.

The [minimal projection note](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/6c0817c69d729d890816f48c1cd778510ce9d0c2/research/2026-09-30-query-resumption/MINIMAL-PROJECTION.md) gives **O(n) field symbols**, each of **O(log n) bits**, for an exact sparse power-sum sketch when a common circle is supplied and the source split bound applies. This is stronger aggregate access than legal quartet queries. Its elementary Newton-identity/root-scan decoder costs O(n³) field operations; sketch acquisition is not free. Total representation is Theta(n log n) bits in the stated labelled setting.

Two further literal formula candidates are documented:

- The early proposed linear query rule **3n−10** was explicitly refuted as an all-size formula by the [tree-count audit](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/6c0817c69d729d890816f48c1cd778510ce9d0c2/research/2026-09-30-query-growth-pattern/REPORT.md): its counting lower bound first contradicts the rule at n=34. Finite small-n agreement was never an all-size theorem.
- The [order-free reference decoder](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/6c0817c69d729d890816f48c1cd778510ce9d0c2/research/2026-09-30-astra-structural-four-score/ORDER-FREE-RECOVERY.md) explicitly costs **O(2^n n^4)** scalar operations by enumerating splits. It labels itself exponential and credits earlier polynomial split-decomposition methods. It is not a sub-n-log-n claim.

These notes supply exact resource accounting, hand proofs/reviews and their named finite computational evidence. This audit does not promote all such complexity claims to checked Lean theorems merely because related structural modules are checked.

## 3. Thue–Morse: genuine logarithmic-index evaluation

The accepted [DigitRecurrence source](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/8839f3ba07a74888307c3cb6a8454eb13714c466/research/2026-10-11-dot-accepted-connected-union1581-0718z/source-store/73ca15003264178d04284508c61d6eba358b397e57830acb798c9f1186114637.lean), SHA-256 `73ca15003264178d04284508c61d6eba358b397e57830acb798c9f1186114637`, defines a ten-integer state evaluator that removes one binary digit at a time. `evaluate_actual_height` identifies its L-coordinate with the actual attained maximum matching height, and `evaluate_isMaximum` removes any formula premise at the endpoint. These declarations are checked in connected 1581.

Here the input is **one integer starting index v for one fixed avoidance graph and fixed Thue–Morse target**. If b is its binary input length, the recurrence uses O(b) fixed-size integer updates. The [previous cost assessment](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/6c0817c69d729d890816f48c1cd778510ce9d0c2/research/2026-10-09-dot-source-quantum-baseline-assessments-1557z/THUE-MORSE-DERANDOMIZATION-CHECK.md) gives conservative O(b²) bit time and O(b) space from the growing integer coordinates. This is elementary cost analysis, not a separately formalized complexity endpoint. No randomness is involved.

The [aggregate-height hand proof](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/6c0817c69d729d890816f48c1cd778510ce9d0c2/research/2026-10-05-dot-sequence-reading-editions-0100z/Thue-Morse-Hand-Proofs.md), Part II §5, computes the sum of heights for v<N in **O(log N) fixed-size integer-matrix operations** without scanning N starts. It explicitly withholds a separate bit-complexity theorem. The same note proves a mean proportional to log N on dyadic ranges and recalls the sharp individual-height bound 3L(v)≤8v−1. The latter is a bound on a graph statistic, not a running time.

This is a genuine structured-sequence compression. Arbitrary integer products are not encoded by these ten coordinates merely because both inputs have binary digits; no costed multiplication reduction appears in the checked source or notes.

## 4. G7 finite-dimensional exact-law compression

The checked [G7ExactLawRankCompression](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/8839f3ba07a74888307c3cb6a8454eb13714c466/research/2026-10-11-dot-accepted-connected-union1581-0718z/source-store/c2aa5ffb8617013c0a4fb11bc7aa20481a6e4c90a8c177856c442d80cf762bd2.lean), SHA-256 `c2aa5ffb8617013c0a4fb11bc7aa20481a6e4c90a8c177856c442d80cf762bd2`, proves `pointwise_total_strategy_has_finite_informative_horizon`.

Its hypotheses include responses affine in a base vector in a finite-dimensional real vector space V, deletion-closed physical legality, the specified list update, and an already supplied pointwise-total correct policy. It bounds **informative physical exact-law queries by dim(V)**, while retaining a virtual transcript of redundant calls. The dimension is neither the original taxon count nor an integer's bit length without another theorem.

The source explicitly separates this existence result from effective span testing, virtual-policy execution, algebraic selection and a runtime bound. An exact-law response is an entire function/vector of real values, not a bounded-size bit answer. Thus this is not a unit-bit-cost algorithm disguised as a linear query theorem.

Its triple formula is classical and exact. This is a real saving against materializing the full law, not a below-n-log-n algorithm for arbitrary input or multiplication. The theorem counts exact complex scalar arithmetic at unit cost, with specified-root preparation, bounded integer/index values, and the charged operations in the declared RAM model. The selected model also charges a bounded O(log n)-bit integer/index operation as a primitive, rather than expanding it into bit operations. ## 7. Exact transfer test and stopping result

A transfer to B-bit integer multiplication needs: a uniform encoding of arbitrary integer pairs; an exact correctness reduction; charged construction and decoding; bounds on intermediate representation size/precision; and a bit-cost implementation of every primitive. None is supplied merely by a low count of core vertices, queries, digit updates or complex arithmetic operations.

The model distinction is especially decisive for the Fourier result: even length-one convolution performs multiplication of arbitrary complex inputs as one charged scalar primitive. Encoding arbitrarily long integers as those inputs therefore cannot itself establish a fast bit algorithm; the primitive already hides the integer multiplication cost at issue. This does not refute the separately developed CrocSwap bit-model results. It only prevents importing this Fourier arithmetic count as their missing bit-cost proof.

**Recovered matches:** checked G1 2n−2 hybrid normalization; logarithmic-index Thue–Morse evaluation and prefix aggregation; finite-dimensional G7 query compression; linear-symbol sketches; and a genuine external exact-arithmetic sub-n-log-n Fourier theorem with an updated recorded formal status. **No new cost-preserving reduction to improved integer multiplication was found.** Further work is justified only by a concrete encoding and primitive-cost theorem, rather than another generic mixture or binary-digit analogy.

## 8. What can actually be reused in either direction

The concrete shared-bank/probability interface is already recorded in the [published mixture audit](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/2d1d2bbb1828c9c8ac9e9d5d158fac55d4375805/research/2026-10-11-dot-mixture-circuit-transfer-1027z/EXACT-MIXTURE-TRANSFER-AUDIT.md). From a deterministic endpoint equality on an admitted full classical state, the generic checked `G1ContextualForestReplacement.map_eq_of_eq_on_support` transports **every supported joint PMF**, including correlated data and dirty helpers. `G1ActualJointProgram.bind_eq_of_eq_on_support` is the stochastic-row version. The converse from equality for every supported PMF follows by Dirac laws. A bank drawn once must remain in the composed state; averaging each step independently changes the program. Zero-initialized private work is part of the external entry subspace, and endpoint equality alone does not preserve intermediate adaptive observations.

This is an importable correctness interface, not a speedup: neither direction proves the external program's endpoint identity or costs, nor constructs a positive original biological source. In the reverse direction, an external verified tensor/DFT engine could be used only for a Commons computation actually reduced to its exact array transform, with the desired precision and cost model discharged. The G1 graph census, the G7 abstract affine response model and the ten-coordinate Thue–Morse recurrence supply no such transform reduction by themselves.

Likewise the normalized positive rank profile is a finite Laplace transform, so existing Mathlib finite Jensen applies exactly under positive weights. That reusable inequality and its fixed-profile scope are already proved in the mixture audit; it neither raises a multiplication exponent nor bounds all possible profiles. No duplicate wrapper or further parameter search is justified by the present comparison.
