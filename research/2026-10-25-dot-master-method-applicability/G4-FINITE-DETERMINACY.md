## 1. Grouped mixtures: correct rival quantifier, failed target premise

**Primary theorem.** Vandermeulen and Scott, *An Operator Theoretic Approach to Nonparametric Mixture Models*, arXiv:1607.00071v2, Definitions 3.2–3.3 and Theorems 4.1, 4.3 (PDF pp.4–6): an m-component target mixture is 2m-determined, meaning uniqueness against all finite competing mixture orders. The weaker 2m-1 identifiability statement restricts competing order. The observations in the stronger theorem have law V_n(P)=sum_i w_i mu_i^(tensor n): one component is chosen, then n conditionally iid samples are drawn from it. The proof of 4.3 on printed p.18 uses positive tensor Gram rank, not genericity. This is genuinely target-selected finite determination, not merely identification with a known rival dimension.

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.00071v2

**Actual source assessment.** The original compiler does not license replacing current-root routing by a single shared latent route. Independent repetitions redraw a history and yield the product of marginal experiment laws, not the required shared-history mixture. More importantly, there IS an actual source-valid grouped construction, and its failure is already stronger than this informal warning.

Accepted A9 fixes a private word with a strictly positive leading ordinary edge. For every positive finite old-label output-forest event E, disjoint fresh-pair same-block indicators Z_j have a Bernoulli mixture directed by R=sum_i P_i^2 conditional on E. Its directing law has a positive nonatomic submeasure. Consequently all H_d(E)=[E(R^(i+j)|E)]_(i,j=0)^d are positive definite, and Cov(Z_1,Z_2|E)>0. This holds even for one fixed positive ordinary target. The finite probabilities and ratios are licensed via the already accepted private fixed-wrapper tomography, not extra physical access to an internal cut.

A9 already includes the following important answer to the arbitrary-component-measures loophole. If richer iid group outcomes Y_j admitted a finite mixture and a groupwise measurable statistic mapped Y_j to these Z_j, pushing each component forward would give a finite Bernoulli mixture, contradicting A9. Enriching these same groups therefore does not rescue the theorem. Finite old-label conditioning trees and the stipulated polynomial recalibrations of R are also covered by A9. An unrelated statistic without this marginal is not excluded, but no sufficient COMMON decoder for such a statistic is supplied.

There is another scope guard: on an infinite sample space, knowing the whole grouped measure is not automatically a finite list of scalar observations. A finite observation compiler, or a finite alphabet retaining the needed information, must be proved. A finite partition only identifies the pushed-forward mixture, not its unobserved distinctions.

## 2. Flat moments: positivity is powerful only for the correct moments

**Primary result.** Curto and Fialkow, *Truncated K-moment problems in several variables*, states that a positive semidefinite moment matrix with a rank-preserving moment extension has a unique representing measure for the extended data, finitely atomic with cardinality equal to the rank. Constraint-localizing matrices govern prescribed semialgebraic support. This is about the extended moment data; existence of some flat extension must not be misread as uniqueness from a shorter unextended data vector.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0507067v1 . This pass checked the primary abstract; the September framework audit had already checked the full theorem and its source hypotheses.

**Assessment.** A9's actual conditional moment matrices are never flat at finite order. Finite source topology does not imply finite atomic coalescent-frequency mixing. Quadrature matching finitely many moments is a measure-realization result, not an actual original-source rival with the same complete topology observations. Thus neither the positive nor negative master follows. The September source-constrained moment audit already records the distinction between an abstract positive representing measure and an admitted shared-tuple coalescent source.

## 3. Finite forcibility beyond finite rank: graphons

**Primary results.** Lovasz and Szegedy, *Finitely forcible graphons*, arXiv:0901.0929v2, studies uniqueness among all graphons from finitely many subgraph densities. Its introduction attributes stepfunction finite forcibility to Lovasz–Sos; Theorem 5.1 supplies a non-step class from monotone symmetric polynomial threshold sets. Therefore infinite latent complexity alone does not rule out finite forcibility. Proposition 2.3/Claim 2.4 gives the simpler finite-valued moment mechanism: a squared polynomial annihilating target values forces any matching measure onto those values, after which a Vandermonde system recovers weights.

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.0929v2

**Exact encoding failure for the direct pair graph.** Suppose one proposes to encode the A9 indicators as edges on disjoint pairs of iid sampled vertices in ONE deterministic graphon W. Such edges are independent: their vertex variables, and any edge-specific randomness, are disjoint. Thus E[Z_1 Z_2]=(E Z_1)^2. A9 gives the strict opposite inequality after every admitted positive finite old-label conditioning. This contradicts that proposed group-preserving deterministic-graphon encoding already on an ordinary target.

A random graphon can restore dependence by sharing a global random environment, but a theorem forcing one deterministic graphon cannot simply be applied to that random law. This is an applicability obstruction for the stated encoding, not a no-go for every relational representation or every nonnegative observable identity. In particular, A9 does not prohibit a different exact source-derived square identity on the target fibre.

## 4. Noetherianity up to symmetry: missing functor, not just missing computation

**Primary theorem.** Draisma, *Topological Noetherianity of polynomial functors*, JAMS 32 (2019), Sections 1.1–1.2 and main theorem: finite-degree polynomial functors over an infinite field are topologically Noetherian. The uniform bound is on the polynomial degree of the induced maps on Hom spaces. Closed subspaces must be compatible with the functor's morphisms. This is not a theorem about arbitrary infinite collections of unrelated polynomial coordinates.

Source: https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/137137903/polfunctorsJAMS.pdf

**Assessment.** The original hierarchy has increasing copy arity, opaque forest state spaces, and unknown source architecture. No one finite-degree polynomial functor representing that hierarchy with the required morphisms has been supplied. Fixed-chart polynomial response formulas do not establish this hypothesis. Also, the equations comparing a rival with ONE distinguished target are not automatically stable under arbitrary linear-coordinate maps. Permuting labels and pruning are not a substitute for the theorem's full morphism contract. A joint target/rival construction could conceivably address this issue, but its finite-degree and source-faithful properties would require proof, not invocation of the theorem.

**Nearby primary result.** Draisma–Eggermont, *Finiteness results for Abelian tree models*, JEMS 17 (2015), Theorem 1.1, bounds defining-equation degrees independently of tree for a fixed finite alphabet and Abelian action. Its object is the Zariski closure of the tree substitution model. It is not finite determination of one stochastic coalescent hierarchy against all network rivals, and bounded equation degree is not bounded observation arity.

Source: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/32026

## 5. Recent phylogenetic all-positive results do not fill the bigon gap

Brits, Holtgrefe, van Iersel and Martin, *On Tree-Network Distinguishability and Full Identifiability of Phylogenetic Networks*, arXiv:2607.12919v1: Section 4 addresses all-positive level-1 semi-directed topology under JC/K2P/K3P substitution models. Corollary 5.8 distinguishes networks with nontrivial blobs of at least three attachments from those without, for JC/K2P. Section 6 discusses related coalescent consequences. The substitution experiment is not the natural I coalescent-topology experiment. Suppression/exception of 2-blobs is material: those are precisely where retained private-return ambiguity can live. Neither genericity language nor the word 'full' may be read as the original all-rival entire-law certificate.

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12919v1 , especially Sections 2.1–2.2, Corollary 5.8, and Discussion. The September literature map already contains this paper; this is a renewed scope check, not a new discovery.

## 6. Exact remaining applicability gate

The audited external methods would become relevant only after an actual NATURAL-I observable representation supplies their hypotheses for every original rival, while the target has a finitely forcing object and equality of that object forces C_T. The obvious conditional-grouped representation is rigorously excluded by A9, including richer iid groups retaining its indicator. The direct deterministic pair-graph encoding fails the same covariance check. No alternative representation satisfying the needed conditions was found in this focused pass.

This is a negative applicability finding, not proof that G4 is false. No exact source realization of moment quadrature was found; no fixed-target all-panel rivals were constructed. No new finite guard valid against all original rivals was established. Further progress must supply a genuinely different observable identity/representation, rather than retry the accepted failed finite-mixture encoding or count this audit as a closing theorem.
