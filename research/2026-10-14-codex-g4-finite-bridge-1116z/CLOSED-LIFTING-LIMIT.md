## General closed-lifting statement

Fix ANY finite legal I panel J, and a finite legal COMMON panel C containing
chi. Write i_N=I_J(T_N), c_N=C_C(T_N), and i_T,c_T for the target values.
Let K be a closed subset of the finite-dimensional space

    R^|J| x R^|C| x R^r,

covering every actual source's joint response: for every admitted S there
exists u with (I_J(S),C_C(S),u) in K. Suppose K also satisfies the intended
forcing implication on its ENTIRE lifted set:

    (i_T,c,u) in K => c=c_T.                           (2)

Then, for EVERY choice of feasible witnesses u_N with (i_N,c_N,u_N) in K,

    ||u_N|| -> infinity.                             (3)

Proof. If (3) failed, infinitely many u_N would lie in some fixed bounded
set. Finite-dimensional compactness supplies a convergent subsequence of
these auxiliary points. The response coordinates c_N lie in [0,1]^|C|,
so pass to a further convergent subsequence. The i_N converge to i_T by
the supplier. Closure of K puts the resulting (i_T,c_*,u_*) in K, while
(1) gives c_*(chi)!=c_T(chi), contradicting (2). This proves (3). The
statement is vacuous rather than falsely compact when r=0: such a K cannot
exist at all. Equivalently, the infimum of feasible auxiliary norms above
(i_N,c_N) tends to infinity, since otherwise bounded witnesses could be
chosen along a subsequence. QED.

## Applicability and surviving exact obligation

Thus no closed compact-auxiliary polynomial/SOS/moment relaxation containing
ALL actual joint responses can force this target COMMON panel from finite
exact I equality. Already an unlifted closed endpoint relaxation contains
the bad exact-I limit. Adding finite auxiliary variables without allowing
witness escape does not repair it.

This concerns soundness on the full CLOSED relaxation. It does not rule out
strict/nonclosed constraints, unbounded reciprocal or finiteness witnesses,
a source-size-dependent lift, or an exact equality guard directly on the
NONCLOSED admitted finite-source set. Such a guard need not be continuous
at the target. A semialgebraic rule can enforce a singleton fibre while
being discontinuous nearby. No exact all-prefix rival follows from (1).

The older closed-marker/reciprocal warning and transfer discontinuity retain
priority. This note states the finite-dimensional witness-escape consequence
explicitly and imposes no new rival-size bound. The required original finite
legal-I to finite-COMMON-certificate bridge still has to be proved on actual
admitted sources, or refuted by actual exact rivals at the same fixed target.
