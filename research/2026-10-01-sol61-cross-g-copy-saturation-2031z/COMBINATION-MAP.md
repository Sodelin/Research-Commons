## 3. The selected source-specific implication

The proposed new branch compares two POSITIVE sources with exactly one complete original named hybrid H and the same natural rare-parent probability epsilon.

1. Effective source: CG2's donor graft from a's pendant branch to b's hybrid H at fixed age h; donor subdivision age d satisfies 0<h<d<both original pendant-parent ages
2. Neutral source: replace b's original pendant interval by a parallel-arc bigon P->H, with P age d, H age h, all original b-segment/arm rates equal to rho_b. Both H incoming arcs have the SAME parent-bit labels 0,1 as the effective source. They are different arc occurrences even though their tail vertex P is the same
3. All original source admission conditions hold: binary degrees, positive calendars/rates/interior epsilon, root LSA, hybrid-child bridge, outer-face leaves, admitted parallel arcs
4. Before H both sources have the SAME recipient Kingman process. Couple the coins and complete histories. Until the first rare route, both histories coincide with the original tree. Thus passive full-calendar TV is at most epsilon C_h uniformly over every finite copy allocation; the neutral independent bigon is NOT claimed exactly equal to the tree law naturally at multiple copies
5. The SAME original-ID action 'force H to parent 1 for every live lineage' is legal in both sources. Neutral forcing produces the original tree's exact calendar process; effective forcing produces an early selected a,b merger with fixed positive probability c, independent of epsilon

Expected conclusion to prove: at any fixed confidence alpha<1/2, passive adaptive all-copy locus cost is Theta(1/epsilon), but this single legal forcing configuration gives Theta(1) locus cost as epsilon->0, with fixed source constants. An explicit upper has O(log(1/alpha)) forced loci. No logarithmic-alpha passive lower is inferred from the new pair without a separate likelihood proof.

The expected passive lower will be proved by a stopped first-rare coupling, not by copying CG2's tree-direction weighted-measure argument into a different source pair. Natural one-copy early-merger detection gives the matching passive order. A pure tree has no named H, which would make a source-dependent force action invalid; the neutral bigon is the necessary matched-registry repair.

This is an IDEAL CONTROL theorem only when that complete original actuator is actually supplied/accessible. It does not advertise a biological experiment, infer hidden-ID access, calibrate DNA, or contradict CG3's stronger universal Q/S task requiring two configurations at one hybrid. The specified paired family is a smaller competitor class and has a target-relevant informative row.
