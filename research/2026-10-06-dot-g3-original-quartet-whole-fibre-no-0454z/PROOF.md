## 1. The original input, rather than an internal kernel observation

Keep the original finite positive binary rooted-LSA, outer-labelled planar, cut-child source class, including multi-hybrid retained blobs, parallel arcs and unbounded ancestral completion. Take four taxa A,B,C,D and the finite allocation of four labelled copies a1,a2,a3,a4 from A and one copy from each other taxon. The declared observation is the restriction of the final rooted labelled unranked seven-copy genealogy to the four A copies, suppressing unary vertices. This is a finite deterministic coarsening of the original permitted topology observation. The row is natural/passive; no hidden population, forest or routing flag is measured.

We construct an effectively real-algebraic, strictly positive probability vector on the 15 rooted binary labelled quartet topologies which is in the closure of this original observation image but is realized by NO finite strictly positive source of ANY admitted core size. This is one original G3 negative instance family/construction. It is not a complete G3 recognizer or a bound on positive witnesses for arbitrary inputs.

The proof reuses the [original source-complete compiler, Sections 1–5](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/07c9a510b594655a62c609a7e354ecfe5752e35f/research/2026-10-01-g4-admitted-testers-0819z/PROOF.md) and the accepted [completed-quartet formula and chronological cocycle](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/270793b319af174014f1b7de6e3849209c3476aa/research/2026-10-06-dot-g3-independent-quartet-topology-invariant-0403z/PROOF.md), proof SHA256 4ce42cd54c2467dba71a6a721ac892cbbdeb75b4e294544b2cf8427d8d56bfb5. ## 2. Ancestry of one taxon in the actual cut-child class

Fix any admitted source and the focal leaf A. Remove every directed vertex/edge which is not ancestral to A. This is a proof operation on the selected-lineage marginal, not a re-rooting or a claim that the resulting one-leaf diagram is an admitted original source. The original root and the other taxa remain in the actual source.

If a hybrid h is ancestral to A, its unique child edge is a cut edge in the underlying source graph. Every root-to-A path crosses that edge and therefore h. Such hybrid cut edges lie on the one path in the bridge/block tree from the root component to A, and are linearly ordered there. In particular, two hybrids ancestral to A cannot be different sinks in the same blob: each hybrid's child exits the blob across its own bridge, and the root-to-A path can select only one such exiting component. Other hybrids of that blob lead into different descendant components and are absent from the ancestral restriction.

Within a blob that contributes an ancestral hybrid, the retained ancestry has that one indegree-two vertex and no other hybrid. Its two parent paths run through ordinary vertices and meet at a single split vertex: if they met, split and met again there would be another indegree-two vertex. Ordinary side branches not ancestral to A carry no selected lineage and may be removed. Suppress the resulting indegree-one/outdegree-one vertices, combining the finite positive population durations along each path. This gives one two-arm bigon, with positive finite arm durations and its original inheritance weight. Between the successive contributing blobs the ordinary paths likewise combine. The pendant population incident to A gives a strictly positive leading ordinary passage. A final ordinary passage may have zero combined length if the top split is the retained root; this causes no problem since it is followed by the original unbounded ordinary completion.

Thus the selected four-copy lineage process, until its ordinary completion, is exactly a finite chronological independent-routing bigon chain with a positive leading ordinary population and positive intervening populations. This statement does not require the full source to be level 1. It uses the original cut-child condition in every retained multi-hybrid blob.

Selected-label projectivity justifies deleting the other sampled lineages: ordinary Kingman restriction retains the pair rate, and every surviving selected ancestral block routes once at an independent hybrid. A merger with an unselected block becomes a suppressed unary event and does not cause the selected descendants to acquire separate routing coins. The inherited full joint compiler supplies precisely this restriction law.

If the compared menu also allows COMMON inheritance, each such cell has, conditional on its one parental bit, an ordinary passage and has quartet contrast zero. Its unconditional no-merger multiplier is in [0,1]. The argument below covers any finite mixture of these cells and independent cells. A finite original whole-lineage forcing program can similarly be conditioned on its declared joint configuration; forced passages are ordinary and the same uniform strict bound holds in every configuration. No arbitrary hidden correlated natural independent-lineage environment is added to the source class.

## 3. A compact algebraic one-cell reward ratio

For a capped kernel K let c(K)=b4(K), and let T(K) be the probability of a balanced completed rooted quartet minus 1/3. The accepted theorem gives

    c(KL)=c(K)c(L),
    T(KL)=T(K)+c(K)T(L),
    T(E(z))=0.

For a closed independent cell B=B(x,y,p), with q=1-p and (p,x,y) in [0,1]^3, put u=p(1-x), v=q(1-y), d=pu+qv. Then

    b2(B)=1-d,
    T(B)=(2/3)[q u^3+p v^3-3pq(u-v)^2],
    c(B)=p^4 x^6+q^4 y^6+4p^3q x^3+4pq^3 y^3+6p^2q^2 xy.

The accepted finite-ULC proof and continuity give c(B)≤(1-d)^3; the accepted one-cell bound gives T(B)≤144d^3. Also c=1 implies d=0, and when d=0 the cell is the identity on every finite forest space (every arm which is chosen has zero duration).

For d>0 define H(B)=max(T(B),0)/(1-c(B)), and set H=0 at d=0. Since 1-c≥1-(1-d)^3≥d,

    0≤H(B)≤144d^2.

Away from d=0 the denominator is positive. This bound proves continuity at its entire zero-loss locus, including p=0 or 1 corners. Hence H is a continuous semialgebraic function on the compact cube. It has a maximum M. Balanced strict cells p=1/2, x=y=s with 0<s<1 have T=(1-s)^3/12>0, so M>0.

The number M and its maximizing set are definable over the rationals by a finite RCF formula: use the polynomial quotient relation where c<1 and the value zero where d=0, and quantify the maximum over the cube. The maximum is unique as a VALUE, so M is real algebraic and standard RCF algorithms give an effective algebraic representation and a maximizing real-algebraic triple. This is a terminating prescription, unexecuted here.

For every closed independent cell,

    T(B)≤M(1-c(B)).                                      (1)

Ordinary and COMMON cells also satisfy (1), since their T is zero and 0≤c≤1. Positive ordinary padding does not break (1): trailing padding leaves T unchanged and decreases c, and leading padding multiplies T by its c while also increasing the right-hand loss budget.

## 4. Uniform all-word inequality and its strictness

For any two source factors satisfying (1), the chronological cocycle gives

    T(KL)≤M(1-c(K))+c(K)M(1-c(L))=M(1-c(KL)).

Induction proves this inequality for every finite selected-lineage chain. Every strictly positive finite independent or ordinary factor has positive c, as does a finite COMMON mixture. Therefore every finite source has

    T < M,                                               (2)

because T≤M(1-c) and c>0. Final unbounded ordinary completion is the readout used to define T; it is not an extra factor whose c=0 would erase the strict estimate. Finite original randomized configurations preserve (2) after averaging.

By Section 2, this is an inequality on the actual four-copy observable marginal for EVERY admitted source core, not a test of one chosen hidden kernel.

## 5. Sharpness in the original observation closure

Choose a maximizing closed cell B*. Its T* is positive, and c*<1. Algebraic repetition gives

    T((B*)^N)=T* sum_{j=0}^{N-1}(c*)^j=M(1-(c*)^N)→M.

For each fixed N, approximate its closed parameters by strict ones and insert strictly positive leading/connecting ordinary populations whose survivals tend to 1. The finite polynomial compiler ensures convergence to (B*)^N. Put that finite strict chain on the A pendant bridge of any fixed positive four-taxon binary tree. Such insertion is admitted by the inherited bridge grammar and preserves the full root/LSA, cut-child and outer-labelled embedding conditions. The ordinary tree above that chain changes no selected quartet contrast. Thus a diagonal sequence of actual finite positive original sources has observed contrast tending to M.

All selected-lineage laws are exchangeable, so the three balanced labelled quartet topologies have equal probabilities, as do the twelve comb topologies. Consequently their entire observed law converges to

    P*(each balanced topology)=(1/3+M)/3,
    P*(each comb topology)=(2/3-M)/12.                    (3)

These are effectively real-algebraic numbers and sum to one.

They are strictly positive. It remains to justify M<2/3 rather than merely M≤2/3. Positive T* in the displayed cell formula forces p* and q* positive and x*,y*<1: if one arm loss is zero, the square term makes the contrast nonpositive. With probability (p*)^4 all four roots enter the first arm. That arm has positive or infinite duration, so with a fixed positive probability all four coalesce there; conditional on full coalescence the embedded ordinary Kingman topology is a comb with probability 2/3. This is a strictly positive probability of a completed comb already inside the FIRST B* cell. Subsequent cells cannot change that topology. Therefore every repeated-cell law has comb probability bounded below by the same positive constant. Its limit in (3) does too, proving M<2/3.

Equation (2) excludes (3) for every actual positive source, regardless of its core and word lengths. Section 5 proves it is nevertheless in the closure of the original observation image. Thus this supplies a genuinely all-core finite original G3 NO instance, with strictly positive observed outcome probabilities, at an explicitly permitted topology coarsening. It does not assert a full seven-copy uncoarsened law with these properties, and does not decide other inputs or arbitrary observation maps.

## 6. Prior and scope assessment

The direct methods are inherited source projectivity, the original cut-child bridge grammar, the accepted quartet cocycle/formula and elementary discounted telescoping. Finite semialgebraic maximization and algebraic sampling are standard real algebraic geometry. This is not a new optimization principle.

The original whole-fibre G3 completeness obligation remains open; this theorem aims to discharge the narrower previously missing transfer from a private-word nonattainment mechanism to a complete original all-core negative input.
