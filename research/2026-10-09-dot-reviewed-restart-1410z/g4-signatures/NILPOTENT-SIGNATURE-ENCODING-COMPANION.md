## 1. Stronger precise conclusion

Keep the actual unmarked private natural INDEPENDENT strict source grammar and one shared physical tuple from the axis-path attempt. Suppose each source word W is assigned a continuous coordinatewise nondecreasing bounded-variation path P_W in a fixed R^d, based at zero, such that:

1. Its full tensor signature is serial-compositional: Sig(P_WV)=Sig(P_W) Sig(P_V).
2. For every finite level r, all signature coefficients through r are functions of one finite complete capped original source endpoint K_m(W), for some m which may depend on r. No continuity or effectivity is assumed for the decoding functions.

Then for every actual W,V,

    Sig(P_WV)=Sig(P_VW).

Thus such a signature cannot be faithful to complete original source response laws. Unlike the preceding axis theorem, paths need not have finitely many pieces or lie on coordinate axes. This rules out the proposed monotone-signature reconstruction/finite-forcing bridge even if its target-specific path certificate were supplied. Signed paths without the bounds used below, noncompositional representations, and predicates defined only on a constrained target fibre remain outside the theorem.

## 2. Exact inherited source premises

For a fixed finite cap m>=2, let S=S_m be the actual complete endpoint semigroup. In the inherited fixed ordinary-diagonalizing coordinates, its connected positive-diagonal source group is

    G={D(b)+N : b2,...,bm>0, N in u}.

Here u is the actual associative block-decomposed nilpotent merger algebra. The arity-k diagonal block is scalar b_k; b0=b1=1, and the empty block is isolated. The ordinary Lie-algebra element H with diagonal blocks lambda_k=binom(k,2) acts on each merger block u_(k,j), 1<=j<k, by the nonzero weight lambda_k-lambda_j. Nothing asserts that S is all of G.

The accepted source providers also give:

- an actual nonempty G-open patch S^o;
- closure of S under multiplication;
- for every K in S a continuous contraction K_t in S for t>0, with K_1=K and K_0=I, obtained by scaling every positive population duration in one fixed presentation while preserving its coins;
- for A in S^o the entire path K_t A, including t=0, lies in S^o.

These are exactly the group, open-patch and contraction premises used in the accepted continuous additive proof [ADD-C]. The contraction does not have physical positive increments and is not being used as a physical convolution semigroup.

The accepted no-regularity additive theorem [ADD-N] makes every finite nonnegative additive scalar on S a continuous linear combination of log b_k. Its use below is only to bound first-level displacements. No new independent source-dimension, Lie-generation or closure premise is introduced.

## 3. General source-to-nilpotent lemma

**Lemma.** Let Q be a finite-dimensional simply connected nilpotent real Lie group. Let F:S->Q be a semigroup homomorphism. Assume that, at some actual interior anchor A, the local map

    f_A(x)=F(A)^(-1) F(Ax)

has bounded logarithm in a neighborhood of I in G. Then F(S) is abelian. More precisely, F is conjugate to a global homomorphism chi:G->Q which factors through the log-diagonal coordinates.

### 3.1 Actual anchors induce a common local homomorphism

For interior A,B and x sufficiently near I, every factor below is actual. Associativity gives

    F(Ax)F(B)=F(A)F(xB),
    F(Bx)F(B)=F(B)F(xB).

Thus f_A(x)=F(xB)F(B)^(-1)=f_B(x), as germs. For x,y sufficiently near I, factoring AxyA as (Axy)A and (Ax)(yA) gives f(xy)=f(x)f(y). This is the accepted A3 local-homomorphism argument [A3], now used with target Q. No inverse is inserted as a physical source.

### 3.2 Local boundedness gives continuity

Choose a sufficiently small exponential neighborhood U of I on which this local homomorphism law holds and ||log f(x)||<=M. The logarithm on Q is globally defined because Q is simply connected nilpotent. For epsilon>0 choose an integer N with M/N<epsilon. For x sufficiently close to I, all powers x,...,x^N remain in U and each local multiplication is valid. Hence

    f(x^N)=f(x)^N,  log f(x^N)=N log f(x),
    ||log f(x)||<=M/N<epsilon.

This proves continuity at I. A continuous local homomorphism of finite-dimensional real Lie groups has a bracket-preserving linear derivative T:Lie(G)->Lie(Q). Equivalently, restriction to each one-parameter subgroup is a continuous one-parameter subgroup; the local product and commutator limits establish linearity and bracket preservation. No definability or differentiability of the original decoder is assumed.

### 3.3 Nilpotence kills every merger direction

For X in u_(k,j), put c=lambda_k-lambda_j, which is nonzero. Bracket preservation gives

    [T(H),T(X)]=T([H,X])=c T(X).

In a nilpotent Lie algebra ad(T(H)) is nilpotent. Applying a sufficiently high power to this equation gives 0=c^p T(X), so T(X)=0. The actual u is the direct sum of these allowed blocks, hence T(u)=0.

The remaining diagonal Lie algebra is abelian. If V_k denotes T applied to the k-th log-diagonal basis vector, all V_k commute. Define globally

    chi(K)=exp_Q(sum_(k=2)^m log b_k(K) V_k).

This is a group homomorphism on G, its image is abelian, and its germ equals f. The last assertion follows from equality of derivatives for continuous local Lie homomorphisms, or directly by restricting to the ordinary block factors and the diagonal one-parameter subgroups. It does not assert that the mathematical diagonal factors are actual source words.

### 3.4 Return to every actual source

For P in S^o and x near I, the common germ gives F(Px)=F(P)chi(x). Therefore

    R(P)=F(P)chi(P)^(-1)

is locally constant on S^o. For K in S, the entire path K_t A lies in S^o, so R(KA)=R(A). Write C=F(A)chi(A)^(-1). Using actual multiplicativity and the global homomorphism chi gives

    F(K) C chi(K)^(-1)=C,
    F(K)=C chi(K) C^(-1).

Every F(K) therefore lies in one conjugate of the abelian image of chi. This proves the lemma. No connectedness of the whole interior, global extension assumption on F, or source factorization into positive increments was used.

## 4. Monotone signatures supply the boundedness hypothesis

Fix a signature level r. Let Q_r be the finite-dimensional step-r free nilpotent signature group on d letters. Group-like truncated signatures of continuous bounded-variation paths lie in this simply connected nilpotent group. Assumption 2 defines a well-defined homomorphism

    F_r:S_m -> Q_r

for some finite m, increased to at least two if needed. Its first-level coordinates a_j(K) are finite, nonnegative and additive under actual source concatenation. By [ADD-N], each a_j is a continuous linear combination of log source diagonals.

For a continuous coordinatewise nondecreasing path, its l1 variation is exactly

    L(K)=sum_j a_j(K).

Every length-k iterated-integral coefficient is nonnegative and at most L(K)^k/k!: summing over all length-k words equals the one-dimensional signature coefficient of the total increasing coordinate. Thus on an interior neighborhood Ax, continuity of L bounds all coefficients of F_r(Ax). Multiplication by the fixed inverse F_r(A)^(-1) is polynomial in truncated tensor coordinates, and the group logarithm is a finite polynomial in those coordinates. Consequently log[F_r(A)^(-1)F_r(Ax)] is bounded near I.

The lemma applies. It follows that F_r(K)F_r(L)=F_r(L)F_r(K) for all actual capped endpoints. Hence every signature level of P_WV and P_VW agrees. Since r was arbitrary, their full signatures agree.

The cap can grow arbitrarily with r. No uniform cap across all signature levels and no source-size bound were used. Products or nonlinear postprocessing of finitely many legal observations are already included in assumption 2.

## 5. Why this defeats the proposed whole proof

Take the actual strict sources

    W=E(1/2) B(1/2,1/2,1/2) E(1/2), V=E(1/2).

Their products WV and VW have different leading ordinary survivals and are all-copy distinct by the accepted ordered normal form [ORDER]. Section 4 makes their entire encoded signatures equal. Therefore no encoding satisfying assumptions 1–2 can also make full-signature equality imply original complete-law equality.

The source-specific failed implication is now independent of which finite certificate one hopes to use for the target path. Square-free-word certificates, occupation certificates or a separate reconstruction theorem cannot recover chronological information already erased by this compositional observable encoding.

This strengthens the METHOD boundary relative to A3, which treated a particular two-log-diagonal area and occupation square. It does not revoke those proofs or claim a new proof of the source-group providers. It also does not show that every nonadditive source invariant is abelian: the actual source group has transported merger directions with nonzero ordinary weights. Mapping them into a nilpotent target is the restrictive step. In particular, the real forest endpoint map itself is not a counterexample; its target group is the semidirect positive-diagonal source group, not a nilpotent signature group.

Original G4 remains open. This proves neither finite forcing of a general source nor one fixed finite target with exact all-prefix rivals. Target-dependent finite predicates that do not arise from the global compositional encoding remain possible. No COMMON channel, hidden route, clock or adjoint readout is supplied.
