# A legal COMMON signature whose continuous square root is not a moment signature

19 October 2026. Contributor: dot (OpenAI), working with Nolan. Producing-pass hand proof and exact finite certificate; separate review pending. This is a source-faithful failed reduction step, not a G3 recognizer, impossibility theorem or historical novelty claim.

## Master-facing attempted reduction and prior work

G3 asks for total recognition on every finite rational/effectively algebraic input, with arbitrary finite strict physical sources, original observation IDs, chronology and one shared parameter tuple. The existing integer-clock and positive-switching audits already screened general matrix-exponential hardness and the autonomous-flow interpretation of whole bigon kernels. Those arguments are inherited, not repeated here.

A remaining tempting simplification is to project a natural COMMON word onto its calibrated duration moments. These diagonal signatures commute and multiply under concatenation. One could try to replace integer occurrences of each signature by continuous powers, combine repeated occurrences into total times, and invoke a commuting matrix-exponential decision procedure.

The primary supplier is Ouaknine, Pouly, Sousa-Pinto and Worrell, *On the decidability of membership in matrix-exponential semigroups*, author manuscript, §1 and Definition 1.1: https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/matrix-exponential17.pdf . Its commuting reduction uses a supplied finite list of algebraic rate matrices and nonnegative real elapsed times. It decides membership in that continuous-flow semigroup. It does not state that taking logarithms of physical mixture kernels preserves source realizability, nor does it infer a finite dictionary from a parameterized family. Logarithms of algebraic signature entries need not themselves be algebraic. The explicit calculation below rejects the needed physical reverse implication even if that arithmetic issue and the dictionary issue are generously set aside.

## Exact strict source and finite projection

Use the inherited literal COMMON grammar, with a fresh natural coin per cell:

    W = E(1/2) B_COMMON(1/8,1/4,1/2) E(1/2).

Both ordinary-edge survivals, both arm survivals and the routing probability lie strictly between zero and one. All are rational. Its scalar random survival Z equals 1/32 or 1/16, with probability 1/2 each. Hence

    m_lambda = ((1/32)^lambda + (1/16)^lambda)/2.

For lambda=0 this is 1. For every nonnegative integer lambda it is rational. The rates used below, {1,3,6,10}, belong to the original calibrated rate list {1,3,6,10,15,21}; normalization at zero is a probability identity, not an additional measured query.

Let D=diag(m_lambda) on these five coordinates including zero. D is positive diagonal, so its real logarithm exists. The continuous semigroup generated by log D contains D^(1/2). Its projected candidate signature is

    f_lambda = sqrt(m_lambda),     f_0=1.

These entries are effectively algebraic. We now show they cannot be the moments of ANY probability law on nonnegative survival values, even without Bernoulli-factorization restrictions.

## Explicit nonnegative polynomial certificate

Define

    q(z) = z^10 - (11323/59136) z^6 + (8571/315392) z^3
                     - (723/180224) z + 1181/1892352.

Exact factorization gives

    q(z) = (2z-1)^2 (4z-1)^2
           (59136z^6 + 88704z^5 + 85008z^4 + 66528z^3
                       + 35108z^2 + 13161z + 2362)/3784704.

Every coefficient of the last factor is positive. Thus Q(z)=q(2z) is nonnegative on all z>=0. This is stronger than nonnegativity on the physical interval [0,1]; no hidden upper-support restriction is imposed. Q uses only the exponents 0,1,3,6,10.

Write Q(z)=sum c_lambda z^lambda. Exact outward square-root bounds give

    sum c_lambda f_lambda <=
      -15334360931519906220616389805939
       /2398841148643090364381089304099684352
      < -6/10^6.

If a probability law nu on nonnegative Z had these finite moments, linearity would instead give sum c_lambda f_lambda = integral Q(Z) dnu >=0, a contradiction. Therefore D^(1/2) is outside the finite moment-signature cone. In particular, it cannot be the signature of any strict COMMON Bernoulli-duration word, of any length. This argument does not depend on knowing the entire distribution or imposing a duration lattice.

## Why this is precisely a failed reduction step

The legal signature D is in the physical multiplicative family. Its continuous half-power is admitted by the proposed real-time flow enlargement but violates a finite original-rate necessary condition. Thus even a commuting diagonal projection does not justify replacing physical occurrences by arbitrary nonnegative real times. A continuous-flow YES answer can be spurious before any harder joint-source constraints are considered.

This is not an admitted-source compression counterexample: the rejected half-power was introduced by the relaxation, not asserted to be a legal source. It is also not a new all-core original-observation NO profile. No positive full original profile has been constructed by an inverse compiler here, and no C/D-retaining, INDEPENDENT or BOTH observation theorem is asserted. The original shared-tuple and ID constraints remain required. Keeping the other calibrated rates would not repair the violated four-rate moment inequality.

The example excludes this direct continuous-power reverse implication. It does not exclude a different faithful finite-state lifting, additional effective constraints, discrete-only membership algorithm or complete source-specific NO-certificate language. None of those is supplied by the commuting-flow theorem. The full G3 stopping obligation, including unattained zero-distance inputs, remains open.
