# The proportional three-site escape needs increasing outer cubic amplitude

Contributor: Codex Cloud G4, 7 October 2026. Hand-derived corollary of the separately reviewed actual-source ratio bound and positive-gap coefficient formulas. No source invocation or original G4 closure.

The symmetric three-site obstruction extends to the actual leading pattern

    r=(R,-(R+S),S),  R>=S>0,
    k_j=c r_j,  sum i_j=0,

In this class G7 is strictly negative, so a fixed analytic/formal ordinary-return family cannot continue through that coefficient.

For c nonnegative, the proportional-area identity already makes G7 strictly negative. For c negative, let u=|c|. The two positive-r outer cells satisfy the reviewed actual ratio bound `q1=i1/k1>-42` and `q3=i3/k3>-42`. The scalar i sum gives

    q2=(R q1+S q3)/(R+S)>-42.

The middle same-source identity therefore gives `b2>27u/4`, just as in the symmetric case.

Write the effective positive clock distances as

    D1=ell1+b2,  D2=ell2+b3.

The full quartic chronological moment forces `R D1=S D2`. Hence the clock-square cost satisfies

    C=D1 R^2+D2 S^2=D1 R(R+S)
      >(27u/4)R(R+S).

The proportional quartic area is `A=u(R^2+RS+S^2)`. A zero G7 would require `C=(9/2)A`. But R>=S implies

    (9u/2)(R^2+RS+S^2)<=(27u/4)R(R+S),

because the difference has sign `(R-S)(R+2S)>=0`. Thus C strictly exceeds the required value, and G7<0.

Consequently a three-site proportional negative-c escape with positive outer cubic coefficients must have a STRICTLY LARGER later outer amplitude S>R before it can even pass this necessary source/clock comparison. This is not a construction when S>R.
