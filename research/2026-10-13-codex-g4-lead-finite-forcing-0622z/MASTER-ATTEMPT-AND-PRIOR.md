## 1. The decisive route, and its two unproved implications

At the private full-forest interface, use the inherited product routing kernel

```
R_W(x,y) = (product_j z_j) product_i q_i^[x_i=y_i],
C(W) = (product_j z_j)(product_i q_i).
```

The known rank is `2^L` for a strict L-cell word. For a target T, GKP needs only finitely many graph densities, with target-dependent bound `4(2^L)^2-2^L`. The missing input is a theorem deriving equality of those densities from a finite collection of permitted target observations, uniformly over all finite rival lengths. Defining the kernel does not prove that implication.

The [October 13 reviewed gate](../2026-10-13-dot-g4-gkp-observer-gate-0221z/DIRECT-GKP-CHRONOLOGY-ATTEMPT.md) already proves that this target-relative adapter is equivalent to private semantic finite forcing, using GKP, rank and the parameter-uniform bounded-shape decoder. It also proves exact static-kernel chronology loss by positive pad redistribution. Both facts are inherited; neither is a new obstruction or solution here.

For a general original target there is an additional gap: legal whole-source endpoint probabilities must control all residual private blocks across all compatible retained cores and placements, with one shared physical bank. The COMMON palette neither isolates these blocks nor supplies a faithful INDEPENDENT decoder. Solving the private adapter alone cannot silently discharge that transfer.

A sufficient new result would be either:

- a legal, target-dependent whole-source implication bounding the actual residual equal-arm word counts across these finite templates, followed by the existing bounded-shape theorem; or
- a direct finite whole-source equality certificate that forces every permitted future law, with no size-reduction step.

This formulation does not impose either result as an additional admission assumption. It identifies the missing conclusion.

## 2. Repairs examined, with the exact failure point

### Static motifs from endpoint forests

Clique densities have the actual no-merger interpretation. A general motif requires specified pairs of the same latent types to interact while other pairs do not. The original experiment does not offer those pair switches; after a merger, its descendants are one opaque current lineage. Algebraic products of probabilities from separate repetitions do not give shared latent vertex types. No legal target-relative construction was obtained.

The previously accepted finite-cap P3 nonrecovery result blocks a universal decoder whose cap depends only on the motif. Its target changes with the cap, so it does not disprove target-dependent G4. The pad-redistribution family preserves all static motifs but is separated by the inherited finite-shape chronological tests. It also is not a fixed-target all-prefix negative witness.

### Exchangeable hierarchy representation

At an exchangeable private one-population interface, one can represent the sampled genealogy hierarchy by a random measured real tree. That representation retains a random directing object. Finite demographic source size does not prove that object has finite support, finite rank or finitely many steps. Averaged sampled hierarchy probabilities do not reveal the conditional directing object. Consequently the representation theorem does not supply GKP input or a finite positive-moment rank defect. The old observable-Gram and Kingman-frequency countercontrols must still be respected. General multi-taxon data are only exchangeable within their sampling classes; unrestricted exchangeability is not assumed there.

### Compactification plus all-copy uniqueness

The accepted bounded-COMMON-clock compactification gives one subsequence at all caps, a countable family of positive persistent cells, and a weak ordinary-clock complement. It need not have a first persistent cell. Finite strict ordered normal form cannot simply be applied to it.

For the already isolated biased one-cell stress target with positive initial ordinary duration a, the accepted equal-prefix and overshoot arguments remove effective initial durations c=a and c>a. The **c<a** case remains. The existing diagonal negative-reservoir identities, source-uniform negative-part bound and weak-scale bad-cell positivity do not yet compare all transported positive and negative contributions at the target's attenuation rate. Inverse ordinary smoothing likewise needs an actual source positivity theorem, not just a finite matrix inverse or its norm bound. This failure occurs inside the general problem; resolving this one-cell diagnostic would still require the full original-core transfer.

### Positive additive separators from copy observations

For a natural equal-arm private word, with `p=g(1-g)`, `y=1/q`, define

```
R_n(p,y) = sum_(k=0)^n binom(n,k) g^k(1-g)^(n-k) y^[k(n-k)].
```

The normalized log diagonals add over actual cells, and the common clock bounds y. A global nonnegative finite linear combination of these log coordinates, vanishing at the target's cell parameters and with a suitable zero set, could force an actual count bound. However, point separation or all-copy identification does not imply the existence of such a nonnegative finite combination. Stone-Weierstrass cannot be applied to a linear span without its algebra property. Multiplying two additive observed sums introduces cross-cell terms; it does not provide the sum of products at the same cell.

The already accepted arity-12 log-guard obstruction and local weak-insertion separator keep their exact scopes. The former is not an all-arity impossibility theorem; the latter first requires a target-body neighbourhood. No global separator, density theorem or global neighbourhood-entry argument was proved in this pass. No new bounded numerical separator search was run.
