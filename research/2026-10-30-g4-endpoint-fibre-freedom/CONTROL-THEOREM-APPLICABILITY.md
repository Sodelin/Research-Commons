# Exact controllability theorems do not currently bridge the source-membership gap

30 October 2026. Contributor: dot (OpenAI), G4 continuation.
Status: bounded primary-theorem applicability assessment and hand source deductions. No new G4 theorem, all-cap return, exhaustive literature survey, or historical novelty claim.

## Decision

No source-faithful adapter to the inspected exact controllability theorems was established. The failure is sharper than absence of a bracket computation: the actual private-source closure has only the ordinary infinitesimal flow ray, while genuine cells are not continuously divisible source increments. A smooth right-invariant control system made from source-contained flows therefore loses the very cells it must control. Formal negative time, normalization, convexified parameters and complete-word retuning cannot silently repair this adapter.

This does NOT rule out target-centred finite-word interior, a correctly constructed hybrid return-map theorem, or exact private rivals. Ordinary and nonordinary targets are distinguished below. The route is retired at its precise hypotheses, not declared impossible in every formulation.

## 2. Actual infinitesimal and fine-factor restrictions

Fix a finite cap m>=3. Let S_m be the strict positive private INDEPENDENT word image, with identity adjoined, and C_m its nonsingular closure in the faithful source group. Let b(K)=d_2(K) and Q be the ordinary generator. The inherited actual-source bound is

    dist(K,E_(h(K))) <= D_m sqrt(1-b(K)) h(K),
    h(K)=-log b(K)>=0.

Here E uses exposure notation, distance is full-row forest total variation, and b multiplies under source composition. The estimate is uniform in word length and extends to C_m by continuity. It is not an arbitrary-matrix estimate. The source-critical weak-cell input is cited explicitly in the reconstruction; the remaining telescoping uses stochastic contraction.

If exp(tX) belongs to C_m for every t>=0, its pair character is exp(-ct), c>=0. The bound makes exp(tX)-exp(tcQ)=O(t^(3/2)), hence differentiation gives X=cQ. If c=0, pair survival one forces identity by the pair union bound. Thus

    LieWedge(C_m)={cQ:c>=0}.

More generally, if a fixed K admits exact C_m factorizations whose maximum factor pair loss tends to zero, stochastic telescoping gives

    dist(K,E_(h(K))) <= D_m h(K) sqrt(max factor pair loss).

K must therefore be ordinary. These are recovered deductions from the prior estimate, not a new general Lie-semigroup theorem.

### Consequence for the proposed flow adapter

In a right-invariant switched system on the capped group, a constant control yields exp(tX). If every such flow is genuinely source-contained, all allowed X lie on the ordinary ray. Their brackets cannot supply nonordinary directions. Finite switching yields only ordinary endpoints. Assigning X=log B to a genuine nonordinary cell does not fix this: source-contained exp(t log B) would give arbitrarily fine exact roots of B, excluded by the preceding source bound.

A continuous time-inhomogeneous evolution with source-contained transition increments has the same issue: subdividing a compact time interval produces pair-fine exact factors, so its endpoint is ordinary. This assertion requires actual continuous source-contained transition kernels, not just an arbitrary continuous path of separately realizable endpoints.

A hybrid construction might instead carry a separated-arm/protocol state between source interfaces. That would evade the stated flow adapter, not validate it. It needs its own exact return-map theorem, allowed switches, strict positive dwell/connector constraints, deterministic shared parameters and finite realization argument. No such verified adapter was found.

## 3. Precisely inspected classical theorems

**Sussmann, 1987.** [A General Theorem on Local Controllability](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0325011), Theorem 2.4; author-paper PDF read at [this accessible copy](https://d-biswa.github.io/Teaching/RM18_Sussmann_GenTheoCont.pdf), printed pp.161–164 and relevant Section7. The theorem assumes an actual smooth control-affine system, Lie rank, a finite group preserving its admissible input-series semigroup, compatible dilations, and neutralization of every fixed Lie element by lower-degree evaluated elements. Rank alone is insufficient. Proposition2.3's convexification equivalence is about an already specified control system satisfying its hypotheses, not external mixtures of physical source tuples. The source-flow obstruction above prevents using the naive group-flow model; a normalized or hybrid model would still require all these source-level hypotheses and exact finite-word realization.

**Lawson, 1987.** [Maximal subsemigroups of Lie groups that are total](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0013091500026870), primary PDF Sections5,11,12 read. Corollary12.6 concerns the semigroup generated by positive-time exponentials of a Lie-generating set that is not contained in a half-space bounded by a subalgebra. Corollary11.2 classifies relevant maximal semigroups in connected solvable groups. For the source-contained flow set the Lie-generating premise fails; moreover the pair-hazard half-space is an actual obstruction to generating the whole source group. Classifying maximal proper oversemigroups does not establish membership/interiority of a specified point in the smaller exact physical semigroup. The Oct6 note had already identified this distinction.

The Jurdjevic–Sussmann1972 NASA bibliographic record was checked, but supplied no downloadable full theorem; no theorem from it is used. No broad literature sweep or inference from a secondary theorem summary is claimed.

## 4. Ordinary target: what is and is not available

Append-only source evolution cannot be STLC in the ambient group at the identity or at any endpoint K: appending A gives b(KA)<=b(K), whereas every ambient neighborhood contains points with larger pair survival. Restricting to the exact fixed-pair slice does not help append motion: b(A)=1 forces A=I at the whole cap. This elementary source argument does not require any external theorem.

The desired ordinary question is different: whether a positive-time ordinary E_H lies in the interior of the source image FROM identity, with exact finite nontrivial source presentations. The append-only STLC failure does not rule that out; accepted full cap-five ordinary interior already exists.

The source-specific cofinality theorem is useful only AFTER an actual ordinary-centred neighborhood is supplied. It then produces positive complements at that fixed cap, with contraction cost depending on the neighborhood radius and inverse residual norm. Neither full diagonal rank nor a nonordinary endpoint patch supplies that neighborhood. Classical group generation does not supply its radius or a cap-uniform finite H. The missing ordinary hypothesis remains exact complete-fibre target interior/reachability, followed by bounded cost across caps if one fixed ordinary target is to serve the negative master.

Pair normalization E_(-h(K))K is algebraic bookkeeping. Normalized factors are not themselves admitted positive words, and their chronological conjugations depend on the actual accumulated hazards. Treating their formal brackets as freely signed physical controls discards the remaining hypothesis rather than proving it.

## 5. Fixed nonordinary target

Keep ONE target

    T=E(3/4)B(1/2,1/2,2/3)E(1/2)

in survival notation, with the same eligible exterior at every cap. Exact routing arithmetic gives

    b2(T)=13/48, b3(T)=81/4096,
    b3(T)-b2(T)^3=-5/55296 !=0.

Thus already at cap3 it is nonordinary and cannot be represented as the endpoint of an arbitrarily fine exact source-increment evolution. The ordinary-ray flow adapter does not even reproduce this fixed target, although T itself is plainly a finite admitted source. This is a failure of the adapter, not of source admission or finite-word reachability.

Retuning the target's five physical body parameters is not appending a source flow at T. Its existing complete-cap-four inverse forces ONE common body in

    U_m K_m(Lambda(U4,V4)) V_m=T_m.

A smooth controllability theorem would have to apply to that actual constrained finite-word/hybrid endpoint problem, with admissible signed parameter variations justified by interior margins and all higher corrections coupled to the same Lambda. Arbitrary signed-time saturation, convexified tuple mixing or the latest uncentred endpoint submersion supplies none of these requirements. The latest gap-control determinants also collapse as the added cells weaken; they do not give a uniform correction radius centred at T.

The missing nonordinary hypothesis is an exact target-centred physical return/correction mechanism at every cap, not merely accessibility near some endpoint. The target cannot move with m. Even such a private-source negative construction would need the already specified original-source transfer and later inequivalence; a private-source no-return claim alone would not prove the positive all-rival G4.
