## Bottom line

Some work gives stronger, reusable formal statements or connects abstract statements to the actual source model. This is a source-backed report of the cases inspected, not an exhaustive attribution history of every manuscript or module. Mathematical correctness, new formal coverage, historical novelty and discovery causation are separate questions.

## 2. A strengthened construction developed in the formalization programme

### G6: a rational witness inside the same natural cell
**Earlier route:** the accepted cell-witness cloud argument can take an algebraic witness inside a feasible cell and approximate it by a nearby rational source, potentially outside that cell.

**Stronger construction:** for the finite rational natural-cell grammar, replacing positive rates by positive inverse-rate variables makes the supplied constraints rational affine. One can obtain a rational physical source **inside the same cell**, preserving all shared ages, rates and inheritance coordinates.

The [18:35 natural-cell packet](../2026-10-09-dot-g6-natural-cell-feasibility-1835z/README.md) records the hand theorem and first formal equivalence. The [19:02 decision packet](../2026-10-09-dot-g6-proved-natural-cell-decision-1902z/README.md) proves the finite affine decision/witness results. The [rational actual-source consumer](../2026-10-09-dot-g6-rational-source-cell-2015z/README.md) reconstructs the actual Calendar/rate/gamma bank and derives the actual rational uniformization mean used by the inherited error certificate.

**What expanded:** the construction avoids the algebraic-to-nearby-rational detour for this grammar and removes a supplied mean-equality premise from the final local consumer.

**What did not:** arbitrary nonlinear constraints, fixed irrational rates, complete legal-chart generation and exact unknown-size recognition are outside this result. Reciprocal substitution and affine elimination are classical. The hand manuscript states the strengthened construction before the final Lean consumer, so the latter formalizes that improvement rather than independently discovering it. Historical novelty is not established.

## 4. Corrections and rediscoveries that changed the plan

### Whole-calendar laws were already available
The apparent “whole-calendar gap” was too broad. Actual composition, refinement, once-drawn natural-register mixing and completed past/suffix laws already had compiled providers. The [public correction](../2026-10-09-dot-g5-timed-cut-source-integration-1943z/WHOLE-CALENDAR-PRIOR-AUDIT-1957Z.md) preserves this distinction. The remaining work is the precise application to an observation/conditioning/decoder interface.

### Parent-probability conventions differ
Legacy G7 Python and some G4 formulas use gamma for parent0. Current Lean natural initialization uses gamma for parent1. A faithful asymmetric connection needs the complement substitution or a proved simultaneous parent/bit swap. Both conventions are valid; equal-arm expressions depending on gamma(1-gamma) are invariant. This is an integration correction, not a refutation of an older theorem.

### Zero-duration support was a recovered premise
The finite count proxy needs b(0)=0 so a zero-duration interval cannot invent mergers. That support issue was already explained in the accepted prior density/support argument. Making it explicit in the new formal consumer is useful, but it should not be presented as a new mathematical discovery.

### Richer G2 results were outside the recent small dependency set
The [October7 accepted timed endpoint](../2026-10-07-dot-g2-timed-endpoint-ordinary-integration-0941z/SCIENTIFIC-ACCEPTANCE.md) already includes natural and fixed-ID controlled timed all-panel projectivity, literal pruning, faithful topology/ages and exact time-forgetting. The later G5 hidden-register timed consumer also compiled and passed independent review. These results should be imported where applicable rather than rebuilt. Named-report acceptance and complete closure/audit/replay remain distinct verification levels.
