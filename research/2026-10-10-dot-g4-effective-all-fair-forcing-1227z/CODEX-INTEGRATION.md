# Codex implementation contract

This packet specifies a mathematically terminating algorithm. It contains no implemented QE search, tested runtime, concrete cap or Lean theorem. All earlier source/build certificates keep their own exact scopes.

## Inputs and output

Input a known finite strict equal-arm private target with all coins 1/2 and effectively algebraic survivals q_i,z_j. Include their exact minimal-polynomial/root-isolation representations. Compute the common survival c, rational Hbar>-log c and Q>1/c. Unknown rival words need no size bound and may use arbitrary strict real coins.

Output a complete-forest cap, the COMMON pair test, the finite local-body certificate, and finitely many real-algebraic nonexistence certificates for the bounded proxy families. The original legal test menu is a further compilation through the exact all-J/same-bank BOTH decoder, including its calibration rows. No altered source, added time readout or independent row fitting is permitted.

## Implementation order

1. Reuse the accepted finite forest compiler and supplied finite normal form. Enumerate a finite fair-body response map with isolated target fibre, using exact real QE in the fixed body parameter ring.
2. Search for the compact power inequality, the higher-jet separator and a certified open local neighborhood. Keep the one-sided p<=1/4 condition. Bounds may be extremely large.
3. Derive computable complete-row weak-cell constants using the mixed derivative estimate. Use p t^2<=t w for the global approximation. A rare-coin O(w^2) substitution is not valid.
4. At stage r choose cap m_r and then epsilon_r with C_(m_r) Hbar epsilon_r<=1/r. Enumerate all bounded head counts and test the exact compact semialgebraic proxy formula.
5. Stop only when every Bad-proxy formula is certified FALSE. Store the exact source variables, local certificate and finite QE evidence. A plateau, a numerical search, or failure to find witnesses is insufficient.

The proof of halting realizes every relaxed two-clock proxy by an approximating actual strict same-clock word, with filler strengths tending to zero. The positive retained target cells must then come from old proxy heads. The open good-body neighborhood gives the contradiction. A formalization must retain those quantitative/chronological links rather than assert that the proxies are already strict sources.

## Limits and attribution

The result covers supplied all-fair algebraic private targets, including repeated durations and arbitrary finite serial cell count. It is not a general G4 algorithm for biased targets or arbitrary original networks. Original reticulation levels and parallel ordered edge occurrences remain governed by the separately accepted reduction/decoder contracts; no graph reduction is added here.

Reuse the pinned skeleton, local higher-jet theorem, source compiler, normal form, compactification and calibrated decoder. Classical real quantifier elimination and the semialgebraic power inequality are inherited tools. No historical novelty claim or new empirical performance claim is made.
