Nearby regular source words, closure membership, a stochastic residual, or an infinite product whose limit is not an admitted target do not supply the premise.

## 2. Precise sufficient hypotheses

Suppose T is one fixed finite actual source. Suppose there is one sequence of actual strict finite words A_1,A_2,... with infinitely many total bigons, such that for every finite cap m:

1. The chronological endpoint products K_m(A_1)...K_m(A_n) converge to K_m(T).
2. For some finite r=r(m), freely varying finitely many existing physical parameters theta in the first r factors gives a smooth prefix map P_m(theta), with baseline theta_0 strictly inside its legal parameter domain, whose differential has full rank dim(G_m). Here G_m is the inherited actual positive-diagonal source group in its faithful finite forest algebra.

The first hypothesis is equality to the **same actual T** at every cap, not a capwise choice of target or factor sequence. The second is rank at this very factorization, not at a nearby word with a different endpoint. These hypotheses may also be formulated with an actual open prefix image rather than a full-rank chart, but the differential version gives a direct finite correction.

### Tail limits are nonsingular

For fixed m and r, put P_0=P_m(theta_0) and R_n=K_m(A_(r+1))...K_m(A_n). Each finite prefix endpoint is invertible in G_m. By hypothesis 1,

    R_n -> R_infinity = P_0^(-1) K_m(T).

The right-hand side is an invertible group element because both P_0 and K_m(T) are actual strict finite endpoints. This conclusion does not require treating the inverse as a physical source. The tail limit may fail to be an actual finite endpoint.

## 3. Exact finite truncation correction

Work in a local chart on G_m. Select dim(G_m) physical prefix parameter coordinates whose Jacobian minor is nonsingular at theta_0 and hold every other prefix coordinate fixed. Write u for the selected coordinates and P(u) for the resulting prefix endpoint.

Consider the finite-dimensional equation

    P(u) R = K_m(T)

near (u_0,R_infinity). Right multiplication by the invertible R_infinity is a local diffeomorphism on G_m. The selected derivative in u is therefore invertible. The ordinary inverse/implicit-function theorem supplies a continuous local solution u=u(R), with u(R_infinity)=u_0.

For all sufficiently large finite n, R_n lies in that neighborhood. Thus u_n=u(R_n) is well-defined, converges to u_0, and satisfies

    P(u_n) R_n = K_m(T) exactly.

Strict positivity persists because u_0 is an interior physical parameter point. The corrected first r factors followed by the unchanged finite factors through n are a genuine finite strict source W_(m,n), in their original chronological order. No signed-time operation, arbitrary stochastic kernel, mixture, or independently retuned arity is used. Every arity receives the same corrected finite tuple; equality through cap m follows from the one complete endpoint equation.

As n grows, the number of bigons in W_(m,n) is unbounded; changing strict parameters in the fixed prefix does not delete any bigon. Choose n so that its count differs from the finite count of T. The accepted all-copy count invariant then proves that W_(m,n) and T are not fully response-equivalent, although their complete kernels through cap m agree. Hence some later admitted private test distinguishes them. Choosing m large enough for any supplied finite original observation prefix gives precisely the negative G4 rival quantifier for this fixed T and wrapper.

This is an existence argument. It does not claim an effective computed inverse radius, algebraic corrected parameters, or execution of a source compiler. The original real positive rival contract is sufficient for this conditional reduction.
