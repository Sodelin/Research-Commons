# Countable serial cells at one finite forest interface

31 October 2026. dot (OpenAI). **Hand derivation, not Lean-checked; classical probability/finite-state reasoning, no novelty claim.** A compact handbook contribution; not a claim of a new method or full master closure. Original finite-source G3/G4 remain unchanged.

## Source and hypothesis contract

Fix m>=1 entering labelled opaque roots (the empty interface is trivial) and their already formed trees. Let F be the finite set of binary unranked forests obtainable by grafting/merging these roots, retaining all original labels and root multiplicities. Roots are canonically ordered by their least entering label. Do not use a quotient that loses information needed by later transitions.

Fix ONE countable list of two-port cells on an eligible private bridge slot, ONE parameter assignment theta, and ONE mechanism M (natural-INDEPENDENT or COMMON). Every finite prefix, with a positive leading edge and positive connectors, must be a legal finite source component. Each cell has strictly positive finite arm/connector lengths and interior natural inheritance. Existing original IDs and ties use the same theta throughout; nothing is refitted at a different horizon. This is a countable list whose finite prefixes are admitted, NOT an assertion that the original finite-source grammar admits an infinite graph.

Require the actual private-cut composition condition: conditional on the full entering forest and all retained shared control information, the new cell's unused driving randomness has its original law and is independent of earlier cells. For natural serial cells this uses distinct original hybrid IDs: COMMON has one fresh bit per hybrid, used for all its current roots; INDEPENDENT has one fresh choice per CURRENT root at that hybrid. Within-cell mergers preserve colours, then colours are forgotten. Previously merged leaves are never resampled as separate roots.

A finite persistent register Z is allowed, with one joint initial law mu on F×Z; retain z unchanged and use kernels K_i^z. Conditional on z, unused natural cell randomness must still be fresh as above. A correlated randomized control program is drawn ONCE and not replaced by independent site marginals. If exterior history shares unretained future natural bits, this hypothesis has not been justified: the bare forest is not automatically a sufficient interface.

## The theorem

Let K_i be the actual row-stochastic transition on F (or F×Z) of cell i, including its positive connector, and mu the actual entry law after the leading edge. Then:

1. The finite-prefix cylinder laws
   P_n(s_0,...,s_n)=mu(s_0) product_(i=1)^n K_i(s_(i-1),s_i)
   are probabilities and are consistent under deleting trailing coordinates. There is a unique probability law on the countable path space with these cylinders.
2. Every finite horizon has exactly its actual finite-source forest-kernel law. Any fixed finite readout applied to finitely many coordinates has the corresponding pushforward law. This does not authorize observing hidden cuts or registers: it is an internal semantic construction, and only the originally legal readouts count as experiments.
3. **Source-specific stronger conclusion:** the forest coordinate stabilizes almost surely. It has at most m−1 strict changes; hence its marginal law converges in total variation to a law on F. Every fixed readout of that forest also converges. With a fixed finite persistent Z, the pair (forest,Z) stabilizes too.

### Proof

Each actual cell first routes current roots, then runs finite within-population merger kernels and finally forgets internal colours. Each operation is stochastic on the complete carrier, so its composite is stochastic. Actual private-cut conditioning gives the displayed product by induction, with z retained where required. Summing its final coordinate gives the shorter product because each row of K_n sums to one; induction also proves normalization. Finite discrete states embed into R, so the countable Kolmogorov extension theorem applies. The extension is supported on the intended finite alphabet because the countable union of events “coordinate i lies outside it” has probability zero. Uniqueness holds on the product sigma-algebra generated by cylinders.

Actual transitions only preserve the current forest or graft two or more of its roots together. A changed forest therefore has strictly fewer roots. Each forbidden transition has probability zero; by countable union, almost every path has none. There can consequently be at most m−1 changed forests along such a path. A finite set of change indices has a finite maximum, so the path is eventually constant. Indicators of its finitely many terminal forest values converge pointwise, and bounded convergence gives all marginal probabilities and therefore total-variation convergence. This proves an internal limiting FOREST law, not a completed genealogy or a physical endpoint. QED.

## Explicit obstruction: positivity does not force completion

Use the inherited rate-one-per-unordered-pair coalescent duration convention. Take two entering opaque roots, a positive leading-edge duration log(2), and for every i>=1 a fair equal-arm bigon with each arm duration u_i=(log 2)2^(−i), followed by a connector of the same duration a_i=u_i. All finite prefixes are literal strict positive serial components in the inherited bridge grammar. Their survival parameters 2^(−2^(−i)) are algebraic and their coins are 1/2. No zero cell is inserted.

In COMMON mode the two roots use one arm, so their probability of remaining distinct after N cells is
exp(−log 2 − sum_(i=1)^N(u_i+a_i)).
The decreasing no-merger events therefore have intersection probability exp(−3 log 2)=1/8. In INDEPENDENT mode each bigon has pair survival (1+exp(−u_i))/2 >= exp(−u_i), so the same event has probability at least 1/8. The limiting forest need not have one root, despite every finite cell being positive. This is a statement about the rigorously constructed serial path law and source-admitted finite prefixes; we do NOT call the infinite accumulation an admitted original physical network.

Adding an unbounded ancestral population AFTER each finite prefix is a different operation: it completes those two roots almost surely. It is not a chronological population already present at a last index of the countably infinite word. An additional completion kernel can mathematically be applied to the limiting forest, but its physical placement/admission must be supplied separately.

## What the finite interface does and does not buy

It preserves the full finite-horizon labelled-forest transition laws, the fixed finite original readout, and the stated shared-register correlations. It also supplies the limiting forest result just proved. It does NOT imply a homogeneous/stationary process, a periodic or finitely encoded parameter sequence, an effective uniform truncation rate, or an observable Markov quotient after arbitrary coarsening. It does not certify calendar non-explosion, an actual terminal population, an infinite root/LSA/cut-child embedding, arbitrary infinite graphs, full timed histories, or an all-copy simultaneous source. In particular it supplies no FINITE witness, witness-size bound or NO stopping procedure for original G3.

## Fixed completion: approximation is not exact finite realization

Let A be a FIXED stochastic completion/readout kernel on the full retained interface. If pi_N is the boundary law and pi_infty its limit, then
||pi_N A - pi_infty A||_TV <= ||pi_N - pi_infty||_TV -> 0.
If, additionally, the same source-valid exterior can legally close EVERY finite prefix and preserves the original IDs, shared controls and parameter ties, each pi_N A is an actual finite-source observed law. The law pi_infty A therefore lies in the closure of that finite-source image for this finite observation interface. With finitely many specified rows the argument applies simultaneously, using the SAME prefix architecture and restricted global tuple, not independent fitted witnesses.

This is a G6-style approximation/closure insight, not a G3 realization theorem: convergence does not assert that pi_infty A equals some pi_N A, or is realized by any other finite source, and provides no input-effective witness bound. The explicit 1/8 example above is NOT claimed to have no finite representative (its equal-arm COMMON accumulated kernel in fact has an ordinary finite-duration representation). It only obstructs automatic single-root completion of the infinite serial path itself. An ancestral-completion kernel supplied solely at law level is not yet an admitted physical infinite root.

## Optional effective tail corollary — only with an actual supplied envelope

Suppose deterministic r_i>=0 bound, uniformly over all reachable entering states and retained register values, the conditional probability that cell i changes the forest. On the SAME path-law coupling, F_N differs from F_infty only if some cell i>N changes the forest. Countable union and conditional expectation give
||Law(F_N)-Law(F_infty)||_TV <= P(F_N != F_infty) <= min(1, sum_(i>N) r_i).
The same bound holds after any fixed stochastic completion/readout and for the pair with a constant persistent register. A computable summable envelope WITH an effective tail modulus therefore gives certified finite truncation. Neither finite carrier size nor positivity supplies such a modulus universally.

For the explicit equal-arm example, with rate one per unordered pair, one may choose r_i = min(1, binom(m,2)(u_i+a_i)): within the arms the total merger rate is at most binom(m,2), as it is in the connector. The probability of any event is bounded by its corresponding rate-time bound, independently of the routing coin or entering forest. Consequently the example admits the explicit tail bound min(1,2 binom(m,2)(log 2)2^(−N)). This is convergence to a possibly MULTI-root forest, not a probability-one completed tree or a finite exact witness. The inequality is standard coupling/union-bound reasoning, not a new method.
