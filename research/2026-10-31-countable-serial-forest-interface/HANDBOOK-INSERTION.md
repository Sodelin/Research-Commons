# Finite interfaces for countable serial source prefixes

**Status:** scoped hand derivation using existing source kernels and classical extension; not Lean-checked, not a new infinite-network admission theorem. Full derivation: HAND-THEOREM.md; immutable source pins and hashes: UPSTREAM-SOURCE-MAP.json.

**Contract.** Fix m>=1 labelled opaque entering roots, one countable sequence of strict two-port cells on an eligible private bridge slot, and ONE parameter/ID assignment. Each finite prefix must be an admitted finite component with positive leading edge and connectors. The interface is the full finite unranked labelled forest, optionally with a fixed finite persistent control register. Conditional on that complete interface, unused natural cell randomness must be fresh. COMMON uses one bit per hybrid for all its current roots; natural-INDEPENDENT routes each current root once. Retain correlated controls jointly rather than resampling their marginals. Arbitrary correlated exteriors require a separate sufficient-state proof.

**Theorem and proof.** Actual cell kernels K_i are stochastic on this finite carrier. Their finite path probabilities are mu(s_0) product_i K_i(s_(i−1),s_i). Summing the last coordinate returns the preceding path law, so classical countable extension gives a unique path law with these exact finite-prefix marginals. Since each actual changed forest has fewer roots, almost every path changes at most m−1 times and is eventually constant. Its forest marginals therefore converge in total variation. These hidden interface coordinates are not thereby authorized observations.

**Why convergence need not mean a completed genealogy.** With rate one per unordered pair, take two roots, leading duration log2, and fair equal-arm cells whose arm and connector durations are each (log2)2^(−i). Every finite prefix is strict and source-admitted; all survival parameters are algebraic. The probability of never merging is 1/8 in COMMON and at least 1/8 in INDEPENDENT. Thus the limiting forest can retain two roots. An ancestral completion applied to that law is an extra continuation; an actual infinite physical endpoint/calendar has not been supplied.

**Useful finite approximation.** If supplied r_i bound the conditional probability of a forest change in cell i uniformly over reachable interface states, then coupling gives
TV(pi_N,pi_infty) <= min(1,sum_(i>N) r_i).
An effective summable tail modulus certifies truncation. In the displayed equal-arm example, one may choose r_i=min(1,binom(m,2)(u_i+a_i)), giving tail bound min(1,2 binom(m,2)(log2)2^(−N)). No universal envelope follows from positivity alone.

A FIXED stochastic completion/readout contracts total variation. If the same actual legal exterior closes every finite prefix, respecting original ties and registers, its limiting observed law is approximated by actual finite-source laws. This is a closure/approximation statement—not exact realization by a finite source, effective G3 stopping, stationarity, periodic parameters, or an arbitrary infinite-graph theorem. The example is not claimed to lack a finite representative. Empty interfaces are trivial. No source-size or observation-menu assumption is silently changed.
