## 1. Perturbation and certified truncation

Use TV(P,Q)=sup_A|P(A)−Q(A)|, between zero and one. Let K_i,L_i be stochastic kernels on the SAME finite sufficient carrier (including any retained finite persistent register), and let d_i=sup_s TV(K_i(s,.),L_i(s,.)). For initial laws mu,nu put d_0=TV(mu,nu). Then

TV(mu K_1...K_N, nu L_1...L_N) <= min(1,d_0+sum_(i=1)^N d_i).

**Proof.** A stochastic kernel contracts TV. Also TV(rho K_i,rho L_i)<=d_i, by integrating the pointwise signed difference against rho. Insert rho L_i between the two updated laws and apply the triangle inequality; induction gives the formula. If both coarsening forest laws converge, passage to their finite-state limits gives the same estimate with sum_(i>=1)d_i. Any fixed common stochastic readout/completion also contracts the bound. Different readouts need their own error term. This is not a same-source statement if the approximating kernels are merely numerical stochastic matrices; physical realization of those kernels is separate.

Suppose the actual infinite law has a supplied tail certificate R_N bounding the probability of ANY forest change after N, uniformly over reachable states/register values. A computed N-cell approximation has the certified error

TV(actual limiting law, computed N-cell law) <= R_N+d_0+sum_(i=1)^N d_i.

If R_N has an effective modulus tending to zero AND each finite-head kernel and initial law can be refined to arbitrarily small certified total error, any desired approximation tolerance is achievable: first choose N making R_N less than half the tolerance, then refine the finitely many head approximations so d_0+sum_(i=1)^N d_i fits the remaining budget. Merely computable fixed nonzero error bounds do not suffice. This can certify robust inequalities separated from zero. It does not decide exact equality from a finite tolerance, supply a legal finite exact witness, or prove original G3 NO termination. Actual rate/coin/duration error to kernel-TV error is an ADDITIONAL source adapter; no constant or conditioning bound for it is assumed here.

**Useful uniform-family corollary.** Suppose a family of such source laws, including its proposed limit, has a COMMON tail envelope R_N→0. If its initial laws and every fixed finite list of kernels converge in TV, then its limiting forest laws converge in TV. For each N the bound is 2R_N plus the finite-prefix perturbation error. First choose N, then take the family limit. A common effective modulus and effective head estimates make this convergence quantitative. Without the common tail hypothesis the next example applies.

**Prior work:** Abate, Redig and Tkachev, *On the effect of perturbation of conditional probabilities in total variation*, arXiv:1311.3066, Proposition 2 and Theorem 1, already give general product-kernel extension and linear perturbation bounds; their Borel-space result is stronger still. Their TV norm ranges up to two, whereas ours ranges up to one. The estimate here is a standard finite-state specialization and telescoping proof, not novelty. https://arxiv.org/html/1311.3066

## 2. Source-specialized moving-tail obstruction

Take the already admitted strict serial finite prefixes with two entering roots, leading coalescent duration log2, fair equal arms u_i=(log2)2^(-i), and connector durations a_i=(log2)2^(-i). Fix COMMON inheritance. The limiting no-merger probability is

p=exp(−log2−sum_i(u_i+a_i))=1/8.

For each integer n>=1 define a new countable list by adding log2 to connector n and changing NOTHING else. Every finite prefix is still an admitted positive source component; all coins remain interior and all survival coordinates remain algebraic. IDs and the remaining tuple can be kept unchanged. This construction uses the untied duration slot in the cited natural serial grammar, not arbitrary additional protected cross-edge equalities.

For any fixed prefix length N, the changed list agrees exactly with the base prefix whenever n>N. Thus all its fixed-prefix laws eventually agree, and every fixed parameter coordinate converges to the base value. Nevertheless its limiting no-merger mass is p_n=1/16 for EVERY n. There are only two possible two-root forests, so the limiting forest-law TV distance is exactly 1/16, not zero.

**Why no contradiction:** the family has no common vanishing tail-change bound. For n>N the late connector alone has an extra hazard log2. Its effect occurs on a positive surviving two-root mass even when every previously observed prefix is identical. Probability mass in the event-time distribution escapes to arbitrarily late indices. The limit-of-family and terminal-limit operations cannot be exchanged without a uniform hypothesis.

This is the standard moving-tail phenomenon specialized to the actual finite-prefix grammar; it is not a claim of a new probability principle, an admitted infinite physical network, a completed-root failure for an infinite ancestral population, or a no-finite-representative theorem. Each limiting two-root law in this COMMON example has an ordinary finite-duration representation. It blocks an invalid compactness/continuity shortcut, not original exact recognition itself.

## 3. Conditional actual within-cell trace concatenation

**Theorem.** Fix deterministic calendar boundaries 0=t_0<t_1<...→infinity. Use a common Polish full-state carrier with its Borel sigma-algebra. For each cell supply a measurable probability kernel from its full entering sufficient state to a standard Borel space of cadlag within-cell traces, with measurable coordinate and exit-state maps. Its exit pushforward must be the ACTUAL K_i; its traces must have consistent endpoints, finite jumps on that cell, and the original labelled merger/routing/calendar semantics. Assume one fixed parameter/ID assignment and the actual conditional freshness or retained-register property needed to compose these kernels.

Then there is a unique countable joint trace law with those recursive conditional laws. Concatenating traces gives a process at every real time with the supplied actual finite-prefix trace laws. With compatible right-continuous boundary conventions, the full state path is cadlag and has only finitely many jumps on each bounded time interval.

**Proof.** Compose the trace kernels recursively, using the previous exit state; classical Ionescu–Tulcea gives the joint product law. Each trace has finite jumps with probability one, simultaneously for all cells by countable union. Since t_n→infinity, a bounded time interval meets only finitely many cells. Concatenation is a measurable coordinate construction with compatible boundary values; finite unions of finite jump sets give local finiteness and the cadlag assertion. The forest projection still has at most m−1 strict mergers globally, but full routing/location histories need not stabilize.

**What remains source-specific:** the earlier finite boundary kernels do NOT themselves supply these trace kernels or a shared physical calendar. For finitely many roots on a specified finite cell, the ordinary coalescent/routing construction offers a natural trace description, but original clocks, rates, exterior ties and boundary register compatibility must actually be checked. A finite accumulation time is deliberately excluded from this theorem: the forest projection can stabilize while location/routing histories need not have a suitable endpoint. Infinite physical source admission is a further definition/theorem, not part of Ionescu–Tulcea.

**Already proved abstractly:** Shalizi, *Building Infinite Processes from Regular Conditional Probability Distributions*, Theorem 33, gives recursive-kernel extension on measurable spaces: https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~cshalizi/754/notes/lecture-03.pdf . Marion, *A Formalization of the Ionescu–Tulcea Theorem in Mathlib*, arXiv:2506.18616v7, reports a Lean formalization of this abstract construction: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18616v7 . No particular installed Mathlib version, our trace supplier, or this source specialization has been compiled here. Reuse the existing abstract theorem rather than claiming it is newly proved.

## 4. Short lower-priority handbook entries

**Real parameters do not imply an effective input language.** A mathematical kernel may depend on arbitrary positive reals. An algorithm still needs finite exact input representations and effective comparisons/operations. Our original G3 permits its stated rational/effectively algebraic observations; none of these probability extensions changes that. Certified approximation with a tail modulus is not an exact equality oracle.

**Vertices versus histories.** A connected locally finite rooted discrete graph has finite balls, hence countably many vertices. Uncountably many vertices therefore cannot be inserted while retaining that class. A binary tree has countably many vertices but uncountably many branches. Our finite unranked forest histories have countable support because at most m−1 changes are specified by finitely many integer indices and finite states. Independent fair routing bits have uncountably many histories with every individual bitstream of mass zero. Arbitrary interior coin sequences need not be non-atomic. These are elementary cardinality statements, not new phylogenetic identification theorems.

**Arbitrary-index laws.** Knapp, *Advanced Real Analysis*, Theorem 9.5, and Anderson, *Advanced Probability II*, Theorems 6.0.6–6.0.7, already separate consistent finite-dimensional extension from path regularity. For real indexing one supplies laws on every finite set of times, not an uncountable ordered product of our serial matrices. Continuum trees and graphons use different objects and observation languages; they are not drop-in witnesses for the original discrete G-solver. Exact primary references and scope are in REAL-TIME-SOURCE-MAP.json.

## Final prioritization

For the current proofs/solver, retain (1) the conditional error-budget theorem, (2) the explicit moving-tail caution, and (3) a named trace/calendar supplier obligation. These strengthen approximation bookkeeping and prevent invalid witness/compactness inferences. They do not close the missing actual positive finite joint-source lift or effective unknown-size stopping.
