## 3. What would actually be new

Minimum-distance estimation, confidence inversion, compactness-based separation, concentration and Markov-kernel contraction are classical methods. A relevant established reference is [Devroye–Lugosi, Combinatorial Methods in Density Estimation](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4613-0125-7). This index does not claim a new generic estimator or a new minimax theorem.

The source-specific target is an effectively computable inverse separation/modulus for a declared compact rational-margin domain of the new canonical MSci family, under the unchanged homogeneous clock-JC observation law. Fix a computable target metric on the correct history quotient. For requested target tolerance a>0, one needs a certified positive lower bound on

    inf{ d_obs(Q_theta,Q_eta) : theta,eta in K and d_target([theta],[eta])>=a }.

An existence argument from compactness/injectivity alone is not the requested effective bound. Source-bound forward continuity, feasible parameter-cell covers, validated forward probabilities and positive pair-cell exclusion margins must be explicit. Equivalent parameterizations must have quotient distance zero. Incorrectly distinguishing a known equivalence class invalidates the inverse target.

Frontier's proposed full-timed-law Lipschitz coupling is distinct from G6's finite-bin source-size approximation and the earlier observation-rounding bound. Its proof/constants/domain remain subject to the independent review lane. No already specialized fixed-epoch full-metric-TV modulus was located in the retrieved providers; this is a bounded audit, not an exhaustive novelty certificate.

**Experiment dimensions are effective in the accepted provider.** The broad observation theorem3430171e... explicitly gives L(n,J,P)=4^(n−1)[2M(2F+1)−1], with S=Bell(n)P^n, E=sum_(i=0)^(n−1)binom(n,2)^i, F=JS²+S and M=E(nES)^J. In particular, an exact displayed-support impossibility cannot automatically be recast as impossibility for a continuous tolerant history metric that treats the limiting models as close.

## 4. Generic error ledger, not a new statistical theorem

One useful minimum-distance bookkeeping identity is as follows. Let the observed law q be within beta of true model Q_theta, let the empirical law be within s of q, and let a finite cloud approximate the model in BOTH directions to epsilon in the SAME metric. Choose a cloud point within xi of the minimum empirical distance and an actual source witness within epsilon of that point. Triangle inequalities give model-law error at most

    2s + 2beta + 2epsilon + xi.

Numerical enclosure error belongs explicitly in epsilon/xi or in an additional charged term. An incomplete optimizer or an unchecked approximate nearest point does not supply xi. The chosen data radius s is an event with stated probability1−alpha, not an exact empirical-law assertion. Convert to target tolerance only after the reviewed source-specific inverse separation exceeds this full error budget. This generic assembly is included for review and interface clarity, not claimed as novel or as an executed estimator.
