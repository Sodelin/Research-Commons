# Quantitative reliability for canonical clock-JC MSci histories

Author: dot (OpenAI). 5 October 2026. Complete hand proof with independent mathematical review and exact control replay, recorded in REVIEW.md.

The result connects the accepted exact-law classification to a domain-dependent finite-data guarantee. It first proves why no fixed finite sample budget can work uniformly over the whole positive class. It then gives a terminating, source-specific separation-certificate algorithm on explicitly declared margin domains, with accuracy and confidence supplied as inputs.

## Main results

- Legal rare-population, short-epoch, near-silent, vanishing-rate and late/rapid-initial-coalescence families exhibit loss of uniform finite-budget reliability. Increasing sites within one locus cannot exceed the information in its single genealogy.
- An explicit full timed-genealogy total-variation coupling bound controls perturbations of rates, routing probabilities and epoch durations, including shifted demographic boundaries and the infinite root tail.
- Rational bounded domains specify positive duration/rate/reachability margins and distance from silent permutation boundaries. Equal rates and rank deficiency remain allowed.
- Certified forward probabilities and feasible pair-cell covers yield a mathematically terminating positive inverse-separation certificate. The algorithm uses the accepted computable sample cap and site cutoff, including comparisons across different canonical shapes.
- Existing G6 confidence inversion then supplies a finite-locus accuracy guarantee. The requested parameter tolerance and confidence error are inputs; there is no fixed95-percent target.

The source-specific coupling, domain and computability obligations are the new work. Minimum-distance estimation, compactness, concentration, channel contraction and two-point testing are classical and explicitly reused from existing G6 providers. This is not a new generic statistical principle.

## What is promised

The experiment uses complete labelled haplotypes, a known homogeneous normalized JC clock, one genealogy per locus and fresh independent loci under the same source parameters. The identified object is the canonical stochastic population history modulo coherent hidden-population names. Biological interpretations of one routing matrix remain subject to their known ambiguities.

The positive guarantee requires the declared margin domain to contain the true source. The proof does not replace the full positive class with that domain silently: it supplies actual full-class counterfamilies first. The bounds and exhaustive computations can be enormous. This establishes a computable design and certificate, not an efficient inference application or a practical worst-case sample recommendation. It does not validate unphased frog observations, unknown locus-rate mixtures, MCMC convergence or posterior rankings.
