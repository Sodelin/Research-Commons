## 5. Attempted boundary descent and its actual failure

The intended final argument was to stratify the remaining source-closure boundary effectively. On each boundary stratum, either an allowed word factorization would reduce to a lower-dimensional source problem, or a finite invariant would exclude the entire target fibre. Strict dimension decrease over finitely many strata would then terminate.

There is no proved source-specific premise furnishing those strata or that reduction. The finite-dimensional algebraic group H is only the Zariski closure; its polynomial identities also hold on nonphysical points. A semialgebraic compact projective carrier is an outer carrier, not the exact generated semigroup closure. Replacing the missing boundary by the carrier boundary is false as a source classification.

The actual COMMON obstruction is decisive for this particular proposed replacement. The accepted original eight-row calibration has a rational complete NO input which is a limit of genuine positive sources and whose corresponding sparse moment point is in the relative interior of the ordinary moment body. All alternative admitted COMMON cores are excluded by the calibrated source reduction. Thus positivity, finite moment-body faces and ordinary support-boundary tests do not exhaust the exact closure-contact cases. The target is algebraic, not an inadmissible transcendental example.

The same original source interface has rational YES inputs of unbounded minimum exact word count, and the accepted calibrated theorem transfers that minimum to the total original hybrid count. Consequently a menu/cap-only finite word catalogue or a uniform finite pivot cover cannot close the proof. This does not disprove an input-effective bound computed from the full encoded response; that is precisely what would still be needed.

The source-specific semialgebraic-template obstruction is also relevant but must not be overstated. Rational residues give algebraic source-component NO targets requiring arbitrarily high polynomial degree in any separator. Thus a fixed finite certificate library cannot suffice. Allowing template complexity to grow with the input remains possible. The known complete failure of every semialgebraic separator uses a transcendental observed coordinate; it is not a counterexample to certificate completeness for the original algebraic input domain. The later logarithmic certificate supplies a richer finite invariant for these families but neither an unrestricted exponential decision algorithm nor global completeness.

These facts stop the attempted boundary induction at a definite place: there is no actual-source theorem replacing arbitrary coupled closure-contact fibres by finitely many effective lower-dimensional recognition problems. They do not prove that such a theorem or a different complete G3 algorithm is impossible.

## 6. Stronger primary theorems checked and why they do not finish this proof

The following were checked as potential whole-proof shortcuts, with their hypotheses kept separate from the actual source.

1. Fijalkow, Ohlmann, Ouaknine, Pouly and Worrell, *Semialgebraic Invariant Synthesis for the Kannan-Lipton Orbit Problem*, Theorem 1, gives effective invariant synthesis for one fixed algebraic matrix orbit and characterizes closed invariant existence by orbit closure. Its input is one A, one initial point and one point target. Here each append chooses new physical parameters from a continuum, with a semialgebraic target fibre and shared static source variables. There is no proved reduction to one such orbit. The paper itself distinguishes nonreachability from existence of a semialgebraic invariant. [Primary author PDF](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/complete-semialgebraic-invariants19.pdf).

2. Colcombet, Ouaknine, Semukhin and Worrell, *On Reachability Problems for Low-Dimensional Matrix Semigroups*, proves membership decidability for finitely generated rational Heisenberg semigroups. The actual source has a continuum of generators, a growing capped forest dimension, diagonal contractions and a coupled polynomial target. No finite-generator or Heisenberg source reduction has been established. This theorem neither proves our positive result nor proves hardness. [Primary ICALP 2019 paper](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2019.44).

3. Karimov, *Verification of Linear Dynamical Systems via O-Minimality of the Real Numbers*, Theorem 1, decides robust safety for a fixed algebraic matrix, bounded semialgebraic initial set and semialgebraic unsafe set. It does not replace exact zero-margin reachability by robust safety, and its fixed-matrix model is not our fresh-append relation. The source envelopes already furnish a robust closure-exclusion branch under their own exact source premises. [Primary ICALP 2025 paper](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol334-icalp2025/html/LIPIcs.ICALP.2025.163/LIPIcs.ICALP.2025.163.html).

4. Hofmann and Lawson, *Local semigroups in Lie groups and locally reachable sets*, studies one-parameter semigroup controls and gives a rerouting criterion under cone/Lie-wedge hypotheses. A bigon is not a freely divisible physical increment: subdividing it and rerouting each new cell changes the same-source law. Replacing finite cell products by a continuous-control reachable set or its closure therefore requires a source realization theorem that is missing. Signed ordinary-time saturation likewise does not preserve the positive word question. [Primary institutional record and DOI](https://repository.lsu.edu/mathematics_pubs/647/).

The general non-subanalytic reachable-set examples of Lojasiewicz and Sussmann show why polynomial/analytic control descriptions alone do not warrant a finite stratification. They are not embedded in this source class and are not used as a G3 impossibility argument. [Primary SIAM paper](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/pdf/10.1137/0323037).

Baker's theorem on linear forms in logarithms is a potentially relevant arithmetic constraint on algebraic observation data; the earlier Commons critical-residue work already uses such distinctions, and the remaining rank-five arithmetic branch is not exhibited by the logarithmic certificate packet. No claim that Baker settles all coupled boundary strata is made. The complementary whole-problem lane is testing the arithmetic and source-faithful hardness alternatives. [Baker's primary 1967 paper](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579300008068).
