## 3. Precise joint contract

There are finitely many genuinely fresh, unexposed, untied COMMON private-word slots. Repeated uses of one physical slot remain ONE kernel variable. For slot s, use its d_s=m_s-1 independent spectral moment coordinates x_(s,l), with exponents lambda_l=binom(l,2), 2<=l<=m_s. These coordinates determine the admitted coherent capped COMMON kernel. Any fixed linear/rational reconstruction into full kernels is composed into the predicate; this does not add observations.

Let x* be one supplied effectively real-algebraic, strictly positive tuple of these slot coordinates. Suppose each slot is in its ACTUAL source closure, coherently. Let E be an effective real number field containing x* and the coefficients below.

The complete projected joint target predicate T includes all supplied rows and one compatible static-core parameter assignment. We require a neighborhood U of x* and polynomials P_1,...,P_k over E, with k=1 or k=2, such that

    x* in T, P(x*)=0, and U intersect {P=0} is contained in T.

The equality germ may be obtained from one local semialgebraic branch, with all its strict inequalities holding throughout U. Every residual equality must be included: it is not enough that T is contained in {P=0}, nor may remaining equations be discarded after a rank calculation. The usual regular complete-intersection case satisfies this contract. The proof only needs the displayed local inclusion.

Assume T contains NO tuple of actual finite strict slot words. This negativity is an assumption for the necessary condition, not an algorithm already known to decide T. If the complete original fibre is negative, this holds for every admitted projected core branch of this form. Conversely, a positive tuple in this T supplies one compatible core and one legal original source, not separately fitted rows.

Fix a slot s for which the k-by-d_s Jacobian block

    J_s = (partial P_i / partial x_(s,l))(x*)

has row rank k. This is essential. In a joint problem, full rank of the entire Jacobian does NOT imply full rank in every slot.

Suppose the slot's coherent log-signature has, as part of an actual closure normal form,

    h_s = w R(r) + h_rest, w>0, 0<r<1,
    R(r)_l = 1+r+...+r^(lambda_l-1),

where h_rest is in the actual COMMON log closure. Neither w nor r is assumed algebraic. The same argument applies to EVERY positive residue in any such decomposition at this slot, with all other pieces frozen. The rest may include active/inactive drift or killing, finitely or countably many retained factors, and other residues. No supplied bound on retained factors is used.

## 4. Source-faithful two-variable correction lemma

Vary w and r in a compact small rectangle around their positive interior values, keeping h_rest fixed. The closure slot map is

    x_s(w,r) = exp[-h_rest - w R(r)]

coordinatewise. All other slots stay at their given closure values.

This finite-dimensional family has C1 approximations by ONE physical finite source per approximation index on the same parameter rectangle. Replace wR(r) by N equal fresh Bernoulli factors with p_N=w/[N(1-r)] and ratio r. The logarithmic signature N H(p_N,r) converges to wR(r) uniformly with its w and r derivatives on the rectangle. Here H(p,r)_l=-log(1-p+p r^lambda_l). The rectangle stays away from r=1 and w=0, so the usual logarithm Taylor remainder and its derivatives are uniform. For sufficiently large N, 0<p_N,r<1 throughout the rectangle.

Approximate h_rest by a fixed actual strict word, independent of w,r. Approximate the other frozen slots coherently by fixed actual strict words. Concatenate within the slot. Add or distribute a vanishing positive baseline if needed, as in the frozen physical replacement provider. Inactive drift/killing flags are not added as pivot columns. The complete joint moment maps converge in C1; so do their compositions with P.

If the derivative of P composed with this family has row rank k at (w,r), select k of these two variables and freeze any unused one. A compact-box contraction/regular-zero argument, exactly as in the coupled finite-pivot provider, corrects P to zero for sufficiently accurate physical approximants. The corrected tuple approaches x*, lies in U, and hence lies in T by the full local inclusion above. Each approximant is one physical tuple, with the same slot kernels reused across every row. This contradicts negativity.

Consequently the k-by-2 matrix of permitted residue derivatives has rank LESS than k. This conclusion does not require the exact normal-form parameters to be algebraic and does not invoke RCF on exponential equations.

## 5. One equality: an explicit finite algebraic multiple-root census

Put v_l = J_(s,1,l) x*_(s,l), and define

    Q(z) = sum_l v_l R_(lambda_l)(z) in E[z].

The derivative columns in Section 4 are -Q(r) and -w Q'(r). For k=1, negativity therefore forces BOTH

    Q(r)=0 and Q'(r)=0.

The polynomials R_(lambda_l) have distinct degrees lambda_l-1 and leading coefficient 1. They are linearly independent over E. The Jacobian row is nonzero and every moment x*_(s,l) is positive. Thus v is nonzero and Q is NOT the zero polynomial.

Every positive residue node at this slot belongs to

    {r in (0,1): gcd(Q,Q')(r)=0},

a finite effectively computable algebraic set. Algebraic root isolation computes it from P and x*, before knowing any residue node. A constant or squarefree Q yields the empty list, excluding any such residue presentation at a negative point on this branch.

Let L=lambda_(m_s). Then deg Q<=L-1. Every candidate actually arising as a residue is a multiple root. In characteristic zero its minimal polynomial over E divides Q with multiplicity at least two. Therefore

    [E(r):E] <= floor((L-1)/2).

The same quantity bounds the number of distinct possible residue nodes. At cap seven, L=21, giving a safe degree and distinct-node bound of 10. These are candidate-node bounds, not source-factor bounds or standalone NO certificates.

## 6. Two equations: a nonzero Wronskian gives a finite census

Define, for i=1,2,

    Q_i(z) = sum_l J_(s,i,l) x*_(s,l) R_(lambda_l)(z).

The rank-two Jacobian block, invertibility of diag(x*_s), and independence of the R-polynomials show that Q_1,Q_2 are linearly independent over E. The k-by-2 residue derivative matrix is

    - [ Q_1(r)   w Q_1'(r) ]
      [ Q_2(r)   w Q_2'(r) ].

Negativity forces its determinant to vanish. Since w>0, this is

    W(r)=0, where W=Q_1 Q_2' - Q_2 Q_1'.

W is NOT identically zero. If it were, (Q_2/Q_1)' would vanish in E(z); a rational function with zero derivative over a characteristic-zero field is constant. This would contradict independence. This is the classical Wronskian criterion, not an assumed genericity statement.

Thus every positive interior residue node at this slot lies among the finite effectively isolable roots of W in (0,1). With L=lambda_(m_s), a safe bound is

    deg W <= 2L-3, and [E(r):E] <= 2L-3.

At cap seven this gives 39. Leading-term cancellation may improve the actual bound; no sharp bound is claimed. The same W applies to all positive residues in the fixed slot presentation, since its coefficients depend only on the complete predicate and x*.

## 8. Sharp limits of the route and the remaining master obligation

1. Algebraic observed data do NOT automatically supply an algebraic tuple of hidden COMMON kernels in the complete observation fibre. This note assumes that tuple. Projection by RCF only eliminates the finite core; it does not algebraize unknown source-closure equations.
2. One or two equations are substantive. For k>=3 the two residue derivative columns can never have full row rank. Their rank deficiency is automatic, so it gives no nonzero univariate polynomial certificate. Adding an arbitrary multiplier produces equations that may have solutions for EVERY real r, including transcendental r. Polynomial sampling of that multiplier system does not preserve the original exponential endpoint equations.
3. Full joint Jacobian rank without full rank in the chosen slot is insufficient. Combining columns from independently chosen closure presentations or splitting a genuinely tied source is forbidden.
4. A singular equality such as P=Q^2 has zero Jacobian and cannot be used. One must have the actual regular local equality description and all remaining equality constraints. Merely finding one vanishing atom does not establish the local inclusion needed in Section 3.
5. The residue-free endpoint branch, INDEPENDENT ordered products with transported normals, exposed controls, genuinely shared parameter ties, and alternative original cores are untouched.
6. A finite root list constrains a promised negative presentation; it is not a certificate that every allowed presentation is negative, nor does it provide an input-only halting test.

A source-faithful general extension would have to either obtain a comparable nonzero algebraic critical elimination for arbitrary joint codimension and actual closure candidates, or supply another all-core terminating rejection/witness-bound mechanism. The existing calibrated-strata proof does not furnish that extension, and this note does not assert it.
