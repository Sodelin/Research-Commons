# Baseball pilot: does handedness adjustment improve recent-change signals?
Author: dot (OpenAI-assisted research), prepared for Nolan. 21 October 2026. Proposed experiment with executed real-data ingestion audit, NOT a validated new statistic.

## Decision and prior art
Test one narrow augmentation of an established projection, not a replacement for WAR, wOBA, xwOBA or existing platoon projections. Hypothesis: recent apparent improvement partly reflects a different mix of left/right-handed pitchers; removing that mixture change before using recent signals may improve forecasts. The falsifiable question is whether this adjustment adds value over an otherwise identical model already using raw recent changes. Success is not guaranteed: shrinkage and established systems may already absorb the information, and changing platoon deployment can itself be predictive.

MLB defines xwOBA from contact characteristics and actual noncontact outcomes; it is not a ready-made longitudinal forecast. Dan Szymborski's 'Yes, Hitter xStats Are Useful' (2023) already studies predictive blending of actual and expected statistics, including sample-size-dependent weights. FanGraphs' projection documentation describes history/age-based systems. Independent review identified the January 2025 Steamer release's context-neutral and platoon forecasts: those are an essential prior-art/comparator constraint. No claim that split adjustment, regression to the mean, component projection or expected batting statistics is novel. The proposed contribution is a measured incremental comparison, if it passes.

## Target, cohort and estimand
Primary target is realized cumulative batting runs above a frozen league reference over the next 60 CALENDAR days, not a supposedly context-free latent talent and not next 200 PA. At each cutoff t use regular-season MLB hitters with >=200 completed PA in the preceding 365 days and >=20 in preceding 60 days, based only on information before t. Eligibility selects established/recently active hitters; rookies and inactive hitters outside this rule are outside the claim. Keep all eligible hitters even if they subsequently get zero PA, are injured, demoted or retire.

For reproducibility, freeze one published pretraining season's event weights w_e, wOBA denominator indicators d_e, league mean mu and run scale s>0. Exact table and source must be archived BEFORE fitting; currently not acquired, so no numerical run target is claimed. Define W_i=sum of w_e across future completed PA and D_i=sum of d_e, and Y_i=(W_i-mu*D_i)/s. No future PA means W=D=Y=0; the rate W/D remains undefined, never .000. Explicit denominator mapping excludes intentional walks, sacrifice bunts and catcher interference and includes unintentional walks, HBP and sacrifice flies; map every remaining terminal event, checking a published formula and official totals. This is fixed-reference batting contribution, not current-year conventional wRAA, WAR, fantasy points, baserunning value or a context-neutral causal effect. Fixed weights make eras comparable at a declared reference, not perfectly park-adjusted.

Estimand: E[Y_i | information available before t] in that eligibility population under realized MLB deployment. Trades, opponents and opportunity are part of the forecast problem, not randomized treatments. Secondary report actual W/D only among D>0, with its survivor-selection limitation; do not rename it population talent. Future-200-PA analysis is optional descriptive sensitivity only and must report non-completers, never quietly omit them.

## Baseline and proposed feature
Baseline B0: ridge forecast of Y using age at cutoff, prior 365/730-day PA and production, recent 30/60/120-day PA, shrunk actual batting/event rates, shrunk contact-quality summaries, prior outcomes versus L/R pitchers, calendar anchor and prior team/park information if historically available. Include raw recent K%, unintentional BB% and hard-hit/contact rates and history counts. Training transformations and shrinkage strengths are fitted using only training folds. Include an exposure prediction Dhat from a separately trained count model, baseline rate forecast rhat, and their interaction as features, not as an identity for expected runs. Never assume E[D*r]=E[D]E[r]. If as-of xwOBA snapshots exist, use their weighted blend with actual outcomes; otherwise primary honest model uses a contact-outcome surrogate trained on earlier data only, and current retrospective xwOBA is a separately labelled oracle/revision-sensitive sensitivity, not as-of validation.

For each component c in {strikeout per completed PA, unintentional BB per completed PA, hard-hit per measured contact}, estimate q_c,h,recent and q_c,h,older for pitcher hand h in {L,R}, with recent=last60 days and older=days61–365. Shrink each count to a training-only prior indexed by batter stance and pitcher hand: q=(successes+lambda_c*prior)/(denominator+lambda_c). Hard hit is measured exit velocity >=95mph; missing velocity is missing, not a failed hard hit. Carry measured/total contact coverage and denominator counts. Use a common pitcher-hand mixture pi_h fixed from training, not each player's future exposure. Delta_c^std=sum_h pi_h(q_c,h,recent-q_c,h,older). The matched raw feature uses identical windows and shrinkage but observed mixture instead.

Compare Braw=B0+three Dhat*Delta_raw terms against Bstd=B0+three Dhat*Delta_std terms with identical tuning budget and ridge family. Fit coefficients jointly with baseline features; identical count-model predictions isolate the rate-feature comparison. Also compare Bboth including both triplets and an ablation removing all change terms. No arbitrary hand-tuned bonus points, no special positive sign imposed. Prior split levels and sample counts remain in every matched model, so improvement cannot simply be credited to introducing platoon information for the first time.

Standardization handles only measured pitcher-handedness mixture. It does NOT remove opposing pitcher quality, park, weather, defense, injury or managerial selection. Switch hitters use observed stance only for past events; sparse cells revert to priors. Flag absent cells and report sparse-platoon subgroup performance. There is no causal exchangeability claim. If future deployment changes the actual mix, adjusted recent signals may be worse; this is precisely why Braw is mandatory.

## Temporal evaluation and falsification
Proposed snapshots May1 and July1 of each season, using data strictly before cutoff; targets t through t+59 days. 2021–23 development, 2024 tuning, 2025 held-out retrospective evaluation; all target intervals from each training fold must end before the next fold begins. Build rolling-origin out-of-fold predictions for stacking/exposure features. Do not random-split pitches or player windows. Same players may recur across years, appropriate for forecasting known players; dependence handled by player-cluster bootstrap with whole-year sensitivity. Only five seasons and two anchors/year limit precision and generalization. 2025 has already happened: a frozen analysis can be an untouched retrospective holdout, not prospective validation. No one has run this holdout here.

Primary score paired difference in player-window MAE of Y; secondary RMSE, bias, calibration intercept/slope, exposure-model calibration and error by prior PA, platoon imbalance and zero-exposure group. Report season/anchor breakdown and concentration among a few stars. Optional prediction intervals use held-out calibration and must report coverage/width. Predeclare useful improvement as >=1% reduction in held-out MAE vs strongest matched baseline, paired player-cluster 95% bootstrap CI excluding zero, no >2% RMSE deterioration, and consistent direction at both anchors. These are proposed decision thresholds, not statistical laws. Given limited seasons, require another future-season replication before deployment or superiority claims. Failure, ambiguity, or baseline dominance is a useful negative result. Compare as-of Steamer/THE BAT X or other archived forecasts if available; without them cannot claim superiority to professional projections.

## Data and leakage guards
Needed: completed-PA outcomes, dates/game identifiers, batter/pitcher IDs, pitcher throwing hand, batter stance, contact exit velocity/angle, historical PA/exposure and age, frozen event weights, and historically valid forecast/contact-model snapshots. Statcast CSV docs identify fields, but a field's presence today does not certify availability at historical t. Use one terminal record per (game_pk,at_bat_number), exclude incomplete/truncated PA, detect duplicates and missing coverage, reconcile game/PA totals. Do not infer final PA from maximum pitch number without checking terminal status.

Whitelist predictors. Forbid days_until_next_game, post-event outcomes used before their timestamps, future roster status, future denominator, final-season age/reference summaries if not derivable at t, and contemporaneously unavailable model revisions. Do not use current full-season sprint-speed or xwOBA reconstruction as historical information without a snapshot. 2026 plate-location/zone definition changes and heterogeneous bat-tracking coverage are independently reviewed risks; neither feature family is needed in this candidate. No broad acquisition or installation has been performed.

## Executed bounded pilot and precise missing step
One public Statcast CSV request for April1,2025 downloaded 2,530,103 bytes. The standard-library audit executed successfully: 3,704 unique pitch records,13 games,262 batters,932 nonempty event rows including1 truncated PA,931 completed terminal PA,612 contact records,3 contact rows missing exit velocity and6 missing expected wOBA. Both future-next-game fields were present and excluded. 924 terminal records have both wOBA fields, with denominator sum921, underscoring that neither raw pitch count nor completed PA count is the wOBA denominator. This audit is NOT a player ranking, fitted model, proof of forecast improvement or complete league coverage certification. Exact source hash and counts are in one-day-audit.json; audit_one_day.py is included. Raw data and derived event-level rows are not redistributed.

Next executable step: acquire a bounded, predeclared two-anchor development cohort with full pre-cutoff histories and 60-day outcomes, freeze/reference weights and historical feature provenance, then fit matched raw-versus-standardized models. That multi-season panel, verified historical snapshots and sufficient fitting/evaluation have NOT been supplied. Do not extrapolate from a one-day schema audit to performance. Independent methodological review is included separately.
