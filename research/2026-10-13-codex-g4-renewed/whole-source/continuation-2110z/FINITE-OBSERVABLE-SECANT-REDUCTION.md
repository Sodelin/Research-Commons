Contributor/publisher: Codex lead G4 researcher, 13 October 2026.

## 1. Algebraic statement

Let z be a finite list of real coordinates, let z_* be fixed, and let
(P_o(z))_(o in O) be any family of real polynomials. The index o labels
actual permitted response coordinates; it need not be bounded. Write
f_o(z)=P_o(z)-P_o(z_*). There are finitely many ORIGINAL indices
 o_1,...,o_d such that, for every o,

    f_o(z)=sum_(i=1)^d h_oi(z) f_oi(z)                 (1)

with polynomial h_oi. Indeed the ideal generated by all f_o is finitely
generated. Express a finite ideal-generating family using the original
f_o and take the union of the finitely many indices used. That union of
original coordinates itself generates the ideal. This argument is
existential; stopping a computed ideal chain at an observed plateau is
not justified by it.

Let z_j tend to z_*, let S_j>0 tend to zero, and suppose
|f_oi(z_j)|<=C_i S_j for these finitely many indices. After a subsequence,

    f_oi(z_j)/S_j -> a_i.

Divide (1) by S_j and use continuity of h_oi. For EVERY original index o,

    f_o(z_j)/S_j -> G_o(a):=sum_i h_oi(z_*) a_i.       (2)

The same subsequence works for all o: no new subsequence is selected in
(2). Thus every normalized all-response change lies in one finite-dimensional
linear image of the vector a. There is no premise that (z_j-z_*)/S_j is
bounded. In particular this argument preserves a fair-coin displacement of
order sqrt(S_j), or a higher-order boundary arc, instead of discarding it
as an unbounded tangent. It asserts neither that every vector a is physically
realizable nor that the h_oi or a are unique under a different generator
choice.

## 4. The remaining obstruction is a constrained cone separation

The inherited uniform first-insertion expansion for weak equal cells is
a source-kernel statement. Postcomposition with arbitrary stochastic
original kernels preserves its remainder estimate. Normalizing extras by
w/S_j and passing on compact slot/position/duration data gives a positive
probability measure nu. At each fixed row, its insertion term has limit

    I_o(nu)=integral V_o(slot,position,t) dnu.

At t>0 the local kernel is partial_p B(t,0)/(exp(t)-1), inserted in place
of the corresponding ordinary interval; at t=0 its continuous extension
is -2Q in that private population. Actual fixed-body prefix and suffix
kernels supply V_o. This is an analysis of genuine row probabilities,
not a permitted query of V_o or a latent cell. Uniform single-cell
remainders vanish because maximum extra strength tends to zero; total
cross terms are O_m(S_j^2). Finite body factors are stochastic also at
boundary parameters, so this expansion does not need a parameter derivative.

Exact eventual matching implies, on every original row,

    G_o(a)+I_o(nu)=0,   a in C_(Gamma,z_*), nu>=0,
                       nu(total)=1.                 (5)

To finish this local route one must rule out a pair (a,nu) in (5) with
its ACTUAL shared-source constraints, for every possible limit body.
The finite-dimensional representation (2) does not do that. Nor does
pointwise all-law commonization imply a sign or differentiability at the
fair and endpoint strata. The separate
[generator checkpoint](ORIGINAL-LOCAL-CONE-GENERATOR-CHECKPOINT.md)
exhibits precisely why raw first-order parameter arguments can fail.
