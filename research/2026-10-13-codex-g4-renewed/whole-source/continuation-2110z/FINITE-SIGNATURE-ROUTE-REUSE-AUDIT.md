## 1. The path theorem has the right quantifier in its positive-axis case

The October 9 [axis-path proof](../../../2026-10-09-dot-reviewed-restart-1410z/g4-signatures/MONOTONE-AXIS-SIGNATURE-FORCING-ATTEMPT.md)
and its [scoped review](../../../2026-10-09-dot-reviewed-restart-1410z/g4-signatures/AXIS-SIGNATURE-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md)
already establish the useful fact: a reduced positive axis path with L segments
is determined, against every longer finite positive axis path, by signature
levels through L+1. Vanishing adjacent-distinct word coefficients of length L+1
exclude longer rivals by positivity; the length-L axis word and its one-letter
duplications recover the ordered segment lengths. This is exactly an
unknown-length finite-forcing lemma, rather than merely reconstruction with a
supplied length bound.

The supplier's primary audit of Lyons–Xu, *Hyperbolic development and inversion
of signature*, arXiv:1507.00286v3, Section 2, supports the reconstruction formulas.
The corresponding general piecewise-linear reconstruction uses all-order
asymptotics. Hambly–Lyons, *Uniqueness for the signature of a path of bounded
variation and the reduced path group*, Theorem 4 and Corollary 1.7, concerns the
full signature. Neither supplies finite observation decoding for our source.
These are inherited primary-source audits, not a new primary-paper read here.

## 2. The positive source adapter was already ruled out more generally

The [nilpotent-signature companion](../../../2026-10-09-dot-reviewed-restart-1410z/g4-signatures/NILPOTENT-SIGNATURE-ENCODING-COMPANION.md)
and its [final scoped review](../../../2026-10-09-dot-reviewed-restart-1410z/g4-signatures/NILPOTENT-SIGNATURE-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md)
prove the stronger obstruction. Suppose each actual strict private INDEPENDENT
word W is assigned a continuous coordinatewise nondecreasing bounded-variation
path in one fixed finite-dimensional space, with:

1. signature multiplicative under actual serial composition;
2. each finite signature level determined by some finite capped actual source
   endpoint, with the cap allowed to depend on the level.

Then the entire signatures of WV and VW agree for every pair of actual words.
They cannot be faithful to the full source response, since actual strict words
with different chronological placement have different all-copy laws. The
decoders need not be continuous, effective or algebraic for this obstruction.

The proof uses the accepted actual-source open patch and additive theorem.
Monotonicity bounds truncated signatures by their additive first-level
variation. The induced locally bounded homomorphism into each finite nilpotent
signature group kills the source merger directions, whose ordinary adjoint
weights are nonzero. What survives is abelian diagonal information.

Consequently replacing axis paths by arbitrary monotone BV paths, raising the
observed copy cap with signature degree, or applying nonlinear postprocessing
does not repair this global compositional adapter. This is a theorem about the
adapter, not about all possible target-relative source certificates.

## 3. The exact routing reset cannot be silently represented by path flow

The reviewed October 8 [source-lift packet](../../../2026-10-08-dot-g4-positive-composition-attempt-1702z/POSITIVE-COMPOSITION-CUBATURE-AND-SOURCE-LIFT-FAILURE.md)
gives, with column vectors and rightmost-first action,

    B(a,b,g) = R exp(a Q0 + b Q1) S_g,
    R S_g = I,       P_g = S_g R,       P_g^2 = P_g.

Here S_g freshly routes each current opaque root, Q0 and Q1 coalesce within its
two colours, and R forgets colour. Already for one root P_g has rank one on
the two-colour space. It is singular, whereas every finite product of finite
matrix exponentials on that space is invertible. Two successive physical cells
have the literal factorization

    B2 B1 = R exp(G2) P_(g2) exp(G1) S_(g1).

Dropping the middle reset retains old colours and changes the source. Keeping
discrete resets is legitimate, but then a theorem for an ordinary path-flow
signature does not identify those chronological gates without an additional
representation and legal observer theorem. This singularity argument does not
rule out every enlarged hybrid representation.

## 4. Positive occupation moments have the same missing observer premise

The elementary moment principle is sound: a positive measure with
`integral p^2 dmu = 0` is supported on the zero set of p. It can force finite
support when the target zero set and required moments have the appropriate
form. What is missing is a legal finite source observation of those moments,
plus an implication from that support to the full chronological response.

This is not merely an untested concern. The reviewed October 8
[occupation-square attempt](../../../2026-10-08-dot-g4-whole-proof-attempt-a3-1345z/ATTEMPT-A3-DIAGONAL-OCCUPATION-SQUARE-NO-DESCENT.md)
uses the actual chronological increments `a=-log b2`, `b=-log b3`, `d=b-3a`.
Neither `J=integral d da` nor the positive square `I=integral d^2 da` is a
function of any finite capped full source endpoint. The concatenation identity

    I(WV) = I(W) + I(V) + 2 d(W) J(V) + d(W)^2 a(V)

would otherwise recover the forbidden Heisenberg area J by choosing one actual
W with d(W) nonzero. Thus even observing only this positive certificate,
instead of the whole signature, fails. The failure does not fix one common
target across caps and therefore is not a negative solution of G4.

## 5. Exact surviving gate

A genuinely different signature-based solution must supply a target-relative
or noncompositional observable construction outside these hypotheses, or a
signed/discrete-gate carrier with its own positive finite equality criterion.
It must prove that finitely many actual topology rows determine the required
coefficients for every admitted finite rival and that coefficient equality
forces the whole original law. Full-signature uniqueness, hidden route
occupation, external cubature mixtures and a supplied path-length bound do
not supply that implication.

The [October 10 reuse audit, Section 4](../../../2026-10-10-dot-g4-source-uniform-primitive-checkpoint-1258z/G4-PRIOR-COMPOSITION-CHECKPOINT-1420.md)
already records this boundary. No additional promising source adapter was found
in this targeted recheck. The finite INDEPENDENT bridge remains unresolved.
