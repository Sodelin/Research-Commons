## 1. Claimed theorem and exact source scope

Let T,N be finite strict sources in the original natural cut-child
contract: binary rooted-LSA outer-labelled sources, positive finite
population exposures, interior independent natural site coins, and
ordinary infinite ancestral completion. The original taxon set is fixed.
Each source uses one physical parameter tuple for all copy allocations.
Suppose their ENTIRE natural INDEPENDENT rooted labelled completed
topology laws agree, for every finite allocation of original taxon
copies. Then their ENTIRE natural COMMON laws agree as well.

All port laws below are computed functionals of those original laws.
No latent state, independently queried port, new taxon, physical
intervention, rival-size bound or independently fitted parameter tuple
is added. Padding and pruning retain the original legal sampling menu.
The result concerns natural routing only, not arbitrary correlated
registers, controlled menus or weaker observation channels.

The essential new source step is recovery of every ancestral hybrid's
child cut, including serial hybrids with the same descendant set. This
aligns finite ordinary regions across the two sources. The previously
reviewed one-orbit observer then recovers each region despite its
unknown upper reticulate continuation.

## 3. Recover every successive hybrid cut on one taxon's ancestry

Fix A with hybrid ancestry h_1,...,h_L in backward order. Write D_j for
the taxon descendant set of h_j's child bridge. Thus

              A in D_1 subseteq ... subseteq D_L,

and all D_j are proper subsets of the original taxa. Equal successive
sets are permitted and are not collapsed.

The first-cut theorem recovers D_1. Its preceding ordinary A path is
private relative to every taxon outside D_1. Canceling this known path
recovers the entire h_1-first joint law J_1: arbitrary many A roots at
the child side of h_1, together with any finite panel of taxa outside
D_1. The word parameters and L come from the completed single-A normal
form.

Suppose D_j and J_j have been recovered, with j<L. Extract the two actual
first routing cohorts of h_j while retaining the outside-D_j panel.
Let H_0,H_1 be their oriented conditional laws when the coin g_j is
biased. At fairness only their independent-orientation average is
needed. In either case the entire table

                         M_j=g_j H_0+(1-g_j)H_1             (1)

is determined. It routes all selected A roots down one chosen arm of
h_j and leaves all older sites INDEPENDENT. It is a proof computation,
not a physical forced setting. Natural projectivity gives its actual
unconditional conditional-routing meaning; there is no conditioning on
a later tree shape or clade-survival event.

Let t_0,t_1 be the full two arm exposures of h_j PLUS the ordinary
connector from their join to h_(j+1). They are positive finite values
known from the single-A normal form. For B outside D_j, let p_n^j(A;B)
be the prescribed B-spine comb probability on n A roots and one B in
the recovered table M_j. Define

 q_n^j=g_j exp(-lambda_n t_0)+(1-g_j)exp(-lambda_n t_1),
                    lambda_n=binom(n,2).                    (2)

**Successive-cut lemma.**

 D_(j+1)=D_j union {B outside D_j:
                           p_n^j(A;B)>q_n^j for some n}.     (3)

If B is outside D_(j+1), both selected parent paths up to h_(j+1) are
A-private. The comb forbids every A-only merger along them, so its
probability is at most their mixture no-merger probability (2).

If B belongs to D_(j+1) but not D_j, choose an arm attaining
t_min=min(t_0,t_1). On that arm the A path to the next hybrid is ordinary.
There exists a positive-probability B routing path meeting it below
h_(j+1), at latest on the mandatory positive child bridge of h_(j+1).
Its B-only routing weight w>0 is independent of n. Before contact it
carries one B root; after contact the selected common path has no hybrid
before h_(j+1). Let b<t_min be the A-exclusive exposure and let
z=t_min-b>0 be the remaining common ordinary interval.

Require all A roots to survive b, then the entire prescribed comb to
complete within z. Exactly as in the first-cut proof this gives

 p_n^j(A;B)>=w' exp(-lambda_n b) kappa_(n+1)
                                     Pr(S_(n+1)<=z),         (4)

where w'>0 includes the chosen natural arm weight. The Kingman descent
probability is uniformly bounded below by Pr(S_infinity<=z)>0, and
log kappa_(n+1)=-O(n log n). Since q_n^j<=exp(-lambda_n t_min), the
ratio in (4) to q_n^j tends to infinity. This proves (3).

Now restrict M_j to panels outside the newly known D_(j+1). Both arm
paths and their connector are private relative to those panels. Thus
M_j is the actual composition

              [g_j E_(t_0)+(1-g_j)E_(t_1)] J_(j+1).          (5)

The known ordinary mixture has strictly positive singleton diagonal
q_n^j at every cap. Completed-row triangular inversion, with the entire
outside panel retained in every term, recovers J_(j+1). This closes the
induction without ever choosing an orientation at a fair mixed arm.

If D_(j+1)=D_j, every outside taxon fails the membership test; the next
hybrid is still retained because L and its index are already supplied
by the single-A normal form. No nonmembership finite certificate is
claimed. No final invisible ordinary tail is used in (2).

## 4. Align the entire hierarchy of physical hybrid cuts

Apply Section 3 to every original taxon. Every physical hybrid occurs
on at least one such ancestry. It occurs on the ancestry of EVERY taxon
in its child-cut descendant set, since that cut is mandatory.

For a fixed descendant set D, all hybrids having that set lie in one
serial chain. Label them by (D,r), where r counts from the youngest to
the oldest among that set. The ordered D occurrences recovered on any
taxon in D give the same list. This canonically aligns the hybrid sites
across two sources with the same entire INDEPENDENT law, even when
several sites share D and even when their numerical parameters coincide.

Adjoin a virtual top entry with descendant set all original taxa. The
parent of a hybrid is its nearest older hybrid, or this virtual top if
none exists. Inclusion and the serial order for repeated equal sets
determine this parent relation. Immediate younger children of a parent
have disjoint descendant sets. The only equal-set case is one serial
child whose set already covers the parent's whole descendant set.

For a parent h, retain the region below its child-side entry and above
its immediate younger hybrid children h_1,...,h_r. Open each child h_i
into its two incoming parent ports and omit everything below its child
cut. Also retain every ordinary taxon exit in D_h not below any child
hybrid. No hybrid remains in this region: any such hybrid would be
between h and one of its purported immediate children, or would itself
be an additional child. A finite reticulation-free directed region is
an ordinary rooted population tree after unary suppression.

This groups regions by nearest older HYBRID, not by an assumed observed
blob decomposition. A region may combine several ordinary-connected
blobs. That causes no problem: after all of its boundary hybrids have
been opened, the whole region is ordinary. The original cut-child
property supplies its common upper cut entry.

## 5. Recover one jointly symmetrized region table from legal laws

For each child h_i choose one representative A_i in D_i. Use arbitrary
copies of these representatives, arbitrary copies of the ordinary
taxon exits, and an arbitrary fixed finite panel outside D_h. Other
taxa below the child cuts are omitted by projective marginalization.

The completed A_i normal form determines its entire truncated ancestry
forest kernel W_i from A_i up to the child side of h_i, excluding h_i's
own routing. It includes all younger hybrids and all ordinary gaps,
with its last gap visible because h_i follows it. Since all other
retained inputs lie outside D_i, this entire lower word is genuinely
private relative to them. Distinct W_i use disjoint child-cut subtrees
and independent natural drivers at the fixed physical tuple.

Their positive singleton diagonals allow successive completed-row
triangular cancellation in each representative's root count. This
recovers the genuine joint table with arbitrary roots presented at all
h_i child ports simultaneously, while retaining ordinary exits and
the outside panel. No product of separately fitted marginal laws is
substituted for that table.

In its countably infinite A_i restriction, each first-cohort extractor
equals the actual h_i Bernoulli partition almost surely. The intersection
of these probability-one events over the finitely many children still
has probability one. Choose finitely many roots from every cohort and
retain all other input panels. Conditional projectivity gives the actual
joint opened-port law: every selected root is sent to its chosen incoming
parent, with all upper interactions and dependencies intact. At biased
sites unequal masses orient the two ports; at each fair site use an
independent auxiliary orientation bit, independent also across sites.

Consequently the recovered table is EXACTLY

                  H_sym=|G|^(-1) sum_(g in G) (gR) U,        (6)

where R is the one actual opened ordinary region, G is the product of
its fair port swaps, and U is one actual shared upper continuation.
There are not independently chosen ordinary trees or independently
chosen upper laws in the summands. This supplies the joint orbit-table
premise that was left open in the earlier conditional observer.

Put the root of R at the LCA of all its opened ports and ordinary exits.
For an inner region the remaining common ordinary stem to h includes
h's strictly positive child edge. Thus U=E_s V with s>0. For the top
region, ordinary Q is the upper completion, possibly preceded by an
ordinary stem which is harmless only there. These are precisely the
positive-entry/Q alternatives in the orbit observer.

## 6. Recover the full region operator, including its common stem

When R has at least two inputs, the orbit observer determines its
stem-free ordinary operator orbit and its effective U, including the
joint outside panels. Its quadratic-rate argument uses all input ports
before the repeating comb tail; it permits at most d-2 mergers below
their LCA. It therefore applies to (6) without knowing an ordinary
ancestral completion inside U.

For an inner region, the first operation in the recovered one-port
completed law U is the positive ordinary stem s followed by the parent
hybrid h. Apply the inherited completed single-taxon normal form to U
to recover s. This is an inference from the recovered completed law,
not a new physical taxon or a hidden forest observation. All roots at
this interface have the actual source's exchangeable natural rule.

There is also a direct consistency formula using already known original
ancestry parameters. If A is an ordinary exit, subtract its reconstructed
stem-free root-to-A length from its known first-hybrid ordinary gap.
If h_i is a child hybrid, subtract the reconstructed root-to-LCA of its
two opened ports from the known ordinary connector between the join of
h_i's arms and h. Both yield s. The pair-LCA coordinate is invariant
under swapping those two fair ports. These formulas retain any stem;
they do not use the false identity E_sU=U.

If the region has only one input, it is a single ordinary path to h:
there are no child hybrids, its descendant set is one taxon, and the
single-taxon normal form directly gives that path length. A child hybrid
would already contribute two opened ports, so no other one-input case
is missed. At the virtual top, any final ordinary stem is immaterial
under Q; the rooted-LSA contract also prevents a proper hybrid child
cut from containing every original taxon.

Thus every finite ordinary region is recovered up to independent fair
port swaps, together with its full common stem whenever that stem affects
later routing. Each child site's coin and placement are aligned by the
recovered cut hierarchy; biased coins orient their ports, while fair
flips are passive relabellings.
