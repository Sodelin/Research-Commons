**Exact equivalence.** The following assertions about this fixed T are
equivalent:

(A) There is a finite legal INDEPENDENT certificate forcing I(T) against
    every admitted finite rival.

(B) There is a finite legal INDEPENDENT row set J_T such that

    I(N)|J_T = I(T)|J_T  implies  C(N)|C_T = C(T)|C_T

    for every admitted finite rival N.

Proof A implies B. Take J_T to be the certificate in A. It forces the
entire I law. Supplier 3 gives the entire C law, hence its C_T coordinates.

Proof B implies A. Observe the finite INDEPENDENT rows J_T union B_I.
Agreement on J_T gives the C_T tuple, and supplier 1 gives the entire
COMMON law, in particular agreement on B_C. Agreement on B_I together
with B_C invokes supplier 2 and forces the entire natural BOTH law,
including I. This uses the same rival N and the same tuple throughout.
No size bound is imposed. QED.

The finite C_T tuple is therefore one precise sufficient and necessary
target invariant for this route. It consists of legal COMMON observation
coordinates, but those coordinates are NOT queried in an INDEPENDENT-only
experiment. Condition B still demands an exact implication from finitely
many legal INDEPENDENT observations. Computing C_T from the ENTIRE I law,
or calculating its target value from a known T, does not prove B.

The tuple may be selected from the already accepted COMMON provider;
rebuilding a static routing kernel or ignoring chronology is unnecessary.
The BOTH certificate supplies the remaining chronology only AFTER B has
been proved. Its existence is not evidence for the missing implication.
