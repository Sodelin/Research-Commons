# Primary prior check against the remaining INDEPENDENT obligation

Contributor: Codex lead G4 researcher, 14 October 2026.
This is an applicability check, not a historical novelty survey or an
independent proof audit of the cited literature.

The exact missing obligation is a finite legal observation certificate
for one fixed strict original target against all finite unknown-size
rivals in INDEPENDENT mode, or a fixed-target exact all-prefix negative
family. A generic reconstruction result within a restricted class does
not supply it. The natural BOTH semantic theorem remains separately
labelled hand mathematics.

A focused alphaXiv discovery search was followed by direct raw primary
PDF-page retrieval for these two papers. The following statements are
paraphrases of the specified results, not reliance on generated summaries.

| Primary source read | Actual result and applicability boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Allman, Ane, Banos and Rhodes, [Beyond level-1: Identifiability of a class of galled tree-child networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21116), v1, Definition 8, Section 5.3, Theorem 5.7 and discussion, pp. 15–20 | It distinguishes NMSC independent and common inheritance. Theorem 5.7 identifies semidirected topology and internal tree-edge lengths in its C4 class from quartet concordance factors with two samples per taxon and generic parameters; a related C5 result uses one sample. The class may have arbitrary level, so calling this merely a bounded-level theorem would be inaccurate. Its topological restrictions, genericity and partial parameter conclusion still do not give the required all-strict-target forcing of the entire rooted law against arbitrary admitted rivals with private bigons. |
| Holtgrefe, Allman, Banos, van Iersel, Moulton, Rhodes and Wicke, [Distinguishing Phylogenetic Level-2 Networks with Quartets and Inter-Taxon Quartet Distances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17308), v1, Proposition 5.3, Theorem 5.4 and discussion, pp. 22–24 | Theorem 5.4 compares semidirected outer-labelled planar level-2 galled networks by displayed quartets and their canonical forms; its distance assertion further uses bloblets. The discussion allows some unrestricted small blobs, so the domain is not summarized as an absolute graph-size bound. Nevertheless canonical displayed-quartet identification is not equality of the entire original rooted coalescent response, nor an unrestricted finite-prefix forcing theorem. |

The first paper is particularly relevant because it includes independent
inheritance, multiple samples and networks of arbitrary level. Its actual
Theorem 5.7 hypotheses still do not establish finite exact COMMON recovery
from INDEPENDENT observations, a positive functional controlling arbitrary
weak private arrays, or an all-cap exact positive return construction.
The second paper provides useful combinatorial source information but no
such missing analytic implication either.

Neither result is used as a new supplier in the natural BOTH proof or the
INDEPENDENT handoffs. This finding does not assert that no relevant prior
theorem exists. Earlier primary checks of the GKP route and newer
quartet/quintet and substitution-model literature remain separately
recorded in the project. Correctness of our hand arguments, applicability
of a prior theorem, historical novelty and formal verification remain
different questions.
