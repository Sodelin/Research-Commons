## Proof using directed differences, not a displayed root height

descendant set D_h is proper by the original root-LSA condition; choose
an original taxon B outside D_h. B need not be an anchor for every other
hybrid. It only needs to lie outside THIS child cut.

The inherited single-A ancestry reduction turns h into two ordinary
arms, rejoining at a common older port before the next A-ancestral hybrid.
Denote their full exposures by t_0,t_1. Fix all COMMON bits except h's bit,
and compare the two resulting conditional ordinary displayed trees.
B's physical route is unchanged by this flip, since its ancestry does
not enter the h child cut. Above the rejoining port the A route is also
the same in both resolutions. Therefore the difference between the total
A-path exposures to any fixed common upper port is t_1-t_0; the total
B-path exposure to that port has no change.

In an ordinary conditional tree, let L_AB be the exposure on the A path
to its first meeting with B, and L_BA the corresponding B exposure.
If d_A,d_B are the path exposures to any common upper port, cancellation
of their shared upper segment gives

    L_AB-L_BA=d_A-d_B.                              (1)

The port in this calculation is a proof variable. It need not be the
displayed retained root, and no root stem is measured. Suppression or
pruning of an unused displayed root stem cancels from (1).

Subtract (1) for the two h choices, while keeping all other bits fixed:

    (L_AB-L_BA)_(sigma,h=1)
       -(L_AB-L_BA)_(sigma,h=0)=t_1-t_0.             (2)

fixed entire ordinary profiles pi(sigma,h=1) and pi(sigma,h=0). Hence
their difference is exactly constant. This proves the assertion for h.
Repeat over the finitely many retained hybrids and taxon ancestries.
