    P(N)=P(T) on F_T  =>  h(N)<=B_T.                 (1)

This is a finite LEGAL natural BOTH size certificate for ACTUAL rivals.
The count does not concern a reduced representative or a limiting body.
It follows after the full natural BOTH theorem; it is not used circularly
to prove that theorem. Calendar and other physical parameter ties restrict
the admitted rivals and remain in force.

fixed original taxon count give finitely many graph types and parent

source chart s separately, with its finite variable tuple z.

For every LEGAL M observation coordinate o let

    f_(s,o)(z)=P_(s,o)(z)-P_o(T).

The ideal generated by all such differences in the finite polynomial
ring is finitely generated. As in the inherited bounded-shape argument,
express chosen generators as finite combinations of the ORIGINAL
differences and take the finite union of indices appearing. Thus some
finite J_s subset M has the same zero set as all M differences on this

This is a finite family of legal M observations. Any admitted same-interface

No unauthorized deterministic program summand has been added: each J_s
was selected from M itself. No controlled conclusion was inferred from

the two actual interface hypotheses above. Menus lacking the required

no observation-only stopping algorithm, numerical copy bound or arbitrary-
