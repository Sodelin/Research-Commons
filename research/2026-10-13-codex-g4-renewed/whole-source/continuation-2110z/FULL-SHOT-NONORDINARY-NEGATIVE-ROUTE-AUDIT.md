# Full-shot negative route: nonordinary targets and the exact topology fibre

Contributor: Codex whole-source lane, 14 October 2026. **Source-level
attempt and precise failure point; no negative G4 theorem.** This follows
the lead's request to challenge the ordinary full-forest return reduction
rather than repeat it as a necessary condition.

## 1. A nonordinary target is a genuinely broader centering choice

The negative master does not require ordinary returns. It would suffice
to exhibit ONE finite strict nonordinary private target T such that,
for every m, some finite strict W_m with different entire law satisfies

                         K_m(W_m)=K_m(T).                    (1)

The target, eligible original bridge and exterior remain fixed. Each
rival uses one physical tuple at all arities. The number and parameters
of its cells may depend on m.

There is no source-valid uniform reduction of (1) to ordinary returns
by appending a fixed actual complement to T. If finite strict V and an
ordinary E_H satisfied TV=E_H at every arity, the inherited nonnegative
integer bigon count would give L(T)+L(V)=0. This is impossible for a
nonordinary T. Formal group inversion of T, or a cap-dependent positive
complement with an uncontrolled clock cost, does not repair that fixed
ordinary-target reduction. This observation does not prove that (1)
exists; it prevents treating the ordinary-return route as necessary.

## 2. Topology projection does not weaken the same-exterior duty

Let C[-] be one fixed strict original exterior with an eligible private
proper-bridge slot. The reviewed
[fixed-exterior observability theorem](../FIXED-EXTERIOR-OBSERVABILITY.md)
gives, for every m, a finite list O_m of original labelled topology
events such that

      P_(C[W])(O_m)=P_(C[T])(O_m)  iff  K_m(W)=K_m(T).        (2)

The same exterior has the same positive triangular decoder coefficients
on both sides, including its actual routing and coalescent histories.
The events use only original taxa; one may sample m copies of a taxon
below the slot and one of the others, then prune to the outside anchor.

Conversely, every finite original experiment prefix has a finite maximum
slot root count. Equality of its complete kernel through that count
preserves the prefix by the inherited opaque-subtree compiler.

Thus within this fixed-exterior replacement class the two systems of
finite equality requirements are COFINAL. Exact original-topology rivals
after every finite prefix exist if and only if exact capped-kernel rivals
exist after every cap, with later inequivalence preserved by the same
observer. Requiring forest equality is not an unnecessary strengthening
of the all-prefix quantifier for this subclass, even though a PARTICULAR
small topology prefix may have a larger fibre.

This does not hold automatically when the rival also changes the exterior
or core. In that case its triangular coefficients change too. An exact
negative construction exploiting this freedom would have to solve the
coupled original topology equations directly, with one fixed target and
one actual shared tuple per rival. No such source construction was found
in this attempt.

## 4. The new physical-repair attempt and its failure

The specific attempted repair was to use a strict reticulate body as a
reservoir of signed response directions. The accepted full source
generator identity

 Q B = B_(t_x)/g + B_(t_y)/(1-g) + g(1-g) B_(gg)/2

relates ordinary-prefix motion, both arm derivatives and inheritance
curvature at EVERY full forest cap. At a fair equal-arm body it produces
the known first-order null direction after ordinary-clock retuning.
This is more freedom than at an ordinary target and is a legitimate
reason to test a nonordinary center.

It does not give a positive chronological correction word. The attempted
integration through an associated Wright--Fisher parameter diffusion
would, even if justified with suitable stopping and domain control, give
an EXTERNAL mixture of differently parameterized cells. The source admits
one deterministic physical parameter tuple, not a random tuple shared
across its entire experiment. Finite positive quadrature retains that
external-mixture defect. Refreshing independent current-root routing
between serial cells is not a physical implementation of the shared
parameter mixture.

Likewise, the generator identity controls the first projected directions
in (3), but does not show that the complete projected higher residual
has an actual positive zero. The already accepted ambient derivative
surjectivity and signed-time saturation leave precisely this membership
gap. No valid integration of the fair null direction into a source path
with exact arbitrary-cap equality was obtained. An all-arity finite
analytic family with extra cells would in any event contradict the
inherited entire-law normal form; the allowed construction must genuinely
depend on the requested cap.

## 5. Exact remaining shot

The best unresolved negative formulation retained here is (3) for its
ONE fixed nonordinary target, with finite actual positive U_m,V_m of
unbounded permitted length. Prove an actual zero for every m, preserving
strict margins of the retuned body and adding at least one genuine cell;
or construct a changing-exterior family directly on the complete finite
original topology fibre. Neither has been achieved here.

The result of this attempt is therefore a precise failure point, not a
new bounded-cap success or a claim that all negative routes fail. It
removes two misleading shortcuts: ordinary returns are not necessary,
while topology projection alone is not a weaker all-prefix problem in
the fixed-exterior subclass. The target-centred positive membership
obligation remains concrete and unresolved.
