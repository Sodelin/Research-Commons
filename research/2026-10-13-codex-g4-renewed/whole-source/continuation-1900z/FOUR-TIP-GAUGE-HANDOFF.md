## Exact count-mixture formulation

For a fixed four-tip opening and allocation n,m, write r for the number
of A roots routed to A0 and s for the number of B roots routed to B0.
Let x_A0,x_A1,x_B0,x_B1 be their pendant survivals, g,h their routing
coins, and zeta the internal-edge survival tuple. Define K_(n,m;r,s)(tau;
zeta) by averaging uniformly over the choices of WHICH labelled roots
occupy the prescribed port counts, then running only the internal ordinary
tree and ancestral completion. This definition is a mathematical finite
compiler, not an available observation.

The exact equation is

    V_(n,m)(tau) = sum_(r=0)^n sum_(s=0)^m W_(n,r) Z_(m,s)
                                      K_(n,m;r,s)(tau;zeta),

    W_(n,r)=binom(n,r) g^r(1-g)^(n-r)
                    x_A0^lambda_r x_A1^lambda_(n-r),
    Z_(m,s)=binom(m,s) h^s(1-h)^(m-s)
                    x_B0^lambda_s x_B1^lambda_(m-s).

All singleton entrance roots and the same original tuple are retained.
The natural route-count weights are binomial because choices are per
current lineage and, on an Omega outcome, no younger same-colour merger
has occurred. The common values g,h are shared across all allocations.

The finite target COMMON support additionally constrains zeta and the
within-colour arm ratios to finite observed atom-ratio possibilities, when
the complete corresponding source route configurations are matched to
their support atoms. For instance in the balanced case, holding all

All four belong to the recovered finite joint directed-profile support;

## Why raw count-column inversion fails even in a strict balanced core

Let Q_(n+m)(tau) be the infinite ordinary completion probability. In the
balanced core, the following four columns on Omega are proportional:

    K_(n,m;n,m) = Q_(n+m),
    K_(n,m;0,0) = Q_(n+m),
    K_(n,m;n,0) = z0^lambda_n z1^lambda_m Q_(n+m),
    K_(n,m;0,m) = z1^lambda_n z0^lambda_m Q_(n+m).

For the first two, every selected root enters one common population and
then follows only ordinary completion; its finite initial duration makes
no difference to the completed topology. For the last two, the two colours
occupy different populations until the root. Omega excludes every merger
in those monochromatic populations, giving exactly the displayed survival
factors. This proves a linear dependence for EVERY n,m, with strict
internal lengths. It is not merely a numerical rank defect, a zero-length
exception, or a port-permutation symmetry.

Thus one cannot invert the ordinary count-column table to recover all
latent binomial weights, then cite their identifiability. A valid proof
must exploit the shared constrained dependence of W,Z across allocations
and the remaining mixed columns. The successful three-tip contrasts
annihilate one common Q direction; in the balanced four-tip case, even
the same-A-port sectors include multiple B-port populations, so their
contribution is not a single Q vector and that argument does not transfer
unchanged.
