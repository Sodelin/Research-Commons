## Verdict and dependency boundary

I found no concrete source-transfer failure. The composition is valid
**conditional on the full strict equal-arm private BOTH finite-forcing
theorem** and the stated original polynomial/neutral source semantics.
The new meromorphic exclusion of infinite persistent carriers by itself
does NOT suffice: private finite forcing additionally needs the countable
compactification, finite-body chronology, and uniform mixed-target local
exclusion. This audit does not independently certify those analytic/local
steps. If their synthesis is withdrawn, both whole-source claims lose their
key premise and must be downgraded accordingly.

The checked source conclusion is restricted to all-pair COMMON calibration
and natural BOTH full rooted topology data. It is not full general G4,
INDEPENDENT-only forcing, or a result for arbitrary correlated entrance
states/exported registers. Nothing here establishes effective caps or Lean
verification.

## 1. Does COMMON calibration actually constrain INDEPENDENT routes?

The critical point is not merely equal numerical path lengths. The COMMON
crossed-cherry/comb formulas give E[X] and E[X^3]; their exact Jensen
equality forces each directed exclusive survival to be constant in EVERY
complete natural bit configuration, since every configuration has positive
probability.

I challenged the passage from those COMMON configurations to separately
chosen INDEPENDENT paths. Take any complete A route and B route, and stop
them at their first common vertex w. Their prefixes below w are disjoint,
so no hybrid bit is assigned incompatible parents on those prefixes.
Choose one common continuation above w and extend all unused bits. This
constructs a COMMON configuration with exactly those two prefixes and
first meeting w. Consequently the directed exclusive durations of every
pair of independently chosen routes equal their calibrated values.

Hold the B route fixed. Its possible first meetings with varying A routes
are nested vertices on that one B route. Moving to a distinct older vertex
adds a nonempty positive population path and strictly changes B's exclusive
duration. Hence its meeting is independent of the A route. Reverse A,B;
the same argument fixes it independently of the B route. There is one
actual vertex v for all route pairs. Equal sums on unrelated paths cannot
defeat this nested-path argument.

This checks the substantive geometry underlying the October11
[actual-calendar separation refinement](../../../2026-10-11-dot-g4-calibrated-meeting-source-bridge-0315z/CALIBRATED-MEETING-TO-SOURCE-SEPARATION.md).
At the calendar cut after both incoming edge exits at v, the selected
panels are simultaneously at v. Before that cut they occupy disjoint
populations. Natural INDEPENDENT routing reads no COMMON register, so the
conditional product does survive averaging the natural initialization.
For a general correlated old-history mixture this factorization would be
false; the claimed theorem does not use that broader entrance contract.

## 2. Are the decoded objects strict private words, with literal cells?

The cut-child condition is essential. In the bridge/block ancestry toward
one selected taxon, a relevant hybrid exits its blob through its child
bridge. Two successive ancestral hybrids therefore cannot remain inside
one such blob. After deleting irrelevant branches only in the selected
calculation, the one relevant hybrid's two parents have ordinary paths to
their first common ancestor. This is one actual two-arm cell, not an
abstract factor fitted to scalar survivals.

The fixed meeting geometry puts every exclusive cell entirely before v.
The COMMON exclusive duration is the sum of the actual selected arm
durations and ordinary passages. Varying one natural bit while holding
all others fixed proves equal arms individually; ties in numerical
parameters do not tie the independent natural bit draws.

Each arm contains a positive original edge. Between consecutive relevant
cells, the connecting passage includes a positive original bridge edge.
Thus internal ordinary gaps are positive. The target and rival may have a
zero terminal selected segment, but applying the same fixed E_epsilon on
both ends makes the auxiliary words strictly admissible for the private
theorem. Their capped kernels are computed from already decoded kernels;
no modified physical observation is requested. Ordinary E_epsilon has a
positive triangular diagonal at every cap and is algebraically invertible,
so later equality of padded laws permits cancellation. No signed inverse
is used as a biological source.

## 3. Is there an unknown-upper-source error in the decoder?

For a mixed-colour genealogy with no monochromatic nontrivial clade,
opacity excludes all lower mergers and gives b_a b_b Q_(a+b). Natural
neutrality makes Q exchangeable in the current opaque roots. Its value can
still depend on the unknown reticulate upper source.

The decoder first determines b2 from three-root symmetry. At step n>=3,
a balanced mixed comb on n+1 leaves has both colour counts at most n−1,
so its lower no-merger factors are already known. This determines the
unknown upper comb probability Q_(n+1), which then gives b_n from the
n-versus-one comb. Balanced colouring of every rooted shape similarly
recovers the full upper completed law needed by the triangular forest
inversion. There is no circular dependence on b_n in that recursion.

In the inversion, a contributing lower forest must refine the observed
forest read from branches off the B-spine. Equal component counts force
the same forest, and the diagonal upper comb probability is positive.
All coefficients are therefore known from those finite legal observations,
despite changes in the rival's exterior. The same physical tuple supplies
every row. This avoids the genuinely invalid shortcut of comparing two
different unknown exterior matrices as though they were equal.

## 4. Does a private response bound really bound the whole graph?

For an original hybrid h, its child bridge separates a nonempty descendant
taxon set D. If D contained every taxon, the child endpoint would be a
stable ancestor below the retained root, contradicting root-LSA. Choose
A in D and B outside. A must traverse h; B cannot enter the descendant
component in the ancestral direction. The calibrated meeting argument
therefore puts h in the A-exclusive word as one of its literal cells.
It cannot disappear as an unobserved shared upper hybrid for this pair.

The full private finite-forcing theorem, if valid, gives an entire-law
equality for each of finitely many target exclusive words. A finite strict
equal-arm chain's cell count is an all-law invariant: its diagonal's
quadratic and linear asymptotic coefficients determine the duration/bias
terms, and the remaining logarithmic coefficient is L/2. Endpoint padding
adds no cells. Hence every pair's literal count equals the target count,
and summing over all ordered pairs bounds every rival hybrid at least
once. No simultaneous edge assignment of the pairwise words is needed.

Finally the binary degree equations give E=2|X|+3h−2, so the derived h
bound leaves finitely many original graph shapes, including parallel-edge
occurrences. The inherited finite-shape polynomial/Hilbert statement then
applies with one parameter tuple per actual source. This final step does
not add a previously unavailable size hypothesis; it uses the bound
derived from the preceding legal observations.
