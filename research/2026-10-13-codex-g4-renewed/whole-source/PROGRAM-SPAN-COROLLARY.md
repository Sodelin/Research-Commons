## 1. Actual finite joint programs can expose a deterministic response by linear postprocessing

Keep the h original hybrid sites H, distinct original IDs, same target/rival shared tuple, and the target promise that every target hybrid lies in H. Let Omega={0,1}^H be the finite set of complete deterministic parent assignments.

For mechanism M in {COMMON, INDEPENDENT}, suppose the actually authorized rows r=1,...,r_M first draw one COMPLETE assignment sigma from a supplied joint law pi_(M,r) on Omega, then force every current lineage at every marked site according to that assignment for the entire locus. Unmarked rival sites retain their natural mechanism M. These are full assignment programs, not a product of undocumented marginal controls. A row leaving a marked site naturally INDEPENDENT cannot in general be represented this way by a single once-drawn bit, so such rows are not silently included.

Let P_M be the r_M by 2^h matrix of those known joint configuration probabilities. Assume some same sigma_0 in Omega obeys

```
e_(sigma_0) in row_span(P_COMMON),
e_(sigma_0) in row_span(P_INDEPENDENT).
```

Choose exact coefficient vectors c_COMMON,c_INDEPENDENT satisfying c_M^T P_M=e_(sigma_0)^T. They need not be nonnegative. Full column rank of each matrix is sufficient but not necessary.

For any actual source N, finite-copy allocation and final-topology event q, source conditioning gives the exact identity

```
response_(N,M,r,q) = sum_sigma P_M[r,sigma] response_(N,M,sigma,q).
```

Here the conditional deterministic responses are proof variables. The experiment observes only the authorized mixture responses on the left. Linear postprocessing yields

```
response_(N,M,sigma_0,q)
  = sum_r c_M[r] response_(N,M,r,q).                 (1)
```

One graph and one physical parameter tuple provide every term. Correlation among controlled sites is retained in P_M. The coefficients depend only on the supplied program laws, not on a refitted rival or on the sample size.

Ask each authorized mixture row for the finite allocations used in the inherited ordinary-target paired certificate. If an arbitrary rival matches the target on those legal queries, (1) makes their two sigma_0 forced-row laws match on that certificate. The controlled-resolution adapter then excludes every rival hybrid outside H. The bounded-shape continuation gives semantic finite forcing of the entire declared response law.

This is not a claim that randomized-only menus automatically reveal their deterministic summands. It states an explicit, verifiable sufficient linear identity under which a particular summand's response is already a function of legal observations. A single strictly mixed program usually fails the condition.

As a finite scalar illustration, for h=1 take two full assignment programs with parent0 probabilities p!=q. With Y_p=pF_0+(1-p)F_1 and Y_q=qF_0+(1-q)F_1,

```
F_0 = ((1-q)Y_p-(1-p)Y_q)/(p-q),
F_1 = (p Y_q-q Y_p)/(p-q).
```

Both mechanism rows must provide the applicable program pair, with the same original tuple. This algebra does not estimate finite-data probabilities or claim stable numerical inversion near p=q.

## 2. The cap can be uniform over target parameters at fixed n,h and interface

The fully forced or program-span certificate supplies a hybrid bound h from at most the inherited forced allocations of size n+2, executed across the finite legal program family. After that bound, finitely many admitted source shapes and ID placements remain.

Inherited ALL-CAP TheoremE is uniform in the two shapes' positive parameter tuples, not just pointwise at one target. Fix n,h, the finite program/observation interface and any declared parameter-tie grammar. Maximize its finite shape-pair caps across the finite shape list. Together with n+2, this gives a **semantic finite cap depending on n,h and the interface** that works for every target in the present fully resolving branch against all initially unknown-size admitted rivals.

No formula or executable procedure computing that maximum is provided by the Hilbert basis argument. The theorem is stronger than a promise of a rival-size bound: the bound h is first derived from the actual legal observation equalities. It remains a stronger-menu branch and does not bound hidden equal-arm chronology for a natural reticulate target without these program identities.
