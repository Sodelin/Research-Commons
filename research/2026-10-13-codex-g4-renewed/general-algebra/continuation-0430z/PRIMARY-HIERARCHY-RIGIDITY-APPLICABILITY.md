## 1. Grossman--Larson positivity has a stronger algebraic hypothesis

Shengjun Zhang, [Positivity Rigidity for Grossman--Larson Characters and
the Kingman Face of the Hoffman Rooted-Tree Graph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31026),
version 1. Inspected returned PDF pages 1--5, 7--12 and 15, particularly
Theorems A/B (page 3), Theorem 3.4 (page 9), and Lemma 3.8 (page 11).

The paper's real character is a MULTIPLICATIVE linear functional on the
Grossman--Larson grafting algebra, nonnegative on its unlabelled tree
basis. The smallest commutator gives

    F_tau F_C3 - F_C3 F_tau = F_Y,

where Y branches away from its root. Multiplicativity into the real
numbers makes this commutator vanish. Positivity then propagates that
zero through positive grafting products to every non-path-forest tree.
This is a concrete, finite initial zero followed by algebraic propagation.

For the larger class of normalized positive left Pieri STATES,
Theorem B instead needs chain identities at EVERY level m>=3. It also
states that for each m there is a state satisfying all the other chain
identities but not the one at m. Lemma 3.8 expresses each defect as a
positive sum of trees branching at the corresponding depths. Thus the
same paper explicitly separates its strong character rigidity from a
countable, independent state test family. Its exposed Bauer face does
not automatically have a finite exposing catalogue.

Original coalescent completed topology probabilities are not supplied
as characters of this grafting algebra. They are laws of binary
leaf-labelled genealogies, with current-root routing and chronological
ordinary evolution; disjoint topology events do not acquire the stated
Grossman--Larson multiplication rule from opacity or pruning. In
particular, attaching a unary dummy root to a binary genealogy puts its
branching away from that root, while the genealogy has positive ordinary
Kingman probability. That direct basis-preserving augmentation therefore
cannot be a positive character: the primary theorem would force its
positive event weight to zero. This checks one direct encoding, not every
possible representation.

Remark 3.7 (pages 10--11) is another useful distinction: in the DIFFERENT
Connes--Kreimer algebra, arbitrary nonnegative tree weights extend to a
positive forest-basis character, so that generic character construction
imposes no branching rigidity. Switching algebras cannot preserve the
rigidity conclusion without proving its actual product hypotheses.

**Applicability gate:** construct a source-derived positive functional
with the relevant algebra identities, one actual tuple across all rows,
and a finite legal topology adapter. No such chronological adapter was
found here. The primary theorem is not a proof of G4 and its independent
state examples are not admitted coalescent rivals.

## 2. Recursive paintboxes identify an all-level growth boundary

Shengjun Zhang, [Recursive Paintboxes and the Martin Boundary of the
Hoffman Rooted-Tree Graph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30135), version 1.
Inspected returned pages 1--2, 4, 12--13, 15, 25--27, 31, 34, 42 and 47.
Relevant passages: Definition 4.2 (page 12), Theorem 4.27 (page 31),
Definition 5.1 and Lemma 5.3 (page 34).

The sampling construction labels VERTICES: the least label in each
recursive block is its root, and the other labels independently choose
child boxes or dust. The Hoffman growth chain adds a leaf vertex, and
centrality imposes uniform endpoint-conditioned weighted path laws.
These are distinct assumptions from exchangeable leaf labels with
genealogical pruning. Original G2 projectivity does not assert these
growth cotransitions or centrality.

The boundary is described by deterministic recursive paintboxes modulo
agreement of their finite sampling laws at ALL levels, and every central
law is a mixture over that boundary. Its marked root encoding is

    nu = sum_i p_i^2 delta_(p_i,z_i),

with child marks z_i and countably many possible root masses. Lemma 5.3
recovers it through all collision coordinates
`sum_i p_i^k F(z_i)`, k>=2, with a separating class of F. The accompanying
small-box bound is an approximation estimate, not exact finite
identification. There is no inspected theorem producing the required
finite chronological certificate for an unknown-size physical rival.

The finite atom trick would be useful IF the legally recovered object
for a fixed source were a finite discrete marked measure with a
source-valid nonnegative annihilating polynomial. A finite parameter
source does not itself give that premise: the current finite witnessed
clade masses at the correctly canceled B-first interface have universal
support, as proved in the [signed chronology successor](../../whole-source/continuation-0430z/SIGNED-CHRONOLOGICAL-BRIDGE-ATTEMPT.md).
The actual all-law cohort catalogue and these fixed finite witnesses are
different objects. No latent marked root-density experiment is admitted.

## 3. Exchangeable hierarchy representation applies, but omits time

Noah Forman, Chris Haulk and Jim Pitman,
[A representation of exchangeable hierarchies by sampling from random
real trees](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.5619), returned version 4.
Inspected returned pages 1--3 and 5--6. The article appeared in
Probability Theory and Related Fields 172 (2018); the arXiv version
identifier is retained separately from the publication date.

Definition 2 is precisely a consistent sequence of finite restricted
hierarchies; Theorem 2 gives the extreme-point/conditional independently
generated decomposition; Theorem 5 gives a random rooted weighted real
tree representing the hierarchy conditionally on its tail. These
representation premises are available for the A-only positive projective
completed rows in the signed-prefix argument. They justify a general
hierarchy description, not a finite source word or parameter realization.

The paper says on page 1: "time plays no role in our definition of a
hierarchy" and it "includes no information about the order in which
these blocks appear." This is exactly the static chronology limitation
in the master prompt. Equality of every finite hierarchy law, or a tail
representation of it, does not by itself give a finite observation set.
