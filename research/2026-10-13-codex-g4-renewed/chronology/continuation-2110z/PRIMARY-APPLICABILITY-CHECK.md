# Primary-source applicability check for the continuation route

Codex lead G4 researcher, 13 October 2026. Bounded targeted search and source
inspection; not a comprehensive novelty search or formal verification.

[Pila, Note on Carlson's theorem (2003)](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/pila/carlson.pdf)
was reread at the opening theorem and sharpness examples. Its uniqueness
hypotheses justify the zero-type applications here. It does not justify the
older unequal continuation with quadratic vertical growth. Its Gamma-growth
variant still imposes a strict growth tradeoff; it cannot simply remove the
moving-phase issue. The new zero-density argument is a classical
Green/Carleman mechanism, proved explicitly in the accompanying review.

The authoritative [Gamma shift](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.5.E1) and
[uniform Stirling formula](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11.E3) were checked in DLMF.
These provide the Gamma-ratio manipulations and uniform sector bounds; they
do not prove the Gaussian sum's source interpretation or finite forcing.

The search identified Karp and Prilepkina's
[delta-neutral H-function paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06612) and
[general Gamma-ratio inverse-factorial paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10958-018-4036-1)
as directly relevant to the remaining inverse-Mellin analyticity step.
The delegated general-algebra lane reports reading the former's Theorem 1
and the latter's Theorem 5/Corollary 1. Those are fixed-shift results. The
unbounded integer shifts in the present Gaussian sum require a separate
uniform bound; a detailed direct Binet argument and independent audit are
being prepared. No application is accepted solely from the search result.

For the original source question, the primary article by Allman, Ané, Baños
and Rhodes,
[Beyond Level-1: Identifiability of a Class of Galled Tree-Child Networks](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-025-01545-8),
was inspected at its abstract, model definitions and Theorem 5.7. Its
two-sample coalescent result assumes the specified galled tree-child class
and generic parameters and identifies semidirected topology and certain
lengths. Those restrictions do not supply this task's fixed-target,
all-parameter full-law forcing against every admitted unknown-size rival.
No reconstruction of that prior theorem is claimed here.

Sokal's [research statement, Theme 3](https://www.homepages.ucl.ac.uk/~ucahad0/sokal_EPSRC_research.pdf)
also records Gaussian-damped deformed binomial series, warning against any
novelty claim for merely writing such a series. Its discussed root/positivity
questions are not the moving-arity interpolation and uniform source
finite-persistence obligation proved or pursued here.

Correctness rests on the displayed proofs and audits. Applicability rests
on the source factorization and growth hypotheses. Historical novelty is
unassessed. None of this literature search is a Lean verification or a proof
that no stronger prior theorem exists.
