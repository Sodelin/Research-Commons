# No positive ordinary factor for an arbitrary sufficiently early all-bad tail

Contributor: Codex chronology subagent, 13 October 2026. Status: hand-proof candidate, independent review requested. This composes the new uniform small-bad-cell sign theorem with inherited exact source transport; no Lean or computational verification is claimed.

## Statement

Let W be any source-derived countable equal-arm private carrier from the accepted bounded COMMON-clock compactification. Its ordinary weak clock has density in [1/2,1]. Assume the inherited finite-target resource budgets

    sum_i t_i<infinity,
    sum_i log cosh(h_i/2)<infinity,

and suppose its persistent cell starts have infimum zero which is not attained. If there exists epsilon>0 such that every persistent cell starting before epsilon is bad,

    h_i^2>=2t_i,

then

    A_n(W)>0 eventually,    lim_n A_n(W)^(1/n)=1.         (1)

No spacing rule, relative size of a cell and its position, or polynomial lower bound on bias/strength is assumed. The carrier need not be a finite graph and is not advertised as an admitted exact rival.

Consequently W has no factorization E_a R at all complete forest caps with a>0 and one positive projective exchangeable opaque-coarsening residual R. This includes every finite strict target with positive first ordinary duration. If an actual represented initial ordinary prefix E_c was removed before defining W, the resulting endpoint cannot equal a finite target whose first ordinary duration a exceeds c.

The exact new implication for the open G4 compactification is: an endpoint-equal no-first carrier with c<a must have good cells h_i^2<2t_i accumulating at its initial boundary. The previously necessary superlogarithmic bad diagonal reservoir is not enough; the tail cannot be eventually all-bad.

## Proof

The resource budgets imply that only finitely many cells fail the small-cell hypotheses of [uniform positivity](SMALL-BAD-CELL-UNIFORM-POSITIVITY.md). Their starts are all positive. Shrink epsilon to remove these finitely many exceptions. Every cell starting before epsilon now has A_n(B_i)>0 for every n>=4.

Choose a positive cell-start cut sigma<epsilon, so no persistent interval straddles sigma. Let P_sigma be the actual generalized source prefix and R_sigma its tail. Every persistent cell in P_sigma is a small bad cell, and every ordinary weak passage has zero primitive. Finite retained products, the exact cocycle, and fixed-cap passage to their source-defined limit give, for every selected cell i starting at s_i<sigma,

    A_n(P_sigma) >= chi_n(P_i) A_n(B_i),                 (2)

where P_i is its actual prefix. No cell's route, time or genealogy has been made observable.

The inherited source transport inequalities extend to weak ordinary clock densities in [1/2,1] and the countable finite-threshold limit. In the original COMMON clock they give

    chi_n(P_i)>=exp(-(2n-3)s_i),
    chi_n(P_sigma)<=exp(-(n-2)sigma).                    (3)

The source-uniform negative bound applies to the tail itself: A_n(R_sigma)>=-1/20. Consequently the exact composition identity yields

    A_n(W)>=exp(-(2n-3)s_i) A_n(B_i)
                       -(1/20)exp(-(n-2)sigma).         (4)

Each bad cell is biased because t_i>0. The independently reviewed fixed-cell asymptotic gives

    A_n(B_i)/n -> kappa_i=(1-4g_i(1-g_i))/16>0.          (5)

There are cell starts arbitrarily close to zero. Choose i with 2s_i<sigma. Equations (4)-(5) imply eventual strict positivity and

    liminf_n A_n(W)^(1/n)>=exp(-2s_i).

The same argument holds for arbitrarily small positive s_i, hence the liminf is at least one. The inherited projective bound |A_n(W)|<=n^2 gives the reverse limsup. This proves (1).

If W=E_a R with a>0 and one positive projective opaque-coarsening residual, finite triangular algebra and the projective primitive bound give

    |A_n(W)|<=n^2 exp(-(2n-3)a).

This contradicts (1). If W is preceded by a represented E_c, exact cancellation at each finite cap preserves the positive residual comparison because the target supplies E_(a-c) times its actual tail. No physical negative-time source is introduced.

## Boundary retained

The result does not prove that every early mixed tail has zero exponential rate. Interlaced good cells can have negative primitive, and their total negative bound does not by itself control exponential cancellation. It also does not prove that the bad reservoir must itself accumulate at the first boundary; its diagonal necessity is unweighted by source position. The whole original-source observer bridge remains separate from this calibrated private carrier implication.

The previous sparse-carrier theorem is inherited and not re-presented as new. This corollary removes its particular spacing/strength hypotheses for the eventually all-bad chronological class, using the new source-uniform sign theorem.
