# Analytic finite persistence and private BOTH finite forcing

Codex G4 lead and delegated chronology/critical-review contributors,
13 October 2026. **Independently hand-reviewed result; original G4 remains open.**

Every fixed finite strict equal-arm private target admits a finite legal
BOTH observation certificate forcing its entire private law against all
strict finite private rivals of unknown size. Arbitrary unequal-arm rivals
are included by the two-COMMON-row strict-Jensen reduction below. The target
class includes arbitrary fair/biased mixtures and repeated parameters.
This is semantic finite forcing, without an explicit cap or stopping algorithm.

The new argument replaces the unresolved private finite-persistence bridge:
the exact even-copy diagonal has a complex continuation controlled by the
inherited summable clock/bias budgets. Every strict cell has a pole at one
of two fixed points. Infinitely many shrinking cells would force infinitely
many zeros approaching one of those points, incompatible with a finite
target. Existing chronology and local forcing then finish the private theorem.
Complex arities, zeros, and hidden forests are not extra observed quantities.

For clarity, the review's extension is elementary and exact. For any finite
private COMMON rival let X be its conditional ordinary pair survival after
drawing its original independent site bits. Then D2=E X and D3=E X³. The
equal-arm target satisfies D3=D2³. Strict Jensen makes X deterministic;
positivity of every original bit assignment makes each rival's two arm
exposures equal. Thus adding those two legal COMMON rows reduces all private
rivals to the proved theorem. No conditional bit is separately observed.

The proved [fixed-exterior adapter](../../whole-source/FIXED-EXTERIOR-OBSERVABILITY.md)
transfers this to private-slot replacements in one unchanged admitted
positive exterior. It does not cancel different unknown exteriors.
