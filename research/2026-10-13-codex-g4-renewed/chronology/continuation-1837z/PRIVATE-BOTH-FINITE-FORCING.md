chain the entire COMMON law is E_H; its pair survival is exp(-H).
Thus agreement on the COMMON pair row fixes the same H for every rival.

Suppose no such M exists. For every integer r choose a strict finite W_r
on that same COMMON clock agreeing through INDEPENDENT cap r but differing
at some later permitted private row. Each W_r supplies all its rows with its
own one fixed tuple. This is a contradiction sequence, not an admitted
