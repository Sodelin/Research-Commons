## Local argument checked

The source is the complete forest operator in
[the mixed-target proof](../general-algebra/MIXED-TARGET-DIFFERENTIAL-LOCAL-CANDIDATE.md),
not merely its diagonal. Positive leading ordinary factors are cancelled at
each finite cap. This algebra does not insert negative physical time.

For a first cell with positive arms, the exactly-two-output-root mass measure
has positive atoms at its cohort masses g and 1-g. Conditional finite arm
root counts have Dirichlet frequencies. Proper nonconstant subset sums are
atomless; the first coin changes cohort mass locations, while later parameters
change finite-root history weights. The full source generator therefore makes
the leading-gap derivative contain a nonzero second derivative of an atom.
Shrinking smooth tests isolate it, then isolate the first derivative from
the first coin. At a fair cell the exact full-forest identity is

    Q B = 2 B_t - B_p/4,

so the possible local null direction is (v_a,v_t,v_p)=(4r,-8r,r).
This is not deduced from diagonal data.

The passage from finite rows to these distributional derivatives needs care.
For root masses M_i and a pair sum s, the finite sample statistic has factors
s^n times a Bernstein polynomial in M_i/s. With g in a compact interior
interval, |M_i'| <= C M_i and |s'| <= C(1-s). Thus its first two derivatives
are controlled by bounded expressions n^r(1-s)^r s^(n-r), r<=2, and smooth
Bernstein derivative bounds. For more than two positive roots, these terms
vanish as n tends to infinity. For exactly two roots, C^2 Bernstein convergence
applies. Positive arm time gives all moments of the finite entrance count;
the displayed exponential count bound in the proof suffices to dominate the
sum over root pairs. Time/tail derivatives can be taken after an earlier
fixed positive arm time and are finite signed measures with total variations
bounded by moments of that count. This also covers the second coin derivative
used for a fair tail p derivative. These facts justify the derivative limits;
the infinite hierarchy is a proof device, not an extra observation.

After eliminating the coin variation, the cohort extractor is one fixed
measurable map for the fixed first coin. Cylinder uniqueness for the finite
signed derivative permits its pushforward. The joint diagonals d11=s2(U) and
d20=exp(-t)s2(U), with s2(U)>0, force the remaining first time variation to
vanish. Finite triangular cancellation then peels the tail. This proves the
claimed differential kernel, including repeated parameters.

The physical tangent cone is essential. At a fair target r<=0, and every
all-law null tangent satisfies DH=-4 sum r >=0. Augmenting the law by H
removes the kernel on this cone. Compactness of its unit slice selects finitely
many finite-cap coordinates and gives the local Lipschitz estimate. There is
no asserted unrestricted immersion at fairness.

For arbitrarily many weak extras, w=p(exp(t)-1) and S=sum w control stochastic
telescoping and its O(S^2) cross terms. A single limiting probability measure
records gap, position and duration. At positive duration the normalized
diagonal derivative n(1+exp(t)+...+exp((n-2)t)) grows exponentially, whereas
finite target derivatives grow polynomially. Positivity forces the measure to
duration zero. There the full-forest insertion direction is -2Q. The shifted
null tangent has DH=-2, contradicting the cone sign. This is a BOTH argument:
the equality DH=0 before that shift comes from the actual COMMON clock.

## Claim control

The proof's private neighborhood hypothesis is supplied globally only through
the separate compactification/persistence/boundary arguments. The original
source is then reached only under the calibrated-target decoder hypotheses.
Neither the current audit nor the earlier theorem gives general uncalibrated
natural BOTH forcing, INDEPENDENT-only forcing, or an exact fixed-target rival
after every finite prefix. Those master obligations remain open.

If a counterexample or failed hypothesis is subsequently found, it must be
published with a correction pointer to the precise affected theorem. The
present audit found no such object and does not invent one to answer the
challenge. Its useful outcome is an explicit account of the highest-risk
interchanges and dependencies.
