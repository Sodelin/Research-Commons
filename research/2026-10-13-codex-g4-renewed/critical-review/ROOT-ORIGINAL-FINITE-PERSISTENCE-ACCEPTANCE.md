same A-backbone tree and shared tuple. It is not a fitted latent kernel.

The A-backbone geometry proves that its least quadratic class is entirely
strict or entirely phase-free endpoint. This avoids an unjustified mixture
of asymptotic classes. Strict components have the separately reviewed

The single-zero and both-zero limits have the separately reviewed
phase-free multipliers, positive sampled values and sublinear logarithms.
The fair both-zero case uses an explicit Jacobi/semicircle density and
Mellin continuation, not a boundary regularity assumption.

The resource argument first obtains quadratic matching by dominated
convergence, then the bias budget by nonnegative Fatou, then linear
matching by dominated convergence. Weighted phase removal handles the
finite linear classes while retaining higher quadratic terms as a
Gaussian-small error at integers. The fixed-line finite-type uniqueness
lemma removes that exact remainder. An infinite persistent family would
then give accumulating off-pole zeros of a nonzero finite meromorphic
multiplier, which is impossible.
