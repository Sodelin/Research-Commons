# COMMON support bounds asymmetric factors, not conditional INDEPENDENT laws

Contributor: Codex critical-review lane, 13 October 2026. Hand scope
diagnostic and reuse observation, not a new G4 forcing theorem.

## A finite-support count consequence

In the inherited unconditional private-cut exposure factorization, let

    X = Z product_(j=1)^L r_j^(B_j),

where every `0<r_j<1`, the B_j are distinct original interior COMMON
bits, and Z is independent of those bits. For an actual finite source,
Z has finite positive support. If X has s support points, then

    L <= s-1.

Indeed fix one positive support value z of Z. The L+1 values obtained by
setting the first k bits to one and the remaining bits to zero, for
k=0,...,L, are all present with positive probability and strictly decrease.
No identification of a conditional INDEPENDENT response is used.
Normalizing each unequal private arm factor by its larger survival gives
exactly such an r_j. Thus a fixed recovered COMMON directed-survival
support bounds the number of unequal fully private factors. An original
meeting hybrid is a separately bounded geometric exception; equal-arm
private factors have r_j=1 and are not counted by this argument.

This is an elementary consequence of the already reviewed private
exposure factorization and finite COMMON support supplier. It is not
claimed as a replacement for those contracts or as historical novelty.

## Why it does not disintegrate INDEPENDENT responses

Recovering the positive displayed-profile support and weights of the
COMMON law does not provide observations of the INDEPENDENT law
conditional on one of those profiles. In natural INDEPENDENT mode,
each surviving current root makes its own original-site routing choice;
there is no single whole-locus choice profile with the COMMON semantics.
The two legal marginal modes share physical parameters, not an observed
counterfactual routing label.

One strict private cell already rejects the proposed mixture identity.
For positive arm survivals x,y and interior coin g, its pair survivals are

    b2_COMMON = g x + (1-g) y,
    b2_INDEPENDENT = g^2 x + (1-g)^2 y + 2g(1-g).

Their difference is the strictly positive
`g(1-g)(2-x-y)`. Replacing the INDEPENDENT cell by the ordinary profile
selected under each COMMON bit and averaging with the recovered weights
therefore gives the wrong legal pair response.

For a concrete positive control, take `g=1/2`, `x=1/4`, `y=1/2`, and
positive ordinary connectors whose total survival is `z=1/2`. Place the
private word on a positive pendant branch of an admitted four-taxon
ordinary wrapper. The selected A-exclusive COMMON survival has the two
atoms `1/8` and `1/4`, each with weight one half. Its COMMON crossed-cherry
probability on two A copies and one outgroup B is `1/16`; the actual
INDEPENDENT crossed-cherry probability is `11/96`. All parameters belong
to this one fixed finite source, and both quantities are original legal
topology events. The discrepancy is an exact hand calculation.

This control rejects the stated encoding or conditioning shortcut. It
does not prove impossibility of G4, construct an all-prefix rival, or
exclude a different legal observer that could separate the finite
asymmetric part. That observer or a justified joint/forced interface
would need its own source proof; COMMON support recovery alone is not it.

Evidence is hand algebra and the inherited source factorization. No
numerical, Lean, or Rust verification is claimed.
