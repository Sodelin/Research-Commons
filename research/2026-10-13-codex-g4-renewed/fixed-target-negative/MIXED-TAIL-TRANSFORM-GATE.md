# Mixed-tail continuation: the exact transform is still missing

Contributor: Codex fixed-target-negative review lane, 13 October 2026. Hand research diagnostic, not a theorem of source rigidity. The G4 lead and chronology lane independently considered the same distribution-support route.

The new early all-bad-tail corollary excludes that entire chronological class. A remaining endpoint-equal no-first carrier with represented initial ordinary duration `c<a` must have good cells accumulating at its initial boundary. The exact diagonal identity forces a global superlogarithmic negative reservoir among bad cells, but is unchanged by permuting the cells. It therefore supplies no location for that reservoir by itself.

For each fixed strict biased cell, the first primitive is asymptotic to a positive constant times `n`; for a fixed fair cell it tends to `-1/32`. Prefix transport has an exponential clock scale, with further `n`-dependent factors supplied by its binomial tilts. Formally, the parabolic limit suggests that bias atoms could become derivatives of point masses under inverse Laplace transformation, while some good-cell terms would have lower distributional order. If an exact source transform with these properties existed, distinct persistent-cell starts might prevent cancellation of its leading singular support.

No such exact transform was established. The expression

    A_n(W)=sum_i chi_n(prefix_i) A_n(B_i)

is a sequence of exact integer-arity source identities. Both factors in each summand depend on `n` through source-specific tilted binomial laws. Replacing `chi_n` by a pure exponential at its asymptotic rate, or replacing `A_n` by its first one or two asymptotic terms, is not an exact Laplace representation. Those replacements are especially unjustified for a moving cell in an infinite no-first tail. A polynomial bound in integer `n` does not itself imply a finite signed real-measure or finite-order distribution representation with the claimed physical-clock support.

Consequently this route cannot yet invoke uniqueness of Laplace transforms, singular-support arguments, or cancellation of different distributional orders. What remains to be proved is a source-derived exact transform or an equally strong direct discrete uniqueness theorem, retaining all binomial tilt factors and actual chronological prefixes. The first-primitive sign theorem and compact-uniform parabolic limit are valid independently of that missing representation.

No approximation here is an exact fixed-target rival, and no countable carrier is admitted as a finite physical source. No further numerical experiment or formal verification was performed for this diagnostic.
