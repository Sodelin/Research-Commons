## Exact source calculation

Let labels 1,2,3 enter the box. Put F=P3 with centre 1 and edges 12 and 13. A natural legal full-forest event is

    A_F = {1 is in a different output component from both 2 and 3}.

It permits 2 and 3 to merge. Write b2 and b3 for the source's pair and triple no-merger probabilities. Projectivity and exchangeability imply

    Pr(A_F)=(b2+b3)/2.                                      (1)

Indeed, each of the three particular two-block partitions has the same probability a. Pair survival is b2=b3+2a, while A_F has probability b3+a. This is an actual endpoint event; no hidden routing variable is observed.

For a positive ordinary passage E(q), q=exp(-t), formula (1) is

    Pr(A_F)=(q+q^3)/2.

The static routing kernel is the constant q, so its two-edge-star density is q^2. Their difference is

    q(1-q)^2/2 > 0.                                        (2)

Directly in current-root dynamics: the no-event path contributes exp(-3t). The permitted merger 23 at time s contributes integral_0^t exp(-3s)exp(-(t-s)) ds. After that merger, the two forbidden leaf-pair clocks have become ONE current-root clock. Their sum is (exp(-t)+exp(-3t))/2. Counting the two original forbidden pairs at rate one each after this merger would violate opacity.

For one unequal-arm bare cell B(x,y,g), set h=1-g. Its actual diagonals and auxiliary star density are

    a=g^2 x+h^2 y+2gh,
    b=g^3 x^3+h^3 y^3+3g^2 h x+3gh^2 y,
    M=g(gx+h)^2+h(g+hy)^2.

Exact expansion gives

    (a+b)/2-M = [g^3 x(1-x)^2+h^3 y(1-y)^2]/2 > 0.         (3)

For equal arms q this specializes to q(1-3g(1-g))(1-q)^2/2. All source parameters remain strict. Thus replacing latent motif products by legal graph-avoidance events already fails in a single source factor, before chronology or unknown size is considered.

## A bound that does hold at every finite length

For each ordinary or bare-cell factor j, denote its two diagonals by a_j,b_j and its auxiliary star by M_j. Source projectivity gives 0<b_j<=a_j. Formula (2) or (3) gives M_j<(a_j+b_j)/2 for every strict factor.

For nonnegative pairs with a_j>=b_j,

    product_j (a_j+b_j)/2 <= [product_j a_j+product_j b_j]/2. (4)

To verify (4), write a_j=m_j+d_j, b_j=m_j-d_j, with m_j>=d_j>=0. The right side equals the sum of all even-cardinality products in the d_j,m_j expansion; the empty product is product_j m_j and all others are nonnegative.

Both the no-merger diagonals and the static star multiply under serial concatenation. Hence every nonempty strict finite private word obeys

    b2(W)^2 <= M(W) < [b2(W)+b3(W)]/2 = Pr_W(A_F).         (5)

The lower inequality is the usual degree-variance identity for a kernel: M is the expectation of squared weighted degree and b2 its expected degree. Equivalently it follows factorwise. For a bare cell,

    M-a^2=gh[g(1-x)-h(1-y)]^2 >=0.

Equality in the lower bound for a finite word holds exactly when every bare cell has balanced losses g(1-x)=h(1-y); ordinary factors have constant degree. Under an independently justified equal-arm BOTH reduction, that means all cell coins are fair. This observation does not re-prove or extend the accepted all-fair forcing theorem.

Crucially, the target itself has a strict gap in the upper bound. Formula (5) therefore supplies no zero predicate for a general strict target and no rank bound. It is a diagnostic inequality, not the missing adapter.
