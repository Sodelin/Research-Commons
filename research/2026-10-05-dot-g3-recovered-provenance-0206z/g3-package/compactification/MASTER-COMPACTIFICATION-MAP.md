## 2. The inherited common compactification

Let c run through the finite source-complete retained-core family, with all protected original IDs retained. Let D_c be the actual original-core parameter domain, in finite survival/inheritance coordinates, including all fixed/read-only values and existing ties. Let S_s be the actual strict finite-word family for physical slot s at its required finite cap. If the interface pairs mechanisms or retains a register, S_s means the actual COUPLED slot tuple generated by one physical parameter assignment, not independent marginal families.

Write C_s=closure(S_s) in the finite forest probability space, including singular and zero-pair-survival kernels. Let F_c be the complete joint continuous polynomial response compiler. Then the actual image I and its closure obey

    I = union_c F_c(D_c x product_s S_s),
    closure(I) = union_c F_c(closure(D_c) x product_s C_s).       (1)

Only physically independent fresh slots are product factors. Any genuine cross-slot or slot-to-core parameter constraint requires the closure of the corresponding JOINT parameter/kernel set instead; a product of marginal closures would not preserve it. Merely writing such a tie nominally in D_c while treating the slot as independent would also be invalid. The accepted source grammar does not add such arbitrary fresh-position constraints.

**Proof of the second identity.** Each core domain lies in a finite bounded survival/inheritance cube; its closure is compact and retains the given fixed values and constraints by taking closure of the actual domain, rather than independently closing a list of inequalities. Each C_s is a closed subset of a finite product of probability simplexes and hence compact. The finite product is compact. Its continuous image is compact, and the finite union on the right is closed and contains I.

Conversely, strict core points and actual finite slot words are dense in each corresponding closure. Simultaneously choose such approximants for the finitely many factors and insert all words into that same legal core. The result is ONE admitted positive source for every approximant, with one original parameter assignment across every row. The joint polynomial response converges to F_c of the closure tuple. This gives the reverse inclusion. Equivalently, a convergent sequence of source profiles can first select one of the finitely many cores and then take a compact subsequence of its parameters and slot kernels. No endpoint parameter is declared an actual source. QED.

This is the elementary compact-product formulation of the already accepted G6 source-faithful joint closure. The G6 finite positive approximation/net construction, not this compactness observation alone, supplies effectivity. That inherited effectivity assertion is restricted to the reviewed G6 finite-profile, FIXED-MECHANISM contracts. A paired or enlarged joint family has the compactness identity above, but needs its own applicable SAME-parameter uniform approximation theorem before effective joint nets are inferred; separately approximating its mechanisms is insufficient.

## 3. The three named boundary branches are retained together

**Coarsening.** A fixed finite observation channel/coarsening Lambda is continuous. Since closure(I) is compact,

    closure(Lambda(I)) = Lambda(closure(I)).

The same holds for any separately admitted finite continuous observation compilation. Therefore coarsening causes no missing compactification branch. It can enlarge the fibre and hide every useful survival event; positivity of a coarse coordinate cannot be promoted to a full rooted-topology floor.

**Core-parameter faces.** Formula(1) includes the actual closure of D_c, including zero/one survival or inheritance limits when permitted as limits by that domain. These are analysis points, not admitted positive parameters. A profile represented on one such face may still have a different strict witness or a witness in another core. Merely locating a face witness is neither SAT nor NO.

**Unobserved survival degeneration.** Formula(1) uses full C_s and does not require b2>0 or b3>0. No observed survival floor is added. The ordered finite-loss normal form and the nonsingular interior theorem cover useful portions of these closures, but their missing hypotheses do not remove the remaining points from(1).

In particular the inherited G6 clipping estimate is uniform. If K is a source-derived prefix and L any legal suffix, then at cap m

    d(K*L,K) <= binom(m,2) b2(K),                         (2)

because the whole already-formed forest can change only if at least two current roots remain; projectivity and the pair union bound control that event. The same inequality extends to closed source limits. It handles hidden near-zero pair survival without an observation oracle and preserves the already formed genealogy. It is an approximation statement, not exact suffix deletion at positive b2.

For a single fixed mechanism, a useful descriptive consequence is that the b2=0 stratum is the closure of {P*E(0): P is an actual positive finite prefix}, where E(0) is the closed ordinary complete-coalescence operator. Given K with b2(K)=0, take actual P_n->K; (2) gives P_n*E(0)->K. Conversely each P*E(0) is a zero-pair closure kernel. E(0) is not a positive finite internal population, and this description is not finite strict attainment. The earlier subtrees and their genealogy shapes are preserved: b2(K)=0 does NOT imply K=E(0).

For a paired-mode or conditional-register tuple, (2) applies componentwise. Use beta2(K)=max_j b2(K_j) over the relevant retained components/conditional rows and d=max_j TV, and require the suffix to leave the retained read-only register unchanged. Then d(K*L,K)<=binom(m,2)beta2(K). The joint zero-survival stratum means beta2=0, so EVERY retained component has collapsed. Its completed-prefix closure uses the SAME physical ordinary E(0) limit in every component. A single mode or marginal pair-survival coordinate does not establish this joint premise.

## 4. What remains to be decided

The common compactification covers all three branches at the closure level. The missing master statement is selection of the strict finite subimage in(1):

    Is there some c, theta in D_c and FINITE strict slot words,
    using one assignment jointly, with F_c(theta,words)=p?

Equivalent profiles can have many closure witnesses. Every alternative core and every coupled strict fibre must be considered before terminal NO. Passing polynomial identities or finding a closure tuple does not establish realization.

The effective G6 clouds give approximations to distance from closure(I). They do not decide exact zero distance. Even after closure membership is known, exact finite strict attainment remains separate. A total computable input-dependent bound on ONE realizing source, a terminating coupled-word method, or a valid impossibility result embedded in this actual class is still required.

The regular and second-order finite-pivot results apply when one witness has the stated coherent jointly legal pivot presentation and full response/image-chart hypotheses. They are tactical sufficient criteria. Their bounded-index necessary condition is not a substitute for the compactification branches above, and a presentation with no useful two-sided pivots may yield only a vacuous condition.
