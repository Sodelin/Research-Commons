# Signed-change and interval wording correction

This tiny successor corrects wording in the [original protocol at main 59446c2](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/59446c27064798ec4d317412c408846a7a664056/research/2026-10-08-cloud-alphagenome-source-review-sol-0010z/VALIDATION-PLAN.md).

PSI and proximal usage levels belong to [0,1]; their signed REF-to-ALT differences belong to [−1,1]. The [plan](VALIDATION-PLAN.md) and [machine-readable protocol](VALIDATION-PROTOCOL.json) now state MAE of signed changes in fraction units explicitly. The planned 95% bootstrap intervals are labeled nominal, with coverage for these datasets unestablished.

The metrics, 2,000 component/locus resamples, split rules, source gates and unfitted comparison method are unchanged. This correction supplies no experimental result, admitted benchmark or G1–G7 completion.
