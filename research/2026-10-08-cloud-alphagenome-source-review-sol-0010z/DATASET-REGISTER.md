Siraj supplies a concrete **measured combined-variant candidate** rather than an inferred combination of single-variant measurements. Its RNA/DNA reporter activity is an expression/regulatory endpoint. It is not an endogenous RNA-seq assay of the target gene in an intact tissue; interpreting a genomic RNA-seq/CAGE score against that assay is a separate, explicitly labeled transport evaluation. HepG2 is not equivalent to liver merely because both have hepatic associations, and HEK293 is not interchangeable with either. A missing matched biosample or defensible readout correspondence prevents primary endpoint admission.

The official TERT example, `chr5:1295113 G>A` plus `chr5:1295135 G>A`, selected with liver `UBERON:0002107`, remains a hypothetical cis reproduction. Its reference and annotation checks belong to the application implementation. It supplies no measured combined label here. DNM1 is a reproduction of the Atlas paper's own experiment, **not an independent benchmark**. Neither is admitted to held-out evaluation.
