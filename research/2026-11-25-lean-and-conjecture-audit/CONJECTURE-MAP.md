**Concrete example of successful reuse:** Sutherland reports that the published RH/GRH proofs were combined with the pinned [Robin1984](https://github.com/kimihiro64/Robin1984/commit/5ce18bf3) work to obtain Lean statements for Robin, Lagarias and a corrected cyclotomic ideal criterion with no RH hypothesis in the resulting statements. This is the standard for promoting a connection: match exact statements and compatible pins, replay the composition, and examine its axioms. It is independent upstream work, not a new Commons theorem.

## Twin-prime intake: preserve the Chen weights

The [earlier exact restricted-list identity](../2026-11-23-goldbach-residue-obstruction/argument-catalog/continuance/three-proof-checkpoints.md#twin-primes-exact-condition-on-the-restricted-list) already records its unweighted Liouville/Möbius and square correction with 2,034 finite checks. The [other-gaps example](../2026-11-23-goldbach-residue-obstruction/argument-catalog/continuance/README.md#twin-primes-other-gaps-alone-do-not-force-gap-two) is a synthetic obstruction, not a claim about the behavior of primes. The weighted formulation below is an intake target, not a new analytic result.

If an explicitly supported family has prime p and Ω(p+2)∈{1,2}, where Ω counts prime factors **with multiplicity**, and uses nonnegative weights w_p, set W=∑w_p and S=∑w_p λ(p+2). Its weight on prime p+2 is exactly (W−S)/2, since the two-factor cases, including squares, have λ=+1. A fixed strict fractional deficit S≤(1−δ)W on arbitrarily late windows with W>0 would therefore suffice. The open input is a bound on this exact supported weighted sum, or another rigorously equivalent fixed-shift estimate. An assertion about unrestricted μ(p+2), such as the [Lü–Wang–Wu preprint claim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20242v2), does not distinguish one from three distinct prime factors and does not address this restricted weighted class. [Murty–Vatwani](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2017.05.011) is a theorem conditional on additional shifted-Möbius progression distribution and Elliott–Halberstam; do not count those assumptions as discharged by Dirichlet GRH or by the Goldbach model.
