### 2. Exact questions we answered
SAT, kernel-checked in Commons. Lean verifies that one Boolean helper can be eliminated pointwise for arbitrary finite positive and negative requirement families. The paired selectors for the three-clause example have counts 5, 1, 1, 1 among eight equally likely triples. No independence assumption enters the pointwise projection; fairness enters only the counts. [Compiler and axiom receipt](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/8628e9207768672ec08f44b6f5b05c4c6b911e0a/research/2026-11-25-lean-and-conjecture-audit/LEAN-REPLAY.md).

SAT, exact finite replay and hand proof. With two same-polarity ports per terminal and fair degree-three labels, the 43,248 isolated cores have minimum cross ratio 17/32. The 2,815,584 cores with exactly one connected extra variable have minimum 35/64. Neither realizes the required 1/2 in those classes. A separate eight-clause witness has branch ratios 1 and pooled ratio 511/517; pooling cannot preserve the branch minimum blindly. The sharp 9/10 pooling factor is a hand result for the stated nested-table conditions, not a general gadget obstruction. [Isolated replay](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/fabd166ef70c5dd0a117e6a6cb1a10c6dd6caebe/research/2026-11-24-vp-vnp-boolean-boundary/COMPACT-CUT-RANK-ISOLATED-FOUR-PORT-EXHAUSTION.md) and [pooling proof](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/c275cc5724d5895ea4d0f67bdd22d25c9240b29f/research/2026-11-24-vp-vnp-boolean-boundary/COMPACT-CUT-RANK-HELPER-SWITCH-POOLING.md).

Conductor-23 RH/Artin, hand proof. The one-sided balanced split-prime bound is equivalent to the joint critical-line assertion for the three specified factors. Removing all critical-line modes changes the full test by O(√X), not the equivalence. Under joint RH, fixed localized pieces can still move adversely above square-root scale after any fixed list of zero modes is removed. This rules out an individual-box proof strategy, not the conjecture. [Exact scope and spectral identity](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/7311a4eb97c8c33a289d6c70978d1561dde74514/research/2026-11-23-rh-proof-reading/2026-11-25-conductor23-uniform-critical-mode-removal.md).

Goldbach, mixed verification. Five narrow Lean components cover cutoff, budget, assembly, factor boundaries and finite coverage. The newer pen-and-paper result shows cancellation for the combined small-prime marks and divisor model near the square-root divisor level. The model proof does not bound the genuine endpoint residual or prove the all-large-powers-of-two assertion. [Existing Lean catalog](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/8628e9207768672ec08f44b6f5b05c4c6b911e0a/research/2026-11-23-goldbach-residue-obstruction/argument-catalog) and [stronger model range](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/df06475120762f1ba71847a2ea668981e1ad4006/research/2026-11-25-five-lane-parallel-pass/CHECKPOINT.md#goldbach-larger-joint-model-range-not-a-bound-on-the-true-row).

Twin Primes, hand proof and finite replay. On the declared restricted candidate list, twin weight is half the total weight minus half the Liouville-signed weight; the Möbius form needs a separate prime-square correction. This is exact bookkeeping, and an earlier unweighted version has 2,034 finite checks. Other small gaps cannot be treated as a certificate for gap two. [Restricted-list checkpoint](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/7311a4eb97c8c33a289d6c70978d1561dde74514/research/2026-11-23-goldbach-residue-obstruction/argument-catalog/continuance/three-proof-checkpoints.md#twin-primes-exact-condition-on-the-restricted-list).

BSD, hand proof with exact checks. A centered rank-two Haar symmetric pairing splits with probability 1/2 for odd p. Its finite-precision null-direction count has constant first moment and linearly growing second moment. These are exact properties of the local model, with conditional arithmetic transfer under specified lifts. They do not identify a canonical spectral pairing or produce a regulator formula. [Split law](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/28cc3ba8a4ca82ee1c1ca1f73a9cdd9a258a21e1/research/2026-11-24-vp-vnp-boolean-boundary/BSD-TAME-BINARY-ISOTROPY-LAW.md) and [direction-count and moment proof](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/df06475120762f1ba71847a2ea668981e1ad4006/research/2026-11-25-five-lane-parallel-pass/CHECKPOINT.md#bsd-how-long-two-zero-height-directions-look-the-same).

Yang–Mills, hand proof in a comparison model. The flowed Gaussian plaquette channel can display an apparent physical gap 4π/ℓ at fixed spatial torus side ℓ and still be gapless in infinite volume. Its fifth inverse spectral moment grows as log(ℓ)/(80π²) before normalization. This closes the Gaussian diagnostic, not a theorem about Wilson theory. [Moment proof](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/7f7837680f95079a6e82395bd3f3e0e26ea55afc/research/2026-11-24-yang-mills-mass-gap-reading/FLOWED-GAUSSIAN-INVERSE-SPECTRAL-MOMENTS.md).

G3/G4, internal scoped results only. The fixture covariance rejects at 170 hypothetical copies within the specified second-moment test; the matched equal-arm G4 grammar has exact pair-survival closure [C,(1+C)/2]. Neither finishes total source recognition or finite all-rival forcing. ## The open targets worth tracking
These statements are next obligations, not claims of progress inherited automatically from an analogy. The external classical/Dirichlet results should be pinned as dependencies where definitions match.

### Closest to current exact reductions
1. Conductor-23 nonabelian RH. Prove the signed balanced lower bound on the actual split-prime statistic at all sufficiently large X, for every positive epsilon at the X^(1/2+epsilon) scale, or give a different rigorous bridge. The verified abelian results can discharge the ζ and Dirichlet factors after the factorization is matched. This targets one nonabelian factor, not general automorphic GRH. Ramanujan Δ RH remains a separate target. [Audited reduction](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/7311a4eb97c8c33a289d6c70978d1561dde74514/research/2026-11-23-rh-proof-reading/2026-11-25-conductor23-uniform-critical-mode-removal.md).
2. Binary Goldbach, first at powers of two. Prove a compatible lower bound for the remaining signed row and other unresolved contributions at the same N=2^k. A result for a low-divisor model, a favorable average across N, or the ternary theorem does not discharge that pointwise requirement. [Signed model and its boundary](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/df06475120762f1ba71847a2ea668981e1ad4006/research/2026-11-25-five-lane-parallel-pass/CHECKPOINT.md#goldbach-larger-joint-model-range-not-a-bound-on-the-true-row).
3. Twin Primes, fixed shift two. Establish a signed Liouville deficit on the exact weighted Chen-candidate support, or a different fixed-shift prime-pair lower bound. Preserve multiplicity of prime factors and the actual weights. Goldbach reflects a variable across N; twin primes translate it by 2. [Polymath bounded gaps](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.4897) and [Murty–Vatwani's conditional result](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2017.05.011) are comparison points, not gap-two certificates.
4. SAT/complexity. Understand how the covariance term and branch slack behave when multiple auxiliary variables are eliminated, or exhibit an exact fair OR gadget outside the exhausted classes. A success here is a gadget theorem, not itself P vs NP or VP vs VNP. [Shared-switch proof](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/c275cc5724d5895ea4d0f67bdd22d25c9240b29f/research/2026-11-24-vp-vnp-boolean-boundary/COMPACT-CUT-RANK-HELPER-SWITCH-POOLING.md).

### Parked research, available for Lean audit
5. BSD. Match a canonical rank-two height/regulator to the second Taylor coefficient, with the arithmetic assumptions explicit. Local distributions are checks on a proposed model, not a regulator identity. [Official problem statement](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/birch-and-swinnerton-dyer-conjecture/).
6. Yang–Mills. For the actual Wilson theory, obtain continuum reconstruction and a volume-stable physical-time gap for a dense gauge-invariant family. A single finite-volume plateau cannot certify it. [Jaffe–Witten statement](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf).

No theorem-level dependency on RH, Goldbach, or Twin Primes was found. Shared-dependence bookkeeping and finite obstruction tests are plausible tools. A prime-based bridge would need to preserve the same source, IDs, shared parameters and quantifiers. ## Lean rigor and audit guide
For the temporary catch-up period, the Artin, Goldbach and SAT slots check their own existing arguments rather than opening new analytic fronts. The cross-lane slot checks Twin Primes, Yang–Mills and BSD. The 15-minute cadence is unchanged; the independent Δ schedule is separate. Use the GitHub handoff to record one next owner per item.

### What to pin
1. Source theorem or exact conjecture: title, prior author where known, exact quantifiers and assumptions, repository path and immutable commit.
2. Evidence label: external independently verified result, local Lean kernel result, exact finite replay, hand proof, conditional result, open premise, or failed claim. A theorem conditional on the missing estimate is not a proof of the estimate.
3. Reproduction: compiler and dependency pins, build command and outcome, source hash and named axioms. Record separately whether the proof is in the actual aggregate import graph.
4. After the weakest correct statement compiles, remove assumptions one at a time or strengthen its conclusion. Preserve the original statement. Record any counterexample, including which quantifier, independence condition, cutoff, or normalization failed.
5. Before claiming a named conjecture, verify literal statement match or prove equivalence, run the complete dependency chain, and attribute external results. A GitHub commit or a finite fixture is not automatically a submission.

### First targets by lane
Artin: factor-zero transfer, exact S3 class factors and the balanced spectral correction interface; write analytic estimates as separate obligations. Goldbach: signed transpose, cutoff preservation, budget and residue receipts; use the existing five checked modules. SAT: extend the checked helper rule to the pooled slack identity and single-switch bound, then audit which hypotheses compose. Cross-lane Lean: the twin Liouville and square accounting, BSD finite-precision counts, and Yang–Mills positive spectral criteria with the reconstruction hypotheses visible.

### Storage and handoff
Use the [existing package build and source locks](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/8628e9207768672ec08f44b6f5b05c4c6b911e0a/packages/research/README.md) instead of duplicating dependencies. Keep small immutable source and receipts on GitHub main. Back up durable source, manifests, replay scripts and receipts in a private Drive folder. Do not copy credentials, private reasoning, or rebuildable dependency caches. Copying a backup does not free local disk; no automatic deletion or full-profile rebuild is part of this handoff. Re-run an exact target before promoting an imported theorem or reusing a build result.
