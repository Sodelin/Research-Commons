## 2. The full all-copy observable coordinate ring is non-Noetherian

Let X be the set of these actual finite source descriptions, identified or not by their all-copy laws; either convention gives the same response functions. Fix the original positive private pendant-bridge wrapper and its admitted full passive context menu. Let A be the real algebra of functions on X generated by ALL scalar probabilities of these finite legal response experiments. Its elements use only finitely many response coordinates at a time. Equality in A means equality as functions on every actual source, so all genuine source identities have already been quotiented out.

Write b_n(W) for the no-merger probability on n entering roots. This is an element of A, not an added hidden observation: the inherited crossed-cherry experiment gives an actual rooted-topology event with probability c_n b_n(W), where c_n is a fixed known positive real number supplied by the common exterior. Multiplication by 1/c_n is permitted scalar postprocessing. Full finite forest tomography is an alternative.

Fix ONCE any q in (0,1), for example q=1/2, and set lambda_n=binom(n,2). For m>=2 define the ideal in the FULL ring A

    J_m = (b_2-q^lambda_2, ..., b_m-q^lambda_m).

**Claim A.** J_m is properly contained in J_(m+1) for EVERY m>=2. Consequently A is not Noetherian.

Proof. The accepted UNIFORM-TIME-DIAGONAL-FINAL theorem, at cap m+1 and the SAME q, gives a strict finite actual word W* with

    (b_2(W*),...,b_(m+1)(W*))
       = (q^lambda_2,...,q^lambda_(m+1)),

and differential rank m for the full diagonal map at that physical parameter point. All coordinates are supplied by one shared parameter tuple. The real submersion theorem therefore gives an open neighborhood of this vector in the image of a strict local physical-word chart. Choose in that neighborhood a vector with the first m-1 coordinates unchanged and its last coordinate changed. The resulting actual finite word V satisfies

    b_n(V)=q^lambda_n  (2<=n<=m),
    b_(m+1)(V)!=q^lambda_(m+1).

Evaluation at this genuine V is a real-algebra homomorphism A->R. It kills every generator of J_m and hence the entire ideal, but it does not kill b_(m+1)-q^lambda_(m+1). Thus that next generator cannot belong to J_m. This proves the strict inclusion, including when coefficients of a putative ideal identity involve arbitrary OTHER full-forest/context coordinates. QED.

This last point matters: showing that an algebra contains a non-Noetherian subalgebra would not suffice. The evaluation argument proves strictness of the EXTENDED ideals inside A itself.

## 3. Finite-state Hankel flatness already fails on the ordinary target

The natural allocation-concatenation Hankel matrix of the no-merger sequence is

    H_ij = b_(i+j), i,j>=0.

For the positive ordinary target E(q), b_n=q^lambda_n. Its N-by-N initial block has entries

    q^lambda_(i+j) = q^lambda_i q^lambda_j (q^i)^j,
    0<=i,j<N.

Extracting the nonzero row and column factors leaves a Vandermonde matrix at the distinct points 1,q,...,q^(N-1). Therefore

    det H_[0,N-1]
      = q^(2 sum_(i=0)^(N-1) lambda_i)
        product_(0<=i<j<N) (q^j-q^i) != 0.

**Claim B.** The raw no-merger Hankel matrix has infinite rank even for a zero-bigon positive ordinary target. Thus finiteness of the physical source graph does not imply a finite-dimensional linear representation with b_n=u M^n v, nor a finite-rank stopping certificate for this observable representation.

The statement concerns this specified representation. It does not exclude every nonlinear transformation, tree-indexed representation, or other full-forest finite certificate. All these b_n values are available from the original legal response menu by the preceding source bridge.

## 4. Ordinary-clock normalization does not give a positive moment model

One might remove the quadratic ordinary factor before testing rank. For any actual word K define

    r_n(K)=b_n(K)/b_2(K)^lambda_n.

Then r_0=r_1=r_2=1. The 3-by-3 moment-shaped Hankel determinant is exactly

    det [[1,1,1],[1,1,r_3],[1,r_3,r_4]]
       = -(r_3-1)^2.

This is negative whenever r_3 differs from one, independently of r_4.

There is a completely strict source witness:

    K = E(1/2) B(1/2,1/2,1/2) E(1/2).

The actual bigon formula gives b_2(B)=3/4 and b_3(B)=13/32. The two ordinary pads contribute 1/4 at arity two and 1/64 at arity three, so

    b_2(K)=3/16,
    b_3(K)=13/2048,
    r_3(K)=26/27.

Consequently this Hankel determinant is -1/729. Every displayed parameter is strict; there is one source shared across all coordinates.

**Claim C.** The ordinarily normalized no-merger sequence is not, for all actual sources, a moment sequence of a positive measure on the real line. Such a moment sequence would have positive-semidefinite Hankel matrices, since each quadratic form is the integral of a polynomial square. Hence a positive-moment flat-extension theorem cannot be applied to these normalized coordinates without a new source-specific representation theorem. It would already reject the physical example above.

More generally, the principal submatrix on indices {0,1,k} has determinant

    -(r_(k+1)-r_k)^2.

If an actual source matches the ordinary diagonals for many arities and first differs later, the initial rank-one normalized blocks therefore do not justify a globally positive moment extension. The issue is a false positivity premise, not numerical rank tolerance. No source coefficient expansion or numerical calculation is used in this exact example.

This is separate from the inherited symbolic q-holonomic obstruction: that result excludes a specified symbolic recurrence class for a one-bigon family. Claims B and C directly test the raw finite-state rank and normalized positive-moment proposals at their actual endpoint coordinates.

## 5. What G3 witness selection would actually have to preserve

The distinction between one representative and all representatives must be kept exact. A G3 bound need only supply ONE finite source realizing each nonempty supplied data fibre. It need not bound every source in that fibre.

For a fixed actual target T, this ordinary witness condition is especially weak: for every true finite prefix, T itself is already a witness of length L(T). Thus a uniform short witness exists for every target prefix whether or not those prefixes exclude any longer rival. No G4 stopping conclusion follows from that fact alone.

The relevant fibres for stopping exclude T's ENTIRE all-copy equivalence class. Let C_m(T) be the set of actual private words W satisfying BOTH:

1. W matches every complete labelled forest row of T through cap m;
2. W differs from T on at least one later original legal response.

By the inherited private full-topology tomography and all-copy normal form, condition 2 is exactly all-copy inequivalence in this private class.

Define the following bounded-representative property for T:

    There exists a finite B such that, for EVERY m,
    if C_m(T) is nonempty, it contains at least ONE V with L(V)<=B.

There is deliberately no bound on the length of EVERY member of C_m(T).

**Claim D.** In the actual private chain class with its accepted supplied-shape known-locus theorem, this bounded-INEQUIVALENT-representative property is equivalent to mathematical target-specific finite forcing.

Forward proof. Let T have supplied finite shape length L*. For each j=0,...,B, the accepted all-copy equality locus and fixed-pair Hilbert/QE argument give a finite cap d(L*,j) such that complete equality through that cap between those two shapes implies all-copy equality. Take d to be their finite maximum. If C_d(T) were nonempty, the bounded-representative property would supply V of length at most B in it. The relevant known-shape theorem would instead make V equivalent to T, a contradiction. Hence C_d(T) is empty.

Reverse proof. Suppose some m* has C_(m*)(T) empty. The sets C_m decrease with m. For each of the finitely many m<m* at which C_m is nonempty, choose one finite V_m in it, and take B to be the maximum of their lengths (or zero if there are none). This B has the property above. QED.

Claim D is a precise diagnosis of the missing implication, not a newly solved premise or a stronger source-size requirement. It is also not a decision procedure by itself: a mathematical B need not be computably obtainable or certified from observed data.

### Marking one later disagreement does not repair ordinary G3 automatically

For a FIXED later experiment e, the finite system

    prefix_m(W)=prefix_m(T),
    response_e(W)!=response_e(T)

is the appropriate disagreement-marked feasibility question. On a supplied finite shape, it is an ordinary finite semialgebraic formula; an auxiliary proof variable can encode the disequality as z*(response_e(W)-response_e(T))=1. That variable is not a physical source parameter or new observation.

Original G3 finite exact-profile recognition does not automatically solve this enlarged existential inequality problem. Even a method that did so for each fixed e would leave the unbounded quantifier over later experiments. A bound for witnesses of each augmented finite system that grows with e or m does not imply Claim D's uniform B. Conversely, requiring a bound on all source descriptions is unnecessary.

The accepted all-size polynomial-identity test R+E*D_M has exactly a different scope: it certifies universal polynomial equalities on a fixed finite declared profile. D_M and the coordinate set depend on that profile's cap. It is neither a witness bound for the full positive fibre nor a disagreement-preserving bound uniform over later experiments. Claim A provides a concrete reason not to identify those changing finite algebras with one Noetherian all-copy object.

## 6. Effectivity and full-original scope remain explicit

If an effective version of the bounded-inequivalent-representative property were proved from a supplied private target description, the already accepted pair-shape finder would compute a determining cap. That would first solve a supplied-description target branch, stronger input than the original exact-response-only problem.

Under an ADDITIONAL promise of effectively enumerable finite algebraic source descriptions and exact comparison of their legal responses with the target oracle, uniformly certified candidate caps could support candidate enumeration and finite acceptance. This promise must be proved or supplied; it is not inferred from arbitrary exact response values. A general original target or multiport/shared-register interface also needs its own exhaustive equality relation and candidate extraction mechanism.

The concrete results here do not supply those missing inputs or Claim D's bound. They remove the global-Noetherian and elementary Hankel/flatness shortcuts on actual sources. They leave open whether the FULL forest target fibre becomes finitely forcing through a source-positive rank invariant of a different kind, another inequality, or a target-specific exact certificate.

The continuing positive attack is therefore on the full-target equations and preservation of a genuine later disagreement, not on bounding all word descriptions or repeatedly extending a scalar diagnostic cap. General G4 remains OPEN; no new full-prefix counterexample or positive stopping theorem is claimed.
