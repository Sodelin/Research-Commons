## 1. Behavioral coding: Gabriel and Lonborg
Greeson, Gabriel, Mulcahy, King Hendrickson, Lonborg and Holloway (2022), “An Evaluation of Ethograms Measuring Distinct Features of Enrichment Use by Captive Chimpanzees (Pan troglodytes),” Animals 12, 2029, DOI 10.3390/ani12162029. Methods, coding definitions and discussion checked.
[Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9404423/)
[Gabriel — official CWU profile](https://www.cwu.edu/about/directory/psychology/kara-gabriel.php)

The study records object use, manipulation and social context in selected archival videos. Event frequencies and state durations are different quantities. It evaluates coding and individual variation; a welfare-effect experiment is a separate task.

Candidate benefit: an explicit export/recoding audit could establish which joint summaries remain recoverable. Define the raw record space X, export C, and researcher-chosen target T. The decisive check is C(x)=C(y) implies T(x)=T(y) for admitted records. A counterexample pinpoints an information loss; a factorization certificate establishes only that target's recoverability. The record schema, overlap conventions and missing-video rules remain to be supplied. It does not infer welfare or temporal independence.

Later context: the 2026 PWIN protocol already addresses longitudinal, context-sensitive welfare assessment and behavioral categorization for laboratory macaques. Its May 19 publication describes subsequent deployment/testing. It is a different species/context, not a replacement validation of the CWU chimpanzee study.
[PWIN primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1017/awf.2026.10061)
[December 2025 enrichment review citing the CWU study](https://doi.org/10.3389/fvets.2025.1724923)

## 2. Computational representation: Andonie
Andonie and Muşat (2024), “Signs and Supersigns in Deep Learning,” DOI 10.15837/ijccc.2024.1.6392. Full paper, particularly sections 4–5, checked.
[Primary paper](https://univagora.ro/jour/index.php/ijccc/article/download/6392/2009/14167)
[Andonie — official CWU bibliography](https://www.cwu.edu/about/directory/computer-science/razvan-andonie.php)
[2022 information-bottleneck predecessor](https://univagora.ro/jour/index.php/ijccc/article/view/4650)

The authors already investigate spatial-entropy proxies, layer freezing and pruning, with empirical accuracy/compression results. Generic aggregation or entropy-loss framing would overlap this prior work.

Candidate benefit: certify a specified compressed representation for a specified output and input domain, or exhibit two admitted inputs with the same representation but different required outputs. Entropy decrease alone does not prove such preservation. A useful approximate version needs an output metric, domain, error budget and comparison with existing neural-network verification methods. Current later-work screening is incomplete; no novel theorem or unresolved field-wide question is asserted.

## 3. Shared mathematical supplier and limits
[Exact supplier — Identifiability.lean, immutable September 30 source](https://github.com/Sodelin/Formalizing-Soft-Sciences/blob/8e665d2a9f6168e6448b2ca49fccbec87d57d3ad/SocialScience/Identifiability.lean)

The inspected declaration postprocess_preserves_equivalence states that applying a fixed transformation cannot distinguish outputs already equal on every allowed probe. This is an elementary exact fact. It neither proves that a chosen compression is sufficient nor supplies a noise model. The factorization test above is a proposed study-specific contract. No new compile or study-specific proof occurred in this audit. G6 requires calibrated confidence sets and its other stated hypotheses; G7's finite exact response contract does not automatically apply to these observations.

## 4. Conditional psychology lead
Radeke and Stahelski (2023), DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1174662, compare social-perception ratings of generated faces across samples. Relevant limitations were read. A measurement-bias sensitivity analysis is conceivable only after a defensible measurement model and bias bounds. Ratings do not establish actual personality traits. Full later-work review remains unfinished.
