# CWU research connections: dated audit addendum
16 October 2026. Prepared by dot. Literature review and connection design; no outreach, new empirical analysis, or newly accepted theorem.

## Executive summary
Two earlier CWU connections have public source dossiers: Tsuga phylogenetic discordance and Klyve/Thue–Morse juggling sequences. The broader audit adds several useful candidates, with very different kinds of gaps. Geology offers explicit observation and inverse-model uncertainty questions; developmental biology offers established nonlinear modeling and important 2026 coarse-graining prior. Psychology, philosophy and sociology require their actual experimental or interpretive assumptions, rather than a forced mathematical model.

No claim is made that all CWU research has been covered. This addendum records what was screened, what was read in detail, what is already known, and what remains unverified. None of the new candidates below is a solved field open problem or an accepted G1–G7 transfer.

## 2. Developmental biology: Scoville, ModEDI and later prediction theory
CWU connection: Alison Scoville appears on the current Biology roster.
https://www.cwu.edu/academics/biology/faculty-and-staff-directory.php

Brooks, Roberts, Scoville and Jagodzinski (2017), “ModEDI: An extendable software architecture for examining the effects of developmental interactions on evolutionary trajectories,” DOI 10.1109/ICCABS.2017.8114300.
https://facultyweb.cs.wwu.edu/~jagodzf/pubs/brooksICCABS2017.pdf

Methods, results and future-work sections were read. The existing software implements Rice's developmental-interaction theory and contrasts fixed genetic covariance with a nonlinear Daphnia trait model. Its stated extension concerns efficient custom trait relationships and simulations. Parameters include both empirical inputs and plausible choices. Existing simulation support is therefore real; universal biological validity is not established.

Essential prior: Rice (2002), DOI 10.1073/pnas.202620999.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC137749/

Essential later progress: Milocco and Uller, PNAS, 11 March 2026, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2529820123.
https://lisandromilocco.github.io/papers/MiloccoUller_2026_PNAS.pdf

The latter already links developmental sensitivities to quantitative-genetic parameters under small perturbations over a finite developmental window. It uses time-series Kalman filtering, separates developmental from measurement noise, and explains covariance alignment using developmental and population-level conditions. Relevant equations and estimation sections were read; the public code was not run.
https://github.com/lisandromilocco/DevStat-Bridge

Milocco's 2026 perspective, “Mapping the possible: developmental coarse-graining for evolutionary prediction,” published 8 August and typeset 17 September, DOI 10.1093/evolinnean/kzag023, explicitly asks how developmental accessibility should be represented for prediction across timescales. It proposes empirical estimation of successive developmental descriptions. Publisher-indexed sections were inspected; direct full-page retrieval failed.
https://academic.oup.com/evolinnean/article/5/1/kzag023/8757727?searchresult=1

Possible contribution: audit a particular published surrogate's prediction error or target preservation. G2 requires an actual dynamics-preserving condition; matching covariance or low-rank sensitivity alone is insufficient. G6 needs calibrated observation laws and effective image bounds. G7's exact affine response registry does not automatically handle noisy developmental time series. Prior theory and computational implementations must be used as the starting point. Original phylogenetic G3/G4 remain different obligations.

## 3. Geology: observability and nonunique source reconstruction
Szeliga, Reisinger and MacInnes (2022), “Historical tsunami observability for Izu–Bonin–Mariana sources,” DOI 10.1186/s40623-022-01748-6.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40623-022-01748-6

Full main-text methods and discussion were read. Nine source scenarios are modeled at 23 locations. Wave-height categories depend on tidal range; surviving historical records additionally depend on preservation, access and observation conditions. The authors explicitly suggest studying horizontal-flow observability at low-wave-height locations. Their bounds depend on the scenario class and record completeness.

MacInnes's April 2026 Scripps seminar confirms current CWU affiliation and ongoing 1952/2025 Kamchatka comparison:
https://scripps.ucsd.edu/events/geophysics-planetary-seminar-0429-dr-breanyn-macinnes

Existing method: Cifuentes-Lobos et al. (2023), DOI 10.1007/s00477-023-02397-1, already generates heterogeneous slip fields and restricts them using observations. Abstract and references read; full article subscription-limited.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00477-023-02397-1
https://github.com/RCifuentesLobos/PaleoTsunami/tree/v1.0.0

Later primary progress: Lorito et al., 25 September 2026, DOI 10.1038/s43247-026-04050-5, jointly inverts SWOT, DART, InSAR and GNSS data, explicitly retains nonuniqueness, and reports a regularized misfit-weighted ensemble. Inversion methods were read.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s43247-026-04050-5
https://doi.org/10.82554/sdl-582

Possible G6 interface: certify that every admitted source compatible with observations and calibrated error bounds has the same finite scientific label. An ensemble mean does not by itself establish this universal claim. Required inputs include source-class coverage, missingness, forward-model error and effective outer bounds. A fixed linear Green-function surrogate may offer a narrow G7 interface; nonlinear inundation is not automatically an exact affine response model. This is a proposed verification/application target, with no novelty claim or executed certificate.

## 4. Philosophy and consciousness: Bartlett's testability argument has a direct 2026 response
Current CWU profile:
https://www.cwu.edu/about/directory/philosophy-religious-studies/gary-bartlett.php

Bartlett (2022), “Does integrated information theory make testable predictions about the role of silent neurons in consciousness?”, DOI 10.1093/nc/niac015.
https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2022/1/niac015/6761527

Ponce de Leon and Yoshimi, 8 August 2026, “Integrated information theory (IIT) and the testability of the silent neuron predictions,” DOI 10.1093/nc/niag037.
https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2026/1/niag037/8756945

Relevant arguments were read. The response distinguishes a silent main complex from an entirely silent brain, discusses external reporting mechanisms and proposed tests, and acknowledges that its reporting explanation needs additional formal specification. Thus Bartlett's 2022 argument cannot be presented as an uncontested current impossibility result.

Existing experimental-design work includes Takahashi et al.'s 2025 optogenetic protocol:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11867336/

Possible contribution: make intervention, hidden causal consequences and reporting assumptions explicit, then compare induced observable laws. This can clarify testability under a model; it cannot validate a theory of consciousness. G6/G7 require calibrated models and admissible probes absent from the present bridge. Protocols are not completed experimental results. Later-result coverage is incomplete.

## 5. Anthropology and primate behavior: context-dependent dominance
Funkhouser, Mayhew, Sheeran, Mulcahy and Li (2018), Scientific Reports 8:13909, DOI 10.1038/s41598-018-32243-2.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-32243-2
Current Anthropology roster:
https://www.cwu.edu/academics/anthropology/people.php

Methods and discussion were read. Ranking procedures can agree within a behavioral context while rankings differ across contexts. Missing dyads and uneven observation remain substantive limitations. The authors recommend species-relevant contexts and validation before using ranks to explain other outcomes.

Possible contribution: check whether a particular event aggregation preserves a declared interaction-prediction target. A chronology, observation/missingness model and group-membership contract are necessary. Existing correlations are not a Markov closure theorem. No source-faithful obstruction or predictive certificate has been proved here, and the later-primary review remains incomplete.
