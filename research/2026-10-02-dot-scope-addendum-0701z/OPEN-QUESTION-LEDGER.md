## Active user-authorized extensions

3. **Richer biological observations:** which precisely specified, source-feasible additional readout recovers nonclique graph densities and yields a forcing advantage? Current proposed extension records full independent private itineraries of labelled probes; it changes the original menu. A latent-color oracle, separate pairwise rerouting, or marginal path laws without joint source coupling do not constitute the experiment. The exact contract/proven boundary is in CURRENT-SCOPE.md. It is not old G4 closure.
4. **Sokal reciprocal-zero coefficient positivity:** for every integer k,n≥1, are the coefficients â_{k,n} in x_k(q)=−k q^(1−k)/(1−Σ_{n≥1}â_{k,n}q^n) nonnegative? This is the stronger of the two positivity conjectures and implies ordinary normalized-root coefficient positivity, which implies unit-disk convergence. Closest result: Kuznetsov's all-k rational coefficient formulas and recurrences, with numerical verification for n≤300. These coefficients directly govern graphon part weights c_k(q)=q^(k−1)(1−Σ â_{k,n}q^n)/k. [Primary statement, p.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.02462), [2024 Sokal source](https://www.icms.org.uk/sites/default/files/downloads/sokal.pdf). Latest resolved publication: Math. Comp.95(2026),2917–2939, DOI10.1090/mcom/4131, electronically23July2025; confirmed by the author's [September2026 CV](https://kuznetsovmath.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/cv.pdf). No subsequent all-order solution was found in this targeted pass; that is not a global absence theorem. The exact construction attack will seek a positive recurrence/probabilistic interpretation, not just another coefficient-count ladder.
5. **All-zero unit-disk analyticity/convergence:** do the normalized zero branches extend through |q|<1 as conjectured? Closest established uniform disk is |q|<0.207875, with larger disks for the first three branches, reproduced in Kuznetsov's Theorem1. A proof would justify globally convergent root-weight expansions; real-q all-clique camouflage already exists without it. This is a supporting analytic gate, not an unsolved prerequisite for Shapira–Tyomkyn's theorem.
