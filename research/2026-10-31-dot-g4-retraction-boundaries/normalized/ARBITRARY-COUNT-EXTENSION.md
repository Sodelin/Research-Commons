# Arbitrary-count locally inserted blocks: uniform complete-five retraction

31 October 2026. Hand extension of the fixed-finite-chart proof `245d9611a6f67e8997ef4a6c8c640579472653246aefe52fa257b3c5ec02699f`, pending independent review. The finite-dimensional IFT is applied to measured source summaries, not to an ever-growing list of physical variables. No arbitrary desired summary is asserted physically realizable.

## Source class and exact bounds

Retain the old eight-cell family, its uniformly positive old-coordinate box, fixed pair H, and one chosen adjacent ordinary gap ℓ(τ,x)≥ℓ0>0. Fix a finite B>0. For every sufficiently small τ>0, let J be ANY finite actual strict insertion word with positive connectors, using coupled cells

    p_i=τ z_i,  0<z_i≤1,  rare survival a_i∈(0,1),
    common-arm duration β_i p_i, 0<β_i≤B.

The number of cells is not bounded. All parameters are one actual shared source tuple. Put

    C=−log d2(J),   η=Σ_i z_i³,

and require C≤ℓ0/2. Insert J locally by replacing E_ℓ with E_(ℓ−C)J=E_ℓ F, where F=E_(−C)J. The source remains positive and its pair budget stays H.

For every fixed finite carrier there is a constant A_m, independent of cell count, a_i and β_i in these ranges, such that the individual residuals obey

    ||E_(−k_i)B_i−I||≤A_m p_i³.

Indeed the complete cubic cancellation is analytic in (p,a,β) on a neighbourhood of the compact parameter box p∈[0,τ0], a∈[0,1], β∈[0,B], with the exact pair survival uniformly bounded away from zero. The boundary values are only estimates, not actual inputs. Exact divisibility by p³ and compactness bound the quotient. This uses the full current-root cancellation, not just diagonals.

Move the ordinary factors through the residuals algebraically WITHOUT changing chronological order. Since the cumulative pair/ordinary time of J lies in [0,C]⊂[0,ℓ0/2], conjugation norms are uniformly bounded. The finite product estimate gives

    ||F−I||≤exp(A'_m Σp_i³)−1
            ≤A''_m τ³ η                            (1)

when η and τ are bounded sufficiently small. Ordinary connectors have zero normalized residual. Negative ordinary factors occur only in this proof identity.

For each fixed r≥3, the exact source filtration gives D_r(B_i)=p_i^r A_r(p_i,a_i,β_i), with a uniform compact-box bound. Since logarithmic diagonal differences add under actual composition,

    γ_r:=D_r(F)/τ^r=D_r(J)/τ^r,
    |γ_r|≤A_r Σz_i^r≤A_r η.                         (2)

The last inequality uses r≥3 and z_i≤1. Neither bound has an unrecorded factor equal to word count.

## Finite-dimensional IFT with actual-compatible inputs

Use the finite source summary

    e=(γ3,γ4,γ5,U5),     U5=(F5−I)/τ³.

Its norm is at most Aη by (1),(2). Define an auxiliary analytic extension of the old eight equations as follows:

* add γ_r to the r-th old normalized diagonal equation, r=3,4,5;
* evaluate the five old complete-forest residuals with the local factor I+τ³U5 inserted, and divide them by τ³.

The second operation is jointly analytic at τ=0: the old residual already has a τ³ factor, and its change is divisible by τ³ because the inserted factor has that form. Its x-Jacobian at (τ,x,e)=(0,x0,0) is the original invertible normalized Jacobian. The analytic IFT therefore supplies x=x(τ,e), on a neighbourhood with e-radius independent of small τ, satisfying all eight extended equations and

    ||x(τ,e)−x(τ,0)||≤A_* ||e||≤A_** η.             (3)

The coordinates in e are NOT independently chosen physical observables. For this theorem they are filled with the actual compatible summaries of J. Then the auxiliary equations coincide with the true source equations: ordinary compensation changes no D_r for r≥3, and the inserted complete kernel really is F5. The corrected physical word thus has exact complete-five equality and pair H. The x correction is shared at every higher arity. A generic e in the analytic extension is never declared realizable.

The bound C≤ℓ0/2 and uniform x-box keep the actual shortened pad positive. All old seam pads remain unchanged positive τ; no inverse source or negative physical duration is introduced. This proves retraction uniformly over the UNBOUNDED finite cell count within the stated small-cubic-mass class.

## Interface to a genuine higher residual obstruction

Suppose an inherited higher diagonal has the OLD-FAMILY analytic form

    D_r(K_old(τ,x))=τ^r φ_r(τ,x)

throughout the same x-box. Then for the actual corrected inserted word,

    D_r(K_new)/τ^r = φ_r(τ,x(τ,e))+γ_r.

Consequently its difference from φ_r(τ,x(τ,0)) is bounded by A_r^*η, uniformly in word count. If the inherited solved family has φ_r(τ,x(τ,0))≤−c<0 for sufficiently small τ, choose η<c/(2A_r^*) to retain a negative defect. This is a real source-relative exclusion of exact ordinary matching in that neighbourhood, NOT an assumption that arbitrary complete-kernel residuals have a sign.

The old sixth-diagonal supplier and its strict negative coefficient are bound separately in the accompanying fixed-finite seed review. This extension alone does not substitute an unproved value for c or claim a numerical threshold. It states exactly how that supplier would apply to arbitrary-count small-cubic-mass insertions.

## Limits

This is still local to the inherited old source family. It leaves open larger cubic mass, insertion parameters outside the bounded coupled chart, other compensated profiles, and exact higher-cap centering elsewhere. The source has unrestricted finite inserted count but a fixed adjacent-gap clock reserve and a SMALL aggregate η. The result is not a general private-word nonattainment theorem, much less an all-core G4 positive result.
