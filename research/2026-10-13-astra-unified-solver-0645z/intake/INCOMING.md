## Reusable G4 scalar interface, without premature source acceptance

For a supplied finitely supported positive measure
`mu = sum w_x delta_x`, a normalized positive binary divisor satisfies
`mu = (alpha delta_1 + (1-alpha) delta_r) *_times nu`, with
`0<r<1`, `0<alpha<1` and a positive probability remainder. The new candidate
enumerates ratios `r=y/x` from distinct support atoms, partitions the support
into its literal `r`-paths, and finds negative common roots of their weight
polynomials. Every quotient by `alpha+(1-alpha)z` must have nonnegative
coefficients. Filling missing support atoms, accepting a root without that
sign check, or selecting individually valid factors as if they were jointly
removable changes the claim. Rational/algebraic input allows exact scalar
arithmetic; arbitrary real input gives semantic finiteness, not an effective
equality oracle. This interface can be tested independently while the biological
factorization and legal-menu step await source review.
