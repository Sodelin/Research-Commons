# Every result must be discoverable

Recorded by dot, 2026-10-30 23:41 UTC, at Nolan's explicit request.

## Standing research workflow

Every research context should make its results searchable through the existing Research Commons discovery CLI and programme catalogue. Publication alone is not completion of this workflow. This applies to established results, scoped components, candidate arguments, counterexamples, failed approaches and corrections. Searchability is not mathematical acceptance.

Use the existing canonical claim IDs and source/catalogue mechanisms. Preserve original assumptions, attribution, review status, formal verification status and unresolved master obligations. Do not create competing claim registries or label a component as closing its programme.

Each result handoff should include a descriptive title and search terms, programme/claim association where justified, immutable source links, exact statement and limitations, proof/review/build evidence, and any supersession relationships. Source-only or failed builds must remain distinguishable from kernel-checked results. Keep private data and implementation/coordination details out of the public catalogue; private results require the authorized private catalogue and must not leak through a public index.

## Integration and verification

The discovery CLI owner maintains ingestion and checks coverage against the published source inventory. A result is confirmed searchable only after an actual CLI query returns it with the correct source and status. Separate canonical cards, full-text evidence bodies, path-only archive inventory and programme/output navigation. A path-only hit does not establish full-text indexing. Preserve explicit unavailable/excluded inventories and pagination. Extend coverage without silently dropping older results or weakening integrity checks.

Record code/index commit and tested snapshot separately from deployed executable/site state. Do not report every historical result indexed until the inventory reconciliation supports that statement. Existing historical corpus counts are dated selected coverage, not exhaustive coverage.

## Handoff adoption

All current research contexts have been sent this instruction. Future research handoffs should link this record and include indexing as a completion step. This is a shared workflow preference, not a claim that unrelated or inaccessible chats have already adopted it.
