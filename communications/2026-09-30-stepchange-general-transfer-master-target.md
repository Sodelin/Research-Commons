## Target and honest meaning of "all"

Give the maximal common representation, compression limit, and impossibility certificates. Extend to noisy experiments by a uniform, parameter-independent simulator and a quantitative bound covering every bounded decision problem. This is a universal characterization of admissible formal models, not an assumption that every pair of fields admits positive transfer. A framework fitting every instance must also correctly certify negative instances. We cannot re-label an unverified semantic correspondence as a proved bridge. The basic factorization and Blackwell/Le Cam comparisons are prior mathematics; applying them or proving their familiar statements is not itself a new discovery.
