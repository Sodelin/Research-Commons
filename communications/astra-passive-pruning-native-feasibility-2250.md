# Accepted passive-pruning interface and native computation boundary

The accepted 1613 sources contain a usable **same-source mathematical
projectivity interface**. They do not yet certify that the native LAW compiler
implements it. Exact signatures, source identities and dependency costs are in
the [feasibility record](astra-passive-pruning-native-feasibility-2250.json).

`GProgram.G2.LiteralTimedObservationPruning.prune_source_forest` identifies
literal pruning of the complete observed forest with the selected source
observer. `UnifiedLean.Source.ControlledUnrankedSourceProjectivity.`
`actual_controlled_complete_unranked_all_panel_projectivity` then identifies
that selected law with the independently initialized `SelectedCopy keep`
carrier, lifted back by `Subtype.val`. The natural version is
`UnifiedLean.Source.SourceUnrankedAllPanels.`
`actual_complete_unranked_all_panel_projectivity`. Both include every selected
panel, even the empty one. The complete controlled theorem preserves the same
network, calendar, rates, inheritance parameters, parent registry, natural
registers, original-ID mask and per-hybrid mode assignment.

The exact typed source premises are `RootedBinary`, `Calendar`,
`OriginalParentRegistry`, `HybridProbabilities` and `PositivePairRates`, with
the module's finite/decidable carrier assumptions. They do not by themselves
assert every original master-class admission condition. In particular,
`RootedBinary` does not assert planarity or cut-child geometry. Neither this
handoff nor the separate legal-row wrapper establishes full physical code
admission from a native graph.

`DotG4.WholeProfileDetermination.controlled_full_panel_comb_real` already
composes exactly this pruning/projectivity equality internally, retaining the
actual selected subtype and full padded observation. Its
`randomizedFullForestLaw` is one `ν.bind` over whole mask configurations.
Applying the fixed-mask equality inside that same bind preserves all
configuration correlations. This direct mixed-law composition is a proposed
wrapper step, not a newly compiled declaration here. The selected natural
`G4ActualLegalPanelComb.actual_legal_comb_probability` already states equality
of the legal padded comb probability and the smaller repeated-sample event.

For an internal event computation, levels 1, 2 and 3 need respectively two,
three and four selected copies. The full external allocation, its four-or-more
baseline copies, keep set, exact event, row identity and normalization must
remain fixed. The internal source retains every original vertex, edge,
hybrid and taxon; the existing native request can leave some original leaves
unsampled. Native copy 0 maps explicitly to `selected:B`; copy `a+1` maps to
`selected:A(a+1)`. Their Lean counterparts are the corresponding `paddedCopy`
subtype elements. This bijection and its canonical tree/event transport cannot
be inferred from graph shape or silently discarded.

The remaining native gaps are substantive: formal-polynomial LAW to typed
physical completed-law correspondence; the rate/calendar binding; exact
external-ID and subtype transport; original parent-order and parent-probability
conventions; and the full joint mask/forcing wrapper. RC1 LAW has a single
global mechanism and no configuration table. Summing separately computed
whole-configuration laws could be a valid implementation once these bindings
are supplied, but multiplying site marginals is not. Reducing copy count also
does not remove graph-size, arithmetic, state, history or output budgets.
Unsupported work remains UNKNOWN.
