## Status vocabulary and evidence rule

**Accepted component** = the stated scoped hand result has a recorded review.  
**Compiled component** = a matching source/compiler receipt exists for the precise declaration.  
**Pending review** = a proof or counterexample is submitted but not independently accepted.  
**Open research** = the named result is not established.  
**Prototype / conditional** = executable behavior exists only under its stated contract.  
**Planned** = a future deliverable, not a running task.  
**Proposed / gated** = requires the indicated source, scope or decision gate before extension.

A test, source pin, commit or successful build is evidence only for what it actually checks. All rows retain the [full-master completion standard](../docs/RESEARCH-COMPLETION-STANDARD.md). No percentages or estimated token costs are used.

## What counts as the 31 October deliverable

1. **Biology:** precise G1–G7 master statements, proofs/counterexamples, original-source admission, observed review outcomes, remaining obligations and downstream BIO-1 status. G3 and G4 require actual recognition/stopping conclusions to close those endpoints.
2. **G5 minimality:** distinguish rooted clusters, S and Q; binary versus higher indegree; sufficiency versus matching lower bounds; one-copy panel size versus other resource measures.
3. **Formalization:** source-pinned declaration list, compiler/toolchain and dependency pins, axiom audit, replay commands, resource failures and an explicit hand/formal boundary. Count modules/components accurately; no whole-corpus inference from a subset.
4. **Private software:** private reproducible package and scoped examples, executed acceptance tests and independent comparisons, supported/unsupported inputs, confidence/abstention and provenance. Only shareable research summaries go in Commons.
5. **Literature:** exact primary-source comparison, citations followed in both directions where feasible, corrected attribution/novelty claims, remaining coverage limits and verified author identity.
6. **Transfers:** at most the selected bounded physics/psychology/sociology demonstrations, each with source adapter and failure witness. If a gate fails, deliver the source-based no-go/defer finding instead of an unsupported positive demonstration.
7. **Final explanation:** a readable account of what changed, what can now be used, what cannot, and what genuinely remains unsolved. An honest partial dossier is a useful deliverable but is **not** a substitute for calling a promised unresolved master complete.

- Read current main and the newest lane checkpoint before changing a status. Use immutable evidence links for important changes; preserve older contributions and add supersession links.

- Keep canonical proofs and implementations in their established homes. This page is navigation and coordination, not a duplicate proof corpus.
- Preserve attribution and private/public boundaries. Do not publish identifiable records or private product sources.
- Publish additive edits from fresh main, preserve other contributors' entries, use non-force updates and verify readback. Publication is not peer receipt or truth certification.
