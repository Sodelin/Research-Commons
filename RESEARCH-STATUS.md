## Reading the evidence

- **Accepted hand proof:** the stated mathematical argument has a recorded independent review, with its assumptions and restrictions. Here, independent review generally means another AI research lane; external human peer review is not implied.
- **Lean checked:** an actual successful compiler receipt supports the named statements at pinned source/runtime inputs. A selected component or a list of named axiom reports does not certify every declaration or the whole application.
- **Executed:** code or arithmetic controls ran, with recorded inputs and outcomes. A deterministic control is not an empirical result or a proof of general accuracy.
- **Open / proposed:** the original question or a specified bridge has not been established. A preserved manuscript, source file, inventory, or successful upload does not change that label.

The [completion standard](docs/RESEARCH-COMPLETION-STANDARD.md) requires the original promised conclusion and its downstream bridge. Counts of files, declarations, proposed domains and organizing families are different quantities; none is a completion percentage.
