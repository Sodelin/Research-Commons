## Reading the evidence

An **accepted hand proof** has a recorded review at stated assumptions; here independent reviews generally mean another AI research lane, not external human peer review. **Lean checked** means named source/runtime inputs actually compiled with recorded audit scope. **Executed** means code controls ran; this does not establish empirical accuracy. **Mathematical open** identifies an exact unresolved implication after acceptance reconciliation. **Author-complete, review pending**, **formalization pending**, **implementation pending**, **empirically unadmitted**, and **status unverified** are separate labels. A failed lookup is not mathematical evidence of openness.

Use theorem → assumptions → actual source provider → review → build/audit receipt. A named endpoint audit, a complete owned-declaration census and an entire-repository build are different checks. Do not add overlapping inventory counts or call publication proof of correctness. Historical novelty requires its own prior-work evidence.
