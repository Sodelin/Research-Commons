## First understand the project

Commons is not just a list of conjectures or a folder of Lean files. It connects **sources, observations, compatible explanations, recoverable targets, legal experiments and checkable computational evidence**. The biological G programme supplied the initial detailed source contracts; other fields require their own proofs that those interfaces really match.

The working vision, [Lossless Compression / Modular Maps](docs/MISSION-AND-VISION.md), is a direction rather than a universal theorem. A representation can preserve a chosen target while losing hidden edges, chronology or parameters. Much of this work identifies exactly where that loss occurs and whether another observation can repair it.

### What kind of mathematics, and why?

| Tool | Job in this project | A mistake it must prevent |
|---|---|---|
| Graphs, stochastic processes and joint laws | Define one actual source and all permitted observations. | Fitting each marginal with a different hidden source or treating a new sensor as already available. |
| Identifiability, equivalence classes and inverse problems | Determine which target properties survive observational ambiguity. | Calling a convenient representative the uniquely recovered physical system. |
| Moments, polynomial/semialgebraic methods and analysis | Certify finite constraints, exact identities, positive representations and approximation boundaries. | Treating closure as attainment, an abstract positive measure as a physical source, or a bounded search as global NO. |
| Information/query complexity and design | Prove which legal experiments are necessary/sufficient at a stated cost. | Confusing population-law queries with samples, physical interventions or running time. |
| Lean, certificates, native code and independent checkers | Connect a precisely stated conclusion to inspectable formal or computational evidence. | Equating emitted proof text, tests, successful compilation and full end-to-end refinement. |

The [solver explainer](docs/LEAN-RUST-SOLVER-EXPLAINED.md) separates the actual practical Python/checker/Rust paths, Lean certificate emission, checked examples and the unfinished universal bindings.

### Which “open problems” do we mean?

There are four different categories:
1. **Original project masters**, such as G3's total recognition and G4's finite I-only forcing, with fixed full quantifiers.
2. **Published questions or conjectures**, addressed only at the exact model/statement identified in a linked paper.
3. **Formal/engineering obligations**, such as a verifier's full Lean check or a native certificate's source-binding proof. These are not automatically new mathematical conjectures.
4. **Exploratory connections and empirical goals**, which may be promising but are not adopted solutions or demonstrated improvements.

The current G and field tables below show which category is actually advanced. The [monthly question register](docs/OCTOBER-2026-RESEARCH-MAP.md#question-register-addressed-open-and-only-explored) also covers the less-developed directions. **A dossier is not a solved problem, and a classical theorem repackaged is not a new discovery.**
